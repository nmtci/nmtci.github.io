# Chapter 1027: Great Sage's Heir (10/10), Awakening Desolate Ox

The moment Zhang Yu decided to gather Immortal Dao Technology, Desolate Ox, Immortal Slayer, and the Sect Examination Clone — existing as his multi-threaded thoughts — all sensed his intention.

The Sect Examination Clone said, "Gathering Immortal Dao Technology, is it? Since the main body has come begging me, I might as well show my skills and indulge him. I'll also let Zhang Yu see clearly and understand that our way is the most efficient and the most profitable! Fu Ji, do you have any ideas?"

Fu Ji said, "We definitely can't be like those two idiots, Desolate Ox and Herald God, slowly gathering it ourselves. That efficiency is far too low. We should swindle it out of them, making this world hand over its knowledge to us of its own accord."

Hearing this, the Sect Examination Clone couldn't help but chuckle. "Well said, Fu Ji. Your style of doing things is truly suiting This Prince's taste more and more. What do you plan to do specifically?"

Fu Ji said, "This is the Desolate Ox Great Sage's sea of consciousness world. In the end, every single person here is a part of Desolate Ox's collective will, and Desolate Ox's technological knowledge lies within their memories. Aren't we going to establish our own Monster Protection Organization? Then during the interviews, we'll add a technical interview and have them directly write down the Immortal Dao Technology they master and hand it over to us."

The Sect Examination Clone smiled and nodded. "A good idea. We can do that."

Thus, during the subsequent interviews, the Sect Examination Clone constantly required the interviewees to submit various technical materials, rapidly collecting all sorts of Desolate Ox Immortal Dao knowledge.

Yet, feeling that even this was not fast enough, the clone soon stirred up a new wave of events in this world.

In the video, the Sect Examination Clone spoke with righteous indignation, "Recently, I studied history and discovered that all important Immortal Dao technologies throughout history were invented by monsterfolk. Humans stole the achievements of monsterfolk and disguised them as their own inventions."

Watching the video's popularity surge under the Sect Examination Clone's fueling of the flames, and seeing the people on the internet gradually devolve into fierce arguments over his words, Fu Ji praised, "Brilliant. As long as we keep hyping up this topic, the humans and monsters of this world will continuously bring out all kinds of Immortal Dao materials on their own."

She could not help but sigh with emotion, "This human-monster conflict is truly useful."

Fu Ji had once scoffed at the human-monster conflict, but now, having put it to use herself, she found that it really had its merits.

The only pity she felt was...

Fu Ji said, "What a shame that Kunxu outside has already used this trick for so many years; ordinary methods won't work anymore."

---

Just as the Sect Examination Clone made his move, inside a cell on the other side...

A fellow imprisoned human race cultivator looked respectfully at Immortal Slayer and said, "Mentor, this is the Immortal Dao Technology I have mastered. I am willing to share it so we can study it together."

Watching this unfold, Auspicious God thought to himself, *"The many cultivators of this world are, after all, part of the Desolate Ox, bearing the essence of the Desolate Ox Great Sage. Immortal Slayer's approach can easily mobilize them."*

At this moment, Immortal Slayer had surprisingly become the spiritual mentor to the numerous cultivators in the prison, uniting them to combat the corruption brought upon this world by the Monster God.

With Immortal Slayer taking the lead in sharing, the other cultivators followed suit, offering their own Immortal Dao Technology in hopes of learning and improving together.

Meanwhile, the Desolate Ox continued quietly gathering various materials.

Standing beside him, Herald God thought, *"Does Zhang Yu want to secretly gather information on Immortal Dao Technology?"*

He nodded slightly, analyzing, "True... Immortal Dao wisdom arises from Immortal Dao Power. Without sufficient technological strength as backing, all philosophies are merely flowers in a mirror or the moon reflected in water."

"The collection on the Sect Examination Clone's side is too fragmented, broken, and superficial."

"Immortal Slayer's side has deeper and more complete knowledge, but the efficiency is far too slow."

"Zhang Yu is definitely dissatisfied with the current method! It's time for me to prove myself."

---

*"Too slow."*

In another corner of the world, Zhang Yu felt the various kinds of Immortal Dao knowledge brought in by the Sect Examination Clone, Immortal Slayer, and the Desolate Ox, thinking to himself, *"Although the Desolate Ox's Immortal Dao technical knowledge should exist within this world... scattered across the memories of countless wills, relying solely on this kind of communication, collection, and reorganization is simply too inefficient."*

Zhang Yu thought, *"Nearly two hours have passed in the physical world, which means more than half a year has gone by in this world. I don't have that much time to keep wasting here like this."*

Based on the intelligence gathered from the Sect Examination Clone, Immortal Slayer, and the Desolate Ox, Zhang Yu's mind rapidly analyzed the situation: *"This place is the Desolate Ox's consciousness world, and the memories of the collective will are the memories of the Desolate Ox."*

*"Just as different regions of a brain participate in the storage and processing of different memories, the Desolate Ox's sea of consciousness world should be the same."*

*"Every single will belonging to the Desolate Ox in this sea of consciousness world stores different memories. Then is it possible... that a large amount of technical memories are stored within certain specific wills?"*

Just as Zhang Yu was pondering, he received a report from the Herald God through Desolate Ox.

"Your Highness," said the Herald God, "based on my observations and analyses over these past days, I judge that the vast majority of Desolate Ox's Immortal Dao Technology data resides within the memories of this world's scholars, professors, and experts."

The Herald God explained, "The experts and scholars in the Ten Great Sects are all mercenaries who do whatever they are paid for; even after winning a Celestial Evolution Award, they never forget to peddle their courses. But this sea of consciousness world is different. The scholars, professors, and experts here likely represent Desolate Ox's rationality. They are the beings within the collective will who possess the most technical knowledge, and at present, it seems... they are also the group of wills least involved in the human-monster conflict."

"Therefore, whether it is the Sect Examination Clone or Immortal Slayer, it is difficult to extract knowledge from them."

"And if we were to take the initiative to communicate with them slowly, the efficiency would be far too low."

Listening to the Herald God's report, Zhang Yu nodded slightly. "Since you came to report this to me, I presume you have a solution?"

Herald God said, "This humble one has a few immature thoughts and would ask for Your Highness's guidance."

Zhang Yu replied, "Let's hear them."

The Herald God said, "Since they embody the Desolate Ox's rationality, we will destroy that rationality. First, have the Sect Examination Clone attack them, dragging them into the conflict between humans and monsters. For example, by asking them whose average intelligence is higher — humans or monsters. Then, accuse them of discriminating against monsterfolk, throw them into prison cells, and push them toward Immortal Slayer's faction."

"In this process, these beings who represent the Desolate Ox's rationality will gradually be influenced and forced to take a side in the human-monster conflict."

"And I believe that, as representatives of the Desolate Ox's rationality, they will choose Immortal Slayer's side, because this side opposes the human-monster conflict and the pollution brought by the Celestial Monster Sect. It is relatively more rational and objective."

Zhang Yu nodded slightly. After pondering the Herald God's words in his mind, he said, "Then let's give it a try."

Soon, the Sect Examination Clone sprang into action, directing the increasingly fierce human-monster conflict toward the Immortal Research Realm and dragging numerous professors, scholars, and experts into the fray.

"How dare you say that the average intelligence of monsterfolk is lower than that of the human race? Don't give me any talk about objective facts. Stop pretending to be rational, objective, and neutral — you're simply discriminating against them."

"Monsterfolk were created by humans? Don't cite history to me. You are simply attacking monsterfolk, and I feel ashamed for you."

And as one expert and professor after another came under attack, branded as discriminators against monsterfolk and instigators of human-monster conflict, they were swiftly locked up — where they met Immortal Slayer in their cells.

Through their conversations with Immortal Slayer, they joined him in criticizing the current Monster Protection Organization's extremism and irrationality, analyzing the dangers of continuing down this path, and soon rallied under his banner.

Looking through the technical documents Immortal Slayer had sent over, Zhang Yu quickly synthesized them into cultivation arts.

"As expected, the Immortal Dao knowledge possessed by these technicians is far more systematic and complete, making it much better suited for refining into cultivation arts."

Right at that moment, a dragon monster appeared before Zhang Yu, seemingly out of nowhere.

The newcomer was none other than the Monster God of the collective consciousness.

"Hmph, it seems I still underestimated you," the Monster God said faintly, looking at Zhang Yu. "Diverting strands of your consciousness to join all those arguments was nothing more than a smokescreen, wasn't it? Your true objective was secretly gathering the Immortal Dao Technology of the Desolate Ox?"

At this point, the Monster God let out a cold snort. "But if you think that's enough to get your hands on the Immortal Dao Technology you desire, you're underestimating me far too much."

As if aiming to completely shatter Zhang Yu's confidence, the Monster God continued, "Who do you think was behind the Celestial Monster Sect listing the Monster Saint Cultivation Art? It was the great Celestial Monster Immortal Emperor! His goal was to bait out all of you false successors who dared try to lay your hands on the Monster Saint Cultivation Art."

"Zhang Yu, the moment you expressed interest in purchasing the Heaven-Opening cultivation art, the Celestial Monster Dharma Body, I knew your true aim was the Monster Saint Cultivation Art."

"And how could I not understand the importance of Immortal Dao Technology? Where do you think the source of the Monster Saint Cultivation Art that appeared in Kunxu came from?"

"With guidance from the Celestial Monster Immortal Emperor, I know far better than you where the core technology of the Desolate Ox Great Sage is hidden. And the very first thing I did upon arriving in this world was turn those most crucial technicians... into supporters of the Monster Protection Movement, and then hide them away."

Speaking up to this point, a trace of pride appeared on the Monster God's face. "These individuals are exceptionally rational, and so they understand all the more clearly that the broader trend cannot be opposed — that once this world's ideological transformation begins, it is unstoppable!"

"The more rational they are, the more they act in pursuit of profit. They will inevitably choose the side that brings them the greatest benefit and conform to the ideological tide of this world."

"And those you can recruit now are merely useless wretches I've discarded. None of them possess the core techniques of the Desolate Ox Great Sage in their heads."

Hearing this, Zhang Yu looked calmly at the other party and sighed, "What you are doing now has already twisted the will of the Desolate Ox Great Sage. If you truly are the Great Sage's personal successor as you claim, you shouldn't be doing this."

The Monster God sneered, "Don't act in front of me as if you understand the Desolate Ox Great Sage better than I do."

"I was born to carry on the heritage of the Desolate Ox, and now I am updating his ideals and feeding them back to him."

"As for the core techniques belonging to the Desolate Ox Great Sage, and that Heaven-Opening cultivation art you lack, the Celestial Monster Dharma Body, you will never obtain them. Everything you do in the sea of consciousness world will only be a waste of time!"

Watching the other party's figure dissipate, Zhang Yu thought to himself, *"The collective entity known as the Monster God, serving as the vessel that stirs up the conflict between humans and monsters in the sea of consciousness world and pollutes the Great Sage's will, seems to have also... become more prone to extremes and more easily influenced by emotions because of it."*

Zhang Yu lowered his head, looked at the cultivation arts he had collected in his hands, and thought, *"A waste of time, is it?"*

As he browsed through them, the assembled cultivation arts before his eyes indeed did not contain the Heaven-Opening cultivation art he wished to purchase, but...

*"Basic-grade cultivation art, Ox Monster Great Strength Divine Fist, belongs to..."* Zhang Yu thought to himself, *"the Chain Formation Diagram [Great Sage's Heir]."*

Zhang Yu mused silently, *"This is an original cultivation art belonging to the Desolate Ox Great Sage, yet it seems it was never introduced to Kunxu, much less castrated or updated."*

*"Yet right from this cultivation art, I can directly sense the Desolate Ox Great Sage's most primordial and pure fighting spirit and pursuit."*

The next moment, Zhang Yu's numerous clones remaining inside the Immortal Grotto-Heaven began cultivating this basic fist art together.

*"This Ox Monster Great Strength Divine Fist gathers the martial dao intent condensed from the Desolate Ox Soul-Quelling Heart Art and Crippled Ox Body-Sacrifice Heart Art directly into the fist art..."*

As clone after clone cultivated it, Zhang Yu's understanding of this cultivation art rapidly advanced.

*"Using these three cultivation arts as a foundation, this fighting spirit can be carried forward into subsequent Great Sage cultivation arts, truly binding each and every Great Sage cultivation art into a unified whole..."*

At that moment, a strange thought suddenly flashed through Zhang Yu's mind. He felt as though this Ox Monster Great Strength Divine Fist had been created specifically to fit the Chain Formation Diagram, crafted merely to synergize with the other cultivation arts.

"Could it have been specially cobbled together by the Desolate Ox Great Sage? But if so, that would make eleven cultivation arts for Great Sage's Heir."

"Did the Desolate Ox Great Sage also know about the Chain Formation Diagram and do this to increase its tiers? If that were the case, there shouldn't be only eleven Great Sage's Heir cultivation arts."

Shaking his head, Zhang Yu temporarily pushed his speculations aside and focused on his cultivation.

A moment later, the final effect of Great Sage's Heir was also completely unlocked by Zhang Yu.

Ultimate - Great Sage's Heir (10/10): The Great Sage's Intent connects you and me.

After pondering it for a while, Zhang Yu already understood the effect of this final tier.

*"This final tier's function is meant to be used in conjunction with collective lifeforms."*

Zhang Yu thought to himself, *"If I use the Saints' Spirit Source Wisdom Scripture to refine others into clones and create a collective will...*

*"As long as I use this tier's ability, no matter how the collective will splinters, it can be forcibly bound together, allowing the user to maintain control over the collective will."*

*"And if it isn't a collective will... then it requires the other party's consent to connect."*

A flash of realization crossed Zhang Yu's mind. He understood that this function was meant to safeguard the user's dominance after creating a collective will.

*"Yet I haven't created any collective will."*

Zhang Yu thought to himself, *"Right now, the Desolate Ox Great Sage's collective will is torn apart. Besides, since they weren't created by me, I have no way to take control by force. At most, I can only link minds with them after obtaining their consent."*

At that thought, an idea suddenly struck him. *"It just so happens... right now, quite a few within the Monster Protection faction should be willing to connect with the Sect Examination Clone."*

*"Meanwhile, the opposition is willing to connect with Immortal Slayer."*

*"In that case, as long as it goes through me, the thoughts of both sides can be linked together. Perhaps..."*

Just as Zhang Yu was pondering this, Immortal Slayer and the Sect Examination Clone had already sprung into action, each rallying their supporters to link their thoughts together.

As the power of Great Sage's Heir erupted with a roar, the minds of the Sect Examination Clone and Immortal Slayer surged upward, sweeping across the entire sea of consciousness world.

At this moment, every human or monster in the sea of consciousness world heard the calls of Immortal Slayer and the Sect Examination Clone.

Sect Examination Clone: "Join me in supporting the cause of safeguarding monsterfolk rights!"

Immortal Slayer: "Join me, and let us put an end to the conflict between humans and monsters together!"

By now, the Sect Examination Clone and Immortal Slayer had already gained considerable popularity within the sea of consciousness world, each amassing a vast number of supporters in their respective domains.

Furthermore, [Great Sage's Heir] transformed their thoughts into the Great Sage's Intent, evoking a profound sense of warmth and familiarity among the countless humans and monsters in the sea of consciousness world, as if they were inherently one with them.

Thus, countless minds across the sea of consciousness world responded at this very moment.

The Monster Protection faction, the opposition faction... Thoughts from both sides continuously connected with Zhang Yu.

At this moment, the minds of both sides interlinked, transmitting their respective thoughts, ideals, emotions... The collective will that had long been fractured was now faintly showing signs of mending.

*"Oh?"* Sensing all of this, a flicker of surprise appeared on the Monster God's face. *"Is this technique a hidden card left behind for him by the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor? Or did he develop it based on his gains in this world?"*

Though surprised, no tension showed on the Monster God's face. He simply said indifferently, "Everyone..."

In an instant, the Great Sage's Intent rose from his body as he addressed the entire sea of consciousness world.

Zhang Yu felt it as well, thinking to himself, *"As expected of the Desolate Ox's Successor nurtured by the Celestial Monster Immortal Emperor. Has he achieved the ability of my [Great Sage's Heir 6/6] and can release the Great Sage's Intent as well?"*

He then heard the Monster God say, "Please remember, our goal is to establish monsterfolk priority, so that all future resources are prioritized for the monsterfolk."

In an instant, Zhang Yu felt an intense desire surge among the monsterfolk. The collective will, which had just begun to knit together, fractured once again.

Zhang Yu thought, *"Is he making the monsterfolk remember the interests they've been chasing all this time, using those interests... to reawaken their desires?"*

The Monster God continued, "Everyone, do you remember your true thoughts? The oppression of the monsterfolk by the human race throughout history must be answered for. All human assets should be thoroughly confiscated and distributed to the monsterfolk."

The next moment, Zhang Yu felt that as the monsterfolk continuously recalled the benefits pursued by the Monster Protection Movement, their desires grew increasingly fierce, like a bottomless abyss, driving more and more of them to disconnect.

At the same time, the Monster God had once again appeared before Zhang Yu, Immortal Slayer, the Desolate Ox Spirit Division, and the Sect Examination Clone.

The Monster God said indifferently, "This is the trend of the times. The world is driven by self-interest, and when faced with profit, no matter if human or monster, their thoughts will inevitably change. Even the Desolate Ox Great Sage of the past was no exception."

"Even if you temporarily connect them, so what? As long as they remember the interests they pursue, their desires will drive them to change."

"I should thank you. By connecting both sides this time, you allowed them to feel their respective interests and demands much more clearly, accelerating this ideological shift. This will only drastically speed up my progress."

Sensing all this, Fu Ji was inwardly alarmed. "Ah! What this guy says is the truth. No matter how Zhang Yu and I try to stop it or obstruct it, we ultimately cannot change this process."

Auspicious Guy secretly sighed in his heart, *"Ah, to be honest, I've wanted to say this for a while — there really is nothing wrong with the Monster God's judgment. The thoughts in this sea of consciousness world are bound to be altered by self-interest; this is an inevitability proven by generations of Kunxu People. Once the Desolate Ox Great Sage is awakened, he will never be the Desolate Ox Great Sage he once was."*

Auspicious Guy glanced at Immortal Slayer beside him and thought to himself, *"Either way, I didn't contribute much to the planning, so even if it fails, the blame won't fall on me."*

The Herald God looked toward the nearby Desolate Ox Clone, sensing this clone that came from Zhang Yu, and secretly thought, *"Zhang Yu still seems completely confident? Does he have another trick up his sleeve?"*

Right at that moment, countless thoughts crossed the Spirit Realm, surging toward the Desolate Ox Great Sage's sea of consciousness world.

The guarding rooster and tiger monsters shouted, "Impudent! This is the property of the Celestial Monster Sect! Who dares trespass?!"

Yet when they set up the Spirit Realm Restriction, they found that these streams of thoughts passed through as if entering an empty land, effortlessly penetrating the restriction once again.

Seeing this familiar scene, the twin monsters were startled. Recalling how Zhang Yu had broken in, they thought, *"Could it be that guy playing tricks again?!"*

*"But why..."*

Looking toward the torrent in the Spirit Realm, they saw millions of thoughts sweeping in.

*"How can there be so many?!"*

The twin monsters once again used an advanced talisman to summon a god to block them.

"Great God of Myriad Paths and Four Directions! Please immediately stop unauthorized personnel from entering the Restricted Zone!"

Great God of Myriad Paths and Four Directions: "I have no authority to stop the Crown Prince of Myriad Laws."

Great God of Myriad Paths and Four Directions: "Judging by their Spirit Realm Identifiers, they all belong to the will of the Crown Prince of Myriad Laws."

On the other side, Zhang Yu looked at the Monster God before him and said calmly, "Indeed, pursuing profit is an instinct of intelligent life. But the reason the Desolate Ox Great Sage's collective will acts like this is simply because he has fallen into a slumber. At this moment, he... is merely making instinctive choices within a dream."

"True intelligent life does not simply surrender to instinct. Rather, it can overcome instinct, transcend instinct, and make judgments that truly belong to its own will."

"It is precisely because of such judgment that the Desolate Ox Great Sage is the Desolate Ox Great Sage. If he were truly awake right now, he would never agree with your notions."

The Monster God said coldly, "I told you, don't pretend to understand the Desolate Ox Great Sage before me. I am the true successor of the Desolate Ox Great Sage..."

"Is that so?" At this point, a wicked smile surfaced on Zhang Yu's face. "Then let's just wait until he wakes up and ask him ourselves."

"It's time to accelerate this sea of consciousness world."

"Since I can't win this dream, then it's time to wake up."

In an instant, countless wills from the Myriad Laws Sect descended upon this sea of consciousness world.

All because just a moment ago, across the territory of the Myriad Laws Sect on layers 11 through 19, countless Sect Staff, Soul Cultivators, contractors and others had simultaneously received a mass message from Zhang Yu.

"I am Zhang Yu, Crown Prince of Myriad Laws. I now have a mission for all of you..."

After all, for the vast majority of Myriad Laws cultivators, they would naturally only choose to accept a connection request from the Crown Prince of Myriad Laws.

Feeling the Myriad Laws cultivators linked with him, the Sect Examination Clone burst into wild laughter. "Desolate Ox Great Sage! What fun is there in mere human-monster conflicts and racial discrimination? That's far too petty!"

"Old Desolate Ox, take a good taste of the true modern Kunxu!"

"Come forth, my Race Knight Bei Wufeng, along with the Academic Knight, Regional Knight, Income Knight, Original Gender Knight, Appearance Knight, Age Knight, Rank Knight, Realm Knight, Outsourced Knight, Death Knight..."

Then Lu Hengzhang asked further, "Why are all of you outsourced workers here? What tier is your unit? What rank is your leader? Where did all the regular employees go?"

The Monster God likewise discovered that, without realizing it, he had been surrounded on all sides by the consciousnesses of Myriad Laws cultivators.

Bei Wufeng pointed at the Monster God, and Shi Yunxiang beside him chimed in, shouting at the Monster God, "You little bea—"

Although he felt that his consciousness was already connected to Zhang Yu's, he still habitually showed deference at this moment, asking softly, "Your Highness, what is our mission?"

Zhang Yu: "Be yourselves."

At the same time, the wills of even more cultivators continued to descend, with a large number of Soul Cultivators mingled among them, curiously sizing up this world.

A flash of realization struck the Herald God's mind. "I see. Since he can't stop it, he's simply accelerating it?"

"That's it. The Monster God only dared to alter the Desolate Ox's thoughts step by step because, at his core... the Desolate Ox would inevitably loathe the Kunxu of today."

"What would happen if the modern Kunxu were thrust before the Desolate Ox all at once?"

"It would be a nightmare for the Desolate Ox, one that would... shock him awake."

The next moment, roaring flames ignited across the entire sea of consciousness world. As countless Kunxu cultivators rampaged through it, a single unified emotion surged within the collective will of the Desolate Ox Great Sage.

***Rage!***

Zhang Yu said indifferently, "Great Sage, this era has become unrecognizable to you, and the entire world is on the brink of destruction."

"So... wake up."

The entire sea of consciousness world violently shook.
