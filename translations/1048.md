# Chapter 1048: Missing the Beauty of Kunxu, Conquering the New World

Curious, Bai Zhenzhen turned her head toward Zhang Yu and asked, "Yu Zi, what did you just say?"

For a moment, Zhang Yu didn't know how to answer and could only say, "Nothing."

Looking at the residential complex before them, Bai Zhenzhen said, "This place looks a lot like the First Layer. Are you sure it's not some city in the First Layer of Kunxu?"

The next moment, Bai Zhenzhen rubbed between her eyebrows and said, "Huh? I have... an identity here?"

Just as Bai Zhenzhen was sensing the memories surfacing in her mind, filled with immense confusion, she realized that Zhang Yu beside her was already pulling her forward.

"Yu Zi, where are you going?"

Zhang Yu didn't answer. Bai Zhenzhen noticed that he seemed remarkably familiar with the neighborhood, turning left and right before already entering a passcode to step into a residential building.

"Yu Zi, do you know this place?"

Zhang Yu still didn't answer, but Bai Zhenzhen felt his hand tighten slightly, his whole body seemingly somewhat nervous.

A-Zhen simply followed Zhang Yu all the way up to the sixth floor with him.

The next moment, she watched as Zhang Yu expertly unlocked the fingerprint lock on the front door and walked into the apartment.

Bai Zhenzhen said thoughtfully, "Yu Zi, do you have an identity in this world too? Did the memories also just flood out of your head?"

Zhang Yu sighed, "I suppose so."

---

Within the Heavenly Sword Sect.

Immortal Hengan was an Immortal of the Heavenly Sword Sect, belonging to the Heavenly Tribulation Dao Lineage and upholding the Heavenly Tribulation Immortal Venerable.

The Hengan Securities Company under his name had been the very brokerage firm that served Zhang Yu in the past.

Back then, the futures collapse triggered by Zhang Yu had dealt him massive losses, and he had been ruthlessly reaped by the Heavenly Sword Immortal Emperor.

Ever since, Immortal Hengan had worked even harder to make money, desperate to earn back the Immortal Dao Potential he had lost.

When the ten Immortal Emperors departed and the Battle of the End broke out, his actions grew increasingly unscrupulous. Exploiting various insider trades to reap cultivators, his wealth swelled drastically within a few short months.

Yet the more he earned, the more Immortal Hengan felt it was not enough.

*"It's far too little! Compared to the Immortal Venerables of each sect, what I earn now is nothing!"*

*"With the Ultimate Doomsday drawing near, only by earning more money, acquiring more Immortal Dao Potential, and gaining greater authority to mobilize Immortal Dao resources can I improve my chances of surviving the end..."*

As his assets grew exponentially, Immortal Hengan became all the more anxious about the Ultimate Doomsday.

He certainly did not want to earn so much money and enhance his Immortal Dao Potential only for it all to end up benefiting the Immortal Emperor of the End.

Therefore, upon learning of the existence of the "New World" and knowing that such a way of retreat existed, Immortal Hengan was exceedingly exhilarated.

After all, as long as an escape route existed, who would want to fight the Immortal Emperor of the End?

*"I am an Immortal myself..."* Immortal Hengan thought inwardly. *"How could I not understand what the Immortals think? As soon as the news of the New World spreads, every single one of the Ten Sects Immortals will definitely flee; absolutely no one will stay behind to risk their lives against the Immortal Emperor of the End."*

*"However, why are the higher-ups only revealing the news now? Heavenly Tribulation Immortal Venerable has probably already been researching it for quite some time, haven't they?"*

As for descriptions like nightmare and hell, Immortal Hengan did not take them to heart at all.

After all, in his eyes...

*"So what if the environment is harsh? It's nothing more than spending a bit more money to terraform it."*

*"The key is to take the initiative. The earlier one places bets and invests in the New World, the larger the share one can occupy in the future. Perhaps that place might even become a New Kunxu down the line."*

Thinking up to this point, Immortal Hengan dispersed countless strands of intent and began gathering relevant intelligence.

While in the process of collecting intelligence, he also started attempting to enter the so-called New World.

Yet, even after coming into contact with several people who had experienced the nightmare in succession, Immortal Hengan still failed to enter the New World.

He analyzed inwardly, *"Is it because I haven't had enough contact?"*

*"Hmm? Bai Zhenzhen got in?"*

*"Let me try again."*

Seeing this, Immortal Hengan's gaze shifted. He dispatched a clone, sending it directly to Bai Zhenzhen's side to attempt catching the so-called nightmare and entering that New World.

The next moment, the world before him shifted. Immortal Hengan sensed that his clone had departed for another world.

*"I finally succeeded. Is it because there's something special about Bai Zhenzhen? Or is it because all the nightmare experiencers I came into contact with earlier... a quantitative change led to a qualitative change?"*

*"So it truly is another world? It has even severed my connection with my clone for now."*

*"Then all I can do now is wait for the clone to report back."*

---

On the other side, Immortal Hengan's clone opened his eyes, taking in the unfamiliar world before him and the unfamiliar memories in his mind.

*"I am... Li Hengan? No, I am Immortal Hengan, the Immortal Hengan of the Heavenly Sword Sect!"*

Immortal Hengan frowned slightly, feeling his own body as he thought to himself, *"Upon entering this world, one is given a corresponding identity?"*

Two possibilities immediately surfaced in his mind.

*"First, it matched me with someone, allowing me to perform a body seizure on them."*

*"Second, based on my circumstances, it created a person in this world out of thin air — perhaps even fabricating this person's entire past?"*

Immortal Hengan could not determine which case it was, but regardless of which one... in his view, neither seemed like a natural phenomenon.

*"Some kind of Dao Lineage? If it is the first possibility, it would still be considered ordinary."*

*"However, if it is the second, this New World is probably already blanketed by an exceptionally formidable Dao Lineage."*

At the same time, memories were gradually surfacing in Immortal Hengan's mind, allowing him to slowly gain an understanding of this world.

*"Hmm... there are firearms, cars, airplanes, and even landing on the moon is extremely difficult. It's a bit like the First Layer, yet noticeably inferior. This seems to be a world that, in terms of technological development, lags vastly behind Kunxu."*

*"Yet it is also a world where money is of paramount importance."*

*"That is only natural. As a bargaining chip used to mobilize resources within a society, money has always been important."*

*"It seems that if I wish to develop myself in this New World, the urgent priority is to make money and become a wealthy man."*

As Immortal Hengan recalled some general knowledge, he realized that the efficiency of this memory recovery was far too slow.

Immortal Hengan tried to channel Immortal Dao Technology, only to find no response whatsoever: *"A nuisance. Am I left with no choice but to wait for this physical body to slowly squeeze out its memories?"*

He tested the quality of his physical body, then shook his head in dissatisfaction: *"An inferior mortal body. If this were in Kunxu, I wouldn't even deign to use a body like this as a footstool."*

*"Forget it. Though this kind of physical body can hardly bear my Immortal-grade wisdom and techniques, I have no choice but to make do with it for now."*

Immortal Hengan understood in his heart that an Immortal's wisdom required an Immortal's body to sustain it.

A mortal body like this could not even achieve the most basic thought acceleration, so it was inevitably impossible to unleash his true wisdom.

*"However, with my memories and experience from Kunxu, executing a dimensional-strike on this backward world shouldn't be difficult."*

Right at that moment, he sensed someone nearby gazing at him. Immortal Hengan turned to the person and asked, "Do you know me?"

The fried chicken shop boss looked at Immortal Hengan, who had been standing dazed in place for quite a while, and said offhandedly, "Now that you've picked up the food, hurry up and deliver it to the customer."

"Pick up the food?" Immortal Hengan looked at the environment of the fried chicken shop, his own attire, and the food bag in his hand, understanding dawning in his heart at once.

Spurred by this, a great deal of memories regarding delivering takeout even surfaced within Immortal Hengan's mind.

*"Heh, the identity of this physical body is actually a food delivery driver?"*

The moment he realized this, Immortal Hengan had already made up his mind.

*"A pity. The target you're foolishly trying to exploit this time happens to be an Immortal."*

*"Working a nine-to-five is out of the question."*

As an Immortal from Kunxu, Immortal Hengan had the confidence that even starting from scratch in such a backward New World, he could undoubtedly become a wealthy man.

And from his vast experience and wisdom, the very first step to becoming rich was to avoid working a regular job.

In his long reservoir of memories, countless examples of those he had exploited in Kunxu proved that one could never get rich by working for others; wage slaves ultimately ended up as nothing more than Soul Cultivators.

*"Especially a gig like delivering food — it will only deplete what little physical strength and energy this frail body already has, squandering my time and further hindering me from utilizing my Immortal-grade wisdom to make money."*

*"With that kind of time, the returns from just a bit of contemplation would likely far exceed delivering takeout."*

And so, holding the bag of food, Immortal Hengan walked out of the fried chicken shop without the slightest intention of making the delivery. Instead, he unlocked his phone and began gathering information, pondering ways to make money.

*"Huh? I've got an idea!"*

*"Does this world have futures and stocks too?"*

As an immortal who had dominated financial markets and owned multiple related companies, Immortal Hengan was clearly brimming with confidence in this area.

*"Without insider information, making a fortune might be difficult, but with my market sensitivity and grasp of finance, earning more than a food delivery driver shouldn't be an issue at all."*

A few days later.

"Madmen! The vast majority of people participating in investments in this world are absolute madmen! Completely devoid of reason, utterly emotional and deranged... There are hardly any real investors at all!"

Immortal Hengan had initially thought the retail investors of Kunxu were foolish enough, but the sheer lack of a baseline among this world's retail traders blew his mind, leaving him completely unable to predict market trends with any certainty.

"Everywhere are fools who don't know a damn thing. In such a chaotic market... there's hardly any room for real investors like me to survive."

At the same time, Immortal Hengan sensed that over the past few days, his memories of Kunxu had blurred considerably.

A bad feeling immediately welled up in his heart. *"If I stay in this world for too long, could I actually forget my memories of Kunxu?"*

*"And the passage of time here — several days have already passed, yet I haven't returned? It seems time flows differently here compared to Kunxu."*

Immortal Hengan shook his head. Unable to resolve this issue for the moment, he began thinking of other ways to make money. *"What kind of company would be suitable for me to start?"*

Naturally, Immortal Hengan did not rush into founding a company. Instead, he first researched the knowledge required to start a business in this world.

And then, he was utterly shocked.

*"What? An eight-hour workday, two days off every week, and overtime pay for extra hours? Are they crazy? How can any company stay in business like this?"*

*"Oh? So one can simply ignore all that? But it might lead to lawsuits?"*

*"What? As the business owner, on top of paying wages, I have to cover so many kinds of insurance for the employees?"*

*"What? If I fire an employee, I have to pay *them* money based on their length of employment?"*

*"There's no Company Protection Act? Only a Laborer Protection Act? Has this world gone mad?"*

At this moment, Immortal Hengan felt that the local business environment was nothing short of appalling.

*"The rulers here must be suppressing the lower layers, terrified that people below might succeed in starting businesses and threaten their status, scheming to keep us poor our entire lives."*

During his days of study, Immortal Hengan felt his memories of Kunxu growing increasingly faint, like an old dream that was gradually dissipating in his mind.

In contrast, memories of this New World were becoming clearer and more real to him.

Sometimes, a thought would even bubble up in Immortal Hengan's mind: *"Could everything in Kunxu have just been a dream?"*

The next moment, however, he would shake his head, suppress his stray thoughts, and return to his studies and reflections.

By this point, Immortal Hengan had already abandoned his plans of starting a business and made another decision.

"I'm going to take the Sect Examination!"

In the course of his studying, Immortal Hengan discovered that this world had Sect Staff as well, though the name and functions were somewhat different.

"Yet the essence is the same! I will join this world's ruling clique, climb up step by step, and become part of this world's ruling class."

Immortal Hengan firmly believed that with his abilities, passing the Sect Examination to become Sect Staff would be a walk in the park.

And so, he began reading the study materials and watching online lectures.

Yet as he went on, the more Immortal Hengan saw, the more uncomfortable he felt.

*"I'm taking the Sect Examination just to serve others? Then why am I even taking it?"*

*"What kind of twisted heresy is this?"*

Even though he felt intense revulsion inside, Immortal Hengan forced himself to keep studying.

*"Phew, there's no other way... Taking the Sect Examination means shifting into exam-prep mode. I must let the sect's will flood my soul!"*

As time slipped by, however, his memories of Kunxu grew increasingly faint and blurred in his mind. More and more details... were beginning to elude his recall.

All of this made a trace of panic rise uncontrollably in his heart.

*"If... Kunxu really was just a dream, just a figment of my imagination, and this place is the real world, what am I supposed to do?"*

*"Indeed, could an Immortal Dao World as wonderful as Kunxu truly exist in this universe? Could there really be such an ideal cultivation environment?"*

Fear began to well up in Immortal Hengan's heart.

Especially when he considered that if Kunxu was fake, he would have to spend eternity living in a world without the Immortal Dao, surrounded by madmen, where he was nothing more than a pauper. Strands of terror continuously seeped out from this frail, weak body.

*"Right! If my memories are real, then other cultivators from the Heavenly Sword Sect also entered the nightmare state and arrived in this world."*

*"Then, besides me, there should be other Heavenly Sword Sect cultivators here."*

*"If I can find them, it proves that Kunxu really exists..."*

*"Also, if I can band them together to do something big, it will make it even easier to gain a firm foothold in this world."*

And so, after a search, Immortal Hengan was astonished to find a novel titled *Kunxu* on the internet.

After getting in touch with the author, he confirmed that the other party was indeed a cultivator from the Heavenly Sword Sect, and even learned that a gathering of Heavenly Sword Sect cultivators would take place in just a few days.

At this moment, Immortal Hengan felt a slight sense of relief: *"It's not a dream. Kunxu really exists!"*

*"However, the location of this gathering is a bit far from me. I've nearly depleted my living expenses preparing for the Sect Examination lately... It's time to tap into the reserve funds."*

In Immortal Hengan's eyes, meeting up with fellow cultivators of the Heavenly Sword Sect was an essential opportunity, and such reserve funds were meant to be used for crucial moments like this.

With that, he opened a lending platform...

At the same time, Immortal Hengan was invited into the Heavenly Sword Sect's group chat and began conversing with the cultivators inside.

Through their exchange, he gradually learned an important piece of intelligence.

*"Every cultivator who entered the New World was assigned an extremely inconspicuous identity. Almost all of them are at the very bottom of society, with virtually no relatives, truly the poorest of the poor."*

Soon after, he also learned the identities of some cultivators in the group.

"Divine Monarch Hong Shanlang, who was in charge of thunder tribulation duties in the sect, is currently an elite food delivery courier — this month's top deliverer, making over ten thousand a month."

"Divine Monarch Zhentian, who held a martial dao research and development post in the sect, is now the top salesperson at a health supplements company, with a monthly income exceeding fifty thousand."

"Heavenly Monarch Lu Jingsong, formerly a senior executive at a securities firm... one of my old subordinates."

Although many of his memories regarding Kunxu had already grown hazy, the memory of Lu Jingsong remained deeply etched in Immortal Hengan's mind even now.

It was solely because Lu Jingsong had caused Immortal Hengan to lose a fortune when handling Zhang Yu's Thunder Tribulation Futures.

*"After arriving in the New World, Lu Jingsong is currently working as a financial blogger? He's already amassed 5,000 followers..."*

As he summarized it in his mind, Immortal Hengan secretly thought, *"My Heavenly Sword Sect truly is brimming with talent. In such a short time, they've already flourished everywhere, and all of them possess considerable influence in this world."*

*"Hmph, but deliberately displaying this information in the group is just their way of showing off their achievements and gaining more influence, isn't it?"*

Immortal Hengan understood that this New World before them might be a sanctuary for countless cultivators. Before they could transform this world, any cultivator capable of quickly harvesting societal resources here would be of great use.

*"Everyone possesses only a frail, weak body, and Immortal Dao Technology temporarily cannot be used. Be it low-tier cultivators or Immortals, it seems everyone is temporarily standing at the same starting line."*

*"For the lower-tier cultivators, this is an opportunity to overtake on the bend."*

*"Everyone wants to demonstrate their ability and seize more of a voice."*

Right at this moment, a female cultivator named Chu Jianshuang sent a screenshot showing a deposit of 400,000.

The moment they saw the screenshot, the group chat instantly boiled over.

Hong Shanlang: "400,000? Didn't you have barely over 10,000 in savings just before this?"

Divine Monarch Zhentian: "How did you make 400,000 in such a short time? Begging on my knees for your method!"

Lu Jingsong: "[Shocked] Open source it, and I can give you Immortal Coins when we get back."

Information about her flashed through Immortal Hengan's mind as well: *"Dao Monarch Chu, Chu Jianshuang, is in charge of flying sword research and development in the sect. Her current job in the New World should be a real estate agent. How did she make so much in one breath?"*

*"A scam? Commission from selling a luxury mansion? Won the lottery?"*

Just as Immortal Hengan's mind was running wild, Chu Jianshuang replied nonchalantly: "It's the betrothal gift I just received."

While everyone was still marveling at Chu Jianshuang's achievement, someone else posted screenshots.

Bai Zhenzhen: [Property Deed]

Bai Zhenzhen: [Vehicle Registration]

Seeing this, the crowd was once again astounded.

Hong Shanlang: "A house and a car?! How is it possible she already has a house and a car?!"

Divine Monarch Zhentian: "She's far too good at making money! Truly worthy of the Heavenly Sword Goddess! Even after coming to the New World, she's still so formidable!"

Immortal Hengan: "Bai Zhenzhen... This woman might be the Heavenly Sword Sect's key to operating in this world, capable of influencing the Immortal Venerables' plans to conquer it."
