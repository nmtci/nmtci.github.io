# Chapter 1007: The Immortal Emperors Pare the Books

Over the following days, Zhang Yu controlled one clone after another and began cultivating the arts given to him by the Ten Immortal Emperors. He had a persistent sense of foreboding about the Chain Formation Diagram designed by the Ten Immortal Emperors.

He thought to himself, “For them to reach an agreement, they must have already held countless discussions in secret.”

“How laughable that I actually thought I had a little influence, that I could affect the direction in which this Chain Formation Diagram was created.”

“But the truth is, this time the Ten Immortal Emperors aren’t giving anyone a chance to interfere. They don’t even want to share a single spoonful of soup with anyone else.”

Immortal Slayer said, “The Ten Immortal Emperors spend all day thinking about how to torment Kunxu's lowly cultivators, so they can get one step closer to becoming the Immortal Emperor of the End. For all ten of them to act together this time and agree so completely on their goal, there must be enormous interests involved.”

Zhang Yu couldn’t help thinking that interests vast enough to unite the Immortal Emperors’ goals were most likely not going to benefit the other beings in Kunxu.

Meanwhile, as Zhang Yu cultivated, his formidable cultivation and abilities also allowed him to keep up with his other work.

At that very moment, Zhang Yu was reading a passage of lost history that had surfaced in the Feather Book.

And the more he reread its contents, the more surprised and astonished he became.

He heard him think, “Ying Xintian sparked the Great Righteous-Demonic War, unified the Righteous and Demonic Paths, used righteousness to control demons, and became the Supreme Immortal Emperor.”

“After that, he campaigned across the world, exterminated the various Immortal Emperors, united all the realms, and brought Kunxu together as one, ushering in an Immortal Dao Golden Age....”

Immortal Slayer exclaimed in surprise, “So in this lost history, it was Patriarch Ying who started the Great Righteous-Demonic War? And he even won decisively.”

A flash of insight struck Zhang Yu, and all the scattered pieces of information from the past converged in his mind.

“This clearly went against the wishes of certain people, so the world was rolled back, and the outcome of this battle was overturned as well. Under the world’s renewed guidance, things took the shape Kunxu has today.”

“Senior Supreme Truth was right. Someone was guiding the Great Righteous-Demonic War from behind the scenes. These people pulling the strings are deeply connected to the World Rollback. From what we can see, they deliberately ignited the Great Righteous-Demonic War early, leaving Senior Ying Xintian without enough time to build up his strength. In the end, he failed to unite the Righteous and Demonic Paths.”

Immortal Slayer said solemnly, “Perhaps the being behind the World Rollback feared Patriarch Yingxintian, and so secretly suppressed him as well, preventing him from attaining the rank of Immortal Emperor to this day. Otherwise, Kunxu would never have ended up as it is now.”

Zhang Yu nodded in agreement with Immortal Slayer’s speculation, then continued studying the lost history that followed.

Zhang Yu heard himself think, “In this lost history, after Senior Ying Xintian unified Kunxu, he began working for the benefit of all under heaven.

“He established schools to train all cultivators uniformly, with everything decided solely by their results....”

“He also took back control of the Dao Seed and Chain Formation Diagram Dao Lineages, then made them public, allowing everyone under heaven to use Chain Formation Diagrams and Dao Seeds and ushering in a golden age of the Immortal Dao for the entire world.”

“But...” Zhang Yu sighed inwardly. “The improvement in aptitude and the increase in cultivation efficiency greatly fueled every cultivator’s ambition.”

“Everyone believed they were heaven’s favored child, everyone believed they were a genius of the Immortal Dao, and everyone was sure they could achieve immortality. So, spurred on by their Immortal Dao Aspiration, countless cultivators charged headlong down that path.”

“Most cultivators, the more their aptitudes improved and the greater the increase in their cultivation efficiency, the more they wanted to reach the peak of the Immortal Dao — and more easily they were influenced by their aspiration for the Immortal Dao.”

“Indeed, because the Technique-Chaining and Dao Seeds were used by all the people under heaven, and the people’s aspiration for the Immortal Dao grew stronger, the Technique-Chaining and Dao Seeds eventually became disconnected from the Books.”

When he first read this passage, a flicker of uncertainty had crossed Zhang Yu’s mind. This was the first time he had learned that theof the Technique-Chaining and Dao Seed Dao Lineages could actually become disconnected from the Books.

“The Books connect the many Dao Lineages of past and present. Since they can be connected, naturally they can also be disconnected.”

Looking at the information appearing in the Feather Book, Zhang Yu could sense that whoever had written this passage about “disconnection” seemed to understand its reasons very well. It was as if they were recalling something they had experienced firsthand, recounting everything they had seen, heard, and felt...

“Did I experience this myself in the future? Or was my future self merely repeating someone else’s account?” Zhang Yu suppressed his doubts and continued to ponder the content before him.

“Aspiration for the Immortal Dao is rooted in the most fundamental heavenly principles, arising from humanity’s boundless yearning for self-improvement.”

“The Books are likewise rooted in the most fundamental heavenly principles, arising from humanity’s boundless yearning for reproduction and inheritance.”

“Both are human instincts, the very foundation of what makes us human, and one of the sources of the changes now sweeping the world.”

“The struggle between them is endless. Once any Dao Lineage becomes connected to either one, it will gradually drift away from the other.”

“If Chain Diagrams and Dao Seeds were used to enhance the aptitude and innate talent of cultivators throughout the world, they would inevitably greatly strengthen the influence of the Immortal Dao Aspiration, causing the Technique-Chaining Dao Lineage and the Dao Seed Dao Lineage to gradually connect with the Immortal Dao Aspiration lineage, and ultimately sever their connection to the Books.”

Zhang Yu’s heart stirred. “So something like this really happened? The existence of the aspiration for the Immortal Dao means that making the Chain Formation Diagram public and connecting the Books to the Chain Formation Diagrams are at odds with each other.”

“Did the Immortal Ancestor think of this back then?”

“No.” Zhang Yu suddenly realized. “When the Immortal Ancestor created the Technique-Chaining Comprehensive Manual, he wanted the Chain Formation Diagrams to be used by everyone in the world, so that anyone could use it.”

“If everyone could use it, then why would the Books be needed to connect to the Chain Formation Diagram?”

Looking at the lost history recorded before him, Zhang Yu fell into thought. “So the Immortal Ancestor must have run into the same problem as Senior Ying Xintian did in the lost history. He discovered that if everyone could use the Chain Formation Diagrams, it would strengthen the influence of the Immortal Dao Aspiration lineage.”

“So he made it impossible for the public to use the Chain Formation Diagrams, and connected the Books to it, so that only some book-holders could use the Chain Formation Diagrams.”

Thinking of this, Zhang Yu couldn’t help sighing again. “That’s what a Dao Lineage is like. Even the slightest deviation can make the world worse, and then correcting the mistake takes even more effort and requires even more measures, making the world increasingly complicated.”

The next moment, another ominous suspicion suddenly rose in Zhang Yu’s heart: “Could it be that what the Ten Immortal Emperors want to do this time is sever the connection between the Chain Formation Diagrams and the Books?”

But Zhang Yu couldn’t be sure. He would only know once he saw the effects of the Chain Formation Diagram. For now, he could only set aside his distracting thoughts and continue to comprehend the information about the lost history.

“Qualifications and innate talent accelerate the growth of ambition in people’s hearts.”

“As Ying Xintian brought blessings to all under heaven, heroes also rose everywhere, and countless people wanted to climb over others in their pursuit of the Immortal Dao Apex.”

“The endless strife and conflicts... Even someone as powerful as Ying Xintian could not suppress them all, unless he was willing to monopolize the Immortal Dao and rule the world alone with an iron fist.”

Zhang Yu thought to himself, “But the less Senior Ying Xintian is willing to rule the world with an iron fist, the more opportunities he gives countless cultivators to pursue the peak of the Immortal Dao — and the more competition, conflict, and strife erupt among them.”

“Gradually, even Ying Xintian, as the Supreme Immortal Emperor, became a thorn in the eyes of countless Immortal Venerables, Immortals, and lower-rank cultivators. Driven by their aspiration for the Immortal Dao, everyone wanted to take his place.”

“By then, all under heaven stood as enemies before Ying Xintian.”

“And as more and more cultivators achieved Immortality and established their Dao, the world grew ever more turbulent.”

Zhang Yu sighed inwardly. “Ying Xintian believed that as long as he didn’t ban the Immortal Dao or suppress everyone’s cultivation realm, then one day there would inevitably arise a group powerful enough to rival him, yet interested only in climbing to the peak and exploiting all under heaven to achieve the final end.”

“Senior Ying Xintian further believed that to eradicate this problem, he had to eradicate the aspiration for the Immortal Dao. And to eradicate the aspiration for the Immortal Dao, he had to wipe out the Immortal Dao and sever everyone’s hope of climbing to the apex of the Immortal Dao.”

“So, to accomplish this, he cut short the lifespan of all living beings, setting 100 years as the limit; shattered Reincarnation; eradicated Monster Blood; crushed the Yin Aura; slaughtered the gods; and ensured that the world would know no more Immortals, monsters, ghosts, or gods… no more chance whatsoever of achieving Immortality — not even for himself.”

As he repeatedly pondered this passage from the lost history, Zhang Yu couldn’t help but feel deeply moved.

He recalled the plan Fu Xiantian had once told him about during the University War in the lower realm.

“Wasn’t the limit supposed to be 1,500 years?” Zhang Yu thought. “Who would’ve guessed that in another fragment of lost history, Senior Ying Xintian went even further, imposing a limit of 100 years.”

Immortal Slayer sighed, “If there were no more Immortal Dao in the world, perhaps Kunxu as it is today would never have come to be.”

The Sect Examination Clone snorted. “Nonsense! Did we strive to reach the peak of the Immortal Dao just to do something this stupid?! Even if everyone’s lifespan were capped at 100 years, would people stop striving to rise higher?”

“This has merely temporarily restrained the intensity of conflict. As long as humanity continues to reproduce and the world continues to develop, the desire to strive upward will be endless, and conflict will never cease. One day, things will return to their original course.”

The Sect Examination Clone said, “Zhang Yu, do you understand? His whole stupid idea is wrong. After going around in circles, things will still end up back at Kunxu as it is today. That proves the Kunxu of today is the righteous path of Heaven and Earth! The only problem is that there are too many Immortal Emperors. Once we become the one and only Immortal Emperor, everything will be fine.”

Listening to the two sides argue, Fu Ji couldn’t help thinking to herself, *“This should count as… Zhang Yu’s own inner conflict of thought, shouldn’t it?”*

But Zhang Yu paid no heed to the quarrel between the two thoughts and simply continued to comprehend the abilities of the Book.

And after being incorporated into the Feather Book, the book that had brought about this lost history transformed into a new chapter, called the World-Domain Chapter.

After studying the World-Domain Chapter’s abilities, Zhang Yu found it wasn't complicated. He only needed to record the cultivation arts he knew that contained spatial technology, and their effects would be enhanced.

Zhang Yu ran a few tests. After registering all his cultivation arts related to spatial technology, he found that whenever he used them again, he could faintly sense the entire world working in concert with him. It was as if all the spatial structures actively adapted to his needs.

“The operating costs of the transportation hub can be reduced by over 30%.”

“With the World-Domain Dao Lineage and the World-Domain Chapter helping me in turn, there probably aren’t any cultivators below the Immortal level whose spatial technology is stronger than mine.”

With a thought from Zhang Yu, the Feather Book before him flipped through the Immortal Foundation Chapter, the Technique-Chaining Comprehensive Manual, the Great Sage Chapter, the Heavenly Tribulation Chapter, and the World-Domain Chapter in succession.

He thought to himself, “The Feather Book is gaining more and more new chapters, and there are more and more directions in which it can be upgraded.”

In the days that followed, Zhang Yu continued to cultivate, seek enlightenment, and work.

However, no new chapters appeared in the Feather Book. It merely gained a few modules for specialized cultivation arts, along with some ordinary Chain Formation Diagrams, which offered Zhang Yu only a negligible improvement.

After some time, Zhang Yu finally finished cultivating all 10 cultivation arts provided by the Immortal Emperors.

As he cultivated them, he could sense that although all 10 were basic cultivation arts, they contained extraordinarily profound techniques. They were easy to learn and practice, yet could greatly improve a person’s attributes in every respect.

Zhang Yu could especially tell that these cultivation arts could be practiced starting from any realm. Whether one was a Qi Refining Cultivator or an Ascension Realm cultivator, they could get started right away.

“These cultivation arts must have been meticulously designed by the Ten Immortal Emperors. They have unparalleled adaptability and comprehensiveness.”

Next, Zhang Yu opened the Technique-Chaining Comprehensive Manual and tried entering the cultivation arts one by one.

A moment later, information on the Chain Formation Diagram that could be created from these ten cultivation arts appeared before Zhang Yu.

“The Chain Formation Diagram’s name is… Immortal Emperor’s Successor?”

“Function one: it can enhance vitality and survivability, allowing one to stay alive even under the most extreme conditions — even running on nothing but breathing and sunlight?”

“Function two: learning ability and Immortal Research ability both soar, greatly improving the efficiency of studying and cultivating the Immortal Dao. It can even convert reproductive essence to further enhance the effects.”

Although these two functions both sounded like they would benefit all living beings, Zhang Yu, who already understood Kunxu’s ecosystem very well, saw only a future of even fiercer competition.

The Sect Examination Clone said, “Converting reproductive essence to improve aptitude? Doesn’t that mean cultivators can sterilize themselves for free? What a great benefit. And you don’t need medicine or even spiritual energy — just breathing and light to stay alive? That’s an even greater benefit.”

“In that case, before long, they’ll probably start charging for all sunlight and air, and most areas will likely be plunged into darkness. If our company provides lighting, that’ll count as an employee benefit.”

“And now that learning ability can be improved too, we can raise our company’s hiring requirements again.”

Zhang Yu said nothing and continued reading: 

“Function Three: Strengthen the dao heart, turn distracting thoughts into resolve, and make cultivators devote themselves wholly to the Dao, with nothing else in their lives…”

After reading this, Immortal Slayer couldn’t help criticizing it: “Isn’t the aspiration for the Immortal Dao already competitive enough? Now you want to eliminate distracting thoughts and push things even further? What are you going to turn all of Kunxu into?”

The Sect Examination Clone said, “From what I’ve seen, the cultivators in the sect have plenty of potential to get even more competitive. At the very least, a tenth of them probably go bankrupt every year. The way things are now, a single Sect Staff member can work for decades, even a century or more. The sect is supporting far too many idle people.”

“Hmph. If I become an Immortal Emperor, I’d definitely push for this kind of Chain Formation Diagram, too.”

Zhang Yu, however, thought of another problem: “In Senior Ying Xintian’s lost history, the spread of the Chain Formation Diagram, along with its enhancement of cultivators’ aptitudes, eventually caused the Technique-Chaining Dao Lineage to become disconnected from the Books.”

“If this Chain Formation Diagram from the Immortal Emperor’s Successor is promoted by the Ten Immortal Emperors and changes cultivators’ aptitudes on a large scale, could it cause the same problem?”

Zhang Yu guessed to himself, “Free sterilization, changing one’s aptitude, casting aside distracting thoughts, and strengthening one’s dao heart — this will inevitably make the aspiration for the Immortal Dao more influential. As more and more people use this Chain Formation Diagram…”

“Aspiration for the Immortal Dao will stir the Technique-Chaining Dao Lineage, gradually severing the connection between the Books and the lineage.”

Zhang Yu said gravely, “If that happens, what consequences will there be as the Feather Book and the Chain Formation Diagrams gradually become disconnected?”

“Will I gradually lose the ability to use more and more Chain Formation Diagrams as they become disconnected?”

The Sect Examination Clone grew serious as well. “Could these Ten Great Emperor Beasts be trying to restrict us?”

“Probably not us,” Immortal Slayer guessed. “We’re likely just collateral damage. The real target may be… the Immortal Ancestor.”

Zhang Yu also recalled the matter of the Immortal Ancestor’s revival and thought to himself, “The Immortal Ancestor created the Books. Now the Ten Immortal Emperors are promoting this Chain Formation Diagram to sever its connection with the Books. They must be doing it to deal with the Immortal Ancestor.”

“If that’s the case, the Chain Formation Diagram is probably only the beginning…”

“And the Immortal Ancestor will awaken in a year. In other words, more moves will probably be made over the course of that year.”

At the thought, Zhang Yu’s heart sank.

“Over the next year, starting with this Chain Formation Diagram, the Immortal Emperors will gradually employ all kinds of measures… All of them will be aimed at weakening the Immortal Ancestor and gradually severing the connections between the Books and each Dao Lineage.”

“If that happens, my Book will be weakened too, and many of its functions will gradually stop working!”

Immortal Slayer’s Sigh said, “Further squeezing the world dry, or weakening books and restricting our abilities… Those are just the aftershocks — the consequences of the Immortal Emperors’ efforts to weaken the Immortal Ancestor.”

The Sect Examination Clone said unwillingly, “But even the aftershocks of a martial duel between the top powers will greatly affect our strength! Without the aid of the Feather Book, how much harder will it be for us to contend with the Immortal Emperors in the future?”

He shouted, “We can’t just stand by and watch this [Immortal Emperor’s Successor] get created!”

As Zhang Yu pondered, he discovered that news had already been announced in the Spirit Realm.

“The All-People Chain Formation Diagram will be completed today!”

“The Celestial Evolution Award winner, Heavenly Monarch Overdrive, is livestreaming with you as you wait for the Chain Formation Diagram results.”

“With everyone eagerly anticipating it! Zhang Yu has been in seclusion for days...”

Looking at the string of news headlines, Zhang Yu wondered, “Is this news being pushed by the Immortal Emperors?”

He gave a bitter laugh and thought to himself, “They even calculated how long it would take me to finish cultivating the 10 cultivation arts? They’ve already arranged the publicity for the Chain Formation Diagram, too? Is this... a warning to me?”

Zhang Yu wondered, “If I switch to a different cultivation art right now and create another Chain Formation Diagram, what will the Immortal Emperors do? Suppress me?”

“No. I can’t confront them head-on.”

Zhang Yu took a deep breath. Then an idea suddenly struck him: he could try using the power of his Spirit Root to derive cultivation arts.

“If I can derive a different effect from the cultivation arts, then perhaps these same 10 cultivation arts could create a different kind of Chain Formation Diagram.”

With that thought, Zhang Yu tried to contact Bai Zhenzhen and exchange Spirit Roots with her.

But no matter how he tried to reach her, Bai Zhenzhen didn’t respond at all.

As he looked at the name of the Heavenly Sword Immortal Emperor in his mailbox, Zhang Yu felt a foreboding stir in his heart.

Then he tried contacting his elder sister, Zhang Pianpian, but she didn’t respond either.

Zhang Yu then began trying to contact Tai Yuebai, but he sent him only one message: “Protect yourself. Just stay behind the strong.”

Zhang Yu’s heart stirred. “Is this… similar to what Brother Dao told me in the lower realm? Is it a message from Senior Ying Xintian?”

“If all I want is to keep myself safe, then I should temporarily go along with the ten Immortal Emperors. But could doing so lead to an irreversible outcome?”

As he pondered, Zhang Yu contacted Kuang Tianqing at the Former Days Graveyard.

But in response, Kuang Tianqing merely left him a message: “Minister Zhang, I don’t have time right now. Let’s get in touch again tomorrow.”

Zhang Yu thought to himself, *“Is this the Reincarnation Immortal Emperor’s stance? I suppose so. The ten Immortal Emperors have joined forces this time.”*

A moment of clarity flashed through Zhang Yu’s mind. *“In the past, one of the main reasons I managed to turn danger into safety time and again was that I took advantage of the conflicts of interest among the ten Immortal Emperors, exploiting the rifts between them.”*

*“But if the ten Immortal Emperors share a common goal, if they stand on the same side… then in Kunxu, they’ll be invincible.”*

Just then, a call came in from the 33rd layer. Jing Hui asked Zhang Yu, “Minister Zhang, everything all right on your end? Have you finished creating the Chain Formation Diagram?”

Zhang Yu said, “I need a little more time.”

Jing Hui reminded him, “Minister Zhang, all we need to do is follow the Immortal Emperors’ orders. The last thing we, their subjects, should do is act on our own initiative. If we disrupt the arrangements made by those above us, the Immortal Emperors certainly won’t let us off lightly.”

After ending the call, Zhang Yu felt that every possible escape route before him had been cut off, one by one, leaving him trapped in darkness.

The only path that still shone with light was the one the ten Immortal Emperors had jointly pointed out.

But Zhang Yu knew in his heart that if he kept following the path laid out by the ten Immortal Emperors, this time it would be the Chain Formation Diagram, and next time it would be something else.

As he watched the Immortal Emperors chip away at the Books, severing its connections to the Feather Book and one Dao Lineage after another, his chances of resisting them would keep dwindling until he was ultimately served up at their table.

Immortal Slayer said grimly, “Damn it, are the ten Immortal Emperors really going to stand so firmly together this time?”

The Sect Examination Clone said helplessly, “We aren’t in a position to bargain right now. Even if it means watching the ten Immortal Emperors chip away at the Books, we can only accept it.”

Immortal Slayer said, “Today they take five cities; tomorrow, ten... But our lands are limited, while the tyrant’s greed knows no bounds.”

The Sect Examination Clone said, “Maybe things aren’t that bad. After all, if the Immortal Emperor keeps stripping away the Books like this, weakening us more and more until we’re too weak to achieve Immortality, then his investment will fail!”

Immortal Slayer said, “Maybe the Immortal Emperor wants to strip away the Books first so he can control us more easily, then use other methods to bring us to maturity, fattening us up to achieve Immortality in a more controllable way before swallowing us whole?”

Seeing that Zhang Yu remained silent, Fu Ji couldn’t help asking, “Zhang Yu, what’s wrong?”

Zhang Yu didn’t answer. He racked his brains, trying to think of some other way to fight back.

He turned the pages of the Feather Book before him one by one, but found nothing.

Only after Jing Hui’s third call urging him had ended did Zhang Yu suddenly notice lines of text appearing in the prologue of the Feather Book.

“Yu, to resist the Immortal Emperor, combine bloodline technology with a renewed derivation of the Immortal Emperor’s cultivation arts, slightly altering the effects of the Chain Formation Diagram…”

As he read the messages, Zhang Yu’s heart jolted. “Could this also be lost history? Is it only appearing now because it was set to be revealed at this moment?”

“Does this record the choice I made the last time I created the Chain Formation Diagram?”

“Using bloodline technology to derive the cultivation arts slightly altered the effects of the Chain Formation Diagram, altering the diagrams effects for all cultivators descended from my bloodline…”

Zhang Yu quickly read on: “But in the end... every cultivator descended from my bloodline had their physical bodies erased.”

“My method failed. It only caused many people to change jobs, switching from their original positions to Soul Cultivator posts.”

Zhang Yu sighed inwardly. He saw a final message appear on the prologue.

“I have no options left. I can only submit to the ten Immortal Emperors. I leave this message here in the hope that you won’t follow my example and cause enormous, pointless losses.”

Looking at the message, Zhang Yu let out a long sigh and thought, “Is there really no other choice?”

But just then, another line appeared beneath the message.

“I’ll help you.”

“So will I.”

“And me, too.”
