# Chapter 1008: A Bond That Transcends Time and Space

Above the Nine Heavens, Ying Xintian gazed down at the land of Kunlun, a trace of loneliness appearing in his eyes.

After unifying the myriad realms, subduing the various sects, and throwing open the gates of the Immortal Dao, the Immortal Dao Golden Age had arrived, and countless heavenly chosen had emerged. Yet he only felt increasingly alone.

The reason was simple: the more cultivators’ abilities grew and the stronger their innate talents became, the more their ambitions and desires took root in their hearts.

And this being, who stood at the very pinnacle of the Immortal Dao, had become the target of countless people’s pursuit — the opponent they all longed to defeat.

Ying Xintian understood even better that as long as he was unwilling to control, suppress, or restrain the cultivators of the world, and continued to give them enough room to grow, then one day someone would surpass him and turn the entire world into fuel for their personal advancement.

“The Immortal Dao flourishes, and heavenly chosen emerge one after another — a scene of great prosperity. But what is immortality sought for? What is the pinnacle desired for? In the end, it all comes down to personal desire.”

“Whether it takes thousands of years or tens of thousands, no matter how Immortal Dao Technology develops or what new advances it brings, the efforts of generation after generation will ultimately serve only one person’s desire, culminating in the birth of a single individual.”

Starting in the very next moment, he would prepare to sever the Immortal Dao, and his own strength would gradually begin to decline. The moment he made this decision might also be the moment when both his personal strength and the technological prowess of the Immortal Dao were at their peak.

At that very moment, a silent, spacetime-transcending disturbance swept across the entire world, and was detected by its ruler, Ying Xintian, hailed as the strongest Immortal Emperor of the age, the Supreme Immortal Emperor, the one and only Immortal Emperor.

“This is a spacetime disturbance unlike any before.”

Ying Xintian’s heart stirred. “The world is retreating, rolling back? And the source… isn’t in the present, but in the future.”

A glimmer of understanding flashed through his mind. “Did I fail in the future? The entire world is rolling back from the future, gradually returning to the past.”

“Or… did my opponent unleash this move from the future precisely to avoid facing me in the present?”

His cultivation at the peak of the Immortal Dao allowed Ying Xintian to keep thinking even as the rollback began, like a proud reef standing firm against the tide of spacetime rolling backward.

But Ying Xintian could feel that even he would not be able to withstand for long the tide building up in the future and crashing toward him with overwhelming force.

“This spacetime trend is beyond my power to stop.”

“At most, I have one more instant before I can no longer remain clear-headed.”

“Especially as the world continues returning to the past, I too will gradually grow weaker, until I can no longer perceive this change, right?”

“Then at the very least, I have to leave behind this information, so those who come after will know that this power exists in the world…”

At a mere thought from Ying Xintian, the shadow of The Book appeared before him.

In his judgment, if anything could convey this message, it would be this book connecting the various Dao Lineages throughout history.

But just as Ying Xintian was about to leave a message in the book, lines of text began to appear.

“What is this...?”

Ying Xintian looked at the writing before him. “Monster Saint?”

“Divine Dynasty?”

“Yu? Ten Immortal Emperors? Chain Formation Diagram...”

Having learned of the existence of rollback and read the mass of information appearing in the book, Ying Xintian instantly pieced together most of what had happened.

“Someone is constantly changing history, guiding its course. I, along with many cultivators, am doomed to fail.”

“But there’s still hope for those in the future. Unlike us, he discovered the rollback at an earlier stage of his life.”

“He even used the tide of rollback to send a message through the book, right before my eyes.”

To Ying Xintian, the World Rollback felt like wave after wave, pushing the entire world into the past.

And the information in the book before him had ridden the tide of the World Rollback, carrying information from the future to him, a failure.

“What a clever method. You turned the World Rollback to your advantage and sent the information to me just before I was rolled back?”

“Although, because of this, we who are about to be rolled back can no longer change history, but...”

“The future version of him is asking us for help.”

Ying Xintian looked at the information appearing in the book. It included Immortal Dao Technology for replying to messages in the book.

“The past can no longer be changed, and lost history cannot be recovered, but at least there is still hope in the future.”

As his thoughts raced, a faint smile had already appeared on Ying Xintian’s face.

“Good. Then at least in this moment before the world is completely rolled back, before I fail completely, I can still do one small thing that matters.”

So Ying Xintian left a message: “I’ll help you.”

---

In the infinitely high upper space of Kunlun.

The Monster Saint sensed the strange changes between Heaven and Earth and felt that the world was about to roll back into the past.

In that instant, the Monster Saint was shocked. “Who? Who has a technique like this?”

“Does that mean all my past failures were tied to this technique?”

The Monster Saint roared, and the fangs in his mouth grew dramatically, as if he wanted to declare war on the expanse of Heaven and Earth about to be rolled back before him.

But as he gazed upon the boundless world, no matter how fiercely the Monster Saint’s formidable will swept across it, he could not find a single enemy.

An unrivaled power surged within him, yet with no opponent, there was nowhere to unleash it.

Feeling that his body, will, and thoughts... were gradually unable to withstand the surging tide of space and time, and that he was about to be swept backward into the past, the Monster Saint felt a fierce pang of unwillingness.

But just then, the Monster Saint seemed to sense something.

The shadow of the Book stirred before him, though there was no wind, and in an instant, lines of text appeared across its pages.

“Haha! So that’s how it is. Success doesn’t come in my generation? Were there so many others who lost just like me?”

“Then, future person, take everything I have.”

The Monster Saint extended a gigantic, furry palm and left a message in the Book: “Count me in.”

---

Beneath the glorious Divine Dynasty, all living beings seemed to enjoy peace and harmony.

Immortal Emperor Heavenly Virtue sensed it all, a hint of contentment on his face.

Ever since he had steadily striven toward the peak of the Immortal Dao, established the Dao and achieved Immortality, founded the Heavenly Tribulation Dao Lineage, and finally risen step by step to become an Immortal Emperor, the entire world had undergone tremendous changes.

But the next moment, Immortal Emperor Heavenly Virtue’s gaze suddenly sharpened. He had already sensed the world rolling back.

Looking at the words appearing on the shadow of the Book, he understood everything in an instant.

“This path is impassable?”

“But at least someone still remembers us.”

“Then let’s make a few more notes.”

He reached out and wrote in the book, “Me too.”

---

At that very moment, Zhang Yu saw the Feather Book start flipping wildly on its own, as if driven by a wind, while new messages kept appearing.

“Who are these people? Could they all be me?”

Zhang Yu said in disbelief, “Are they communicating through the Feather Book?”

As if responding to the question in Zhang Yu’s mind, lines of messages appeared one after another.

“I am Ying Xintian.” Ying Xintian: “Since you’ve taken charge of the book, you should have a Feather Book, right?”

Immortal Heavenly Virtue: “I am Heavenly Virtue, from the Divine Dynasty. If you have a Feather Book, then as long as you parasitize the Technique-Chaining Dao Lineage before the Chain Formation Diagram and the book are completely disconnected, you can serve as a middleman, allowing the book to access the Technique-Chaining Dao Lineage through you. Then you won’t need to worry about the book being disconnected from the Chain Formation Diagram anymore...”

Monster Saint: “People of this era call me the Great Sage. I’ll compile the Immortal Dao Technology of the Chain Formation Diagrams into a cultivation art and give it to you...”

Watching the lines of text flash by, Zhang Yu felt as though he were witnessing a detailed plan being completed at an incredible speed through everyone’s exchanges.

In an instant, a solution appeared before Zhang Yu: a way to keep the book in his hands functioning as a Chain Formation Diagram even after its connection to the Book and the Technique-Chaining Dao Lineage was severed.

At that very moment, Zhang Yu suddenly noticed that some information was beginning to disappear.

It was precisely the future information about Zhang Yu creating the Chain Formation Diagram.

Zhang Yu immediately committed it to memory, sealed it away, and left a countdown.

Looking at the information that still remained in the book, Zhang Yu felt a violent shock in his heart. “These messages… could they be from Ying Xintian, the Monster Saint, and Heavenly Virtue, all in the lost history?”

Zhang Yu still remembered that one of the messages during the Seventh Thunder Tribulation had been from Immortal Heavenly Virtue.

“Why haven’t their messages disappeared?”

“Is it because they’re in the lost history, and can no longer change the future?”

A glimmer of understanding flashed through Zhang Yu’s mind. “Perhaps this is a flaw, a loophole in a World Rollback. Information about the future that reaches this point will be erased.”

“But information left in the book by history that has already been lost in the past won’t be erased.”

“No, it’s not just because of this. Otherwise, why am I only seeing this information now? There must be another reason that triggered all of this...”

Zhang Yu carefully studied the lines of messages that continued to appear, hoping to find more clues among them.

Ying Xintian: “Since there are ten Immortal Emperors ruling the world, if you want to oppose them, you’ll need to have accumulated more by the time you achieve Immortality.”

Monster Saint: “After achieving Immortality and establishing your Dao, aside from your own Dao Lineage, you’ll need to master at least four more Dao Lineages to have a chance of holding your own.”

Heavenly Virtue: “If the Four Books: Feather, Transformation, Flight, and Ascension could each connect to one of four ownerless Dao Lineages, allowing you to obtain their Dao Lineage's true intent, you’d have a chance to master four additional Dao Lineages immediately after achieving Immortality. If our Dao Lineages still existed, they’d be a good choice. The specific method is as follows...”

Advice on acquiring additional Dao Lineages surfaced one piece at a time. They also told him about the Dao Lineages of Ying Xintian, Monster Saint, and Heavenly Virtue. Zhang Yu quickly absorbed and memorized all the relevant Immortal Dao techniques, then formed a rough summary in his mind.

“To oppose the Ten Immortal Emperors’ attempts to cut away the Books's powers, I need to parasitize the Technique-Chaining Dao Lineage.”

“To stand against the Ten Immortal Emperors, I need to gather the Four Books of Feathered Ascension and use them, along with the techniques taught by the three Seniors, to claim the ownerless Dao Lineages. That way, after achieving Immortality, I’ll gain control of four additional Great Dao Lineages, making me the sole master of five Great Dao Lineages.”

“But how difficult it would be to accomplish all this under the watchful eyes of the Ten Immortal Emperors!”

For a moment, Zhang Yu’s thoughts raced. Then the final messages from the three appeared in the Feather Book.

Heavenly Virtue: “Do good deeds without asking what lies ahead. I’m off.”

Great Sage: “The achievement need not be mine. Those who come after us… take our failures as stepping stones and make free use of them.”

Ying Xintian: “I’m about to be rolled back, but I believe every rollback represents another attempt. As long as we can pass this experience on, there will surely be someone who comes after us.”

As he read each message, a flicker of understanding crossed Zhang Yu’s mind. “So they communicated at the very moment the rollback occurred?”

“Could it be that when World Rollback begins, the Book can use the power of the rollback to communicate with people from the lost history of the past?”

He looked at the sealed future information and thought to himself, “Could this future information be what set everything in motion?”

“Did the future me discover this loophole in the rollback and win their support by leaving messages in the book?”

At that moment, fighting spirit surged within Immortal Slayer. “We aren’t fighting alone. The seniors who fought to change Kunxu in the past are also pushing us forward.”

Sect Examination Clone sighed inwardly. “Mother Supreme Truth opened our eyes and made us aware of rollback. Our three fathers — Ying Xintian, the Monster Saint, and Heavenly Virtue — answered our call for help, giving us more support and crucial information now that our eyes are open.”

“Zhang Yu! We must achieve Immortality and establish our Dao, master the five Great Dao Lineages, and gain the means to contend with the Immortal Emperors without being annihilated by the Ten Immortal Emperors!”

Desolate Ox was also fired up. “The Book has passed down the will of the powerful from ages past, helping us fight the Ten Immortal Emperors and this world, warped by the aspiration for the Immortal Dao. At the final moment of rollback, they lit up the path ahead that had once been pitch-black.”

“At last, we’ve found a faint hope of resisting the Ten Immortal Emperors!”

Zhang Yu didn’t respond to the excited thoughts stirring one after another in his mind. Instead, he seized the time to begin cultivating the art left behind by Monster Saint.

This mental cultivation technique was called the *Technique-Chaining Heart Sutra*. With his supreme cultivation, Monster Saint had gathered within it all kinds of Immortal Dao knowledge concerning the Chain Formation Diagram.

When Zhang Yu quickly finished cultivating this mental technique, countless pieces of knowledge related to the Chain Formation Diagram had already flowed into his mind.

The next moment, he turned the Feather Book to the Immortal Foundation Chapter and saw that among the Dao Lineages available for parasitism, the Technique-Chaining Dao Lineage now ranked first in compatibility.

Zhang Yu immediately switched the target of the parasitic Dao Lineage, then poured the remaining Great Dao Essence into it. In an instant, he drove his Dao-Seeking Progress in the Technique-Chaining Dao Lineage all the way to 45%.

Sensing the changes within and around his body, Zhang Yu thought to himself, “The Chain Formation Diagram is opening up to me. Even without the support of The Book, I can now use the Chain Formation Diagram.”

“However, with only 45% Dao-Seeking Progress, I can use only the Chain Formation Diagrams for these four realms: Qi Refining Realm through Nascent Soul Realm…”

A flicker of understanding crossed Zhang Yu’s mind. “So my next objective is clear.”

“While the Ten Immortal Emperors promote the new Chain Formation Diagram and gradually cut down on the time spent using the Book, I must keep collecting Books and advancing my Dao-Seeking Progress.”

“Before the connection between the Chain Formation Diagrams and the Books is severed completely, I must advance my own Dao-Seeking Progress in the Chain Formation Diagram to 100%.”

“By then, even if the connection between the Book and the Chain Formation Diagrams is severed, I’ll still be able to use the Chain Formation Diagrams perfectly through myself.”

Just then, Zhang Yu received another message from Jing Hui, urging him to finish creating the Chain Formation Diagram as soon as possible. Her wording had grown increasingly stern.

---

Not long afterward, Lianzi Zi, who was frantically gathering intelligence from all quarters on behalf of the pharmaceutical company, felt his heart jolt. “Has the Chain Formation Diagram been created?”

“Could it be one of the cultivation arts I bought?”

Tense, doubtful, curious, hopeful, excited, and beset by a jumble of other emotions, Lianzi Zi opened the announcement. “The newly created Chain Formation Diagram is called Immortal Emperor’s Successor?”

Uninterested in seeing the specific effects of the Chain Formation Diagram, Lianzi Zi quickly skipped past them and looked at the cultivation arts it contained.

“The first one isn’t… The second one isn’t either…”

He scanned the list of cultivation arts and checked them all in an instant. Greatly disappointed, Lianzi Zi then felt anger well up inside him. “Not a single one of the cultivation arts I bought in advance made the cut?”

“Zhang Yu, you little brat, you tricked me!”

But when he thought of Zhang Yu’s current status, all Lianzi Zi could feel was a trace of bitterness.

He knew there was no way he could settle this score with Zhang Yu.

On the one hand, the other party was powerful and well-connected. On the other, what grounds did he have to go after him? Was he supposed to say he wanted to learn sect secrets ahead of time so he could make a fortune?

As the Dharma Drug took effect, Lianzi Zi quickly calmed down. He consoled himself: “At least I only got scammed out of a few million. If I’d been in Zhang Yu’s position, he’d have scammed me out of at least ten million.”

At the same time, many cultivators within the sect were consumed by the same bitter fury as Lianzi Zi.

Some were cultivators Zhang Yu had called to a meeting to collect books from; others were targets Zhang Yu had set his sights on over the past few days.

Cang Juehuan: “Zhang Yu, you beastly superior! Myriad Laws, you Emperor Beast! You teamed up to scam us poor people!”

Bu Aixian, a Tribulation Transcendence Cultivator from the Myriad Laws Immortal Clan in charge of charitable work, roared inwardly: “That was charity money! You even scammed me out of that?! You’re unfit to be human! Unfit for Immortal Cultivation!”

Venerable Myriad Affinity, a Joyful Union Sect cultivator who had once served in Coitus Conscription alongside “Zhang Yu,” felt a surge of dark fury: “These cultivators from the Myriad Laws Sect even scam their dual cultivation partners? That’s so against the rules. We in the Joyful Union Sect would never pull something so underhanded!”

---

Inside the Immortal Grotto-Heaven, Zhang Yu looked at the 100 million Immortal Coins in his account and thought, “It really is easy to rake in money by invoking the Immortal Emperors.”

These 100,000,000 Immortal Coins were the proceeds from selling off all the cultivation art copyrights he had stockpiled.

Fu Ji said, “We still didn’t rake in enough. If you ask me, we should’ve sent out scam emails far and wide. Why target only a few people?”

The Sect Examination Clone said, “Although the Ten Immortal Emperors decided in advance who would get the slots for these ten cultivation arts, they’re the ones most to blame. But the blame still falls on us. It’s fine to scam a few people we have a grudge against, but if I scam everyone I meet, my credit in other people’s eyes will be completely used up. If my reputation in the sect gets too bad, how am I supposed to keep earning a steady stream of money in the future?”

Zhang Yu said nothing. Instead, he seized the time to push ahead with the next phase of the Book Collection Plan.

“We have to collect Books as quickly as possible and advance our Dao-Seeking Progress...”

Cultivating, collecting books, working,... Days passed one after another as Zhang Yu kept himself busy.

Before he knew it, it was already September of the next year.

And on this day, Zhang Yu received a message inside the Immortal’s Cave.

“Is the Book-Wielder Assembly starting?”

Seeing the notification, Zhang Yu thought to himself, “Looks like this Assembly is where we’ll complete the final collection of Books and revive the Immortal Ancestor next month.”
