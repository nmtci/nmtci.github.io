Lu Jingsong could be heard saying, "Keep placing short orders for me. Borrow as many shares as you possibly can!"

"We'll eat the very last bite of meat off these retail cultivators before we exit the market!"

Looking at the profit chart for this run, a trace of joy flashed in Lu Jingsong's heart. He turned to He Zhaisheng beside him and said, "President He, although Zhang Yu consecutively triggered five thunder tribulations in just a short month and a half, which somewhat disrupted our plans, with you presiding over the big picture, seeing opportunities early on, and strategizing, the higher-ups will definitely be satisfied with the yield this time."

He Zhaisheng nodded slightly. This time, there was a surge followed by a plummet... They had profited from both swings, making an absolute fortune.

He Zhaisheng couldn't help but think to himself, *"I wonder if the Immortal will assign me to a higher-tier company to manage even larger-scale funds..."*

Just as his imagination was running wild, a sliver of doubt crept into his mind: *"Why hasn't there been a reply from the Immortal yet?"*

Right at that moment, Lu Jingsong spoke up from the side, "President He, it seems Zhang Yu is about to initiate the sixth thunder tribulation."

"Oh? Is that so?"

He Zhaisheng looked at the live broadcast screen and thought to himself, *"He really is in a hurry..."*

In He Zhaisheng's view, Zhang Yu's Qi Refining and Golden Core thunder tribulations were too weak and had long been exhausted, but even so... over the past month, it was equivalent to triggering the Foundation Establishment, Nascent Soul, and Spirit Transformation thunder tribulations all at the same time.

This gave He Zhaisheng the feeling that something was chasing Zhang Yu from behind, forcing him to initiate one thunder tribulation after another.

"And now he wants to start the sixth thunder tribulation? Can he even handle it?"

A trace of doubt welled up in his heart: "Why is he in such a rush... what exactly is the reason?"

He Zhaisheng had also asked Zhang Yu, but unfortunately, the other party never answered.

But the next moment, accompanied by a flash of bright light, the live broadcast suddenly disconnected.

"Hmm?" He Zhaisheng thought to himself. "The stream stopped?"

At first, He Zhaisheng thought there was a problem with the Spirit Realm connection on his end.

A few seconds later, he discovered that the entire company could not see the live broadcast.

A moment later, he confirmed that a malfunction had occurred in the streaming room. Just as Zhang Yu started the sixth thunder tribulation, all the viewers in the stream... could only see a black screen.
