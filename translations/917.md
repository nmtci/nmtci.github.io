Within that endless shadow, Lie Jinghong could distinctly feel the surging Thought Tide rising and falling, baring its vicious glare and roaring at him.

"Whether it's you incompetent commoners or Zhang Yu... none of you will ever defeat me."

"I, Lie Jinghong, will absolutely not admit defeat!"

Lie Jinghong was going to mortgage his assets; he was going to gamble everything.

Even if he could only hold on for another 0.1 or 0.2 seconds, he would continue to hold out.

However, in this split second of fierce battle, streams of light and shadow burst from Zhang Yu's palm as he tore at the Law Edict... Impressively, it was the data proof of the blank Law Scroll.

Lie Jinghong: "There really is a blank Law Scroll?"

"A blank Law Scroll specially approved by the Immortal Emperor?!"

At this moment, he felt the pitch-black hands, formed by the malicious thoughts of the masses, suddenly surge in strength, dragging the better half of his body into the abyss of defeat.

Meanwhile, Zhang Yu's hand, bearing the proof of the blank Law Scroll, seemed to transform into the giant hand of the Immortal Emperor at this moment... It slammed ruthlessly down on his head, aiming to crush him and thoroughly defeat him without leaving the slightest chance of victory.

"Those who submit to Heaven prosper, those who defy it perish, and Grandpa Immortal Emperor... is the Heaven of the Myriad Laws Sect."

He sighed in his heart, *If Heaven wants me to lose, then I have no choice but to lose.*

Having lost his will to fight, Lie Jinghong, who was already at an absolute disadvantage, couldn't delay for even another 0.1 seconds.
