# Chapter 1031: Zhang Yu's Black Wall

"This is bad, My Lord! The Great Sage's Heir has roared across the Immortal Realm, broadcasting a message to every cultivator and Immortal from the 21st layer to the 29th layer!"

Hearing this report, Bing Yisi, who was in charge of tracking down the Great Sage's Heir, felt his spirits lift. "It's time for me to earn merits again! A Great Sage's Heir capable of roaring across the Immortal Realm? If I can participate in the manhunt..."

Yet before the thoughts could finish running through Bing Yisi's mind, another report appeared before him.

"This is bad, My Lord! The one speaking across the Immortal Realm with the Great Sage's Intent is the Crown Prince of Myriad Laws, Zhang Yu!"

Bing Yisi felt another jolt through his heart and abruptly froze.

Remembering how the Great Gods had stopped him last time, he could not help but think to himself, *"The Crown Prince of Myriad Laws... is truly the Great Sage's Heir?"*

Bing Yisi also recalled several years ago, when he had tested Zhang Yu in the laboratory of the Edict Pavilion.

*"To think that back then, the Great Sage's Heir was right in front of me, yet I failed to detect it."*

Just at that moment, another report sent from the Immortal Realm appeared before his eyes.

"Zhang Yu is threatening to shut down the Spirit Realm's functions, demanding a dialogue with all the Immortals of the Myriad Laws Sect?!"

Bing Yisi's heart shook violently. *"Making such a massive scene?!"*

And his very first thought was that he absolutely could not let himself get dragged into this.

A subordinate asked, "My Lord, according to protocol, we're supposed to go up to the Immortal Realm to track down the Great Sage's Heir. What should we do?"

Bing Yisi snapped, "What report? There is no report!"

The subordinate blinked in confusion, glancing down at the report that had arrived from the Immortal Realm.

Bing Yisi said indifferently, "I haven't received any report."

"What I left behind here is merely a projection. My main body went down to the 1st layer to track down the Great Sage's Intent. The Spirit Realm signal was poor, so I haven't received any report yet. If anyone comes asking about this matter, that's exactly what you tell them."

---

Within the Grotto-Heaven of the Myriad Laws Immortal Clan.

Bu Yingshu was among the very first sect cultivators to notice this incident.

After all, Spirit Realm transmissions were constantly flowing between the sect layers and the Immortal Realm.

Moreover, Zhang Yu's Great Sage's Intent had been broadcast simultaneously to all cultivators and Immortals across the 21st to 29th layers, naturally causing a massive stir. Word quickly made its way down to the sect layers.

Furthermore, Bu Yingshu had long since set 'Great Sage's Heir' as a monitored keyword, so the moment any wind blew regarding the Great Sage's Heir, the relevant information would immediately pop up before her eyes.

*"Zhang Yu is the Great Sage's Heir?"*

*"A broadcast across the entire 21st to 29th layers?"*

*"Threatening the Immortal Class?!"*

With every piece of information Bu Yingshu read, she was struck with intense shock.

And among all these news pieces capable of shaking the sect to its core, what instinctively drew her attention the most was...

"Zhang Yu used the Great Sage's Intent to broadcast to the Immortal Realm."

Bu Yingshu clenched her fists tightly, her heart burning with resentment. *"So it wasn't just the Crown Prince position — he also stole my Great Sage Power! No wonder I never sensed my Great Sage Power again after that!"*

"Celestial Inquiry Star, I have a friend..."

After recounting her grievances, Bu Yingshu asked, "What should my... that friend of mine do?"

Celestial Inquiry Star (Thinking deeply): "The user's statements might be a form of delusional/paranoid expression, or perhaps an internet meme. Proceed with caution, show empathy, do not reinforce the delusion, clarify the facts..."

Bu Yingshu: "Take this seriously."

Celestial Inquiry Star: "You're absolutely right, and I completely understand that feeling of having something 'stolen'. However, rather than obsessing over sunk costs, it might be better to view it as an investment in that person and try to gain a return from them."

Bu Yingshu thought to herself, *"Right, now isn't the time to think about this. I've helped Zhang Yu so much; I deserve an even greater return."*

Bu Yingshu asked again, "But he's threatening the entire Immortal Class of the Myriad Laws Sect right now! If he gets killed by the Immortals, won't I lose everything?!"

Celestial Inquiry Star: "The user's mention of 'threatening the Immortal Class' might be a test for me. I need to avoid escalating or diagnosing, but adhere to factual boundaries..."

*"Useless,"* Bu Yingshu thought to herself. *"Sigh, now that I'm gradually getting involved in the clashes at the peak of power within the sect, Celestial Inquiry Star is becoming less and less helpful."*

Thinking of this, Bu Yingshu quickly tapped on Bu Yuangui's avatar, deciding it was best to consult her father. Bu Yingshu: "Dad, did Zhang Yu really threaten the Immortals? What should I do?"

From the moment Zhang Yu broadcasted to the Immortal Realm, to the news spreading, to Bu Yingshu's rapid chain of thoughts, barely a second or two had passed.

Thus, when Bu Yingshu sent the message to Bu Yuangui, he had only just learned of the news himself.

At that moment, a tempest seemed to rage within his heart, and he was filled with utter shock.

Bu Yuangui first replied to Bu Yingshu: "The sect has its own plans. Do nothing for now, and obediently stay in your Immortal Abode."

Next, Bu Yuangui looked toward the Spirit Realm projection of Zhang Yu before him, an inevitable question arising in his mind.

What kind of trump card did this unfathomable Crown Prince of Myriad Laws truly possess to dare threaten the Immortals with disconnecting from the Spirit Realm? Did he truly have no fear of the Immortals escalating sanctions, or even triggering an armed conflict?

At the same time, Fa Liuyuan, standing nearby, spoke up, "Your Highness the Crown Prince! Did you just issue an ultimatum to the Immortal Realm?"

Having confirmed what Zhang Yu had done, Fa Liuyuan immediately urged caution: "Your Highness, the Immortal Class has deep roots and vast influence. Not only do they run all kinds of Dao Lineages, but they also command countless cultivators enforcing Law Edicts. It would still be best to prioritize negotiations..."

Zhang Yu said indifferently, "Heavenly Monarch Fa, say no more. My mind is made up."

"When Heavenly Fortune Immortal Venerable threw the sect into chaos, did he negotiate with us beforehand? No, he did not."

"Because the Immortal Class has looked down upon lower-tier cultivators for far too long. Unless we show sufficient leverage, they will never sit at the same table to speak with us."

"I will bring the Myriad Laws Immortals under control, and the first step begins today."

Heavenly Monarch Fa sighed helplessly, while beside him, Dugu Ming looked at Zhang Yu, unable to stop a thought from surfacing in his mind: *"At this moment, Zhang Yu actually carries a hint of an Immortal Emperor's bearing."*

*"Provided, of course, that Zhang Yu can truly intimidate the Immortals this time."*

*"Can he... really pull it off?"*

---

The Immortal Realm.

Ling Chenji of the Innate faction, an Ascension Realm cultivator who looked like a young matron, was originally a member of the Immortal Ascension Hall. But ever since the Immortals of the Myriad Laws Sect initiated the sanctions and summoned cultivators, she had arrived in the Immortal Realm alongside Yi Yaosheng, Su Xianchen, and others.

Although her relative authority had diminished considerably after arriving in the Immortal Realm, and she had to take on a great deal of work serving the Immortals, the superior cultivation environment of the Immortal Realm and having more avenues to approach the Immortals also made her see greater opportunities.

For instance, plans such as advancing the schedule for the Ascension Selection to cultivate more Immortals were things Ling Chenji could now learn about a step ahead of time and begin preparing for.

Yet, when Ling Chenji heard Zhang Yu's speech, the first thought that flashed through her mind was: *"Has Zhang Yu lost his mind?"*

*"Threatening the Immortals? What in the world is he trying to do? Does he really think having the title of Crown Prince of Myriad Laws makes him the next Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor?"*

*"No, could it be an impostor — a Great Sage's Heir pretending to be him? Purposely trying to stir up internal conflict within the Myriad Laws Sect?"*

*"Even if it is fake, the Immortals might use this opportunity to harvest the lower realm even further."*

At this moment, Ling Chenji could not help but secretly feel fortunate: *"Thank goodness I didn't stay down there. Otherwise, I might have been dragged down by Zhang Yu as well."*

At the same time, she saw messages popping up in the group chat.

It was a chat group formed by the Myriad Laws Sect cultivators who had ascended to the Immortal Realm together.

Right then, Yi Yaosheng from the pharmaceutical company was asking: "Was that speech really from Zhang Yu? Could someone be impersonating him?"

Su Xianchen remarked with a sigh: "Whether it's really him or not no longer matters. After stirring up such a massive commotion, the Immortals will definitely not let this go, and they will likely escalate their sanctions even further."

Su Xianchen even suspected that the Immortal Class had deliberately framed Zhang Yu, fabricating an excuse to keep cracking down on him.

He thought to himself, *"In the end, even if Zhang Yu sits on the throne of the Crown Prince of Myriad Laws, he is still too weak compared to the Immortal Class."*

*"Were it not for the impending doomsday and the other nine sects watching covetously like tigers eyeing their prey, even with the protection of the Eight Divisions Great Gods, the Immortals could easily depose him as Crown Prince of Myriad Laws if they were willing to bear the losses."*

At this moment, a trace of relief arose in the hearts of both Su Xianchen and Yi Yaosheng.

They could already imagine that had they remained in the lower realm, they might very well be shivering inside the Immortal Ascension Hall right now, constantly sending messages to the Immortals in a desperate attempt to explain themselves, only to receive no reply at all.

Meanwhile, inside the immortal abodes of the two Immortals of the Reformist Faction, the initial reactions of Immortal Roujia and Immortal Yi Xuan upon hearing Zhang Yu's broadcast were surprise and bewilderment.

It was not until they sent a message to Fa Liuyuan that they finally confirmed... it truly was Zhang Yu who had made that speech just now.

Immortal Roujia: "Preposterous!"

Immortal Yi Xuan: "What a childish threat. Does he truly believe that holding the Crown Prince Authority allows him to check and balance the Immortals in the Spirit Realm?"

The two Immortals had just made sweeping promises before Heavenly Fortune Immortal Venerable, assuring that they would persuade Zhang Yu to surrender, yet Zhang Yu had stirred up such massive chaos in the blink of an eye.

This completely shattered their plan of acting as middlemen to control the communication channel between the Immortal Realm and the sect.

Immortal Roujia immediately sent a message to Zhang Yu, only to discover...

"I've been blacklisted?"

A hint of bewilderment also surfaced in Immortal Yi Xuan's heart. "I've been blacklisted as well."

"Can a lower cultivator blacklist an Immortal? Doesn't that violate sect law?"

When the two Immortals brought this question to Fa Liuyuan, they heard him reply, "His Royal Highness the Crown Prince altered that sect law. Immortals can now be blacklisted."

Immortal Yi Xuan said, "Outrageous! He is staging an outright rebellion!"

At the same time, the wills of numerous Immortals converged within the Immortal Realm to discuss the incident.

The controller of the Innate Dao Lineage, an existence revered as the Innate Immortal — wherever his will swept across, countless lower-tier cultivators felt their hearts tighten, overwhelmed with boundless reverence toward the Immortal Class.

Even the immortal aura, magic power, and spiritual energy in the atmosphere fell into rank and order according to status and hierarchy.

Countless cultivators discovered to their horror that their cultivation had ceased to grow at this moment. No matter how hard they tried or how diligently they worked, they could no longer improve their existing cultivation arts, learn new ones, let alone develop Immortal Dao Technology or advance their cultivation realms.

A terrifying thought rose in their minds: "The Innate Immortal is here!"

Yet in the very next instant, the cultivators enveloped by the Dao Lineage returned to calm. Not only did they accept the reality that their cultivation had halted, but they also felt no unwillingness whatsoever, their minds filled with tranquility — an absolute, profound tranquility.

A cultivator sighed with emotion, "Innate Distinction, each following their nature, returning to the state of an infant... I am already at this level, so I ought to maintain this natural state, rather than rashly advancing and disrupting the balance of this world."

However, in the following moment, another will arrived, shouting at the Innate Immortal, "Old monster Innate, rein in your Dao Lineage! Don't affect everyone's profits."

The Innate Immortal sighed, "Alas, if the common folk of the world were not swept up by their aspiration for the Immortal Dao, but rather pacified by my Innate Dao Lineage, having everyone accept their respective ranks, cultivation realms, and assets, how could there ever be an Ultimate Doomsday?"

"And where would there be people blinded by greed like Zhang Yu? Even with the title of Crown Prince and the Ascension Realm, he remains unsatisfied. Now, with doomsday approaching, he still strives for power and profit. Before even achieving immortality, he vainly attempts to restrain Immortals and stir up chaos."

Immortal Yi Xuan of the Reformist Faction snorted coldly in his heart, thinking to himself, *"If your Innate Dao Lineage really swept across Kunxu, how could anyone make progress? How could anyone make money?"*

Both Immortal Yi Xuan and Immortal Roujia knew that precisely because of the hazards of the Innate Dao Lineage, the Immortals had always consciously suppressed it. Yet, because the Innate Dao Lineage had its merits in governing lower-tier cultivators, they had not suppressed it too harshly either.

*"That's why, although the Innate Immortal is a veteran among the Immortals, his strength is merely middling, and he is firmly kept in check by the Immortal Venerables."*

At the same time, the power of the Dharma Realm violently trembled.

Immortal Yi Xuan understood in his heart that this meant the controller of the Dharma Realm Dao Lineage, the entity known as the Dharma Realm Immortal, had arrived.

The purpose of the Dharma Realm Dao Lineage's existence was to use Immortal Dao Technology to quantify everything in the world and turn it all into tradable commodities.

And as the controller of the Dao Lineage, the Dharma Realm Immortal was the executor of all these transactions. Theoretically, he could employ all the Immortal Dao Technology encompassed within the Dharma Realm Dao Lineage and trade all the Immortal Dao resources contained within it.

Immortal Yi Xuan knew even better that because the Dharma Realm Dao Lineage could support the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor, the current Dharma Realm Immortal — though possessing theoretically boundless potential — was firmly suppressed by the numerous Immortals, who refused to permit him any breakthrough.

Immortal Yi Xuan sighed inwardly, *"The moment a Dao Lineage is established, the upper limit of one's Immortal Dao might very well be decided alongside it."*

He then heard the Dharma Realm Immortal say, "We must ascertain whether Zhang Yu is merely bluffing, or if he truly possesses the means."

Immortal Yi Xuan understood in his heart that much of the Dharma Realm's technology was built upon the foundation of the Spirit Realm; Zhang Yu's warning posed a grave threat to the Dharma Realm Dao Lineage.

Yet the Innate Immortal said indifferently, "Regardless of what hidden cards Zhang Yu holds, an inferior defying a superior and offending the dignity of Immortals cannot be lightly forgiven. I propose that he be stripped of his position as Crown Prince."

Even though Zhang Yu held the title of Crown Prince, as long as he had not ascended to become an Immortal, in the eyes of the Immortals he remained merely a low-tier cultivator — a mortal, not an Immortal.

Another Immortal spoke up, "Zhang Yu is under the protection of the Eight Divisions Great Gods. Resorting to force against him at this juncture would disrupt our overall plans."

An Innate Immortal said, "Zhang Yu comes from the Reformist Faction, so the Reformist Faction should be the one to deal with him."

"Yi Xuan, Roujia, this matter ought to be undertaken by you."

"You go hold back the Eight Divisions Great Gods, and then we will suppress Zhang Yu."

Immortal Yi Xuan and Immortal Roujia's hearts sank. *"Coming after us? You want us to bear the cost of dealing with the Eight Divisions Great Gods while you reap the benefits of handling Zhang Yu?"*

Immortal Yi Xuan said, "There is no need for such trouble. Just raise the sanction level again, reduce the downward supply of resources like immortal aura and the sun and moon, and Zhang Yu and his people won't last long."

Just as the wills of the numerous Immortals were clashing and communicating back and forth, roughly ten seconds had already passed since Zhang Yu's warning.

And right at this moment, Dharma Realm Immortal spoke up, "Everyone, there is a new development that you all ought to see."

Ever since Zhang Yu issued his warning, Dharma Realm Immortal had been conducting observations, wanting to understand what gave Zhang Yu the confidence to speak like that, and whether he truly possessed the power to threaten the Spirit Realm and the Dharma Realm.

And during this time, he gradually perceived a wave of darkness spreading through the Spirit Realm between the 19th and 21st layers. 

**Boom!!**

Within the Spirit Realm, a black wall suddenly erupted into existence with a roar, thrusting itself between the two layers.

The tall wall was entirely pitch-black, as though forged from pure darkness, swallowing all information that flowed past it.

When Dharma Realm Immortal first discovered this black wall, it had only covered a few thousand square meters of the Spirit Realm at most.

Yet soon, the black wall expanded at an unprecedented speed, endlessly stretching through the Spirit Realm like a black canopy, looming over the entire 19th layer.

"Between the 19th and 21st layer of the Spirit Realm, a black wall is rapidly expanding and is about to sever the Spirit Realm between the 19th and 21st layer."

Along with the message sent by Dharma Realm Immortal, the numerous immortals all looked over.

"Is this Zhang Yu's trump card?" Immortal Yi Xuan snorted coldly. Sweeping his consciousness forward, he intended to use his own Spirit Realm techniques to breach this so-called black wall.

After all, in his eyes, how could the Spirit Realm techniques of lower-tier cultivators ever compare to those of an immortal?

Yet the moment his consciousness touched the black wall, he discovered that the Spirit Realm ahead seemed to have vanished without a trace.

It was not some restriction, nor was it any kind of Spirit Realm offense, defense, or shielding...

Rather, as far as Immortal Yi Xuan was concerned, the Spirit Realm ahead had completely ceased to exist.

Since the Spirit Realm was gone, then naturally, no matter how he transmitted messages or what Spirit Realm techniques he employed, it was all utterly futile.

Even so, a notification still surfaced before Immortal Yi Xuan's eyes.

"Unauthorized Spirit Realm connection detected. Would you like to apply to the Crown Prince of Myriad Laws to establish a new connection?"

Immortal Yi Xuan felt as if he might laugh out of sheer fury. As a dignified Immortal, using the Spirit Realm actually required him to apply to Zhang Yu? At the same time, he felt a flicker of shock in his heart and said, "Did Zhang Yu shut down the Spirit Realm here? We have to apply to him just to connect down below? Where did he get this kind of authority?"

Dharma Realm Immortal said indifferently, "I pulled some Spirit Realm logs. Zhang Yu has likely taken control of the Desolate Ox Great Sage and seized a portion of the authority over the Spirit Realm Dao Lineage."

Immortal Roujia exclaimed in dismay, "The Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor even gave him authority like that?"

Dharma Realm Immortal replied, "Now is not the time to dwell on that..."

Right at that moment, the black wall completely severed the Spirit Realm between the 19th Layer and the 21st Layer.

Immediately afterward, under the observation of the Immortals, the black wall continued to spread toward the Immortal Realm, gradually expanding from a black wall into a dark domain.

Within the Immortals' Spirit Realm Vision, the Spirit Realm of the entire 21st layer gradually began to dim. Boundless Spirit Realm information and Spirit Realm assets vanished into thin air, and even the Dharma Realm lost its brilliance along with it.

Dharma Realm Immortal said with a solemn expression, "If we allow the black wall to continue expanding, by the 60th second after Zhang Yu finishes speaking, the entire Spirit Realm will turn into absolute darkness for us."

Realizing this, the hearts of the two Immortals of the Reformist Faction sank.

In the next moment, each employed their own methods to investigate the status of the other sects.

Immortal Roujia said coldly, "The other sects are unaffected. Only we can see the black wall, because the Spirit Realm has been shut down specifically for us, as well as for everyone under our companies and platforms."

Both Immortals understood in their hearts that a significant portion of their various assets was stored in Spirit Realm accounts. Being unable to connect to the Spirit Realm meant an immense loss.

Beyond that, the pharmaceutical platforms, gambling platforms, cultivation method platforms, and more under their control all relied on the Spirit Realm to conduct business.

If these platforms were cut off from the Spirit Realm, how could they continue to harvest the Ascension and Tribulation Transcendence cultivators?

How could the Ascension and Tribulation Transcendence cultivators harvest the Void Refinement and Spirit Transformation cultivators?

How could the countless Qi Refining and Foundation Establishment cultivators of the lower realms provide for the Golden Core and Nascent Soul stages? And how could the Golden Core and Nascent Soul cultivators work for the Void Refinement and Spirit Transformation masters?

Both Immortals understood that while the lowest tier of cultivators might not hold any direct significance to them, it was precisely through this layer-by-layer tribute, cultivation, and upward transfer of resources that their own cultivation bases and wealth could continually expand.

*"Whether it's assets or cultivation, things are only healthy when they flow. But right now, Zhang Yu is severing that liquidity."*

At the thought that the favorable business and cultivation environments they had spent years painstakingly building might be ruined by this, both Immortals felt a pang of heartache.

What worried them even more was that if they stopped expanding, the Immortals of other sects would not stop. Instead, they would seize the market vacancies left behind, widening the gap between them at an even faster pace.

As with the Spirit Realm, so too would the Dharma Realm be affected.

The thought of falling behind the Immortals of other sects in these days leading up to the end of the world sent a faint shiver of danger through all the Myriad Laws Sect Immortals present.

Innate Immortal said, "What are you all waiting for? Before the black wall seals off the Spirit Realm, Zhang Yu should be thoroughly suppressed."

Dharma Realm Immortal countered, "We cannot! Just because Zhang Yu set a sixty-second timer doesn't mean it will actually take him sixty seconds to complete the lockdown. What if he completes the seal the very instant we make a move?"

"You should all be well aware of Zhang Yu's ruthlessness. If we act rashly, he truly might throw all caution to the wind, tear off all pretenses of cordiality, and immediately lock down the Spirit Realm. When that time comes, both sides will suffer, and the situation will become troublesome."

As the saying goes, a person's reputation precedes them. If another cultivator were to make such a threat, even if they also held the Spirit Realm Dao Lineage, the Immortals might still doubt whether that person possessed the resolve to fight to the death.

However, Zhang Yu's past performances time and again, and his decisiveness when facing various upper-tier cultivators, were enough to convince the Immortals of his resolve.

Immortal Roujia said, "Dharma Realm, you definitely have a backup plan, don't you? Using other technological means to replace the underlying architecture of the Spirit Realm."

Dharma Realm Immortal replied, "Who doesn't have a backup plan? But replacing the entire underlying architecture of the Spirit Realm with new technology in the remaining 46.78 seconds — who do you think could pull that off?"

In just a single second, the Immortals had already engaged in thousands of rounds of negotiations, yet they still failed to reach a consensus on a plan.

For instance, if they were to strike at the lower realm, who would handle the Eight Divisions Great Gods? How much effort would each contribute? Who would bear the losses? Every single detail, down to the minutest fraction, had to be debated by the Immortals.

If they were to negotiate with Zhang Yu, how should they go about it? Who would take responsibility? Who would bear the cost?

If they were to devise an alternative to the Spirit Realm, countless benefits and costs would likewise need to be redistributed.

And as everyone knew, Immortals possessed flawless bodies; they accepted no losses and paid no price, which only pushed the discussions further into a stalemate.

Just as the impasse dragged on, the immortal aura between heaven and earth surged. Immortal Venerable Profound Wisdom's will crossed the world through the immortal aura, entering the minds of the many Immortals.

"The other sects are watching us like tigers eyeing their prey, and the competition on the 30th layer is fast approaching. It is unwise to lightly provoke conflict within the sect."

With the descent of Immortal Venerable Profound Wisdom's will, the Immortals favoring negotiation instantly gained the upper hand.

Another Immortal asked, "What does Zhang Yu want?"

Heaven Realm Immortal said calmly, "First of all, he likely wants Heavenly Fortune Immortal Venerable to cease interfering with layers 11 through 19."

Many of the Immortals present were aware of the situation and knew that this conflict had stemmed from precisely that.

Immortal Roujia advised Heavenly Fortune Immortal Venerable, "Consider the bigger picture. For the time being, our Myriad Laws Sect still needs to do business using the Spirit Realm. We cannot disrupt internal unity."

Immortal Yi Xuan also tried to persuade him, "We can't fight, we really can't fight. If we fight, how will we make money? If we fall behind the Immortals of the other sects, how will they deal with us?"

Immortal Roujia added, "Yes, exactly! Immortal Venerable, let's stop for now and buy some time. Once we finish our alternative plan and transfer our Spirit Realm assets, we can deal with him however we like."

Right at that moment, Heaven Realm Immortal's thoughts transmitted to all the Immortals.

"The expansion of the black wall is accelerating. Zhang Yu is speeding up."

"30... 20... only 5 seconds left!"

"He's forcing our hand."

---

Inside the Immortal Ascension Hall of the Myriad Laws Sect.

Just as Fa Liuyuan was filled with worry, the Eight Divisions Great Gods seemed to sense something. Their projections descended abruptly, manifesting behind Zhang Yu with a resounding boom and radiating layers of divine power, surrounding him like stars clustering around the moon.

Meanwhile, before Zhang Yu, beams of immortal light pierced through the layers of Kunxu and manifested in front of him, forming a constantly repeating word that almost crammed into every inch of space inside the hall.

"Stop!"
