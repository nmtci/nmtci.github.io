# Chapter 1047: Is This the New World You Spoke Of?

This time, Zhang Yu sent an avatar to descend upon the Heavenly Sword Sect, originally intending to act alone in secret. 

However, after conducting some reconnaissance with the help of the Heavenly Court's power, he discovered that it would be very difficult to carry out an investigation within the Heavenly Sword Sect relying solely on himself. 

Thus, after much deliberation, Zhang Yu still decided to cooperate with Bai Zhenzhen. 

On one hand, it was naturally because A-Zhen, as his companion of many years, was someone he trusted completely. 

On the other hand, it was because A-Zhen currently held a decent standing within the Heavenly Sword Sect, as well as an angle from which to delve into the relevant matters. 

As he pondered, various pieces of information surfaced in Zhang Yu's mind: "Back when the ten Immortal Emperors departed and the Battle of the End began, the entire Heavenly Sword Sect was sealed off..."

Having lived through that period, Bai Zhenzhen understood that it was all because the two most powerful, wealthy, and authoritative Immortal Venerables in the Heavenly Sword Sect had started a conflict. 

These two Immortal Venerables were none other than the Extreme Emotion Sword Immortal and the Heavenly Tribulation Immortal Venerable, who oversaw the Heavenly Tribulation Dao Lineage. 

At that time, Bai Zhenzhen had also been caught in the crossfire of the two Immortal Venerables' clash. The companies under her name collapsed, and in an instant, she went from the wildly popular, highly sought-after Heavenly Sword Goddess and Lower Realm Concept Stock to a reviled fraudster despised by all, arrested to face liquidation.

Things like deviating from her original purpose, impurity of Sect Nature, abandonment of the Immortal Dao Philosophy, irreproducible experiments, and an enormous sum of fraudulent gains... Back then, as Bai Zhenzhen looked over her long list of charges, she had already prepared herself to have her assets wiped clean.

In Zhang Yu's mind, Bai Zhenzhen's later lamentations resurfaced.

"Fortunately, the Extreme Emotion Sword Immortal thought I had remarkable innate talent and wanted me to keep advancing the sword technique grades, treating me as a sword-training machine that worked for free."

"Later on, Yu Zi, you became the Crown Prince of Myriad Laws and stated multiple times that you would keep a close eye on this case, which made the upper layers of the sect — especially Extreme Emotion Sword Immortal — change their minds once more."

"Hehe, they said that not only was I your classmate in the First Layer, but I was also your companion who climbed alongside you from the First Layer all the way to the sect. And since you've always been promoting the ancient style in the Myriad Laws Sect, if we were in ancient times... that would make me equivalent to the Crown Prince's personal confidant from his days in his pre-ascension residence! So, I still had value to be used."

Bai Zhenzhen had sighed, "And so, Extreme Emotion Sword Immortal decided to switch tactics and personally guide my cultivation — in a pleasurable way..."

Zhang Yu understood that in the modern Heavenly Sword Sect, the sword techniques emphasized using Spirit to govern matter, utilizing various emotions to raise cultivation and nurture sword intent.

Yet different emotions also had their own ranks of nobility and baseness.

Negative emotions like pain, resentment, madness, and anger were relatively easy to collect. They were abundant, came with numerous side effects, and had low efficiency, all falling under the category of cheap emotions.

In contrast, emotions like happiness, pride, and arrogance were comparatively much rarer. Their production costs were higher, and so were their prices, but they carried few side effects and offered high cultivation efficiency, making them premium emotions.

Therefore, within the Heavenly Sword Sect, happiness was often an emotion that only the wealthy could possess in large quantities. Likewise, using 'happiness' as one's primary path of cultivation was something only the rich could afford.

Bai Zhenzhen sighed with emotion, "In the past, I always thought cultivation was meant to be dull, dreary, difficult, and even painful."

"Even though I could convert sword intent, I still had to endure the influence of those negative emotions during the conversion process."

"Only later did I realize that cultivation could actually be joyful, happy, and immensely enjoyable. Compared to the miserable grind of converting emotions like before... it is vastly more efficient and far simpler."

Bai Zhenzhen still remembered when Extreme Emotion Sword Immortal gave her guidance on cultivation for the first time, asking her: What brings you the most happiness right now?

Back then, Bai Zhenzhen had knelt on the ground and said, "Being your daughter brings me the greatest happiness."

Extreme Emotion Sword Immortal chuckled. "As expected of a classmate of the Crown Prince of Myriad Laws. You've learned the art of finding a backer quite well."

And so, Bai Zhenzhen was taken in as his adopted daughter on the spot. Her joyful emotions transformed into surging sword intent, rushing straight through the limbs and bones of Bai Zhenzhen's body, allowing her to push the Bliss Sword Dao to her peak limit at the time overnight.

Extreme Emotion Sword Immortal then began his instruction.

"Acknowledging the strong as your parents is indeed simple and effective, but how many times can you do it? How many can you take on? All of it depends on the choices of the strong."

"However, if you take the weak as your children, proactively acting as someone else's mom and then harvesting their happiness, it becomes far simpler."

"This is called emotional exploitation. Rather than generating happiness within yourself, it is far better to create happiness through others and then harvest it..."

"As long as you master emotional exploitation, you won't just be exploiting happiness. Any emotion, once exploited, will be able to bring you happiness..."

"Today, I shall impart to you the supreme sword technique of emotional exploitation, the true Extreme Emotion Sword Dao — my newly developed Sentient Living Beings Sword."

In the days that followed, Bai Zhenzhen practiced cultivating the Dao of Emotional Exploitation under the guidance of Extreme Emotion Sword Immortal.

---

At that moment, Zhang Yu transformed into a current of qi, gathering in Bai Zhenzhen's dantian as he communicated via mental thought: "Emotional Exploitation — how exactly does it work?"

Bai Zhenzhen explained, "Simply put, other people's joy, anger, sorrow... can all be transformed into my power. For instance, I can insult someone to reap their anger and turn it into my sword intent."

"And if I apply my Law Edict on top of that, I can even tax them once more."

Zhang Yu was intrigued, thinking to himself, *"I remember A-Zhen's Law Edict involves levying an energy tax on sword intent conversion, which falls under one of the Heavenly Sword Sect's tax laws."*

*"In other words, after A-Zhen stirs up someone's emotional fluctuations, she first harvests their emotional energy to advance her cultivation, and then, as their emotions fluctuate and their sword intent circulates, she harvests the resulting energy tax burden to reap a wave of Immortal Coins?"*

The Sect Examination Clone marveled in praise, "Damn, so after insulting someone, not only does that person boost her cultivation, they even pay her money? Double-dipping on a single target — isn't that straight-up cheating?"

"Zhang Yu, this is amazing! We should really learn from the Heavenly Sword Sect on this front. Our Myriad Laws Sect should promote this too! We could first copy the Heavenly Sword Sect's cultivation arts, then enact a similar tax law edict. From then on, every single emotion felt by everyone in the sect should be taxed!"

Meanwhile, Zhang Yu had already followed Bai Zhenzhen to the office building of the Heavenly Tribulation Corp.

At this moment, not only had Zhang Yu's various fundamental stats broken past the ceiling of the Ascension Realm and entered the Immortal-grade tier, but many of the cultivation arts he practiced had also broken through the level 80 cap with the support of the Emperor Creation Seed, reaching the Immortal Realm.

This was precisely the case with the *Blood Tide Essence Qi Sacred Contemplation* he was currently circulating to transform into the wind, which vastly enhanced his concealment capabilities.

Beyond that, the blessings from the Spirit Realm Dao Lineage, the deployment of Heavenly Court God-Seizing King-grade talismans, and the support of various Dao Seeds and Chain Formation Diagrams all caused Zhang Yu's concealment abilities to skyrocket.

At this point, Zhang Yu was confident that by possessing Bai Zhenzhen like this, not even the Immortal Class could easily detect his presence.

Sensing the Heavenly Tribulation Corp's office building, Zhang Yu asked, "This shouldn't be the headquarters of the Heavenly Tribulation Corp, right?"

"Of course not, it's just a branch. From the 11th to the 29th layer, every layer has a similar branch handling various heavenly tribulation businesses."

Bai Zhenzhen introduced, "Heavenly Tribulation is a very complex Dao Lineage. Aside from the thunder tribulations of the Tribulation Transcendence Realm and the heavenly tribulations of ascending and becoming an Immortal, there are also Mortal Tribulations, Killing Tribulations, Fire Tribulations, Wind Tribulations, Heart Tribulations... all sorts of varied tribulations."

"In ancient times, Immortal Dao Technology was not sufficiently developed, and cultivators were also relatively superstitious. They viewed tribulations as an ill omen, believed that tribulations were linked to one's moral character, and compiled all kinds of classical experiences to avert them. At its peak... it even formed an entire set of moral standards."

Zhang Yu nodded slightly to himself. He knew from the records of the Heavenly Virtue Immortal Emperor that the Heavenly Tribulation Dao Lineage was originally supposed to punish evil, eradicate wickedness, and promote universal righteousness.

Bai Zhenzhen said, "However, in modern times, after the Heavenly Tribulation Dao Lineage was brought under control, tribulations became a scarce resource. Truth be told, these tribulations are actually good things. Each of them can yield various special Immortal Dao Materials, and nowadays, you can only trigger a tribulation if you pay for it."

"Then, with the flourishing of trading and commerce, a futures market emerged from it as well."

"A whole series of industrial chains also sprang up around protective equipment for undergoing tribulations and tribulation transcendence, as well as relevant cultivation arts for facing them."

Bai Zhenzhen sighed with emotion, "From producing tribulations to crossing them, and then to the tribulation futures market, as well as the processing of tribulation equipment and materials... the entire industrial chain is held firmly in the hands of the Heavenly Tribulation Corp."

Immortal Slayer remarked, *"The Dao Master monopolized the tribulations, artificially creating scarcity and manipulating tribulation prices across the entire supply chain to reap colossal profits."*

Meanwhile, on the light screens outside the building, prices for various tribulations were displayed. The numbers fluctuated constantly, tracing lines across a futures market chart. With every upward and downward tick of those line graphs, the wealth of countless cultivators seemed to be funneled directly into Heavenly Tribulation Corp — lining the pockets of the Heavenly Tribulation Immortal Venerable.

Thinking of how Kunxu's social atmosphere had been corrupted by such a thing, Immortal Slayer felt a wave of irritation.

At the same time, the moment Bai Zhenzhen stepped into Heavenly Tribulation Corp's office building, Zhang Yu felt sword qi soaring into the sky both inside and outside the premises. Countless surges of sword intent erupted upward like startled beasts, glaring intensely in Bai Zhenzhen's direction.

An executive of Heavenly Tribulation Corp appeared before Bai Zhenzhen and said, "Bai Zhenzhen, do not push us too far! You come to audit our taxes every other day. If you disrupt the vital operations of heavenly tribulations, can you bear the responsibility?"

Bai Zhenzhen merely clenched her fist slightly and sighed, "And can you withstand this authority of mine?"

The next moment, two large words flashed across Bai Zhenzhen's fist: Tax Audit.

Countless Spirit Realm sword shadows crashed down, transforming into dense banners that unfurled throughout the entire building.

Wherever the Spirit Realm banners of the tax audit reached, the various sword intents and sword qi that had been brimming with hostility just moments ago vanished immediately, melting away like snow in the sun.

At the same time, Bai Zhenzhen's Law Edicts began to function in tandem. Under the third-generation Law Edict technology rooted in Emotional Sword Intent, every sentient being became an operational node within the Law Edict True Domain. Every stir of emotion fueled the execution of the third-generation Law Edict, transforming into Thought Tides that amplified its power.

"This is truly what they call 'one tax breaks ten thousand swords'," Bai Zhenzhen marvelled inwardly. "Yu Zi, auditing taxes really works so much better than sword techniques."

Even as Bai Zhenzhen spoke, a sweep of sword intent resonated inside and outside her body. The wisps of negative emotions stirred up within and around the building by the tax audit were absorbed by her in massive amounts, transforming into various sword intents within her form.

Bai Zhenzhen continued, "Still, you are the Crown Prince of Myriad Laws. With that Crown Prince Authority, passing your own policies and drafting your own Law Edicts must be even more effective, right?"

Zhang Yu sighed. "A-Zhen, being the Crown Prince actually has its own difficulties. Across the 1st to 19th layers of the Myriad Laws Sect, countless cultivators need to earn money, cultivate, seek stability, and face life and death. I bear the primary responsibility for all of it."

"When what I do aligns with their interests, I am their Crown Prince of Myriad Laws."

"When what I do doesn't align with their demands, I'm nothing more than a royal bastard to them."

"Yet what is truly right? And what is wrong?"

Zhang Yu said faintly, "Back when we were studying, every question had a correct answer. But with the paths laid out before me now, sometimes even I don't know which one is right and which is wrong."

At this moment, in front of Bai Zhenzhen, Zhang Yu revealed a trace of emotion he had never shown since becoming the Crown Prince.

For Zhang Yu understood that as the Crown Prince, he had to maintain complete confidence and resolve in front of all his companions and subordinates.

Sensing Zhang Yu's thoughts, Bai Zhenzhen seemed to feel the pressure in his heart as well.

Bai Zhenzhen said, "Yu Zi, no matter which path you choose from now on, I will walk it with you to the very end."

Right then, the leader from Heavenly Tribulation Corp spoke up: "Bai Zhenzhen, I've already reported the situation here. Just wait to apologize to the company's senior management."

"Apologize?" Bai Zhenzhen sighed. "I'm afraid your superiors won't get the chance to hear it."

Hearing this, the other party was startled, countless speculations flashing through his mind: *"Could something have happened up top?!"*

Bai Zhenzhen continued, "Because I never had any intention of apologizing in the first place."

*"You motherf..."* Emotions surged within the man, quickly giving rise to wisps of negative feelings that were harvested by Bai Zhenzhen and converted into a trace of her cultivation.

Under the effect of the Law Edict, a portion of tax was deducted directly from his account as well.

Sensing all this, the Heavenly Tribulation Corp leader immediately circulated his cultivation art to calm his emotions, while firmly resolving in his heart, *"I can't talk to her anymore. This Bai Zhenzhen's sword technique has reached perfection. Every single sentence I exchange with her gets exploited. If I keep talking to her, she'll slice me into poverty sooner or later."*

Watching him hurry away, Bai Zhenzhen did not pursue him. She simply walked through the office area, looking into Heavenly Tribulation Corp's tax affairs along the way.

Bai Zhenzhen asked, "Yu Zi, if you want to investigate the heavenly tribulations, you'll probably have to start with their technical data."

Zhang Yu replied, "See if there are any technical projects you can access through the taxation records."

Nodding slightly in her heart, Bai Zhenzhen said, "Mm. Lately, both Immortal Venerable Extreme Emotion and Immortal Venerable Heavenly Tribulation have entered secluded cultivation. It's the perfect time for us to make our move."

Zhang Yu's interest was piqued, and he asked, "Do you know why they went into seclusion?"

Bai Zhenzhen said, "Supposedly, they are using the majority of the Immortal Dao resources to conduct some kind of technical research and breakthrough."

Just as Zhang Yu and Bai Zhenzhen were taking action, an anomaly occurred within Heavenly Tribulation Corp's building.

Bai Zhenzhen also received the news shortly after. Looking at the message's contents, she said, "Yu Zi, a nightmare phenomenon has occurred here."

Curious, Zhang Yu asked, "A nightmare?"

Bai Zhenzhen explained, "It's an anomalous phenomenon that has been happening within the Heavenly Sword Sect over the past month or two. Some cultivators will suddenly enter a sleepwalking-like state, and according to what they say after waking up, they were trapped in a nightmare at the time. Currently, more than a thousand people have experienced these nightmares. Although no one remembers the specific details of the nightmare, everyone who has gone through it shares the same consensus... that the nightmare was extremely terrifying and left them thoroughly shaken."

At the same time, a cultivator inside the building had already entered a state resembling sleepwalking. The cultivator was pacing back and forth, a terrified expression surfacing on their face from time to time, muttering, "Fake, it's all fake."

By the time Bai Zhenzhen arrived at the scene, several cultivators from the Heavenly Sword Sect had already gathered around.

This was the first time Bai Zhenzhen had witnessed this sleepwalking-like phenomenon with her own eyes. Of course, she knew that although it was called sleepwalking, Kunxu cultivators had long ceased sleeping; there had to be another reason for this sleepwalker-like state.

As she sensed the sleepwalking cultivator's condition, Bai Zhenzhen immediately realized the gravity of the situation and exclaimed in surprise, "I can't sense his emotional fluctuations, so I can't levy taxes on him. How can this be? This is severe tax evasion!"

Right then, the man fell to his knees with a thud, clutching his head and crying out in agony, "I can't tell! I can't tell them apart!"

Zhang Yu thought to himself, *"Is he roleplaying Dao of the Bizarre Immortal or something?"*

Sensing the man's state, Zhang Yu said thoughtfully, "A-Zhen, in situations like this, can he usually wake up?"

Bai Zhenzhen replied, "As far as I know, yes. Currently, none of the cultivators who have experienced these nightmares have suffered any real harm; they just feel scared and terrified even after waking up. It's just that the number of people experiencing these nightmares has been growing, as if the phenomenon is continuously spreading."

Zhang Yu said, "Once he wakes up, I want to talk with him."

Thus, after the man ended his sleepwalking and regained consciousness, Bai Zhenzhen used a tax investigation as an excuse to take him to an office for questioning ahead of everyone else.

Bai Zhenzhen asked the question Zhang Yu wanted answered: "How much do you still remember about what happened in the dream?"

The person replied, "It was terrifying, that place was simply terrifying..."

"I can't remember the details. All I remember is... in the dream, I became dirt-poor, surrounded entirely by lunatics, and I couldn't even use Immortal Dao Technology!"

Bai Zhenzhen thought to herself, *"Unable to use Immortal Dao Technology? And reduced to being dirt-poor? That truly is a nightmare."*

Under Bai Zhenzhen's questioning, the person kept repeating, "That place was a living hell! You couldn't find a worse place in all of Kunxu; it's a hellish environment that could never exist in the physical world!"

Bai Zhenzhen continued her interrogation, but the person merely repeated the same few phrases back and forth. They knew none of the specifics of the nightmare, retaining only vague sensations and emotions.

After concluding the questioning, Bai Zhenzhen drafted a tax report to fulfill her duty. But in the next moment, a projection descended — it was none other than the mysterious figure who had originally taught her the Bliss Sword Dao.

The mysterious figure glanced over Bai Zhenzhen's report and said, "You don't need to concern yourself with this matter any further."

Surprised, Bai Zhenzhen said, "Not concern myself with it? But this is tax evasion! Once they enter that nightmare state, I won't be able to collect taxes from them at all."

The mysterious figure said casually, "Of course you can't collect it, because at that moment... their minds are no longer in Kunxu."

Hearing this, Bai Zhenzhen was slightly taken aback. "Not in Kunxu? Then where are they?"

The mysterious figure chuckled and said, "Actually, even if I don't tell you, you'll probably hear about it in a few days anyway. It's another world — a new world."

Bai Zhenzhen asked in shock, "A new world? You mean... wait, you're messing with me, aren't you?"

The mysterious figure burst into laughter. "Not bad, not bad. It's not a bad thing for you to think that way. Of course, if you believe me, I can tell you more. It's a new world beyond Kunxu, an unprecedented discovery. Currently, the higher-ups are trying to explore this world, hoping it can serve as a refuge — a place to flee to if the Ultimate Doomsday cannot be stopped."

Finally, the mysterious figure cautioned, "Because you just came into contact with that cultivator who experienced the nightmare, it might spread to you. If you suddenly find yourself in that new world, it means you've entered the nightmare state. Remember, if you end up in that new world, don't panic. Try your best... to find a way to bring information back."

In a daze, Bai Zhenzhen ended the conversation with the mysterious person, her heart still thoroughly shaken. *"Yu Zi, did you hear that? A new world outside Kunxu! Is there really another world beyond Kunxu?"*

She said with some excitement, *"He didn't even care about tax evasion anymore, so he probably wasn't trying to trick me, right?"*

*"A new world... If we really can hide out over there, wouldn't we be able to escape the Battle of the End?"*

*"No wonder, no wonder I felt that nobody in the sect seemed enthusiastic about resisting The End."*

*"The entire upper echelon of the Heavenly Sword Sect must be escapees, right? They're planning to flee to another world to avoid the End, aren't they?"*

*"Now that they're barely hiding it anymore, does it mean they're close to succeeding? Are they planning to sell tickets to other sects? Are they going to shock all of Kunxu?"*

Lost in thought, Bai Zhenzhen took one step after another toward the office door. But in the next instant, as the office door swung open, the entire world shifted around her, and she felt as if she had already appeared in another world.

At the same time, Zhang Yu stood right beside her, gazing at this world in astonishment.

Bai Zhenzhen said in pleasant surprise, "Yu Zi, is this the new world?"

Looking at the world before him, Zhang Yu muttered, "Becoming paupers, everyone insane, devoid of Immortal Dao, a true hell, a harsh environment that would never exist in the physical world..."

"The place they're talking about... is Earth?"
