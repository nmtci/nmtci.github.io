# Chapter 995 Self-Developed Chain Formation Diagrams, Obtaining a New Book

Upon realizing that the master-selection simulator for the new book before him was actually meant to create new Chain Formation Diagrams one after another, a trace of astonishment welled up in Zhang Yu's heart.

Ever since unlocking Chain Formation Diagrams in the Feather Book, Zhang Yu had obtained numerous Chain Formation Diagram Sets. However, creating a brand-new Chain Formation Diagram Set was something he had never encountered before.

"According to the intelligence I've gathered from the Heavenly Surveillance Hall recently, some Book bearers can master abilities related to Chain Formation Diagrams, but before this, none seemed to have encountered a Book that can create Chain Formation Diagrams."

"In the understanding and conjectures of relevant cultivators, Chain Formation Diagrams should be an Immortal Dao phenomenon that relies on an unknown Dao Lineage, objectively exists within the Kunxu World, and can currently only be observed and invoked by a Book."

"As for creating Chain Formation Diagrams... there are no relevant records yet."

Thinking up to this point, Zhang Yu was already filled with anticipation for this unknown Book.

"If I can create Chain Formation Diagrams... then there are simply too many ways to utilize it. I can forge Chain Formation Diagrams that are better suited to me across different domains. I could even forge exclusive Chain Formation Diagrams for each of them, like A-Zhen and Yu Xinghan."

Thoughts flashed through his mind like lightning. With just a rough estimate, Zhang Yu could already imagine that a Chain Formation Diagram capable of synergizing with ones own Dao Seed, cultivation arts, and job would bring unimaginable enhancements.

If applied to work, the wearer's work efficiency would multiply by who knew how many times, and the resulting increase in Salary Ability would be utterly terrifying.

Originally, although Zhang Yu had come to participate in this New Book-Master Selection, he had not harbored a must-win determination; it had mostly been an intention to gather intelligence.

Yet at this moment, a fierce resolve welled up inside Zhang Yu to seize this book no matter what.

At the same time, he also began trying to select more cultivation arts from the simulator's cultivation art pool to combine, creating one Chain Formation Diagram after another.

"I need to select a combination of cultivation arts that adapts to security work and find the strongest Chain Formation Diagram."

The sets of Chain Formation Diagrams he possessed and the cultivation arts they comprised surfaced in Zhang Yu's mind.

"The cultivation arts that make up a Chain Formation Diagram generally share certain affinities, complement one another, or are even derived from splitting a single cultivation art."

"I need to analyze the characteristics of security work, then assemble a combination of cultivation arts that is well-suited for security work, shares a cohesive style, and can complement one another..."

In an instant, as Zhang Yu ran calculations in his mind, numerous combinations of cultivation arts surfaced in his thoughts.

"Actually, reaching this step is something many cultivators here can probably do. The key lies in how to verify which combination is better."

Zhang Yu understood that the next step would likely be his greatest advantage at the moment.

He glanced at the simulation records in the venue that were constantly iterating, being tested, and being projected, thinking to himself, "Other cultivators probably need to run the simulation before they can know the results."

"Whereas I only need to select the cultivation arts, see the newly generated Chain Formation Diagram's name, and then I can guess its approximate strength based on that name..."

With ten cultivation arts selected, Zhang Yu saw the name of the newly formed Chain Formation Diagram: Security Newbie.

Next, as Zhang Yu continued to pick and choose, the name of this newly formed Chain Formation Diagram constantly changed — Security Beggar, Full Attendance Security Guard, Salary Thief, Delivery Killer...

"Hmm, I'll make a note of this Full Attendance Security Guard combination for now and list it as a candidate."

As Zhang Yu continued trying, he took note of the Chain Formation Diagram combinations whose names seemed decent.

Because there were simply too many cultivation arts in the cultivation art pool, Zhang Yu still had not found a satisfactory combination even after spending over an hour selecting, combining, and discerning names.

"Still nothing sufficiently powerful, the kind of Chain Formation Diagram name where you can tell at a glance that it's utterly dominant in the field of security..."

Just then, Zhang Yu's eyes suddenly lit up. "Security Sacred Body? This is it!"

Without hesitation, Zhang Yu immediately started the simulation process.

At the same time, he thought to himself, "The vast majority of cultivators here probably can't see the information on Chain Formation Diagrams. But Source Saint, Bai Gusheng... as Book-Wielders of the Immortal Armaments Bureau and the White Bone Cult, they definitely know of the existence of Chain Formation Diagrams."

Zhang Yu wasn't sure if Source Saint and Bai Gusheng could directly see the names of the Chain Formation Diagrams like he could, or if they had already detected the mechanism for creating Chain Formation Diagrams within this Simulator.

However, Zhang Yu did not dare underestimate them in the slightest. "As Book-Wielders of the Ten Sects, they must possess many Books with various different abilities, and Bai Gusheng has even mastered the Ascension Book."

"With them as my opponents, I should assume... they might be able to see the Chain Formation Diagram names just like me. At this very moment, they might be using their own methods to find the most suitable Chain Formation Diagram combination."

Thus, Zhang Yu made up his mind: as soon as he found a Chain Formation Diagram combination he deemed powerful enough, he would immediately initiate the simulation to be recorded as the first original creator and seize the top score.

That way, even if others tried this combination later, they would forever remain beneath him.

With the start of the simulation, Zhang Yu's consciousness seemed to gradually witness the entire life of a security guard blessed by the Security Sacred Body.

Under this perception, it seemed that the Feather Book had once again come into play, allowing Zhang Yu to truly see the enhancement effects that this Security Sacred Body bestowed upon security personnel.

"The first effect compresses one's own survival, cultivation, and maintenance costs to the absolute limit. It is truly plug-and-play — even if they simply plug themselves into the spiritual energy circuit of some random office equipment, they can maintain operation."

"Second effect: when facing property owners or company employees, offensive capabilities such as strength, size, destructive power, and mental pressure surge across the board."

Zhang Yu thought to himself, "Third effect: when facing criminals, evasion, speed, acuity, sensory perception, survivability... all skyrocket across the board."

Year 1: You firmly kept the security code of conduct in mind, striking hard against employees and fiercely cracking down on all non-compliant behavior.

Year 3: Your security intimidation grew even more oppressive; outsourced workers would drop straight to their knees in terror under a single glare from you.

Year 10: Under your strict enforcement, the company's average profit increased by 0.3%, while the average lifespan of employees decreased by 5%. Your formidable reputation began echoing throughout the entire industrial park.

Year 20: You became an expert in security training. Multiple companies invited you to impart your security expertise and explain how you maintained the office environment.

Year 150: You firmly kept the security code of conduct in mind. Facing legal robberies, you still provided smiling service, minimizing the company's losses to the lowest possible level.

Year 400: At an age when you should have met your demise and begun a second life, you had spare funds for longevity extension — all because your earnings had long been among the highest in the security industry. Your security training had become a mandatory course for countless security personnel; you were a legend in the security world.

Year 600: You exhausted all your lifespan and funds, reaching the absolute end of longevity extension. Amid the cheers from countless office buildings, you marched toward your death.

Score: 633 points

The next moment, the rankings in the sky instantly shifted, thrusting Zhang Yu's name and record straight to the first place.

Looking at this score, Bai Gusheng's expression changed slightly: "633 points?"

He immediately examined the specific process and combination of Zhang Yu's record, and the astonishment in his heart grew even deeper.

"He actually lived for over 600 years? And..."

What surprised Bai Gusheng the most was not just Zhang Yu's score, but the combination of cultivation arts he had chosen.

Bai Gusheng had already practiced all these cultivation arts to level 1 through his clones, but he had not seen what kind of synergy these cultivation arts could form when put together.

A flash of insight instantly crossed Bai Gusheng's mind: *"There's some kind of bonus effect that I couldn't perceive, taking effect in the simulator."*

Bai Gusheng, who had originally been completely confident and certain that he could make a move at the last moment to seize first place, found his confidence wavering at this moment.

Next, relying on his matchlessly powerful Ascension Realm computing power combined with the abundant resources behind him, he rapidly compared the records generated by Zhang Yu's simulator run, analyzing the gap between these simulator records and the real-world data of the cultivation arts.

Previously, because Bai Gusheng had only cultivated each cultivation art to level 1, his understanding of the arts was not profound.

However, he was ultimately an Ascension Realm cultivator who possessed vast resources. Under the stimulation provided by Zhang Yu at this moment, he quickly discovered the anomaly within.

*"The performance of the cultivation arts in the simulator is stronger than their performance in reality. It's not just Zhang Yu — all the records that haven't formed a Chain Formation Diagram... exhibit this exact behavior. Could it be..."*

A speculation surged in Bai Gusheng's heart. At this point, he didn't dare wait any longer and immediately took out the optimal Chain Formation Diagram combination he had found, applying it to the simulator.

In an instant, a new security guard career began.

The Chain Formation Diagram combination Bai Gusheng had found was called Spirit Realm Replication, which was a Chain Formation Diagram capable of quickly replicating Spirit Realm Techniques.

Under the enhancement of Spirit Realm Replication, the security personnel in the simulator soon displayed their skill in replicating the Spirit Realm and were then reassigned as a Spirit Realm technician.

"Year 655: Having plagiarized countless Spirit Realm Techniques for the company, you were invaded during a Spirit Realm offensive and defensive battle. Your bionic was damaged, your body was ruined by rampant magic power, and you were ultimately fired because you were unable to continue working..."

"Year 656: Having remained in Spirit Realm technical positions for many years, you spent countless Immortal Coins preparing for Spirit Realm offensive and defensive engagements. As a result, your savings were nearly depleted, and you declared bankruptcy shortly after losing your job..."

"Score: 688 points."

Bai Gusheng thought to himself, "Because of my proficiency in replicating Spirit Realm techniques, I managed to transfer to a new position. But after getting drawn into Spirit Realm offense and defense, I had to constantly consume vast amounts of Immortal Coins..."

Bai Gusheng understood very well that when cultivators continually participate in Spirit Realm offensive and defensive battles, they must constantly spend Immortal Coins on computing power, Spirit Realm services, learning talismans, and mastering the latest techniques for combat readiness.

"Spirit Realm technicians may earn a lot, but their expenses are just as high."

"Even with the Chain Formation Diagram for Spirit Realm Replication, the escalating costs eventually become too much to bear."

Meanwhile, Bai Gusheng's simulation result had already broken the record, pushing Zhang Yu to second place and successfully taking the top spot.

Yet Bai Gusheng felt not the slightest bit of joy; instead, a certain suspicion was gradually solidifying in his mind.

"Under the enhancement of Spirit Realm Replication, the security personnel in the Simulator exhibited performance in their cultivation arts that far exceeded the norm. This extraordinary performance is very similar to the records of Zhang Yu and the others — those simulation records where I couldn't see any Chain Formation Diagrams."

Simulations where he could see Chain Formation Diagrams and those where he couldn't... both showed similar signs of cultivation art enhancement. What did that mean?

Bai Gusheng speculated internally, "There are Chain Formation Diagrams that I cannot see, and it is very likely that these diagrams were newly created within the Simulator."

"Could the ability of this new book... be related to creating Chain Formation Diagrams?"

The moment this possibility crossed his mind, Bai Gusheng became determined to obtain this new book at all costs.

At the same time, a second guess surfaced in his mind: "I can't see the newly created Chain Formation Diagrams, but can Zhang Yu?"

"Did he achieve that result in the simulation because he could see them, or was it merely a coincidence?"

Bai Gusheng could not be certain, which led to a new thought emerging in his mind: "Regardless, if I want to increase my win rate without being able to see information about the new Chain Formation Diagrams... I need more opportunities for trial and error."

The figure of Source Saint immediately surfaced in Bai Gusheng's mind.

*"That guy probably doesn't know this intel yet. I can find an excuse to try to trade with him, draft a contract, and purchase the usage rights to a batch of cultivators from him."*

While pondering, Bai Gusheng had already unblocked Source Saint from his contacts and sent him a message.

Seeing the message from Bai Gusheng, Source Saint thought to himself, *"This new book... Has he already realized what its ability is? And found it very useful? So he's determined to get it?"*

Glancing at the rankings, where Bai Gusheng had already taken first place, Source Saint thought, *"Hmph, he and Zhang Yu got high scores right away without much trial and error, which means they can see more Chain Formation Diagram information than I can."*

As he pondered, Source Saint had a sudden thought and sent a message to Zhang Yu, only to find that he had already been blocked.

At the same time, he laid out his terms to Bai Gusheng: "I want the Sect Diary in your hands."

Source Saint knew that there were many different types and functions of Books in this world, and precisely because of their varied abilities, combining certain Books often yielded miraculous synergy.

Seeing that Bai Gusheng was determined to win at all costs, Source Saint analyzed the situation: "It's highly likely that Bai Gusheng has already discerned the ability of this new Book and believes that combining it with the Book in his hands will produce some kind of miraculous effect."

Likewise, Source Saint also wanted the *Sect Diary* in Bai Gusheng's possession. That Book had originally belonged to a cultivator of the Immortal Armaments Bureau before being seized by the White Bone Cult.

Source Saint knew that combining the ability of this *Sect Diary* with another Book he possessed would unlock another wonderful use, which would aid his subsequent Book-collecting plans.

*"If Bai Gusheng can give me the Sect Diary, then in exchange, it wouldn't be impossible for me to let him have this new Book."*

---

Meanwhile, just as Source Saint and Bai Gusheng were communicating, Zhang Yu had already begun the next iteration.

Looking at Bai Gusheng's first-place simulation record, Zhang Yu thought to himself, "Was he drawn out by my record?"

At this moment, Zhang Yu felt secretly fortunate: "Good thing I didn't save this combination for the very end, or I wouldn't have even had a chance to turn the tables."

Zhang Yu could already imagine that if he had held back the Security Sacred Body until the final moments, Bai Gusheng's release of that 688-point combination would have handed him an immediate defeat, leaving him no time to make further attempts.

"Since it allows the creation of new Chain Formation Diagrams, this New Book-Master Selection is likely designed to make people keep experimenting and iterating, ultimately choosing the best of the best to find the person with the highest score."

Next, based on the results of his first simulation, Zhang Yu tried fine-tuning the Security Sacred Body, constantly swapping out certain cultivation arts within it to achieve a better combination.

Security Grandmaster, Security Legend, Security Emperor...

Upon seeing the name "Security Emperor", Zhang Yu's eyes flickered, and he prepared to test this combination.

However, in the next moment, he looked over at Liu Qianxian beside him and said, "Profound Monarch Liu, I'll give you a combination. Why don't you give it a try?"

Just moments ago, when Zhang Yu had briefly taken first place, Liu Qianxian was already astonished.

"This New Book-Master Selection is probably testing the Chain Formation Diagrams, isn't it? Does Zhang Yu really have such a profound understanding of it?"

Liu Qianxian's original strategy had been to wait for the other cultivators to go through a few more iterations before making her move. But upon hearing Zhang Yu's command now, she immediately nodded and said, "Alright, tell me what to do."

A moment later, Liu Qianxian had already launched the simulation for [Security Emperor].

In an instant, as the simulation concluded, Zhang Yu focused his senses on the projected results.

"Going to work is just to clock out! The King of Security is governed by no man!"

"In the domain of 500 Immortal Coins, I am the true ace. Even a Rank 10 Sect Staff wouldn't dare compete with me."

"If I'm not happy, I'll switch jobs. If I can't find work, I'll outsource my work. If all else fails, I'll just go back to the Lower Realm!"

"Kunxu's 1st layer is no worse than the 11th layer!"

Sensing the contents of the simulator, Zhang Yu's heart stirred. "Is this... weakening the influence of the Immortal Dao Aspiration?"

The security guard in the simulator lived a happy life, but also met his demise at the age of over 400.

Looking at the final score of just over 400 points, Zhang Yu knew that this iteration had failed.

Over the next two attempts, all three of Liu Qianxian's chances were completely exhausted, yet not a single score surpassed that of the Security Sacred Body.

Zhang Yu felt that this current version of the Chain Formation Diagram had already iterated to its absolute limit.

"Fine-tuning the cultivation art back and forth any further probably won't yield any breakthrough progress."

Frowning slightly, Zhang Yu thought to himself, "Perhaps I need to switch directions."

At the same time, the top spot on the rankings changed once again.

Zhang Yu glanced over and thought, "Did a cultivator copy Bai Gusheng's record and fine-tune it? It seems they added Spirit Realm innate talent, and after becoming a Spirit Realm technician... they lived a few years longer."

Looking at this record, a thought welled up in Zhang Yu's mind: *"I might have already hit the ceiling for security guards. If I want to take this a step further... I have to stop being a security guard."*

On the other side, Bai Gusheng directed the cultivators loaned to him by Source Saint, iterating the Chain Formation Diagram step by step until he finally pushed the lifespan in the simulator to a full 900 years.

According to his agreement and contract with the Source Saint, if the cultivators temporarily lent to him eventually obtained books, they would also hand them over to him. In return, when the time came, Bai Gusheng would have to hand over the *Sect Diary* to the Source Saint.

*"This is just about the limit."*

Just as Bai Gusheng felt that first place was well within his grasp, the top spot on the rankings changed once again.

"Huh? Zhang Yu... 1,000 points?"

Slightly taken aback, Bai Gusheng immediately sensed the contents of the record.

"Year 1: Your performance was abysmal, and you were heavily in debt, yet you still insisted on studying all day long..."

"Year 3: Your performance teetered on the brink of getting you fired, and your loans were maxed out, leaving you on the verge of bankruptcy at any moment..."

Looking at the events of the first few years, Bai Gusheng was astonished. "With such a terrible start, how could he get such a high score later on?" But the next moment, Bai Gusheng understood why.

"Year 5: Insolvent and having borrowed from every micro-loan platform available, just before going bankrupt, you finally passed the Sect Examination and became a glorious Rank 1 Sect Staff member!"

Bai Gusheng was shocked once more. "A security guard could actually pass the exam to become Sect Staff?!"

From a security guard to Sect Staff, in Bai Gusheng's eyes, this was nothing short of defying the heavens to change one's fate.

And the subsequent simulation records proved this point. After becoming Sect Staff... life seemed to become smooth sailing. Various cultivation arts could be copied at will in the Immortal Armaments Bureau, and after just a few years, he broke through to Nascent Soul, living for over a thousand years in one go.

*"Copy him!"*

Bai Gusheng and Source Saint immediately copied Zhang Yu's cultivation art combination and tried fine-tuning and iterating on it, only to find that they could never surpass his score.

Watching the numerous cultivators' attempts, Zhang Yu silently recalled the information about the Chain Formation Diagram in the simulator.

"Sect Examination Scholar... grants an absolute bonus in the Sect Examination. And for cultivators without backing, the score achieved and the position attained in the Sect Examination basically determine the ceiling."

"No matter how they fine-tune and iterate, how could they possibly surpass the top scholar?"

The subsequent attempts by the many cultivators also seemed to prove Zhang Yu's thought.

Right up until the countdown ended, Zhang Yu remained firmly in first place.

When the countdown finished, everything related to the New Book-Master Selection vanished without a trace, like a fleeting dream.

Bearing a fierce, imposing pressure, the projections of Source Saint and Bai Gusheng descended toward Zhang Yu immediately.

"Congratulations, Profound Monarch Zhang!"

"Brother Zhang, wait!"

In the next moment, Bai Gusheng watched as Zhang Yu gave no response at all; after taking hold of the new Book that had suddenly materialized in his hand, his entire person stepped into the Immortal Grotto-Heaven and vanished from sight.
