# Chapter 1005: Great Sage Chapter

Listening to Zhang Yu’s speculation, Desolate Ox, who had not spoken for a long time, finally said, “Indeed... I’ve always had a sense that something was out of place. The history related to the Monster Saint has always felt somehow at odds with Kunxu as it is now.”

“If the world underwent a rollback, then that would explain it.”

Desolate Ox said gravely, “Much of the history related to the Monster Saint was rolled back. As a result, the lost history that managed to survive has all kinds of contradictions with Kunxu’s true history.”

Immortal Slayer asked in puzzlement, “But if a rollback has already happened, who recorded these lost histories?”

The Sect Examination Clone gave a cold laugh, looking at the Feather Book that had appeared before them. “Isn’t it right here before us? If we can use the books to send messages, then it stands to reason that others could have done the same.”

“Throughout Kunxu’s long history, perhaps one generation after another of the books’ holders secretly recorded those lost histories in the books — the experiences that should have vanished from this world along with the rollback...”

“Some of this may have been completely lost for various reasons, while some has outlasted the rollback period and gradually been passed down.”

Hearing this, Zhang Yu nodded slightly in agreement, then sighed. “If our guess is right, that means the Supreme Immortal Artifact capable of rolling back the world came into being much earlier than we imagined.”

Immortal Slayer said, “Indeed. Just look at the eras these Monster Saints lived through — they were clearly far earlier than the era now ruled by the Ten Immortal Emperors.”

“Then there are only two possibilities. Either the Ten Immortal Emperors have existed for far longer than we originally thought…”

“Or someone wielded the power of World Rollback before the Ten Immortal Emperors, repeatedly stopping the Monster Saints from changing the world. If so, we have no idea who originally created this Supreme Immortal Artifact, or how it eventually ended up in the hands of the Ten Immortal Emperors.”

As he looked at line after line of text recounting lost history, a thought suddenly occurred to Zhang Yu. “I wonder if the Dao Lineages founded by these Monster Saints still exist?”

“If the Dao Lineages they founded still exist, and they may have been born before the Ten Immortal Emperors of today, then perhaps even the current Ten Immortal Emperors... have yet to master the Dao Lineages created by these Monster Saints.”

As he pondered this, Zhang Yu began to comprehend the functions revealed by the Great Sage Book after it was merged into the Feather Book.

He saw that another chapter had appeared in the Feather Book, titled Great Sage Chapter.

Zhang Yu thought to himself, “According to the Immortal Ancestor’s message, the book is used to record and connect Dao Lineages across the ages. Could this Great Sage Chapter be specifically for recording and connecting the Dao Lineages of the Monster Saints?”

Immortal Slayer said, “You want to parasitize these Monster Saints’ Dao Lineages?”

Zhang Yu nodded slightly and said, “The Dao-Seeking Progress for the World-Domain Dao Lineage is already at 100. With the remaining Great Dao Essence, plus the books I collect in the future... I can definitely parasitize a second Dao Lineage.”

“And the only Dao Lineages that might be as safe as the World-Domain Dao Lineage are perhaps the unclaimed ones left behind by these Monster Saints.”

“But it will still take more information about these Demon Sage Dao Lineages, as well as related Immortal Dao Technology, for that to be possible...”

“Right now, collecting books is a perfect opportunity to take care of this as well.”

As Zhang Yu continued his study, he gradually figured out what the Great Sage Chapter could do.

“According to the Great Sage Chapter, it’s about creating your own Mental World and constantly copying your own thoughts to form Mental Incarnations...”

“But based on everything I’ve learned about the Spirit Realm over the years, the so-called Mental World is the Spirit Realm, and the so-called Mental Incarnations are Spirit Realm Incarnations.”

Zhang Yu thought to himself, “That makes sense. The so-called Spirit Realm was originally a Mental World jointly created by the Ten Great Sects. The Mental World this Great Sage Chapter aims to create is actually a prototype of the Spirit Realm — a miniature Spirit Realm.”

In Zhang Yu’s view, the Great Sage Chapter could create a Spirit Realm node centered on himself.

By using this node to connect to the Spirit Realm, he could easily disguise his identity and origins, and use the Spirit Realm Incarnations within it as his alternate accounts.

“Externally, it can provide me with a force of Spirit Realm troll armies that obey my every command.”

“Internally, it can create a minor Spirit Realm that belongs entirely to me and is under my control.”

With that thought, Zhang Yu had already decided to integrate the Dark Spirit Realm Fu Ji had built, then use this minor Spirit Realm to gradually cover his Immortal Grotto-Heaven.

The Sect Examination Clone sighed, “We now have independent space, layering, and even an independent Spirit Realm. This Grotto-Heaven of ours is looking more and more like a miniature Kunxu. This is the foundation for reaching the pinnacle!”

Zhang Yu sensed the independent Spirit Realm gradually spreading over his Immortal Grotto-Heaven and thought to himself, “The Great Sage Chapter can now generate three to five Spirit Realm Incarnations for me each day. And as the Independent Spirit Realm operates, its range can expand day by day, eventually covering the entire Immortal Grotto-Heaven.”

“If I get a chance to upgrade it, perhaps it could have another use, much like how the Immortal Foundation Chapter advances my Dao-Seeking Progress by devouring books.”

While Zhang Yu’s true body was comprehending his newly acquired Book, his many clones were handling various affairs for him.

For example, Zhang Yu’s inbox was receiving more and more mails by the minute. New cultivation art copyrights were constantly being granted to him for free.

A sliver of Zhang Yu’s consciousness skimmed through the cultivation arts being offered for free. He thought, “These are all low-value cultivation arts. They want to be selected for the Chain Formation Diagram I create, so they can expand their market.”

“They have a point. Cultivation arts that are too rare and valuable would be beyond the means of most cultivators, even if they were incorporated into the Chain Formation Diagram.”

At the same time, Zhang Yu received a message from Gao Chongguang.

Gao Chongguang: “Zhang Yu, a lot of people have recently expressed interest in investing in real estate in the Immortal Grotto-Heaven. How should we choose?”

Zhang Yu already had a plan in mind. He replied, “Get ready to sell land. Sell them 100-year usage rights to a few plots I’ve marked out. But don’t just sell to whoever offers the most money. We also need to consider their construction plans for the land.”

“Their plans must be compatible with the overall transportation hub, and the specific plans must also be written into the contract…”

Zhang Yu understood the upper-tier cultivators of Kunxu all too well. They would always go for whatever was most profitable and easiest.

If left unchecked, these upper-tier cultivators would likely speculate wildly on plots of land in the Immortal Grotto-Heavens, then keep raising the prices as they sold them to newcomers, ultimately leaving behind a huge mess.

Gao Chongguang replied, “Don’t worry. I understand these things.”

He sighed. “Over the past two years, I’ve visited quite a few Grotto-Heavens in the sect and seen far too many unfinished projects abandoned by people who took the money and ran. So much vast land that had been made available to the public has simply gone to waste.”

“I absolutely won’t let that happen here.”

As Zhang Yu was talking with Gao Chongguang, he suddenly received another communication request, this time from Dugu Ming.

The other party was the Hall Master of the Heavenly Surveillance Hall, one of the senior Ascension Realm cultivators. Zhang Yu naturally wouldn’t slight him, and immediately split off a sliver of his consciousness to communicate with him.

Looking at Zhang Yu’s projection before him, Dugu Ming felt both envy and jealousy. “That kid… He actually managed to be entrusted with such an important task by the Immortal Emperor.”

As the Hall Master of the Heavenly Surveillance Hall, Dugu Ming controlled a great deal of the sect’s intelligence and naturally knew all about Zhang Yu’s recent conduct.

Now, facing Zhang Yu, he couldn’t help but remind him, “Minister Zhang, the Immortal Emperor entrusted you with such an important responsibility. He didn’t do so to let you abuse his authority.”

“There are many things that we, as his subordinates, should figure out how to resolve ourselves, instead of always thinking about invoking the Immortal Emperor’s name.”

Zhang Yu said, “Is that what the Immortal Emperor told you? He certainly didn’t say that to me.”

Dugu Ming snorted. “How could the Immortal Emperor possibly tell you something like that? As his ministers, we should understand this for ourselves and be considerate of His Majesty…”

The next moment, Zhang Yu’s mind stirred.

“Door! Open!”

Boom! As the door to the 33rd layer swung open with a thunderous crash, the force sent Dugu Ming dropping to his knees.

Dugu Ming said, “Your subordinate welcomes the Immortal Emperor…”

His excitement surged, and he thought to himself, “The Immortal Emperor came in person to see me?!”

But the next moment, Zhang Yu’s projection said, “The Immortal Emperor isn’t here. I opened the door.”

Dugu Ming was shaken and was about to retort when he saw Zhang Yu’s projection bring his hands together. The door leading to the 33rd layer vanished abruptly.

Then Zhang Yu clapped his hands again, and the gate appeared before Dugu Ming once more.

Zhang Yu said, “The last time I met with the Immortal Emperor, he gave me the Palace Gate Restriction and allowed me to meet him whenever I wish.”

Dugu Ming watched this, his heart jolting. *“Could it be that earlier… the Immortal Emperor didn’t temporarily open the gate to the 33rd layer for Zhang Yu? Zhang Yu can open it whenever he wants?”*

*“I’ve served the Immortal Emperor for so many years, yet I’ve never been to the 33rd layer even once. And this kid has already gotten the keys to the Immortal Emperor’s home? He can even come and go as he pleases?”*

Watching the gate appear and disappear at Zhang Yu’s command, Dugu Ming couldn’t help but say, “How dare you! Is this something you can just play around with? Stop it!”

Zhang Yu replied casually, “Isn’t a gate meant to be opened and closed? And if we’re attacked, I might even use it to block the assault…”

Dugu Ming glared at Zhang Yu, but when he thought of the gate he’d yearned for, now being toyed with so wantonly by Zhang Yu, he fell silent again.

Zhang Yu continued, “Hall Master Dugu, if you disagree, why not come up with me and ask the Immortal Emperor?”

“You don’t mean you lack the authority, do you?”

Dugu Ming felt another pang of heartbreak and thought to himself, *“Damn it... My ability to network upward can only pierce through 25 layers at most, but this kid is already at 33.”*

*“Why? If the Immortal Emperor favored me this much, I’d never abuse the trust Grandpa Immortal Emperor placed in me like this.”*

Unwilling to keep dwelling on the subject, Dugu Ming could only return to the reason he had come and get straight to the point. “Minister Zhang, now isn’t the time to discuss these trivial matters.”

“Have you decided what direction to take with the Chain Formation Diagram you’re going to create?”

At these words, Zhang Yu’s thoughts stirred. “Hall Master Dugu, do you have any suggestions on that?”

“I wouldn’t presume,” Dugu Ming said. “It’s just that some Immortals are very concerned about this...”

After a rapid exchange with him, Zhang Yu gradually understood what he meant.

“Many Immortals hope I can create a Chain Formation Diagram that’s sufficiently versatile — ideally, one that every cultivator can use.”

“And they hope...”

Zhang Yu asked the Dugu Ming before him, “They hope it will increase every cultivator’s comprehension, innate talent, and aptitude?”

“Not bad.” Dugu Ming said with a look of deep concern for the sect and the Immortals. “Minister Zhang, haven’t you noticed that cultivators these days are getting worse with each generation? Over the years, how many cultivators who’ve reached Ascension, Tribulation Transcendence, or Void Refinement haven’t been veterans?”

“Among the new generation of cultivators, very few can break through beyond Void Refinement, and those who can contribute to the field of Immortal Research are rarer still.”

“The sects have been investing heavily in cultivating lower-tier cultivators, yet this is the result. That’s why we need to strengthen their abilities…”

Zhang Yu nearly laughed in anger when he heard this. Was the reason it was so hard for the new generation of cultivators to advance really a question of ability?

Zhang Yu didn’t believe Dugu Ming was unaware of the reason. In his view, this was nothing more than a pretext.

Dugu Ming continued, “Kunxu is far too comfortable these days. Low-tier cultivators can support themselves just by working. Unlike in our day, when we had to take part in wars and fight for our lives just to survive!”

“Now we have this opportunity to create a Chain Formation Diagram. We should use it to increase the potential of all living beings across Kunxu and get low-tier cultivators to compete harder…”

Zhang Yu neither agreed nor disagreed with Dugu Ming’s suggestion.

Dugu Ming added, “As it happens, my Myriad Laws Sect has several basic cultivation arts that are perfectly suited to transforming low-tier cultivators from the ground up. Minister Zhang, take a look and see whether they could be combined into a Chain Formation Diagram.”

Zhang Yuxin thought, “Here it comes... So he really is going to recommend a Chain Formation Diagram to me?”

At the same time, Dugu Ming said, “If Minister Zhang is willing to make this contribution for the sake of all under heaven, the sect’s interests, and the Immortal Emperor’s assets, there are many Immortals who would be willing to lend you a hand by giving up some promotion slots for you.”

“Putting everything else aside, it wouldn’t be difficult to let you have ten more Void Refinement cultivators under your command...”

Hearing this, Zhang Yu’s heart stirred, and he understood what the other party meant.

Within the sect, there were too many monks and too little gruel. Every promotion and every vacant position was fiercely contested.

Even with Zhang Yu’s current influence, grooming ten Void Refinement cultivators would still take a great deal of time and resources.

But the other party was willing to give him ten Void Refinement positions, asking only that he add the recommended cultivation arts to the Chain Formation Diagram.

Hearing this, the Sect Examination Clone couldn’t help feeling tempted. “Does that mean... they’re giving us ten Void Refinement positions? Then we’ll have ten more Void Refinement Sect Staff under our command — ten people who can establish laws and support us, helping us elevate the Law Edict into a Law Decree.”

The Sect Examination Clone analyzed, “And this should still be far from their bottom line.”

Dugu Ming said, “Well, Minister Zhang, give it some careful thought. You can also make specific requests.”

Not long after Dugu Ming and Zhang Yu finished talking, Lie Jinghong of the Dharma Realm Faction also sent Zhang Yu a message.

This sect prodigy, who had taken part in Law Establishment alongside Zhang Yu, had once been filled with admiration for the Immortal Emperor and had gotten along famously with Zhang Yu during law establishment training.

At that moment, the other party said, “Minister Zhang, the cultivators of the world have suffered under pharmaceutical companies for far too long.”

“Whether it’s treating injuries, advancing one’s cultivation, or improving work capacity, all kinds of treatments and medicinal pills are consumed every day. Over the years, they add up to a hefty expense for any cultivator, and the bulk of the profits don’t go to the Immortal Emperor. How can we build our sect properly if this goes on?”

“We selected several cultivation arts from the Dharma Realm database that are best suited to improving physical constitutions and can reduce the burden of medical expenses.”

“If they could be used to create Chain Formation Diagrams, making cultivators more resilient and less prone to injury, it would greatly boost the sect’s productivity...”

Zhang Yu listened to the other party's pitch and eventually learned what they had to offer.

“One cultivation art slot can be exchanged for five Law Establishment positions?”

*“If I pick two cultivation arts, would I gain ten Law Establishment positions? Even the Dharma Realm Faction would have a hard time pulling that off, wouldn’t it? They’d probably have to make deals with other factions, and might not be able to get it done in one go. It could take two or three rounds…”*

From what Zhang Yu could tell, the other party was open to further negotiating the terms, and could sweeten the deal with more bargaining chips.

Before long, more and more cultivators Zhang Yu knew came to see him, each proposing something different.

“Some want to improve the aptitude of all living beings; some want to extend the lifespans of all under heaven; some want to improve Divine Soul Toughness and increase how long Soul Cultivators can work after death…”

Zhang Yu could tell that these factions proposing one idea after another didn’t just want to insert cultivation arts and reap benefits. They also wanted to influence the effects of the Chain Formation Diagram as they inserted those arts.

“It seems these factions all understand that cultivation arts have a profound impact on the effects of the Chain Formation Diagram.”

Immortal Slayer sighed, “But these Chain Formation Diagrams all seem, one by one, to be designed for the good of all under Heaven. Yet the more capable cultivators become, the longer they live, and the more resilient they are, the more they’ll only make Kunxu increasingly cutthroat.”

“With Kunxu’s current overall structure, any enhancement or benefit for lower-tier cultivators will ultimately just be redirected to upper-tier cultivators.”

“No matter how we try to strengthen lower-tier cultivators, it will only result in them being exploited even more ruthlessly, allowing the Immortal Class to reap greater benefits and grow stronger in turn.”

The Sect Examination Clone said, “This is simply a privilege of high-ranking cultivators. If those of lower cultivation cannot aid upper-tier cultivators or direct benefits toward them, then they have no reason to exist as far as the high-ranking cultivators are concerned!”

“Or are you saying, Immortal Slayer, that you want us to create a Chain Formation Diagram that... turns every cultivator into a good-for-nothing?”

As the debate played out in his mind, Zhang Yu had already turned his attention to another Book.

After this mid-grade Book was fused with the Feather Book, records of lost histories also appeared in the Feather Book, one passage after another.

Zhang Yu read through its contents and summarized, “The Heavenly Tribulation Dao Lineage punishes evil-doers throughout the world.”

“The Karma Dao Lineage brings about karmic retribution, punishing evil and promoting good.”

“The God-Investiture Dao Lineage selects and confers godhood based on virtue, creating a Divine Dynasty on earth...”

“All the sects in the world were disbanded, and the cultivation arts of every school were placed under the Divine Dynasty’s unified administration...”

Zhang Yu’s heart stirred. “The sevenfold Thunder Tribulation I survived, as well as that so-called ‘Paragon of Virtue’ label, seem to be related to this lost history.”

Zhang Yu continued reading, thinking to himself, “The Divine Dynasty should have left a glorious mark on history and achieved all manner of great things. Yet before it could even be born, it faced one crisis after another. With each successive failure, it vanished completely from history.”

Desolate Ox said, “Just like the Monster Saints, this Divine Dynasty probably met its end through repeated rollbacks, eventually becoming a lost part of history.”

As Zhang Yu looked at the words in the Feather Book, he seemed to glimpse, amid the fog-shrouded history of Kunxu, someone repeatedly reaching in to alter the course of history and change the direction in which Kunxu advanced.
