# Chapter 1017: This Time, It's My Turn to Help You

*"Can I accomplish this in 33 seconds?"*

The thought flashed through Ying Xintian's mind, yet the resolve in his heart did not waver in the slightest.

For he understood clearly that regardless of whether he could do it, he had to do it.

There was no retreat left before them.

And right at that moment, a will descended from above, reaching him from afar.

"Brother Dao, this time, it's my turn to help you."

Ying Xintian was taken aback, thinking in surprise, *"Is this... Zhang Yu's voice?"*

Deep within Zhang Yu's consciousness, he noted internally, "The bloodline information of mortals, much like DNA or genetic information, has carried the information of the past forward, turning into our aid."

Zhang Yu understood that just as a normal human being would not know what was contained within their genetic information, the mortals of Kunxu naturally could not know what had been left behind in their bloodline information.

"Generation after generation, they were merely struggling just to survive, breeding and carrying on their lines. In the eyes of cultivators, Immortals, and Immortal Emperors, they were nothing more than fragile bottom-feeders scraping by on the fringes of the Kunxu world."

"Yet this bloodline inheritance of mortals, this struggle to survive along the way, this connection of bloodlines passed down through generation after generation, proved so incredibly resilient that it could break through the barriers of time and space — barriers that even the ten Immortal Emperors struggled to cross — and bring support from the past into the present."

"Furthermore, this connection was firmly rooted within the chronicles of time, such that even the Immortal Emperors, currently in the cooldown of their rollback, could not sever it."

"And finally, after generations upon generations... it has surged forth within my own flesh and blood."

Zhang Yu understood that it was precisely the original owner of his physical body and generations of ancestors reproducing, passing the torch in a relay across generations as they struggled to survive on this land of Kunxu, carrying the bloodline forward to this very moment, that had allowed the Immortal Ancestor's Will to successfully obtain the bloodline information within it.

Moreover, Senior Supreme Truth's inheritance, the assistance of past Immortal Emperors, and the struggles of generations of Immortal Ancestors... not a single piece could have been missing.

"This is the collective result of countless cultivators and mortals striving together."

"Yet even if all this has been achieved, will it be enough to defeat the ten Immortal Emperors?"

Just as Zhang Yu's thoughts flashed like lightning, the Immortal Ancestor's will resounded swiftly within his mind.

"Zhang Yu, relying solely on transmitting memories will provide only limited improvement to Ying Xintian in the short term."

"After all, memories are just memories. Though they can enhance part of his Immortal Dao Technology and raise the upper limit of his current strength, Ying Xintian simply doesn't have enough time to develop."

Hearing this, Zhang Yu deeply agreed. He knew that relying on memories alone would inevitably yield limited combat power, which had been his lingering concern just moments before.

However, the Immortal Ancestor's will's next words stirred his heart.

"We can help him break through once more!"

As the Immortal Ancestor's will spoke, the Feather Book before Zhang Yu fluttered open on its own without any wind, abruptly flipping to the page of Dao Seed Register II.

The function of Dao Seed Register II was that it could cultivate a Dao Seed every year and bestow it upon someone who lacked one.

Through this ability, Zhang Yu had already developed new Dao Seeds for Old Gao, Magnetic Pole, and Ye Xingli, while the latest year's new Dao Seed from September had yet to be used by him.

At this moment, the Immortal Ancestor's will explained, "Zhang Yu, now that you are the Immortal Ancestor's successor, with my help enabling you to wield the Book's power, its range of influence will far exceed anything in the past! Even across great distances, you can bestow a new Dao Seed upon Ying Xintian and infuse him with a Chain Formation Diagram."

Zhang Yu's eyes lit up, but a moment later, he said, "Even if I bestow a new Dao Seed on him, it might not necessarily be of any help in a battle at their level."

Zhang Yu knew that whenever a new Dao Seed was created, although it would align with the cultivator's characteristics, its effects were often unrelated to combat.

Yet at this moment, what Ying Xintian clearly needed most was combat strength.

The Immortal Ancestor's Will said, "When ordinary cultivators use this function of the Book, they can only leave it to fate. But you are the Immortal Ancestor's successor, and with me operating the Book's Dao Lineage to assist you from the side, you can completely designate a specific Dao Seed while bestowing it."

Zhang Yu was shaken. "You mean..."

The Immortal Ancestor's Will said, "In the process of bestowing a new Dao Seed upon Ying Xintian, we can designate it as any Dao Seed on your body."

Zhang Yu marveled inward, "As expected, being able to operate the a Lineage means being both player and referee, allowing all sorts of cheating when using the powers associated with it."

At this moment, he thought of Yun Taizhen from back then.

When she passed down the inheritance and gave him a Dao Seed, she had also demonstrated her mastery over the Dao Seed Dao Lineage and her familiarity with the Books.

Zhang Yu realized now that after experiencing archeological excavations time and time again, Yun Taizhen had likely already conducted a certain amount of research into the Book Dao Lineage back then. 

At this very moment, the Immortal Ancestor's Will was directly channeling the Book Dao Lineage, using methods that were even more seasoned and formidable. 

Zhang Yu had also figured out which Dao Seed he should choose to aid Ying Xintian. 

"Dao... Senior Ying Xintian, this is the Dao Seed created by Senior Supreme Truth. Use this power to turn Kunxu upside down!" 

Zhang Yu's voice surged directly into Ying Xintian's ears, carried through the Immortal Ancestor's will. 

The next moment, Ying Xintian felt a familiar aura rising within his body. 

The level 9 Emperor Creation Seed descended into Ying Xintian's body at this moment. 

To Ying Xintian, that was the scent of Yun Taizhen. 

"Taizhen... then today, let us fight hand in hand once more to put a period to this protracted Righteous-Demonic Conflict." 

***Boom!***

With 32 seconds left on the countdown of the future information, a terrifying aura erupted from Ying Xintian's body, and all of his stats shattered their original limits in this instant! 

Yet, this power that had broken through his limits felt incomparably familiar to Ying Xintian's senses. 

It was all because the memory he had just acquired contained countless experiences on how to wield power of this caliber.

With 31.5 seconds left on the countdown, the power of the Chain Formation Diagram known as Inverted Kunlun was channeled jointly by Zhang Yu and the Immortal Ancestor's will into the Technique Aperture Ying Xintian had manifested upon acquiring the Emperor Creation Seed.

Violent thrumming vibrations resounded across the layers of the sky sealed off by the Righteous Gods and the Immortals of the various sects.

At this moment, the Dao Masters of the Kunlun Dao Lineage each seemed to sense the Dao Lineage under their grasp showing faint signs of slipping out of control.

Feeling this equally familiar power, Ying Xintian thought to himself, *"Using a Chain Formation Diagram to let me stir up the Kunlun Dao Lineage? It's a bit weak, but barely enough to suffice."*

At the same time, through the Immortal Ancestor's will, Zhang Yu also sensed the Dao Seed [Immortal Descendant] on Ying Xintian's person, as well as the Technique Aperture behind this Dao Seed.

Zhang Yu thought to himself, "Right. Back then, I copied the Dao Seed [Immortal Descendant] from Brother Dao. This was originally a Dao Seed prepared by Senior Ying Xintian for himself, his incarnations, and his inheritance."

With 31.2 seconds left on the countdown, the Chain Formation Diagram [Formless Martial Art] was infused into Ying Xintian's Technique Aperture for the Immortal Descendant. At this moment, Ying Xintian felt as though every martial dao technique in his mind and memories could be simulated and executed by him.

Countdown: 30.9 seconds. Palm Technique Books, Fist Technique Books, Breathing Technique Books, Mental Cultivation Books... A whole series of Cultivation Art Specialization Books had already been brought out by Zhang Yu and sent over by the Immortal Ancestor's will, plunging straight into Ying Xintian's body.

Ten seconds later, the countdown reached 20.9 seconds.

The pressure erupting from Ying Xintian surged frantically, warping and distorting the barriers and seals between the layers of Kunxu.

"With my current strength and the power of the Books, spending ten seconds was enough to restore some of the primary cultivation arts from my newly recovered memories to eighty percent of their peak."

"My strength has soared to such an extent that it is enough to contend with an Immortal Emperor... but it is still not enough."

With a sudden reach of Ying Xintian's hand, accompanied by a slight tremor throughout the entire 22nd layer of Kunxu, two figures were forcefully seized and pulled right before him.

Immortal Roujia and Immortal Yi Xuan cried out in shock, "Senior... Senior Brother!"

Standing before Ying Xintian, the two Immortals felt as though they had returned to the distant past. All of their power, techniques, and secrets... were laid completely bare to his gaze at this very moment.

What answered them was an unmatched power as vast and majestic as that of the ten Immortal Emperors, along with a palm that expanded boundlessly, filling the world in the blink of an eye.

Countdown: 14.8 seconds.

Immortal Roujia and Immortal Yi Xuan had already been heavily wounded by Ying Xintian, who had stripped away part of their authority over the Kunlun Dao Lineage.

"That's about enough." Thoughts raced through Ying Xintian's mind. "I can't waste any more time building up my strength. In these remaining 14.8 seconds... I must temporarily deprive the ten Immortal Emperors of the possibility of a rollback."

How could he make it temporarily impossible for the ten Immortal Emperors to perform a rollback?

"As long as they cannot reach an agreement, they won't be able to initiate a World Rollback."

Ying Xintian could feel that his current state — this absolute peak — was only possible thanks to the desperate struggles of Zhang Yu and the Immortal Ancestor's will.

If the Immortal Emperors initiated a rollback after 14.8 seconds, they could easily erase this peak state of his, stripping away the combat power capable of rivaling an Immortal Emperor.

Ying Xintian thought, "If that happens, we will never have another chance."

And to sow discord among the ten Immortal Emperors, the figures of Celestial Monster Immortal Emperor, Joyous Union Immortal Emperor, and White Bone Immortal Emperor immediately surfaced in Ying Xintian's mind.

Through the information transmitted by the Immortal Ancestor's will, Ying Xintian knew that because White Bone Immortal Emperor, Celestial Monster Immortal Emperor, and Joyous Union Immortal Emperor had acquired vast seas of memories, their strength was very likely affected at this moment, leaving them in a state of cognitive conflict.

"Especially since the ten Immortal Emperors are now locked in internal strife."

"If I can seize this opportunity to inflict a heavy injury on any one of White Bone Immortal Emperor, Celestial Monster Immortal Emperor, or Joyous Union Immortal Emperor..."

Ying Xintian understood that despite how keen the ten Immortal Emperors were to carve up and devour the Immortal Ancestor, should any one of them suffer an injury and see their strength diminished, they would become an even more enticing target, triggering further infighting among the ten Immortal Emperors.

"Even better, if I can use the memories they've acquired to strike at and alter their thoughts, that would be ideal."

His thoughts resolved in an instant. Amidst the thunderous shattering of spatial layer after spatial layer, Ying Xintian tore through tens of thousands of restrictions like a bolt of lightning.

With 14.8 seconds left on the countdown, the divine light of countless Righteous Gods burst into view, transforming into an endless celestial net blocking Ying Xintian's path.

"The Eight Righteous Gods are here!"

"Stop him! Don't let him enter the 30th layer!"

"Hurry, summon the Eight Roaming Great Gods of Penal Law! Summon the Great God of Universal Inspection!"

13.5 seconds. The righteous gods filling the sky crumbled into specks of light, and massive chasms were torn through the 21st to 29th layers that had just been sealed off.

12.6 seconds. Several Immortals formed a great formation, barring Ying Xintian's path.

"Traitor Ying! How much more damage will you bring upon Kunxu?!"

"Stop him! We cannot let him enter the Immortal Emperor Domain!"

"Stay behind and pay your debts!"

11.9 seconds. The great formation was shattered, the Immortals were repelled, the firmament of the 29th layer was torn open, and Ying Xintian stepped into Kunxu's 30th layer.

If one had to go through layer after layer of cultivation, work, and assessments... even after three thousand years, a cultivator might still fail to climb into this 30th layer of Kunxu.

Yet at this very moment, in less than three seconds, the gates to the Immortal Emperor Domain had been forcibly blasted open by Ying Xintian.

---

Within the pitch-black Sea of Consciousness.

At some unknown point, the Immortal Ancestor's will had ceased communicating with Zhang Yu.

In this Sea of Consciousness devoid of perception, Zhang Yu seemed unable to sense the passage of time, with no idea how long he had remained in this muddled stupor.

Until at a certain instant, he felt the physical sensations of a body once more, regaining control over his physical body.

"Did Senior Ying Xintian succeed? Am I saved?"

Thoughts flashed through Zhang Yu's mind in an instant. The next moment, he forcefully took control of his body, opened his eyes once more, and looked at the world before him.

On the enormous Ascension Platform, it seemed as if he were the only one left.

No, he was not alone... Following his senses, Zhang Yu turned his head to look to his side.

"That's..."

Zhang Yu's gaze focused slightly, and he saw Ying Xintian kneeling motionless on one knee.

In front of Ying Xintian stood a somewhat familiar figure, gently stroking Ying Xintian's head with a hand.

Looking at that familiar figure, waves of chill welled up in Zhang Yu's heart.

"Myriad Laws... Immortal Emperor..."

Standing before Ying Xintian at this moment was none other than the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor.

The Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor withdrew the hand that had gently brushed Ying Xintian's head and spoke slowly, "Supreme Immortal Emperor Ying Xintian, you truly are formidable. In your era, you were truly invincible in the world."

"But now is our era. How could an Immortal Emperor of the past ever compare to an Immortal Emperor of today? Any one of the ten Immortal Emperors of this age commands Immortal Dao Technology far superior to yours."

The next moment, Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor turned his head, casting his gaze toward Zhang Yu. "Using mortal bloodline propagation to aid you?"

"Rather than an aid, it is more accurate to say that these bloodlines, woven across generations of mortals, are the very fishing lines in Zhen's hands — which have finally reeled these old ghosts out of lost history."

The Sect Examination Clone roared frantically in his heart, "That useless piece of trash Ying Xintian — he still lost even with all that?! Zhang Yu, what are you standing there like an idiot for? Kneel down quickly and beg Grandpa Immortal Emperor for mercy!"

Immortal Slayer muttered, "I knew there would be a gap, but... to think the gap would be this immense?!"

At the same time, the power of the other nine Immortal Emperors descended from the sky, encircling the entire Ascension Platform.

Only a single second remained on the countdown for the future information.
