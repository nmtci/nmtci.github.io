# Chapter 996 Chain Law Comprehensive Manual, From the Future

As the countdown for the New Book-Master Selection was about to end, Zhang Yu had already prepared to flee.

"Although I currently hold the identity of a diplomatic envoy from the Myriad Laws Sect and should be protected by the relevant Law Edicts, veteran Ascension Realm cultivators like Bai Gusheng and Source Saint might still have ways to deal with me, especially since Source Saint is a local tyrant of the Immortal Armaments Bureau..."

To avoid complications, Zhang Yu had activated the ability of his Immortal Grotto-Heaven early on, ensuring he could retreat immediately at the first moment.

As for Liu Qianxian, Zhang Yu could only wish her luck.

However, Zhang Yu believed that whether it was Bai Gusheng or Source Saint, their targets were ultimately himself and the new book. After he retreated... there was a high probability they would have no interest in doing anything to Liu Qianxian.

Thus, at this very moment, watching Zhang Yu's vanishing figure, Bai Gusheng and Source Saint briefly glanced over Liu Qianxian before their gazes met.

Bai Gusheng said with a face full of regret, "Alas, an opportunity missed today. He managed to run away ahead of time... What a pity, what a pity."

With an expression of deep sorrow and indignation, Source Saint said, "Hmph, that brat sure ran fast..."

On the other side, Liu Qianxian had watched the entire New Book-Master Selection process and was inwardly astonished: "Is it really that powerful...? Did the *Feather Book* actually allow Zhang Yu to win this New Book-Master Selection?"

In Liu Qianxian's eyes, Zhang Yu's cultivation and wealth naturally could not compare to those of the Source Saint and Bai Gusheng. The most likely reason he could win this New Book-Master Selection was precisely the *Feather Book*.

"As expected of the foremost among the four Books..."

Recalling Zhang Yu's own attempts, as well as the three cultivation art combinations he had reported to her, Liu Qianxian could not help but think to herself: "Through the abilities of the *Feather Book*, Zhang Yu seems to be able to see far more information about the Chain Formation Diagrams than ordinary cultivators. That must be how he found the most suitable Chain Formation Diagram for the current scenario within a limited number of trial-and-error attempts."

The next moment, a thought suddenly welled up in her heart: "Zhang Yu... Feather Book... Feather... Is there any connection?"

Shaking her head, Liu Qianxian thought to herself: "They just happen to share the character 'Yu', meaning 'Feather'. Their origins are worlds apart; what wild thoughts am I having?"

---

At the same time, Zhang Yu had just returned to the Immortal Grotto-Heaven, his gaze naturally sweeping toward the new book materializing in his hand.

Looking at the title on the cover, Zhang Yu thought to himself: "Is this book called the *Chain Law Comprehensive Manual*?"

As if sensing Zhang Yu's gaze, the *Chain Law Comprehensive Manual* before him began to flip on its own without any wind, its pages turning open with a resounding flutter.

At the same time, Zhang Yu quickly sensed a surge of information flowing out from it, transmitting into his brain as if someone were speaking directly in his mind.

"Today, this seat establishes the Chain Law Dao Lineage, linking all the myriad laws under heaven..."

Hearing this message, Zhang Yu was inwardly moved. *"Is this... someone's left-behind message? Establishing the Chain Law Dao Lineage... So the Chain Formation Diagram also originates from a Dao lineage? And this is the message left by the creator of the Chain Formation Diagram?"*

*"Then, is this creator of the Chain Formation Diagram the same person as the creator of that volume of the Feather Book from Fa Liuyuan?"*

As he pondered, another guess suddenly surged in Zhang Yu's heart: *"If that's really the case, then the history of this Chain Law Comprehensive Manual is even older than other books where the Chain Formation Diagram can be seen. It should be the very first book related to the Chain Formation Diagram."*

Thoughts flashed through Zhang Yu's mind like lightning, but the next moment... he snapped the *Chain Law Comprehensive Manual* in his hand shut.

"You two, I don't recall inviting you over as guests, did I?"

Even as Zhang Yu spoke, the entire Immortal Grotto-Heaven trembled faintly. The figures of Bai Gusheng and Source Saint were squeezed out as if by invisible space, appearing right before Zhang Yu.

The two men, who had just been expressing regret and disappointment to each other outside, now swept their gazes around and exchanged a knowing smile. Bai Gusheng thought to himself, *"That Source Livestock pretended to miss Zhang Yu, but in truth, he was prepared all along... sneaking in right behind him."*

Source Saint gave a cold scoff internally, *"These lunatics from the White Bone Cult who hike up medical costs to exorbitant prices — word is they even banned medical consultations from Intelligent Artifact Spirits recently. Truly, not a single word coming out of their mouths can be trusted."*

It turned out... though Zhang Yu had long prepared to flee, Bai Gusheng and Source Saint had likewise anticipated that he would run away. In fact, as the countdown had neared its end, they had long since abandoned any hope of winning, focusing entirely on Zhang Yu and standing ready to covertly follow him at any second.

These two veteran Ascension Realm cultivators had also demonstrated formidable spatial technology, each sending a clone in pursuit almost the exact moment Zhang Yu slipped into the Immortal Grotto-Heaven.

Having their tracks exposed with a single point from Zhang Yu right now took both Bai Gusheng and Source Saint slightly by surprise.

Bai Gusheng thought, *"When the new book opened just now, Zhang Yu seemed to read some information from it. What a pity... this book has already recognized its master. Neither Source Saint nor I could intercept the information it transmitted."*

*"So... did we give away our whereabouts when we cast magic power just now, trying to pry into this new book?"*

*"Still... even though this is merely an avatar of mine, he actually forced me out directly. This person's attainment in spatial technology and mastery over this Grotto-Heaven far surpass cultivators of the same realm."*

Beside him, Source Saint harbored similar thoughts: *"...As expected of someone who received Traitor Ying's legacy. He truly has quite a knack for spatial technology."*

Zhang Yu, on the other hand, secretly rejoiced: *"Fortunately, with the boost from the Immortal Foundation Chapter, my Dao-Seeking Progress for the World-Domain Dao Lineage has already reached 48%, significantly advancing my spatial technology. Otherwise, I might not have seen through these two Ascension Seeds so quickly."*

Although Zhang Yu's mastery over spatial technology was somewhat unexpected, Bai Gusheng and Source Saint still maintained the lofty posture of Ascension Realm cultivators.

After all, within Kunxu, the disparity in cultivation realm was decisive.

*"Don't be fooled by the fact that he could detect our whereabouts. If it truly comes to blows, a single thought from me would suffice to turn this Grotto-Heaven upside down, inflicting catastrophic losses on Zhang Yu."*

*"And if it were permissible to seize it by force, it would take me a few breaths at most to snatch the book from Zhang Yu's hands."*

With this thought, Bai Gusheng inwardly sighed with regret.

Were it not for Zhang Yu's identity and background, both Bai Gusheng and the Source Saint were confident they had ten thousand ways to suppress Zhang Yu and seize the book.

The Source Saint thought to himself, *"Although this Zhang Yu's strength is far inferior to ours, this volume of the Feather Book truly lives up to its name as the foremost of the four Books. The Chain Formation Diagram information it allowed him to perceive... is likely superior to ours as well, which is how he won this New Book-Master Selection."*

At this moment, the Source Saint made his judgment: *"Lacking sufficient strength, his efficiency in collecting old Books will naturally be far below ours. However, in the New Book-Master Selection, facing the various competitive mechanics of the new Books, he, possessing the Feather Book... might hold a greater advantage."*

Beside him, Bai Gusheng came to a similar conclusion: *"Zhang Yu's raw strength ranks at the very bottom among the Book-Wielders of the various sects. But with the backing of the entire Myriad Laws Sect enhancing the abilities of his Feather Book and other books, for now... it makes him extremely competitive in the New Book-Master Selection."*

Bai Gusheng tried to gauge Zhang Yu's mindset: *"Yet new books are ultimately limited, while old books make up the vast majority. Zhang Yu should be able to realize this as well, knowing that in the grand scheme of collecting the Books of the world, he will most likely fall behind the other sects..."*

*"Perhaps, knowing he is bound to fall behind, he doesn't even view us as competitors."*

*"I could try making a deal with Zhang Yu."*

With these various thoughts flashing through his mind, Bai Gusheng spoke, "Zhang Yu, given your strength, you must also understand where you rank among the Ten Sects Book-Wielders. For you... lacking hard power, the importance of intelligence speaks for itself."

"Now that you have the chance to exchange intelligence about the Books with me, you should be the one begging us."

At his side, Source Saint thought, *"Heh, Bai Gusheng truly cares about this new Book."*

The more he realized this, the more curious and attentive Source Saint became regarding intelligence on this new Book.

At the same time, an avatar materialized beside Zhang Yu.

Then, under the watchful eyes of Bai Gusheng and Source Saint, the avatar took the *Chain Law Comprehensive Manual* handed over by Zhang Yu, flickered, and teleported into the depths of the Immortal Grotto-Heaven.

Zhang Yu, however, said, "Gentlemen, forgive me, but I have pressing matters to attend to and cannot entertain..."

Sensing the subtle shift in the surrounding spatial structure and realizing that Zhang Yu was about to see them out, Bai Gusheng let out a cold snort. His avatar stomped its left foot, temporarily stabilizing the spatial structure.

"Zhang Yu, don't think you're the only one who can manipulate space. With the gap between our cultivation realms, if I truly wanted to stay, could you really drive me away?"

As Bai Gusheng spoke, he saw the scenery of the Myriad Laws Sect gradually emerge around them, immediately realizing that Zhang Yu was drawing over the sect's power.

He sighed inwardly, *"People with powerful backgrounds are such a nuisance. Kunxu has become this chaotic precisely because of these people acting willfully without restraint."*

Bai Gusheng immediately spoke again, "Profound Monarch Zhang, have you heard the saying about 'When the Four Books gather, the Immortal Ancestor appears'?"

Zhang Yu halted his movements.

At this moment, Zhang Yu's clone was researching the *Chain Law Comprehensive Manual*, while his true self personally dealt with Bai Gusheng and the Source Saint.

To say that Zhang Yu had no interest in the intelligence regarding the Books Bai Gusheng mentioned was naturally impossible.

However, both of them were Ascension Realm cultivators who had maintained an aloof, condescending attitude ever since entering the Immortal Grotto-Heaven. Thus, Zhang Yu intended to pressure them a bit to better seize the initiative in the conversation.

So, after Bai Gusheng took the initiative to disclose information, Zhang Yu stopped what he was doing without saying a word, merely looking at Bai Gusheng in silence.

Bai Gusheng thought to himself, *"It seems he doesn't know yet? Zhang Yu will learn about these rumors sooner or later anyway. Even if I don't tell him, the Myriad Laws Sect will. I might as well use it here to show some sincerity."*

With this thought, Bai Gusheng spoke, "The so-called Immortal Ancestor is the creator of all kinds of Books in this world, including the four Books of Feathered Ascension."

"It is said that the Immortal Emperors intend to gather the four Books, and even all the world's Books, in order to revive this Immortal Ancestor..."

Hearing this, Zhang Yu's heart sank. *"Could this Immortal Ancestor... also be of the Immortal Emperor level? An entity once defeated by the Ten Immortal Emperors in the past?"*

*"What the hell... Are the Ten Immortal Emperors planning to pull ancient zombie meat out of the cold storage and eat it to break through to the ultimate realm?"*

While Zhang Yu conversed with Bai Gusheng and Source Saint, his clone, holding the *Chain Law Comprehensive Manual*, arrived at the deepest depths of the Immortal Grotto-Heaven.

After using his "surveillance clone" to inspect the *Chain Law Comprehensive Manual*, Zhang Yu opened this new Book and continued reading the information within.

"The Chain Law Dao Lineage pioneered by this seat allows all laws under heaven to find their rightful place, enabling lower-grade cultivation arts to combine and triumph over superior cultivation arts..."

"From now on, any cultivator cultivating their arts will gradually uncover subtle connections between them, continuously discovering new Chain Formation Diagrams..."

Listening to the message inside the Chain Law Comprehensive Manual, Zhang Yu thought to himself, "It seems my previous guess was correct. This volume of the Chain Law Comprehensive Manual truly has a long history, born before all the Chain Formation Diagrams."

"But why did a Chain Law Comprehensive Manual with such an ancient history reappear in the world only now?"

"Moreover... according to this Senior's message, any cultivator should be able to discover Chain Formation Diagrams when practicing cultivation arts? This clearly doesn't match the current situation."

"So did the Chain Law Dao Lineage... fail to be established back then? Or did it run into some issue later on, leading to today's state where only holders of certain Books can unleash the power of the Chain Formation Diagrams?"

Zhang Yu shook his head, feeling only more doubts welling up inside him as he listened up to this point.

Meanwhile, the message inside the Chain Law Comprehensive Manual continued.

"Following the Chain Law Comprehensive Manual, the Four Books of Feathered Ascension will also soon be completed by my hands. These four Books shall record and connect the various Dao Lineages from ancient times to the present. From then on, the mortal world will have four more shortcuts to achieve immortality...

"One day, the world will usher in an Immortal Dao Golden Age where everyone achieves immortality, and propelled by all living beings under heaven, we shall scale the absolute peak of the Immortal Dao together!"

After listening to the other party's message, Zhang Yu thought to himself, "As expected, the creator of this Chain Law Comprehensive Manual is... the creator of the *Four Books of Feathered Ascension*, which means..."

The intelligence obtained from Bai Gusheng surfaced in Zhang Yu's mind, and he thought, "...the Immortal Ancestor."

From this message, Zhang Yu could sense the Immortal Ancestor's confidence, pride, and anticipation regarding the *Chain Law Comprehensive Manual* and *Feathered Ascension*.

Immortal Slayer sighed, "Unfortunately, the Books... in the end, failed to carve out a shortcut to immortality for the people of the world as this Immortal Ancestor had envisioned. And as for this Immortal Ancestor, I fear he ultimately met with misfortune."

"If what Bai Gusheng said is true, now that the Ten Immortal Emperors want to revive this Immortal Ancestor, they certainly harbor no good intentions."

Zhang Yu nodded slightly. What he worried about most was that once this Immortal Ancestor was revived, he would be devoured by the Ten Immortal Emperors, ultimately triggering the Ultimate Doomsday altogether.

"There seem to be more messages below..."

Zhang Yu continued reading, and the very first few words of this message stunned him.

*"I am Zhang Yu."*

*"Right now, it is October 22nd of the second year after you saw the Chain Law Comprehensive Manual..."*

Zhang Yu froze slightly and read through it rapidly, his heart utterly overwhelmed with shock.

Immortal Slayer said, "So that's how it is... It turns out that I..."

The Sect Examination Clone said, "Ah?! So it's..."

Zhang Yu thought to himself, "Turns out... what? What are you guys trying to say?"

Immortal Slayer said, "We didn't say anything just now."

Zhang Yu frowned slightly, a strange feeling welling up inside him.

However, this slight strange sensation quickly dissipated from his heart, completely forgotten by him.

Zhang Yu looked at the *Chain Law Comprehensive Manual* before him and thought, "The messages... did we finish reading them without even realizing it?"

He recalled the Immortal Ancestor's message he had just read in the book, yet a strange feeling surfaced in his mind: "Was there only the Immortal Ancestor's message? Weren't there any other messages?"

The Sect Examination Clone said, "Since this *Chain Law Comprehensive Manual* was created by the Immortal Ancestor, whose messages could there be besides the Immortal Ancestor's?"

Thoughts raced through Zhang Yu's mind. Instantly recalling his memory of reading it just moments ago, he gave a slight nod. "Indeed, besides the Immortal Ancestor's message, what other messages could there possibly be on it?"

"I must be overthinking things..."

Just as Zhang Yu was thinking this, his heart suddenly sank.

"The time... something is wrong with the time."

Because he had to handle all kinds of work affairs, manage the construction of the Immortal Grotto-Heaven, oversee the laboratory, the production line... as well as various messages sent by his subordinates, Zhang Yu often created several clones to work continuously.

These continuously working clones were connected to the Spirit Realm at all times and were extremely sensitive to time.

And right now, based on the time perceived through the clones...

"Reading the message just now should have taken me around 3.58 seconds altogether."

"Yet in my memory, reading the Immortal Ancestor's message took me at most 0.82 seconds."

"The time in between... why don't I remember it at all?!"

Almost the instant he realized this, with a single thought from Zhang Yu, the entire Immortal Grotto-Heaven was completely locked down.

"Who... influenced me?"

---

"The Immortal Ancestor is said to be the creator of the Books, aiming to use the Books to connect all the Dao Lineages from ancient times to the present."

"But how could the Dao Lineages of the world be something he alone could connect?"

Bai Gusheng gave a faint smile and said, "His approach was like demanding that major corporations and platforms all open a backdoor for him so he could offer it to everyone in the world for free — who would ever agree to that?"

"It is precisely for this reason that the Immortal Ancestor was besieged and fell early on, leaving behind only a sliver of a remnant soul anchored between heaven and earth."

"Now that the Immortal Emperors want all four Books gathered so the Immortal Ancestor will appear, they're probably looking to thoroughly obliterate this fellow who messed around with open-sourcing."

As he spoke, Bai Gusheng glanced at Source Saint, who was standing not far away.

Bai Gusheng's understanding of "when the Four Books gather, the Immortal Ancestor appears" was actually largely his own speculation. However, since he possessed a vast amount of intelligence regarding the Books, he believed that his guesses were certainly correct in the grand scheme of things.

Right then, Bai Gusheng sensed a change in the space and looked at Zhang Yu in confusion.

At this moment, Zhang Yu — having locked down the entire Immortal Grotto-Heaven — was rapidly scanning and searching the whole Grotto-Heaven, tracing every single anomaly.

Facing Bai Gusheng and Source Saint, he maintained an impassive expression and said, "I browsed through the Spirit Realm, but I didn't seem to find any historical records concerning this Immortal Ancestor."

Zhang Yu thought to himself, *"The one who influenced me and made me lose that moment of memory... could it be one of them?"*

Hearing this, Bai Gusheng chuckled and said, "This Immortal Ancestor committed heinous crimes and was wiped out by numerous high-level cultivators, so it's only normal that no historical records have survived."

Before the *Chain Law Comprehensive Manual*, Zhang Yu's clone stared at the book, repeatedly going over every single detail in his mind.

*"What exactly did I forget?"*

*"Forget?"*

*"Memory..."*

A sudden thought struck Zhang Yu's mind.

Immortal Slayer also spoke up, "A memory that fades naturally... in all our time in the Kunxu World, we've only encountered that once, haven't we?"

Zhang Yu nodded slightly and said, "Mm... is it that?"

He recalled how Immortal Emperor Supreme Truth had once used bloodline power to transmit information from before the World Rollback.

"Could it be that the Ten Immortal Emperors... rolled back the world again?"

"Did someone transmit a message to me from the future, which is why I forgot it?"

Immortal Slayer said, "If it were anyone else, their first reaction upon encountering this kind of future information would likely be to ponder it, verify it, and then forget it, leaving behind no lasting impact."

The Sect Examination Clone said with confidence, "But we are different. We knew about the World Rollback in advance and that future information gets erased. So, after confirming the information came from the future..."

"I would definitely take certain measures..." Zhang Yu swept his gaze across the area and discovered that at the very depths of the entire Immortal Grotto-Heaven, a 13th layer had appeared at some point.

When he arrived at this 13th layer, he saw a cluster of sealed information floating within it.

Seeing the familiar countdown upon it, a flicker of realization flashed across Zhang Yu's eyes. "After receiving the future information, ...after receiving the information from the future, I directly carved out a new space, sealed the information, and stored it here."

Glancing at the shifting numbers of the countdown, Zhang Yu thought to himself, "October 22nd of next year? One year later... This rollback... was actually over a full year?"

"As expected, the duration of the rollback used to suppress Senior Supreme Truth last time was not their limit."

Zhang Yu's mind raced. "Even if I have now forgotten its contents, this countdown itself... is a hint in and of itself."

"When the Four Books gather, the Immortal Ancestor appears. According to Bai Gusheng, the time the Immortal Emperors arranged for the Immortal Ancestor's revival is roughly one year from now."

"So... did something go wrong after the Immortal Ancestor's revival, forcing the Ten Immortal Emperors to roll back the world, all the way back to one year ago?"

Immortal Slayer, however, voiced a different conjecture: "Is it possible that it was precisely because they ran into some sort of trouble one year later that the Immortal Emperors initiated the rollback and arranged the mission to gather the four Books and revive the Immortal Ancestor?"

The Sect Examination Clone said, "Hey, isn't that the least of our concerns right now? Someone sent us information from the future through the Book... What does that mean?"

Zhang Yu looked at the Chain Law Comprehensive Manual, thinking to himself, "The Book can transmit information from before the rollback."

"The Book can record... non-existent history?"

"Why? Is it because of its connection to a Dao Lineage?"

Immortal Slayer asked, "Also... who passed the message through the Chain Law Comprehensive Manual?"

The Sect Examination Clone said, "We obtained this Chain Law Comprehensive Manual, so more than a year later, the person holding the Chain Law Comprehensive Manual..."

Zhang Yu said, "It was very likely me, too..."

"The message from the future might have been sent by me."

With all these thoughts racing through his mind, Zhang Yu had already opened the Chain Law Comprehensive Manual once again, browsing through its contents.

"The ability of the Chain Law Comprehensive Manual is just as I thought — it can create Chain Formation Diagrams."

"However..."

Looking at the consequences of creating a Chain Formation Diagram with the Chain Law Comprehensive Manual, Zhang Yu couldn't help but draw in a sharp breath. "What the Chain Law Comprehensive Manual creates... are genuine Chain Formation Diagrams."
