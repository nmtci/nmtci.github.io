# Chapter 1006: Zhang Yu’s Choice

“If, throughout Kunxu’s long history, there really has been a person — or a group of people — who used the power of the World Rollback to guide the entire world’s course time and again, what exactly was their purpose?”

“And how did the Ten Immortal Emperors of today obtain this World Rollback power?”

“How much do the Ten Immortal Emperors know about these lost histories? How much have they uncovered?”

Looking at the text that had appeared on the Feather Book before him, Zhang Yu sighed inwardly. The more he learned, the more he felt that layers of mist shrouded all of Kunxu, from top to bottom — and that the Ten Immortal Emperors were unfathomable.

The Sect Examination Clone said, “It’s a shame Supreme Truth isn’t here. She’s excavated so many ancient ruins; perhaps she could give us some clues.”

Zhang Yu nodded slightly to himself and thought, “If I get the chance next time, perhaps I can ask Senior Ying Xintian. I wonder how much he knows about these lost histories.”

Setting aside his thoughts for the moment, Zhang Yu looked at another chapter that had appeared in the Feather Book: the Heavenly Tribulation Chapter.

It was only after the Feather Book devoured this Heavenly Tribulation Chapter that the lost history of the Divine Dynasty came to light.

Now, as Zhang Yu carefully sensed it, he gradually came to understand the purpose of this Heavenly Tribulation Chapter.

“It can refine the Dao Heart with thunder tribulations and accelerate the increase in its level?”

After testing it briefly, Zhang Yu had a rough assessment. “For other cultivators, this function is hardly worth mentioning.”

“After all, anyone below the Tribulation Transcendence Realm simply can’t withstand the technical backlash triggered by a thunder tribulation, let alone use one to refine their dao heart.”

“Only at the Tribulation Transcendence Realm can you make use of it while undergoing a thunder tribulation. And once you reach the Ascension Realm… you’d have to purchase additional thunder tribulations to refine your heart.”

For Zhang Yu as he was now, this ability to refine his heart with thunder tribulations was indeed very useful.

“My thunder tribulations already yield a huge amount, so I don’t mind using up a few more.”

“Especially since my magic power and physical strength are improving more and more rapidly. My Dao Heart level is gradually starting to fall behind, and this ability to refine my heart with thunder tribulations can make up the difference.”

Zhang Yu glanced at his basic stats. His Dao Heart had reached level 63 (1.88%), his magic power had surpassed 40 million units, and his physical strength had reached 65.04 points.

“The physical strength cap in the Tribulation Transcendence Realm is 70.0 points, the magic power cap is 100 million, and the Dao Heart cap is level 70. By comparison, it’s clear that my Dao Heart is lagging the furthest behind.”

With the support of various Chain Formation Diagrams and Dao Seeds, Zhang Yu’s magic power and physical strength had always improved much more rapidly than his Dao Heart level.

Recently, after meeting the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor and gaining access to the Dharma Realm that the other party had granted him, his magic power and physical strength had surged forward even faster.

If this continued, these two foundational attributes would inevitably be held back by his insufficient Dao Heart level, forcing him to slow down.

“Now that I have this Thunder Tribulation Heart Refinement function, at least my Dao Heart level won’t fall too far behind.”

Zhang Yu thought to himself, “I wonder what other wonders the Heavenly Tribulation Chapter might offer if it’s upgraded?”

Although the gap between his Dao Heart and his physical strength and magic power would narrow somewhat, Zhang Yu knew that this was still far from enough.

He glanced at the Body Refinement Book module and thought, “If I break through to the Ascension Realm in the future, I can use the Body Refinement Points accumulated in the Body Refinement Book to boost my physical strength all at once.”

“As for magic power, as long as I have money, I’ll have an endless supply.”

“Only my Dao Heart level can’t be raised in an instant, no matter how much money I have…”

Thinking of this, Zhang Yu couldn’t help looking forward to the upgrade effects of the Heavenly Tribulation Chapter. He then thought of the contents of the Immortal Foundation Chapter and the Great Sage Chapter, and mused, “I wonder which direction the Feather Book will upgrade in next time. It would be great if I could choose.”

As he considered this, Zhang Yu felt that it might not be impossible.

In his view, the Feather Book reflected his potential. Its various functions had always been remarkably user-friendly, and had grown increasingly refined with each upgrade.

“I had no choice before — maybe it was simply because there were no directions to choose from.”

“But now, as I keep integrating new books into it, different upgrade paths are gradually opening up.”

Just as Zhang Yu’s true body was patiently studying the Feather Book, his gaze suddenly shifted slightly.

Through his perception, Fa Liuyuan had found him and was communicating with one of his clones.

“Profound Monarch Zhang.”

Fa Liuyuan smiled at Zhang Yu and said, “I still remember when you stopped the Heaven Collapse at the Former Days Graveyard, and I taught you a technique. Who would have thought that in the blink of an eye, you’d have grown to where you are now? Not only have you broken through to the Tribulation Transcendence Realm, but you’ve also accomplished one astonishing feat after another.”

“Now, you’ve taken on the vital responsibility of creating the Chain Formation Diagram — a task that will affect countless cultivators throughout Kunxu…”

Listening to Fa Liuyuan’s string of compliments, the Sect Examination Clone grew wary. *“He’s laying it on so thick. He’s definitely up to no good.”*

Immortal Slayer sighed. *“He’s probably here to talk us into something, too. Maybe he’s weighed our past actions and decided Fa Liuyuan is someone who can get through to us.”*

*“After all… he did give us the Feather Book. From that perspective, we owe him a favor.”*

Watching Fa Liuyuan lavish praise on him, Zhang Yu couldn’t help but give a wry smile. “Heavenly Monarch Fa, please get straight to the point. If you need my help, I’ll do whatever I can.”

Fa Liuyuan said earnestly, “Minister Zhang, I’d like to ask what you think of the real estate and construction industries across Kunxu, as well as the Kunlun Dao Lineage.”

Zhang Yu thought for a moment. “As far as the sect is concerned, there’s no prospect of large-scale infrastructure development.”

“As for the lower realm, there was a lot of upheaval there in recent years, with several rounds of renovations. But now, it’s more or less finished…”

Zhang Yu didn’t continue, but Fa Liuyuan understood what he meant. Clearly, Zhang Yu believed that both real estate and construction were limited in scale.

Zhang Yu continued, “As for the Kunlun Dao Lineage, from what I’ve observed, it’s unlikely to change much in the short term.”

Fa Liuyuan nodded. “That’s right. At present, the various cultivation realms in Kunxu are relatively fixed, and the Great Sects aren’t likely to undertake large-scale construction projects. The industry’s profits are limited, so naturally the Kunlun Dao Lineage isn’t given much attention. The Dao Masters aren’t doing as well as they used to, either.”

“However…” Fa Liuyuan looked at Zhang Yu. “The Chain Formation Diagram you create could change that.”

Zhang Yu replied calmly, “I’m all ears.”

Fa Liuyuan said, “If the Chain Formation Diagram used by everyone in their cultivation could change their body size, making cultivators with greater overall strength and higher cultivation realms grow larger — and the larger their bodies, the stronger they become…”

“Then Golden Core cultivators would be as vast as mountains, Nascent Soul cultivators would tower into the clouds, and those at the Void Refinement, Tribulation Transcendence, and Ascension stages would be like mountains and seas. The entire Kunxu would be transformed.”

Zhang Yu paused slightly at this. “Body size? To grow that large wouldn’t just mean changing one’s size — it would mean completely changing one’s structure, wouldn’t it?”

“Exactly,” said Fa Liuyuan. “The structure would certainly change. Starting at the Golden Core stage, each person would be a city. The stronger they were, the larger the city. The spiritual energy and magic power they consumed, the materials needed for magical artifacts and Bionics, even the space they occupied… all of it would have to increase dramatically.”

“What would that mean? The stronger someone became, the larger the city they transformed into, and the more Immortal Dao resources they would need to consume. They would have to make more transactions with all of Kunxu, greatly increasing the circulation of resources throughout Kunxu.”

“At the same time, the drastic structural changes among all cultivators in Kunxu would also make the existing infrastructure obsolete.”

“Whether it’s roads, buildings, all kinds of spatial transport devices, transportation equipment, office equipment, cultivation equipment... everything will be due for a major upgrade.”

“When that time comes, a cultivator’s breakthrough and rise in strength will mean the expansion and renewal of an entire megacity. Every high-tier cultivator’s progress will bring a major wave of infrastructure construction and drive an economic boom.”

Fa Liuyuan said excitedly, “Zhang Yu, if you can create a Chain Formation Diagram like this, all of Kunxu will be transformed.”

Hearing this, Immortal Slayer analyzed the situation inwardly: *“Indeed. If what he says is true, for a time... all of Kunxu will provide a huge number of jobs and consumer outlets. Upper-tier cultivators will spend much more, and a considerable portion of all kinds of resources will trickle down.”*

The Sect Examination Clone sneered. *“Heh, isn’t this just about bringing back the glory days of Civil Engineering? Building more houses to make money? Propping up the Kunlun Dao Lineage? If they really go through with this, every cultivator will be turning themselves into a city as they cultivate. Everyone will end up in Civil Engineering.”*

*“Hmph, Civil Engineering is Civil Engineering. Even the Civil Engineering Ancestor is the same — once you get into it, you’re stuck in Civil Engineering for life, always wanting to build houses.”*

*“If you ask me, isn’t the whole point of building houses to sell them? Why can’t he just sell them directly and not worry about whether they’ve been built?”*

Immortal Slayer said, *“That’s called fraud!”*

Fu Ji said, *“What’s wrong with fraud? Fraud can survive across historical eras and economic cycles. It’s a booming industry that will never disappear. Can Civil Engineering say the same?”*

At the same time, Fa Liuyuan said, “Zhang Yu, I know this isn’t something you can decide lightly. But the Reformist Faction and all the Dao Masters of the Kunlun Dao Lineage are willing to support you.”

“If you’re willing to do this, the two exalted Immortals are willing to recommend you for the Ascension Selection in the future. And after you achieve immortality, the various Dao Masters are willing to let you take charge of part of the Kunlun Dao Lineage’s authority.”

The Sect Examination Clone said, “The Reformist Faction and the Kunlun Dao Lineage are too weak, so they’re offering a higher price. You can also tell they’re really eager to restart large-scale Civil Engineering and infrastructure projects within the sect.”

A moment later, after Fa Liuyuan left, another Ascension Realm cultivator got in touch with Zhang Yu.

The visitor was none other than Su Xianchen, President of the Law Edict Management Committee.

This Ascension Realm cultivator had already served as a judge when Zhang Yu took part in the Law Establishment.

He himself was also a key researcher in Immortal Qi Technology and one of the core members of the third-generation Law Edict development team.

Looking at Zhang Yu before him, Su Xianchen didn’t say much either, and got straight to the point: “Minister Zhang, there are a few things I’d like to discuss with you.”

“Mainly, Immortal Venerable Profound Wisdom hopes you can add more content related to Immortal Qi to the Chain Method Diagram Set, so that more Kunxu Cultivators can experience the intricacies of Immortal Qi and apply Immortal Qi Technology.”

“We can discuss the specific terms. I’m just here to ask your opinion; I’m not trying to pressure you. If you have any concerns, you can tell me,” Su Xianchen said with a smile. “I can pass messages from them to you, and from you to them.”

Zhang Yu asked in confusion, “But as far as I know, aren’t all the cultivation arts related to Immortal Qi currently within the sect classified as sect secrets?”

Su Xianchen had clearly anticipated Zhang Yu’s question. He smiled slightly and said, “If you, Minister Zhang, are willing to add cultivation arts involving Immortal Qi Technology to the Chain Formation Diagram, then we can put some of the Immortal Qi cultivation arts up for sale to the public.”

“Then we can establish a joint-stock company to hold the complete copyrights to the cultivation arts, and you, Minister Zhang, would naturally receive a share of the copyright as well.”

“As more and more cultivators begin to use the Chain Formation Diagram to sense the mysteries of Immortal Qi, our Myriad Laws Sect will be able to establish new basic technical standards for Law Edicts across all of Kunxu.”

Watching Su Xianchen’s retreating figure, Fu Ji couldn’t help sighing. “They’re giving us a share of the profits directly? If this takes off, how much could we earn every year?”

The Sect Examination Clone said, “Immortal Qi Technology has an extremely high barrier to entry. If it’s incorporated into the Chain Formation Diagram, its sales will inevitably lag behind those of more broadly applicable cultivation arts.”

Meanwhile, as Zhang Yu sensed his connections with more and more cultivators, countless conditions, demands, and suggestions also flashed through his mind...

Zhang Yu sighed. “Sigh, everyone wants to get involved. They all want to use their own will to influence the world and, in the process, accumulate Immortal Dao resources.”

Immortal Slayer said, “It’s also a test. They want to communicate with us to confirm what we — and the Immortal Emperor behind us — actually have in mind.”

Zhang Yu nodded slightly, knowing that Immortal Slayer was right.

By now, many people throughout the Myriad Laws Sect had probably come to see him as the representative of the Immortal Emperor’s will.

In their eyes, although Zhang Yu held the power to create the Chain Formation Diagram, the final decision... still rested in the hands of the Immortal Emperor.

“They’ve all been coming to talk with me, hoping to use me to find out what the Immortal Emperor intends, right? I should probably report to the Immortal Emperor now.”

As he spoke, Zhang Yu’s thoughts stirred, and he opened his mailbox.

“Cultivation Exam Clone, Fu Ji, Herald God, Auspicious God… It’s your turn to get to work.”

Zhang Yu said, “Report the current situation and everyone’s opinions to the Immortal Emperor. Then try asking whether he has any suggestions.”

Zhang Yu understood that although the Immortal Emperor had said he was leaving the matter to him, if he really dared to make every decision himself, that would be taking matters into his own hands.

“If I want to create a Chain Formation Diagram, there’s no way I can ignore the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor’s opinion.”

While Zhang Yu was thinking, a reply from the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor had already arrived in his mailbox.

The reply was simple, containing not a single word, just a basic cultivation art.

“Just one basic cultivation art?”

Zhang Yu thought, “So this is the Immortal Emperor’s suggestion? The Chain Formation Diagram should include this cultivation art?”

After looking over the basic cultivation art before him, Zhang Yu breathed a sigh of relief. “It seems I have a lot of freedom. The Immortal Emperor is truly magnanimous and knows how to delegate!”

Just as Zhang Yu was secretly feeling exhilarated, a notice about changes to copyright suddenly appeared before his eyes.

“Hmm? I’ve directly gained the right to use another cultivation art? A cultivation art from the Vajra Sect?”

Just as Zhang Yu was wondering about it, another mail suddenly appeared in his inbox, automatically pinned above the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor’s.

The moment he saw who had sent it, Zhang Yu’s heart gave a jolt.

“The Buddha Emperor?”

“Is this a scam?”

But before Zhang Yu could open the Buddha Emperor’s mail, he sensed even more changes.

One cultivation art after another was added to his name without requiring his consent.

At the same time, the number of pinned mails in his inbox kept growing.

“Heavenly Sword Immortal Emperor, Reincarnation Immortal Emperor, Celestial Monster Immortal Emperor...”

Before he knew it, ten mails from ten Immortal Emperors had all been pinned at the top of Zhang Yu’s inbox.

There were no messages or voice recordings in the mails, yet they weighed on him like ten enormous mountains.

As Zhang Yu opened the mails one by one, he saw that each contained a basic cultivation art.

“Did the ten Immortal Emperors each choose one cultivation art?”

At that moment, with all ten mails having arrived, an illusion rose in Zhang Yu’s heart: it was as if ten pairs of eyes had pierced through time and space simultaneously, fixing their gaze on him.

Zhang Yu now understood. When it came to creating the Chain Formation Diagram, his body and his will had long since lost all autonomy. He had to create it according to the choices of the ten Immortal Emperors.

“Ten Immortal Emperors — so you decided this long ago?”

“But what kind of Chain Formation Diagram are you trying to create?”

With a grave expression, Zhang Yu began cultivating the arts the Ten Immortal Emperors had given him.

Once he finished cultivating them, he would use the Technique-Chaining Comprehensive Manual to see what effects the Chain Formation Diagram formed from these 10 cultivation arts would have.
