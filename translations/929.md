# Chapter 929: Specially Bestowed by the Immortal Emperor, the Myriad Laws Divine Sword

If he weren't facing a desperate situation, Ban Jinchen naturally wouldn't want to surrender, wouldn't want to admit defeat, and wouldn't want to give up.

But watching his company's stock price continuously plummet, watching his assets continuously evaporate, and watching his friends block him one by one...

He felt as if a pair of giant hands were squeezing his Dantian and Primordial Spirit, forcing him to watch his Immortal Dao Potential collapse and dissipate bit by bit.

Meanwhile, all the various connections, human networks, and immortal networks he had built up in the past... all abandoned him without hesitation, and even wanted to turn around and bite off a piece of his flesh.

Even when he asked Celestial Inquiry Star how to counter the Law Edict Expiration Date, the other party replied to him: "I cannot answer this question for you at the moment. Let's change the subject."

"Even the membership fee was paid for nothing, who else can I trust?"

At this moment, Ban Jinchen completely recognized reality; the situation was already set in stone.

And after pleading everywhere to no avail, Ban Jinchen ultimately thought of Zhang Yu.

"Should I just kill him?"

Murderous intent had also crossed Ban Jinchen's mind. After all, theoretically speaking... killing someone only required paying a fine, but having his own Law Edict frozen was a catastrophic event that would cost him his entire fortune.

But this thought only lingered in his mind for a moment before he completely cast it aside.

"No, he's too highly sought after right now. Who knows how many people would protect him? Even if I kill him, there will likely be plenty of people willing to pay to resurrect him, and I'd face a reckoning and retaliation afterward."

"I still have to find a way to beg him."

"At the very least, I must beg him before Si Shian does, and beg Zhang Yu to freeze Si Shian's *Four-Hour Workday Law Edict* first next year, and freeze my Law Edict later."

According to the intelligence Ban Jinchen had obtained, for Zhang Yu to freeze someone else's Law Edict, he first needed to study the underlying technical principles of the Law Edict, and then cover it with his Law Edict True Domain.

If the other party wanted to freeze a Law Edict, he had to study it first and then overlay it, a process that would inevitably consume a lot of time.

"In that case, the order in which the Law Edicts are frozen is quite significant."

And in Ban Jinchen's view, dying in Kunxu wasn't terrifying; what was truly terrifying was not being able to make money or participate in Kunxu's economic activities. That was true death.

"Even for an Immortal, unless they reach a cultivation realm where all the common people of the world are reduced to a useless class, otherwise... if they stop seeking external resources, stop participating in the governance of Kunxu, and their assets cease to inflate and grow, then they are effectively dead."

In Ban Jinchen's eyes, this was the exact same thing as someone practicing Immortal Cultivation refusing to use breathing techniques and rejecting the absorption of the world's spiritual energy.

The deeper one participated in Kunxu's economic activities and the more profit they extracted from it, the stronger their body's ability to absorb nutrients became. Just like having a higher efficiency in using breathing techniques to absorb the world's spiritual energy on the Immortal Dao, this was the common pursuit of all the people in the world.

Now that the dust had settled and the Law Edict was destined to be frozen, if Ban Jinchen wanted to do his utmost to preserve his own interests, it seemed there was only one way left...

"How should I go about begging Zhang Yu?"

Ban Jinchen's mind rapidly reviewed all sorts of information regarding Zhang Yu.

He quickly remembered Zhang Yu's sentimental nature — his care for old subordinates and former classmates, as well as his protection over lower realm cultivators.

Last time, it was precisely because of this weakness that he had chosen to target Shi Yunxiang.

This time, he could also use this same weakness and make a move on Shi Yunxiang again.

Seeing Ban Jinchen first kneel to Shi Yunxiang, and then kowtow to Zhang Yu while calling him Dad, Fu Ji said disdainfully, "You think you can ask for a favor just by kneeling and calling someone Dad? There's no such cheap bargain!"

Zhang Yu merely gave Ban Jinchen a faint glance, then turned his head to look in another direction.

In his Spirit Realm vision, Si Shian was prostrating himself, shouting loudly, "Grandpa, please save your grandson."

Zhang Yu thought to himself, "These guys... really don't surprise me at all."

As far as Zhang Yu was concerned, for this bunch of Sect Staff from the upper realm of Kunxu, surrendering, begging for mercy, and selling out their teammates was simply second nature.

With a mere thought from Zhang Yu, his Spirit Realm vision was shared with both parties.

Thus, Ban Jinchen and Si Shian instantly saw each other's presence: "Why are you here?"

"Didn't we agree not to surrender?"

"You secretly sought out Dao Monarch Zhang to negotiate terms behind my back?"

On the other side, Si Shian sneered, "Well played, Ban Jinchen. Hugging thighs? Pretending to be pitiful? You have a lot of nerve. Are you trying to get Dao Monarch Zhang to get things done without spending a single coin?"

His Law Edict should be the first to be frozen as a warning to others!"

"I have already reported your transfers. I will not accept a single coin."

When Zhang Yu said these words, his heart was bleeding.

But he understood that since he had been pushed out by the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor to establish the expiration date for Law Edicts, and was currently the center of attention, he absolutely could not accept money from other Law Establishment cultivators.

"Otherwise, I'd be slapping the Immortal Emperor's face..."
