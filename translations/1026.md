Watching this scene unfold, a spark of realization flashed through Herald God's mind: *"An ordinary person could only choose one path, but for Zhang Yu, choosing three directions at once is nothing. "Let's go, Fu Ji. Come with me, and together we shall deceive this world."

On the other side, the Auspicious God took the initiative to run toward Immortal Slayer, thinking to himself, *"I've already found the best choice. The Herald God silently followed behind Desolate Ox, thinking to himself, *"Following Immortal Slayer or the Sect Examination Clone would just make me an assistant. As the Herald God followed Desolate Ox along the way, he discovered that this Sea of Consciousness World was self-sufficient and orderly; no one seemed to need to hustle or compete for basic survival.

The Herald God thought to himself, *"The technological level of this world is probably on par with the sect, but because the Desolate Ox Great Sage's collective will leans toward an ancient style, the living environment is far more relaxed than that of the real sect, and many areas appear rather backwards."*

"From the looks of it, even though this world was constructed within the Desolate Ox Great Sage's Sea of Consciousness, it simulates the physical world almost perfectly. Feeling the difference in the flow of time between this place and the physical world, the Herald God thought to himself, *"However, time has been accelerated. Time here flows faster than in the outside world and is continuously speeding up... Right now, if an hour passes outside, several months elapse here. Was this also caused by the Celestial Monster Immortal Emperor?"*

Glancing at Desolate Ox, who was rapidly sifting through information at high speed, the Herald God noted in his mind, *"There is also the effect of one's cultivation in reality... Although one's consciousness possesses no combat strength after entering this world, a higher cultivation grants greater computational power, allowing for far swifter actions across the board here."*

Shaking his head, the Herald God continued his analysis.

"Because of Desolate Ox Great Sage's origins, his collective will contains both the Human Race and monsterfolk, though monsterfolk are greater in number."

"At the same time, the history of this Sea of Consciousness World shows obvious signs of manipulation."

As the Herald God observed, he analyzed the situation, gradually piecing together a complete picture of this world in his mind. "So that's how it is. It's likely that the ten Immortal Emperors sealed Desolate Ox's collective will here, and then drove this world forward continuously, step by step causing Desolate Ox's collective will to fracture incessantly."

"From the very beginning, when they were united as one for the sake of survival and construction, to the present... each part of the collective will has developed different pursuits."

"And because of this excessive division, Desolate Ox Great Sage's will could no longer coalesce, plunging him entirely into a deep slumber."

"In other words, the Celestial Monster Sect wants to corrupt the Desolate Ox's collective will, reunify its thoughts, and awaken a brand-new version of Desolate Ox with entirely new ideologies?"
