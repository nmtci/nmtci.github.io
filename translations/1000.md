# Chapter 1000: Do Not Believe!

The 17th layer of Kunxu.

Inside an Immortal Grotto-Heaven also created by Ying Xintian.

Tai Yuebai looked up at the sky, seemingly gazing toward the upper layers of Kunxu of unknown heights, a layer of worry flashing in his eyes.

"I wonder how things are going with Zhang Yu meeting the Immortal Emperor..."

Recalling everything that had happened before, Tai Yuebai sighed inwardly. "Why would the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor suddenly summon Zhang Yu? Could he have noticed something?"

What Tai Yuebai was most worried about at this moment was that the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor had discovered their secret — that they had uncovered the existence of the rollback, or even that they had already received messages from the future.

"Knowing of the rollback's existence is currently our only advantage if we want to defeat the Ten Immortal Emperors and prevent the Ultimate Doomsday."

"It is also the final help Senior Supreme Truth left us."

"If even this is discovered by the Immortal Emperor... we probably won't have any chance of winning."

Thinking of this, Tai Yuebai looked toward a cluster of information data left behind by Zhang Yu.

Tai Yuebai thought to himself, "Zhang Yu purged some of his crucial memories, then scrambled, reorganized, and encrypted them, ultimately storing them in different locations to prevent the Immortal Emperor from probing critical intelligence."

"On top of that, it is thanks to the Immortal Dao information technology sent over by Senior Ying..."

Tai Yuebai knew that part of the Immortal Dao information technology Ying Xintian had transmitted this time originated from the Reincarnation Immortal Emperor.

And among the Ten Immortal Emperors, the Reincarnation Immortal Emperor was renowned as number one in intelligence, having always been dedicated to cracking the investigative and surveillance methods of the Great Sects and Immortal Emperors.

"The Reincarnation Immortal Emperor has long studied and cracked... the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor's information technology as well."

"It was also with the Reincarnation Immortal Emperor's support that Senior Ying was able to develop information technology specifically targeted at the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor."

"This way, relying on the restrictions we set before Zhang Yu's audience with the Immortal Emperor, if Zhang Yu's memory is scanned or if surveillance measures are placed on him during the audience with the Immortal Emperor... we should be able to discover it upon examining him after his return."

At the same time, Tai Yuebai recalled Ying Xintian's assessment of Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor.

"Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor... He's a control freak. He wants to manage heaven and earth, Gods and Immortals — he wants to control everything, to hold everything in his grasp."

"Yet he also understands that if cultivators knew their every move was monitored, it would only plunge all living beings into despair and lower society's Immortal Dao productivity."

"Still, that doesn't mean Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor would ever give up governing the world. He simply changed his approach: it's not that he isn't controlling them, but rather that he makes all his subjects... believe they aren't being completely controlled."

Tai Yuebai understood that if, upon returning from his audience with the Immortal Emperor, Zhang Yu was found to be under surveillance and watched by Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor, this crucial part of his memories could not be returned to him for the time being.

"When that time comes, we can only temporarily... use Zhang Yu as a decoy to draw the Immortal Emperor's attention."

"Only when an opportunity arises in the future will we restore this part of Zhang Yu's memories."

Lost in recollection, Tai Yuebai cast his gaze toward the depths of his immortal abode, resting his eyes upon that mass of information from the future that was ticking down continuously.

"This information sent by Zhang Yu... during this period, I am the only one who can keep it safe. The Immortal Emperors must never be allowed to obtain it."

Tai Yuebai understood clearly just how crucial and vital this information from the future was.

He thought to himself, "As controllers of the Supreme Immortal Artifacts, the Ten Immortal Emperors will inevitably carry information about the future after the World Rollback... returning to the past, to the world after the rollback."

"As for how much information they can carry, how much of their own future memories they can bring along... perhaps all of it, perhaps only a part."

"Even if the Immortal Emperors possess all their own future memories, that doesn't mean they know everything about the future."

"After all, even the Immortal Emperors are not omniscient."

Looking at the future information ticking down before him, Tai Yuebai felt a surge of quiet excitement. "Before the World Rollback, even though the Ten Immortal Emperors, as high-ranking cultivators standing at the pinnacle of Kunxu, possessed an immensely vast perspective, it is still impossible for them to know everything."

Tai Yuebai could think of many examples of this, such as the message passed by Senior Supreme Truth last time, which was a blind spot that had completely flown under the Immortal Emperors' radar.

"Even if their perspective transcends time itself, there are bound to be systemic vulnerabilities."

"If this intel transmitted from the future really was sent by Zhang Yu... it might contain data points completely dark to the Immortal Emperors."

"By grasping this information, we hold the information advantage."

"On the flip side, if an Immortal Emperor gets their hands on it, it will patch the blind spots in their future-sight and grant them an even bigger competitive edge..."

The next moment, the image of Tai Yuebai froze, and the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor withdrew his gaze from it.

Then, before the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor, the scenes began to replay — scenes of Tai Yuebai coordinating with Zhang Yu, scenes of him communicating with Ying Xintian, scenes of Tai Yuebai pondering alone, and scenes of him focusing on the information from the future...

Next, the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor looked toward the images on the other side, across which countless figures of Zhang Yu were flickering.

Among them, beginning right from the moment Zhang Yu entered the 33rd layer, was everything that could be detected both inside and outside his body.

All around the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor, countless other images flickered as well.

As the figures of one cultivator after another emerged within them, they were like blinking surveillance feeds that ultimately transformed into the trillions of stars in the darkness, forming the Sea of Myriad Laws that Zhang Yu had seen.

At this moment, the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor's consciousness was connected to this Sea of Myriad Laws, sensing the information across countless images before finally locking onto the scene of that future message in Tai Yuebai's Grotto-Heaven.

"So this is where Zhang Yu's letter to Us was?"

The Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor's consciousness gently vibrated in the void like a string bridging worlds.

The next moment, accompanied by a flicker among the trillions of stars in the Sea of Myriad Laws, both the layered spatial defenses and the encryption methods that even massive computing power could hardly crack were silently penetrated by the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor.

In the very next instant, the contents within that clump of future information... had already been read.

It was even directly copied... and then unfolded within the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor's consciousness.

Unlike Zhang Yu and Tai Yuebai, as one of the controllers of the Supreme Immortal Artifact, the information read by the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor... neither disappeared nor was forgotten by him. It flowed within his consciousness as though he was naturally meant to remember it all along.

With a faint smile, the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor separated a wisp of his consciousness, which traversed across trillions of starlights to descend upon... Zhang Yu, who had just arrived at the 33rd layer and the Sea of Myriad Laws not long ago.

After a brief conversation, he looked at Zhang Yu and sighed with emotion, "Having read the letter you wrote this time, We are deeply gratified."

Stepping out of the Immortal Gate, Zhang Yu had already returned to the 16th layer.

Recalling his experience of going to the 33rd layer this time, Zhang Yu felt as if everything had been an illusion of a dream.

In particular, the words the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor threw out at the very end still left Zhang Yu's mind churning with agitation, unable to stop pondering them over and over.

"A way to return to Earth?"

"Does the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor truly know?"

"If it is true... then what exactly is the relationship between Earth and Kunxu? Are they in the same universe? Or different universes? Or..."

The thoughts in Zhang Yu's mind grew increasingly active, all sorts of ideas surging uncontrollably.

Even though Zhang Yu had spent so many years in Kunxu, the longer he stayed, the more disappointed he grew with the place.

"If I could go back to Earth, taking A-Zhen, Yu Xinghan, Teacher Magnetic Pole, Teacher Gao... taking everyone back to Earth together..."

Scenes of returning to Earth with his companions flashed through Zhang Yu's mind — feasting, drinking, having fun, no longer having to worry about the Immortal Emperor's oppression, no longer fearing world crises, and free from the constant revulsion caused by a twisted Kunxu...

With his current strength, he would even be invincible upon returning to Earth, more than capable of reshaping the entire planet into the world he desired, turning it into a paradise...

The next moment, however, Immortal Slayer's voice interrupted Zhang Yu's reverie: "Zhang Yu, don't let Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor mess with your mind! He's lying to you! He's trying to erode your will!"

The Sect Examination Clone said, "Really? I think Grandpa Immortal Emperor actually makes a lot of sense."

"Let him become the Immortal Emperor of the End first. Once Kunxu becomes meaningless to him, we can step up and become the Immortal Emperors," the Sect Examination Clone mused. "When the time comes, we can learn how to reach Earth through Grandpa Immortal Emperor, travel between the two worlds, and live like a Homelander who doesn't eat beef... Wouldn't that be a blast?"

Immortal Slayer gave a cold snort. "That's just handing all the power of decision over to the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor. Why should we trust him?"

The Sect Examination Clone replied, "Just how formidable is Grandpa Immortal Emperor? What we've seen today is merely the tip of the iceberg, yet it's already left us utterly helpless. Do you really think we have a choice? Trusting the Sovereign Father is our only choice as subjects."

Zhang Yu could sense that this audience with the Immortal Emperor had truly supercharged the Sect Examination Clone's Sect Nature, pushing his loyalty to an unrivaled extreme.

Immortal Slayer argued, "The sacrifices of countless seniors, a relay passed down through generations of cultivators — only through that did we get this tiny sliver of a chance today! How could we give it up so easily?"

Letting Immortal Slayer and the Sect Examination Clone continue bickering in his mind, Zhang Yu recalled the process of meeting the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor. "It seems like he didn't notice any flaws? Nor did he place any means of control over me?"

"On the contrary, he rewarded me with quite a few things."

Thinking of the Hundred-Billion Flying Swords and the Palace Gate Restriction within his body, the magic power inside Zhang Yu surged even faster once more.

Right at that moment, a figure suddenly rushed up from the side; it was none other than Bu Yingshu, who had been waiting here all along.

"Zhang Yu..."

Looking at Zhang Yu before her, a subtle shift seemed to appear in Bu Yingshu's gaze.

She asked softly, "Did you... finish meeting with the Immortal Emperor?"

Seeing Zhang Yu nod, Bu Yingshu quickly followed up, "Why did the Immortal Emperor summon you?"

Zhang Yu pondered slightly, not answering right away.

Seeing Zhang Yu like this, Bu Yingshu's heart sank halfway.

*"Zhang Yu only went to the 33rd layer once, and now he's giving me the cold shoulder? He won't even share a bit of information with me?"*

Bu Yingshu gave a cold snort in her heart and shifted the topic, "Then come join the meeting quickly."

Zhang Yu said, "I still have some business to attend to. I'm afraid I can't make it for now."

Hearing this, Bu Yingshu couldn't help but frown again. "But... several Immortal Clan representatives are still waiting."

Just then, Zhang Yu received a message from Tai Yuebai. His eyes flickered, and he said to Bu Yingshu beside him, "Profound Monarch Bu, I have some matters to attend to. I'll contact you later."

Watching Zhang Yu disappear in an instant, Bu Yingshu froze slightly, and in the next moment, a raging fury surged within her heart.

*"So many representatives are waiting. If he doesn't go... won't my face be completely ruined in front of the Immortal Clan?"*

*"Zhang Yu... does he think he's climbed up the ladder and now only wants to cling to the Immortal Emperor's coattails, losing all interest in mine?"*

*"After all my wholehearted efforts to cultivate him, acting as his Promotion Accelerator, pulling strings for his promotions, and lending him my Immortal Shuttle, now he just turns around and acts like he doesn't know me..."*

Thoughts raced through her mind. Watching Zhang Yu's disappearing figure, Bu Yingshu was consumed with rage.

Bu Yingshu immediately contacted Bu Yuangui and shouted, "Dad! I want to go meet the Immortal Emperor too."

Bu Yuangui replied, "Why don't you come meet your dad instead?"

Bu Yingshu retorted, "What use are you? You're not the Immortal Emperor!"

Bu Yuangui said, "So you do know your dad isn't the Immortal Emperor. Stop constantly trying to compare yourself to Zhang Yu. He's different..."

Because of Bu Yingshu's previous inquiries, Bu Yuangui knew that Zhang Yu was heading to the 33rd layer to meet the Immortal Emperor.

Hearing Bu Yingshu's words now, Bu Yuangui quickly guessed what had happened.

Bu Yuangui continued, "Being granted an audience with the Immortal Emperor proves how much the Immortal Emperor values him. I'm afraid he will be entrusted with heavy responsibilities moving forward."

"You might even have to cling to his coattails in the future."

"Me? Cling to him?" Hearing this, Bu Yingshu said in shocked fury, "Is this still the land of Myriad Laws?"

Bu Yuangui replied, "This is Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor's land. Maybe it won't just be you... Dad might have to cling to him along with you when the time comes."

On the other side, after Zhang Yu and Tai Yuebai met, Tai Yuebai nodded slightly. "I checked the restrictions. The Immortal Emperor indeed probed you, but he shouldn't have left behind any surveillance methods."

After confirming this, Zhang Yu used the information he had left with Tai Yuebai to retrieve his scattered, encrypted memories from Tai Yuebai, Bai Zhenzhen, Yu Xinghan, the Supreme Clarity Realm, and Fu Ji, successfully reorganizing them once more.

Feeling the memories reviving in his mind, various pieces of crucial information regarding the World Rollback, the Feather Book, Immortal Emperor Supreme Truth, Ying Xintian, and the World-Domain Dao Lineage all returned to his thoughts at this moment.

"Phew..."

Zhang Yu recalled the process of meeting Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor once more, and Tai Yuebai asked from the side, "How did it go?"

Zhang Yu pondered for a moment, reviewing every single second of his meeting with the Immortal Emperor. Only after a long while did he say, "I didn't sense any issues. I shouldn't have given anything away."

Tai Yuebai said, "Recount the meeting to me from start to finish. Let me hear it..."

After discussing it thoroughly, Zhang Yu looked at Tai Yuebai and asked, "Big Brother Tai, what about you? Is there any issue with the future information?"

Tai Yuebai shook his head. "I've been keeping an eye on it the entire time. Nothing happened. It seems we've made it through the hurdle of meeting the Immortal Emperor for now."

Zhang Yu said, "According to what Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor said, absolute surveillance only breeds despair. Since he wants me to push for a higher cultivation realm, he is bound to give me some leeway. On top of that, he doesn't know yet... that we know about the rollback. That's why he's granting me immense leeway and tolerance... Thinking about it this way, it makes sense."

Tai Yuebai nodded, but still cautioned, "Be careful. Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor's desire for control is exceptionally strong right now. Even if he didn't take control of you this time, there's no telling when he might make a move in the future..."

After their conversation, Zhang Yu bid farewell to Tai Yuebai, a surge of fighting spirit welling up within his heart.

And now that their memories of the crucial information had been restored, the attitudes of Immortal Slayer and the Sect Examination Clone had also undergone a significant change.

Immortal Slayer said, "Being able to hide information right before the Immortal Emperor today is our first victory. It proves that the Immortal Emperor is not invincible and does not control everything."

The Sect Examination Clone analyzed, "Hahahaha, since the Immortal Emperor is so arrogant and indulges us like this, we must make full use of his leniency. We'll maximize the power in our hands, using the Technique-Chaining Comprehensive Manual, the Hundred-Billion Flying Swords, and the Palace Gate Restriction to leverage even greater Immortal Dao resources... Given time, it's not impossible for us to ascend to the Supreme Seat."

Meanwhile, Zhang Yu returned to the Immortal Grotto-Heaven to retrieve Fu Ji and the other Evil Gods.

Fu Ji hurriedly asked, "Zhang Yu, how did it go?"

Zhang Yu casually shared what he could.

Even so, when Fu Ji, the Auspicious God, and the Herald God heard about the handling of the Technique-Chaining Comprehensive Manual, as well as the news regarding the Hundred-Billion Flying Swords and the Palace Gate Restriction, they were shocked to the extreme.

Fu Ji said excitedly, "Wahahaha! A Hundred-Billion Flying Swords, plus the right to see the sovereign at any time! Who in the Myriad Laws Sect can stand in our way now?"

The Auspicious God thought in alarm, *"This fellow Zhang Yu... is about to hold absolute sway over the Myriad Laws Sect!"*

The Herald God's heart trembled. *"...With such a lavish reward, the return on investment that the Immortal Emperor expects from Zhang Yu must be terrifyingly high — something Zhang Yu might not be able to accomplish even if he gambles his life."*

After finishing his series of tasks, Zhang Yu contacted Bu Yingshu once again. "Profound Monarch Bu, are the Immortal Clan representatives still there?"

Seeing no reply from Bu Yingshu, Zhang Yu could not help but say, "Fu Ji, you send her a message."

Pausing for a moment, Zhang Yu added a reminder. "Profound Monarch Bu still has her uses after all. Make sure to coax her nicely."

Fu Ji patted her chest and said, "I know. How could any other member of the Immortal Clan be as easy to control and as trustworthy as her?"

"Don't worry, when it comes to fooling idiots, I'm a professional."

Inside Bu Yingshu's Immortal Abode, several Immortal Clan representatives were currently complaining.

"Yingshu, what is going on with Zhang Yu? Why hasn't he shown up yet?"

"Does he think that just because he is presiding over the Book Collection Plan, he can look down on the entire Immortal Clan?"

"A mere Tribulation Transcendence cultivator, yet he expects us to wait for him?"

The projections of the five Immortal Clan representatives chimed in one after another, venting their complaints.

Listening to them, Bu Yingshu frowned slightly, at a loss for what to do. She thought to herself, *"Am I supposed to go beg Zhang Yu to come over?"*

Just then, one of the representatives spoke up. "Yingshu, didn't you say that you personally groomed that Zhang Yu, and that he has always been obedient to your every word? What happened? Can't even you get him to come over now?"

Hearing this, Bu Yingshu felt a surge of secret anger—not because the other party was talking nonsense, but because they had hit the nail on the head.

*"I'm so mad! That guy Zhang Yu made me lose face in front of so many people."*

*"What should I do? What should I do?"*

Just as Bu Yingshu was racking her brains over how to settle the situation before her, she received a message from Zhang Yu.

"Humph, so you finally thought to reach out to me?"

While Bu Yingshu was fretting over how to reply, the other party sent another message.

Zhang Yu (Fu Ji): "Profound Monarch Bu, no matter how high I climb in the Myriad Laws Sect in the future, I will never surpass you. You will always be my old leader."

Bu Yingshu couldn't help pursing her lips slightly, her heart already blooming with joy: *"I knew it! Dad misjudged him again."*

Zhang Yu (Fu Ji): "Is the meeting being held at your Immortal Abode? I'll come over right away..."

The grievance in her heart melted away instantly like ice and snow. Bu Yingshu replied: "Mm."

At the same time Bu Yingshu was contacting Zhang Yu, she gave a gentle pulse of the magic power in her body and said calmly to the surrounding representatives, "Zhang Yu was caught up with something just now, so he'll be arriving a bit late."

Immediately, another representative shouted, "What could be more important than us? Is this still the land of Myriad Laws or not?"

Bu Yingshu gave a cold snort and said, "This is the territory of the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor! What? Zhang Yu just went to the 33rd layer for an audience with the Immortal Emperor. Are you implying... that Zhang Yu should have come to see you first and kept the Immortal Emperor waiting?"

"I... That's not what I meant..."

"Yingshu! Is what you're saying true? Zhang Yu went for an audience with the Immortal Emperor?"

Feeling the surprised, shocked, and incredulous expressions of the representatives all around her, a satisfied smile appeared on Bu Yingshu's face.

While continuing her communication with Zhang Yu (Fu Ji), she went on to say to the representatives, "Yes, Zhang Yu told me himself. He wouldn't lie to me."

"He said... he went to the 33rd layer to discuss the new policies regarding the Books with the Immortal Emperor."

One representative said in shock, "Discussing new policies with the Immortal Emperor?"

Bu Yingshu waved her hand with a helpless air. "I told him not to blabber, but the kid insisted on reporting to me."

"He was even afraid I wouldn't believe him, so he insisted on recording a video for me to see..."

Bu Aixian from the Immortal Clan Charity Association looked at Bu Yingshu and thought to herself, *"More likely, Bu Yingshu forced Zhang Yu to record it..."*

Right then, Bu Yingshu had already projected a video clip: "Since everyone wants to see it too, I might as well play it for all of you."

Bu Aixian thought to herself, *"Who said they wanted to see it!?"*

Yet, watching the contents within the projection, everyone present shuddered in unison.

Based on various records, they could still recognize the image of the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor. Moreover, as far as they were concerned, neither Bu Yingshu nor Zhang Yu would have the guts to impersonate the Immortal Emperor.

Seeing the Immortal Emperor gently rest his hand atop Zhang Yu's head in the footage, Bu Aixian was shocked to the extreme: *"Zhang Yu... is actually this close with the Immortal Emperor?"*

A representative to the side sprang to his feet and said, "Regarding the collection of the Books, I fully support Minister Zhang."

Another person chimed in, "Where is Minister Zhang right now? How could we make him come over? It should be us going to report to Minister Zhang."

Bu Aixian said, "I just received a low-grade Book. Yingshu... please pass it to Minister Zhang on my behalf this time as well."

---

Inside the Immortal Grotto-Heaven.

Looking at the low-grade book delivered by Bu Yingshu, Zhang Yu thought to himself, *"These Immortal Clan guys... sure are quick to trim their sails to the wind."*

After a thorough inspection, Zhang Yu merged this low-grade book into the Feather Book.

"Saber Technique Book? Looks like it's just like the previous low-grade books, adding another module for a cultivation art path."

Just as Zhang Yu had expected, a new cultivation art specialization slot appeared in the prologue of the Feather Book, allowing him to specialize in saber-type cultivation arts.

Just as Zhang Yu thought this book was nothing more than that, several lines of text slowly surfaced on the prologue of the fused Feather Book.

"If you have already met the Immortal Emperor, do not save this message."

"If you have not met the Immortal Emperor, you may save this message."

As he read, realization dawned upon Zhang Yu. "Does this Saber Technique Book contain information from the future? And... it only surfaced after being merged into the Feather Book, making it future information that truly only I can see?"

While his thoughts raced at lightning speed, Zhang Yu continued reading.

"Do not believe! Do not believe! Do not believe any future information from outside the Feather Book!"
