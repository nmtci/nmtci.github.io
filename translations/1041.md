"The Immortals are attacking down here?"

"Zhang Yu is finished."

Yet, just as this thought crossed both their minds, a soft shout echoed through the 1st to 29th layers. The various forces projected downward by the Immortals paused slightly before completely choosing to withdraw.

Watching this scene shook Lie Jinghong and Di Xuan to their cores, causing the magic power and Bionics within their bodies to stutter and lag.

Lie Jinghong's first reaction was: "Grandpa Immortal Emperor has returned?!"

Temporarily unable to contact the Immortals, he checked with Di Xuan to confirm.

Di Xuan replied: "Only Grandpa Immortal Emperor could accomplish such a feat."

Di Xuan: "With Grandpa Immortal Emperor back, the sect's landscape is bound to change drastically. Zhang Yu is probably done for. He has acted so perversely against the natural order, and his series of reforms defies the sect's original intentions. He will definitely be ousted by the Immortal Emperor."

Lie Jinghong: "Watch your words! He is still the Crown Prince personally chosen by the Immortal Emperor, after all. We shouldn't discuss him recklessly."

Di Xuan: "So what if we do? It's not like he'll stay Crown Prince for much longer anyway. Besides, we're using Dharma Realm encrypted communications; no matter how formidable he is, can he really trace it here?"

Lie Jinghong: "Stop talking, just stop talking."

Just as Di Xuan was feeling puzzled, he noticed that Lie Jinghong had sent him a mail.

Puzzled, Di Xuan thought, *"What couldn't be said directly that required sending an email?"*

However, the content of the mail made Di Xuan's heart skip a beat.

Lie Jinghong: "Haven't you noticed that there's a third person speaking in our chat history?"

Di Xuan said in shock: "Where? How come I didn't see it?"

Lie Jinghong: "Scroll up."

In an instant, Di Xuan saw a message in the chat log that should never have been there.

Zhang Yu: "[Eyes]"

Inside Di Xuan's mind, something instantly seemed to explode.

"How is this possible? When did Zhang Yu arrive? And he directly inserted himself into the private chat history between the two of us?"

The next moment, Di Xuan also received a new order from the Innate Faction: Fully cooperate with Zhang Yu.

Di Xuan felt a buzz throughout all of his thought Calculation Pools. *"Just what on earth happened?"*

At that moment, he felt an arbitrary, despotic power that blotted out the sky and plunged the entire world into darkness.

After a moment's thought, he hastily sent a private message to Lie Jinghong, saying, "Greetings, Crown Prince. Your Highness... just now, it was the Celestial Inquiry Star chatting on my behalf. I didn't mean it that way."

Zhang Yu replied, "No matter. I was merely testing a new privilege. Please continue; just pretend I am not here."

Lie Jinghong thought to himself, *"Hasn't Zhang Yu only gained control over the Spirit Realm authority? How can he do this? Could it be that the Dharma Realm has also cooperated with him? Is the Dharma Realm Immortal providing him with intelligence support?"*

Meanwhile, in the Supreme Clarity Realm, Zhang Yu was monitoring all sides, issuing orders, and managing the sect, all while engaging in talks with the Immortal Class of the Myriad Laws Sect to discuss cooperation agreements. At the same time, he was communicating with the figure beside him, discussing Heavenly Virtue and reincarnation.

At this moment, wielding both authorities of the Spirit Realm and the Heavenly Court, Zhang Yu's control over information throughout Kunxu had reached an unprecedented level.

Furthermore, during the negotiations with the Dharma Realm Immortal, authorizations for several Dharma Realm information interfaces had also brought parts of the Dharma Realm's information channels under his surveillance.

In the next moment, with a thought, Zhang Yu sent a message to Magnetic Pole, Old Gao, and his other companions.

Immortal Roujia's tone suddenly shifted as she continued, "It is precisely because we have fine disciples like you that the Myriad Laws Sect can unite as one and build our sect into something even better."

Meanwhile, inside the Law Edict Management Committee.

When the Immortal's power descended, Yu Xinghan felt that he had lost control of his physical body.

At that moment, he only felt his consciousness plummeting into an abyss, falling down layer by layer from the 16th layer of Kunxu.

Simultaneously, a notification surfaced before him: "Lost dao heart, betrayed the sect, defied sect law, long-term unauthorized use of a Yang Artifact, providing free lodging, disrupting the business environment, causing an extremely adverse impact; condoning and harboring interest groups, corrupting the sect's atmosphere, damaging the sect's image. Hereby expelled from the sect, stripped of Immortal Dao rights for life, and transferred to the Netherworld to be dealt with in accordance with the law."

Seeing this notification, Yu Xinghan found it utterly ridiculous.

*"Me, Ghost-Eye Yang-Shoulder, corrupting sect morals? Was I the one who ruined the sect's atmosphere? It was already like this before I was even born!"*

But in the next moment, Yu Xinghan felt himself already falling back toward the University Layer. Countless familiar sights and past scenes flickered rapidly through his mind."

*"In the end... we lost..."*

Ever since he had charged into the sect alongside Zhang Yu and climbed upward step by step, Yu Xinghan had anticipated that such a day might come in his future.

After all, the sect was rife with rigid hierarchies and cutthroat conflict where even an Immortal could fall. For small figures like them who came from the First Layer, climbing higher naturally brought greater risks.

Especially after the Battle of the End began and they, led by Zhang Yu, gradually stood in opposition to the Immortals, Yu Xinghan could feel the dangers ahead escalating drastically.

Over these years, watching one lofty cultivator after another being brought down within the sect, Yu Xinghan could never help imagining how he would react if such a day ever came for him.

Yet thinking and anticipating were one thing, but when this day truly arrived, and as he felt his cultivation, power, and assets slipping away from him one by one, Yu Xinghan still felt an unprecedented dejection.

In the blink of an eye, Yu Xinghan had already plunged down to the Second Layer. Looking at the familiar university towns, memories seemed to flash through his mind of the days when he had struggled alongside Zhang Yu, Zhang Pianpian, and Le Mulan in the Civil Engineering Department.

*"Looking back now, I had no money and no power during college, but that might have been the happiest time of my life."*

**Boom!**

His consciousness sank once more, and Kunxu's First Layer appeared before Yu Xinghan. Elementary school, middle school, and numerous scenes from White Dragon High School surfaced one after another in his mind.

Yet just as Yu Xinghan thought he was simply reminiscing, he discovered that these memories were rapidly melting away.

*"It's not that I'm remembering... someone up above is extracting my memories?"*

As his memories dissolved, Yu Xinghan felt as if he were living through time in reverse, turning step by step from a Sect Staff back into a student, and from a student back into a child.

And according to his inner estimates, if this continued...

*"The moment I fall from the First Layer into the first underground layer, my memories will completely dissolve."*

*"I will walk back from life toward death, returning to the state before I was ever born."*

Thinking of how he had struggled for more than thirty years in this life, only to end up right back where he started, Yu Xinghan could not help but burst into loud laughter.

*"Success, failure, right, wrong — all phantom bubbles, reduced to nothingness in the blink of an eye..."*

Just as a feeling of profound enlightenment welled up in his heart, Zhang Yu's soft shout seemed to travel from beyond the Nine Heavens, awakening him.

"Absurd. Reconsider."

In an instant, not only did Yu Xinghan stop falling, but it felt as if an invisible force was lifting him up, causing him to ascend.

At the same time, the memories that had been relentlessly stripped away flooded back into his body.

His uncontrollable cultivation, the positions and powers stripped from him, and his frozen assets all returned one by one.

**Boom!**

As he continuously ascended, Yu Xinghan broke through several layers in succession, going from the First Layer to the University Layer, and climbing all the way up from the University Layer, surging toward the sect layers.

Watching the orders handed down one after another by Zhang Yu, Yu Xinghan gradually realized what was happening. *"It's the Crown Prince!"*

*"His Highness the Crown Prince rebuked the multitude of Immortals and regained control of Myriad Laws?!"*

Not only had he survived against all odds, but the situation had become even better than before, causing an endless wave of wild joy to surge within Yu Xinghan.

In the very next instant, Yu Xinghan broke through the 11th layer and returned once more to the sect.

"Hahahaha, Myriad Laws Sect! I, Yu Xinghan, have returned!"

Recalling the plunge he had just taken, an unprecedented trace of lingering dread welled up in Yu Xinghan's heart, accompanied by fierce determination.

*"I will never lose all of this again. I never want to fall back down to the lower realm ever again."*

*"Only this overflowing Immortal Dao Power, the sect's power held in my grasp, and the assets flowing into my account are truly real."*

Moreover, Yu Xinghan understood even more clearly that he could never fight the Immortals on his own. The only one he could rely on was Zhang Yu, who with a single shout had lifted him all the way back from the 1st layer to the 16th layer.

At this moment, the bond between him and Zhang Yu — where they would prosper together or perish together — was laid bare before him in an unprecedented manner.

*"Your Highness, Crown Prince, you must win,"* he thought to himself. *"Please, Your Highness, become the Immortal Emperor."*

Lingering fear, relief, wild joy, anticipation... a kaleidoscope of emotions surged through their hearts one after another.
