# Chapter 1018: Those Who Come After

The vast and mighty power of the nine great Immortal Emperors gathered continuously, wrapping around the entire Ascension Platform layer upon layer. It was as if the Nine Heavens and Ten Earths were pressing down together, radiating a soul-stirring, terrifying momentum.

At the same time, Ying Xintian's will surged once more, burning with a ferocious, indomitable fighting spirit.

He looked at the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor before him, recalling how the man had intercepted him: "I lost, but not to the ten Immortal Emperors. I lost to the gap between eras."

"The gap in Immortal Dao Technology carved out by the passage of time is something that sheer talent, intellect, and unity can never bridge."

The next moment, Ying Xintian's surging intent conveyed what he wanted to say to the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor.

"Myriad Laws, I am indeed no match for you... but what if we add their reinforcements to the mix?"

Encircled by the wills of the nine great Immortal Emperors, surging power locked onto the area in layers. All manner of Immortal Arts and Divine Techniques were poised to strike, coiled and ready, and their target was none other than the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor himself.

It turned out that the one surrounded by the nine Immortal Emperors was not Ying Xintian, but Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor beside him.

Ying Xintian understood in his heart that if all cultivators throughout history were divided into two factions, one would be the Monopoly Faction like the ten Immortal Emperors, who lashed out at the world merely to climb to the peak of the Immortal Dao themselves. The other faction was the Open Faction, or Legacy Faction, represented by the Immortal Ancestor, who hoped to pass down heritage from generation to generation, keep techniques open, continually gather more and stronger cultivators, and ultimately combine the wisdom of countless people to scale the apex of the Immortal Dao together.

Ying Xintian knew even better that the so-called Immortal Emperor of the End was meant to stand above all living beings. Such a figure was not merely the enemy of the Legacy Faction, but inevitably the enemy of the Monopoly Faction as well — and an enemy of the utmost priority.

Especially when facing an existence about to make a push toward becoming the Immortal Emperor of the End, even the Legacy Faction and the Monopoly Faction, originally mortal enemies, could join hands at this moment.

And the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor, who had already been regarded as one of the top three strongest, was now perceived by the nine great Immortal Emperors as the existence among them closest to the End. Naturally, he became the enemy of all the Immortal Emperors.

Ying Xintian thought to himself, "Man's desire for the apex of the Immortal Dao... does not merely give rise to the Monopoly Faction and the Legacy Faction. When facing the end of all things, it also drives the ultimate form of cooperation. One could say that the closer they draw to the end, the more it compels the realm's strongest beings to join hands. Such is the relationship between the Monopoly Faction and the Legacy Faction — mutually generating yet mutually restricting."

In the flash of a thought, the battle intent within Ying Xintian's body had already soared, and he said, "Myriad Laws, what comes next is you alone against the ten of us."

In Ying Xintian's eyes, the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor before him had already fallen into a dead end, for his foe had already obtained his memories, his legacy.

This meant that even if Myriad Laws agreed to a rollback, possessing the rollback authority meant he... would not lose the memories from this iteration, nor would he lose this vital gain. He would still remain the Immortal Emperor closest to the end, so the Immortal Emperors after the rollback would never let him off either.

And if the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor refused to roll back, the ten Immortal Emperors would be even less likely to allow him to continue suppressing and thoroughly digesting Ying Xintian.

"The greed of the Monopoly Faction and the authority of the World Rollback have already bound you fast within this dead end."

The thoughts flashing through Ying Xintian's mind, the eruption of his battle intent, the clash of their wills... all of this transpired in a mere instant.

Within that fleeting instant, the mighty power of the nine great Immortal Emperors converged upon Myriad Laws from all sides, carrying the momentum of heaven collapsing and earth cleaving to thoroughly suppress the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor.

At the same time, Ying Xintian rose inch by inch, seemingly attempting to break free once more from the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor's suppression.

Sensing all of this, the profound depths of the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor's eyes remained an abyss of bottomless darkness. *"Yes, just like that, Ying Xintian... this is your only move right now."*

*"By surrendering the inheritance you obtained and the memories you hold entirely to one Immortal Emperor, you can borrow the hands of the other Immortal Emperors to launch a coordinated assault."*

*"Even though you know this might accelerate the birth of the Immortal Emperor of the End, you will still do it — because this is the ambition of your Legacy Faction. So long as there is the slightest sliver of a chance, you seek to defeat the Immortal Dao Monopolists, yearning to lead the world to scale the peak of the Immortal Dao through the ways of the Legacy Faction."*

*"The other Immortal Emperors are no different. Driven by their ambition for the Immortal Dao, they cannot tolerate me making further progress and breakthroughs. While I suppress Ying Xintian and digest the gains from this battle, they will inevitably join forces to hunt me down with all their might, even plotting to divide and consume me to increase their own odds of breaking through their cultivation realm."*

*"And what of me? I am the very same. Driven by my surging ambition for the Immortal Dao, I am more than willing to take this risk and chase that opportunity to break through to the final end."*

With a light downward press of his palm across the void, the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor once again suppressed Ying Xintian.

At the same time, his intent erupted, transmuting everything around him back into earth, water, wind, and fire to ward off the siege of the nine great Immortal Emperors.

And through this clash of intent, the will, thoughts, and words contained within it were perceived by all the mighty experts present.

Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor: "Be it the Immortal Ancestor, you, Ying Xintian, or us Immortal Emperors, everyone has been driven by that single ambition in their hearts, ultimately reaching this very point."

Amidst the clash of their respective mighty forces, the cracks across the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor's body multiplied, and within every single crack lay endless darkness, resembling abysses of unfathomable depth.

Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor: "If humanity wishes to survive, and if the Immortal Dao wishes to progress, then it inevitably requires this ambition as a driving force. This is the cornerstone of the world, and as long as this ambition burns, then..."

***Boom!***

Covered in cracks under the siege of the nine great Immortal Emperors, the flesh and blood of Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor finally shattered with a deafening blast at this moment, revealing the humanoid form composed of pure darkness within.

Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor: "...this world is bound to head toward its end. Everything has long been predestined."

Zhang Yu had long been unable to sense the Immortal Emperors' strikes, simply because nearly everything he could perceive had already become part of the Immortal Emperors.

However, he could still faintly sense the wills of Ying Xintian, Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor, and the other nine great Immortal Emperors. Thus, he knew that at this moment, Ying Xintian and the other nine Immortal Emperors were besieging the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor together.

At this moment, Zhang Yu could only think to himself, *"The control of these Immortal Emperors is truly formidable. Every single one of them is focusing solely on their opponent, which is probably why I haven't died yet."*

*"However, as the battle escalates, it's only a matter of time before I'm killed by the aftershocks."*

Zhang Yu knew that he was utterly powerless to struggle at this moment, yet he still could not help but ponder a question.

*"Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor... do you truly possess such confidence that you can withstand the siege of the other Immortal Emperors?"*

He believed that, given Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor's wisdom, possessing such confidence was by no means mere arrogance; rather, he had some kind of trump card.

Without knowing why, Zhang Yu found himself thinking of how the emperor had defeated the Rollbacker at this moment.

*"Just how did Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor manage to do it?"*

Right then, Ying Xintian made a move as well. A surging grotto-heaven force erupted across the void, violently blasting into... Zhang Yu.

Zhang Yu felt his body lighten, and he was already hurtling downward toward the lower layers from the Ascension Platform on the 20th layer.

Only now did he realize that the battlefield of the Immortal Emperors had already enveloped the entirety of the 20th layer. Countless Immortal Abodes, buildings, magical artifacts, formations... every single thing was turning to ashes, converted into resources for the Immortal Emperors' battle.

And the only reason it was confined to the 20th layer was that the 20th layer had long since broken away from its original position.

*"Before I woke up... the 20th layer had already been moved away? Was it done by Senior Ying Xintian?"*

*"And was his final strike meant to send me out of this layer that was moved away?"*

On the other side, Ying Xintian, having unleashed that blow, was caught off guard by the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor, who seized the opportunity to suppress him further.

The Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor opened his mouth and inhaled, releasing an equally violent surge of grotto-heaven force that stretched Ying Xintian into long, ribbon-like strands before swallowing him whole into his body.

Yet, a surging battle intent continued to erupt from Ying Xintian. Working in coordination with the other nine great Immortal Emperors, he stirred the surrounding space incessantly, causing distortions to ripple repeatedly both inside and outside the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor's body.

At the same time, the consciousnesses of both sides violently collided, exchanging thoughts in that very moment.

"Have you placed your bet on Zhang Yu?" the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor asked. "Do you truly believe he can grow... to the point of interfering in this battle before the victor between us is decided?"

"If one only ever thinks of winning entirely alone, if one cannot place faith in their own successor, then how are we any different from you Immortal Dao Monopolists?" Ying Xintian retorted.

"Very well," the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor replied. "Then I shall raise the stakes, and we will see whether he has a chance to turn the tide, or merely becomes our post-battle dessert."

With that said, before the 20th layer was completely severed, the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor's divine will had already sent out messages to the various halls and pavilions of the Myriad Laws Sect. 

Among them, the one that shocked the cultivators the most read: "...Presently, there is Zhang Yu, of loyal and pure character, exceptional talent and strategy, who has repeatedly rendered extraordinary meritorious service, bringing great comfort to Our heart. We hereby specially designate Zhang Yu as Crown Prince, establishing his rightful place in the sect to consolidate the imperial foundation. During Our absence from the sect, all matters from the 18th subterranean layer to the 29th layer above ground shall be handled by the Crown Prince as he sees fit, with full authority..."

Adopting a similar approach to that of the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor, the other nine great Immortal Emperors also sent out their own respective decrees. 

Feeling the 20th layer gradually disappear, Zhang Yu began to understand Ying Xintian's intention. 

"Senior Ying Xintian hopes that I will stay behind and seize the time to keep growing, growing until I am strong enough to intervene in this battle?"

"In their pursuit of Myriad Laws, the nine great Immortal Emperors can probably only take their main forces with them, leaving them no time to refine the numerous other assets across Kunxu."

"But... can I really grow to that level before a victor emerges among the ten Immortal Emperors?"

Zhang Yu understood clearly in his heart: "Even if the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor fails, with his vast accumulation, Senior Supreme Truth simply cannot compare. Once the remaining nine great Immortal Emperors carve up and devour Myriad Laws, I'm afraid yet another Immortal Emperor will approach the End, and the fighting will still not cease."

A chill also crept into Immortal Slayer's voice: "From the moment the war between the ten Immortal Emperors broke out, a death spiral had already begun."

"This war among the Immortal Emperors will only stop when the Immortal Emperor of the End is born. One could say the Ultimate Doomsday has entered its final countdown."

Just as a wave of apprehension struck Zhang Yu, he also noticed that the future information within the Immortal Grotto-Heaven was finally unsealed.

"Yet things have already come to this. This future information can no longer alter history, nor can it offer much help to the present situation."

Even with such thoughts flashing through his mind, Zhang Yu still separated a thread of his consciousness to look at the future information that had unsealed once the countdown hit zero.

As lines of messages surfaced before Zhang Yu, he seemed to see another version of himself, writing out message after message in The Book.

"Are these... messages asking questions of the Immortal Emperors from the lost history?"

"Speaking of which, did the version of me before the last rollback learn how to leave messages in The Book from the Immortal Ancestor's Will?"

"Though this time seems somewhat different. I didn't learn the technique for it."

Lost in thought, Zhang Yu swiftly sorted through the information, gradually piecing together the outline of the messages in his mind: "This aims to bolster Senior Ying Xintian's strength through the aid of the Lost Immortal Emperors and the passing down of mortal bloodlines."

"And as long as Senior Ying Xintian commands the power to self-destruct before the ten Immortal Emperors and destroy vast amounts of Kunxu's assets, that will be enough to serve as the key to initiating a rollback..."

Reading up to this point, a thought stirred in Zhang Yu's heart, and he continued his deduction: "So Senior Ying Xintian and I can also possess the option to actively trigger a rollback? And then transmit information through The Book."

"That way, we can trigger rollbacks time and again through Senior Ying Xintian, compile the information in The Book right at the moment of each rollback, and step by step close the distance between ourselves and the ten Immortal Emperors across each cycle..."

Looking at the contents of this future information, doubt welled up in Zhang Yu's heart: "Is this plan really feasible? If there are a few more rollbacks, won't the Immortal Emperors crack it? And... this time, things haven't progressed according to these plans at all. Why?"

Reflecting for a moment, Zhang Yu immediately realized, "Because this time, I triggered the infighting among the ten Immortal Emperors ahead of schedule, altering the course of events before obtaining the future information."

And the reason that led him to trigger their infighting...

Zhang Yu thought, *"The Immortal Ancestor's Will. It was because of his warning that I ended my own life, prematurely triggering the infighting among the ten Immortal Emperors."*

Right at that moment, the somewhat weak voice of the Immortal Ancestor's Will sounded, "Zhang Yu, you have already seen the future information, haven't you?"

"I am about to leave Kunxu together with Ying Xintian to assist him with all my might in confronting the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor."

"The Immortal Emperors possess too much formidable power. On this battlefield, I'm afraid I will soon be unable to maintain contact with you."

"The message left in the Book is a false plan you and I agreed upon. While the future information is secretive enough, once the ten Immortal Emperors sensed that something was amiss, they became capable of intercepting it through various means."

"So we devised a fake plan and left it in the message, deceiving everyone at the same time — including you yourself, Zhang Yu — in an attempt to mislead the Immortal Emperors."

Zhang Yu asked calmly, "Then what is the real plan?"

The Immortal Ancestor's Will said, "In fact, after Ying Xintian forced the ten Immortal Emperors into a second rollback through self-destruction, you and I realized that continuing like this was not a viable solution."

"With every rollback, the ten Immortal Emperors have ample time to lay out their plans and dismantle our technology."

"If we kept triggering rollbacks, it wouldn't be long before the ten Immortal Emperors completely cracked our Immortal Dao Technology step by step. In the end, we wouldn't even have the right to self-destruct before them, and they would squeeze every last bit of profit out of us."

"Or rather, from the very moment the ten Immortal Emperors set their plans in motion, the countdown to doomsday had already become inevitable."

"No matter how we sought help from the past or sought help from the present, it still wouldn't be enough to fight the ten Immortal Emperors."

Zhang Yu asked, "And so?"

The Immortal Ancestor's Will said, "The past, the present... they are all useless now. That leaves only seeking help from the future."

"Rather than letting the ten Immortal Emperors widen the gap with every rollback, it was better to facilitate one Immortal Emperor approaching the end to stop the rollbacks, thereby buying time to grow."

Hearing this, Zhang Yu felt a jolt in his heart and said, "So Senior Ying Xintian did it deliberately? He deliberately lost to the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor?"

The Immortal Ancestor's Will replied, "Probably not. Although Ying Xintian could not have known our plan, we judged that upon realizing he couldn't defeat an Immortal Emperor, he would recognize this as the only way out. He would use himself as bait to provoke a siege against one Immortal Emperor, and then shift the battlefield to grant you more time to grow."

"Even if he hadn't realized it, I would have nudged him from the sidelines. However, he shouldn't have chosen the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor, but rather one of the three Immortal Emperors temporarily disrupted by lost memories—Celestial Monster, Joyous Union, or White Bone... It was the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor who took the initiative to intercept him."

Surprised, Zhang Yu said, "You mean the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor took the initiative to reach this stage? Did he detect our plan and intentionally make this choice, wanting to become the besieged Immortal Emperor?"

At this moment, Zhang Yu sensed the confidence and assurance of the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor even more acutely, and a trace of an ominous feeling surged within his heart.

Immortal Ancestor's Will: "I cannot determine what the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor is truly thinking, but in short, the world can no longer be rolled back for now. What you need to consider next are the things to come."

"What I will write down next is the final message you left during the last rollback."

Looking at the message written out by Immortal Ancestor's Will, Zhang Yu felt as if he could see another version of himself in a hopeless situation, uttering his final words to him.

"Future me, please forgive me for handing such a desperate situation over to you, but I truly had no other choice."

Zhang Yu understood in his heart that as his present self crossed the temporal node of the world rollback and stepped forward into the future... to the Zhang Yu who had left this message, he was indeed that future Zhang Yu.

Zhang Yu continued to read the message left behind.

"I can only place my hopes upon you. Please grow recklessly and grant us the power to stand against the ten Immortal Emperors, future me."

Reading the other's message, Zhang Yu seemed to also see that other self... calling upon the Lost Immortal Emperors of the past for help, calling upon mortals for help, calling upon Ying Xintian in the present for help, calling upon Immortal Ancestor's Will for help. Through repeated pleas for aid and ceaseless struggles, he had still remained utterly unable to shake the mighty power of the ten Immortal Emperors.

Until the very end, he had created this single thread of an opportunity for growth, calling out to his future self for help.

"I have received your plea for help."

"I have also received all the Dao Lineage information you brought me through the lost history, as well as the time to grow that you won for me through this incident."

The Immortal Ancestor's Will said, "Grow stronger, Zhang Yu, and then join us in defeating these Immortal Dao Monopolists. Both Ying Xintian and I have chosen to believe in you..."

Zhang Yu called out, only to find that the Immortal Ancestor's Will had vanished without a trace once more, leaving behind only a vast amount of information within The Book, all related to The Book's Immortal Dao Technology.

Now, without Ying Xintian and without the Immortal Ancestor's Will, Zhang Yu seemed to be left entirely alone.

Right at that moment, stream after stream of messages crossed the Spirit Realm and arrived before him.

"Yu Zi! Are you there?"

"Minister, are you alright?"

"Profound Monarch? Are you okay?"

Looking at the messages from Bai Zhenzhen, Yu Xinghan, Le Mulan, Zhao Tianxing, Old Gao, Magnetic Pole, and the others, a faint smile gradually crept onto the corners of Zhang Yu's lips.

"I am not alone."

"Ten Immortal Emperors, I will grow strong enough. I will..."

"Join this battle."

The Sect Examination Clone also spoke up, "Today we are not yet mighty; once mighty, change shall come."

Just then, a message arrived from Dugu Ming: "Your Highness the Crown Prince, are you alright?"

Zhang Yu froze slightly. "Crown Prince?"
