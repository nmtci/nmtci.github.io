# Chapter 1038: God-Investiture Edict, Authority of the Righteous God

Hearing the other party's words, Zhang Yu's spirits lifted.

Within his mind, the Sect Examination Clone also grew excited: *"A shortcut? A backdoor to take? That's definitely way better than grinding yourself to death like those Divine Children out there, farming Morality Points."*

Zhang Yu immediately asked, "Senior, please enlighten me."

The figure before him sighed with emotion: "Zhang Yu, I am well aware of how you clawed your way up from the 1st layer to become the Crown Prince of Myriad Laws today."

"I have also witnessed how you just reduced the Spirit Realm fees for all living beings under heaven, benefiting Kunxu, as well as how you cast aside all your personal assets."

"Having no self, yet your self endures; being selfless, yet achieving the greatest selflessness. In this day and age, you are already a sage."

Zhang Yu nodded and said, "Senior, you flatter me."

"I do not flatter you, nor do I need to flatter you," the figure sighed. "Look at those so-called Ten Greats Good People outside — what kind of trash are they? Compared to them, if you aren't a sage, then what are you?"

"The morality of Kunxu today ought to take you as the standard."

"If that were possible, the situation wouldn't have deteriorated to this point."

Shaking its head, the figure stopped indulging in nostalgic laments and returned to business.

"Zhang Yu, having overcome the Tribulation of Heart Thunder and been recognized as a Paragon of Virtue, you can break the rules and forcefully seize a high position in the Heavenly Court."

Hearing this, a strange look flashed in Zhang Yu's eyes. "A Paragon of Virtue is allowed to violate the Heavenly Court's rules?"

"Of course," the figure replied as if it were only natural. "Rules are rigid, but people are adaptable. There never should be rules in this world that remain unchangeable for eternity. Look at the Morality Field outside today — hasn't it become a mass of dead rules constantly exploited by people through loopholes?"

"When the Divine Court governed the world with virtue back then, it placed morality above rules. Rather than rigid regulations, we placed our faith in each generation's Paragons of Virtue. If they chose to break the rules, it must have been because the rules were flawed, and those in the upper layers had made a mistake."

"Therefore, within the framework of the original Divine Court, a Paragon of Virtue was a being who could disregard many rules."

As it spoke, the figure looked directly at Zhang Yu and explained its plan: "Using this Supreme Clarity Realm as the foundation, I will help you seize the other fragments of the Supreme Clarity Realm. Then, with your status as a Paragon of Virtue, it will be enough to forcefully claim a high position in the Heavenly Court...

"It just so happens that you also possess the Spirit Realm authority, and now that the Supreme Clarity Realm is connected to the Spirit Realm, it becomes even more convenient..."

Accompanied by the rapid exchange of thoughts between both sides, Zhang Yu quickly understood what it meant.

*"The Supreme Clarity Realm is actually the Heavenly Court's Inner Court? The true nexus of power?"*

*"Besides Elder Sister, several other Divine Children also hold portions of the Supreme Clarity Realm. As long as we snatch them away one by one, we can reconstitute the Inner Court."*

Naturally, not every Divine Child could possess a piece of the Supreme Clarity Realm, and exactly which Divine Children possessed them had all been clearly seen by the figure.

Zhang Yu thought to himself, *"And with my Paragon of Virtue tag, reconstituting and forcefully claiming the Heavenly Court's Inner Court like this will also be deemed legitimate and compliant, allowing Elder Sister and me to directly obtain the supreme authority of the Heavenly Court?"*

Sensing the other party's thoughts, Zhang Yu swiftly voiced his doubts.

Zhang Yu asked, "However, if I obtain supreme authority just like that, won't it provoke a joint attack from the Immortals of the Ten Great Sects?"

The figure said, "Originally it would have. However, the entire ritual process of the Divine Child and Morality Field has already been interfered with by someone, causing a mutation."

Stirred, Zhang Yu said, "I heard from the Great God of Sentient Creation that it seems to be the Reincarnation Immortal Emperor who interfered?"

The figure replied, "I do not know this Reincarnation Immortal Emperor. But under the interfered Divine Child ritual, the Divine Emperor's authority is bound to be split..."

Listening to the other party's explanation, an understanding dawned in Zhang Yu's heart.

The Sect Examination Clone said, "Under this mutated ritual, the Divine Emperor's authority has been split into ten shares, forming a board of directors that has been taken over by the Ten Greats Good People outside and the Ten Sects Immortals behind them."

"And once we take charge of the Inner Court, we can become the chairman of this board of directors, possessing even the power of a single-vote veto."

Understanding the other party's meaning, Zhang Yu said, "In other words, after succeeding, I won't hold the Heavenly Court Authority alone, but will instead co-rule the Heavenly Court alongside the Immortals of the Ten Sects?"

The figure said, "Once you succeed, you must act as a referee between them rather than stepping into the ring to compete with them. That way, the Immortals of the Ten Sects won't unite against you, and you will gradually be able to secure your position."

Zhang Yu looked at the figure and realized that although he came from the past and held high moral standards, he was not in the least unfamiliar with political struggles.

Zhang Yu then raised his second question.

"Isn't the Supreme Clarity Realm... Elder Sister's Dao Seed? Why would it be the Inner Court? And why would it fall under the control of a Divine Child?"

After all, the figure before him was an old antique from ages past. Zhang Yu needed to ensure he hadn't misunderstood the technical framework, lest everything fall apart at the final hurdle.

The figure sighed with emotion. "I also never expected that in this new era, so many years later, the Supreme Clarity Realm would have been turned into a component of a Dao Seed by someone."

"However, I can tell you with absolute certainty that I personally took part in creating the Supreme Clarity Realm back then. There is no way I could mistake this place. Moreover, the portion of the Divine Court's authority I currently wield also tells me that this is indeed the Supreme Clarity Realm of old, the Inner Court."

"Perhaps someone wanted to leave behind a seed for the Divine Court, waiting for the day the  Supreme Clarity Realm matures back into the Inner Court."

Saying that, the figure chuckled again. "It's even better that it was made into a Dao Seed. From what I see, you're quite adept at harnessing the power of Dao Seeds and Dao Lineages, aren't you? With the blessing of a Dao Seed, seizing the Inner Court will be all the more convenient..."

As their minds clashed, Zhang Yu sensed the message conveyed by the other party and thought to himself, *"Treating others' Supreme Clarity Realms directly as nutrients to elevate Elder Sister's Supreme Clarity Realm — can that directly raise the rank of the 'Supreme Clarity Talisman' Dao Seed?"*

*"By advancing step by step in rank, it will naturally return to the Inner Court's original divine authority."*

At the end of their discussion, Zhang Yu made a suggestion.

"It just so happens that I also have some preparations that can be used to improve our chances of success this time."

Back then, the Immortal Emperors of the lost history had left Zhang Yu a great deal of technical materials.

Among them, Heavenly Virtue Immortal Emperor alone had left him materials concerning the Karma Dao Lineage, the God-Investiture Dao Lineage, and the Heavenly Tribulation Dao Lineage.

Ever since the Battle of the End began, even though Zhang Yu had been constantly occupied with countless affairs and his personal cultivation was as vast as mountains and seas, he had never neglected studying the knowledge left by Heavenly Virtue Immortal Emperor.

Especially after he sought to further bring the Righteous Gods under his control, his research into the God-Investiture Dao Lineage had deepened. In the process, he had gradually mastered all the relevant techniques and cultivation arts.

It was only recently that he had fully cultivated these arts to completion, forming an intact Chain Formation Diagram.

And just as Zhang Yu had lent the Chain Formation Diagram to Magnetic Pole, Old Gao, Ye Xingli, and Bu Yingshu, Zhang Pianpian had also been fitted with this Chain Method Diagram Set via a Technique Aperture.

"This is the Chain Formation Diagram I mastered, the God-Investiture Edict. It should be of help."

Zhang Yu gave a brief overview of the Chain Formation Diagram's contents.

The first tier function allowed one to disguise oneself as a Righteous God.

The second tier function made it possible to usurp a Righteous God Talisman Structure.

The third tier function went even further, enabling one to parasitize a Spirit Realm Grotto-Heaven.

At this moment, Zhang Yu made no effort to conceal his Chain Formation Diagram or his ability to lend it out, taking the opportunity to explain it all.

Hearing this, Zhang Pianpian was shocked in her heart. *"So what Zhang Yu transmitted to me last time was actually a Chain Formation Diagram? I thought it was some kind of Dao Seed Ability."*

"Good, good, good!" The figure was overjoyed upon hearing this. "This God-Investiture Edict is truly useful. With this Chain Formation Diagram, not only can we seize the Supreme Clarity Realm much faster, but our efficiency in concealing information will be far greater as well."

"I'm afraid that even after we regain control of the Inner Court, those Divine Children won't realize that their Supreme Clarity Realms have been seized."

At this point, the figure suddenly sighed with emotion. "Whoever created this Chain Formation Diagram must have been from my era..."

"Could that person... have prepared it for this very moment?"

After some preparation, the figure said, "I will sense the locations of the other Supreme Clarity Realms. Zhang Yu, you will be responsible for channeling the Spirit Realm Dao Lineage to help us link into them silently..."

Zhang Yu felt the Spirit Realm address transmitted by the other party and immediately activated the power of the Spirit Realm Dao Lineage.

The next moment, in utter silence, Zhang Yu and Zhang Pianpian's consciousnesses flashed, and they found themselves in another Spirit Realm Grotto-Heaven.

It was surrounded by the same mountains and shrouded in the same clouds and mist, yet the specific layout of the buildings was somewhat different.

Just as Zhang Yu and Zhang Pianpian arrived, this Supreme Clarity Realm trembled slightly, seemingly about to enter some kind of alert state.

**Boom!**

Zhang Yu and Zhang Pianpian instantly activated the power of the God-Investiture Edict together, surrounded by divine light as they disguised themselves as two of the Eight Divisions Great Gods.

Sensing the fluctuations of Great Gods, the Supreme Clarity Realm — which had just been about to enter an alert state — seemed to instantly fall into hesitation.

The next moment, Zhang Yu and Zhang Pianpian once again utilized the power of the God-Investiture Edict. Each unleashed streams of Righteous God Talismans to begin isolating the Supreme Clarity Realm and sending out disguised signals to the outside.

Under the cover of these disguised signals, the Divine Child outside had no idea whatsoever that his Supreme Clarity Realm had already been infiltrated.

Meanwhile, the figure had already descended from the heavens, exuding an awe-inspiring divine might.

"Is the dignified Inner Court of the Divine Court still planning to harbor dirt and shelter evil?"

"A Paragon of Virtue stands right before your eyes. Why are you hesitating instead of submitting immediately to bring blessings to the world?"

The figure's thoughts continuously bombarded the Supreme Clarity Realm before it, setting off a series of crisp, crackling sounds.

The next moment, the entire Supreme Clarity Realm shattered with a deafening roar, turning into boundless nourishment that surged toward Zhang Pianpian.

Amidst the rumbling, Zhang Pianpian's Supreme Clarity Realm expanded violently. The four paths — east, west, south, and north — that had originally been closed off within a daoist temple immediately reopened, and four great halls at the ends of those paths rose from the ground along with them.

Zhang Yu also immediately felt the advancement of his Dao Seed, watching as Supreme Clarity Talisman on the Feather Book upgraded from level 4 to level 5.

During this process, Zhang Yu sensed something unusual and said, "Sis, do you feel it? This upgrade feels very different from before."

Zhang Pianpian nodded and said, "Brother, I feel it. The Dao Seed and the God-Investiture Edict... the two Great Dao Lineages have connected. Compared to past upgrades of the Supreme Clarity Realm, this upgrade is what truly unlocks its authentic form."

"Without the help of you and Senior, I probably wouldn't have been able to unlock this form even if I raised the Supreme Clarity Realm by another five or ten levels."

At the same time, unfamiliar information flooded into her mind, and Zhang Pianpian exclaimed in surprise, "Brother, with this upgrade to the Supreme Clarity Realm, a lot more information has appeared..."

As Zhang Pianpian actively transmitted it over, a vast amount of information also surged into Zhang Yu's mind.

Looking at the contents of this information, Zhang Yu thought to himself, *"This is information left behind by the Heavenly Virtue Immortal Emperor, meant for the true future master of the Supreme Clarity Realm."*

Perusing its contents, Zhang Yu summarized, "I see. Back in the lost history, although the Heavenly Virtue Immortal Emperor failed to remember what happened after undergoing the rollback, he sealed away the relevant memories of the future, preserving them until now."

The figure beside him said, "Indeed. It was only after waking up in this new era and reading the unsealed memories within that I heard his praise for you — claiming you're the sole hope of the future, the ultimate choice to save Kunxu."

Zhang Yu smiled awkwardly, then continued summarizing the information, saying, "Although the Heavenly Virtue Immortal Emperor underwent a rollback and forgot the conversations he had with me in the lost history, he still preserved a proposal through the Book he held at the time."

"He didn't know why this proposal existed, nor did he know its purpose."

"Yet looking at the proposal's contents, he gradually deduced the existence of a Rollbacker and decided to carry out the plan according to the proposal, staking the outcome on the future."

However, this memory information ended there, offering nothing about what followed.

Before Zhang Yu could say anything more, the figure beside him urged, "Alright, leave Heavenly Virtue's message aside for now. How well have you mastered the new Supreme Clarity Realm?"

Zhang Pianpian remarked with feeling, "The newly opened Four Directions Divine Temple can deploy sixth-rank and below Righteous Gods to different areas for work."

The figure nodded and said, "That is already the authority of at least a fifth-rank Righteous God. Very good... Let us continue."

---

Meanwhile, on the outer perimeter of the Heavenly Court.

Immortal Roujia and Immortal Yi Xuan of the Reformist Faction were observing the environment within the Morality Field.

"The Ten Greats Good People have once again completed their monopoly."

"The negotiations among the Immortals of the various sects have also nearly finished dividing up the Heavenly Court's authority."

"What about Zhang Yu? How is he doing? Is he still making attempts?"

At this very moment, with the Heavenly Court's authority nearly in their grasp, Immortal Roujia casually took command of the Great God of Myriad Paths and Four Directions and inquired about Zhang Yu's situation.

A light screen instantly materialized before the Immortal, accompanied by the reporting voice of the Great God of Myriad Paths and Four Directions: "Ever since he was knocked out of the Morality Field, Zhang Yu has given up on making attempts and has been merely observing."

"Hmph? Given up? I wouldn't be so sure," Immortal Roujia said indifferently. "With his life on the line, there is no way Zhang Yu would give up so easily. Keep a close watch on everywhere else besides this physical body of his, and report back immediately if anything goes wrong."

---

**Boom!**

Yet another part of the Supreme Clarity Realm transformed into nourishment, pouring into Zhang Pianpian's body.

Innumerable fragments of information were transmitted once more.

Perusing the contents, Zhang Yu thought to himself, *"In the process of executing his plan, the Heavenly Virtue Immortal Emperor realized that the entire plan... was meant to aid someone in the future."*

*"He realized that the vast number of Chain Formation Diagrams and Dao Seeds he was to create were all meant for a single future existence who might turn the tide."*

*"That person would inherit their will and transform Kunxu."*

*"And to increase the plan's chance of success, he also created the Righteous God Ritual, intending for the Righteous Gods to assist that person in the future..."*

Reading the contents of the message, Zhang Yu mused inwardly, *"Could the Evil God Rituals have originated from this as well? If so, it means the ritual created by the Heavenly Virtue Immortal Emperor has long been utterly twisted, reduced to a tool used by Evil Gods for fraud, entrepreneurship, profiting, and fighting against the Righteous Gods."*

At the same time, as the Supreme Clarity Talisman thundered into level 6, the Supreme Clarity Realm shook violently once more, and a total of twelve palaces rose from the ground.

Sensing the changes in the Supreme Clarity Realm, Zhang Pianpian said, "The twelve Primordial Destiny Palaces can dispatch Righteous Gods of the fourth rank and below at different times." [^1]

The figure rejoiced, "Good! Now that you have grasped the authority of a third-rank Righteous God, on to the next!"

---

Outside the Heavenly Court.

At this very moment, the entire traffic situation on the 29th layer had been brought completely under the control of the Great God of Myriad Paths and Four Directions.

Any movement made by any cultivator on this layer could not escape his detection.

At the same time, the Great God of Myriad Paths and Four Directions looked at the Great God of Sentient Creation's Spirit Realm avatar and thought to himself, "You might have chosen the wrong person."

"In today's Kunxu, kindness... is ultimately useless."

"Only sufficiently powerful Immortal Dao Technology can decide everything, just like the Ten Sects Immortals right now."

Just then, he received a message from the Great God of Heavenly Covenant and Equal Contract inquiring about the situation.

The Great God of Myriad Paths and Four Directions knew that behind the Great God of Heavenly Covenant and Equal Contract stood the backer he had recently latched onto: Immortal Venerable Heavenly Fortune.

"Sigh, Heavenly Covenant made the right choice, after all."

As he pondered, the Great God of Myriad Paths and Four Directions had already transmitted the overall situation's information to the other party.

On the other side, the moment the Great God of Heavenly Covenant and Equal Contract received the information, he shared it with Immortal Venerable Heavenly Fortune beside him.

Immortal Venerable Heavenly Fortune glanced over it and said indifferently, "Keep monitoring changes across the entire field. The ritual has reached its final critical juncture, and I don't want anything to go wrong."

"Especially regarding the Ten Great Emperor Beasts. That bunch has never harbored good intentions, and they might butt in somewhere to ruin our success at the final step."

The Great God of Heavenly Covenant and Equal Contract nodded, expressing that he would certainly take the matter seriously.

As for Zhang Yu? In their eyes, it was already impossible for him to intervene in the grand situation unfolding before the Heavenly Court.

Soon after, as the ceremony concluded, the roster of the Ten Great Divine Kings was proclaimed above the Heavenly Court; they would jointly share the Divine Emperor's authority and preside over the Heavenly Court.

Naturally, these Ten Great Divine Kings were none other than the Ten Great Good People chosen by the Immortal Class.

And almost the very moment the Ten Great Divine Kings were announced — without pausing or hesitating for even 0.000001 seconds — the Immortals of the Myriad Laws Sect sprang into action.

Descending in unison, they projected their might toward the Myriad Laws Sect's territory from the 1st to the 19th layers.

Heaven-piercing immortal light instantly illuminated the skies of layer after layer within the Myriad Laws Sect.

Within a single second, the Immortals had locked onto every single entity valued at 0.01 immortal coins or more across the 1st to the 19th layers.

The non-interference treaty within the Myriad Laws Sect was torn to shreds the very instant the Immortals seized control of the Heavenly Court.

At the same time, an eleventh name began to appear high above the Heavenly Court.
