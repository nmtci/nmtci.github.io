# Chapter 932: Zhang Yu Is Here, I Will Go With You

The cultivator standing beside Yue Qinglan was a great cultivator at the Tribulation Transcendence Realm named Lianzi Zi.

He was also the new person in charge sent down by the higher-ups after Le Mulan's project was upgraded.

It was precisely because of the great importance attached by the higher-ups that Yue Qinglan now felt more confident, no longer as worried about Zhang Yu's influence as before.

But to say she wasn't worried at all was naturally impossible.

After all, Zhang Yu was currently blazing hot in the Myriad Laws Sect; his every move attracted the attention of the Immortals and gods in the heavens.

Thus, after pondering for a moment, Yue Qinglan still asked, "This Le Mulan seems to have a good relationship with Zhang Yu. Should we consider Zhang Yu's opinion?"

Lianzi Zi merely gave a cold snort and said, "The massive improvement in Le Mulan's efficacy is the result of our tireless investment and nurturing. What does it have to do with Zhang Yu? Did he give birth to Le Mulan? Or did he upgrade her?"

"Moreover, the news regarding Le Mulan's new efficacy has been kept strictly confidential. Zhang Yu doesn't know about it at all. Wouldn't telling him just be asking for trouble?"

Yue Qinglan said, "But what if? If we keep cutting off their communication, what if Zhang Yu gets suspicious? I heard Le Mulan seemed to be his dual cultivation partner before, and the relationship between the two is quite deep..."

Lianzi Zi said disdainfully, "Then we'll talk about it when he actually becomes suspicious. At the end of the day, Le Mulan is nothing but a cultivator who climbed up from the First Layer. Zhang Yu might not take her that much to heart. At worst, we'll pay him some mental damage compensation. If that still doesn't work, you can just accompany him in dual cultivation, that should be enough, right?"

Yue Qinglan said, "I heard this Zhang Yu... is actually quite mindful of old affections."

"Mindful of old affections?" Lianzi Zi said flatly, "Then we'll pay more. Three times the mental damage compensation. With me joining in alongside you, that should surely be enough, right?"

In Lianzi Zi's eyes, a tiny bit of compensation, compared to the markets that could be opened up and the profits that could be squeezed from Le Mulan... was not worth mentioning.

As for this so-called mindfulness of old affections, Lianzi Zi considered it to be nothing more than an excuse for bargaining and promoting one's intimates; it was merely a type of tactic.

At the end of the day, Lianzi Zi and Yue Qinglan... had never worried about something like Zhang Yu coming to rescue Le Mulan, or rather, they had simply never even considered such a possibility.

What they were worried about was that Zhang Yu would find an excuse to forcibly take a share of the spoils.

"Anyway, even if we clash with Zhang Yu, the price is negotiable," Lianzi Zi thought to himself. "We have an information advantage that Zhang Yu lacks, so no matter how we negotiate, we'll make a profit. Zhang Yu would never in a million years guess Le Mulan's new utility."

Hearing this, Yue Qinglan also nodded slightly, thinking to herself, "Indeed, after all, Zhang Yu doesn't know Le Mulan's latest situation. No matter how nostalgic he is, he couldn't possibly go to war over Le Mulan."

As for the Ten-Billion Flying Sword in Zhang Yu's hands, Yue Qinglan actually wasn't that afraid of it anymore.

The reason was also very simple...

"Because I'm not worthy."

Yue Qinglan thought to herself, "Without knowing the latest intelligence on Le Mulan, is she really worth Zhang Yu using the Ten-Billion Flying Sword?"

"Even if something unexpected happens and Zhang Yu decides to use the Ten-Billion Flying Sword to deal with our project... it still wouldn't be my turn to take that sword strike."

"After all, even if he bankrupts me, it won't stop this project."

"And Zhang Yu can only unleash one sword strike. If he strikes, he will definitely target the most valuable person."

"Because I am unworthy of it, I am unafraid. I am not afraid of him using this Ten-Billion Flying Sword."

Compared to the Ten-Billion Flying Sword, Yue Qinglan was actually more worried about Zhang Yu's position as Minister of the Effectiveness Review Department.

"However, I have only just established the Law Edict. Even if he wants to make a move against me... that would be something for 50 years from now."

Give up the massive profits right before her eyes on Le Mulan for something 50 years down the line?

This thought only flashed briefly in Yue Qinglan's mind before she outright rejected the option.

At the same time, Le Mulan, who was soaking in a massive amount of potion, opened her eyes and said, "Dao Monarch Yue, Profound Monarch Lianzi Zi, I have already completed the new phase of the mission. Can I contact the outside?"

Lianzi Zi kept a straight face and said, "This matter is of grave importance. We cannot take the risk of any information leaking."

Le Mulan looked at Yue Qinglan and said, "Dao Monarch Yue, you promised me last year..."

Yue Qinglan snorted coldly and said, "Le Mulan, don't think you're anything special just because you know Zhang Yu. Many people with higher cultivation realms and higher positions than Zhang Yu are already paying attention to this project of ours."

"If you contact Zhang Yu and inadvertently let him know things he shouldn't know, it will only harm him."

Because she had been disconnected from the Spirit Realm and Dharma Realm for a long time and was unable to obtain outside information, in Le Mulan's impression at this moment, Zhang Yu was still the Former Days Salary King who had made great contributions in the Former Days Graveyard last year, a Spirit Transformation cultivator.

It was precisely because she knew this that Yue Qinglan could rely on this information gap to speak up, "Not to mention that I am now among the first batch of cultivators to establish the third-generation Law Edict, Senior Lianzi Zi beside me is a veteran cultivator at the Tribulation Transcendence Realm, a future Ascension High Official."

"Any one of us could easily deal with Zhang Yu, do you understand?"

Hearing this, Le Mulan's expression dimmed as she thought to herself, *"The gap between Zhang Yu and them is too great. If he comes into conflict with them, I'm afraid he will suffer a massive loss. But..."*

Le Mulan thought of the times in the past when Zhang Yu had stood up for his companions and the poor time and time again, and she couldn't help but think to herself, *"What if Zhang Yu can't contact me for a long time and comes looking for me? If he blows things out of proportion and offends Yue Qinglan and the others, I'm afraid Zhang Yu's future immortal path will face enormous obstacles."*

Thinking of this, Le Lan spoke up, "Dao Monarch Yue, I am worried that if Zhang Yu cannot reach me, he might come looking for me himself, causing trouble and offending you all..."

"Could you let me send a message? Just one will do, just to let him know I am safe."

"I can let you review the specific content of the message, and I will only send it if you agree."

Yue Qinglan looked at Lianzi Zi beside her. Lianzi Zi thought for a moment and said, "Write it down first. As for whether we will let you send it or not, we still need to discuss it."

Yue Qinglan asked in a private chat on the side, "Are we really going to let her send it to Zhang Yu?"

Lianzi Zi replied indifferently, "How is that possible? Zhang Yu now holds a position of high authority; he might have long forgotten this Lower Beast. If we let Le Mulan send a message now, wouldn't that just be reminding him?"

"However, we can let her write a few messages first so I can see what exactly she wants to say."

"If there is a need in the future, we could send them to Zhang Yu on her behalf."

Inside Zhang Yu's Immortal Grotto-Heaven.

The Feather Book was turned to the section of the Dao Seed Register. 

That [Profound Cold Meridian] belonging to Le Mulan... was now astonishingly upgraded to Level 5. 

Zhang Yu instantly understood; it was obvious that Le Mulan had been continuously receiving investment and cultivation from the pharmaceutical company, to the point that her Dao Seed had leveled up again. 

"I remember that to upgrade the Profound Cold Meridian to Level 5... it requires a magic power level at the Spirit Transformation Realm." 

Recalling the contents of his previous exchanges with Le Mulan, Zhang Yu thought to himself: "That is to say, under continuous investment and cultivation, Le Mulan has already broken through from the Nascent Soul to the Spirit Transformation over the past year?" 

"They gave her resources and found ways to raise her rank. They really value Le Mulan a lot." 

And when Zhang Yu comprehended the effects of the Profound Cold Meridian upgrading to Level 5, a surge of emotion welled up in his heart. 

"It actually has this kind of effect?" 

Bu Yingshu, who was circulating her cultivation alongside Zhang Yu, quickly noticed the anomaly and said, "Little Yu! Focus, don't get distracted!" 

Zhang Yu sighed and said, "Profound Monarch Bu, I have some things to take care of, I need to leave for a moment." 

Sensing the profound cold magic power circulating within his body, Zhang Yu thought to himself: "A Level 5 Profound Cold Meridian can actually freeze Primordial Spirit consciousness and extend the lifespan of Soul Cultivators, this is really..."

The moment he confirmed this effect, Zhang Yu immediately saw the benefits and potential contained within it.

Especially the part about the longevity-extension for Soul Cultivators...

"Freezing the Primordial Spirit and freezing one's thoughts looks very powerful and high-end, capable of taking effect on high-tier cultivators. But in terms of its impact on the landscape of Kunxu, it's far inferior to the longevity extension ability for Soul Cultivators."

"Longevity-extension for Soul Cultivators... If this can be promoted on a large scale, it is something that could change the industry and business landscapes, affect a market of one hundred billion or even one trillion, and influence the Dao Lineage Struggles."

And at the thought that this Dao Seed ability was currently in the hands of Le Mulan, who had just broken through to the Spirit Transformation Realm, Zhang Yu sensed the enormous risk involved.

"I wonder if the pharmaceutical company has discovered its longevity-extending Soul Cultivator effects now that the Profound Cold Meridian has upgraded."

"Once they find out, I'm afraid Le Mulan..."

Based on Zhang Yu's understanding of Kunxu, once the pharmaceutical company discovered the secret within, in order to fight for profit, as long as it was necessary... they could easily crush Le Mulan into "Le Mulan paste."

Hearing that Zhang Yu was going to leave temporarily, Bu Yingshu furrowed her brows and said, "What is so important? We're only halfway done, and you're leaving?!"

At this moment, Bu Yingshu was very unhappy.

Zhang Yu said, "I have a friend who came up with me all the way from the First Layer. I'm afraid she is currently facing oppression from the pharmaceutical company, and I want to go give her a hand."

Hearing this, Bu Yingshu became even more unhappy. A Lower Beast from the First Layer actually counted as an excuse? 

Did she even deserve to interrupt their affairs?

A wave of coercive pressure from the Tribulation Transcendence Realm swept over, seemingly about to ruthlessly bear down upon Zhang Yu's body.

Sensing this change, Bai Zhenzhen, who had been completely immersed in a cycle of Extreme Emotion, Sorrow, Supreme Bliss, and Shock, instantly jolted awake.

Bai Zhenzhen hurriedly sent a voice transmission to Zhang Yu, "Yu Zi, this person Bu Yingshu is easy to deceive but hard to reason with. Rather than telling her the truth, it's much more convenient to just lie to her."

The two of them engaged in a rapid exchange of voice transmissions. Finally, quickly recalling what Fu Ji had once shared and combining it with the situation before them, Bai Zhenzhen continued, "Yu Zi, try doing as I say..."

Listening to Bai Zhenzhen's words, Zhang Yu thought to himself, *"A-Zhen, this girl, ever since she started practicing the Extreme Emotion Sword Technique, she's really become even more..."*

Zhang Yu was then heard saying, "Profound Monarch Bu, I am your person in life and your ghost in death. Those subordinates of mine... perhaps they don't know it themselves, but they were also all born for the sake of uplifting you."

"Those guys from the pharmaceutical company know that the people they touched are mine, and they also know I am your person. I'm worried they want to affect your immortal path."

"I'll go test the waters first, wait here for my news..."

Hearing the first half of Zhang Yu's words, Bu Yingshu immediately felt much more at ease, but upon hearing the second half, a thought suddenly surfaced in her mind.

"That's right, Zhang Yu is my little pet, so his little pet is my little pet's little pet."

"The pharmaceutical company is bullying my little pet's little pet. Could it really be an attempt to deal with me?"

If it were anyone else saying this, Bu Yingshu might not have easily believed this claim.

But looking at Zhang Yu in front of her, she couldn't help but believe it somewhat.

Zhang Yu took such a huge risk to uplift me and untie my restraints. Even Bu Yuangui said that Zhang Yu had forsaken his path to achieve immortality. Could Zhang Yu really lie to me?

So, upon hearing that Zhang Yu was going to test the waters, Bu Yingshu directly spoke up: "Hold on. Tell me who you are trying to help, and I will investigate it first."

Zhang Yu said, "Her name is Le Mulan..."

Bu Yingshu nodded and said proudly, "Wait here, I'll first see what's going on. Remember, these pharmaceutical companies are also operating on the land of the Myriad Laws Sect."

Not long after Bu Yingshu left, a message from Kuang Tianqing floated before Zhang Yu's eyes.

Kuang Tianqing: "Same old place, I have something I need to discuss with you."

Just as Zhang Yu was about to say that he didn't have time right now, he saw the other party send another message: "About your old friend."

Seeing this message, Zhang Yu's heart stirred, thinking of Le Mulan's Profound Cold Meridian and the Reincarnation Faction behind Kuang Tianqing.

"Could the Reincarnation Group have already found out about Le Mulan's situation?"

Thinking of the immense power of the Reincarnation Group's intelligence network, Zhang Yu couldn't help but feel somewhat certain of his guess. He spoke up, "A-Zhen, I need to head out for a bit."

"Is it about Le Mulan?" Seeing the resolute look on Zhang Yu's face, Bai Zhenzhen did not say much else, only saying, "Be careful."

Watching Zhang Yu's departing back, Bai Zhenzhen felt the four strands of Sword Intent in her body melting together in a single furnace. She thought to herself, "The path is correct, the various Sword Intents can be integrated and mastered..."

Over this past period of time, with the blessings of the True Spirit Root and the Spirit Transformation Dao Body, Bai Zhenzhen's cultivation could be said to be advancing by leaps and bounds.

Particularly, the True Spirit Root was still continuously adapting to the Spirit Transformation Dao Body, subsequently erupting with new Immortal Dao reactions.

The most notable change was that when Bai Zhenzhen cultivated a new sword technique, not only did her learning speed skyrocket once again, but she also constantly had various ingenious ideas welling up in her mind during the learning process, which she used to upgrade the grade of the sword technique, ultimately modifying it into a higher-version sword technique that suited her even better.

The sword techniques currently fused together within her body were exactly like this.

"Directly purchasing high-grade sword techniques doesn't suit me."

"I should buy military-version sword techniques, then upgrade them to the Immortal Sect True Inheritance-grade, transforming them into sword techniques that are more suitable for me."

"Aside from Supreme Bliss, Extreme Emotion, Shock, and Sorrow, there is also Anger, Disgust, Lust, Hatred... I can take all kinds of sword techniques, modify and upgrade them one by one, and integrate them into my system."

At the thought of all kinds of sword techniques potentially being upgraded in grade by her in the future — from military to Immortal Sect True Inheritance, then to Strategic, to Sect-Guarding, and even Heaven-Opening, all while being Bai Zhenzhen versions that interconnected and fused together, returning ten thousand swords to one...

Thinking of all this, Bai Zhenzhen felt the Sword Intent within her body become restless once again.

"The only troublesome thing is... it seems that my spirit inspiration only gushes forth the most, and the two sword intents of Extreme Emotion and Supreme Bliss in my body are most stimulated, when I am observing Yu Zi."

Bai Zhenzhen could feel that Extreme Emotion and Supreme Bliss were like two of the fiercest, most vast primary driving forces, perfectly suited to serve as the Comprehensive Manual and starting point for her fusion of the myriad swords.

"However, some people observe the great sun to create cultivation arts, some observe the earth to create cultivation arts, others observe factories, some observe dual cultivation, some observe amusement, and some observe the news... So what if I observe Yu to create my sword art? I'm just looking at my own son, that's all."

Inside a secret stronghold of the Reincarnation Group.

Zhang Yu looked at Kuang Tianqing in front of him and said, "Speak, what exactly is the matter?"

Kuang Tianqing smiled, and Su Lingyou, who was beside him, had already sent a message directly to Zhang Yu's Law-Evading Talisman.

"Congratulations, Dao Monarch Zhang. The higher-ups have approved your promotion to Rank 7 Sect Staff of the Heavenly Demon Sect, placing you among the candidates for the Heavenly Demon Prince."

Su Lingyou said flatly, "All corresponding benefits will be temporarily kept by the sect for you, and will be issued to you once you return to the Heavenly Demon Sect."

Hearing this, Zhang Yu nearly laughed out of anger, saying coldly, "You might as well figure out a way to just send me some money directly."

Kuang Tianqing said, "You must be joking, Dao Monarch. Wouldn't casually sending you money ruin your spotless reputation and affect your immortal path in the Myriad Laws Sect?"

The next moment, Kuang Tianqing stared at Zhang Yu in front of him, his tone shifting as he said, "Le Mulan, you still remember her, don't you?"

Zhang Yu thought to himself, *"As expected"*, but on the surface, he remained impassive and asked, "What happened to her?"

Kuang Tianqing carefully observed Zhang Yu's every move, every trace of expression, and every glance, while the various detection magical artifacts around them fed back a massive amount of data.

Looking at the changes in the data, Kuang Tianqing thought to himself, "Heh, Zhang Yu... has really become experienced. I can't see through him at all."

Although Kuang Tianqing was also very curious about what exactly had happened to Le Mulan, since Zhang Yu didn't reveal the slightest flaw, Kuang Tianqing stopped wasting time and cut straight to the chase, "Something went wrong with Le Mulan. The higher-ups in the group want her dead."

"Dead?"

Zhang Yu's heart sank, knowing that the group the other party mentioned was naturally the Reincarnation Group. Zhang Yu said, "You want me to arrange for her to enter the Soul Cultivator Company?"

Kuang Tianqing shook his head and stared at Zhang Yu, saying word by word, "Dead, completely and thoroughly dead. No becoming a Soul Cultivator, no physical body, no... not even the slightest trace left behind. Send her straight into Reincarnation."

Hearing this, Zhang Yu sighed inwardly, "As expected."

After understanding the efficacy of Le Mulan's Profound Cold Meridian, Zhang Yu immediately thought of the risks involved.

"Providing longevity extension for Soul Cultivators is a massive market, but it also severely offends the Reincarnation Group."

Zhang Yu thought to himself, *"Allowing Soul Cultivators to live longer and consume more during their lifetimes... before finally entering Reincarnation, greatly impacts the quality, quantity, and overall revenue of the Reincarnation Group's clients."*

This act of snatching meat from the Reincarnation Immortal Emperor's hands was, in Zhang Yu's eyes, no different from snatching food from a tiger's jaws.

"It would be one thing if it was another Immortal Emperor stepping in to compete, but Le Mulan..."

If the one making a move was an Immortal Emperor, or even just an Immortal Venerable or an Immortal, the Reincarnation Immortal Emperor would probably need to give it some careful consideration...

Immortal Slayer said, "But if simply eliminating a lower-tier cultivator like Le Mulan is all it takes to stop the loss of profits, then for the Reincarnation Immortal Emperor, it becomes a simple math problem."

The Sect Examination Clone said angrily, "What the hell? How long has it even been since Le Mulan developed it? And the Reincarnation Group and the Reincarnation Immortal Emperor already know? These pharmaceutical companies are practically riddled with holes! Just how much information about our Myriad Laws Sect has been leaked to the Reincarnation Immortal Emperor?"

The Auspicious God listened to their conversation in a daze, his mind a complete blank.

*"What's going on? What the hell is going on?"*

The Auspicious God's mind raced: *"Why is it that from the conversation with Bu Yingshu just now, to Kuang Tianqing speaking here, as well as Zhang Yu, Zhanshen, the Sect Examination Clone... so many people, why have they all suddenly started talking about Le Mulan one by one?"*

*"What the hell did Le Mulan even do? Why do all of you know about it so clearly?"*

At this moment, Auspicious God even suspected that he had gone dormant for a while and missed some crucial information.

*"Damn it, Fu Ji definitely knows,"* Auspicious God thought inwardly. *"In Zhang Yu's heart, we ultimately just can't compare to an old retainer like Fu Ji."*

Meanwhile, a strange look flashed through Herald God's mind: *"Sure enough, Zhang Yu possesses a method that we cannot see, touch, or perceive."*

Recalling Zhang Yu's explosive cultivation speed, his interactions with his numerous subordinate cultivators, and the Dao Seed replication ability...

The Herald God thought to himself, *"Is it some kind of... Book? Or some kind of potential that this fellow Fu Ji has stimulated?"* The Herald God was uncertain in his heart.

*"But whatever the case, based on my summary so far, Zhang Yu's special ability can enhance his subordinates' cultivation and work capacity, and he can even learn about Le Mulan's situation across vast distances..."*

Just as the Herald God was deep in thought, Zhang Yu was already saying with a calm expression, "You want to send Le Mulan into reincarnation? Then what is the meaning of telling me all this?"

Kuang Tianqing said, "Because you are a very suitable person to make the move."

"Everyone in the world knows that you, Dao Monarch Zhang, value old ties and are an old acquaintance of Le Mulan."

"After not being in contact for over a year, you go looking for Le Mulan. During the search, an accident unexpectedly occurs, resulting in Le Mulan's death..."

Listening to Kuang Tianqing's words, Immortal Slayer knew that Zhang Yu would definitely refuse, so he said, "Don't rush to refuse just yet..." Zhang Yu said lightly in his heart, "I know. I'll coax out more intel."

*"Refuse? Why would he refuse?"* Auspicious God had just wanted to say these words out loud, but directly swallowed them back down.

Although in the Auspicious God's view, what could possibly be wrong with completing tasks for the Reincarnation Immortal Emperor to obtain benefits?

But on second thought, he realized, *"Even if it succeeds, the rewards will just be fed to Fu Ji. We might as well not do this kind of task."*

Zhang Yu was then heard saying, "An accident? Does that mean you'll have the undercover agents you arranged... cooperate with me?"

Kuang Tianqing said, "That's right. I guarantee it will be flawless; absolutely no one will suspect you."

Zhang Yu said, "If we're cooperating, you should at least tell me their name, right?"

Kuang Tianqing said, "There's no rush. Just match the secret signal, and you will naturally know when the time comes."

As he spoke, Kuang Tianqing had already sent the secret signal to Zhang Yu: "The Great Enemy of the Soul-Severing, Life-Extinguishing Heaven"

Zhang Yu silently memorized the signal and asked, "And the specific plan?"

Kuang Tianqing tapped his fingertip lightly, and with a flash of a light screen, a three-dimensional map appeared.

"Le Mulan, through the methods of the pharmaceutical company, has now been granted special approval to be in a secret laboratory on the 16th Layer."

"We need you to cause a major uproar, then find a chance to activate the Immortal License Plate Shuttle..."
Zhang Yu studied the map before him while listening to Kuang Tianqing's plan.

He thought to himself, "Deploy the Immortal License Plate Shuttle, and then have the collaborators place Le Mulan in the shuttle's path to make it look like an accidental death caused by the shuttle?"

Kuang Tianqing said, "...After the matter is done, we will provide you with Evil Gods, just like the previous batch."

Right after Kuang Tianqing finished explaining the plan, Zhang Yu said calmly, "What if I disagree?"

Hearing this, Kuang Tianqing and Su Lingyou were both slightly taken aback, seemingly not expecting such an answer from Zhang Yu.

"Disagree? Why disagree?"

In Su Lingyou's view, a lower realm cultivator, someone who was already useless to Zhang Yu, could just be killed. What was there to disagree with? Not to mention there was a reward.

Kuang Tianqing, possessing longer ancient memories, looked at Zhang Yu now, a flash of understanding seemingly passing through his eyes: "Are you... reluctant to act against your own people?"

*"Reluctant? How is that possible?"* The Herald God, however, felt a stir in his heart and thought to himself, *"This Zhang Yu is unfathomable. Every action he takes has deep meaning; that is the only way he could have climbed to his current position."*

*"What sentimentality... In my eyes, it's all just an act he puts on to win over his subordinates. This trick works exceptionally smoothly in Zhang Yu's hands; it's both effective and economical."*

*"If he doesn't want to touch Le Mulan, the most likely reason is that this Le Mulan still has value to him — immense value, so much so that Zhang Yu is willing to defy the Reincarnation Immortal Emperor's mission."*

Recalling Zhang Yu's interactions with his numerous subordinates, his mastery over their various abilities, and even his act of replicating Dao Seeds during law establishment...

A bolt of lightning seemed to strike through the Herald God's mind: *"Zhang Yu... could it be that he replicates Dao Seeds and enhances Dao Seed Power through his interactions with his subordinates? Could it be that Le Mulan is also a Dao Seed owner, and he has already replicated and enhanced it?"*

*"He doesn't want to kill Le Mulan because he wants to continue possessing and enhancing this Dao Seed for greater profit?"*

At this moment, the Herald God suddenly felt that everything made perfect sense.

Meanwhile, Zhang Yu spoke up, "Le Mulan, after all, climbed all the way up with me from the First Layer. To kill her... my heart is filled with immense reluctance."

Saying that, Zhang Yu sighed, looked at Kuang Tianqing, and said, "You have to raise the price."
