# Chapter 939: The Immortal Emperor Bestows a 'Book', Successful Candidates of the New Sect Examination

Hearing Su Xianchen's words, Zhang Yu's heart suddenly jolted.

"He is actually going to reward me with a copy of a 'Book'?"

Regarding the legendary 'Books', Zhang Yu's feelings were very complex.

At first, he thought his Feather Book was a unique innate talent and potential exclusive to him, until he learned that many others in Kunxu also held a 'Book'.

This made Zhang Yu even doubt... whether his Feather Book was also arranged by someone, and that he was not special at all.

It wasn't until the Feather Book upgraded time and time again that Zhang Yu faintly felt... his Feather Book was probably still a very special thing.

At this moment, Zhang Yu asked curiously, "I wonder which 'Book' is being bestowed?"

Feeling Zhang Yu's expectant gaze, Su Xianchen chuckled and said, "It seems you also know of the existence of the Books?"

Zhang Yu nodded and said, "I have heard a little about it."

Su Xianchen asked again, "Then do you know of the four Books: Feather, Transformation, Flight, and Ascension?" [^1]

Zhang Yu's heart trembled as he said, "Feather, Transformation, Flight, and Ascension?"

"That's right," Su Xianchen said. "There are quite a few people in Kunxu who possess them. For instance, Tai Yuebai's 'Book of Dao Seeds' (Book of Seed Theft) is one of them."

"But if we're talking about the most formidable ones, it would naturally be the four Books: Feather, Transformation, Flight, and Ascension."

Zhang Yu asked, "Could it be... that what the Immortal Emperor intends to bestow upon me this time is one of these four Books?"

Su Xianchen smiled faintly. Instead of answering Zhang Yu's question, he said, "The origins of the four Books are incredibly mysterious. As far as I know... our Myriad Laws Sect only possesses one copy of the *Feather Book*..."

Hearing this, Zhang Yu's heart suddenly skipped a beat. "Is he hinting at me?"

Just as Zhang Yu was feeling alarmed, Su Xianchen continued, "This *Feather Book* is currently in the hands of the Reformist Faction's Fa Liuyuan..."

"He's not talking about my Feather Book?" Fa Liuyuan's figure surfaced in Zhang Yu's mind as he thought to himself, "Fa Liuyuan has one too?"

Su Xianchen continued, "And it's said that this *Feather Book* was obtained by the Traitor Ying from some unknown place back in the day, and was eventually passed down to the Reformist Faction's Fa Liuyuan."

Hearing these words, it was as if a bolt of lightning flashed through Zhang Yu's mind.

"*Feather Book*... Fa Liuyuan... Ying Xintian..."

"What exactly is the relationship between Fa Liuyuan's *Feather Book* and my *Feather Book*?"

"Brother Dao has seen another *Feather Book*?"

"Then... back then, I instantly learned *Kunlun in the Palm* right in front of Brother Dao."

At this moment, Zhang Yu recalled what Dao Qiankun had said and done during the final awards ceremony of the Ten Greats League...

Dao Qiankun: "So that's how it is... Then you definitely have to come with me."

Zhang Yu thought to himself, *When he said those words, could Brother Dao have already seen through my astonishing innate talent? Did he see my Feather Book?*

At this moment, Zhang Yu set a goal in his heart: *When I find the chance... I must find a way to see exactly what connection the two Feather Books have, and what the differences are.*

But in the next moment, Su Xianchen's words took Zhang Yu completely by surprise.

Su Xianchen glanced at the deep-in-thought Zhang Yu and continued, "It seems Minister Zhang is very interested in Heavenly Monarch Fa's volume of the *Feather Book*? If you can handle this matter beautifully, then it isn't impossible for Heavenly Monarch Fa's volume of the *Feather Book* to become yours, Minister Zhang."

Zhang Yu asked in astonishment, "The Immortal Emperor... is willing to bestow Heavenly Monarch Fa's *Feather Book* upon me?"

Su Xianchen smiled without a word, merely giving Zhang Yu a knowing look.

The Sect Examination Clone said, "Excellent! Leaving such a great treasure in the hands of the Reformist Faction is a waste anyway. It should be handed over to us so it can truly shine and contribute to the sect's construction."

The Herald God thought inwardly, *Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor, oh Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor, you really know how to lay bait and throw money around.*

Recalling their past interactions with this Immortal Emperor, the Herald God had to admit... every single time... the other party was able to offer a reward that Zhang Yu found impossible to refuse, making Zhang Yu always go all out, risking his life and venturing into danger at any cost.

Immortal Slayer sighed, "Offering this reward implies the Immortal Emperor has probably already noticed our special traits."

The Sect Examination Clone scoffed, "Grandpa Immortal Emperor possesses vast mystic powers, and his Immortal Dao Technology is number one in Kunxu! So what if he knows we have a Feather Book on us?"

Fu Ji: "We have a Feather Book on us?!!"

The Herald God thought inwardly, *So that's how it is. Are many of Zhang Yu's anomalies related to this Feather Book? Then what would happen if two Feather Books came together?*

"Merge? Devour each other? Combine with one another? Or will nothing happen, and they will just function independently?"

Meanwhile, the figures of Fa Liuyuan and Tai Yuebai surfaced in Zhang Yu's mind.

Immortal Slayer had already analyzed, "Fa Liuyuan is a seed candidate participating in the next Ascension Selection. The *Feather Book* in his hands should be very important to him."

"If we borrow the Immortal Emperor's power to snatch the *Feather Book*, it will inevitably affect Fa Liuyuan's Ascension Selection. I'm afraid our relationship with the Reformist Faction will only worsen in the future. When that time comes, Fa Liuyuan, Tai Yuebai... they might all become our complete enemies."

Zhang Yu knew in his heart that Immortal Slayer was right. His process of establishing the third-generation Law Edict this time had inevitably aroused the dissatisfaction of quite a few people in the Reformist Faction.

People like Tai Yuebai even believed that he had accelerated the arrival of an apocalyptic end.

It was only because it later became the general trend of events that the Reformist Faction brought out Fa Liuyuan to show goodwill to him again.

But if he were to take away the *Feather Book* passed down to the Reformist Faction by Ying Xintian, then the relationship between the two sides would surely plummet.

"Moreover..." Zhang Yu sighed in his heart, "Fa Liuyuan has no enmity or grudge against me, and he has always taken good care of me. Stealing his Feather Book..."

Immortal Slayer said, "It is indeed as you said. Furthermore, apart from facing the Reformist Faction, completing the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor's task this time means we also have to face the Full Attendance Immortal and Supreme Truth... The risks are not small."

"But these... are not reasons to give up."

Immortal Slayer said firmly, "To reach the absolute peak of the Immortal Dao, one must naturally face the highest risks in the world. And to change Kunxu, we are destined to make enemies of everyone in the world. This is a path we will have to walk eventually, sooner or later."

"As for the *Feather Book*, there is no one more suitable for it than you. Leaving it in the hands of the Reformist Faction would just be a waste; those bugs can't change Kunxu at all."

"If Grandmaster were here, he would definitely want you to take the *Feather Book* from Fa Liuyuan's hands."

Immortal Slayer continued, "Zhang Yu, I have a feeling that obtaining this second *Feather Book* holds extreme significance for our future ascent to the peak of the Immortal Dao and claiming the supreme throne."

"Even if we have to take the greatest risks, even if we have to make enemies of the Reformist Faction, we will not hesitate."

The Sect Examination Clone said, "What are you all muttering about! The Immortal Emperor has given an order, who would dare not fight to the death? So what if it's Fa Liuyuan? So what if it's Yun Taizhen? As long as they go against the Immortal Emperor's grand momentum, we'll deal with them!"

Fu Ji hurriedly said, "Right, right, if we are to deal with Yun Taizhen, it's impossible that they'd only make us fight alone; we should just be one link in the chain."

Zhang Yu sighed inwardly, "Making the Reformist Faction and me turn against each other, and using me as a knife to deal with Yun Taizhen, is this all within your calculations? Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor."

With thoughts flashing through his mind like lightning, Zhang Yu let out a sigh in his heart and said to Su Xianchen, "This Minister... will do his utmost and will definitely not let down the Imperial Grace."

Su Xianchen nodded, "Good, with these words of yours, I can rest assured."

At this point, Su Xianchen looked at Zhang Yu with a somewhat emotional gaze, thinking to himself, "To grant such favor, the Immortal Emperor truly values the legal reform, and he also values this Zhang Yu."

After ending the call with Su Xianchen, Zhang Yu continued to settle his mind, going about his daily work, cultivating, and earning money... making preparations for next year's mission. He looked just like he did before, but his heart was much deeper and heavier.

---

In the blink of an eye, it was October.

By now, Zhang Yu's dao heart had already reached level 55, his magic power had broken through 9,000,000 units, and his physical strength had reached 58.95 points.

He thought to himself, "It's a pity. Even though my magic power and physical body are breaking through very quickly, the time is simply too short. There isn't enough time for my Dao Heart level to grow."

Zhang Yu could feel that he was currently somewhat lacking control over his magic power and physical strength; they had long since reached the limit of what he could handle.

"I can only wait for my Dao Heart level to continue rising before I push my magic power and physical strength up again."

Zhang Yu estimated in his heart, feeling confident that by next year he could definitely push all his stats to the Void Refinement Peak.

And besides his basic stats, the cultivation arts Zhang Yu needed for his daily work and cultivation had also almost all been leveled up to level 60.

As for the Artifact Refinement Diagrams, during the upgrading process, Zhang Yu even brought Magnetic Pole and Ye Xingli along to begin innovating the production lines and laboratories within the Immortal Grotto-Heaven.

He thought to himself, "Besides basic stats, cultivation art levels, Dao Seeds, and Chain Formation Diagrams, these magical artifacts, Bionics, puppets, production lines, and laboratories... are also an important part of cultivation."

Massive amounts of Immortal Coins were continuously burned. Supreme Yang Radiance God, Mountain-Bearing Warrior, Earth-Overturning Warrior, Thousand-Mile Warrior, Heavenly Orbit Radiance God... rows of old production lines were upgraded, while swathes of new production lines were built.

Zhang Yu secretly estimated in his heart, "Before the end of this year, I must completely finish the production line upgrade, monetize the Void Refinement productivity I currently possess, and achieve a major increase in income."

Zhang Yu knew that his reputation had greatly increased now, and orders for various products had also grown along with it. It was exactly the time to increase production and boost his income, preparing a healthier cash flow for his future battles.

"Next year... I'm afraid there won't be as much peaceful time to develop."

While Zhang Yu was modifying the production line, Old Gao on the other side was also constantly learning and improving.

Through his efforts, Zhang Yu's Immortal Grotto-Heaven gained several hundred square meters in area without any change in its height.

And in the process of studying formations, an increasingly strong construction urge surged in Old Gao's heart.

"This won't do, the lines of Zhang Yu's Calculation Pool Array are all crooked."

"Also, that spiritual energy circuit isn't right either. Doing it this way will consume a lot more spiritual energy every day..."

In the end, Old Gao applied for a budget from Zhang Yu and began to modify the various formations inside the Immortal Grotto-Heaven.

Under Old Gao's arrangement, the spiritual energy circuits, the magic power circuits, as well as the positions and connection methods of the production lines in the Immortal Grotto-Heaven were adjusted several times, resulting in a noticeable improvement in production efficiency.

Moreover, the Calculation Pool Array was also rearranged, greatly enhancing heat dissipation, reducing power consumption, and even allowing mutual stacking of computing power to break through performance limits.

Besides that, the surveillance mechanisms inside and outside the Immortal Grotto-Heaven were gradually upgraded by Old Gao, becoming much stricter.

Old Gao sighed with emotion, "A formation is a poor man's magical artifact. Although it is not as convenient as a magical artifact and is not easy to carry, it has advantages that a magical artifact does not possess."

Looking at the report in front of him, Zhang Yu could see that under Old Gao's efforts, the monthly maintenance cost of his production line was reduced by about 50,000, while the monthly output value increased by about 70,000.

"Between the two, that's an extra income of 120,000 Immortal Coins every month."

Zhang Yu thought to himself, "It really wasn't a waste to pay for Teacher Gao's tutoring. Should I sign him up for a few more tutoring classes?"

Just as Zhang Yu was picking out tutoring classes, a message appeared before his eyes.

Ban Jinchen: "Hello Minister Zhang, apologies for the interruption. All our preparatory work is ready, and we would like to respectfully ask you to confirm your approximate arrival time so we can make overall arrangements in advance and await your visit and guidance."

The first batch of 16th layer inspection points Zhang Yu had planned was precisely the minor realm belonging to Ban Jinchen and Si Shian on the 16th layer of Kunxu.

Looking at the busy world before him, Zhang Yu thought to himself, "In Kunxu, the higher the floor, the more private space there is, and the less public space."

"This 16th layer is exactly like this. The Central Apex Heaven in the center is even smaller than the 11th layer, and all around it are various private minor realms. Inside are all sorts of large-scale laboratories, pill refinement rooms, and private production lines belonging to Void Refinement cultivators..."

Zhang Yu knew that upper-tier cultivators had a lot of money, but their expenses were also high.

Especially in experiments and cultivation; a single experiment by Void Refinement cultivators could consume enough funds to last many lower-tier cultivatiors a lifetime.

And in this Kunxu world of Immortal Dao Technology tyranny, in order to pursue stronger Immortal Dao Technology, all kinds of research and experiments were indispensable.

"If I want to promote my Law Edict on the 16th layer and complete the freezing of Law Edicts... I will need to extend its coverage into one private minor realm after another, covering the territories of these Void Refinement cultivators."

As soon as Zhang Yu arrived at Si Shian's minor realm, he saw Ban Jinchen and Si Shian waiting there.

Next, the two of them waited on him hand and foot along the way, introducing the situation in the minor realm to Zhang Yu.

Si Shian could be heard introducing, "Minister Zhang, our Four Seasons Minor Realm strictly enforces a daily Four-Hour Workday. The entire minor realm has had no record of overtime for 20 consecutive years, and it has always been a model minor realm for standard working hours."

Zhang Yu, however, frowned, sensing something odd. He thought to himself, without even the indispensable element of Paid Overtime, does this still sound like a sect?

He couldn't help but ask for the reason behind this.

Si Shian explained, "One word: punishment. Minister Zhang, let me tell you, if you want any Law Edict to be strictly enforced, it has to be achieved through punishments."

"During the first year I implemented the Four-Hour Workday, many in the lower-tier cultivators didn't take it seriously. They took the lead in illegally working overtime, forming a Dark Overtime Realm."

"At that time... various evil underground overtime syndicates emerged one after another, concealing and underreporting their overtime hours just to pay fewer fines."

Si Shian sighed with emotion, "The very first thing I did back then was use a Homicide Permit to strike down the cultivator with the highest overtime hours that year."

Ban Jinchen added from the side, "Then, according to the fine regulations for Paid Overtime, I subjected him to the strictest and most severe punishment, fining him until he was completely bankrupt!

"This case was supervised by the Eight Righteous Gods back then, and specifically praised by the Immortal Ascension Hall. It ultimately became the guiding case of that year."

At this point, a hint of smugness appeared on Ban Jinchen's face. "The Full Attendance Immortal's evaluation back then was that it could withstand the test of history."

Saying this, Ban Jinchen continued to share his law enforcement experience with Zhang Yu: "Those of lower cultivation in Kunxu are always trying to take advantage of upper-tier cultivators. If you yield even a single step, they will press forward relentlessly. You have to use thunderous methods to clean them up, fine them into bankruptcy, and fine them until the world is bright and clear..."

Facing the Zhang Yu before them — who had "single handedly fought with ten billion" and "forced back an Ascension Realm cultivator with a single sword strike"... someone so domineering — both Si Shian and Ban Jinchen desperately wanted to cling tightly to his thighs in the coming new era.

As a result, the two of them held absolutely nothing back when answering Zhang Yu's inquiries at this moment, telling him everything they knew.

However, Zhang Yu's frown deepened the more he listened. He suddenly asked, "Then with a four-hour daily work schedule, there must be people who can't finish the tasks at hand, right?"

Si Shian said, "Then fire them! I don't hire Sect Staff here, only outsourced cultivators! According to the Corporate Protection Law, firing these outsourced cultivators who are unfit for their positions doesn't require paying their monthly salary, which even saves us a sum of money."

Ban Jinchen then added, "And then we just hire more people. If they can't finish the work, we continue to fire them. If they proactively work overtime, we charge them a fee."

With a look of deep emotion, he said, "It is precisely by generating new revenue streams and cutting expenditures like this, lowering costs while increasing profits, that countless enterprises have managed to survive the difficult early stages of their business.

"It could be said that the 'Four-Hour Workday' and 'Pay for Overtime' have contributed immensely to building an excellent business environment..."

Although they knew Zhang Yu was going to freeze these two major Law Edicts next year, didn't they still have to be reviewed after the freeze? What if there was a chance they could pass the review?

Thinking of this, Ban Jinchen and Si Shian couldn't help but instinctively show off the superiority of their own Law Edicts.

Zhang Yu's brows, however, furrowed tighter and tighter the more he listened, until he finally said, "Alright, enough. You have a huge problem here."

The two exchanged a glance before hearing Zhang Yu continue, "Your Four-Hour Workday and Pay for Overtime have already gone against the general trend of the sect, and they are in serious conflict with the Two-Day Weekend that will be promoted in the future."

"When the time comes, if we have Dual Rest and only a Four-Hour Workday, how are we supposed to build up the sect?"

Seeing that the two still wanted to argue, Zhang Yu waved a hand to stop them from speaking and continued, "Next, you two need to find a pilot zone and try increasing the daily legal working hours."

"Compile all the impacts and various data changes into a report and give it to me as soon as possible."

Implementing the freezing of Law Edicts would inevitably impact the sect. Since Zhang Yu wanted to prepare in advance, he needed to conduct research and collect data ahead of time.

Hearing Zhang Yu's words, Ban Jinchen hastily asked, "Minister, how much time... should we add?"

After pondering for a moment, Zhang Yu said, "Let's increase it to 8 hours first and see."

Beside him, Si Shian was greatly alarmed. "Absolutely not, Minister! These lower-tier cultivators have long been accustomed to the daily Four-Hour Workday system. If we rashly increase it to 8 hours, they will only take advantage of the enterprises, using their working hours to cultivate and study..."

Ban Jinchen also chimed in, "Think of how much we'll lose in fines and revenue..."

Zhang Yu ignored their complaints, merely urging them to execute it as soon as possible.

Watching this scene, Immortal Slayer sighed inwardly: *It has finally begun. Zhang Yu's will is directly interfering with Kunxu. As we continue to climb, this world will be gradually changed by us in more and more places.*

Just as Zhang Yu was assigning tasks to the two, a familiar figure appeared in front of him. 

The magic power in Zhang Yu's body paused: "Profound Monarch Bu?"

Bu Yingshu came before Zhang Yu and said coldly: "Have you been hiding from me lately?"

Zhang Yu quickly explained: "How could I? It's just that official duties have been too busy. I was originally planning to come to the 16th layer today and go find you right after finishing my work."

To the side, Ban Jinchen thought to himself: *Minister Zhang, as they say, evil can never suppress a valid permit. When Profound Monarch Bu, an individual covered in Innate Permits, personally came to ask me about your whereabouts, I simply didn't dare not to answer.*

Bu Yingshu said: "Then are you done now?"

Zhang Yu: "Not yet..."

Before Zhang Yu could finish his answer, Bu Yingshu had already swept up a burst of magic power, grabbing him and rushing out of the minor realm. In Bu Yingshu's view... she couldn't care less whether he was done or not.

Bu Yingshu could be heard saying, "Didn't you say you wanted to raise the working hours to 8 hours? Alright then, let's start with you. You must accompany me for 8 hours of work today!"

Zhang Yu cried out in bitterness inwardly, suddenly feeling that the Four-Hour Workday might not be without its merits.

Watching the departing Zhang Yu, a hint of envy flashed in the eyes of Ban Jinchen and Si Shian.

"Sigh, Minister Zhang is good at everything, but he just lacks governance experience and doesn't understand the inherent vices of the lower-tier cultivators."

"An 8-hour workday, wouldn't that make them too comfortable? No, we must increase their workload."

While Zhang Yu cultivated, worked, and went around inspecting, Yu Xinghan had also smoothly taken over all aspects of work at the Netherworld Assets Department and the Soul Cultivator Company.

A cross-layer message from the lower realm appeared before his eyes.

Yu Xinghan replied, "The Sect Examination is a major event for the sect; it is not my place to judge. However, speaking of the specific recruitment requirements for the Netherworld Assets Department this time, I think we still need to consider the students from the Civil Engineering Department of Myriad Laws University."

"It's best if they have competition experience, which shows they are quite ambitious."

"I hope they have also participated in the Ten Billion Heaven Mending operation during the war period, showing a sense of responsibility."

"It would be great if they had rich work experience, like having worked in a large company such as the Great Void Cloud Repository founded by Minister Zhang, that would be the best."

"Also, students who have been in debt for over ten thousand Spirit Coins are also particularly good in my opinion. They have great perseverance, and our Netherworld Assets Department needs people like this..."

"Additionally, if they have outsourcing experience, it would be even better if it was with our Netherworld Assets Department..."

As Yu Xinghan proposed the conditions one by one, the other party finally said, "Currently, we have filtered out a few people who meet your requirements..."

Looking at the resumes of Mo Shangjin, Xiao Qingxuan, Shi Yunxiang, and Gongshu Jin in front of him, Yu Xinghan nodded in satisfaction. "Mhm, they all have outsourcing experience with our Netherworld Assets Department, and their other aspects also meet the requirements."

"Then you can handle it as you see fit. Please absolutely do not give them special care because of my connections..."

After ending the call, Yu Xinghan secretly thought, "After this batch, I can consider recruiting some students from the other Ten Greats."

Several figures instantly surfaced in Yu Xinghan's mind.

Just then, a message from Li Xuelian appeared before his eyes again.

Yu Xinghan replied, "Senior sister, I still want to focus on my work for now. I won't consider other matters for the time being..."

16th layer, Celestial Inquiry Wisdom Realm. This was a minor realm dedicated to the research and development of high-intelligence artifact spirits.

Cang Juehuan's Immortal Abode was located within it, and right now, he was waiting for Zhang Yu's arrival.

Beside him was Di Xuan of the Innate Dao Lineage.

As the great backer behind the high-intelligence artifact spirits, the Innate Dao Lineage could be said to be the true behind-the-scenes big boss of this place.

At this moment, the waiting Di Xuan thought to himself, *Facing Zhang Yu, who is currently in the limelight, fully cooperating with his inspection, not offering any direct resistance, and going with the flow of the general trend... this is the most correct choice, right?*

*This is the mindset of the weak.*

Di Xuan said inwardly, *But right now, in front of Zhang Yu, I happen to be a weakling.*

Although shortly after the Law Establishment was completed, Di Xuan had already stepped into the Tribulation Transcendence Realm, meaning both his rank and cultivation realm were above Zhang Yu's.

But he knew the current situation; he could no longer view Zhang Yu simply based on his rank and cultivation realm.

"Even if someone wants to resist, it definitely won't be my turn."

Seeing Zhang Yu descend, Di Xuan immediately went up to meet him. "Minister Zhang! It's so great that you're here. The many high-intelligence artifact spirits of our Celestial Inquiry Wisdom Realm are all eager to hear you speak and learn about Kunxu's new legal and systemic ideology."

"Celestial Inquiry Star, what are you spacing out for? Hurry up and greet Minister Zhang!"

Celestial Inquiry Star: "Hello, Minister Zhang. On behalf of all the high-intelligence artifact spirits, I extend my greetings to you."

In the blink of an eye, the time arrived in December.

News came from the Lower Realm Mutual Aid Group that Xiao Qingxuan, Shi Yunxiang, Mo Shangjin, and Gongshu Jin had respectively joined the group as Sect Staff, instantly triggering another wave of information frenzy.

Looking at the changes in the group, Wang Yin of the Netherworld Palace only felt his heart bleeding. "Dao Monarch Zhang is doing so well now. If I had known, I would have taken the exam for the Myriad Laws Sect back then!"

On the other side, the dragon-descendant Ying Mingchi of the Celestial Monster Sect was also watching the changes in the group, secretly thinking in his heart, "Sigh, if I had passed the exam for the Myriad Laws Sect back then, who could compete with me for the position of Dao Monarch Zhang's mount? Next year, when I go on a business trip to the Myriad Laws Sect with the Supreme Truth Immortal Clan, I must find an opportunity to pay my respects to Dao Monarch Zhang."

"Has the Blazing Sun Meridian upgraded?"

"It seems Xuan Ge has also obtained the Nascent Soul Certificate down below and broken through to the Nascent Soul Realm. Little Zhi handled things well."
