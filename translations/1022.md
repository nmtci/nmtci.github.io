# Chapter 1022 The Crown Prince Has It Rough

In the Lower Realm Mutual Aid Group.

Tian Shengong: "Sigh, if I had known, I should have taken the entrance exam for the Myriad Laws Sect back then."

"Due to a violation of Immortal Armaments Bureau sect regulations, display of your message has been restricted."

Seeing that the content he had just posted was immediately blocked, a wave of helplessness washed over Tian Shengong.

"Damn Immortal Armaments Bureau, now they're even censoring my personal messages?"

Tian Shengong only felt that the entire management of the Immortal Armaments Bureau had grown increasingly reckless, even descending into utter chaos.

As for the policy that supposedly abolished Realm Certificates, Tian Shengong had initially felt overjoyed. He had believed that his long-suppressed cultivation and his peerless talent would finally no longer be restricted, and that his strength, productivity, and earning power would all experience a massive surge.

Yet soon enough, the prices of materials required for breakthroughs skyrocketed, job requirements rose, salaries plummeted, and performance load ratios climbed... A cascade of changes filled Tian Shengong's heart with deep bitterness.

*"Cultivators from 10 years ago, 50 years ago, 100 years ago, 200... 400 years ago are all breaking through alongside me, competing against me."*

At this moment, he truly and acutely felt that once the restrictions were lifted, the competition within the sect had escalated to an unprecedented degree.

Even Tian Shengong — who had tested into the Ten Greats step by step, risen from the Lower Realm into the sect, and been an Involution King since childhood — felt a trace of dread looking at this spectacle of dozens of generations competing together.

*"Right up until the moment Realm Certificates were cancelled, they were still talking about quota-based cultivation and stable resource prices, telling us there was no need to hoard cultivation materials in advance."*

*"Now the fucking prices have skyrocketed across the board, and they tell me that everyone is primarily responsible for their own cultivation."*

At this moment, he envied the lower realm students who had been admitted to the Myriad Laws Sect even more.

*"Zhang Yu has already become the Crown Prince of Myriad Laws!"*

Tian Shengong could not help but look back to the past; when the Ten Greats League started, Zhang Yu had stood among the ranks of Myriad Laws University like a nobody.

*"Even in the semifinals, I only lost to him by a single narrow move — we were on the exact same level."*

*"And now? The gap between us is immeasurable. What does this prove?"*

*"There are massive problems with the Immortal Armaments Bureau's governance, especially when it comes to nurturing talent. The main reason such a vast chasm opened between me and Zhang Yu is that the Immortal Armaments Bureau held me back..."*

Just then, another message popped up in the group chat.

Wang Yin: "I think we should change the name of our Lower Realm Mutual Aid Group. How about the Blazing Heart Group?" [^1]

Ying Mingduan: "Great, great, great! Everyone in the group is a follower of Heavenly Monarch Blazing Light, so this is precisely what it should be called."

Someone remarked: "Since when did you become a follower of the Heavenly Monarch?"

Ying Ming: "Though I'm in the Celestial Monster Sect, the blood of His Highness the Crown Prince of Myriad Laws flows through my veins as well, so I count as half a Myriad Laws person."

Wang Yin: "Me too. Back then, Myriad University was the university I wanted to take the entrance exams for. Unfortunately, I filled out my application wrong. Otherwise, I'd also be a graduate of Myriad Laws University and a Sect Staff member of the Myriad Laws Sect now."

Glancing over the messages in the group, Yu Xinghan did not agree to the name change request for the time being. He thought to himself, *"Do foreign sect members like you even deserve to call yourselves Myriad Laws University people?"*

In Yu Xinghan's eyes, how could these people from other universities possibly compare to their direct lineage of the Myriad Great Gang? 

*"Only those who truly graduated alongside His Highness the Crown Prince and climbed up together from Myriad Laws University are the core members of the Crown Prince Faction."*

*"The Blazing Heart Group? If I really let you change the group's name to that, who knows if you won't use it to swindle people and make outsiders believe you're genuinely under the Crown Prince's banner."*

*"Still, the Blazing Heart Group is a good name. It's mine now, and I can use it somewhere else in the future..."*

Yu Xinghan even felt that Zhang Yu should leave this group. Otherwise, wouldn't merely staying in it allow these lower-cultivation foreign sect disciples take advantage of him?

However, his ponderings about the group chat were merely a wisp among the countless thoughts swirling in Yu Xinghan's mind right now.

His gaze was fixed intently on Ye Xingli, Feng Tingting, and the other cultivators of the Artifact Refinement Faction, wanting to see what positions Zhang Yu had promoted them to.

"Ye Xingli went to the Edict Pavilion to serve as Vice Pavilion Master, in charge of patents for artifact refinement technology?"

"Feng Tingting actually went with her, serving as a minister underneath her."

"Bei Wufeng, Jin Jun, and Yan Qianji are next in line to take charge of the Soul Cultivation departments and companies in the Former Days Graveyard?"

Cultivators like Ye Xingli and Feng Tingting had originally worked in the Former Days Graveyard, handling Soul Cultivation affairs.

However, on the day of the Book-Wielder Assembly, Tai Yuebai had taken them away for protection, safely escorting them back only after the incident was over.

Afterward, Tai Yuebai explained everything to Zhang Yu and greeted Ye Xingli and the others, which finally brought the matter to a close.

---

Looking at the promotion and appointment notifications, Feng Tingting exclaimed in pleasant surprise, "Senior Sister, His Highness the Crown Prince hasn't forgotten us! Entering the Edict Pavilion to oversee the management of artifact refinement technology patents is a heavyweight post — there are tons of perks to be had!"

Ye Xingli understood that in Kunxu, a world under the tyranny of Immortal Dao Technology, any post that managed such technology carried immense authority.

Looking at the message sent by Zhang Yu, Ye Xingli thought it through calmly and said, "Regardless of how much profit is involved, this time we must break the rules. Only by breaking the rules can we help the Crown Prince reap massive gains."

Feng Tingting asked, "Senior sister, with so many technology patents, won't this infringe upon the interests of the Immortals? Aren't you afraid?"

Ye Xingli replied, "I am, but I'm even more afraid that the Crown Prince won't reap enough. I would rather grab too much than fall short and leave him dissatisfied. Only if the Crown Prince is satisfied will our path forward last long. This time, even if we have to smash the rules to pieces, we must bring the Edict Pavilion entirely into the crown prince's grasp before establishing new rules."

Feeling a surge of dread, Feng Tingting said, "Senior sister, hearing you say that makes me even more uneasy. Are we going to fight a battle of wits against Immortals?"

Ye Xingli sighed and said, "Don't panic. The upper and lower echelons of the Sect are divided right now, and the Immortals' interests are entangled within it all. A conflict is bound to happen sooner or later, and the main battlefield won't be with us anyway."

Feng Tingting asked, "Then what should we do next?"

Ye Xingli: "Take up the post first."

---

In another corner of the Former Days Graveyard.

Looking at the notification of his promotion and transfer, Bei Wufeng felt a surge of smug satisfaction.

He glanced over at Jin Jun and Yan Qianji beside him and said, "Gentlemen, now that we're taking charge of the Former Days Graveyard, what are your plans?"

Jin Jun replied, "Repay the Crown Prince, of course. I'd say we should strive to boost our performance by more than 50% this year!"

Yan Qianji: "The many Soul Cultivators in the Former Days Graveyard have always been the Crown Prince's core base. Even if performance doesn't see massive gains, absolutely nothing can be allowed to go wrong."

Bei Wufeng said, "What you've both said is very good, but I believe the top priority right now is to sort and classify the monsterfolk Soul Cultivators and the human Soul Cultivators."

Following Zhang Yu's series of maneuvers, winds of change swept across the entire Myriad Laws Sect.

News of one cultivator after another being promoted, dismissed, or reassigned drew the attention of countless people.

Bu Yingshu, the current Pavilion Master of the Martial Arts Pavilion, was one of those observers.

Ever since Zhang Yu had become the Crown Prince of Myriad Laws, Bu Yingshu had been waiting.

Her very first thought back then had been: It would be so nice if Little Yu could just give the position of Crown Prince of Myriad Laws to me.

Yet Bu Yingshu knew that was impossible, so she shifted her train of thought: How will Zhang Yu raise my rank? What better post will he transfer me to?

However, neither news of a promotion nor a transfer ever came. What Bu Yingshu received instead was the news that all research on cultivation arts in the Martial Arts Pavilion had been suspended, and its R&D funds were frozen.

At that moment, panic began to set in for Bu Yingshu. *"I invested so much into Zhang Yu, and isn't he known for valuing old ties? He wouldn't suppress me, would he?"*

*"Could it be that great favors breed enmity? An original shareholder getting kicked out, an angel investor getting stabbed in the back..."*

*"Or is it because I am the Great Sage's Heir?"*

A series of suspicions ran continuously through Bu Yingshu's mind, leaving her with no choice but to wait on edge for further developments.

As news of various cultivators' promotions spread, resentment gradually welled up inside her heart.

*"Ying Xin? Some low-tier cultivation scrub who does Civil Engineering got promoted to Rank 4? And was even transferred to the Tax and Revenue Department?*

*"Am I really not even comparable to him?"*

Just as Bu Yingshu was growing angrier and angrier, she noticed she had received a message from Zhang Yu.

Just as Bu Yingshu finished browsing and was about to reply, she discovered that Bu Yuangui had already replied on her behalf.

---

Within the Feathered Ascension Grotto-Heaven.

Surging, turbulent magic power swept in incessantly, converging through layer upon layer of Magic Power Circuits and pouring into Zhang Yu's body.

At the same time, a vast amount of immortal aura also swept over, continuously captured by Zhang Yu to further upgrade and transform the cultivation environment of the entire Grotto-Heaven.

Relying on his authority as the Crown Prince of Myriad Laws, Zhang Yu easily mobilized vast quantities of foundational Immortal Dao Resources such as spiritual energy, magic power, and immortal aura.

This would further and significantly accelerate his cultivation.

However, when Zhang Yu wanted to mobilize a batch of medicinal pills, he encountered obstacle after obstacle.

Zhang Yu quickly realized why: "Even though we agreed to divide our rule between the upper and lower layers, and Immortal Venerable Profound Wisdom and I agreed to non-aggression, numerous assets on the 11th to 19th layers theoretically belong to the Immortals."

"Even if I want to mobilize many of these resources, those below wouldn't dare deliver them easily without approval from the Immortal faction."

Thinking of this, the Sect Examination Clone said in frustration, "We are the Crown Prince of Myriad Laws! With the Immortal Emperor gone, we are the foremost authority of the Myriad Laws Sect. How can there be anything in the sect that we cannot mobilize?!"

Soon, Zhang Yu discovered that it was not just medicinal pills — many supplies such as Bionics, flying swords, and Immortals, as well as numerous sect enterprises, companies, and research institutes, were entirely out of his control as well.

It was precisely because of this that Zhang Yu had been transferring personnel everywhere, hoping to seize control of more resources and power within the Myriad Laws Sect.

At the same time, before Zhang Yu's eyes, the sect management platform displayed the various options of the Edict Pavilion.

Just a short while ago, Zhang Yu had wanted to select a few cultivation arts to cultivate.

"After all, I’ve suffered for so long. Now that I’ve become the Crown Prince of Myriad Laws, it’s about time I cultivated a few Heaven-Opening cultivation arts." Zhang Yu had already made up his mind to cultivate several Heaven-Opening cultivation arts and Sect-Guarding techniques, assembling a powerful Chain Formation Diagram.

However, the first obstacle Zhang Yu encountered was similar to the previous problem.

Within the Myriad Laws Sect, the copyrights to many Heaven-Opening cultivation arts and Sect-Guarding Techniques were held in the hands of Immortals; even with his current status, he could not access them at will.

As for the remaining few Heaven-Opening cultivation arts he did have the authority to access, they were all sect assets, and Zhang Yu did not plan to cultivate them.

"These cultivation arts are all under the control of the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor. Every time I cultivate these top-tier cultivation arts, I fear it will establish a connection with his Dao Lineage."

"Especially... if I have to face the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor in the future, how can I fight him if the Heaven-Opening cultivation art I painstakingly practiced has already been cracked by him, or even has a backdoor left behind?"

"Sure enough, if I'm going to choose a cultivation art, it still has to be a black art."

Thus, Zhang Yu planned to retrieve Heaven-Opening-Tier and Sect-Guarding-Tier black arts through the Edict Pavilion.

"If I can find a black art belonging to the Great Sage's Heir, that would be even better."

Yet, during this attempt to retrieve them, Zhang Yu encountered two more problems.

"Ever since the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor left, the Dao Repository Catalog has been disconnected and unusable."

"And retrieving the black art records from the Edict Pavilion's internal archives actually requires the Immortal Emperor's approval? If the Immortal Emperor is absent, it even requires the consent of an Immortal Venerable or multiple Immortals?"

Moreover, the application Zhang Yu submitted had not been approved by the Immortals to this moment.

"These Immortals pay lip service to non-aggression, but they don't actually support me becoming the Crown Prince of Myriad Laws. They'll even clash with me over the disposal of many assets on the 11th through 19th layers."

At this moment, Zhang Yu once again felt how the Immortal faction constrained his authority as the Crown Prince of Myriad Laws.

He channeled his talisman and asked the Great God of Heavenly Knowledge of All Questions how such an approval process could be bypassed.

The answer he received was simple: change the Edict Pavilion's procedures and rules through the Immortal Ascension Hall.

Zhang Yu thought to himself, "As expected, thoroughly controlling the Immortal Ascension Hall is an indispensable step to taking command of the Myriad Laws Sect."

With a mere thought, the roster of members in the Immortal Ascension Hall appeared before him.

"The simplest approach would certainly be directly promoting someone I trust."

"However, Yu Xinghan and the others have cultivation bases that are far too low. Even if they get promoted, they wouldn't be able to hold their ground."

Zhang Yu knew very well that the Immortal Ascension Hall, as a central organ, was filled with Ascension Realm cultivators who represented different factions, with the interests of various Immortals and Dao Lineages behind them.

If he promoted people who merely held positions without any Immortal backing or an aligned faction of their own, they would find themselves constrained at every turn, unable to exercise any real power.

"I'll make use of the people already in the Immortal Ascension Hall first. Once Yu Xinghan and the others grow stronger, I can replace these old-timers."

"Who among these people would be useful?"

Zhang Yu's gaze drifted over Bu Yuangui, and he asked, "Fu Ji, how did your chat with Bu Yingshu go?"

Fu Ji analyzed, "The person talking to me probably wasn't Bu Yingshu."

"However, I just checked, and there's no mark of the Celestial Inquiry Star either."

"It might be some high-ranking member of the Immortal Clan. At present, it seems the Immortal Clan leans toward our side."

---

Within a Spirit Realm space.

Under the enveloping divine power of several Great Gods, Zhang Yu began granting audiences to each Ascension Realm cultivator to confirm their stance.

The first one he received was Bu Yuangui.

Looking at Zhang Yu, who was surrounded by Great Gods, Bu Yuangui appeared increasingly deferential.

Zhang Yu said, "Do you wish to break the Immortal Clan's ban on achieving immortality?"

Hearing Zhang Yu's direct question, Bu Yuangui felt a jolt in his heart as an overwhelming greed surged within him.

After pondering for a mere moment, he immediately dropped to his knees in homage.

"Greetings, Crown Prince Your Highness!"

Bu Yuangui thought to himself, *"Yingshu, an Immortal is indeed about to emerge from our Immortal Clan — and that will be your father."*

A moment later, Zhang Yu received Ling Chenji from the Innate Dao Lineage Faction.

This cultivator, who had the appearance of a young married woman, had also been one of the judges during the initial third-generation law establishment.

At this moment, she likewise bowed down to the ground, displaying the hallmark trait of the Innate Dao Lineage and expressing an attitude of utter submission.

The Sect Examination Clone laughed heartily and said, "This Innate Dao Lineage was certainly a nuisance back when we had lower-tier cultivation. But after becoming the Crown Prince of Myriad Laws, I've realized this Innate Dao Lineage does have its merits after all."

Zhang Yu sighed internally, *"They can be used for now, but who knows if this Innate Dao Lineage will turn against us if we face the Immortal Emperor in the future."*

Meanwhile, another strand of Zhang Yu's consciousness met with Yi Yaosheng.

After being kicked out of the Immortal Ascension Hall by Zhang Yu, this former Ascension Realm cultivator felt nothing but boundless regret.

The moment he saw Zhang Yu, he eagerly admitted his fault, "Your Highness Crown Prince, this old subject was foolish and shortsighted. Please forgive me, Your Highness."

Looking at the ten million Immortal Coins sent by the other party, Zhang Yu said indifferently, "Is this what you're apologizing to me with?"

Yi Yaosheng said, "Your Highness Crown Prince, the situation is turbulent nowadays, and this old subject's hands are tied for funds as well. However, this old subject knows that Your Highness is worried about the current state of affairs and must want to mobilize all kinds of medicinal pills within the sect, right?"

With that, Yi Yaosheng began airing his grievances while simultaneously explaining what kind of assistance he could offer Zhang Yu if he were allowed to return to the Immortal Ascension Hall.

On the other side, Fa Liuyuan, acting as a representative within the Immortal Ascension Hall, also met with Zhang Yu.

Looking at Zhang Yu, who was surrounded by Great Gods, Fa Liuyuan could not help feeling a slight surge of emotion.

He still remembered the very first time he met Zhang Yu to confer a Superior Merit upon him. He never could have imagined that just a few years later, it would be his turn to be granted an audience by him.

Fa Liuyuan also voiced his stance: "Your Highness the Crown Prince, with the apocalypse looming near and Your Highness being the successor recognized by Martial Uncle Ying Xintian, our Reformist Faction will naturally support you with all our might."

Zhang Yu asked, "Good. Then what if I wish to implement reforms?"

"Reforms?" Fa Liuyuan was slightly taken aback and asked, "How do you intend to reform?"

Zhang Yu said, "Senior Fa Liuyuan, though I occupy this position of Crown Prince and may appear awe-inspiring, holding boundless sway over Myriad Laws, in truth, my head is crowded with all kinds of Immortals."

"Even to allocate a single batch of medicinal pills, I have to act according to the whims of the Pharmaceutical Faction's Immortals."

"Under such circumstances, how can we unite our strength to resist the apocalypse?"

Fa Liuyuan understood in an instant — Zhang Yu wanted to centralize power.

*"He doesn't just want to be the Crown Prince of Myriad Laws; he wants to go a step further and become the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor — or at least the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor across layers 1 to 19."*

Although Fa Liuyuan felt Zhang Yu's reasoning held merit, he was not optimistic.

After all, aside from their immense strength, the Immortal Class controlled countless cultivators and assets across Layers 11 to 19, weaving an intricately entangled web of vested interests.

Fa Liuyuan advised, "Your Highness the Crown Prince, this matter requires careful, long-term deliberation."

Zhang Yu sighed. "Do you think I don't want to proceed with caution and deliberate thoroughly? But the apocalypse is nearing, and The End is imminent; there is no longer time for drawn-out deliberation."

"Furthermore, my demands are not excessive. Although the 21st layer and above hold the essence of the sect, I can leave them alone. However, the Immortal Class must no longer interfere with layers 1 through 19."

Right then, Fa Liuyuan's gaze shifted slightly, and he said to Zhang Yu, "Your Highness, two Immortals wish to speak with you."

A moment later, projections of Immortal Roujia and Immortal Yi Xuan from the Reformist Faction descended one after another, appearing before Zhang Yu.

On the day the ten Immortal Emperors initiated the decisive battle, these two Immortals had their authority over the Kunlun Dao Lineage stripped by Ying Xintian, yet not the slightest trace of resentment or abnormality could be seen on their faces now.

Immortal Roujia spoke indifferently, "Zhang Yu, do you know that by issuing thirteen consecutive reassignment orders in the Immortal Ascension Hall and kicking out representatives of various factions, you have already angered several Immortals?"

Immortal Yi Xuan nodded slightly. "Fortunately, out of respect for us, they are willing to give you a chance. As long as you invite back the cultivators you kicked out, we can pretend this never happened."

Seeing that Zhang Yu did not answer, Immortal Roujia frowned and said, "Zhang Yu, don't think that just because Immortal Venerable Profound Wisdom agreed to a pact of non-aggression with you, the Immortals are powerless against you."

"Yes, the Immortals truly do not wish to clash with the Eight Divisions Great Gods by your side right now."

"But do you have any idea how many supplies the 11th through 19th layers have to provide to the Immortals every month? It is the demands of the Immortal Class that sustain more than half the cultivators in this sect."

"If sanctions are imposed and all transactions cease throughout the sect, leading to severed trade, collapsing enterprises, and fleeing assets, what will you do then?"

"Furthermore, if supplies of various critical Immortal Dao materials are cut off and licenses for Immortal Dao Technology are revoked, just how long do you think you can hold out?"

Immortal Yi Xuan advised, "Zhang Yu, though you are the Crown Prince of Myriad Laws, your foundation is shallow. You must consider the Immortals' perspectives more when doing things, rather than acting recklessly."

"In the future, do not mobilize the cultivators of the Immortal Ascension Hall without the Immortals' consent, lest it trigger unrest in the sect that you cannot control."

At this moment, Zhang Yu once again deeply felt the Immortals' direct interference, as well as the heavy constraints bound to his position as Crown Prince.

The Sect Examination Clone said irritably, "Do those Immortal Livestock up there want to act like Empress Dowagers and Retired Emperors? How can we allow this? Our power within the Myriad Laws Sect shouldn't have any boundaries! Sect enterprises, companies, research institutes, laboratories... all of them must be under our control!"

Immortal Slayer said, "Zhang Yu, remember this: we are only fighting for power and profit to gather our strength and counter the apocalypse."

The Sect Examination Clone said, "Zhang Yu, these Immortal Dao monopolists of Kunxu will only make things worse if left to their own devices. Kunxu can only head toward a healthy state if we are the ones in control of everything."

Listening to the argument between the two wills, the Herald God thought to himself, *"The Sect Examination Clone has gotten smarter. He actually knows how to use opposing the Immortal Dao Monopoly as an excuse to cover his own tracks."*
