# Chapter 1014: Secrets of the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor

Just as winds surged and clouds gathered in the outside world, Zhang Yu had focused his entire mind on his Feather Book, striving to comprehend the new chapter within and read the information surfacing across it.

Having formed the Dao Succession Chapter by fusing the Transformation Book, Zhang Yu looked at those lines detailing the lost history of the Immortal Emperors, unable to hold back a deep sigh in his heart.

"Since Celestial Monsters, White Bone, and Joyous Union have histories that were rolled back and guided like this, I wonder if the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor does as well."

Right at that moment, Desolate Ox spoke up, "This rolled-back history of the Celestial Monster Immortal Emperor is a bit strange."

Hearing this, Zhang Yu's thoughts stirred, and he said, "Indeed. It says here that he was the last Monster Saint and founded the Celestial Monster Sect, but I remember from the memories Desolate Ox Great Sage passed down to us..."

The Desolate Ox in Zhang Yu's mind possessed a portion of the Desolate Ox Great Sage's memories. At this moment, he said solemnly, "I was the true founder of the Celestial Monster Sect."

Immortal Slayer conjectured, "Is it possible that the period of history you remember was already rolled back? That your memory is actually a piece of lost history?"

Desolate Ox said, "If that were true, why would I remember any of this? Shouldn't I have forgotten these memories along with the World Rollback?"

The Sect Examination Clone had a sudden flash of insight and analyzed, "Desolate Ox, don't you think the contents of your memories bear a resemblance to this Celestial Monster Immortal Emperor's lost history? Both involved guiding humans and monsters to coexist, both involved founding the Celestial Monster Sect... Could it be that you are the Celestial Monster Immortal Emperor, and the Celestial Monster Immortal Emperor is you?"

Desolate Ox denied it, "Impossible. Even now, at least a portion of the Desolate Ox Great Sage's intent is suppressed deep within the Spirit Realm. How could he and the Celestial Monster Immortal Emperor be the same person? Besides, this lost history also stated that the Celestial Monster Immortal Emperor had already reincarnated into a human, which the Desolate Ox Great Sage never did."

"Furthermore, the Desolate Ox Great Sage tried all sorts of ways to change the world, doing far more than this Celestial Monster Immortal Emperor ever did."

Zhang Yu recalled that portion of the Desolate Ox Great Sage's memories he had acquired — it recorded the Desolate Ox Great Sage's repeated attempts to change the world.

In the past, when Zhang Yu viewed those memories, he had merely lamented how twisted human nature was in Kunxu, and how everything would be distorted beyond recognition under the drive of the Dao Lineages.

Yet having lived through countless events and grasped all manner of hidden secrets, looking back now upon those fragments of the Desolate Ox Great Sage's memory sparked a sudden intuition within Zhang Yu: "Were the Desolate Ox Great Sage's experiences also the result of repeated rollbacks? Perhaps many of his original attempts didn't truly fail on their own, but were instead driven to failure across rollback after rollback."

"Furthermore, what he did bears many similarities to the various Monster Saints from that lost history."

"The Desolate Ox Great Sage... His memories share similarities with the lost history of the Celestial Monster Immortal Emperor, as well as with the various Monster Saints, yet he doesn't seem to be the same person as them. In particular, a portion of his intent still lingers deep within the Spirit Realm, and it was even used by the Heavenly Court as bait."

The more Zhang Yu pondered, the more he felt layers upon layers of hidden truths at play, as though cloaked in dense, overlapping mists.

A few conjectures arose in his mind, but ultimately, lacking sufficient evidence for now, he could only suppress these stray thoughts and continue comprehending the Feather Book before him.

This time, he focused his intent on another newly emerged chapter.

It was the new chapter born after the Feather Book fused with the Flight Book — the Pseudo-Dao Chapter.

Meanwhile, within the Myriad Laws Sect.

Tai Yuebai’s figure flickered as he constantly shifted between his Flesh & Blood State and Primordial Spirit State, continuously altering his Spirit Realm data. Like a glimmer of light sweeping across heaven and earth, he leaped between the different tiers of Kunxu in an imperceptible manner.

“The frequency of Senior Ying sending me messages is decreasing.”

Tai Yuebai thought to himself, “It means the Immortal Emperors are tracking him down with increasing urgency.”

Tai Yuebai knew that Ying Xintian and the Reincarnation Immortal Emperor had reached some sort of agreement, allowing Ying Xintian to dispatch a clone to seize the Immortal Ancestor's legacy.

Yet, Tai Yuebai understood even better that this was inherently a trap.

“Even if the one on stage is merely a clone, it still requires Senior Ying’s control in the end. At this very moment, the Immortal Emperors are likely using that clone to trace Senior Ying's actual location in reverse.”

“And with the slightest carelessness, Senior Ying will be caught.”

However, what surprised Tai Yuebai even more was that Ying Xintian had clearly given up on vying for the Immortal Ancestor's legacy at the final moment.

“Is it because of Zhang Yu?” Recalling the recording of Zhang Yu, Tai Yuebai pondered inwardly, “Did Senior Ying approve of Zhang Yu's true heart, willing to entrust the Immortal Ancestor's legacy to him?”

"Because Senior Ying Xintian understands that even if he successfully prevents the Ten Immortal Emperors from obtaining the Immortal Ancestor's legacy, he will still find himself besieged by them. In the worst-case scenario, if he is suppressed by the ten Immortal Emperors, his only choice would be to destroy the Immortal Ancestor's legacy and self-destruct to prevent himself from being consumed by them. Yet even then, that would merely delay the time before the ten Immortal Emperors step into the End."

"Therefore, considering this worst-case possibility, Senior Ying Xintian intends to leave the Immortal Ancestor's legacy to Zhang Yu, and then send Zhang Yu away, leaving behind a beacon of hope capable of defying the Immortal Emperors in the future."

"Can this plan succeed? Can Zhang Yu be sent away successfully? Can Senior Ying Xintian escape the suppression of the ten Immortal Emperors?"

Tai Yuebai did not know, yet he understood that things had come to this point, and he no longer had the right to choose.

In the next moment, Tai Yuebai had already appeared in the Former Days Graveyard.

"Many cultivators of Zhang Yu's core circle are here, aren't they?"

The purpose of his journey was to bring away as many of Zhang Yu's companions as possible, so that when the time came, they could be sent away together with Zhang Yu to support and foster Zhang Yu's growth.

Inside the Reformist Faction's Immortal Abode.

A stone traversed layers of spatial barriers, appearing directly before the two Immortals in complete, silent stealth.

*"This kind of method..."* Immortal Roujia felt a sudden jolt in her heart as she watched this scene unfold. "Senior Brother, is it truly you who has returned?"

Memories of Wang Guiren's various actions at the Book-Wielder Assembly surged through her mind.

The first time she witnessed what he did, she had detected that familiar touch, yet reason had told her that Ying Xintian should not have returned.

On the other side, Immortal Yi Xuan sensed the Grotto-Heaven force on the stone, looked at the words carved upon it, and spoke slowly, "It truly is Senior Brother. He has returned, and what's more, he intends to make a massive uproar."

Reading the carved text, Immortal Roujia said in alarm, "Unite the Immortal Class of the Ten Great Sects? Is Senior Brother trying to rebel against all the Immortal Emperors?"

Even though she had already achieved Immortality, established her Dao, and become part of the Immortal Class that transcended the sects, seeing Ying Xintian's plan now still made her heart pound with a long-forgotten dread.

Right then, Immortal Yi Xuan murmured beside her, "Senior Brother speaks no lies."

Pointing at the words left by Ying Xintian, he continued, "This truly is a moment of crisis for Kunxu."

"The Ten Immortal Emperors wish to carve up and devour the Immortal Ancestor's legacy... Whether that triggers an internal war or one of the Immortal Emperors achieves a breakthrough, I fear the entire Ten Great Sects and the whole Immortal Class will never have a chance to turn the tables again."

"It is only because the awe and majesty of the Ten Immortal Emperors run so deep, and the Immortals of the various sects guard against one another, making it hard to join forces. No one dares to stick their neck out at this moment."

"Yet now, if Senior Brother raises the banner, given his unquestionable integrity and the current state of affairs, he could truly win the trust of the Immortals from all sects and stir up the overall situation across the Ten Sects."

Immortal Roujia looked at Immortal Yi Xuan beside her and asked, "Have you decided?"

Immortal Yi Xuan said, "The Ten Great Imperial Beasts have scourged the realm for so long that discontent has already reached a boiling point. Now, with the Immortal Ancestor's legacy as the catalyst, and our eldest Senior Brother rallying the call, it is the best opportunity to unite all the Immortals under heaven and rise up against them together."

The next moment, his tone shifted as he added, "Once Senior Brother Ying makes his move and the Immortals from the Great Sects enter the fray, we will strike together."

Immortal Roujia nodded slightly, thinking to herself, *"Judging by past experience, we in the Reformist Faction must still conserve our strength and not end up as cannon fodder. Only after the Immortals of the various sects have entered the field one after another will it be our prime opportunity to rise with the momentum."*

Within the space of Kunxu's 25th layer.

Ying Xintian's intent swept silently across heaven and earth, constantly shifting his position through continuous spatial leaps.

One moment he was on the 25th layer, the next on the 12th layer, and an instant later he reappeared on the 24th layer...

Amid these ceaseless, ultra-high-speed teleports, Ying Xintian could feel ten world-spanning wills tightly pursuing his trail.

Yet because these ten wills constantly obstructed one another and gave off the impression of holding back without exerting full effort, they had so far failed to completely catch up with him.

"Even so, it's only a matter of time before they catch me. I don't have much time left."

At the same time, massive amounts of information were inscribed by him onto various flowers, grasses, bamboos, and stones, delivered directly into the immortal abodes of various Immortals.

"Right now, in their struggle for the Immortal Ancestor's legacy, the Ten Immortal Emperors are directing most of their attention and power toward guarding against one another."

Ying Xintian understood that once Zhang Yu digested the Immortal Ancestor's legacy, the Immortal Emperors would inevitably try to fight for control over Zhang Yu, seeking to master the legacy's contents through him.

As for whether Zhang Yu winning meant yielding the entire Immortal Ancestor's legacy solely to the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor? Completely deciding the ownership of the Immortal Ancestor's legacy through a competition among lower-tier cultivators?

Ying Xintian understood that things naturally wouldn't be so simple; how could the Ten Immortal Emperors easily yield such immense benefits to someone else?

"Zhang Yu's victory, at most, gives the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor an advantage in carving up the spoils. As for how it will be divided precisely... they will certainly have to keep negotiating, keep fighting."

"Especially with Myriad Laws gaining the upper hand, the other Immortal Emperors will be all the more wary of him and seek to obstruct him. After all, the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor is one of the Immortal Emperors closest to achieving a breakthrough."

"Therefore, while tracking me, the Immortal Emperors will worry that exerting too much effort might entangle them with me, causing them to lose ground in the division of the Immortal Ancestor's legacy."

Ying Xintian knew this was precisely why the might of the Ten Immortal Emperors had failed to catch up to him immediately.

"This also happens to give me an opportunity, buying me some time to lay down my plans and reach out to the Immortal Class of the various sects."

Although he called it contacting the Immortal Class, Ying Xintian knew his primary targets were the Immortal Venerables of each sect — those who were firmly suppressed under the thumbs of the Immortal Emperors, constantly fearing they would be carved up and devoured themselves.

"Supreme Truth was suppressed, and because of that, her accumulations and foundational reserves all fell into the hands of the Ten Immortal Emperors."

"Now, with the gradual revival of the Immortal Ancestor's legacy, these Immortal Venerables should slowly come to realize that once the Immortal Emperors divide and consume it, unpredictable consequences will be unleashed."

Ying Xintian thought to himself, "Among them, Myriad Laws, Nine Nethers, and the Buddha Emperor are the most likely to approach... or even break through to that cultivation realm during this process."

Ying Xintian could estimate that for one of the ten Immortal Emperors to achieve a breakthrough, they were still short of roughly two Immortal Emperors' worth of accumulated power.

However, whether the Immortal Ancestor's legacy was equivalent to one or two Immortal Emperors was something he could not accurately gauge at this moment.

"Yet, as long as any one of the ten Immortal Emperors... nears that state and shows signs of a breakthrough, it will very likely trigger a civil war among the ten Immortal Emperors."

"Once a civil war among the ten Immortal Emperors breaks out, they will completely abandon any plans for long-term investment, madly devouring everything to convert it into immediate combat power."

"When that time comes, the entire Kunxu will be reduced to an ultimate battlefield. From the 36 layers above to the 18 layers underground, everything in heaven and earth will become resources for the Immortal Emperors to wage war."

"If the rest of the Immortal Class wish to protect themselves in this process, they must band together tightly, making the Immortal Emperors wary enough not to strike at them rashly."

Ying Xintian sighed inwardly: "Until the very last moment, those of the Immortal Class are unwilling to sacrifice their own interests to step forward. And the appearance of the Immortal Ancestor's legacy this time is a crisis, yet it is also an opportunity to trigger a turning point."

"Only under such an unprecedented crisis is it possible for the Immortal Venerables and Immortals of each sect to join forces."

"At the same time, once internal strife erupts among the ten Immortal Emperors and they fail to reach a consensus, the world will also be unable to rollback."

"Reincarnation Immortal Emperor... is this the situation you wanted as well?"

Thoughts flashed through his mind at lightning speed, and Ying Xintian had already made up his mind: "But for me, this alone is not enough. Even if the Immortal Class joins forces, they can at most restrain the Immortal Emperors; it would be very difficult to make them take the initiative to fight the Immortal Emperors."

"And during this period, if an accident happens to me and I am suppressed by the ten Immortal Emperors, then there will be no one left to raise the banner." At this moment, Zhang Yu's figure surfaced in Ying Xintian's mind: "What a pity. If only you could have appeared a bit earlier."

"Now, all I can do is try my best to buy you some time to grow."

"Just hide behind me, and in the future I carve out for you, try to bring a bit more hope to Kunxu."

As Zhang Yu comprehended the Pseudo-Dao Chapter from the Feather Book, waves of thought surged within him.

"The so-called Pseudo-Dao is a state caught between a Law Decree and a Dao Lineage."

"By sacrificing vastness in scale, it allows a Law Decree to advance into a Dao Lineage within a localized scope, creating an objective rule over a small area."

Zhang Yu thought to himself, "This Pseudo-Dao Chapter is indeed a shortcut. However, while a Dao Lineage established this way is rapid, it will later require a tremendous amount of time and accumulation to make up for its flaws."

The shortcuts to achieve Immortality opened by the Feather Book, Transformation Book, and Flight Book flashed across his mind, and he compared them briefly.

"The Feather Book establishes one's own Dao Lineage quickly and broadly through parasitizing an existing Dao Lineage, making it truly the most comprehensive and superior method."

"In comparison, the Transformation Book can only inherit the ownerless Dao Lineages of the ancients, leaving a limited range of choices. As for the Flight Book, while it can establish a Dao Lineage with lightning speed, its range is extremely limited, requiring a massive expenditure of resources afterward to compensate for it."

As his thoughts flickered, Zhang Yu's gaze had already turned to the history emerging upon the Pseudo-Dao Chapter.

"Another lost history? This time it's..."

Upon seeing the content written there, Zhang Yu was inwardly startled. "The Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor?"

Zhang Yu soon summarized the text's contents, sighing inwardly, "Before he even achieved Immortality, the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor had already perceived the existence of the World Rollback and mastered a method to transmit information before rollbacks occurred."

"Taking advantage of one rollback after another, he continuously changed identities and built up his foundation. In the least conspicuous manner possible, he constantly hid outside the gaze of those behind the World Rollback, continuing to grow in the crevices of history."

"He was like a background extra with no presence, playing different bit parts across one lost historical tale after another, becoming a stowaway across the World Rollbacks."

"Until, gradually, he grew unwilling to hide in the dark, unwilling to see his achievements rolled back time and again, unwilling to live in daily terror, hiding outside the gaze of the Rollbacker..."

Immortal Slayer sighed with emotion, "Indeed. If one can be aware of the rollback's existence, transmit messages, and even exploit the rollbacks to accumulate advantages, one could truly achieve rapid growth."

The Sect Examination Clone said enviously, "If we hadn't been noticed by the Immortal Emperor when we realized the existence of the rollback world, maybe we would have had such an opportunity too — hiding beyond the Immortal Emperor's gaze, accumulating advantages across rollbacks, and continually growing."

Zhang Yu said, "But the prerequisite is that while still relatively weak and before drawing any attention, one must become aware of the rollback's existence and master the ability to transmit messages. Just how did Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor manage to accomplish this?"

At this moment, Zhang Yu was thoroughly baffled. He only felt that Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor, who was already enigmatic to begin with, now seemed all the more unfathomable.

At the same time, a doubt could not help but flicker in his mind: "Also, who exactly wrote this lost history?"

As he looked through the words emerging on the book, Zhang Yu felt as if he were watching a calm onlooker, quietly recording every single event that had unfolded in Kunxu's history.

Originally, Zhang Yu had thought that his future self had acquired this information through some channel and written it down. But regarding such deep secrets of the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor, Zhang Yu could not help but wonder now: had he truly written this himself? The stray thought flashed through his mind, and he continued reading, summarizing the information in his thoughts.

"To resist the world's rollback, and to seize the Supreme Immortal Artifact from the hands of the one who initiated it, the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor began a long period of observation and testing."

"Step by step, he determined the rules of the World Rollback, confirmed the other party's identity, and found their weakness."

"The one who initiated the rollback knew nothing of the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor hiding in the shadows. Yet in the eyes of the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor, the Rollbacker, in their bid to alter the course of history, would inevitably expose their own information over time. This was the Rollbacker's weakness."

"Armed with this information disparity, the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor was able to quietly observe the other party's performance through rollback after rollback, seeking out their flaws and continually widening the information gap between them."

"Finally, one day, amidst his protracted spying... he found the opportunity to seize the Supreme Immortal Artifact."
