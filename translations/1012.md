# Chapter 1012: The Four Books Gather

As the numbers, names, and rankings appeared one after another, countless cultivators began analyzing what they truly meant.

Meanwhile, as a recipient of the Celestial Evolution Award who had observed the performance of all ten book-wielders in their entirety and retained a memory of every action each book-wielder took within the Immortal Emperor Simulator, Heavenly Monarch Overdrive made a discovery after a round of calculations.

"The changes in the numbers on this leaderboard are linked to the changes in their worlds."

"The rankings previously predicted by the major platforms were mostly based on the strength and productivity demonstrated by the book-wielders in the simulator, lacking more detailed supporting data."

"What the data on this leaderboard is tallying now... is likely the more precise Immortal Dao Gross Product."

Heavenly Monarch Overdrive explained in the livestream, "On this leaderboard, the trend of data changes for all the book-wielders corresponds to the trend of changes in the Immortal Dao Gross Product they generated in their respective Immortal Emperor Simulators."

"Changes in average cultivation, growth in Immortal Dao products, changes in living and deceased cultivator populations, the spread of Immortal Dao Technology, advancements in Immortal Research... The Immortal Ancestor's rankings weren't made arbitrarily. I estimate that all data related to the Immortal Dao Gross Product has been integrated into it."

Hearing this speech from Heavenly Monarch Overdrive, the nearby  Heavenly Monarch Redundancy agreed as well, "Indeed, to calculate the returns from developing an entire world, one naturally has to cover every aspect. Using the Immortal Dao Gross Product is fairly accurate."

At the same time, looking at the rankings, Source Saint from the Immortal Armaments Bureau had already soared skyward, his rank rapidly climbing.

Heavenly Monarch Redundancy looked at the shifts on the rankings and commented, "Source Saint's Immortal Dao Gross Product has far outstripped everyone else's, demonstrating a dominant advantage and firmly holding the first-place spot."

"This is the advantage of the Immortal Armaments Bureau's open-source world approach. By providing vast amounts of memory and wisdom, they trained these ancient cultivators into qualified modern talents at the fastest speed, causing the Immortal Dao Gross Product of the entire world to rise dramatically as a result."

Source Saint had also started a livestream at this moment. Looking at his solitary lead on the rankings, he said with immense pride, "This is the world's number one open-source power of our Immortal Armaments Bureau! Once again, this proves that open-sourcing is the optimal solution for driving Kunxu's development and boosting Immortal Dao productivity!"

"If anyone wants to purchase high-quality products from the Extreme Dragon Group, feel free to click the link on my lower right..."

For a time, countless cultivators of the Immortal Armaments Bureau throughout the entire Spirit Realm were riding high on momentum, and advertisements promoting various Immortal Armaments Bureau products dominated every advertising slot.

However, as the years on the rankings steadily climbed year after year, Tian Feibao of the Heavenly Sword Sect began launching a fiercely aggressive charge toward the number one spot.

Heavenly Monarch Redundancy said in astonishment, "Tian Feibao modified the emotions and thoughts of sentient beings with his sword intent. After several years of accumulation, he has also trained the entire world into qualified employees who wholeheartedly serve him. His efficiency is even higher than Source Saint's!"

Heavenly Monarch Overdrive also sighed with emotion, "Unified thought, absolute loyalty, no superfluous emotions, and he even has everyone under heaven hand over their cultivation for him to dispatch collectively! What kind of efficiency would that achieve? How could open-sourcing possibly keep up with that?"

Tian Feibao's rank continued to rise: 5th place... 4th place... 3rd place...

His position kept advancing, causing Source Saint to frown inwardly as he watched.

Meanwhile, Tian Feibao chuckled softly as a surging sword intent pierced through the Spirit Realm. Countless livestreams, news articles, reports, and commentaries about him had already inundated the vision of innumerable people.

Tian Feibao said indifferently, "Source Saint, how is what your Immortal Armaments Bureau calls 'memory open-sourcing' any different from body seizure? What is the point of turning individuals into copies of yourself? Can your experiences and your memories adapt perfectly to a new world? Humph, how could such rigid, copy-paste talent cultivation and world development ever compare to my Heavenly Sword Sect's Sword Intent Body Infusion?"

Source Saint replied coldly, "And you think you can adapt? You are merely using your sword intent to forcibly mold everyone in the world."

"Utterly wrong!" Tian Feibao said coldly. "Our sect's sword dao aligns with people's hearts and conforms to the celestial timing, silently nourishing the minds of all living beings. Even if it rectifies their thoughts, it is to guide them toward the development model best suited for their local environment."

As he spoke, Tian Feibao's aura surged wildly, a torrential sword intent soaring into the sky as if driving the movements of heaven and earth, steering the trends of the mortal world.

As if in response to his sky-piercing sword intent, the name 'Tian Feibao' on the rankings accelerated once more. Finally, with the force of a resounding thunderclap, it slammed past Source Saint ahead of him and stepped into the number one spot.

Watching this unfold, Source Saint wore an unwilling expression, wanting to catch up again, yet could only watch helplessly as his own Immortal Dao Gross Product... no matter how fiercely it surged or how frantically it pursued, was only left further and further behind in Tian Feibao's dust.

At the same time, countless cultivators of the Heavenly Sword Sect had already activated their Shock Swords, transmitting the shifting situation at the grand assembly to every corner of the Ten Sects, sparking immense tremors.

Yet before Tian Feibao could rejoice for too long, he discovered that, unbeknownst to him, Wan Yaosheng from the Celestial Monster Sect was already hot on his heels.

Tian Feibao said, "Mere monsterfolk, yet you managed to push your Immortal Dao Gross Product all the way to second place?"

Wan Yaosheng let out a low roar, having already stepped right in front of Tian Feibao, standing face to face with this heavenly chosen of the Heavenly Sword Sect.

"You of the Heavenly Sword Sect like to let mind govern matter? Today I shall show you that no matter how the mind changes, in the end it must still yield to matter! Yield to bloodline!"

Behind Wan Yaosheng, scenes from his Immortal Emperor Simulator were projected.

Countless humans were tainted by the monsterfolk bloodline; all living beings across the realm were gradually transformed into monsterfolk, unleashing unprecedented vitality.

As the transformation of the monsterfolk reached completion and the breeding efforts progressed, more and more monsterfolk were born, grew up, and began cultivating.

Consequently, the world's Immortal Dao Gross Product surged drastically, rising higher and faster, until at this very moment, it launched a charge toward the top of the leaderboard.

"As monsterfolk, we are naturally stronger than humans, reproduce faster than humans, possess superior base stats, and improve our physical strength far quicker! Turning every living being in the world into monsterfolk is truly the optimal path of development for the Immortal Dao!"

"Tian Feibao, no matter how your Heavenly Sword Sect modifies your mindset, you can never erase the physical disparity brought by bloodlines."

"That is why I have surpassed you!"

***Boom!***

Accompanied by the cheers of countless monsterfolk, Wan Yaosheng's name dramatically claimed the top spot, trampling Tian Feibao firmly beneath his feet.

"Awesome!"

Within the Myriad Laws Sect, Shi Yunxiang could not help but nod repeatedly as he watched the scene, a spark of wonder flashing in his eyes.

Not far away, Bei Wufeng watched quietly, making a mental note: *"Monsterfolk will always be monsterfolk. I must find more opportunities to remind Profound Monarch Zhang never to place monsterfolk in positions of great importance within the sect."*

In the Spirit Realm's livestream room, Heavenly Monarch Overdrive remarked with emotion, "In the early stages of world development, different Immortal Dao routes each have their own pros and cons, and the competition for first place is fiercely intense."

Beside him, Heavenly Monarch Redundancy said, "Indeed. The current rankings are likely not the final ones. After all, some technical routes are late bloomers; during the foundation-building phase, they don't boost Immortal Dao productivity by much, but once their foundation is solidified, they soar to the heavens."

Over the past 30 days, both of them had fully watched the governance process of the ten book-wielders. Although they couldn't see the exact Immortal Dao Gross Product of each world at the time, they could sense that Wan Yaosheng's performance would likely not be enough to secure the ultimate first place.

Heavenly Monarch Overdrive continued, "This Immortal Ancestor truly lives up to being the creator of the Books. Not only is he immensely powerful, but his method of choosing a successor far surpasses the ancients. Instead of judging aptitudes, moral character, or raw strength, he selects the cultivator best capable of unlocking a world's potential and enhancing Immortal Dao productivity..."

---

With the release of the rankings, more and more cultivators realized that the final outcome and the Immortal Ancestor's successor were about to be decided. For a time, the number of cultivators arriving to observe the assembly surged dramatically.

Bu Yingshu watched the shifts on the rankings and said, "That guy from Netherworld Palace is surging up!"

Bu Yuangui pulled up the relevant footage, and Qian Airen's performance in the Immortal Emperor Simulator immediately appeared before his eyes.

At the same time, countless upper-tier cultivators observing the rankings performed similar actions. This allowed them to quickly understand the shifts in the rankings alongside what was happening in the corresponding simulations.

In the footage, Qian Airen was using heaven and earth as a formation, refining the entire world into a great formation. Previously, his ranking had lagged behind because during the early stages of development, the vast majority of his power was devoted to forging the great formation. But as the great formation was established and fully sealed, heaven and earth fell entirely under his control.

From that moment on, the Immortal Dao productivity of the entire world skyrocketed violently. The vital energy and thoughts of all living beings in the world were connected by Qian Airen, acting as a complete Immortal Dao machine squeezed for every bit of its potential.

"If you want to get rich, build infrastructure first," Qian Airen laughed loudly and said, "If you are reluctant to invest, how can you ever gain sufficient returns? What use is it to merely stimulate human potential? Change the entire world, alter the environment of all under heaven, and people will naturally adapt, providing value to the world."

"If every great sect took it upon themselves to transform Kunxu and build Immortal Dao Infrastructure like my Netherworld Palace, the growth rate of the Immortal Emperors' cultivation would surely rise year after year!"

Gao Chongguang of the Myriad Laws Sect, having been in the sect for so long and having visited various Grotto-Heavens, already had some understanding of the Netherworld Palace's intelligence as he watched this scene.

Looking at Qian Airen's performance right now, he thought to himself, *"This Civil Engineering Faction of the Netherworld Palace spends all day obsessed with the unity of all living beings, turning heaven and earth into flesh and blood, turning human lives into building materials, and financializing building materials. They have long strayed from the righteous path of civil engineering."*

As Bu Yuangui watched, he remarked, "Renewing heaven and earth through a mega-infrastructure brings a skyrocketing leap in Immortal Dao productivity. I'm afraid Qian Airen will be making a push for first place next."

Watching this unfold, Zhang Yu thought to himself, *"So he's a cultivator from the Civil Engineering Faction."*

Memories of the First Layer surfaced in Zhang Yu's mind, recalling how the Oasis Group — under the Netherworld University, which in turn fell under the Netherworld Palace — had once attempted to transform the First Layer.

*"Could those massive transformations... have also been driven by the Netherworld Palace's Civil Engineering Faction?"*

Meanwhile, Qian Airen had already climbed to first place. Through the Netherworld Palace's mega-infrastructure that turned heaven and earth into flesh and blood, he displayed an incomparable advantage in Immortal Dao productivity.

Yet as the years went by, his lead was gradually being narrowed.

"Mega-infrastructure also degrades with age," Heavenly Monarch Overdrive commented. "Although Qian Airen established an absolute advantage after completing his formation, as the years advance and the mega-infrastructure begins to deteriorate, that advantage is gradually being eroded."

"Especially now that time has passed the halfway mark and the book-wielders have been governing their respective realms for nearly fifty years, some mid-to-late-stage technical pathways should be starting to exert their power."

As if in response to Heavenly Monarch Overdrive's commentary, Bai Gusheng, who had previously lagged behind, made a fierce push. His Immortal Dao Gross Product erupted like a volcano, skyrocketing with world-shattering momentum.

Yet in the recordings watched by countless cultivators, Bai Gusheng had consistently controlled all living beings beneath the heavens through medicinal pills and debt.

Heavenly Monarch Redundancy analyzed, "However, it is precisely due to the continuous modification by medicinal pills that the number of cultivators has grown rapidly over these past few decades."

"Furthermore, driven by the pressure of debt and the allure of medicinal pills, the cultivators' subjective initiative has been fully ignited."

"This differs from Qian Airen's cultivators. The cultivators under Bai Gusheng's rule are no longer mere Immortal Dao Materials, nor are they puppets or artifact spirits. Instead, they actively contribute all their Immortal Dao Wisdom, pushing every ounce of potential to the limit for the world's development."

Heavenly Monarch Overdrive reminded, "It's not just that. Look, to nurture talent, Bai Gusheng directly provided all the low-grade Books and even mid-grade Books in his possession."

Inside the Immortal Emperor Simulator, not only were the cultivations of the book-wielders deduced to the Immortal Emperor level, but even the Books they each possessed were simulated as well.

In the recording watched by numerous cultivators, Bai Gusheng held various selection processes to lend the low-grade and mid-grade Books in his grasp to all sorts of outstanding talents, further propelling the world's Immortal Dao development.

Watching this scene, Bai Gusheng said in a clear, resounding voice, "In this illusionary realm, those who possess more Books naturally have an added advantage. However, only by lending the Books out can their value be maximized — this is making the best use of Books."

"This ought to be a truth that all book-wielders understand. Yet in this illusionary realm, everything stems from the choices of one's true inner heart. In reality, as book-wielders, we place tremendous importance on Books, to the point that even inside the illusionary realm, some people fail to overcome this instinctive mindset and cannot bring themselves to lend them out."

As he spoke, Bai Gusheng's ranking climbed steadily, already charging into the top three.

"And in the end, this simply comes down to a lack of capacity. Wan Yaosheng, Source Saint, Tian Feibao... your pursuit of the Immortal Dao's interests is not firm enough, and you lack the capacity to govern a world."

"This is the gap in Immortal Dao philosophy between you and me. This is why you have been surpassed by me and thoroughly trampled beneath my feet!"

In the next instant, Bai Gusheng reached the top and claimed the number one position.

Under the gaze of countless onlookers, countless advertisements for the White Bone Cult were projected all over Bai Gusheng's body.

Watched by numerous Kunxu Cultivators, these advertisements wound around Bai Gusheng's entire body like the medals and halos of a first-place champion, making him appear incomparably noble and outstanding.

As time continued to advance, the other contestants also repeatedly showcased their capabilities in the recordings, producing quite a few bright spots in the statistical data of the Immortal Dao Gross Product.

In particular, the repeated charges and pursuits by Qian Airen and Tian Feibao nearly threatened Bai Gusheng's dominant position.

"Yet Bai Gusheng remains the most stable," Heavenly Monarch Overdrive analyzed. "From the perspective of this century-long timeframe, I'm afraid Bai Gusheng's approach — choosing straight from his true nature to ultimately develop the world and amass Immortal Dao resources — is the most effective."

Although this was only a simulator and could not fully represent real results, and a mere century could not prove the ultimate superiority of this path, the cultivators of the White Bone Cult still launched a massive promotional campaign, greatly boosting product sales and the company's stock price.

Just as more and more cultivators began to believe that Bai Gusheng would ultimately take first place, an unexpected shift occurred on the rankings.

Heavenly Monarch Redundancy said in surprise, "Bai Gusheng's growth rate has suddenly suffered a noticeable decline. What happened?"

Bai Gusheng immediately began analyzing his own recording, thinking to himself, *"Prolonged drug usage led to a decline in some people's lifespans, and the entire world's mortality rate had risen somewhat over these past few years. But I handled all of that properly. I recycled the waste, using it to bolster my own Immortal Dao foundation."*

At the same time, Qian Airen's growth rate on the other side had also slowed down.

Qian Airen was startled. *"What's going on? Didn't I just devour all the remnant souls that couldn't endure it? Could it be that my processing method wasn't sophisticated enough? Was my conversion rate too low?"*

Seeing the growth rates of the book-wielders on the rankings begin to decelerate one after another, many cultivators were utterly bewildered, eager to figure out what was happening.

Heavenly Monarch Overdrive, as always, was the first to find the answer.

"Out of the ten people, eight of them experienced a slowdown in their growth rates one after another. Only Zhang Yu and Wang Guiren showed no signs of decelerating."

"And the difference between the two sides is..."

Heavenly Monarch Overdrive's gaze shifted as he spoke in the livestream room, "I get it now. This statistic for the Immortal Dao Total Productive Power does not include the book-wielders themselves."

"In other words, any increases or decreases from the book-wielders personally do not affect the statistical data."

"Therefore, as Bai Gusheng and the others appropriate the Immortal Dao production output of various realms one by one for their own use, and as the scale of what they seize grows larger and larger, the Immortal Dao Gross Product tracked on this leaderboard will begin to slow down."

As Heavenly Monarch Overdrive spoke, Source Saint's data plummeted drastically.

Heavenly Monarch Overdrive immediately analyzed, "This means a large number of cultivators, after years of open-sourcing, have been completely assimilated into clones of the Source Saint. A vast amount of Immortal Dao productive value... has been credited under Source Saint's name, so it is no longer reflected on the leaderboard."

Heavenly Monarch Overdrive was not the only one to make this discovery. The sects of Kunxu never lacked clever minds, and soon more and more people came to realize this.

Meanwhile, on the rankings, the data for the book-wielders did not merely slow in growth — it began to decrease substantially.

This meant they were appropriating more and more of the Immortal Dao's production output, and after an initial period of investment, they had finally begun to personally reap the world's harvest.

Only Zhang Yu and Wang Guiren had not done this. As a result, their figures continued to grow, leaving the others far behind as their numbers steadily climbed.

In the Spirit Realm, some cultivators were instantly perplexed: "The Immortal Emperors are the main body of the world. When comparing the Immortal Dao Gross Product, how can you exclude the Immortal Emperor's share?"

Other cultivators voiced their doubts: "Doing it like this... doesn't it become a contest of who can benefit lower-tier cultivators more? Isn't this turning everything completely upside down? It's supposed to be all living beings benefiting the Immortal Emperor, so how could it become the Immortal Emperor benefiting all living beings? Isn't this just utter nonsense?"

Looking at these messages, Heavenly Monarch Overdrive gave a cold sneer and said, "A bunch of fools. If even you understand such a simple truth, would a powerhouse like the Immortal Ancestor not understand it?"

"This is the mighty being who created the Books. Would he have such foolish ideas like yours?"

"Here's a piece of advice for you to take to heart: whenever you think high-ranking cultivators are being foolish, it must be because you haven't grasped their true intentions, and yet you still attempt to project your own foolish thoughts onto them."

Hearing Heavenly Monarch Overdrive's words, Tu Mu from the Former Days Graveyard agreed deeply, thinking to himself, *"Indeed, the Immortal Ancestor is so formidable, there must be a reason for everything he does."*

At the same time, Heavenly Monarch Redundancy beside him spoke up, "I understand now. What the Immortal Ancestor wants to test is not just the ability to develop and gather the world's resources, but also a mindset for long-term investment."

"There is a saying in the field of investment: 'If you aren't willing to hold a stock for ten years, don't even think about owning it for ten minutes.'"

"Applied to today's perspective of investing in worlds, it would be... 'If you aren't willing to develop a world for a century, don't even think about developing it at all.'"

Heavenly Monarch Overdrive nodded in deep agreement. "Developing a world and gathering resources from across the realm — what a colossal undertaking that is! The longer the investment plan, the richer the returns it can bring. But unfortunately, the true cultivation of the book-wielders is limited, and their true natures... are already accustomed to investing and reaping returns within a few decades."

"Driven by instinct, they lack the courage and vision to commit to sustained investments lasting over a century!"

On the other side, Bu Yingshu asked in surprise, "Dad, is that really how it is?"

Upon hearing this, Bu Yuangui nodded and said, "Under the banner of the Immortal Dao Aspiration, cultivators across the realm are all pursuing the benefits of the Immortal Dao. But whether one chooses short-term gains or long-term gains depends on whether their dao heart is steadfast enough."

"And only those who can repeatedly overcome the temptation of short-term gains and seize long-term benefits can go further on the path of the Immortal Dao."

"In the simulation, Zhang Yu probably struggled to suppress his desire to harvest the world as well, choosing a longer-term investment instead."

---

Meanwhile, related discussions circulated within the Spirit Realm, quickly gaining widespread agreement among many Kunxu cultivators.

"Profound Monarch Zhang is truly incredible. How could he hold back on something like this? If it were me, I would've cashed out long ago."

"When I watched the stream earlier, Profound Monarch Zhang was still investing in that world even by year 100. Just how grand is his vision? How massive of a return is he aiming for?"

"This is what holding onto a world long-term looks like! Minister Zhang's approach is following the example of the Immortal Emperors!"

"Those who criticized Minister Zhang earlier for lacking Sect Nature and being politically incorrect within immortal governance were thinking like typical low-tier cultivator minds. Have you ever even been a book-wielder? Spouting all that nonsense over there."

Heavenly Monarch Overdrive exclaimed with deep feeling, "Profound Monarch Zhang didn't just lend out low-grade Books and mid-grade Books — he even lent out his high-grade Book, the *Feather Book*! He has unearthed all kinds of geniuses; he's aiming to cultivate Immortals!"

"While the other book-wielders are still thinking about mortals, Profound Monarch Zhang is already thinking about Immortals. Just how far ahead is he looking?"

Even as endless praise for Zhang Yu flooded the Spirit Realm, Zhang Yu himself was watching the recording from Wang Guiren.

Watching Wang Guiren's actions unfold scene after scene in the simulator, Zhang Yu felt a faint sense of familiarity.

*"This is... It's too similar to Senior Ying Xintian's methods in the lost history."*

Meanwhile, on the other side, Wang Guiren watched the recording of Zhang Yu, murmuring inwardly, *"Books, Chain Formation Diagrams, and even Dao Seeds — you gave them all away to cultivate cultivators? Is this truly your heart's desire?"*

*"Zhang Yu, you have truly surprised me. It's just a pity — if only you had appeared some years earlier."*

Watching his and Wang Guiren's data surge alternately as they constantly vied for first place, a thought surfaced in Zhang Yu's mind.

*"Judging from the Immortal Ancestor's inscriptions in the Book, his approach was clearly on a different path from that of today's Immortal Emperors."*

*"So his conflict with the Immortal Emperors is fundamentally a clash of paths. If one doesn't align with the Immortal Ancestor's direction, taking first place probably still wouldn't make them his successor."*

Looking at the numbers on the rankings, he thought to himself, *"Even in a clash of paths, strength remains the final arbiter. That is why the Immortal Ancestor's succession process ultimately comes down to competing in Immortal Dao production value."*

*"In the end, while the paths of the Immortal Ancestor and the Immortal Emperors differ, both are striving for the apex of the Immortal Dao rather than debating morality or character."*

*"The only difference is that the Immortal Emperors treat the world as their resource to propel themselves toward the apex of the Immortal Dao."*

*"The Immortal Ancestor, on the other hand, believes that only by gathering the power of all living beings throughout the ages can one reach the apex of the Immortal Dao."*

*"Their paths are different, but their finish line... is still the apex of the Immortal Dao."*

Right at this moment, the year on the rankings reached Year 99.

In this very final year, Zhang Yu looked at Wang Guiren in the recording and thought to himself, *"Senior Ying Xintian, is it really you?"*

In the recording, Wang Guiren directly severed the world's lifespans, nearly bringing the entire world's Immortal Dao to extinction.

In Zhang Yu's eyes, this act was virtually identical to Ying Xintian's in the lost history.

At that moment, Wang Guiren thought to himself, *"Seeing your performance, I can finally rest assured."*

*"Zhang Yu, I already understand your thoughts and have felt your Dao Heart. I shall temporarily entrust the Four Books to you."*

*"As for the rest, I'll cover for you."*

Wang Guiren recalled his choices within the illusionary realm. It turned out he had still been able to maintain his real-world thinking inside the illusion, and could even see the performances of the other book-wielders.

It was precisely because he had seen Zhang Yu's performance that he made this final choice.

At the same time, Wang Guiren on the rankings seemed to be stomped downward by Zhang Yu, while Zhang Yu's name used the momentum of that stomp to soar into the sky... firmly seizing the first-place position.

As the year on the rankings halted at the 100th year, the final standings were completely settled.

As if answering a certain summons, the Transformation Book, Flight Book, and Ascension Book soared into the sky from Bai Gusheng, Tian Feibao, and Qian Airen, converging before Zhang Yu.

At this moment, the gathering of the Four Books was right before his eyes.

And as for the countdown of the future information, only 12 minutes remained.
