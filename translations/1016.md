# Chapter 1016: Burning Books and Burying Ancestors, Chronicle of Time

Right before the ten Immortal Emperors made their move, before the sun, the Spirit Realm, the Dharma Realm, and spiritual energy were diverted, at a moment when everything throughout Kunxu was still normal.

At this moment, Zhang Yu was still contemplating the final volume of the Four Books — the Dao Unification Chapter, a new chapter born after the Ascension Book from the Feathered Ascension series had been devoured.

As he comprehended its contents, he thought to himself, "This Dao Unification Chapter is different from the Transformation Book's Dao Succession Chapter and the Flight Book's Pseudo-Dao Chapter. It gathers the power of many, where a group of cultivators band together to establish a Dao Lineage. Once the Dao Lineage is completed... its authority is shared and held by multiple Dao Masters.

"The benefit, of course, is that since each cultivator in the group contributes a portion of the foundation required for the Dao Lineage, they can achieve immortality and establish their Dao much faster. It can truly be called a shortcut to achieving immortality, likely the most efficient method among these four books.

"However, the drawback is also glaringly obvious. Ascending to immortality as a collective like this splits the Dao Lineage's authority, drastically weakening individual strength, and the upper limit falls far short of a normal Immortal."

Zhang Yu thought to himself, "If I want to use this method to achieve immortality and establish a Dao, I will need to cultivate some companions, or subdue some cultivators and unify their Law Edicts... to form a Dao Lineage."

As his thoughts raced, options of various cultivators popped into his mind one after another.

However, as soon as these stray thoughts surfaced, he suppressed them, knowing that now was not the time to dwell on such matters.

Meanwhile, lines of writing had already surfaced upon the *Dao Unification Chapter*. Zhang Yu, having anticipated this, thought to himself, "Is this another record of lost history?"

As his eyes swept quickly over the contents, his expression grew increasingly grave.

"Is this the history regarding the Immortal Ancestor?"

As Zhang Yu read, he quickly summarized it in his mind: "No one knows where the Immortal Ancestor came from or under which master he studied; all that is known is that when he appeared, he already stood at the apex of the Immortal Dao in that era."

"The Immortal Ancestor disseminated his own Immortal Dao knowledge, teaching without discrimination and instructing talents far and wide, attracting more and more people to join his sect."

"And cultivators who were originally separated by vast distances and poor communication were brought together by the Immortal Ancestor, embarking on an unprecedented grand exchange of the Immortal Dao under his leadership."

Zhang Yu thought to himself, "As expected of the Immortal Ancestor who created the Books. Compared to monopolists like the ten Immortal Emperors, he is a textbook open-source advocate, isn't he? With him leading countless cultivators in exchanging insights on the Immortal Dao, an Immortal Dao Golden Age would surely be ushered in."

The thought flashed through his mind and vanished. Zhang Yu continued reading while silently summarizing the text.

"Perhaps the original Immortal Ancestor did this believing that through such communication, cultivators would understand each other, learn from one another, and in turn drive the development of the great Immortal Dao."

"Yet he was mistaken. Once cultivators from various sects and factions engaged in close exchange, they directly confronted their differing technical philosophies and stances on the Immortal Dao, triggering conflict after conflict."

Zhang Yu smiled wryly, thinking to himself, "So communication only fueled clashes because their philosophies differed? Makes sense. Not to mention the divergent views between the Righteous and Demonic Paths, the conflicting ideas between the monopoly and open-source factions would inevitably lead to division. Their aspiration for the Immortal Dao surely played a part in this as well."

Zhang Yu read on: "The Immortal Ancestor realized that overly intimate exchanges would only provoke endless conflicts among the cultivators of that era. Thus, he created the Books, intending for them to serve as a tool for communication and legacy."

"The Immortal Ancestor's goal was for all cultivators under heaven to be able to study and exchange all kinds of Immortal Dao Technology through the Books, and even inherit and comprehend the Dao Lineages of the past and present, ultimately making the Books tools for managing Immortal Dao Technology and Dao Lineages."

"In the eyes of the Immortal Ancestor and his successors, only by uniting countless cultivators across the Four Seas, the Eight Desolations, and all of history could the development of Immortal Dao Technology be advanced step by step, ultimately leading all living beings together to storm the apex of the Immortal Dao."

"Anyone who monopolizes the Immortal Dao is severing the path for everyone else to scale the peak of the Immortal Dao."

Zhang Yu thought to himself, "Speaking of which, was my Feather Book actually created by this Immortal Ancestor? Why does it share the same name as the Feather Book among the Four Books?"

With that fleeting stray thought passing through his mind, Zhang Yu continued summarizing the text: "Then suddenly, one day, the Immortal Ancestor vanished without a trace. No one knew where he had gone, but his Dao Lineage remained, and his inheritance endured."

"Thus, the disciple of the Immortal Ancestor inherited his aspirations and Dao Lineage, becoming the new generation's Immortal Ancestor. The new Immortal Ancestor utilized the Books, spread the Books, and drove the development of Immortal Dao Technology forward, until he encountered and gradually uncovered the existence of the Rollbacker."

"The philosophy of the Immortal Ancestor's lineage clearly clashed fiercely with the Rollbacker, inevitably making them a thorn in the Rollbacker's side."

"Moreover, the Immortal Ancestor's lineage was far too dazzling and conspicuous; they were likely targeted in secret by the Rollbacker long ago, who gathered countless pieces of intelligence on them."

"Thus, across confrontation after confrontation, knowing nothing about their enemy, the Immortal Ancestor's lineage suffered catastrophic losses. Several generations of Immortal Ancestors were suppressed, and countless Immortal Ancestor's successors were either directly obliterated or gradually manipulated across rollbacks into becoming enemies of the Immortal Ancestor. The entire lineage was nearly driven to complete extinction."

"Yet the Immortal Ancestor's lineage did not come away empty-handed. Through repeated confrontations and rollbacks, one generation of the Immortal Ancestor finally mastered the Books' Dao Lineage completely and enabled the Books to pass messages across rollbacks."

"They also gradually realized that the Rollbacker — capable of executing a World Rollback, altering history, and attempting to monopolize the Immortal Dao through their personal will — would be their sworn, mortal enemy."

"Therefore, to prevent the Book from being exploited by the Rollbacker and to continue the fight, generations of Immortal Ancestors and successors inscribed their own wills into the Books' Dao Lineage before meeting their defeat."

"From that point on, generation after generation of the Immortal Ancestor's wills gathered within the Books' Dao Lineage, swearing to fight against the Rollbacker and any cultivator in the world who dared to monopolize the Immortal Dao until the very end of time. From that moment forward, only those who gained the recognition of the Immortal Ancestor's wills could obtain the Book's supreme authority, wielding the Books to resist the rollbacks and transmit messages."

"Yet even so, targeted by the Rollbacker as they were, they were never able to defeat their adversaries."

Zhang Yu thought to himself, "So 'Immortal Ancestor'... is a title passed down through generations? These Immortal Ancestors must have been extraordinarily powerful, but their situation was the exact opposite of the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor's. They became the prey exposed upon the stage, while the Rollbacker made exhaustive preparations in the shadows before launching their ambush."

Immortal Slayer analyzed, "Still, to be able to detect the enemy's presence in time under the Rollbacker's encirclement, perceive the world's rollback, and use the Books to pass on messages across it — the strength of these Immortal Ancestors is indeed astonishing."

The Sect Examination Clone said, "So what if their strength was astonishing? Once the Rollbacker seized the initiative, it was practically impossible to resist. I understand now: in battles involving rollbacks, the information gap is key. Whoever commands more genuine information holds the greater advantage in a rollback."

Zhang Yu continued summarizing the text: "Even though the Rollbacker held the absolute upper hand, as long as the Book's Dao Lineage still existed, even if Books were burned and ancestors buried time and time again, the Books would still emerge in response to destiny. There would always be an Immortal Ancestor's successor appearing, and there would always be unyielding souls in this world who refused to be led astray by him."

"This is the reason why the Rollbacker could never completely erase the Immortal Ancestor's Lineage."

Zhang Yu evaluated in his heart: "Even under the pervasive influence of the Aspiration for the Immortal Dao, where beings like the ten Immortal Emperors emerged in an endless stream, there would ultimately still be people who wished to take a different path to assault the Apex of the Immortal Dao."

He continued reading and summarized inwardly: "The Rollbacker ultimately attempted to completely roll back the existence of the Immortal Ancestor's lineage, yet he still could not prevent the Book from emerging in response to destiny, nor could he stop the birth of this Dao Lineage that connected past and present, much less destroy the Immortal Ancestor's wills residing within it."

"In the end, the Rollbacker could only thoroughly seal away all information concerning the Books, ban its users time and time again, suppress each new generation of Immortal Ancestor's successors, and completely separate the Four Books of Feathered Ascension to prevent them from converging. He repeatedly severed the channels through which the Immortal Ancestor's wills could interfere with the world, ultimately causing the Immortal Ancestor's lineage to gradually fade into obscurity throughout history..."

"Until... after the Rollbacker was defeated by the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor. Without the Rollbacker's continuous suppression, the Books accelerated its reappearance in this world, and the Immortal Ancestor's wills could finally seek out a new Immortal Ancestor's successor to awaken once more..."

Just as Zhang Yu finished comprehending the information brought by the *Ascension Book*, that ancient voice echoed in his mind once again.

"The Rollbacker of the past has already been defeated, and the lineage of the Immortal Ancestor of the past has long vanished into history."

"Yet our battle is not over."

At this moment, Zhang Yu felt only a surging fighting spirit sweeping over him continuously, like a volcano suppressed for far too long, erupting with a thunderous roar in this new era.

"Times march forward, and the Immortal Dao advances. But as long as the aspiration for the Immortal Dao continues to turn, as long as the world has not met its end, our battle against the Rollbacker, against all monopolizers of the Immortal Dao, will never cease!"

"Now, the ten Immortal Emperors have inherited the authority over rollbacks. They wish to monopolize the Immortal Dao, wishing to climb to the pinnacle alone — then we shall resist them to the very end!"

At that moment, Zhang Yu felt countless battle cries defying heaven and earth erupting in his mind, and countless fanatical intents swept in, descending upon this world through him.

These intents cried out to him incessantly, pouring their hearts out to him.

Every single one of them was an Immortal Ancestor's successor who had spent their entire life fighting the Rollbacker.

Every single one of them had vowed to lead all living beings to the apex of the Immortal Dao before their lives burned out.

Every single one of them had also fought, raged, been suppressed, and held hope within the Books Dao Lineage for countless, endless years — until this very moment, when they descended upon the world once more.

"Zhang Yu, the true battle is about to begin..."

"Let us crush all these monopolizers of the Immortal Dao to dust!"

At the same time, the Feather Book moved without wind inside Zhang Yu's body, its pages fluttering frantically, revealing all the various Dao Lineage information regarding the lost history one by one.

Zhang Yu could sense that the Immortal Ancestor's collective will was frantically absorbing the information and intelligence within.

In that instant, a spark of realization flashed through his mind: "Perhaps the true purpose of the information on this lost history wasn't just to be passed down to me, but to the Immortal Ancestor's will? To let the Immortal Ancestor's will use and utilize this information?"

The reply of the Immortal Ancestor's will seemed to confirm his thoughts at that very moment.

"The progress of Immortal Dao Technology is far too rapid. Every single one of these Immortal Emperors has mastered Immortal Dao Technology that already surpasses that of the former Rollbacker."

"And now, they even intend to sever the connections between each Dao Lineage and the Books."

The Immortal Ancestor's will said, "Still, it does not matter. With you parasitizing the Dao Lineages to serve as middleware, along with these Dao Lineages transmitted from the lost history, we can still leverage them."

Zhang Yu asked internally, "Do these Dao Lineages from the lost history truly still exist?"

The Immortal Ancestor's will replied, "After defeating Ying Xintian, Heavenly Virtue, and the Monster Saint, the Rollbacker redirected the emergence of these Dao Lineages once again."

"Fortunately, after Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor and the others defeated the Rollbacker, it appears they failed to inherit the Rollbacker's vast memories and legacy. Naturally, they could not fully grasp these Dao Lineages either. They merely managed to harness a portion of their power through research and their advanced Immortal Dao Technology."

"Perhaps given more time, they would eventually decipher these Dao Lineages and completely master the ownerless ones among them. After all, their Immortal Dao Technology is inherently more advanced and continues to progress."

"Certain things the past Rollbacker could not accomplish, they can."

"For example... devouring us, and then thoroughly unraveling the Immortal Ancestor's Immortal Dao Technology."

In the very next instant, the Immortal Ancestor's will quickly warned, "Careful. The power of the Karma Dao Lineage that I can leverage right now is limited, and it won't hide us for much longer. They are coming."

"Zhang Yu, buy us some time."

Shocked, Zhang Yu exclaimed, "Me? Buy time in front of the Immortal Emperors?"

The Immortal Ancestor's will said, "I need a little time to leverage more Dao Lineages, and the reinforcements you called are already on their way. We just need to buy a little time..."

Zhang Yu was momentarily stunned. "Reinforcements I called? When did I call for reinforcements?"

"Could it be... the me from before the rollback? Are you talking about the me who kept transmitting future messages?"

"But how am I supposed to buy time? The ten Immortal Emperors could devour me with a single thought!"

The Immortal Ancestor's will, however, replied with utter certainty, "You can do it. With your life. That is the method you came up with."

Their exchange took place in the blink of an eye, and in that same split second, Zhang Yu understood what it meant.

"With my life?"

"That really is the only, and greatest, bargaining chip the current me has to influence the Immortal Emperors."

At this moment, countless memories flashed through Zhang Yu's mind.

Immortal Slayer sensed, "Either we die, or we win. We've long had no other choice!"

The Sect Examination Clone cursed, "Damn it, let's fight these Emperor Beasts to the end! We can be Immortal Emperors too!"

Zhang Yu thought to himself, "The past me would probably have hesitated. But now... under the oppression of the ten Immortal Emperors, I don't seem to feel much fear anymore."

"Indeed, compared to the threat of the ten Immortal Emperors, what is a mere life worth?"

"Among the cultivators standing on this Ascension Platform right now, who hasn't risked total ruin and Dao Dissipation for the sake of profit?"

The next moment, the magic power inside Zhang Yu surged. Under his active control, his flesh, blood, and Divine Soul were about to naturally disintegrate, ending his own life.

Yet at the very moment Zhang Yu attempted to take his own life, the commotion caused by his drastic action could no longer be concealed by the power of the Karma Dao Lineage mobilized by the Immortal Ancestor's will.

The Immortal Emperors, who had been watching the Ascension Platform all along, instantly noticed Zhang Yu's actions and intervened one after another.

It was precisely from this moment onward that various infrastructures — the sun, spiritual energy, immortal aura, the Spirit Realm, the Dharma Realm, and more — began to be commandeered by the Immortal Emperors.

Only then did Tai Yuebai, Bu Yingshu, Fa Liuyuan, and the numerous other cultivators within Kunxu sense the anomaly.

On the Ascension Platform, Bai Gusheng only then noticed Zhang Yu, who was enveloped by the power of the ten Immortal Emperors.

Zhang Yu, whose life aura had been plummeting just moments ago, had recovered in an instant, brought back from the dead.

At the same time, however, Zhang Yu could sense that he had completely lost control over his own body, and all perception of his surroundings had vanished without a trace.

At this moment, complete darkness enveloped his vision, absolute silence filled his ears, and only his own existence remained, as if sealed within a void abyss.

Yet his thoughts still turned slowly, continuing to reason: "If I were to die, the strongest among the ten Immortal Emperors — those closest to the Ultimate Realm — would immediately revive me for the sake of the Immortal Ancestor's legacy."

"But the moment they step in to save me, the other Immortal Emperors will make their moves as well, because no one can guarantee that someone won't take the opportunity to pull a trick while I'm being saved."

"The ten Immortal Emperors, already on the brink of conflict in a fragile balance, incapable of trusting one another, and already intent on seizing the Immortal Ancestor's legacy, will plunge into an all-out war because of this."

"And with their wisdom, they will grasp this in an instant, so once the battle begins, they will strike with all their might."

"They will protect me while competing for me, and perhaps weaker Immortal Emperors might even try to seize the opportunity to obliterate me.

"A series of Immortal Emperor wars centered around me will be ignited by my hand, breaking out in full force."

At this moment, with the ten Immortal Emperors taking action, an ultimate war using Zhang Yu's body as the battlefield and mobilizing countless resources of Kunxu had already erupted in total silence.

Yet, aside from the ten Immortal Emperors, almost no one could observe the course of this battle, let alone know that it had been triggered by Zhang Yu himself.

"Yet right now, my thoughts are also sealed deep within my consciousness, leaving me unable to interfere with the outside world in any way." Just then, sinking into endless darkness, Zhang Yu's consciousness suddenly sensed something.

"Is it... the Feather Book?"

Watching the Feather Book gradually emerge within the endless darkness, Zhang Yu was startled inwardly. "More words are surfacing? Is this me calling for help?"

Lines of text continually appeared upon the Feather Book, as if someone were calling out and seeking aid from existences in lost history.

"Calling for help from Senior Ying Xintian, Heavenly Virtue, the Monster Saint, and even the White Bone Immortal Emperor, Joyous Union Immortal Emperor, and Heavenly Monster Immortal Emperor in lost history?"

"But how can those who dwell in the lost history support us?"

"The information the Book can convey is ultimately limited. Besides, can White Bone Immortal Emperor, Joyous Union Immortal Emperor, and Celestial Monster Immortal Emperor even see it?"

Zhang Yu quietly looked at the Feather Book, only to find that no reply ever appeared upon it.

"Did it fail?"

Just at that moment, the voice of the Immortal Ancestor's will echoed in Zhang Yu's mind: "It did not fail. Seizing the opportunity created by the fierce battle among the ten Immortal Emperors, their support is already on its way — it just isn't coming through the Book."

Zhang Yu asked in confusion, "Not through the Book? Then through what?"

The Immortal Ancestor's will said, "They are here."

Zhang Yu's heart stirred, and he saw surges of bloody light suddenly soar into the sky from the depths of his Sea of Consciousness, which had previously been shrouded in darkness.

The Immortal Ancestor's will continued, "At this moment, you may no longer remember, but in the previous rollback, this was the method you and I agreed upon."

"Relying on Supreme Truth's legacy that you obtained, I would connect the Book with the Bloodline Dao Lineage..."

Zhang Yu's thoughts shifted as he recalled how Immortal Emperor Supreme Truth had once used bloodlines to transmit rollback information.

And at this moment, through his communication with the Immortal Ancestor's will, Zhang Yu's gaze seemed to follow the Books' Dao Lineage, catching sight of the flowing Rollback River.

"Through the Books, through Immortal Dao Technology capable of crossing the Rollback River, our call for help was sent across."

"And then through bloodline, through generation after generation of mortals multiplying and thriving, through the inheritance of mortal bloodlines across generations, utilizing this most ordinary, most rustic method — one most overlooked by Rollbacker and most disregarded by the Immortal Emperors... the support was delivered."

Looking at the surging bloody light, Zhang Yu seemed to see generation after generation of mortals struggling to survive, reproducing against all odds, passing their bloodlines down from one generation to the next until this day, coalescing into an epic chronicle of the ages.

Zhang Yu sighed inwardly, "Whether they are cultivators or Immortals, if one traces their lineage upward generation by generation, they all ultimately originate from mortals. It is precisely because of mortals passing the torch down the generations that generations of cultivators and Immortals exist."

"And for me, who has managed to survive all the way to this present day, if my lineage were traced upward generation by generation, the bloodline of my forebears would inevitably be found in any era of history."

The Immortal Ancestor's will said, "Ying Xintian, Heavenly Virtue, the Monster Saint, as well as White Bone, Joyous Union, and Celestial Monsters — they had long hidden the inherited memories that even the ten Immortal Emperors would covet deep within the bloodlines passed down through the generations."

The Immortal Ancestor continued, "It is merely that the current ten Emperors stand far too aloof. They have never paid any heed to mortal bloodlines, until now... when I pry open the Bloodline Dao Lineage and unseal them one by one."

At this moment, although Zhang Yu still did not understand many of the technical details, nor the various operational nuances regarding the lost history, the bloodlines, and the Books, he had already grasped the general idea of the Immortal Ancestor's will.

He summarized it in his heart: "After linking the Books to the Bloodline Dao Lineage, the Immortal Ancestor's Will requested reinforcement through Immortal Dao Technology spanning across time and space, and then that reinforcement was passed down through mortal generations struggling to survive."

At this moment, amid the sky-filling bloody light, a massive torrent of information poured forth.

A hazy figure seemed to gradually emerge before Zhang Yu at the end of the bloodline.

Zhang Yu thought, "Is that... the original owner of this body of Zhang Yu?"

The hazy figure struggled slightly, as though still trapped in that moment of falling from the building.

"I'm sorry, I can't hold on any longer."

Feeling this final will lingering in the bloodline, Zhang Yu was slightly stunned. He looked at the figure, seemingly catching a glimpse of countless ancestors standing behind him as well.

Zhang Yu said slowly, "Thank you for your hard work... passing the torch all the way to this moment, holding on until I arrived."

"Leave the rest to us."

The next moment, the Immortal Ancestor's will erupted violently. As the Immortal Ancestor's will revived, it leveraged and connected more and more Dao Lineages, and the power it could mobilize grew increasingly formidable. In the blink of an eye, it had transmitted the memories from the lost history to the three Immortal Emperors: Celestial Monsters, White Bone, and Joyous Union.

He believed that these three Immortal Emperors, who infinitely pursued the apex of the Immortal Dao, would definitely not pass up this free legacy.

At the same time, Ying Xintian's gaze sharpened on the other side as he felt the vast flood of memories transmitted to him by the Immortal Ancestor's will.

Rapidly digesting the information sent by the Immortal Ancestor's will and this memory from the lost history that belonged to him, Ying Xintian's aura underwent a drastic, violent shift.

"Right now, because Zhang Yu triggered the battle among the ten Immortal Emperors ahead of time, there are roughly 33 seconds left on the countdown until the future information arrives."

"These 33 seconds will be the cooldown period during which the ten Immortal Emperors cannot initiate a rollback."

"During these 33 seconds, relying on the relay of countless generations of mortals and the support of senior after senior of the Immortal Dao, we must... temporarily strip the ten Immortal Emperors of any possibility of a rollback."
