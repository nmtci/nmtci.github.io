# Chapter 1040: Grandpa Immortal Emperor, You're Back?

The proposal to strip Zhang Yu of his authority was rejected in an instant.

The relevant powers of Zhang Yu that had been temporarily frozen were suddenly restored at that very moment.

Almost simultaneously, bound by standard protocol, a vast number of Righteous Gods had no choice but to descend upon layers 1 to 19, halting the Immortals from seizing all manner of assets by hook or by crook.

For a time, the Righteous Gods and Immortals were locked in a stalemate, as though an earth-shattering war might erupt in the very next second.

Yet the Immortals of the Myriad Laws Sect did not make a move. In fact, they showed considerable restraint, proactively making various concessions.

On one hand, the Immortals of the Myriad Laws Sect knew that the other nine Great Sects were lurking nearby. Once they engaged in an all-out war with the Heavenly Court Righteous Gods, it would be all too easy for Immortals from the other sects to swoop in and reap the spoils.

On the other hand, it was because that voice had just resonated through the Lower Realm of layers 1 to 10, the Upper Realm of layers 11 to 19, and the Immortal Realm of layers 21 to 29.

The voice and conscious will that echoed across the three realms, along with the impact it brought, put the Immortals of the Myriad Laws Sect deeply on guard.

"Who? Just who was it that vetoed the proposal?!"

At this moment, numerous Immortals focused their observational senses toward the sky above the Heavenly Court, looking at that twelfth name that had emerged.

"God-Seizing King, who on earth is he? Why does he feel so familiar, almost like..."

Immortal Roujia of the Reformist Faction cried out in terror, "Could it be that the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor has returned?"

Immortal Yi Xuan was also filled with doubt and alarm. "To possess Heavenly Court authority of this level, capable of directly revoking a proposal and, in an instant... utilizing the channels of the Righteous Gods to broadcast across the three realms — could it truly be an Immortal Emperor?"

Immortal Roujia's heart pounded with dread as she murmured, "Has Grandpa Immortal Emperor returned? Has their ultimate war already ended?!"

Similar thoughts surged within the hearts of many Immortals, stirring a tempest in their minds.

In that instant, they no longer cared about their interests from the 1st to the 19th layers, nor did they have any desire to contend against the Righteous Gods.

They had only one thought right now: to figure out who this God-Seizing King was, and whether he was truly the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor.

Meanwhile, the two Reformist Faction Immortals did not even dare to observe the interior of the Heavenly Court anymore, terrified of offending something they shouldn't see and becoming the nail that sticks out among the Immortals. 

They swiftly shifted their observation angles, gazing from afar at the Battlefield of the End outside Kunxu — the domain belonging to the ten Immortal Emperors.

On the other side, the Innate Immortal had already been reviewing the observation reports of the Battlefield of the End for some time. In fact, ever since the ten Immortal Emperors' ultimate war began, many Immortals had maintained continuous observations.

Reading through the report's contents, the Innate Immortal finally let out a slight sigh of relief: "The Battle of the End is still ongoing; that Emperor Beast hasn't returned. But who is this current God-Seizing King? Could it be a hidden contingency left behind by the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor?"

It was impossible for the Innate Immortal not to think this way; in his eyes, the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor had always been an unfathomable entity, and he had persistently suspected that the emperor had left behind some ace up his sleeve in Kunxu.

At the same time, the Dharma Realm Immortal had already dispatched a data comparison report to all the Immortals of the Myriad Laws Sect: "This is the result I obtained after using the Dharma Realm data to compare sound and psychic fluctuations."

Dharma Realm Immortal: "This God-Seizing King who rejected the proposal is very likely Zhang Yu, or an entity simulating Zhang Yu, or manipulating Zhang Yu."

Looking at the report's findings, Immortal Roujia of the Reformist Faction found it utterly unbelievable. "Zhang Yu? It’s actually Zhang Yu? How could it possibly be Zhang Yu?"

Immortal Yi Xuan was continuously calling upon the Eight Divisions Great Gods, thinking anxiously to himself, *"These Eight Divisions Great Gods control the Heavenly Court's internal channels and understand the specific operational processes; they should be the most likely to know the true identity of this God-Seizing King."*

Meanwhile, inside the Heavenly Court, the sudden emergence of a twelfth name had caused an immediate uproar. Almost all of the Eight Divisions Great Gods were doing everything in their power to investigate the information.

The consciousness of the Great God of Myriad Paths and Four Directions surged swiftly through the divine power network, as countless surveillance feeds and cultivator records churned like a tsunami within his sea of consciousness.

Yet, the moment any record came even remotely close to the relevant data on this God-Seizing King, it was entirely blocked.

At the same time, on the other side, the Great God of Heavenly Covenant and Equal Contract was likewise gathering intelligence. However, as file after file emerged before him, each and every one was completely blacked out, impossible to reveal.

In this clash of power, he felt his Heavenly Court permissions crumble like an egg struck against a stone, utterly crushed by the opponent so that none of his investigations took effect.

"No... it's not just that."

Feeling a surge of intent sweeping back through the divine power network, the Great God of Heavenly Covenant and Equal Contract was shocked. "Not good! The divine power is entangled, triggering a karmic backlash — he knows I'm investigating him? He's tracing it back!"

The next moment, Zhang Yu's portrait appeared directly before the Great God of Heavenly Covenant and Equal Contract, saying indifferently, "Are you investigating this king?"

Outside the Heavenly Court.

Immortal Venerable Heavenly Fortune's intent shifted slightly as he sensed a drastic change occurring in the Great God of Heavenly Covenant and Equal Contract standing nearby.

Violent fluctuations rippled across the divine power network, and the Great God of Heavenly Covenant and Equal Contract's figure began to gradually fade away.

Immortal Venerable Heavenly Fortune could clearly feel that the other was severing his connection with him, severing his connection with this world.

Not only was the Great God of Heavenly Covenant and Equal Contract's Heavenly Court authority being frozen, but his various links anchored to the Spirit Realm were also being severed one by one.

And right before vanishing, the Great God of Heavenly Covenant and Equal Contract only had time to leave behind two words.

"Zhang Yu!"

The next moment, Immortal Venerable Heavenly Fortune observed that a new proposal was being raised within the Heavenly Court.

"The Great God of Heavenly Covenant and Equal Contract has lost faith, betrayed his original aspiration, forgotten his mission, abused his divine authority, acted without principle, and vigorously engaged in the worship of Immortals..."

Immortal Venerable Heavenly Fortune said coldly, "Hmph, seeking to dismiss the Great God of Heavenly Covenant and Equal Contract?"

On the other side, Immortal Venerable Profound Wisdom also observed this and sensed that the Divine Kings representing the Heavenly Demon Sect, the White Bone Cult, the Netherworld Palace, the Joyful Union Sect, and the Celestial Monster Sect had already cast votes in favor of dismissing him.

Immortal Venerable Profound Wisdom knew well that the Great God of Heavenly Covenant and Equal Contract was considered a Great God who had actively sided with the Myriad Laws Sect. In the eyes of the other sects, he was one of the tentacles the Myriad Laws Sect had extended into the Heavenly Court. If given the chance to sever this tentacle, they would naturally jump at the opportunity.

Yet at the same time, Immortal Venerable Profound Wisdom also understood that the Immortals of the Myriad Laws Sect were not without allies among the Heavenly Court's representatives.

For instance, the Vajra Sect, the Heavenly Sword Sect, the Immortal Armaments Bureau, and the Pill Cauldron Sect had all voted against it. Adding the Divine Child controlled by the Myriad Laws Immortal, that made five opposing votes in total. 

After all, to carve up the authority and benefits of the Heavenly Court, the Immortals of the Ten Great Sects naturally banded together during their clashes and negotiations. 

Moreover, the conflicts of interest and disputes over Dao Lineages that these Immortals had engaged in over the long ages far exceeded Zhang Yu's in both depth and breadth. 

Immortal Venerable Profound Wisdom thought to herself, *"In the eyes of those Immortals from Heavenly Demon and Netherworld, we are far more worthy of suppression and vigilance than Zhang Yu."*

*"And in the eyes of the many Immortals from the Heavenly Sword Sect, the Vajra Sect, and the Immortal Armaments Bureau, standing against the opposing Immortals is likely far more valuable and necessary than dealing with Zhang Yu."* 

*"Because of this tug-of-war between the two sides, Zhang Pianpian and Zhang Yu have become a pivotal force in the Heavenly Court."*

Looking at Zhang Yu and Zhang Pianpian, who had still not cast their votes, Immortal Venerable Profound Wisdom could guess in her heart, *"Are you waiting for us to take the initiative to negotiate with you?"*

Just then, Immortal Venerable Profound Wisdom received a thought from the Extreme Emotion Sword Immortal: "Have you talked with Zhang Yu yet?"

Immortal Venerable Profound Wisdom replied: "Extreme Emotion, why is Zhang Yu able to sit in that position? Does it have something to do with the seventh Thunder Tribulation he overcame? Just what did he gain after the sevenfold Thunder Tribulation?"

Extreme Emotion Sword Immortal: "Nothing much, just a 'Paragon of Virtue' tag. [Smile]"

Immortal Venerable Profound Wisdom: "Paragon of Virtue?"

As the news gradually spread, many Immortals within the Myriad Laws Sect also came to learn of this.

Immortal Roujia's divine sense vibrated as she said, "A Paragon of Virtue can just do this? They can do whatever they want in the Heavenly Court?"

Immortal Venerable Profound Wisdom: "According to the Heavenly Sword Sect, in the underlying architecture of the Heavenly Court, a Paragon of Virtue truly can do whatever they want. Squander all their wealth however they please. Sacrifice themselves for others however they wish. Give up their own lives however they see fit. Help others however they like. Championing benevolence everywhere, and whoever blocks them is deemed evil."

Innate Immortal said, "Find Zhang Yu's main body immediately. As long as we suppress his main body as soon as possible, it won't matter how much authority he wields."

In the eyes of Innate Immortal, allowing a mortal like Zhang Yu, who had not even achieved immortality, to participate in the governance of the Heavenly Court and negotiate terms with Immortals like them was utterly insolent, an inversion of the natural order of heaven and earth.

Immortal Yi Xuan said, "Indeed! The vast majority of his clones have already been suppressed by us! Things have reached a point of no return. We might as well see this through to the end. As long as we suppress his main body, we will actually gain the upper hand in the Heavenly Court."

At this moment, Immortal Yi Xuan had harvested a substantial amount of assets from the 1st to the 19th layers. In particular, Zhang Yu's numerous clones and related resources were held in his and Immortal Roujia's hands, just one step away from being pocketed, so naturally he was unwilling to let go easily.

However, Immortal Venerable Profound Wisdom made the opposite choice, solely because she saw greater benefits through her communications with other sects and her own contemplation of the situation.

*"The Heavenly Demon Sect, the White Bone Cult, the Netherworld Palace, the Joyful Union Sect, the Celestial Monster Sect... have all already been contacting Zhang Yu."*

*"Once we continue to take action, not only will we clash with the Righteous Gods of Heavenly Court, but Zhang Yu will also completely lean toward the other sects."*

"When that time comes, we could face a pincer attack from both the Heavenly Court Righteous Gods and the other sects."

"And our voice within the Heavenly Court will also be severely diminished because of Zhang Yu joining forces with the other sects."

Immortal Venerable Profound Wisdom analyzed swiftly, "However, if we continue to yield layers 1 through 19 to Zhang Yu and join hands with him ourselves, we can seize greater authority in the Heavenly Court and secure a superior advantage in the maneuvers against the other sects."

In Immortal Venerable Profound Wisdom's view, though layers 1 through 19 held benefits, how could they compare to the immense stakes of layers 21 through 29, or even layer 30 and above?

Securing greater influence and commanding more of the Heavenly Court's blessings than the other sects in the high-stakes game beyond layer 21 was far more advantageous than merely occupying the Myriad Laws Region from layers 1 to 19.

As the divine wills of the Immortals oscillated and clashed, exchanging countless streams of information at blinding speed, the Immortals of the Myriad Laws Sect gradually reached a consensus.

At this moment, Immortal Roujia of the Reformist Faction also acutely realized that Zhang Yu was no longer a Lower Beast to be slaughtered at their whim, nor a low-tier cultivator who could only barely survive before them through threats of mutual destruction.

Without anyone realizing it, Zhang Yu no longer needed to wager his very life just to converse with them as an equal.

*"Zhang Yu... he has truly taken a seat at the chessboard of Kunxu, becoming one of the players holding the pieces."*
