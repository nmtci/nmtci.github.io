# Chapter 982: God of Investment, Proceed With a Free Hand in All Matters

Within the Myriad Laws Sect.

Fa Liuyuan glanced at the price of Zhang Yu's Tribulation Bond, which had already risen to 6234.

"It's rising too fast."

Knowing that the two Immortals of the Reformist Faction were shorting this bond, Fa Liuyuan frequently observed the bond market and paid attention to the related news.

Upon noticing that an anomaly had occurred during Zhang Yu's sixth thunder tribulation, Fa Liuyuan immediately sent a message to the two Immortals.

But even now, he had still not received a reply from either of them.

Fa Liuyuan's figure flickered repeatedly, arriving before an Immortal Abode, seeking an audience with Immortal Roujia and Immortal Yi Xuan.

At the same time, he once again sent a message to the two Immortals and requested a voice call, but he still received no response.

"What exactly is going on? Why have both Immortals become unreachable?"

Fa Liuyuan stood before the Immortal Abode, only to find the main gates tightly shut. No matter how he called out, there was no reaction at all. Just as a growing sense of foreboding welled up in his heart, a voice came from behind him.

"Immortal Roujia and Immortal Yi Xuan have already accepted the Immortal Emperor's invitation and gone to attend a research conference on the Supreme Immortal Artifact. That place is a closed environment, and they cannot be contacted from the outside."

Fa Liuyuan suddenly turned his head, only to see Dugu Ming looking at him with a smile.

*"Research the Supreme Immortal Artifact?"* Fa Liuyuan's heart sank. *"No, how could the Immortal Emperor pull them in to research a Supreme Immortal Artifact for no reason? There must be a catch..."*

He sighed, thinking in his mind: *"But driven by their aspiration for the Immortal Dao, the two teachers couldn't escape being blinded by greed. They still wanted to risk it all on a gamble, and ultimately fell for the trap."*

*"Alas, and this tactic of pulling people into meetings... it really is a tried-and-true trick since ancient times."*

Fa Liuyuan understood in his heart that this was an unavoidable matter. After all, when faced with the opportunity to research a Supreme Immortal Artifact, how many Immortals in this world could give it up? Who could resist this temptation to sprint toward the apex of the Immortal Dao?

At the same time, Dugu Ming held up a finger, and streaks of Zhang Yu's Tribulation Bonds surfaced around it.

"As for these bonds, they were bought from the hands of the two Immortals..."

Fa Liuyuan knew that the two Immortals had been shorting Zhang Yu's Tribulation Bonds, which meant borrowing the bonds at a high price, selling them, and then buying them back at a low price to return them, thereby earning the price difference.

And this final step of returning the bonds, completing the short trade to fully lock in the profit and secure the gains, was called to liquidate.

And hearing Dugu Ming's words about buying them up at this very moment... Fa Liuyuan's heart trembled: "Could it be that every time the two teachers shorted, every time they borrowed bonds and sold them, they were all bought by him? No... with Dugu Ming's level, he absolutely couldn't do this."

He only heard Dugu Ming continue: "The two Immortals sold from 1000 down to 500, and then they were invited to attend the meeting... Calculating it from start to finish, the average buying price of these bonds in my hands is about 700."

Heavenly Monarch Fa trembled slightly, glancing at the bonds that had already risen to 7000, a trace of helplessness appearing on his face.

"If the two Immortals haven't closed out their positions yet because they were stuck in a meeting... then this wave of losses must already be ten times their initial principal, wouldn't it?"

"The key is that this is only the beginning..."

Glancing at the bonds that were still continuing to rise, Fa Liuyuan understood that the loss figures from this short selling were still soaring.

Fa Liuyuan didn't know what the exact loss figures for the two Immortals would be, but any pool of capital that warranted personal execution by two Immortals was bound to be astronomical. Suffering a loss over ten times that base amount, if leverage was involved as well...

Dugu Ming continued, "The higher-ups hope to use this batch of bonds in exchange for the *Feather Book* you possess, Heavenly Monarch Fa."

Hearing this, countless pieces of information rapidly flashed and connected in Fa Liuyuan's mind.

*"Who else could be above Dugu Ming? It is none other than the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor."*

*"The Immortal Emperor... has been keeping a close watch on the two Immortals, even getting his hands on every single bond they sold."*

At this moment, Fa Liuyuan felt as if he could see the two high and mighty Reformist Faction Immortals acting like marionettes, completely controlled by the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor. Even every single movement and every transaction they made was fully seen through by him.

*"The Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor... wants to use a financial strangulation to effortlessly buy our Feather Book using the bonds sold by the two Immortals, leveraging this wave of losses..."*

*"What a brilliant scheme, truly a brilliant scheme."*

More information surfaced in his mind, causing Fa Liuyuan's heart to sink continuously: *"This was not a spur-of-the-moment decision, but a targeted, premeditated plot."*

*"Since when did it start? The moment they decided to demand the Feather Book? When the Heavenly Sword Sect came to visit?"*

Thinking of the Heavenly Sword Sect's visit, Fa Liuyuan could not help but fall into deeper thought.

*"Could it be that during that visit... both sides were discussing this matter? Were the two Immortal Emperors, Myriad Laws and Heavenly Sword, joining forces to set up a trap?"*

*"Even in this bond market, the Immortal Emperors have already invested funds, disguising themselves as retail cultivators to buy up a large number of bonds at low prices..."*

But the next moment, new thoughts surged in Fa Liuyuan's mind.

*"The two great Immortal Emperors joining forces couldn't possibly be just for this small matter. Therefore, setting a trap for the two Teachers and demanding the Feather Book is probably just a part of the plan, or perhaps even just a convenient addition..."*

*"This scheme by the two Immortal Emperors must have a greater goal."*

Just as Fa Liuyuan's mind was racing, Dugu Ming spoke up again to ask, "Heavenly Monarch Fa, what have you decided?"

Inside the Former Days Graveyard.

After Lu Hengzhang exited the market with a 60% loss, he had also been observing the market and reading the news.

He just wanted to see exactly how low Zhang Yu's Tribulation Bond would eventually fall.

And every time he watched the bond prices drop another round, and every time he heard the wails of numerous Soul Cultivators echoing in the Spirit Realm, he felt as if he were drinking immortal wine. His mood lightened considerably, and it seemed that his 60% loss was no longer so conspicuous. Even his risk of experiencing a Mental Meltdown as a Soul Cultivator had decreased a bit, making him increasingly glad that he had gotten out early.

But immediately after, the livestream went black, the thunder tribulation pierced through several layers of Kunxu, and the relevant departments released an investigation report...

Regarding this series of events, although the higher-ups did not conceal them from the Former Days Graveyard on the 11th layer, and even though they released these messages simultaneously across all layers of Kunxu, the effects were vastly different.

The primary reason was that the differing Immortal Dao environments, immortal aura concentrations, and sun and moon blessings on each layer... would all result in different computing power bonuses for each layer.

Within just 1 second after the lower-tier cultivators of the 11th layer received the news... for the upper-tier cultivators on the 16th layer, this 1 second was enough to be used as 100 seconds.

The huge gap in time resources meant that before the lower-tier cultivators could even react, the upper-tier cultivators had already done everything that needed to be done.

And this was only in the scenario where the lower-tier cultivators were actually able to receive the news at the very first moment.

In fact, the vast majority of lower-tier cultivators failed to read the information in the investigation report the moment it was released.

In Lu Hengzhang's case, because he didn't read the investigation report immediately, he was instead puzzled by the sudden increase in bond prices.

"It rebounded to 400? It must be the upper-tier cultivators intentionally pumping the price again to lure us in to take over their positions."

Having lost money all the way from 1000 down to 400, Lu Hengzhang was already full of experience with this and confidently made his judgment: "Don't even think about tricking me again this time!"

But the next moment, the bond sharply skyrocketed, jumping from 400 to 1000 in almost an instant. "What?!"

Looking at this scene in shock, Lu Hengzhang only then discovered the investigation report that had begun to spread wildly in the Spirit Realm.

By the time he finished reading the investigation report, interpreted its analysis, and looked back up at the bond price... it had already risen to 2000.

"No! I haven't gotten on board yet!"

Lu Hengzhang let out a miserable cry, continuously placing orders to buy the bond, only to find that no matter what, he couldn't buy it anymore.
