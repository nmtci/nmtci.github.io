# Chapter 1032 Riding High, Your Highness the Crown Prince

As streaks of Immortal Light descended, Fa Liuyuan, Dugu Ming, and Bu Yuangui all felt their minds shudder. The magic power within their bodies surged wildly, and various alarms throughout the Spirit Realm and beyond were triggered simultaneously.

Right away, they could sense that at this very moment, in this very place, at least ten or more Immortals were projecting their power.

A force capable of completely crushing Ascension Realm cultivators was swirling around the inside and outside of the Immortal Ascension Hall at this moment, in a manner that surpassed everyone's perception.

Feeling all of this, the three of them were struck with terror, nearly believing that the Immortals were about to resort to direct violence.

It was not until the next instant, when the Immortal Light coalesced into words, that they realized the meaning behind the word "Stop".

"The Immortals..." Fa Liuyuan's heart trembled. "Bowed their heads first? His Highness the Crown Prince's threat worked? He actually pulled this off?"

Yet, facing the lines of "Stop" before him, Zhang Yu remained unmoved.

He continued to let the black wall expand and said, "I am the Crown Prince of Myriad Laws. According to the sect's regulations and my agreement with Immortal Venerable Profound Wisdom, I have autonomy below the 21st floor. Even Immortals have no right to interfere with my actions."

The characters in the air shifted: "Didn't you want to speak with us? Zhang Yu, we are here."

Zhang Yu said, "According to sect law, titles must be used in formal professional settings. Are you Immortals still willing to abide by sect law?"

"If you are, then please address me as Crown Prince."

At the same time, whether in the Immortal Realm or within the sect, the black wall was still expanding rapidly. More and more of the Immortals' subordinates, platforms, companies, and conglomerates felt their connections to the Spirit Realm severed. In an instant, production came to a standstill and services were cut off.

One cultivator had just won a round on a gambling platform and was on the verge of winning a second when the service was cut off. Furious, he cursed, "Even an Immortal's casino is afraid of letting me keep winning? Did they see my luck today and decide to duck out?"

Thus, after repeatedly failing to reconnect or submit complaints, watching the incessantly spinning loading wheel, this cultivator angrily headed to platforms belonging to other sects.

At the same time, in the Spirit Realm, news regarding Zhang Yu's Immortal Realm broadcast and the massive breakdown across the Spirit Realm began chipping away at the trending searches. Spurred on by the machinations of the other sects — particularly the Heavenly Sword Sect — the heat climbed steadily higher.

Seeing this once-in-a-lifetime opportunity, countless corporations from the other nine Great Sects were like high schoolers catching the scent of study drugs, or junior college students finding a path into a bachelor's degree program. Channeling the ultimate efficiency of Kunxu culture, they concluded multiple rounds of meetings in an astonishingly short time to finalize promotional campaigns, eager to poach customers from the various platforms and enterprises of the Myriad Laws Sect.

Stock prices for well over a thousand companies under the Myriad Laws Sect began to fluctuate as well, sparking market panic.

Meanwhile, inside the Immortal Ascension Hall, the glowing script hanging in the air shifted: "Your Highness the Crown Prince, I believe we need to have a proper talk."

Zhang Yu replied, "I ask that you Immortals follow standard sect protocol and submit a meeting application through the Spirit Realm."

At that very moment in the Immortal Realm, the Immortals were still communicating at breakneck speeds.

The Innate Immortal said, "Roujia, Yi Xuan, the two of you shall go speak with him as our representatives. Remember to broadcast everything to us."

There was content.

In the eyes of the Innate Immortals, as dignified Immortals themselves, how could they lower themselves to request a connection with Zhang Yu? It was better to leave that to Roujia and Yi Xuan.

Roujia and Yi Xuan sent a request, but finding no response, their hearts sank abruptly. *"Could it be... because we're blocked?"*

Feeling the waiting consciousnesses of numerous Immortals, Roujia and Yi Xuan felt like they were sitting on pins and needles.

Unwilling to admit they had been blocked, they channeled their Immortal Power and sent a direct voice transmission across the void to Fa Liuyuan instead.

For Immortals, transmitting messages across tiers without relying on the mature, convenient services of the Spirit Realm and Dharma Realm Service was entirely feasible relying purely on their own power.

However, doing so not only drastically reduced efficiency and drove up costs, but it also made large-scale cross-tier operations impossible — operations that were crucial to the circulation of their assets.

"Liuyuan, we are the negotiation representatives. Please tell the Crown Prince to remove us from his blacklist..."

Fa Liuyuan glanced at the message that popped up before him, realizing it had come from the two Immortals of the Reformist Faction.

The very next moment, Fa Liuyuan immediately sent a message to Zhang Yu: "Your Highness the Crown Prince, the representatives sent by the Immortals are Immortal Roujia and Immortal Yi Xuan. You have them blocked."

Zhang Yu removed Immortal Roujia and Immortal Yi Xuan from his blocklist, and two Spirit Realm connection requests instantly surfaced before him.

Once he approved them one by one, both sides' conscious minds officially crossed tiers and met within the Spirit Realm Conference Room.

Representing the Myriad Laws Sect's Immortal Class were Immortal Roujia and Immortal Yi Xuan.

On Zhang Yu's side, besides himself, were Dugu Ming, Fa Liuyuan, and Bu Yuangui from the Immortal Ascension Hall.

At the same time, the thought speed within the Spirit Realm Conference Room was frantically accelerated by the two Immortals, granting both sides more subjective time for their conversation.

After all, every second the black wall continued to spread brought tremendous losses to the Immortal Class of the Myriad Laws Sect, something they were utterly unwilling to accept.

Thus, they hoped to conclude this negotiation and restore the Spirit Realm services within the shortest possible real-world time.

Meanwhile, the moment Dugu Ming, Fa Liuyuan, and Bu Yuangui entered the Spirit Realm conference room, all three observed the two minds belonging to the Immortals across the conference table.

This was the first time in their lives that they were able to sit at a negotiation table as representatives and engage in direct talks with Immortals.

The consciousnesses of the two Immortals radiated an incomparably overbearing pressure, wantonly stirring up and seizing all Spirit Realm resources in their surroundings.

Bu Yuangui and the other two could feel that, facing these Immortals, they were virtually unable to mobilize any Spirit Realm power at all, trapped in a lagging state.

The Immortals' thought speed was far beyond their own; in a mere instant, a torrent of information threatened to swallow them whole.

Dense Immortal Light locked onto their minds and physical bodies alike. With just a single thought, the Immortals could activate Immortal Arts to obliterate their physical bodies from afar, or even deploy various financial weapons to directly transfer funds from their accounts as fees for Immortal fortune.

Yet, none of this was the result of an intentional attack by the Immortals.

Merely under the passive influence of the Immortals, the bionics throughout the bodies of these three Ascension Realm cultivators were constantly flashing risk warnings.

These were Immortals — monsters whose authority and strength vastly eclipsed their own, beings standing at the very pinnacle of Kunxu's economic chain, and the counterparts of their negotiation today.

Just as Bu Yuangui and the other two felt the Immortal's overwhelming pressure, Zhang Yu had already used the Desolate Ox Great Sage to subtly mobilize the Spirit Realm Dao Lineage, and the Eight Divisions Great Gods he had summoned had also descended as projections.

In an instant, all three of them felt the Immortal's pressure greatly diminish.

---

Inside the Spirit Realm Conference Room, two distinct sides formed with crystal clarity. On Zhang Yu’s side, the Spirit Realm resources began flowing once more, ready for them to mobilize.

Their thoughts and their physical bodies were also blanketed by layer upon layer of protection within the divine power network, leaving them no longer worried about being struck dead from afar by the Immortals.

Additional calculation power talismans were even applied to them, allowing Zhang Yu and his companions to keep pace with the Immortals' speed of thought.

Immortal Yi Xuan: "Crown Prince, speak. What do you wish to discuss? After all, you hail from our Reformist Faction, so we will still speak on your behalf before the Immortals."

Immortal Roujia: "However... Crown Prince, do you realize that your actions have already severely enraged the Immortals? If not for the Immortals giving us face, they would have made a move against you long ago."

"Furthermore, the Spirit Realm is not the only means of communication. All the Immortals possess backup methods. It would only take a little time to replace it, so Your Highness must not assume that controlling the Spirit Realm leaves the Immortals helpless."

Immortal Yi Xuan admonished, "Your Highness, if the black wall continues to spread and the Immortals truly make up their minds to act, not even the Eight Divisions Great Gods will be able to completely stop them. You can observe for yourself — the Immortals are already massing their power at the border of the 19th layer. The moment our negotiations break down, they will make their move."

Zhang Yu could indeed see from the reports of the Eight Divisions Great Gods that portions of immortal-grade power were being deployed, ready to strike the sect at any moment.

Yet Zhang Yu understood even better that all of this remained nothing more than a threat.

He thought to himself, *"If you were actually going to attack, you would have done so long ago. You wouldn't have bothered notifying me, let alone holding a dialogue with me. This is what Kunxu is today — even the Immortal Class are, in the end, merely a bunch of profit-driven cultivation machines, unable to escape their endless pursuit of cultivation, cultivation realms, and assets. For that reason, they can compromise within a certain timeframe and scope."*

Naturally, Zhang Yu also understood that the Immortals' compromise was bound to be temporary.

*"Once these negotiations end and they catch their breath, they will probably launch the Spirit Realm replacement project immediately. After completing the transfer of assets and technology, they will make another move on me."*

Zhang Yu thought to himself, *"So for the Immortals, this negotiation is meant to stall for time. For me, it is also meant to stall for time, securing a window of opportunity to develop."*

At the same time, Zhang Yu's mind had already connected with the minds of Bu Yuangui, Fa Liuyuan, and Dugu Ming, continuing the negotiations together.

Zhang Yu wanted to use this opportunity to slightly disillusion the three Ascension Realm cultivators about the Immortal Class.

After all, over the coming period, his conflicts with the Immortal Class would be far from few.

The Sect Examination Clone said, "Well said, well said. We must make them realize that under the current situation, the Immortal Class is not unchallengeable. In fact, our side can even profit from confronting them. Only then can we mobilize more people's enthusiasm, get more and more people to follow our lead, and have them place heavy bets on us."

Thoughts surged through his mind in an instant. Facing the pressure from the two Immortals, Zhang Yu spoke directly: "My current will signal is already linked to the Spirit Realm."

"As long as I face any life-threatening danger, or am controlled, suppressed, or sealed by any Immortal of the Myriad Laws Sect, my will will immediately stop sending signals to the Spirit Realm."

"And once that happens, the Spirit Realm will trigger an automated function I've set up — namely, permanently shutting down all Spirit Realm services related to the Immortals of this sect, while simultaneously making all of your Spirit Realm assets completely accessible to the other nine great sects."

At that very moment, as Zhang Yu's words were broadcast, intensely murderous intent flared within the minds of the Immortals throughout the Immortal Realm.

The Innate Immortal said, "Dharma Realm, you have the most comprehensive data. Judging by your estimates, how much of what Zhang Yu said is true? If we strike immediately, what is the probability that he will instantly shut down the services and release our Spirit Realm assets?"

Within the mind of the Dharma Realm Immortal, countless streams of data regarding Zhang Yu circulated back and forth.

From the University War, to confronting the Supreme Truth Immortal Clan, to the past Heaven Collapse, the third-generation law establishment, the Myriad Laws Divine Sword, the Expiration Date Law Edict, the Book-Wielder Assembly...

A series of events and data flashed through his mind in an instant.

Dharma Realm Immortal spoke indifferently, "This person's habit has always been defying superiors, showing contempt for authority, and possessing an unyielding will. The sect's assessment of him is very fitting... as the saying goes, 'His resolve is so unyielding that it cannot be shaken.'"

"Based on his behavioral data, I have already built a Zhang Yu Simulator."

"According to the results of nearly 100,000 simulations, the probability of him acting immediately is approximately 99.9999%..."

Innate Immortal said, "Share it."

Moments later, the Immortals present all received a Zhang Yu Simulator.

At this moment, the Immortals' murderous intent toward Zhang Yu reached a peak.

For no Immortal could tolerate a cultivator using an Immortal's assets to threaten an Immortal.

Immortal assets must not be lost! This was an iron law of the sect!

Yet at the same time, it was precisely Zhang Yu's words, his past experiences, and the results of the Zhang Yu Simulator that instantly plunged the Immortals' fighting spirit to its lowest trough.

Because likewise, no Immortal could accept having their assets in the Spirit Realm wiped clean, let alone transferred to Immortals of other sects.

Their killing intent climbed to its peak, while their fighting spirit plunged to rock bottom; at this moment, the minds of the multitude of Immortals were like polar opposites of ice and fire.

---

Inside the conference room.

When Dugu Ming, Fa Liuyuan, and Bu Yuangui heard Zhang Yu's threat, they felt a jolt of terror running through even their physical bodies back in the material realm, their magic power churning wildly.

In all of Kunxu's history, they had never heard of any cultivator threatening Immortals like this.

And in the next moment, the Immortals' response surprised them even more.

Immortal Yi Xuan said, "State your demands."

Amid the shocked perception of Dugu Ming, Fa Liuyuan, and Bu Yuangui, Zhang Yu said impassively, "Starting today, the Immortals must cease all interference with the affairs of the sect layers. In particular, regarding Dao Lineages, no Dao Lineage may be used to maliciously influence the inside or outside of the sect."

"To ensure this, I will continue to use the black wall to cut off layers 19 through 21. Any Spirit Realm connection by the sect's Immortals must have my approval."

Immortal Yi Xuan said in a heavy voice, "Doesn't such a grave violation of Immortal Seclusion contravene the sect law?"

Zhang Yu said, "The authority to amend and interpret the sect law now lies entirely with me."

At this moment, Zhang Yu had long abandoned any thought of backing down.

Because he knew that at this very instant, it was his turn to step forward and shoulder the responsibility.

The Sect Examination Clone said in his heart, *"The Immortal Emperors cannot be relied upon, the Immortal Venerables cannot be relied upon, and the entire Immortal Class cannot be relied upon! In today's Kunxu, the pillar capable of leading the world against the apocalypse can only be us — it must be us."*

Immortal Slayer sighed inwardly, *"Grandmaster Ying's choice, the Immortal Ancestor's inheritance, the Monster Saint's legacy... Having received all of this, we have no excuse to shirk; we must step forward and take on our due responsibility."*

Zhang Yu looked at the two Immortals and said solemnly, "Immortals, I am the Crown Prince of Myriad Laws. Now that the Ultimate Doomsday draws near, the Myriad Laws Sect must implement wartime sect policy to combat this apocalypse. Within the scope of my governance, I must hold supreme authority over everything within the sect."

Immortal Roujia and Immortal Yi Xuan looked deeply at Zhang Yu and replied, "Very well."

Watching this scene unfold, Dugu Ming was inwardly shaken. *"The Immortals... conceded? That means the threat His Highness just posed truly can affect them."*

Looking at Zhang Yu at this moment, Dugu Ming had a faint feeling as though he were seeing the figure of the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor gradually overlap with his. *"His Highness... he has the bearing of an Immortal Emperor."*

Fa Liuyuan felt a surge of emotion in his heart. *"It worked! It actually worked?! Without the Immortals holding us back, we really can smoothly carry out the reforms."*

Sensing Zhang Yu's intent, Bu Yuangui sighed inwardly. *"So full of vigor and spirit, His Highness."*

With the conclusion of the negotiations, Heavenly Fortune Immortal Venerable's Destiny Dao Lineage also ceased its influence over the sect.

Feeling the turbulent thoughts of Dugu Ming, Fa Liuyuan, and Bu Yuangui, Zhang Yu spoke, "The reason the Immortals agreed is only because this time, I truly have the power to threaten a vast amount of their assets."

Zhang Yu sighed. "Because the Immortal Class is exceedingly arrogant. Wherever they can afford to be unreasonable, they will never be reasonable. If they show even a shred of reason, it is only because their hands were forced."

"So the outcome of these negotiations is merely temporary. They will certainly seek out an alternative to the Spirit Realm, and once they no longer fear the threat it poses, they will launch a counterattack."

Hearing Zhang Yu's words, Bu Yuangui and the other two deeply agreed, and the surging thoughts that had just agitated their minds quickly cooled down.

Zhang Yu understood that the upcoming window of time was of utmost importance; he had to seize every moment to develop his strength.

*"First, I must digest the gains from the Monster Saint this time..."*

While Zhang Yu raced against time to develop his power, all sorts of rumors regarding the Spirit Realm malfunction, the black wall, and the negotiations spread far and wide.

A mixture of true and false information circulated everywhere, and in the process, some began to refer to Zhang Yu — originally known as the Crown Prince of Myriad Laws — as Crown Prince Blazing Light.
