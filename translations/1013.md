# Chapter 1013: The Lost Immortal Emperor

Just as the rankings of the Book-Wielder Assembly shifted violently with the passage of time, more and more people's consciousness and ever-surging traffic converged in the Spirit Realm, all eyes fixed on the final standings.

After all, rumors had been circulating everywhere over these past days that this assembly would ultimately decide who would become the Immortal Ancestor's successor and inherit the Immortal Ancestor's legacy.

Having experienced the newly created Chain Formation Diagram, countless cultivators across the Great Sects had gradually come to understand the existence of the Books, thereby realizing the sheer power of its creator, the Immortal Ancestor.

In the eyes of the various factions' cultivators, this assembly resembled a proxy war between Immortal Emperors, with the book-wielders representing different Immortal Emperors to contend for the Immortal Ancestor's legacy.

As for who would ultimately claim the title of Immortal Ancestor's successor, countless cultivators harbored all sorts of thoughts. Some pondered its impact on the Dao Conflict, some sought cooperation, while others considered the fluctuations in stock prices and industries.

Yet regardless of what the cultivators thought, one tacit consensus had already formed.

That was: the final outcome of the Immortal Ancestor's successor would inevitably have a profound impact on the entirety of Kunxu.

Consequently, as the final places on the rankings gradually became clear, the related traffic reached its absolute zenith.

Whether poor or rich, lower- or higher-tier cultivator, living or dead... and even many who were not human, all were watching the final rankings through various channels at this moment.

Countless gazes and thoughts had already crossed the Spirit Realm, converging above the heads of Zhang Yu and the others.

It was as though they were witnessing the future of Kunxu, stirred up by the gathering of the ten book-wielders.

"It's Zhang Yu!"

Inside the livestream room, Heavenly Monarch Overdrive shouted, "The ultimate first place is out! It is Profound Monarch Zhang, Zhang Yu from the Myriad Laws Sect!"

"With his incomparably far-reaching vision, exceedingly resolute dao heart, and peerless patience, he has claimed victory among the ten book-wielders and pushed the Immortal Dao Gross Product of the world under his rule to first place! Let us congratulate him!"

"Perhaps before the assembly began, no one could have imagined that Profound Monarch Zhang, who had the lowest cultivation realm and the least seniority among the ten book-wielders, would become the biggest dark horse of this assembly."

Beside him, Heavenly Monarch Redundancy said, "Profound Monarch Zhang will become the Immortal Ancestor's successor and receive the Immortal Ancestor's legacy. Although we do not yet know the contents of this legacy, based on available intelligence, it must be related to the Books. A brand-new era may very well be born from this very moment!"

At the same time, the Transformation Book, Flight Book, and Ascension Book seemed to initiate some kind of ritual, merging into Zhang Yu's body one after another with unstoppable momentum.

Watching this scene, Heavenly Monarch Overdrive said, "This should be the start of the Immortal Ancestor's legacy ritual. Profound Monarch Zhang is receiving the specific contents of the legacy. While this is taking place, let us take this time to look back on Profound Monarch Zhang's path of growth, as well as his brilliant performance in this assembly..."

As news of Zhang Yu's ultimate victory was confirmed, all kinds of intelligence regarding Zhang Yu surged forth in the Spirit Realm in an instant.

Numerous cultivators of the Heavenly Sword Sect seized the opportunity to aggressively advertise and hype him up, promoting the growth of the Shock Sword Intent.

Within the Myriad Laws Sect, cultivators continuously praised Zhang Yu's performance and the glorious name of the Immortal Emperor.

Meanwhile, inside the Immortal Armaments Bureau, cultivators constantly claimed that the assembly's rules were unclear and had affected the performance of the Bureau's book-wielder, Source Saint, demanding that the assembly be held anew.

Also demanding that the assembly be held again were numerous monsterfolk from the Celestial Monster Sect. However, their focus was on the fact that, aside from Tian Yaosheng, none of the other book-wielders' Immortal Emperor Simulators had included any monsterfolk, suspecting monsterfolk discrimination.

Amid this clamorous, increasingly boiling Spirit Realm, countless divine thoughts descended upon the scene, attempting to connect with Zhang Yu.

"Profound Monarch Zhang, is there anything you would like to say at this very moment?"

"Profound Monarch Zhang, what are the specific contents of the Immortal Ancestor's legacy? Could you introduce them to us?!"

"Profound Monarch Zhang, why didn't you create monsterfolk in the illusionary realm? Do you treat humans and monsterfolk differently?"

Even as Zhang Yu was heavily surrounded, Bai Gusheng glared fiercely at him from not far away.

Watching his *Transformation Book*, as well as the *Flight Book* and *Ascension Book* that should have belonged to him, throw themselves into Zhang Yu's embrace, an incomparable jealousy surged in Bai Gusheng's heart.

Bai Gusheng had never imagined that the ultimate victor would turn out to be Zhang Yu — the very person he had looked down upon the most, whose work power and productivity had ranked dead last among the ten book-wielders.

*"I still say there's something wrong with this Immortal Ancestor's selection mechanism! It's just one's true nature — how can that represent the choices we would actually make in reality?"*

*"Which of the ten of us sitting here wouldn't sacrifice our true nature for the sake of profit?"*

At this moment, Bai Gusheng wished nothing more than to charge forward and snatch the books back.

Yet under the watch of countless eyes and presided over by the ten Immortal Emperors, no matter what Bai Gusheng felt inside, he could only watch helplessly in broad daylight as the Transformation Book, which he had cherished for so many years, was about to be used at will by Zhang Yu.

And when Bai Gusheng watched the Books being willingly absorbed by Zhang Yu, he couldn't help but sigh inwardly, "When that book was in my hands, it was never this eager."

Staring at Zhang Yu just like Bai Gusheng were Tian Feibao, former master of the Flight Book, and Qian Airen, former master of the Ascension Book. At this moment, both could only helplessly watch their books being completely claimed by Zhang Yu.

Someone even crossed the Spirit Realm to interview, "Heavenly Monarch Qian, regarding this failure, what do you think the main issue was? Who should be held responsible?"

Meanwhile, Zhang Yu turned his gaze in the direction of Wang Guiren.

He thought to himself, *"Could it be Senior Ying Xintian?"*

If the other party's performance during the first ninety-nine years in the Immortal Emperor Simulator had merely raised his suspicions, then their actions in the final year had left Zhang Yu ninety percent certain.

*"Severing the lifespan limit... in all of Kunxu, only Senior Ying Xintian could probably pull that off."*

Zhang Yu mused inwardly, *"However, with the ten Immortal Emperors presiding over this assembly, if Senior Ying Xintian truly came to participate, wouldn't being discovered be walking right into a trap?"*

Immortal Slayer said, "Grandmaster Ying would certainly not be so reckless. This Wang Guiren is likely just one of his clones. His true body must still be hidden somewhere, out of easy reach of the ten Immortal Emperors."

Sect Examination Clone said, "What did this fellow disguise his clone for? Could he be trying to snatch the Immortal Ancestor's legacy? Moreover, he actually replaced a Heavenly Demon Sect cultivator to get here. Could the Reincarnation Immortal Emperor have pulled his punches in secret?"

Immortal Slayer said, "Grandmaster's objective should indeed be the Immortal Ancestor's legacy. After all, judging by his ideals, he would never sit idly by while the ten Immortal Emperors claim the Immortal Ancestor's legacy and trigger the Ultimate Doomsday."

"Is it possible that in the previous rollback, it was Grandmaster Ying who achieved final victory and obtained the Immortal Ancestor's legacy?"

"As for the Reincarnation Immortal Emperor... he probably has his own agenda as well. After all, the ten Immortal Emperors are rife with conflicts among themselves and are hardly a unified bloc of interests."

Zhang Yu replayed the other party's performance in the footage repeatedly, musing to himself, "Competing for the Immortal Ancestor's legacy? Senior Ying Xintian took part in the Righteous-Demonic War and governed an entire sect. He has far more experience than I do. If he hadn't severed the lifespan at the final moment, causing his Immortal Dao Gross Product to plummet, that first place might not have been mine."

A flash of realization crossed Immortal Slayer's mind: "Why such a coincidence? Why did Grandmaster sever lifespan at the very last moment? Could it have been just to leave the opportunity to us?"

A thought stirred within Zhang Yu: "Are you saying Senior Ying Xintian could see how the rest of us were performing back then? So he yielded it to me?"

"That makes sense. With Senior Ying Xintian's cultivation, doing so wouldn't necessarily be out of reach."

"However..."

Zhang Yu looked up at the names of the ten Immortal Emperors, a trace of worry welling up in his heart. "I wonder if the ten Immortal Emperors have noticed Senior Ying Xintian?"

"Probably not. If they had, they would have made a move already. No, wait... It's also possible they know Senior Ying Xintian only sent an avatar. Even if they noticed, they definitely wouldn't strike just yet; they'd want to wait for a chance to find his true body instead."

Thoughts raced through Zhang Yu's mind. For a moment, he felt danger lurking everywhere around them, like a colossal powder keg where the slightest slip could trigger a world-shaking clash — a massive explosion that would blast Kunxu apart.

At the same time, a countdown surfaced before his eyes, like a fuse gradually igniting the scene, bit by bit.

This countdown was precisely the one Zhang Yu had set based on the countdown from the "future information".

"The moment this countdown reaches zero should be the time node of the last rollback."

"I wonder what will happen this time?"

Although myriad thoughts flashed through Zhang Yu's mind in that split second, to the outside world, it was merely an instant.

The *Transformation Book*, *Flight Book*, and *Ascension Book* had merged into his body one by one.

At the same time, an aged voice echoed in his heart, *"Focus your attention. Pour all your strength into absorbing the inheritance now."*

Zhang Yu was startled. Just as he was about to say something, he sensed that the three books had already been swallowed and absorbed by the *Feather Book* within him, and three brand-new chapters flipped open upon the *Feather Book* with a sudden rustle.

Sweeping his gaze over them, the first chapter he saw was the *Dao Succession Chapter*, generated by the *Transformation Book*.

"As I explored the Dao Lineages of past eras, I found that many seniors have perished while their Daos remained, leaving countless Dao Lineages to operate without masters — a situation both lamentable and dangerous."

"I specially pen this book in the sole hope that future generations will inherit the sages' Dao Lineages, ensuring that the painstaking efforts of these many predecessors are not buried in the depths of history forever, but rather put to good use and managed properly..."

Reading the message left by the Immortal Ancestor, Zhang Yu nodded slightly to himself. "The Immortal Ancestor's ideas actually coincide with those of the present-day Immortal Emperors."

"The Dao Lineages of successive generations of Immortals have added various rules to this world. These rules interact with and influence one another, possessing the potential to both benefit the world and trigger immense crises."

"All Dao Lineages must be recorded, controlled, and managed to prevent the dangers they might cause."

After briefly pondering the Dao Succession Chapter, Zhang Yu came to a rough understanding of its contents.

"Another shortcut to Ascension, allowing cultivators to Ascend and become an Immortal without needing a Dao Lineage of their own."

"To put it simply, one finds an ownerless Dao Lineage, substitutes themselves into it, and inherits it."

Just then, Zhang Yu's gaze flickered slightly as he caught sight of words surfacing upon the Dao Succession Chapter.

"Is this... lost history again?"

"And it's lost history concerning an Immortal Emperor, no less."

Zhang Yu read through the text rapidly, continuously summarizing it in his mind. "The founder of the Celestial Monster Sect was the very last Monster Saint. He originally established the Dao Lineage to foster cooperation between humans and monsters, unite the myriad races, and make all under heaven one family."

"Yet after repeated rollbacks and continuous manipulation, this founder's ideals were twisted. In the end, he reincarnated as a human, ruled over all monsters with a mortal body, and proclaimed himself the Celestial Monster Immortal Emperor."

Reading up to this point, Zhang Yu felt a jolt in his heart. "So this is the history that was rolled back for the Celestial Monster Immortal Emperor?"

While contemplating, Zhang Yu swiftly scanned the subsequent contents: "The Joyous Union Immortal Emperor was originally an extraordinary hero of his generation who valued love and righteousness above all else, having the deepest disgust throughout his life for the Immortal Dao's influence of severing all emotions and forsaking morality."

"Later, he entered the Dao through love, created the Sentient Heaven and Earth, and swore an oath to protect all passionate people under heaven. He went on to sweep away all evil, gradually becoming a paragon of the Righteous Path. The disciples under his lineage were also all deeply loyal and affectionate people who accompanied, understood, and aided one another within the Sentient Heaven and Earth, gradually forming a force capable of opposing those whose hearts leaned toward the Immortal Dao."

"Yet precisely because of this, he was targeted. After repeated rollbacks, he ultimately became the Joyous Union Immortal Emperor of the Joyful Union Sect."

Zhang Yu only remembered a wave of chill surging in his heart as he continued reading downward: "The White Bone Immortal Emperor was originally a medical sage of his generation who founded a Dao lineage, wishing for everyone under heaven to receive free medical treatment and to conquer all injuries and illnesses."

"Yet in the end, he still turned into the White Bone Immortal Emperor — someone who could cure people into utter bankruptcy and treat the living until they became Soul Cultivators."

Looking at these fragments of lost history belonging to the Immortal Emperors, a thought couldn't help but surge in Zhang Yu's mind: "The Celestial Monster Immortal Emperor, the White Bone Immortal Emperor, and the Joyous Union Immortal Emperor... did they all end up this way step-by-step because of the existence of the rollback?"

"Did someone use the rollback to quietly guide them? But if that's the case, why do they also possess the power of the rollback now?"

As Zhang Yu's thoughts raced like lightning, Immortal Slayer spoke up: "It means they defeated the previous generation's existence that could use the rollback — the one who wielded that Supreme Immortal Artifact. They seized this Supreme Immortal Artifact, became its masters, and then, alongside the other Immortal Emperors, took control of the authority over the World Rollback."

"Defeated them? But how did they manage to defeat an opponent who controlled the rollback?" Zhang Yu had never imagined that his ultimate goal — to resist the Supreme Immortal Artifact and oppose the World Rollback — might very well have already been accomplished once by the ten Immortal Emperors.

Connecting this to his current situation, he thought: "If that's true, they must have prepared countless measures to prevent themselves from being defeated by a similar method."

---

Just as Zhang Yu was comprehending the new chapter of the Feather Book and deciphering the lost history that had surfaced upon it.

On the 33rd layer of Kunxu.

Within the Sea of Myriad Laws.

The Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor sat cross-legged, surrounded by countless streams of drifting information.

Among them was the crucial intelligence he had obtained 30 days ago, when Zhang Yu had an imperial audience prior to attending the Book-Wielder Assembly.

Just like the first time Zhang Yu was granted an audience, no matter how he hid or prepared himself... the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor had ferreted out, read, and committed to memory every piece of future information Zhang Yu had concealed, without the slightest hint of forgetting any of it.

And right now, surging before the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor were lines upon lines of questions.

If Zhang Yu were to see these questions, he would realize... these were most likely the very messages that had once surfaced in the Feather Book — the inquiries sent from the future into the lost history, addressed to Ying Xintian, Heavenly Virtue, and the Monster Saint.

Although the replies from Ying Xintian, Heavenly Virtue, and the Monster Saint — being messages sent from the lost history — had not vanished from the Book at the time and were not forgotten by Zhang Yu, the part containing the questions sent from the future had still disappeared.

However, before that future information completely vanished and was forgotten, Zhang Yu had sealed and preserved it just like the rest of the future information.

And this batch of future information, just like the future information Zhang Yu had hidden during his first imperial audience, was discovered and read by the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor.

Looking at the contents of these questions, the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor pondered thoughtfully, "Although I can only see the questions, it's still clear enough. Are these inquiries directed at the Immortal Emperors of lost history?"

"Asking them for aid?"

The Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor could not help but smile, his laughter growing louder and louder.

"Traitor Ying from the lost history, along with Heavenly Virtue, the Monster Saint... you have finally taken the bait."

"So what if it is the lost history? From now on, it too shall fall under Our dominion."
