Zhang Yu's gaze then swept across, and countless comments and arguments from cultivators of that time surfaced before him.

"The Dharma Realm Sect Enterprise of the Myriad Laws Sect has already entered the market; it's just that they can't make a public announcement. Lie Jinghong is definitely going to rise this time!"

"With the patents for Law Realm Technology, who among the Ten Great Sects can bypass them? In the future, everyone will be held by the throat; this wave will definitely rise so high it'll make you dizzy."

"Those badmouthing it should just go do their outsourcing work properly. Don't disturb morale here and get in the way of everyone making a fortune."

"Lie Jinghong's upward trend isn't bad at all."

Just as this thought flashed through Zhang Yu's mind, a hint of surprise appeared in his eyes when he looked at the trend of Tai Yuebai's bond, and he asked, "Big Brother Tai... Profound Monarch Tai Yuebai is this formidable?"

Hearing Zhang Yu's words, the manager of the securities company in front of him, Lu Jingsong, laughed heartily and said with a face full of confidence, "I was the one responsible for guiding Profound Monarch Tai's Tribulation Bond back then."

"After all, it is the first Dual Rest Concept Tribulation Bond in the history of Kunxu, something that has never existed since the opening of heaven."

"Dual Rest, what lower-tier cultivator of the great sects doesn't like it?"

Things like the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor having already signed, and that next year the entire sect from top to bottom would start Dual Rest.

Lu Jingsong introduced, "The opening price of 50 was the result of Profound Monarch Tai's past efforts, as well as our assistance... working together."

"But to be able to skyrocket all the way after the market opened, this is because Profound Monarch Tai's own foundation is truly too profound."

Then the camera adjusted, continuously pulling up, and one could see... centered around the location where Tai Yuebai was being struck, surging thunderlight rampaged in all directions, directly covering a radius of several miles.

Lu Jingsong commented, "The thunder tribulation envelops a territory of several miles, with thunderbolts falling like a heavenly river, shaking heaven and earth, causing mountains and rivers to sway, spiritual energy to pour backward, and immortal aura to flutter. And this is only the first thunder tribulation. If this were in ancient times, the sixfold thunder tribulation striking simultaneously would likely be a disaster that destroys a sect and wipes out its members."

"To be able to trigger such a thunder tribulation is something rarely seen in a century. Profound Monarch Tai is indeed a cultivator heavily nurtured by the Reformist Faction; the profoundness of his foundation is rare in this world."

"It is exactly this kind of phenomenon that caused the price of his subsequent Tribulation Securities to climb all the way, having already broken through 100 Immortal Coins per lot."

In the eyes of the securities company, the current Myriad Laws Divine Sword, Zhang Yu, and his Tribulation Transcendence project were obviously also a big piece of fat meat.

*"Zhang Yu's fame is great, and to be able to reach this stage, his foundation will definitely not be bad."*

This time was just a simple meeting. To truly push the project forward, matters such as the specific agreement details, loan amount, the company's data entry for Zhang Yu, and his foundation assessment would all require a considerable amount of time.

However, Zhang Yu merely sent out a clone to follow up, dedicating the majority of his mental effort to cultivating and working.

After all, he knew what the outcome of the Tribulation Transcendence would be; ultimately, it all depended on his own foundational strength.

In the following days, Zhang Yu polished his cultivation daily, advanced his cultivation arts, and purchased a large amount of Immortal Crystals to continue increasing his Immortalization Degree.

From Kunxu Hero to Former Days Salary King, and then to the Myriad Laws Divine Sword... After repeatedly studying, observing, memorizing, and finally uploading the data to her exclusive High-Intelligence Artifact Spirit, Yun Zhiguang knew Zhang Yu's past experiences like the back of her hand.

"Furthermore, the Immortal Emperor is going to push for legal reform next. If I can follow Minister Zhang and participate in this major trend, I'm sure there will be plenty of opportunities to make meritorious contributions and get promoted."

"I must demonstrate my sincerity, determination, and value..."

This Heavenly Chosen of the Vajra Sect held the Law Edict of the Law Establishment Attainment Level within the sect, and similar to Zhang Yu... both had been gradually promoting legal reforms over the past two years.

Waves of attention and endorsements directly pushed Ye Heng's issue price to 100.

Several Ascension Realm cultivators from the Heavenly Sword Sect took action together, joining forces to stabilize the minor realm.

Meanwhile, the opening price of Ye Heng's Tribulation Securities skyrocketed all the way to 800 coins! It caused a sensation across the ten sects!

"A once-in-a-millennium opportunity to get rich is here! Commit suicide and sell yourselves! Go all in!"

"This is the number one foundation among the ten sects in Kunxu for the past hundred years!"

This wave of skyrocketing securities directly triggered a massive suicide tide, leading countless lower-tier cultivators to commit suicide and sell themselves, going all in with everything they had to buy the bonds.

"The target price has no ceiling! First we look at 1000, then 5000! To ascend and become an Immortal depends entirely on this wave!"

Even Shi Huaiyu, who originally paid no attention to Tribulation Securities, was drawn in by this and constantly asked Zhang Yu for his opinion.

Shi Huaiyu asked, "Minister Zhang, with this Ye Heng's foundation, will his future thunder tribulations be even fiercer? Can the Tribulation Bond still go up?"

Zhang Yu did not answer. He just cultivated silently, continuing to advance his Immortalization Degree in preparation for his subsequent thunder tribulation.

He sighed in his heart, *"Has the competition to get on the dining table already started from Tribulation Transcendence?"*

Meanwhile, inside the securities company, it was already bustling with activity.

"Ye Heng's bond price has exploded!"

Lu Jingsong looked at Zhang Yu's avatar. "Zhang Yu is also a pioneer general of the Law Edict reform, as well as the promoter of the Myriad Laws Sect's Law Edict reform. If he is going to undergo Tribulation Transcendence next..."

Just as Lu Jingsong was imagining Zhang Yu's Tribulation Securities price skyrocketing all the way, a report was delivered right in front of him.

At the same time, his superior leader, an Ascension Realm cultivator from the Heavenly Sword Sect named He Zhaisheng, was also one of the participants in the next Ascension Selection.

It was said that the Shock Sword Dao he mastered had already reached the mortal zenith. A single full-powered strike from him could shake the entire internet, catching the eyes of all cultivators from the 1st to the 20th layer and creating world-shocking news.

There were also rumors that he was a key executive of the Instant Sword Network, able to secretly influence the public opinion trends of the major sects, constantly absorbing Shocking Sword Intent from them at every moment.

At this very moment, He Zhaisheng said coldly, "Look at this report."

Lu Jingsong flipped through the report and saw that the document's content was an assessment of Zhang Yu's bond price.

The first half of the article detailed Zhang Yu's identity as the Myriad Laws Divine Sword, stating that he had repeatedly earned Merit Points for the Myriad Laws Sect over the years, and that every entry in his resume was highly valuable...

Lu Jingsong nodded slightly. Reading up to this point, he felt it matched his own thoughts. Just publicizing Zhang Yu's experiences over these past few years would be enough to push his issue price to over 500.

However, the subsequent analysis in the article made Lu Jingsong frown slightly.

"Zhang Yu's cultivation time is too short, and his time researching Immortal Dao Technology is even shorter. In particular, a lack of sufficient background in Immortal Dao Technology research and development will lead to an insufficient foundation?"

Lu Jingsong said, "I remember Zhang Yu has quite a lot of registered cultivation arts, right?"

He Zhaisheng said indifferently, "Haven't you checked his cultivation art copyright list? Almost ninety-nine percent of them are highly repetitive basic cultivation arts with low technological thresholds. Their effect on consolidating a foundation is negligible."

Lu Jingsong added, "In several major incidents, the work performance and combat performance he demonstrated were both quite good..."

He Zhaisheng said, "Those are all inheritances from seniors and rewards from upper-tier cultivators. Or do you think he could open a Grotto-Heaven by himself? That he could design the Law Edict Freezing technology on his own? He has merely applied a lot of Immortal Dao Technology; as for his own understanding, I'm afraid... he knows very little."

"After all, Zhang Yu's growth time is too short. He is not like Ye Heng, a cultivator who progressed steadily step by step and has experienced hundreds of years of Immortal Dao research."

"In the end, he is just an Administrative Cultivator, relying on doing tasks, making contributions, and opportunism in exchange for promotion opportunities. He is just like a medicinal plant forcibly inflated by being continuously watered with Immortal Dao resources..."

Lu Jingsong knew in his heart that the company was accustomed to classifying cultivators into Administrative Cultivators and Technical Cultivators during evaluations.

Unlike Administrative Cultivators who rose through the ranks by making contributions, Technical Cultivators often advanced step by step through Immortal Dao research and technological innovation. Every promotion in rank represented a profound foundation.

For such cultivators, once they triggered a backlash from Immortal Dao Technology, the scale of the thunder tribulation would naturally be larger.

And in Kunxu, a world under the tyranny of Immortal Dao Technology, whether in the bond market or the major sects, the potential of Technical Cultivators... was generally considered to be higher than that of Administrative Cultivators. They would be highly regarded wherever they went.

This was evident from the fact that over eighty percent of past Ascension Realm cultivators were Technical Cultivators.

And in the intelligence currently compiled on Zhang Yu, he was a standard Administrative Cultivator.

Lu Jingsong kept flipping through the information, frowning as he said, "I thought Zhang Yu, like Ye Heng and Tai Yuebai, hadn't stopped exploring technology while doing things, but now it seems he's really weak in Immortal Dao research."

Perhaps the other party was hiding something, or maybe there were some Immortal Dao technologies that weren't registered in the materials.

But from the assessment report in front of him, Lu Jingsong really couldn't see much of Zhang Yu's research background, let alone conclude that he had a deep foundation.

Lu Jingsong asked, "Then what do we do next?"

He Zhaisheng said indifferently, "What else can we do? We only recognize money, not people. Different types of cultivators undergoing Tribulation Transcendence naturally mean we use different methods to make money."

"You guys immediately modify the plan with the others. Fortunately, we have Ye Heng's example. Once we push up the price of Zhang Yu's Tribulation Bonds, we can let those who take over pay the bill..."

Lu Jingsong nodded slightly. Ultimately, their current discussion, although evaluating Zhang Yu, had nothing to do with underestimating or valuing him. They were just objectively assessing him in their own way, and then choosing the most suitable method to make money.
