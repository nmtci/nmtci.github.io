# Chapter 999: Zhang Yu Meets the Emperor!

The moment Zhang Yu stepped through the gate leading to the 33rd layer, the first thing he felt was an intense sense of barren emptiness.

The concentration of spiritual energy plummeted, the level of immortal qi fell drastically, and every element, substance, and energy in the air seemed to be plummeting toward 0%.

"Is this the 33rd layer? Spiritual energy, immortal qi... even basic air is almost non-existent?"

Reflected in Zhang Yu's visual system, the scene emerging before him was an endless expanse of darkness, layered with starlight and a galaxy woven together from these boundless points of light.

"Is this outer space?!"

The vacuum-like environment and the sea of stars naturally raised a suspicion in his mind.

"Did they fit the cosmic starry sky inside the 33rd layer?"

Yet the next moment, Zhang Yu realized that the world before his eyes still differed greatly from a true cosmic starry sky.

The stars twinkling in the dark seemed to form a surging river of stars, sending out layers upon layers of glowing ripples across the pitch-black abyss, constantly churning at a speed visible to the naked eye... roaring just like a true, mighty river.

At the same time, Zhang Yu could distinctly sense surges of dense spiritual energy and immortal qi all converging into those stars.

Through his spiritual energy vision, each star resembled a blazing sun, radiating spiritual energy fluctuations powerful enough to shake the core of any cultivator.

Just as Zhang Yu was stunned by the sight before him, a female voice transmitted a message to him: "Because the entire 33rd layer is the Immortal Emperor's residence, all the spiritual energy, immortal qi, and other resources here are completely at His Majesty's disposal...

"Therefore, to any outsider who arrives here, this place is a complete vacuum without even a trace of air."

Zhang Yu snapped his gaze toward the source of the message and saw the phantom figure of a young girl materializing beside him.

The young girl smiled and said, "I am Jing Hui, the Immortal Emperor's secretary."

Zhang Yu promptly cupped his hands in greeting. "Greetings, Fairy Jing."

Jing Hui looked at Zhang Yu with a benevolent expression. "I have read many of the letters you wrote to the Immortal Emperor, but this time was the one that pleased him the most. After obtaining the *Technique-Chaining Comprehensive Manual*, you did not try to keep it for yourself, but reported it to the Immortal Emperor right away. This proves you have sufficient Sect Nature. The Immortal Emperor appreciates you very much."

Seeing her attitude, Zhang Yu secretly breathed a sigh of relief in his heart. *"For now, it seems... things aren't looking bad."*

The moment Zhang Yu received the invitation from Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor summoning him to the 33rd layer for an audience, his initial feeling was neither honor, excitement, nor joy... but naked terror.

He had no idea what the ultimate outcome of facing this being at the pinnacle of Kunxu's Immortal Dao in person would be. All he could do before arriving was make every possible preparation.

Hearing what Jing Hui said at this moment, the Sect Examination Clone took control of Zhang Yu's body, stiffening all over as he said, "I am the Divine Sword chosen by the Immortal Emperor, nurtured by the Immortal Emperor all the way, and I will forever represent the Immortal Emperor's interests!"

Jing Hui said, "The Immortal Emperor is currently busy with some matters, but he will soon separate a wisp of his consciousness to meet with you."

Zhang Yu nodded slightly, secretly thinking, *"The Ten Immortal Emperors... that's a pure battle royale. In name, they rule Kunxu together, but in truth, it's a life-and-death competition where only one Immortal Emperor of the End will ultimately remain."*

Immortal Slayer said, "Indeed, the competition among the Ten Immortal Emperors is likely the fiercest in all of Kunxu. Not a single Immortal Emperor dares to relax in the slightest, terrified that they might become the one who gets consumed."

The Sect Examination Clone sighed with emotion, "Grandpa Immortal Emperor has it so hard, diligently cultivating every day without even a wisp of consciousness getting any rest."

From the moment he stepped through the immortal gate leading to the 33rd layer, Zhang Yu felt his Sect Examination Clone growing increasingly loyal, forcing him to keep constant watch over it to prevent it from acting recklessly.

Before this audience with the Immortal Emperor, Zhang Yu had made extensive preparations.

For instance... aside from his clone, he brought no one else with him, temporarily sealing away the Evil Gods, including Fu Ji, in the deepest depths of the Immortal Grotto-Heaven.

The Sect Examination Clone continued to lament inwardly, "Normally, given the difference in the flow of time between the upper and lower layers, Grandpa Immortal Emperor taking a moment to reply to our message means we receive it in the blink of an eye..."

Zhang Yu knew that the difference in the flow of time the clone spoke of referred to thought acceleration — an instant of thought in the lower layers could equate to a very long period of contemplation for the upper layers.

The Sect Examination Clone thought with great excitement, "Now, Grandpa Immortal Emperor has summoned us, setting aside a sliver of his consciousness specifically to communicate with us one-on-one in person. What immense regard this is! What an incredible honor?!"

Zhang Yu asked, "Fairy Jing, may I ask what place this is?"

Regarding the expanse before his eyes, which resembled the cosmic starry sky, Zhang Yu felt an immense curiosity.

And toward someone like Zhang Yu, who was a favorite in the Immortal Emperor's eyes, Jing Hui naturally did not hold back in explaining.

She said, "This is the Sea of Myriad Laws, a supercomputing ocean forged from countless Immortal Dao resources and sect crystallizations. Aside from the natural bright light triggered by the convergence of high-density spiritual energy and immortal qi, everything else remains forever in darkness because there is no need to display it to anyone other than the Immortal Emperor." [^1]

"This is also the birthplace of the first-generation Myriad Laws Source Model."

"The Immortal Emperor took all known technological information in the world and, through algorithmic permutations and combinations, exhausted every possibility to generate tens of quadrillions of cultivation arts."

She pointed toward the distant sea of stars and sighed, "In this starry sky, vast as an ocean of scrolls, almost every cultivation art in this world is contained — from the foundational to the military-grade, from the Immortal Sect True Inheritances to the Immortal Sect Heaven-Openings."

"And as long as this Myriad Laws Source Model continues to operate, it can advance with the times, continuously generating new cultivation arts from the new technologies developed across Kunxu at every single moment."

"It can be said that even if you added together all the cultivators throughout the entirety of Kunxu, they still could not match the creativity of this Sea of Myriad Laws when it comes to cultivation arts."

Shocked, Zhang Yu looked at the surging starry sky before him as the sound of Jing Hui's explanation continued beside him.

Jing Hui lamented, "Unfortunately... although the speed of generating cultivation arts is fast, searching through them is far too troublesome."

Zhang Yu thought to himself, *"Indeed, this Sea of Myriad Laws must contain a massive amount of low-quality cultivation arts. If every single art needs to be reviewed and evaluated one by one, it's far more troublesome than generating them."*

Jing Hui continued, "I still remember back then... when the Myriad Laws Source Model was continuously producing cultivation arts, any art created by any cultivator in the world could be matched to a corresponding art retrieved from the Sea of Myriad Laws based on its content. To the Immortal Emperor at the time, the act of cultivators across the world constantly developing cultivation arts was merely like excavators... digging out the cultivation arts that the Immortal Emperor had long since created from this Sea of Myriad Laws."

Jing Hui said with regret, "Later, although the Immortal Emperor continuously updated this method, in the end... he was still unable to create an algorithm that satisfied him enough to quickly filter high-quality cultivation arts from the Sea of Myriad Laws."

The Sect Examination Clone spoke up excitedly, "Grandpa Immortal Emperor's Immortal Dao Technology prowess is truly number one in Kunxu! Seeing this Sea of Myriad Laws today is truly like seeing the clear blue sky..."

However, Zhang Yu also noticed the underlying implication in Jing Hui's words and asked, "Is this Sea of Myriad Laws no longer in use?"

Jing Hui replied, "The efficiency of the Sea of Myriad Laws is still too slow for the Immortal Emperor. He has long since developed more advanced technology, so this place is currently no longer used to generate cultivation arts, but has been repurposed for other uses as it operates..."

Right then, Jing Hui seemed to sense something. Her figure paused slightly, and then she retreated backward, disappearing from before Zhang Yu.

Before Zhang Yu could react, a figure descended right before him.

Though it felt like a figure to Zhang Yu, what he actually saw was a patch of pure, absolute darkness.

That pitch darkness seemed poised to swallow everything in the world. Even the trillions of starlights in this Sea of Myriad Laws gradually dimmed, and even Zhang Yu's gaze was being drawn into the darkness. Even his thoughts... felt as though they were being constantly dragged toward this abyss.

*"He's absorbing our thoughts!"* Immortal Slayer warned in shock within his mind. *"Don't look directly at him!"*

Do not look directly at the Immortal Emperor.

This was the very first truth Zhang Yu understood upon meeting the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor.

And as Zhang Yu lowered his head and his thoughts regained clarity, that abyss-like pitch-black figure repeatedly surfaced in his mind, leaving him with only one thought: *"He... this is no longer any human or living being..."*

*"His very existence is destined to devour everything... whether wealth or knowledge, whether light or heat... He will extract everything in this world, down to the very last faint glimmer of light."*

Just as this thought surged within Zhang Yu's mind, the voice of the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor echoed in his head: "You are mistaken, Zhang Yu."

Startled, Zhang Yu nevertheless found it entirely expected: "The Immortal Emperor can read my thoughts..."

At this realization, Zhang Yu tried his best to control his thoughts and stop his mind from wandering.

The Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor continued, "The reason We need all of this right now is that We have yet to take that final step." [^2]

"Once We step into the realm of the Immortal Emperor of the End, everything in Kunxu will become useless to Us, devoid of even the slightest meaning. Why, then, would We bother to claim any of it?"

At the same time, a palm radiating normal bodily warmth gently pressed against Zhang Yu.

A gentle voice reached Zhang Yu's ears from beside him: "So, do you understand now?"

Zhang Yu raised his head and found that the darkness that had originally devoured everything had now transformed into the image of a gentle, jade-like man.

Yet Zhang Yu did not dare to view him as human in the slightest. He knew this was merely a human skin draped over him; that all-devouring abyss was the true Immortal Emperor.

He heard the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor before him continue, "Only if an Immortal Emperor... remains in this world endlessly will he continuously devour everything, until the very last bit of resources in this world is exhausted, bringing about the end of this realm."

"Only by truly becoming the Immortal Emperor of the End, ensuring there are no more Immortal Emperors in this world and rendering everything in this world truly meaningless, will it be... the only way to save the world, the only Righteous Path for all of you."

Zhang Yu's heart sank once more, feeling as though the countless secrets in his heart were thoroughly seen through by the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor at this very moment.

At the same time, the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor also spoke of a plan completely contrary to those of Tai Yuebai and Ying Xintian...

*"Without the Immortal Emperor of the End, Kunxu would instead be led toward doom? Only by helping the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor become the Immortal Emperor of the End can the apocalypse be averted?"*

For reasons unknown, Zhang Yu felt that the words spoken by the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor before him made immense sense, giving him an overwhelming impulse to believe them.

His Sect Examination Clone was already on the verge of kneeling: "So it turns out Grandpa Immortal Emperor is the one truly saving the world! The burden of Kunxu's thirty-six aboveground layers and eighteen underground layers has always rested on Grandpa Immortal Emperor's shoulders!"

The next moment, the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor shifted the topic, saying, "Reading the letter you wrote this time has brought great comfort to Us."

"You have not failed Us, so naturally We will not fail you."

"The *Technique-Chaining Comprehensive Manual* will remain with you. In some time, We will arrange a new position for you. In addition to confiscating the Books of the world, you will also be fully responsible for creating Chain Formation Diagrams using the *Technique-Chaining Comprehensive Manual*."

As he spoke, the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor lightly tapped his fingertip, and a flying sword materialized before Zhang Yu.

"With this Hundred-Billion Flying Sword in hand, it is as if We are present in person. Be it God or Immortal... whoever dares stand in your way, you have full authority to execute first and report later."

Holding the flying sword, Zhang Yu could not help but feel a surge of emotion in his heart, sensing the magic power in his body surging even faster along with the ups and downs of his thoughts.

Then, with a point of his finger, Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor drove a golden token into Zhang Yu's body.

"This is the Palace Gate Restriction I bestow upon you. Holding this restriction, you may meet Immortals without paying fees, and you may enter and leave this 33rd layer's Sea of Myriad Laws without announcing your presence. As long as you use this restriction to summon the Immortal gate, you can reach the 33rd layer directly and come see me at any time..."

Feeling the restriction driven into him by Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor, Zhang Yu felt that with just a single thought, he could pass through that Immortal gate to reach the 33rd layer directly from any layer of Kunxu.

The Sect Examination Clone said in shock, "Then from now on, wouldn't we be able to cross layers to have an audience with the Emperor at any time? This... what immense honor is this... what terrifying authority?"

Zhang Yu was also momentarily dazed.

"The Hundred-Billion Flying Sword... the Palace Gate Restriction..."

Either one of these rewards was a world-shaking, immense authority in Kunxu, a power granted to him by the Immortal Emperor.

*"The Hundred-Billion Flying Sword is enough to strike fear into any cultivator facing me, and even Immortals would not dare face its edge lightly."*

*"And the authority to meet the Immortal Emperor at any time... is even more terrifying. From now on, every single sentence or word I utter will be scrutinized by countless people, pondered over whether it represents the will of the Immortal Emperor. I will become one of the Immortal Emperor's spokespersons."*

At the same time, the Sect Examination Clone took control of Zhang Yu's body, prostrating completely on the ground: "Your Majesty treats this subject with such grace! Even if I am smashed to pieces, I shall lay down my life in service to the Immortal Emperor! You are my sovereign and my father!"

If not for the fear of overstepping and displeasing the Immortal Emperor, the Sect Examination Clone genuinely wanted to call Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor "Dad" at this very moment.

Yet in Zhang Yu's own heart, a faint trace of dread began to rise.

*"Such immense favor... such grand rewards... It's not normal. It's too abnormal."*

*"What is the price? What is the catch?"*

Zhang Yu knew deep down that Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor would never make a losing deal. Such a tremendous reward... had driven the tension in Zhang Yu's heart to its absolute peak.

Just as Zhang Yu was fraught with worry, he heard Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor speak: "Your current strength is still somewhat lacking. To prevent you from falling victim to underhanded schemes, let Us enhance you a little..."

With that said, Zhang Yu felt a hand press gently onto his head, and in the next moment... a power known as the Dharma Realm surged through his entire body.

The Sect Examination Clone said excitedly, "'The Immortal Emperor pats my head...' What amazing material! Record it, quick!"

Along with the Immortal Emperor's movement, the Dharma Realm's enhancement of Zhang Yu began.

First came a privilege regarding body refinement: starting today, as long as he was within the Dharma Realm and engaged in the process of body refinement, boundless body refinement essence would be channeled into his body through the Dharma Realm, increasing his body refinement efficiency tenfold.

Next was a monthly quota of one billion units of magic power, which he could draw from the Dharma Realm at any time...

Feeling the power of the Dharma Realm continually pouring into his body and surging through every fiber of his flesh, a wave of anxiety arose in Zhang Yu's heart: *"Is the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor... trying to scan my entire body? Read my memories through the Dharma Realm? Or even place a tighter restriction on me?"*

Zhang Yu knew that before coming for this audience with the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor, he must have made plenty of preparations. Yet even the memories of how he had prepared... were completely gone from his mind at this moment.

Zhang Yu could guess... that the relevant memories had likely all been erased by himself for fear that the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor would read them, leaving him with no idea what the outcome of all this would be.

Just then, he heard the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor say, "Zhang Yu, if a person feels that all their trump cards, their every background detail, and everything they wish to hide have been completely exposed, and feels that every step of their life can be monitored and controlled by someone else, what do you think... would happen to them?"

Zhang Yu's heart sank. Without waiting for him to reply, the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor continued, "They would despair."

"What I want is not a person in despair; such a person cannot become an Immortal. What I want is someone who harbors hope, who can exercise their own initiative, and who will strive with all their might, frantically... to improve themselves."

Zhang Yu's heart stirred: *"Does the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor mean... he won't drive me into a corner? Is this a deception? Or the truth?"*

The Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor said indifferently, "Therefore, I will not excessively interfere with you. This reward is merely a reward, nothing more. Think no further of it... Go."

Zhang Yu's body grew light, and in the next moment, he felt himself plunging headlong into the immortal gate.

At the same time, the Immortal Emperor's final words echoed in his ears.

"If I become the Immortal Emperor of the End, I will no longer concern myself with anything within Kunxu."

"If you are willing to aid me, I can tell you how to return to Earth."

Zhang Yu's heart shook violently, and in the next instant, he was submerged into the immortal gate, leaving the 33rd layer.

Just after Zhang Yu departed, a message gradually surfaced before the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor.

It was a heavily encrypted mass of information with a continuously ticking countdown — a message from the future.

"You truly are my peerless sword."

"Zhang Yu, reading the letter you wrote this time brings me immense gratification."
