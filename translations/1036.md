# Chapter 1036: High Morality Map, Hurting Others at One's Own Expense

Along with the information transmitted by the Great God of Myriad Paths and Four Directions, the map he presented also instantly changed.

Countless Divine Children with high Morality Points emerged across the map, transforming the entire chart into a High Morality Map. It was surrounded by holy light and surging with moral radiance, as if innumerable saints, great philanthropists, and supreme sages were walking within it. [^1]

Yet, combining the materials provided to him by both the Great God of Myriad Paths and Four Directions and the Great God of Sentient Creation, Zhang Yu could see the sheer malice hidden beneath.

Beside him, the Great God of Myriad Paths and Four Directions said, "Within the present Heavenly Court, under the shroud of the Morality Field, it has already become an absolute moral slaughterhouse. The exploited class with insufficient Morality Points will fall entirely to the very bottom of the morality chain, suffering repeated humiliation and extortion."

As he spoke, he tapped the map with his finger, revealing ten figures holding hands in a circle.

Moral capital circulated continuously among them, and Morality Points spiraled upward into the heavens above their heads, making them look just like a fully automated point-farming 'Boundless Merit Investment Recycling Machine'.

The Great God of Myriad Paths and Four Directions sighed, "You see, the Immortals of the Ten Great Sects have already decided on the Divine Child representatives for their respective interest groups. Because of this, the Ten Greats Good People have already been determined within the Morality Field, creating a High Morality Monopoly and solidifying the Moral Class."

"Your Highness, you have not achieved immortality after all. Once you enter the field... I fear you, too, will be firmly suppressed by the Virtue Pressure of these Ten Greats Good People."

Looking at the map provided by the Great God of Myriad Paths and Four Directions, Zhang Yu understood that the god was right, yet he still decided to make a trip to the Heavenly Court.

The Sect Examination Clone analyzed, "We must go! If we sit by and watch everything unfold like this, once the incident in the Heavenly Court concludes, the entire Heavenly Court and the Righteous God System will inevitably fall into the hands of the Ten Great Sects' Immortal Class."

"When that happens, without the protection of the Eight Divisions Great Gods, the Immortals will definitely strike immediately to suppress us. So we absolutely cannot sit and wait for death; we must intervene in this matter!"

Immortal Slayer said, "If we don't save Zhang Pianpian now, once this is over, she will fall into the hands of the Immortals, and it will be even more impossible to save her."

The Sect Examination Clone said, "Indeed, we must save Zhang Pianpian. She is the only Divine Child under our control, and she can be used to seize authority in the Heavenly Court during this crisis."

"Besides, if we enter the Heavenly Court, it's not as though we don't have cards to play. The risk is still within controllable limits."

Standing before Zhang Yu's projection, the Great God of Myriad Paths and Four Directions seemed to sense the resolute determination surfacing in his eyes.

He sighed. "It seems Your Highness the Crown Prince is still determined to take the risk. However, if you must enter, you could just use a Spirit Realm Projection. That would be much safer. Otherwise, even with a clone, being suppressed within the Morality Field could implicate your main body."

Zhang Yu shook his head and said, "Relying solely on a Spirit Realm Projection probably won't be enough to accomplish what I want to do."

---

Meanwhile.

Before the Reformist Faction's Immortal Roujia and Immortal Yi Xuan, new information surfaced once again.

Immortal Roujia said in surprise, "Hmm? Zhang Yu wants to enter the Heavenly Court in person, with his physical body? Just to save that Zhang Pianpian?"

Immortal Yi Xuan said impassively, "Zhang Pianpian is the only Divine Child under his command, and his only channel to participate in this distribution of power within the Heavenly Court. Of course he won't give that up."

"After all, he knows in his heart that if he misses this distribution of power in the Heavenly Court, once we gain control of the Righteous Gods, he will undoubtedly be liquidated immediately."

"So no matter what, even if he isn't a Divine Child, and even if the overall situation in the Morality Field is already set in stone, he still wants to make a desperate final struggle. Since a projection wouldn't allow him to exert his full strength, he's sending his physical body into the fray."

Immortal Roujia nodded slightly, her gaze shifting as she perceived another piece of information. "The other major sects don't seem to want to clash with Zhang Yu at this critical moment."

"Immortal Venerable Heavenly Fortune and Immortal Venerable Profound Wisdom have also agreed to it!"

Immortal Yi Xuan analyzed, "That's only natural. The situation is stable now, and the Ten Greats Good People have completed their monopoly. Not to mention that Zhang Yu lacks the identity of a Divine Child and can't farm Morality Points to obtain Heavenly Court Authority at all — even if he were the Divine Child, entering now wouldn't allow him to overturn anything."

"On the contrary, him choosing to enter with his physical body makes it even more convenient for us to suppress him using the Ten Greats Good People."

Immortal Yi Xuan continued his analysis, "Besides that, an even more crucial point is the attitude of the other nine great sects. Right now, the Ten Great Sects are dividing the authority of the Heavenly Court. If we are hell-bent on opposing Zhang Yu's entry, then this matter... would instead become bargaining chips for the other sects to negotiate with us, forcing us to take a smaller share of the Heavenly Court's authority."

Cede more power to the other sects just to keep Zhang Yu out of the Morality Field?

In the eyes of the two Reformist Immortals, this was entirely out of the question, and the Immortal Venerables above would certainly never agree to it either.

Immortal Yi Xuan made his final judgment: "Besides, even if he really stirs something up and tries to take a seat at the table to share in the Heavenly Court's authority, the Immortals of the various sects would never allow it."

Immortal Roujia knew in her heart that if it truly came to that point, the Immortals of every sect would surely join forces to sweep Zhang Yu off the table.

Meanwhile, the thoughts of Immortal Venerable Profound Wisdom and Immortal Venerable Heavenly Fortune collided slightly, exchanging countless streams of information in an instant.

A moment later, Immortal Venerable Heavenly Fortune looked at the Great God of Heavenly Covenant and Equal Contract beside him and asked, "Tianyue, what trump card do you think Zhang Yu has that makes him dare to take such a risk?"

The Great God of Heavenly Covenant and Equal Contract said indifferently, "Zhang Yu did, after all, pass the seventh Thunder Tribulation. That might be the source of his confidence that he can help Zhang Pianpian."

"After all, the Seventh Thunder Tribulation tests moral character. Since Zhang Yu could overcome that hurdle, he naturally received a relatively high rating in the eyes of the Heavenly Tribulation System."

Immortal Venerable Heavenly Fortune asked, "Then do you think he can turn the tables in the Morality Field?"

The Great God of Heavenly Covenant and Equal Contract shook his head and said, "Zhang Pianpian was suppressed by my own hand. He cannot save her."

"As for the influence of the Morality Field? Once any standard is made public and quantified into performance metrics, it ultimately turns into a competition of cultivation and capital. The same holds true within the Morality Field: only wealth yields virtue, and only greater wealth commands higher virtue. Relying solely on Zhang Yu's meager personal moral character, even with certain blessings from the Morality Field, how could he possibly triumph over well-honed capital operations and moral monopolies?"

"Besides..."

A figure from the Heavenly Sword Sect surfaced in the thoughts of the Great God of Heavenly Covenant and Equal Contract.

"Only the high-ranking members of the Heavenly Sword Sect know the detailed circumstances of the seventh Thunder Tribulation. Since the Extreme Emotion Sword Immortal has consented to this matter, it shows there is no significant issue."

The aloof figure similarly came to mind for Immortal Venerable Heavenly Fortune.

"The Extreme Emotion Sword Immortal..." Immortal Venerable Heavenly Fortune sighed. "I fear that not even the Immortal Emperors can fathom what this man is thinking."

"Now that he has taken charge of and reopened the Heavenly Sword Sect, he has grown even more unfathomable."

"I only hope he will not interfere with my grand world salvation plan."

Although a faint doubt still lingered in his heart, Immortal Venerable Heavenly Fortune could not possibly exert all his efforts to stop Zhang Yu over such a slight suspicion — nor would he hand the other sects bargaining chips to corner him at this crucial juncture, forcing him to surrender a portion of the Heavenly Court authority and delaying his progress in saving Kunxu.

Sometimes, Immortal Venerable Heavenly Fortune could not help but think that if there were fewer self-righteous fellows like Zhang Yu and Extreme Emotion in this world, perhaps things would not have come to this point.

Meanwhile, over in the Heavenly Sword Sect's camp on the other side, a burst of laughter suddenly rang out.

Several Immortals looked toward the Extreme Emotion Sword Immortal and asked, "Why do you laugh, Sect Leader?"

"Just watch closely. The fun is about to begin."

From Zhang Yu applying to enter the Heavenly Court to the application's approval, the information circulated among the Immortals and Great Gods in merely a split second.

The next moment, one of Zhang Yu's clones had already utilized spatial technology to traverse layers of Kunxu, descending directly into the sky above the 29th layer's Heavenly Court.

Relying on his authority as the Crown Prince of Myriad Laws and the enhancement of spatial technology from the World-Domain Dao Lineage, there was practically nowhere from the 1st to the 29th layers that Zhang Yu could not go.

It was only due to his contradictions and conflicts with the Immortals of the Myriad Laws Sect, along with the previous non-interference agreement, that Zhang Yu had never dispatched a clone to the regions between the 21st and 29th layers.

---

Now, controlling his clone and gazing down at the Heavenly Court beneath his feet, a wisp of Zhang Yu's consciousness had already descended once more into the Supreme Clarity Realm.

Surrounded by mountains within the familiar scenery of the Supreme Clarity Realm, Zhang Yu could no longer see any trace of Zhang Pianpian.

In fact, as soon as he had lost contact with Zhang Pianpian, Zhang Yu had immediately descended into the Supreme Clarity Realm, trying to call out to his elder sister.

Yet up until this very moment, the wisp of mind he had left in the Supreme Clarity Realm still could not sense any response or presence from Zhang Pianpian.

Meanwhile, right there within the Supreme Clarity Realm, Zhang Pianpian was actually standing directly in front of Zhang Yu.

Looking at Zhang Yu, who was unable to see her, Zhang Pianpian said, "Senior, is this your doing? Did you deliberately make it so Zhang Yu cannot see me or sense my presence? Just to see if he would enter the Morality Field, and how he would perform inside it?"

Beside her, a figure said calmly, "Everything you told me before was merely Zhang Yu's past."

"Yet at this very moment, Zhang Yu occupies a high position. He is no longer the person you once knew."

The figure sighed with emotion, "I have seen far, far too many cultivators who underwent drastic changes as their cultivation, realm, and power grew."

"Just like the ten Immortal Emperors of your era, along with the Immortal Venerables and Immortals of the various sects — do you really think they were always as they are now before reaching their current heights?"

The figure shook his head and said, "I already know what kind of person the Myriad Laws Divine Sword once was. Now, I want to see what kind of person the Crown Prince of Myriad Laws has become today."

Zhang Pianpian said, "But the other Divine Children completed their initial Moral Accumulation long ago. If Zhang Yu enters now, won't he just be crushed by them in an instant?"

The figure said indifferently, "No matter. I am here."

The next moment, in Zhang Pianpian's vision, the entire Supreme Clarity Realm shifted once more to reveal a scene of reality.

The one who appeared before Zhang Pianpian this time was still Zhang Yu.

Except now, high in the sky above the Heavenly Court, Zhang Yu plummeted sharply, falling toward the Heavenly Court.

The layers of restrictions, formations, and powers that had originally enveloped the entire Heavenly Court cracked open a slight slit, permitting Zhang Yu's physical body to descend.

And the instant Zhang Yu entered the Morality Field, an invisible ripple swept over his body — it was the Morality Field scanning him.

Further away, the gazes of several Divine Children had already locked onto Zhang Yu's position.

Watching this scene unfold, Zhang Pianpian hurriedly warned, "Senior! The other Divine Children are heading this way!"

The figure said indifferently, "Don't worry, I will grant him authorization right now."

"Zhang Yu, let me see how you, the Crown Prince of Myriad Laws, will elevate your own morality under the oppression of these Ten Greats Good People."

Zhang Pianpian understood as well. What the figure beside her wanted to see was how Zhang Yu would react under the extreme oppression of these High Morality Monopolists represented by the Ten Greats Good People. How would he raise his Morality Points beneath these crushing layers of pressure?

Yet in the very next moment, Zhang Pianpian seemed to sense a subtle shift in the figure's mood — a hint of surprise, of unexpected discovery, of bewilderment.

The figure spoke, "Zhang Pianpian, why didn't you tell me Zhang Yu has crossed a thunder tribulation?"

Zhang Pianpian replied, "Senior, didn't you ask for touching stories? Is crossing a thunder tribulation... touching?"

The figure said in a low, heavy voice, "Do you not know of the Heart Thunder? Do you not know of the Heart Thunder's trial of one's character and virtue?"

In an instant, the figure exchanged information with Zhang Pianpian, learning all about the new era's thunder tribulations, as well as the thunder tribulation loans and the Thunder Tribulation Bond Market.

The figure sighed. "I thought morality in this world had collapsed and the Heavenly Tribulation System had been destroyed by you all, yet to think it still exists. Though it's been altered into a complete mess, and Heart Thunder has even been sealed away. As for the many profound mysteries of the thunder tribulation... I'm afraid not many people know about them these days."

At the same time, the information obtained after the Morality Field just scanned Zhang Yu surfaced before the figure's eyes.

*"Detected a Paragon of Virtue evaluated by the thunder tribulation!"*

*"Activate Moral Archway?"*

*"Activate Moral Cloak?"*

*"Activate Moral Judgment?"*

A series of notifications regarding Moral Shackles, Moral Halo, Moral Altar, and High Morality Weapon directly emerged before the figure.

The figure immediately chose to conceal them for Zhang Yu.

"Once these things are activated, I'm afraid the Immortals of the Ten Great Sects will notice the anomaly right away. Better hide them for him temporarily."

At this moment, the gaze the figure directed toward Zhang Yu grew increasingly intrigued, seemingly filled with anticipation, eager to see just how Zhang Yu would perform.

Meanwhile, the moment Zhang Yu stepped into the Morality Field, he felt an invisible wave sweep over his power.

In the next instant, a series of notifications surfaced before him.

However, due to deliberate concealment, Zhang Yu could only see two portions of the content.

The first portion concerned the evaluation as a Paragon of Virtue.

The second portion concerned the blessings of the Morality Field being opened to him, along with some general rules within the Morality Field.

"Huh? Because of the evaluation as a Paragon of Virtue, I've also received the Divine Emperor's blessing and can earn Morality Points in the Morality Field?"

Zhang Yu knew that according to the current intel, only the Divine Children could farm Morality Points in the Morality Field to compete for authority over the Heavenly Court. If just anyone could participate, the Immortals would have long stepped in themselves; there would have been no chance for these Divine Children to serve as representatives for the various major interest groups.

Meanwhile, figures shrouded in holy light had already soared into the sky, and the Ten Greats Good People, hand in hand, were sweeping toward Zhang Yu.

Yet Zhang Yu merely smiled faintly, thinking to himself, *"This works out fine, too. Originally, I planned to rely on clones, space, and injury-transferring methods to dodge the Divine Children first, find a way to save my elder sister, and then help her raise her Morality Points.*

*"I didn't expect that I'd be able to farm points myself."*

Under the astonished gazes of the crowd, a character suddenly appeared above Zhang Yu's head, displaying his Morality Points.

This meant that even without the identity of a Divine Child, Zhang Yu was now able to farm Morality Points.

*"Then let's just get straight to it."*

"I announce..."

At that moment, Zhang Yu's voice crossed the Spirit Realm, reaching out across the entire 1st to 29th layers and echoing before every single cultivator covered by the Spirit Realm.

From the Reformist Faction, Immortal Roujia and Immortal Yi Xuan both watched this spectacle in utter surprise.

"How is he a Divine Child too? He actually has Morality Points?"

"What is he trying to do?!"

"It doesn't matter. The Ten Greats Good People will suppress him immediately."

In the very next instant, Zhang Yu's voice had already resounded throughout Kunxu, reaching the ears of the two Immortals as well.

"I announce that Spirit Realm Flow fees will now be discounted and waived for all cultivators!"

Immortal Roujia said in shock, "Has Zhang Yu lost his mind? Does he have any idea how much money is involved here?"

At the same time, even more detailed rules regarding the discounts and fee waivers for the Spirit Realm Flow were broadcast by Zhang Yu before everyone's eyes.

Immortal Yi Xuan quickly skimmed through the details, realization immediately dawning on him: *"Giving away this much free data every day? It's enough for the daily use of most cultivators, which is essentially making the Spirit Realm free of charge. But they have to watch advertisements? This guy definitely didn't come up with this move on a whim; this tactic must have been prepared specifically to deal with us."*

Immortal Yi Xuan understood in his heart that ever since Zhang Yu had threatened the Immortals of the Myriad Laws Sect using the Spirit Realm Dao Lineage, the Immortal Class had been actively preparing other means to replace the Spirit Realm.

And such a replacement was not merely technological; it was also a market replacement. It required migrating a vast number of cultivators away from the Spirit Realm, essentially jumping from one network environment to another — a massive ecological migration.

*"Yet by doing this now, Zhang Yu is using 'free service'... to preemptively seize the market, and then using advertising fees to sustain the Spirit Realm's numerous operating costs."*

*"Even so, the Spirit Realm's revenue will inevitably plummet. He would rather earn less money just to capture the market."*

Immortal Yi Xuan knew that in the past, the Spirit Realm had been a monopoly backed by the ten Immortal Emperors, so there had naturally been no need to rely on being free of charge to seize market share. Now, however, having sensed the Immortals' maneuvers, Zhang Yu was immediately using free access to entice cultivators into continuing to use the Spirit Realm.

*"Everyone was already using the Spirit Realm to begin with, giving it a massive first-mover advantage. Add to that this gambit of making it free, and it will draw countless low-tier cultivators to keep using it, which will greatly delay our progress in replacing the Spirit Realm."*

*"What's more, the advertising slots that everyone must view upon connecting to the Spirit Realm will control a colossal traffic portal, becoming a vital source of authority."*

*"And above all, by pulling off this 'free for all' gambit within the Morality Field, how much Merit will it earn him?"*

At the same time, the Ten Greats Good People had already received the orders from the Immortals.

"Give him no room to grow! Suppress Zhang Yu at once!"

A terrifying Virtue Pressure swept forth, surging with overwhelming goodwill toward Zhang Yu.

"Track down his account wallet!"

"Zhang Yu! Can your wallet withstand the relentless, rotating onslaught of the mega-funds from us Ten Greats Good People?!"
