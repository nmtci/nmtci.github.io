# Chapter 1030: Taking Control of the Spirit Realm

At this moment, Zhang Yu felt four completely distinct auras rise from the Desolate Ox Great Sage, each representing a different Monster Saint.

They hailed from different eras and had lived different lives, yet today, spanning the vast expanse of history to stand before him, they shared a single goal.

Zhang Yu spoke, "Monster Saint Seniors, please lend me a helping hand and join me in averting the apocalypse."

The Desolate Ox Great Sage assumed a solemn expression and said, "This is our bounden duty."

At the same time, the moment he felt the Desolate Ox Great Sage's intent, Zhang Yu directly activated the ability of Great Sage's Heir (10/10). In an instant, their intents were linked together, converging into one and the same Great Sage's Intent.

In that instant, a flash of enlightenment suddenly crossed Zhang Yu's mind.

"Rather than a collective life form, perhaps linking together the intents of different people and different individuals is the most vital function of Great Sage's Heir."

The next moment, countless thoughts and memories of the Desolate Ox Great Sage surged along the connection of intent, surfacing within Zhang Yu's mind.

Meanwhile, the voice of the Desolate Ox Great Sage resonated within Zhang Yu's mind: "Now, only a single wisp of our intent remains, and we are sealed here by the ten Immortal Emperors. What will help you the most are, first, the various memories in our minds concerning lost history, Immortal Dao Technology, and our respective Dao Lineages. Second are the authorities of the Spirit Realm Dao Lineage that we are still capable of operating at this moment. Thanks to how the ten Immortal Emperors utilized me after sealing me, as well as the Ten Great Sects' continuous construction and development of the Spirit Realm, the Spirit Realm today far exceeds the power it had during my lifetime, in terms of coverage area, the number of connected people and devices, and its inherent functions."

Zhang Yu understood in his heart that the Spirit Realm Dao Lineage had initially been like a barren wasteland, merely used to sustain the Desolate Ox Great Sage's collective will, craft collective life-forms, and transform the dead.

Only after years of continuous development and application by the Ten Great Sects had the Spirit Realm achieved unprecedented growth, multiplying its combat and operational capacities countless times over.

Even as their intents communicated, Zhang Yu felt the countless thoughts and memories surging into his mind finally condense into four directories, each representing a different Monster Saint.

Desolate Ox Great Sage continued, "As for our memories, they are simply too vast and chaotic. If I were to transmit them to you all at once, it would only bring you endless adverse effects."

"Therefore, I slightly organized the contents of the memories into these indexes. You can retrieve whatever memory contents you wish based on them."

Seeing these memory contents, the Sect Examination Clone grew exhilarated, "Brilliant, absolutely brilliant! The Fourth, Fifth, Sixth, and Seventh Monster Saint — four entirely different paths of Immortal Dao Technology, as well as the related cultivation arts... How much foundation can this add for us?"

"Not only the remaining Heaven-Opening cultivation art for the Great Sage's Heir, but other Heaven-Opening cultivation arts can be obtained as well!"

Immortal Slayer said, "These cultivation arts not only come with all kinds of requirements related to monsterfolk, but many also originate from lost history, which can minimize prying from Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor to the greatest extent."

The Desolate Ox Clone said, "The most crucial part is the intelligence regarding the Dao Lineages of the four Monster Saints..."

"Well said. The Dao Lineages are very important; the four of you should study them carefully."

Zhang Yu was momentarily stunned. Sensing this voice that had suddenly arisen in his mind, his initial reaction was that Desolate Ox Great Sage was speaking to him.

Yet in the very next instant, Desolate Ox Great Sage's will surged forward once more: "It's not me. Someone has intruded into our consciousness."

Under Desolate Ox Great Sage's control, the surging Great Sage's Intent immediately attempted to retaliate, sweeping across the entire sea of consciousness world like an earth-shattering storm that threw their thoughts into turmoil, shocking countless people into a stupor and leaving their minds completely blank.

"I didn't intrude. Didn't you invite me in?"

Zhang Yu continuously scanned the trails of intent connected to him, trying to trace the origin of this mysterious intent, while probing inwardly, "Who on earth are you?"

"There is another version of the Desolate Ox's tale. Are you interested in hearing it?"

In the next moment, without waiting for Zhang Yu's agreement, the mysterious intent made its move. A flood of information poured into Zhang Yu and Desolate Ox Great Sage, manifesting as countless images and sounds emerging in their minds.

It was in the distant past. A figure stood in the darkness, whispering, "I want to bring an end to the era of the Rollbacker."

"Desolate Ox, help me."

Gazing at the back of the figure in the dark, the relevant information surfaced in Zhang Yu's mind: "The one speaking is... Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor."

"And the one listening to him is the Seventh Monster Saint."

"The Seventh Monster Saint, before splitting into Celestial Monster and Desolate Ox."

In merely a brief instant, accompanied by countless scenes flowing through their minds, Zhang Yu and the Desolate Ox Great Sage felt as though they had traversed endless ages, witnessing the shifts of history and tracing the pulse of time...

Sensing the content of the information, Zhang Yu thought to himself, *"In the memories the Desolate Ox Great Sage gave me before, after he conversed with the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor, the other party vanished without a trace. To deal with the Rollbacker, the Desolate Ox was forced to split off the Celestial Monster Immortal Emperor to draw their attention, only to be betrayed by the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor in the end."*

*"Yet in these recollections provided by the mysterious intent, the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor didn't go missing. He and the Seventh Monster Saint joined forces in secret, seeking to defeat the Rollbacker and end the Rollback Era."*

*"However, the Seventh Monster Saint drew far too much attention. Any major move he made was bound to attract the notice of the Rollbacker."*

*"So the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor decided to turn this to their advantage, using the Seventh Monster Saint's conspicuous nature to draw the Rollbacker's focus, turning a disadvantage into a strength."*

Zhang Yu sighed inwardly, *"In this recollection, it was the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor who helped the Seventh Monster Saint complete the split, forming the Celestial Monster Immortal Emperor and the Desolate Ox Great Sage. Yet the Desolate Ox Great Sage has no trace of this crucial memory in his mind."*

At this moment, Zhang Yu also gradually understood the identity of the mysterious intent.

*"Does this memory belong to the Celestial Monster Immortal Emperor? From his perspective, after joining forces with the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor, he cast aside the Desolate Ox Great Sage as an abandoned pawn and ultimately defeated the Rollbacker."*

*"To him at the time, the Monster Saints' ideals of transforming Kunxu and changing the world, along with all those memories of lost history, had already turned into shackles, becoming obstacles on his path to ascending the apex of the Immortal Dao."*

*"Thus, the Celestial Monster Immortal Emperor cast off the ideological burdens of the past Monster Saints and willingly became a member of the Immortal Dao Monopoly Faction, ascending to the Supreme Seat and becoming an Immortal Emperor."*

*"And at this very moment, could this be a wisp of intent left behind in Kunxu by the Celestial Monster Immortal Emperor before taking part in the battle of the End? Is it communicating with us right now?"*

"In Kunxu, only the strongest can decide everything, and only by becoming the Immortal Emperor of the End can one change everything. To ascend the apex of the Immortal Dao, I am willing to pay any price."

The Desolate Ox Great Sage said coldly, "Even if it means abandoning past experiences? Past memories? Even altering your very ideals for why you sought to reach the summit in the first place?"

"All things change, and change alone remains unchanged. Today, I am willing to reclaim my past memories, inherit the original aspirations of the Monster Saints, and usher in the End's golden age. What right do any of you have to stop me?"

Feeling the Celestial Monster Immortal Emperor's intent, an odd sensation welled up in Immortal Slayer's heart.

If everything truly was as the Celestial Monster Immortal Emperor claimed, did his willingness to retrieve the Monster Saint's memories now signify remaining true to his original aspiration? If he truly remained unswerving even after casting everything aside, still seeking to inaugurate the End's golden age, should he still be stopped?

"Who cares what he's thinking!" said the Sect Examination Clone. "Anyone seeking to ascend as the Immortal Emperor of the End cannot be trusted. Only if we ascend ourselves can we be trusted."

The Desolate Ox Great Sage said, "Celestial Monster Immortal Emperor, regardless of why we split, the present me still carries the ideals of the past, while you have been thoroughly transformed by the new era of Kunxu. The future you is destined to never understand the past me; you and I can never trust each other."

In that instant, Zhang Yu felt the Desolate Ox Great Sage's consciousness violently convulse. In this moment, the Desolate Ox Great Sage finally discovered the location where that wisp of the Celestial Monster Immortal Emperor's will had infiltrated.

The Desolate Ox Great Sage said, "It is the Monster God. He was not a collective lifeform created by the Celestial Monster Immortal Emperor. Rather, I fear that as early as the moment of our division, a portion of the collective will was severed from my sea of consciousness and, nurtured afterward, grew strong into what it is today."

Through communicating with the Desolate Ox Great Sage via consciousness, Zhang Yu quickly understood what had happened.

*"The Monster God and the Desolate Ox Great Sage were originally one and the same, a part of the collective will,"* Zhang Yu thought to himself. *"This time, after invading the Desolate Ox Great Sage's sea of consciousness, it seemed on the surface that he was trying to take control by altering the Desolate Ox's thoughts. In truth, he was quietly merging into the Desolate Ox Great Sage, secretly reading the memories of various Monster Saints."*

The Desolate Ox Great Sage said, "Before I woke up, a small part of the Monster God's consciousness had already successfully merged into my collective will. Fortunately, you arrived in time, or an even greater portion would have been assimilated."

Zhang Yu understood that because the Monster God had originally originated from the Desolate Ox Great Sage, the merging of that part of his consciousness had prevented the Desolate Ox Great Sage from noticing immediately.

Zhang Yu speculated internally, *"However, the Desolate Ox Great Sage was bound to notice eventually. So... it must have been right before the Desolate Ox Great Sage was about to detect it that he took the initiative to reveal himself, creating a diversion to buy time for reading the memories."*

Although they had successfully thwarted the Monster God's plan to control the Desolate Ox Great Sage this time, the fact that the Desolate Ox Great Sage's memories had been accessed still infuriated the Sect Examination Clone: "Those are all our data assets! How much of it has been read by the Celestial Monster Immortal Emperor now?"

Desolate Ox Great Sage shook his head: "We intercepted it the moment we discovered it, so at least he didn't manage to read all the memories. But as for just how much he read... it's hard to estimate the exact extent."

Zhang Yu understood that without the obstruction of the other nine Immortal Emperors, Celestial Monster Immortal Emperor had finally, at this very moment, regained the memories he had discarded for so many years.

Except that the memories of the Monster Saint — once viewed by him as shackles and hindrances — had now become the nourishment for him to take another step forward.

Zhang Yu thought to himself, *"I wonder if the Battle of the End among the ten Immortal Emperors will be affected by this?"*

As they spoke, Desolate Ox Great Sage's mind once again merged with Zhang Yu's.

Desolate Ox Great Sage said, "The Monster God has already severed the connection, but he is hiding right here in Kunxu. With my authority in the Spirit Realm combined with your access, we might have a chance to track his whereabouts."

"If we can capture the Monster God, we might be able to find the wisp of Celestial Monster Immortal Emperor's consciousness left in Kunxu, as well as my physical body that they have hidden away."

Desolate Ox Great Sage continued, "That would be of no small help in stopping the apocalypse."

Zhang Yu nodded in agreement with Desolate Ox Great Sage's proposal, intending to attempt to track the Monster God's whereabouts.

Although the Desolate Ox Great Sage could not leave this place due to the seal of the ten Immortal Emperors, as long as he used Zhang Yu's mind as a relay station to send and receive various Spirit Realm information through it, he could interface with the external Spirit Realm.

Moreover, with the Desolate Ox Great Sage operating the Spirit Realm Dao Lineage, Zhang Yu was able to roam the Spirit Realm with unprecedented efficiency.

The next moment, with merely a thought, Zhang Yu felt as though the entire Spirit Realm were laid bare right before him.

Meanwhile, Zhang Pianpian, who had been waiting for Zhang Yu, noticed that the moment his mind returned, it began ravenously devouring all the data flowing through the area, rising in the Spirit Realm like a budding data black hole.

Zhang Pianpian asked in astonishment, "Brother, what happened to you?"

Zhang Yu sent out wisps of his consciousness to wander the Spirit Realm. Wherever his thoughts reached, countless streams of Spirit Realm information unfolded on their own, laying every hidden secret bare before his eyes.

Seeing the message from Zhang Pianpian, Zhang Yu replied, "Sis, I feel great right now — better than ever."

With a mere sweep of his gaze, the informational traces left behind by the Monster God in the Spirit Realm were directly tagged and highlighted before him.

The next moment, Zhang Yu's thoughts flashed, and he crossed the Spirit Realm to arrive at the outskirts of a certain Immortal Abode in the Celestial Monster Sect area on the 19th layer.

Zhang Yu thought to himself, *"He disconnected from the Spirit Realm here and completely submerged into the material world."*

Desolate Ox Great Sage said, "He knows we would track him down. From now on, he probably won't easily connect to the Spirit Realm, but will instead operate by manipulating his physical body in the material realm."

Zhang Yu asked, "A physical body in the material realm? Could it be your physical body, Senior?"

Desolate Ox Great Sage replied, "It's hard to say. However, even if he only moves within the material world, with how developed the Spirit Realm is today, as long as we keep tracking him, we might still find the traces he leaves behind."

Zhang Yu looked up at the sky, gazing toward the heavens of the 19th layer, and said, "I'm just worried that after disconnecting, he might have headed to the 21st layer. That is the territory of the Immortal Class."

Desolate Ox Great Sage said, "Leave it to me. You just need to spare a wisp of your consciousness to cooperate with me."

Right at that moment, Zhang Yu sensed numerous calls coming from the Spirit Realm. Frowning slightly, he said, "Senior, I'll temporarily leave the task of tracking the Monster God to you. I need to make a trip back to the Myriad Laws Sect."

With a mere thought, the Desolate Ox Great Sage also sensed what was happening in the Myriad Laws Sect through the Spirit Realm. Nodding slightly, he said, "Go ahead. If you need my support, just communicate with me directly through your mind."

Inside the Myriad Laws Sect, chaos was gradually spreading.

As the Chief Soul Cultivator under the Crown Prince, Tianxing watched the continuously updating reports before his eyes, his heart growing increasingly anxious: *"Why hasn't His Highness the Crown Prince replied yet?"*

Just after the Desolate Ox Great Sage fully awakened and began conversing with Zhang Yu, Zhang Yu had temporarily severed his mental connection with the many cultivators of the Myriad Laws Sect.

Not long after shifting his attention back to the sect, Tianxing discovered that across layers 11 through 19, economic disputes kept breaking out, which then quickly escalated into armed conflicts.

At the same time, incident report after incident report floated before Zhao Tianxing.

There was a cultivator who had only wanted a Bionic massage to relieve vital energy depletion, but was forced into dual cultivation by the Joyful Union Sect cultivator in charge; he had resorted to force to reclaim his Dual Cultivation Essence, which ultimately triggered a combat incident.

Zhao Tianxing criticized, "These migrant cultivators from the Joyful Union Sect are getting more and more out of line. They always charge for half a service but dare to do a full one, and most of them are operating without a license. A ruthless crackdown is long overdue."

There was a medical malpractice incident where a medical company excessively extended a patient's longevity by a century, after which the patient was unable to pay the medical expenses.

Zhao Tianxing thought to himself, *"Excessively extending longevity by a century? This patient only has Golden Core cultivation; how could they possibly afford it? It truly is a severe medical malpractice incident."*

Zhao Tianxing knew deep down that no matter how many treatment complications a patient suffered, they could always be cured, which hardly qualified as an incident. Only an excessive surge in bad medical debts that caused losses for medical companies would threaten the healthcare industry — and that was a true medical malpractice incident.

*"Sigh, if the patient can't pay, they can only use their physical body as collateral. To preserve the asset's value and lower wear and depreciation rates, the medical company's attempt to freeze the patient's physical body is fair and legal."*

*"The patient's employer paid their wages and demanded the patient continue working with their physical body, which is also fair and legal."*

*"Both sides fought over the person, and an armed conflict broke out immediately. The medical company holds ownership of the patient's physical body, while the company the patient works for holds short-term usage rights to the patient's physical body. This is a contradiction between mortgage rights and usage rights."*

Zhao Tianxing shook his head as he read through it. All he could say was that the patient's physical body certainly did not belong to the patient himself. But as to whether it should belong to the medical company or his employer in the short term, both sides had a point in his eyes — it simply came down to who had the higher cultivation realm and greater assets.

As more and more reports kept surfacing, Zhao Tianxing felt increasingly uneasy the more he read. *"Something is wrong, deeply wrong. How could conflicts break out among so many people in a single day? They're even coming directly to blows?"*

Although various incidents were quickly intervened in and suppressed under the power of the Righteous Gods, they could not prevent new conflicts from continually erupting.

Zhao Tianxing felt as if a pair of invisible, massive hands were churning back and forth between the 11th and 19th layers of the Myriad Laws Sect, tangling countless people together and sparking all kinds of conflicts that should never have broken out.

"With the Crown Prince supervising the sect right now, this is supposed to be an era where the people's salaries are aligned, governance is smooth and harmonious, and everything is renewed with salary. Stirring up so many armed conflicts, continually causing asset losses to the sect, and disrupting the operations of major corporations — isn't this slapping the Crown Prince right in the face?"

Because Zhang Yu had not replied for a long time, Zhao Tianxing had no choice but to contact Dugu Ming, Bu Yuangui, and Fa Liuyuan of the Immortal Ascension Hall to discuss countermeasures. 

Facing Zhao Tianxing, who was a close confidant at Zhang Yu's side, the three Ascension Realm cultivators showed great respect, sending over a red packet of 10,000 Immortal Coins the moment they met. 

Zhao Tianxing knew he could not accept such red packets carelessly, so he quickly transferred eighty percent of it to Zhang Yu. Only then did his conscience feel clear. 

Zhao Tianxing then recounted the sect's current situation and asked, "How do the three Heavenly Monarchs think we should handle this?"

Recalling the process of connecting his consciousness with Zhang Yu's, Bu Yuangui thought to himself, "That place seemed to be deep within the Spirit Realm, related to the legendary Great Sage's Intent. At this moment, the Crown Prince is likely undertaking an earth-shattering endeavor and temporarily has no spare energy to oversee matters here."

With this in mind, Bu Yuangui said, "I propose that we launch a fundraising drive, encouraging units at all levels to make voluntary donations to offset the losses suffered by the Crown Prince and the sect."

Bu Yuangui thought to himself, *"No matter what is going on, donating money to the Crown Prince can never go wrong. It is a foolproof solution."*

Dugu Ming said, "I propose a sect-wide crackdown, multiplying fines a hundredfold for all unauthorized uses of force to ruthlessly suppress this rampant arrogance of abusing martial might."

Zhao Tianxing nodded slightly and asked again, "But if we do this, won't those licensed to use force have too great an advantage?"

Dugu Ming said, "That is why it must be coupled with a policy of raising license prices to hold a dynamic balance."

Dugu Ming thought to himself, *"In any case, a method that increases revenue for the sect can never be wrong."*

Listening to Dugu Ming and Bu Yuangui's countermeasures, Fa Liuyuan could not help but sigh, "Gentlemen, doing this treats the symptoms rather than the root cause. Can you really not see the true problem?"

Bu Yuangui thought to himself, *"Can the true problem even be spoken aloud? Should we just say outright that Immortal Venerable Heavenly Fortune is operating the Destiny Dao Lineage and churning up the sect's karma? And after saying it, should the three of us Ascension Realm Lower Cultivation figures discuss how to deal with an Immortal Venerable?"*

Just as the three Ascension Realm cultivators were deliberating back and forth, a message suddenly surfaced before Fa Liuyuan. "A message from the Immortal Realm?"

Fa Liuyuan knew that the 21st to 29th layers, having always been the dwelling of the Immortal Class and the destination for cultivators who ascend and become an Immortal, were referred to by many as the Immortal Realm; even he himself often called it that.

After clearly reading the contents of the message, Fa Liuyuan's heart sank, and he spoke, "Someone from the Immortal Realm has sent word down."

Upon hearing this, Zhao Tianxing, Dugu Ming, and Bu Yuangui all felt their hearts sink.

Just at that moment, Zhang Yu's Spirit Realm Projection disregarded all the sects' restrictions and the cultivators' senses, descending with a crash into everyone's Spirit Realm vision.

"Crown Prince!" Zhao Tianxing cried out in surprise. "You have finally returned!"

Zhang Yu nodded slightly to the four of them, glanced at Fa Liuyuan, and sighed softly. "The message you just received was sent by the two Immortals of the Reformist Faction, wasn't it?"

There was no need for Fa Liuyuan to reveal it himself; at this moment, the Spirit Realm held no secrets from Zhang Yu's eyes, and the contents of the messages sent by the two Immortals floated directly before him.

Immortal Roujia: "Tell Zhang Yu that to combat the apocalypse, he must prioritize the big picture, and that he should not continue to disrupt unity between humans and Immortals."

Immortal Yi Xuan: "In the end, the sects must be guided by the ideology of the Immortals and rally tightly around them. What kind of nonsense is this self-governance of the lower-tier cultivators?"

Zhang Yu looked at Fa Liuyuan and said, "Heavenly Monarch Fa, please pass a message to Immortal Venerable Heavenly Fortune for me. I wish to meet with him and have a talk."

Fa Liuyuan nodded slightly, knowing this was his moment to prove his worth — acting as a bridge of communication between the sect layers and the Immortal Realm to ease tensions between both sides.

*"An Immortal Venerable cannot be matched with force, let alone confronted head-on. It's best if we can find a way to resolve this through negotiations."*

Thus, Fa Liuyuan sent a message to the two Immortals of the Reformist Faction.

"Master, Zhang Yu wishes to request an audience with Immortal Venerable Heavenly Fortune to discuss matters concerning the sect."

Fa Liuyuan soon received a response, but it was not from the Immortals themselves — it was merely an automated Spirit Realm status message configured by the two Immortals: "Read."

After a long time, neither Immortal had given any reply. Fa Liuyuan had no choice but to brace himself and send messages to other Immortals he had added as contacts.

Yet, message after message he sent out was like retail capital dumped into a vast stock market, never stirring the slightest ripple, without a single word in reply.

Fa Liuyuan explained the situation, and the Immortal Ascension Hall instantly fell dead silent.

Bu Yuangui sighed inwardly, "Silence is the ultimate contempt. The Immortal Class doesn't deign to negotiate with us at all; they only want to accept our surrender."

At that moment, Bu Yuangui looked up, as if he could see the Immortals up in the Immortal Realm casting down their lowered gazes, directing a silent mockery at them.

Fa Liuyuan looked at Zhang Yu, wondering what this Crown Prince of Myriad Laws would do next.

*"I'm afraid... he can only yield a portion of his power first to stabilize the situation within the sect before doing anything else."*

Just as Fa Liuyuan was thinking this, Zhang Yu spoke, "Heavenly Monarch Fa, please tell them directly that if they do not reply, I will speak with them in my own way."

At the same time, the Desolate Ox Great Sage said to Zhang Yu, "Alright, I have already opened the Spirit Realm communication channels from the 21st to the 29th layers for you. You can directly project your voice using the Great Sage's Intent."

Originally, Great Sage's Heir allowed Zhang Yu's consciousness — or a clone — to communicate across an entire layer of the Spirit Realm after traveling there. But with the aid of the Desolate Ox Great Sage, Zhang Yu could now project his voice to any layer of Kunxu covered by the Spiritual Realm without needing to send down his consciousness or deploy a clone.

Meanwhile, on the other side, Fa Liuyuan looked at the Immortals who still offered no response, shook his head helplessly, and said, "Your Highness, I think it might be better if I make a trip to the Immortal Realm myself and pay them a visit as a representative of the sect."

Zhang Yu shook his head. "No need."

The next moment, with the assistance of the Desolate Ox Great Sage, Zhang Yu abruptly unleashed the power of the Great Sage's Heir.

This time, every cultivator across the entire 21st to 29th layers heard Zhang Yu's voice at the exact same moment.

"I am Zhang Yu, Crown Prince of the Myriad Laws Sect."

"Within one minute, I want to speak with all the Immortals of the Myriad Laws Sect."

"If I do not receive your response within one minute, I will sever you and all companies and platforms under your purview from every communication function built upon the Spirit Realm."

"Functions like the Dharma Realm, which are built upon the Spirit Realm, will also be completely paralyzed for you."

"To you, the entirety of Kunxu will once again be shrouded in primal darkness."

Immediately after, a countdown from 60 to 0 began to appear.

At this moment, Fa Liuyuan, Dugu Ming, and Bu Yuangui were still entirely oblivious to all this. They merely looked at Zhang Yu in confusion, unsure of what he was doing, and even less aware of the massive turmoil his actions had sparked throughout the 21st to 29th layers.
