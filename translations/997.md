# Chapter 997: A Book That Cannot Be Rolled Back?

Inside the Immortal Grotto-Heaven, Bai Gusheng looked at Zhang Yu before him and said, "Zhang Yu, I have already shown my sincerity. Information regarding the Immortal Ancestor isn't something just anyone can casually know..."

Seeing that Zhang Yu seemed to know little about the Immortal Ancestor, Bai Gusheng seized the opportunity... continuously inflating the importance of the intelligence and information he possessed.

"I have wielded the Ascension Book, one of the Four Books, for many years, and have even overseen the collection of numerous Books. The secrets and intelligence I hold are far beyond what you can imagine."

"If you want to exchange more information, we can sit down and talk slowly."

As he spoke, a hint of arrogance surfaced on his face: "I believe this will be far more helpful to you than a mere new Book."

After finishing these words, he turned his head to look at Source Saint beside them: "As for irrelevant people, they can be asked to leave first."

Source Saint sneered and said, "How could intelligence open-sourcing be missing me?"

"And I'm an irrelevant person? Bai Gusheng, as fellow Book-Wielders, you just got lucky by getting the Ascension Book. When it comes to intelligence capabilities, you might not match me. Is your White Bone Cult as open-source as our Immortal Armaments Bureau?"

Bai Gusheng snorted coldly. "It's precisely because you open-source everything recklessly that your Immortal Armaments Bureau can't keep any secrets. Even if you have intelligence, it's just Extreme Dragon Intelligence — completely worthless."

Right at that moment, Zhang Yu spoke up: "Whether it has value or not is something we will only know after we exchange information."

"If the two of you are unwilling to discuss together, then we can communicate separately."

As he spoke, Zhang Yu shifted his thoughts. Magic power circulated within his body, instantly teleporting the two of them to different locations within the Immortal Grotto-Heaven.

Bai Gusheng did not stop him, merely thinking to himself, *"This brat Zhang Yu... though merely in his thirties, he did climb all the way up from the 1st layer of Kunxu after all. Sure enough, he isn't so easily fooled."*

*"Wanting to exchange intelligence with the two of us separately — he's trying to seize the initiative, subtly warning us not to fabricate information during the exchange, reminding us... that he can verify the intelligence with the other person?"*

At this very moment, Zhang Yu sent out two clones into different independent spaces within the Immortal Grotto-Heaven, communicating separately and simultaneously with Bai Gusheng and the Source Saint.

Looking at the Source Saint before him, Zhang Yu said, "Heavenly Monarch Bai Gusheng has already shown his sincerity. If Heavenly Monarch Yuan wishes to exchange intelligence, shouldn't you show some as well?"

The Source Saint thought to himself, *"Hah, this kid... With a mere Tribulation Transcendence level of cultivation, if it weren't for holding the Feather Book, when would it ever be your turn to play this game with me?"*

Though slightly displeased in his heart, the Source Saint was still filled with curiosity about the new book Zhang Yu had obtained. With a calm expression, he said, "Since Bai Gusheng spoke of the Immortal Ancestor, then I shall continue discussing the Immortal Ancestor as well."

"You asked just now... why no historical records regarding the Immortal Ancestor can be found today. I can give you an accurate answer to that: it is because the Ten Immortal Emperors once utilized a Supreme Immortal Artifact to erase all traces of his existence."

"As such, you naturally cannot find any historical records about him. Even memories concerning the Immortal Ancestor... I fear that even if there are upper-tier cultivators who were alive back then, they no longer remember him today."

Zhang Yu's heart stirred. *"Erasing traces of existence with a Supreme Immortal Artifact?"*

*"That sounds somewhat like how the Ten Immortal Emperors dealt with the so-called 11th Immortal Emperor."*

*"However, who knows how long ago this Immortal Ancestor lived compared to the present era.... Was he wiped out long ago and then had his traces erased by the Ten Immortal Emperors later on? Or was he wiped out by the Ten Immortal Emperors in the new era and then had his traces erased...? It's really hard to say."*

At the same time, another question arose in Zhang Yu's mind. "Since a Supreme Immortal Artifact was used to erase all traces, why do you and Bai Gusheng still know information about the Immortal Ancestor?"

Source Saint did not hide anything, because he knew that if Zhang Yu thought about it a little more, he would be able to guess the answer as well.

Source Saint pointed toward the sky — or rather, toward the higher layers of Kunxu.

"The first reason, naturally, is because of the Ten Immortal Emperors."

"Even if the Supreme Immortal Artifact erased all sorts of traces, the Immortal Emperors would inevitably still remember. If they occasionally let something slip, it would naturally spread down to their subordinates over time."

"The second reason, which is also the biggest reason, is... the Books."

With a face full of gratitude, Source Saint said, "The Immortal Emperors took pity on all living beings. Although they wiped away the Immortal Ancestor's traces, they did not burn the Books of the world."

"And it is precisely through the Books' abilities, the messages written within it, and all sorts of information related to it that more and more... intelligence regarding the Immortal Ancestor has gradually been pieced together."

*"Was it truly out of pity for all living beings?"* Zhang Yu thought to himself. *"Was it that they didn't want to erase it? Or that they couldn't?"*

At this moment, Zhang Yu recalled the message from the future transmitted through the *Technique-Chaining Book*.

*"Even back then with Senior Supreme Truth, the Ten Immortal Emperors merely suppressed her with overwhelming force; they didn't directly erase all traces of her existence."*

*"For the Immortal Ancestor to drive the Immortal Emperors to such ruthless measures..."*

Zhang Yu sighed inwardly, "It shows just how deeply the Immortal Emperors feared this Immortal Ancestor. Would they really allow the Books to circulate so freely?"

Immortal Slayer, however, thought of another possibility, "What if... it was a rollback? That all traces of the Immortal Ancestor were erased because of a rollback."

The Sect Examination Clone said, "Then why do the Books still exist? You're not trying to say that even after a rollback, it couldn't erase the Books' existence, are you?"

Zhang Yu thought, "Since the Books can carry rollback information, then perhaps... they really cannot be erased by a rollback?"

Zhang Yu wasn't certain, but he could only consider it a distinct possibility.

"If that's true, then... the hope of defeating the Ten Immortal Emperors suddenly becomes much greater."

The Sect Examination Clone poured cold water on the idea, saying, "If it really is due to a rollback, how many years would it take to roll back and erase every trace of the Immortal Ancestor's existence? Hundreds of years? Thousands of years? Tens of thousands of years? If the Ten Immortal Emperors truly possess that kind of power, then our future path to ascending the Supreme Seat will be far too difficult."

Zhang Yu nodded slightly. "What the actual situation is... still requires more intelligence."

Recalling Source Saint's explanation just now, Zhang Yu's expectations for the Book grew even higher.

He thought to himself, "Since the Books can record information and contain many messages from the Immortal Ancestor, it must hold a great deal of intelligence about the Immortal Ancestor himself, and perhaps... it might not lack intelligence regarding the Ten Immortal Emperors and the world rollback either."

"Especially... since the Technique-Chaining Comprehensive Manual can convey messages from the future, what about the other Books?"

*"Could it be that the future me... also used other Books to send messages?"*

*"Only by mastering more Books can I obtain more intelligence."*

At this moment, Zhang Yu's resolve to seize all the world's Books became even firmer.

Meanwhile, the Source Saint spoke, "Now it's my turn to ask, isn't it?"

"What is the ability of the new book you obtained?"

Zhang Yu did not answer directly, but instead pondered internally.

If it were back in high school, university, or when he had first entered the sect, he probably would not have revealed even the slightest hint about the new book's abilities.

Because he was certain... back then, he had lacked the strength to protect his own book, so he would not have considered making it public at all.

Yet right now, Zhang Yu's background and strength were far greater than in the past. Rather than instantly discarding the possibility of going public, after learning the ability of the Technique-Chaining Comprehensive Manual, he began weighing the impact and consequences of doing so.

*"This is an ability that brings far greater benefits once others know of it, but also carries higher risks."*

*"And once I begin using it on a large scale, it will be absolutely impossible to keep it a secret."*

Zhang Yu thought to himself, *"So, am I qualified now... to openly use this ability?"*

---

"Zhang Yu, the new book you obtained this time is able to create Chain Formation Diagrams, isn't it?"

Looking at Zhang Yu before him, Bai Gusheng calmly voiced his guess.

Zhang Yu appeared expressionless, but he sighed inwardly, *"Sure enough, someone was able to guess part of it through the process of the New Book-Master Selection."*

In the deepest recesses of the Immortal Grotto-Heaven, Zhang Yu's true body had already arrived before the Technique-Chaining Comprehensive Manual.

*"The Chain Formation Diagrams created by the Technique-Chaining Comprehensive Manual will no longer be Chain Formation Diagrams used by me alone; instead, they will become an objectively existing phenomenon in this world."*

*"There's no need to possess the Book — as long as one masters the relevant cultivation art, they will be able to receive the blessing of the Chain Formation Diagram."*

At this moment, all kinds of scenes flashed through Zhang Yu's mind... of what it would be like once such Chain Formation Diagrams were created.

It wouldn't be just him alone who could use them. Bai Zhenzhen, Yu Xinghan, Teacher Gao, Magnetic Pole... countless people could benefit alongside him.

If it were spread even further, everyone in Kunxu could use them.

In that case, the strength and direction of these Chain Formation Diagrams... would be enough to affect countless corporations and industries, influence the entire Ten Great Sects, and even impact the conflict between Dao Lineages.

*"A single Chain Formation Diagram that increases lifespan would be enough to bankrupt countless Longevity Extension corporations."*

*"A Chain Formation Diagram specifically designed to strengthen monsterfolk would be enough to gradually shatter the balance among the cultivators of the Ten Great Sects, perhaps even triggering a race war between humans and monsters."*

*"A Chain Formation Diagram that increases the efficiency of Tribulation Transcendence would also completely revolutionize the Thunder Tribulation Financial Market."*

Zhang Yu thought to himself, *"Bai Gusheng... at most probably only guessed that I could create Chain Formation Diagrams, but never imagined it would be this kind of genuine Chain Formation Diagram."*

Sensing the usage rules within the Technique-Chaining Comprehensive Manual, Zhang Yu thought, *"Although I can only create one Chain Formation Diagram every ten years, every single one... is enough to cause an earth-shattering commotion."*

*"Once the capabilities of the Technique-Chaining Comprehensive Manual are made public, the benefits will be immense, but the risks will be just as monumental. Countless people... will surely want to seize this authority to dictate the creation of Chain Formation Diagrams."*

Immortal Slayer said, "Yet this is precisely the Chain Formation Diagram the Immortal Ancestor intended to create back then — one that all cultivators under heaven could use freely, one that could empower countless cultivators!"

The Sect Examination Clone asked, "Is there no way to control it? Can we... set a scope of use for the created Chain Formation Diagrams?"

"Just like the High School Sacred Body or the Golden Core Sacred Body, only we ourselves can use them, or perhaps those whose Technique Apertures have been opened by us and infused with the Chain Formation Diagram."

Zhang Yu's heart stirred. "That's right. The High School Sacred Body, the Golden Core Sacred Body... Why can't these Chain Formation Diagrams be used by everyone?"

"From the Immortal Ancestor's perspective, isn't it strange for a Chain Formation Diagram to not be usable by all? That completely goes against his original intention in creating it."

Upon hearing what the Sect Examination Clone said, an idea suddenly struck Zhang Yu's mind like a bolt of lightning. Zhang Yu manipulated the Technique-Chaining Comprehensive Manual before him, filling in the cultivation arts of the High School Sacred Body one by one. The next moment, a notification appeared before his eyes.

*"This Chain Formation Diagram has already been created! Duplicate creation is not permitted!"*

Seeing Zhang Yu sink into silence in response to his question, Bai Gusheng merely thought he had guessed correctly.

With a faint, confident smile, Bai Gusheng said, "Actually, the answer isn't that hard to guess."

"You can tell from the process of the New-Book Master Selection that, aside from the pre-existing Chain Formation Diagrams, the other randomly combined cultivation arts... also showed signs of being enhanced during the simulations."

"This wasn't an isolated phenomenon either. I reviewed the simulation records of all the participants at the time, and the performance of the cultivation arts in every simulation process showed signs of enhancement."

"What does this show? It shows that any combination of cultivation arts gained a Chain Formation Diagram. It shows that in every single run of this simulator, if there wasn't a Chain Formation Diagram to begin with, one would be created."

"And this... is precisely the ability of the new Book you obtained this time: creating Chain Formation Diagrams."

Looking at Zhang Yu, who remained silent, Bai Gusheng felt that he had hit the nail completely on the head, and continued, "Zhang Yu, creating Chain Formation Diagrams isn't really that big of a deal, so there's no need for you to keep it hidden and bottle it up."

"I've collected all kinds of intelligence on Books for so many years — what kind of ability haven't I heard of? Books that steal Dao Seeds, Books that steal Chain Formation Diagrams, even Books that steal innate talents, and Books that alter Chain Formation Diagrams... they all exist in this world."

"Creating a Chain Formation Diagram may sound formidable, but if you think carefully about it, you should realize that, in the end, it is merely a type of Immortal Dao Technology. Its utility is by no means irreplaceable..."

Zhang Yu said indifferently, "Putting aside whether your hypothesis is true or false, if it really were as unimportant as you claim, why would you chase after me and probe like this?"

Bai Gusheng stared at Zhang Yu without yielding in the slightest. Their gazes clashed in the air, both seemingly sensing the other's unshakable will.

Recalling Zhang Yu's various fearless deeds, Bai Gusheng said, "Since you've put it that way, I might as well lay everything out clearly today."

"Different Books possess different abilities and are suited to entirely different technological paths of the Immortal Dao. This new Book is useful to me, but it might not be of much use to you."

"To make it even clearer: the so-called 'When the Four Books gather, the Immortal Ancestor shall appear'... have you ever thought about how they are to be gathered? Once gathered, who will temporarily take charge of them? And after the Immortal Ancestor appears, who will continue to hold the Four Books?"

Bai Gusheng said word by word, "As far as I know, this candidate will be chosen from among the Book-Wielders of the Ten Great Sects."

Without the slightest hesitation, he declared, "And I... intend to be that person."

The only reason Bai Gusheng was willing to say all this in front of Zhang Yu was that, in his eyes, Zhang Yu lacked the strength to compete at all and posed no threat as a rival.

Thus, he continued, "Zhang Yu, you should know your own limits. Gathering the Four Books... for a momentous event like this, if they are choosing from among the ten Book-Wielders... it will never be your turn. Rather than being eliminated empty-handed with nothing to show for it, it would be far better to join forces with me."

"I can provide you with intelligence, and we can even trade Books to acquire ones whose abilities suit each of us better."

After saying this, Bai Gusheng thought to himself, *"For instance, the new Book you obtained this time seems far more suitable for me."*

Zhang Yu asked again, "Ten Book-Wielders... how will they compete? How will the final candidate be decided?"

Bai Gusheng coaxed patiently, "I don't know the exact format of the competition yet either, but personal strength and the number of Books one holds should both be fairly important criteria."

In Bai Gusheng's eyes, Zhang Yu lacked sufficient personal strength, which meant his efficiency in collecting old Books within the sect was lacking as well. In the future, the number of Books in his possession... was bound to be the lowest among the ten Book-Wielders.

Zhang Yu, however, sighed inwardly, *"The rollback initiated by the Ten Immortal Emperors this time is very likely related to the revival of the Immortal Ancestor over a year from now. And as for me... whether it's to gather more intelligence, or to be more deeply involved in and influence the event where 'the Four Books gather, the Immortal Ancestor appears,' I need to collect even more Books."*

Seeing Zhang Yu fall silent once again, Bai Gusheng assumed that the young man was hesitating and might be on the verge of being persuaded, as the actual situation was just as he had described.

Bai Gusheng continued, "Zhang Yu, you should understand that even if you can create Chain Formation Diagrams, it won't bring about any qualitative leap in your personal strength."

"And I suspect that this kind of creation is definitely not without limitations, right?"

"What kind of Chain Formation Diagrams can be created, how much time it takes to create one... There must be all kinds of strict restrictions involved."

Already created... Already created... Already created...

In front of the *Technique-Chaining Comprehensive Manual*, Zhang Yu tested one Chain Formation Diagram he possessed after another, only to find that all of them had already been created and could not be created again.

Looking at the results before him, Zhang Yu felt a shock in his heart: "Already created? Could it be that all the Chain Formation Diagrams we possess were created by the *Technique-Chaining Comprehensive Manual*?"

"If they were all created by the *Technique-Chaining Comprehensive Manual*, why can't these Chain Formation Diagrams... be used by the public?"

Immortal Slayer guessed, "Could it be... these Chain Formation Diagrams were just used for testing? They're merely experimental products?"

The Sect Examination Clone said, "Or perhaps everyone used to be able to use them, but later, due to the Immortal Ancestor's disappearance? Or the Immortal Emperor's suppression? Only a select few can use them now?"

Zhang Yu could not be certain which guess was correct, but he felt that the Technique-Chaining Comprehensive Manual before him had gained an added veil of mystery.

"Long before today, someone used the Technique-Chaining Comprehensive Manual to create a vast number of Chain Formation Diagrams. Was it the Immortal Ancestor? Or... someone else?"

Shaking his head, Zhang Yu suppressed the stray thoughts in his mind and resumed pondering the matter of creating Chain Formation Diagrams.

"I can either create a rare combination of cultivation arts so that the Chain Formation Diagram is used only within a small circle, or even exclusively by me. Or... I can create Chain Formation Diagrams suitable for more people and teach them to a broader audience... In this process, the more industries and sects it involves, the greater the influence I will gain, but I will also draw the covetous eyes of more people."

For a moment, Zhang Yu did not know which path to choose. He thought to himself, "I still need to think it over carefully and plan this thoroughly."

For now, he first summoned the Feather Book to merge with the Technique-Chaining Comprehensive Manual before him.

Having long been impatient, the Feather Book materialized with a resounding hum, swallowing the new book into itself and turning it into a new page.
