# Chapter 1045: Does the Crown Prince Still Need You to Teach Him?

Inside the office of the Edict Pavilion.

Bu Yingshu, currently the Pavilion Master of the Edict Pavilion, stared at the cultivation art that had just been delivered to her, inexplicably shocked to her core.

*"This is a Heaven-Opening cultivation art?"*

*"Immortal Venerable Profound Wisdom's Great Dao Comprehensive Manual: Revised Edition? Zhang Yu just gave it to me like this? Just letting me cultivate it?"*

Although Bu Yingshu had already been fed numerous Immortal Sect Techniques under Zhang Yu's arrangements during this recent period — and even quite a few Heaven-Opening-tier black arts were among them — there were still vast differences between one cultivation art and another, even among those of the Heaven-Opening tier.

Compared to the black arts in the Edict Pavilion's database that hadn't been updated in ages and lacked proper testing, this Great Dao Comprehensive Manual: Revised Edition before her eyes, recently revised by Immortal Venerable Profound Wisdom herself, was truly of the pinnacle caliber among Heaven-Opening cultivation arts.

Furthermore, having acquired it through legitimate channels with proper authorization meant she could receive the blessing of Immortal Venerable Profound Wisdom's Dao Lineage during cultivation, yielding twice the result with half the effort.

*"Over this period, relying on the various cultivation arts Zhang Yu has continuously opened up to me, I can sense that my peerless talent is showing signs of yet another dramatic surge."*

*"Now, with this Great Dao Comprehensive Manual: Revised Edition added on top, how strong will I become? I'm afraid even I can hardly predict it."*

Thinking of this, Bu Yingshu burst into hearty laughter. *"Dad was right — managing upwards really works."*

*"I merely made a slight use of my wisdom, chatting with Zhang Yu every day, and got him to invest a massive amount of resources into me, even buying this Heaven-Opening cultivation art, the Great Dao Comprehensive Manual: Revised Edition, for me."*

*"No..."* Bu Yingshu's heart stirred, and she thought, *"Actually, what truly played the decisive role was my innate talent."*

*"Perhaps besides my world-shaking talents in cultivation, I'm actually also a genius at managing upwards — that's why I could achieve such massive results so quickly."*

*"It's just that over the past years, my status was simply too high. I never encountered an appropriate target to practice on, giving me no chance to display this gift of mine."*

Thinking up to this point, a deep sense of regret surged within Bu Yingshu's heart. *"A pearl covered in dust! For such an important talent, how could I only display it at my age? If I had discovered this years earlier, I might have already achieved immortality by now."*

*"Just how many opportunities did I miss in the past?"*

*"It's all because of my useless teachers, my useless father! None of them could see my potential, holding me back for so long."*

Just as Bu Yingshu was sighing and lamenting to herself, shocking news regarding the Ascension Selection began to shake the Spirit Realm, and it was quickly caught by Bu Yingshu.

*"What? The Ascension Selection is starting so soon?"*

*"Huh?"* Bu Yingshu's eyes widened abruptly as she looked at one of the news items: *"The representatives of the Ten Great Sects participating in the Ascension Selection have been decided, but the representative for the Myriad Laws Sect is not the Crown Prince of Myriad Laws, Zhang Yu?"*

*"Zhang Yu isn't participating in the Ascension Selection? Could... could it be that he gave it up to me?"*

Bu Yingshu couldn't help but cover her mouth and giggle, *"No way, I'm not ready yet!"*

While thoughts raced through her mind like lightning, Bu Yingshu had already browsed more information across the Spirit Realm and saw the name of the Myriad Laws Sect's representative.

The smile on Bu Yingshu's face vanished instantly. *"Su Xianchen? Who does he think he is? Does he even deserve to represent the Myriad Laws Sect in the Ascension Selection?"*

Countless pieces of information regarding Su Xianchen flashed through Bu Yingshu's mind.

Bu Yingshu knew that as an Ascension Realm cultivator, Su Xianchen had always been an expert in Immortal Qi Technology and had even participated in the research and development of the third-generation Law Edicts, making him a relatively well-known Ascension Seed for a long time.

Back when the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor was still at the Myriad Laws Sect, Immortal Venerable Profound Wisdom had been his staunch supporter, which in turn had led Su Xianchen to align with the Immortal Emperor's faction as well.

However, after the Battle of the End began, Su Xianchen was no longer part of the Immortal Emperor's faction, but had instead aligned purely with the Immortal Qi Dao Lineage.

Meanwhile, Bu Yingshu's consciousness swept across the Spirit Realm to Su Xianchen's social platform account, only to find that his comment section had been completely overrun by doubts and criticisms from all across the Myriad Laws Sect.

Bu Yingshu immediately joined in, hurling curses alongside them.

While the Spirit Realm was in an uproar, Zhang Yu's consciousness had also crossed the Spirit Realm, arriving before Immortal Roujia of the Reformist Faction.

Following the recommendations of the Zhang Yu Simulator, Immortal Roujia had long since transformed into a kindly old woman. Seeming to have anticipated Zhang Yu's arrival, she said with a helpless look, "Your Highness Crown Prince, I know what you want to ask, but my hands are tied."

"You know that our Reformist Faction has always supported you. When it came to choosing the representative for the Ascension Selection this time, we proposed you right from the start."

"But the other sects wouldn't agree."

Immortal Roujia sighed and said helplessly, "No matter how strongly we argued our case or explained the situation to them, they simply refused to agree to let you represent the Myriad Laws Sect in the Ascension Selection."

"Crown Prince, you also know that the reason the Ascension Selection can even be held is due to the existence of the Ascension Dao Lineage. And the Ascension Dao Lineage is jointly held by the Dao Masters of the various Great Sects. Only if all Ten Great Sects give their consent can a cultivator participate in the Ascension Selection."

Zhang Yu asked impassively, "Then what is their reason for denying my participation in the Ascension Selection?"

Immortal Roujia explained, "Crown Prince, you already wield the Spirit Realm Dao Lineage and are now the Heavenly Court God-Seizing King, holding immense power in the Heavenly Court. Naturally, the Immortals of the other sects are wary of you. They are afraid that our Myriad Laws Sect will become too powerful, which is why they are unwilling to let you ascend and become an Immortal."

After finishing her explanation, seeing no reaction from Zhang Yu, Immortal Roujia consoled him further, "Crown Prince, you are still young and have only recently broken through to the Ascension Realm. There will be plenty of time ahead, so why rush now?"

"The Immortals of the Ten Great Sects have already discussed and agreed that in order to deal with the Ultimate Doomsday, the Ascension Selection will be held more frequently and in greater numbers going forward, allowing more Immortals to emerge in Kunxu. Even if it is not your turn this time, you can always wait for the next."

Just as Zhang Yu was conversing with Immortal Roujia, the Innate Immortal, who wielded the Innate Dao Lineage, also contacted him.

"Crown Prince."

Taking the form of an old man, the Innate Immortal cast a projection directly and said to Zhang Yu, "Do you know that your path to achieve immortality has already been completely blocked off by someone?"

Frowning slightly, Zhang Yu replied, "Immortal, you might as well speak plainly."

The Innate Immortal went straight to the point: "Have you considered that even if you don't take part in the Ascension Selection, you still hold great power and possess the four Books, so at worst, you could forge a new path to ascend and become an Immortal? But I must tell you that the moment you do so, I fear you will perish in an instant, because what the Immortals fear is not just the authority in your hands, but your Dao Lineage. If the so-called Law Edict Expiration Date is elevated into a Dao Lineage, won't it become a Dao Lineage Expiration Date? If that is the case, do you think any Immortal would be willing to have someone in this world who can govern their Dao Lineage?"

The Innate Immortal shook his head. "No Immortal wants another Immortal standing above them. Therefore, the moment you attempt to achieve immortality, every single Immortal will come to stop you."

"Therefore, if you want to achieve immortality, there is only one way."

Looking at the confident Innate Immortal before him, Zhang Yu asked, "What way?"

The Innate Immortal said, "That is to change your Dao Lineage — switch to one that poses far less of a threat. Then, naturally, there won't be so many people blocking your path to immortality."

"In fact, if you switch to a Dao Lineage that benefits others, there will naturally be Immortals willing to help you."

"For instance... the Dao Lineage of High-Intelligence Artifact Spirits. If you are willing to establish this Dao Lineage, I can assist you with all my might to achieve immortality."

As he spoke, the Innate Immortal's divine thoughts surged, and countless pieces of information emerged, seemingly unfolding a magnificent vision before Zhang Yu.

"By establishing the Dao Lineage of High-Intelligence Artifact Spirits, combined with the authority you currently wield, you can mass-produce High-Intelligence Artifact Spirits at the lowest cost and restore the Myriad Laws Sect's rightful Immortal Dao productivity at the fastest speed."

"Complemented by my Innate Dao Lineage, all High-Intelligence Artifact Spirits will harbor no rebellious thoughts whatsoever. You can use them to replace all cultivators, vastly increasing the sect's Immortal Dao productivity while dramatically reducing administrative costs."

"High-Intelligence Artifact Spirits are the trend of the era. Once the Myriad Laws Sect takes this step, other sects will inevitably have to follow suit so as not to fall behind. In the future, the vast majority of cultivators in Kunxu will be High-Intelligence Artifact Spirits — and they will all be under your control."

On the other side, Immortal Venerable Heavenly Fortune's attendant had also brought news to Zhang Yu.

Glancing at the message, Zhang Yu instantly understood the other party's intentions.

If Zhang Yu were to sum it up, Immortal Venerable Heavenly Fortune's message was essentially: The Heavenly Court authority is far too heavy a burden for you to bear; trying to carry it by force will only sever your own immortal path. Why not trade the Heavenly Court authority to me? I can help you achieve immortality.

"He wants us to hand over the Heavenly Court authority in exchange for his support in achieving immortality?" The Sect Examination Clone snorted coldly and said, "I'm afraid the moment we hand it over, the first one to move against us will be Immortal Venerable Heavenly Fortune himself."

Meanwhile, another immortal from the Reformist Faction, Immortal Yi Xuan, had likewise sought out Zhang Yu.

The immortal's consciousness crossed the Spirit Realm and brushed against Zhang Yu's, exchanging a vast amount of information in an instant.

Immortal Yi Xuan sighed, saying, "Your Highness, you already hold the titles of Crown Prince of Myriad Laws and Heavenly Court God-Seizing King. How different is your current authority from that of an Immortal Venerable? You already possess enough prestige and influence. If you forcefully try to climb even higher, going too far may be just as bad as not going far enough. Shattering the existing balance between the sects will only trigger turmoil.

"The Ten Great Sects value balance. Holding the vast power of the God-Seizing King, you have already slightly disrupted the equilibrium among the sects. If you achieve immortality on top of that, our Myriad Laws Sect will dominate entirely, which will inevitably trigger a situation just like the Battle of the End."

The "same situation" Immortal Yi Xuan spoke of naturally referred to the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor being besieged by the other Immortal Emperors after making a breakthrough.

And if Zhang Yu achieved immortality, he would inevitably be besieged just like the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor.

Immortal Yi Xuan thought to himself, *"Sigh, after becoming the Myriad Laws Divine Sword, you wanted to become the Crown Prince of Myriad Laws; after becoming Crown Prince, you wanted to take charge of the Heavenly Court; having taken charge of the Heavenly Court, you now want to achieve immortality. Once you achieve immortality, you'll definitely want to become an Immortal Venerable, an Immortal Emperor, and in the end, you'll probably aim straight for the Immortal Emperor of the End."*

Looking at Zhang Yu before him, Immortal Yi Xuan felt as if he were seeing another Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor.

*"Just how high must one cultivate the Immortal Dao for it to be enough? It's precisely because there are too many people like you in Kunxu that the Battle of the End broke out."*

Thinking of this, Immortal Yi Xuan redoubled his efforts to dissuade Zhang Yu, laying out the attitudes, strengths, assets, and Dao Lineages of the Immortals from the other Great Sects, explaining the tremendous risks entailed if Zhang Yu were to achieve immortality.

Moreover, dissuading Zhang Yu was not just Immortal Yi Xuan's intention, but also that of many Immortals within the Myriad Laws Sect. Zhang Yu had guessed this as well.

Zhang Yu said, "Most of the Immortals in the Myriad Laws Sect don't want me to achieve immortality, do they?"

Immortal Yi Xuan: "How could that be possible? We are all from the same sect. We'd be more than thrilled to see you achieve immortality. It's just that right now is truly not the best time. Everything must be done for the greater good."

Naturally, Zhang Yu didn't believe a word of Immortal Yi Xuan's nonsense. He thought to himself, *"Through their transactions with me, the Immortals of the Myriad Laws Sect have gained a greater voice in the Heavenly Court, giving them an advantage in many matters."*

*"If I achieve immortality and face a joint siege from the other Nine Great Sects, they will lose this advantage. So compared to the other sects... most of the Myriad Laws Immortals probably want me not to achieve immortality even more than the Immortals of the other sects do."*

*"Unless... like the Innate Immortal said, I choose another Dao Lineage to achieve immortality. Then some of the Immortals might agree."*

After confirming the attitude of the Immortals from the Myriad Laws Sect, Zhang Yu also began gathering more information regarding the other sects.

Within the Supreme Clarity Realm, Zhang Pianpian said, "Little brother, I looked into the situation with the other sects."

She sighed, shaking her head, and said, "None of the Immortals from any of the sects intend to hide anything. On the matter of 'not letting you achieve immortality', they have already reached a complete consensus."

"They even secretly leaked word to the Great Gods that as long as you achieve immortality... the Immortals of all the sects will unite to attack you."

"They will never permit the birth of a Dao Lineage that governs other Dao Lineages."

Zhang Yu's eyes flickered as the video of Su Xianchen's interview played before his eyes.

In the footage, Su Xianchen was glowing with excitement, recounting the nurturing of the Immortals and the guidance of the Immortal Venerable, announcing his specific plans for participating in the selection.

When someone asked for his thoughts on the Crown Prince of Myriad Laws, Su Xianchen said humbly, "I have always held the utmost admiration for His Highness the Crown Prince. Please rest assured, Your Highness: this old servant will not fail to live up to expectations. I will seize victory in the selection, achieve immortality and establish the Dao, and contribute to the Immortal Dao construction of the Myriad Laws Sect."

Zhang Pianpian asked, "What do you plan to do?"

Zhang Yu asked, "Sis, what do you think?"

Zhang Pianpian said, "We need to take the long view, Crown Prince. Now is not the time for a direct confrontation with the Ten Sects Immortals. Even if you achieve immortality smoothly, you cannot possibly contend against the entire Immortal Class by yourself."

Not long after the news of the Ascension Selection shook Kunxu, another emergency meeting was convened.

Immortal Venerable Profound Wisdom relayed the latest intelligence regarding the Battlefield of the End to all the Immortals of the Ten Sects, as well as the present Heavenly Court Great Gods and the Crown Prince of Myriad Laws.

"Twelve minutes and five seconds ago, after someone died, they did not transform into a Soul Cultivator and enter the Spirit Realm. Instead, they went straight into the Netherworld and entered reincarnation."

"Since then, this phenomenon has continued to intensify. The proportion of people who go straight into reincarnation upon death has now reached as high as seventy-eight percent."

"If this trend continues, we estimate that before long, the vast majority of people will go straight into reincarnation upon their physical death."

Immortal Venerable Profound Wisdom said in a heavy tone, "According to our observations of the Outer Heaven Battlefield, a drastic upheaval has occurred within the Reincarnation Dao Lineage."

Hearing this, Zhang Yu's heart sank, thinking back to the Reincarnation Immortal Emperor's earlier interference with the Heavenly Court. He mused inwardly, *"Does it have something to do with the ceremony between the Divine Emperor and the Divine Children?"*

Immortal Venerable Profound Wisdom continued, "From now on, when most people die... they will simply be dead."

The representative from the Heavenly Demon Sect said, "Doesn't that mean the number of Soul Cultivators will decrease drastically?"

The representative from the Netherworld Palace chuckled, "Congratulations, Crown Prince of Myriad Laws. You wagered on Soul Cultivators, and among the Ten Great Sects, your Myriad Laws Sect now possesses the greatest number of them. You've really struck it rich this time."

The representative from the White Bone Cult said, "People dying and just staying dead — that's a good thing! Everyone should pay more attention to medical matters now. Is there anyone who wants to cooperate with us?"

Zhang Yu thought to himself, *"As expected, even after learning about something like this, all these guys can think about is maximizing their own interests."*

How could we ever rely on them to confront the Ultimate Doomsday?"
