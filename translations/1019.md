# Chapter 1019: Crown Prince of Myriad Laws, Demise of Civilization?

Hearing the title 'Crown Prince', Zhang Yu was utterly baffled at first, but after an explanation from Dugu Ming, he finally understood what had happened.

"Hall Master Dugu, please send me a copy of the information left by the Immortal Emperor as well."

Looking at the information Dugu Ming quickly sent over, Zhang Yu felt a shock in his heart: *"Crown Prince of Myriad Laws... Why?"*

Meanwhile, the Sect Examination Clone also exclaimed in surprise, "Has this Emperor Beast gone senile in his old age? No, is he confident enough to keep letting us grow unchecked? That's not right either. This guy is cunning and calculating; he'd never do this merely out of confidence. Doing this must benefit him in some way."

Zhang Yu deeply agreed with the Sect Examination Clone's words. In his view... the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor was a creature of pure self-interest; every single thing he did and every word he spoke was aimed at propelling himself toward the apex of the Immortal Dao.

The other party would never make such an arrangement based on mere emotion or personal likes and dislikes toward him. Zhang Yu shook his head. For a moment, countless thoughts raced through his mind, yet he still could not understand why the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor had done this. "There is too little intelligence, and now is not the time to be thinking about this."

He quickly suppressed these stray thoughts as well, secretly thinking, "With such a massive commotion caused by the ten Immortal Emperors this time, the high-ranking cultivators of the Great Sects must have seen it all. I'm afraid it won't be long before a storm sweeps through the Great Sects."

Facing this storm, the original Zhang Yu might have needed to lie low temporarily and build up his own strength.

Yet now, having been granted authority by the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor, the things he could do had suddenly multiplied.

Just as Zhang Yu was pondering this, the Grotto-Heaven force surged within and around his body.

And accompanied by the explosive release of power from the Grotto-Heaven manifestation, the Evil God subordinates Zhang Yu had hidden within the Immortal Grotto-Heaven descended one after another, each slipping into the shadows on Zhang Yu's body.

Fu Ji hurriedly asked, "Zhang Yu! What on earth happened?"

After the Book-Wielder Assembly officially began, Fu Ji and the other Evil Gods had been hidden away inside the Immortal Grotto-Heaven by Zhang Yu.

At first, they could still watch the live broadcast through the Spirit Realm to keep up with the Book-Wielder Assembly's situation.

Later, as the ten Immortal Emperors descended and the Spirit Realm disconnected, Fu Ji, the Auspicious God, the Herald God, and the others lost contact with the outside world, naturally remaining entirely in the dark about what Zhang Yu had experienced.

Zhang Yu did not give a detailed answer to Fu Ji's question. He merely projected a strand of his thoughts and said simply, "Senior Ying Xintian and the other nine Immortal Emperors have joined forces and are currently besieging the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor..."

"Right now, I am the Crown Prince of the Myriad Laws Sect..."

Hearing the initial intelligence regarding the ten Immortal Emperors sent chills down Fu Ji's spine.

And when she heard that Zhang Yu had actually become the Crown Prince of the Myriad Laws Sect, she was instantly dumbfounded.

"Crown Prince of the Myriad Laws Sect? Full authority to handle all matters? The Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor is basically letting you act as regent over the sect!"

At this moment, Fu Ji felt every minute part of her body trembling violently, as if screaming in exultation.

She thought to herself, *"Bwahahaha! As expected, the greater the risk, the higher the profit! Going all-in back then truly was the most successful investment in the history of Kunxu! I... I'm rich!"*

*"Evil God my ass! From now on, I am the enlightener and angel investor of the Crown Prince of the Myriad Laws Sect! It's not out of the question for me to become one of the Eight Divisions Great Gods in the future!"*

Cultivation arts, medicinal pills, magical artifacts, funds... At this moment, countless sect assets flooded Fu Ji's mind, making her so excited that she had no idea what she should take first.

Fu Ji said with a look of pure bliss, "Zhang Yu! Let's download all the cultivation arts of the Myriad Laws Sect first, and then have people move all the Immortal Dao resources they can carry into the Immortal Grotto-Heaven..."

Before Auspicious God could even consider Fu Ji's ideas, he immediately chimed in, "Forget about that for now! The most important thing right now is naturally to seize control of all the formations, restrictions, and platforms as quickly as possible!"

Herald God grew excited at this moment as well, paying no heed to Fu Ji or Auspicious God's thoughts as he spoke up directly to remind him, "Too small, you're thinking too small. Those things will be ours sooner or later. Right now, we should seize this window of time to consolidate this power."

"With the primary forces of the ten Immortal Emperors gone, unrest will inevitably sweep through the Great Sects next. The Crown Prince Authority can deter them for a moment, but long-term rule requires military might to back it up!"

"Contact the Heavenly Court at once. The Heavenly Court is also under the jurisdiction of the Immortal Emperors. As the Crown Prince of the Myriad Laws Sect acting on behalf of the Immortal Emperor, you have every right to take command of all the law enforcement Righteous Gods within the sect! Only with the Eight Righteous Gods backing you will your Crown Prince Authority truly be secure."

Immortal Slayer also agreed, "Indeed, especially the Eight Divisions Great Gods. They cannot participate in the battle among the ten Immortal Emperors because all ten Immortal Emperors possess the highest command authority of the Heavenly Court; the Righteous Gods simply cannot attack any of them. However, the Eight Divisions Great Gods themselves possess power sufficient to rival the Immortal Class, so securing this command authority is crucial."

Zhang Yu nodded slightly, knowing that what the two said was true.

Although he had acquired the Crown Prince Authority, whether he could hold firmly onto it and continue to wield its power depended critically on his next steps.

The very first step was to seize military power and ensure his own safety.

"I am already contacting the Heavenly Court."

At the same time, besides contacting the Heavenly Court, Zhang Yu was also attending to another important matter: gathering intelligence on the current situation, especially regarding the 21st to 29th layers.

"Within the 21st to 29th layers, there is a large number of the Immortal Class. Even if I have become the Crown Prince of Myriad Laws, they won't necessarily obey my commands."

"I must know what their movements are now that the 20th layer has been relocated..."

Just as Zhang Yu's thoughts raced, Dugu Ming had already delivered the latest intelligence to him.

"Your Highness, with the disappearance of the 20th layer of Kunxu, communication between the 19th and 21st layers has been temporarily severed."

"However, the moment the incident occurred on the 20th layer, an Immortal immediately anchored the connection between the 19th and 21st layers and began emergency repairs. Communication was just restored."

"Based on current project estimates, transit between the layers could resume in as little as two days or at most seven. Complete repairs should take no more than half a month."

Hearing this, Zhang Yu felt a slight twinge of surprise. *"That fast?"*

At the same time, beams of divine light crashed down in front of Zhang Yu with a resounding boom.

The divine light converged, coalescing into humanoid projections that all dropped to their knees before him.

They were none other than the numerous third-class Righteous Gods responsible for law enforcement within the Myriad Laws Sect.

Upon descending, they declared in unison, "Greetings, Crown Prince of the Myriad Laws Sect."

Observing the reactions of these Righteous Gods, Zhang Yu felt a slight sense of relief. "It seems the authority of the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor still possesses sufficient binding force over the Righteous Gods."

With that, Zhang Yu proceeded to further confirm his own power. "Within the territory of the Myriad Laws Sect, aside from the Immortal Emperor, do I now hold the supreme command authority over the Righteous Gods?"

The 3rd rank Righteous God leading them, named Shen Jiazi, nodded slightly and bowed, saying, "Yes."

Zhang Yu asked again, "What about the Eight Divisions Great Gods? Why have they not responded to my summons and arrived?"

Shen Jiazi, leading the group, replied, "According to the sect law of the Myriad Laws Sect, without the approval of the Immortal Ascension Hall, the Eight Divisions Great Gods cannot personally descend upon the sect."

"Approval from the Immortal Ascension Hall?"

Zhang Yu frowned, understanding that this stemmed from the conflict between the Heavenly Court and the sect. The sect cultivators had always resisted excessive interference from the Heavenly Court.

Meanwhile, the Immortal Emperor maintained this balance, tacitly allowing the friction between the two sides.

After a moment of thought, Zhang Yu asked again, "Can I approve it?"

The third-rank Righteous God was momentarily taken aback, then quickly said, "The Immortal Ascension Hall has its own procedures, which generally cannot be interfered with from the outside. However, Your Highness, as Crown Prince, possesses veto power as well as the appointment authority for the Immortal Ascension Hall..."

Zhang Yu asked, "How does that work specifically?"

Shen Jiazi immediately sent a Spirit Realm address to Zhang Yu and said, "This is the sect management platform. With your current authority, you should be able to perform the vast majority of operations on the platform."

Zhang Yu logged into the platform, and a light screen instantly floated before his eyes.

Within this light screen, all the halls, pavilions, departments, the roster of every cultivator, and a myriad of management functions... were all laid out before him at that very moment.

Looking at the commands listed on the screen — transfers, promotions, dismissals, rejections, approvals, and more — Zhang Yu could almost see how every tap of his finger would decide the fate of countless cultivators throughout the sect, and even steer the course of an entire era of the Myriad Laws districts across layers 1 to 19.

Such immense authority made Zhang Yu involuntarily take a deep breath.

Steadying his mind slightly, Zhang Yu swept his gaze across the screen, clicked on the Immortal Ascension Hall module, and searched for the Heavenly Court's application documents.

Sure enough, he saw that the application for the descent of the Eight Divisions Great Gods had been rejected by the Immortal Ascension Hall.

Glancing at the rejection override function in the upper right corner, Zhang Yu gave it a light tap.

Immediately afterward, a message box popped up.

After pondering for a moment, Zhang Yu began typing in the message box.

Inside the Immortal Ascension Hall, projections of numerous Ascension Realm cultivators gathered, exchanging vast amounts of information back and forth.

"What on earth happened?!"

"What do we do now? We've already lost contact with the Immortal Emperor!"

"And what is this Crown Prince of Myriad Laws about? What right does Zhang Yu have to wield such authority?"

"I think we should wait for Immortal Venerable Profound Wisdom to descend and let her take charge of the situation."

Watching this scene of numerous cultivators fiercely debating, Bu Yuangui thought to himself, *"If this goes on... they're bound to come to blows sooner or later."*

*"I wonder if Zhang Yu can even keep this situation under control."*

Right at that moment, a notification surfaced in front of everyone in unison.

Su Xianchen said in surprise, "The document rejecting the descent of the Eight Divisions Great Gods... has been dismissed?"

Seeing the origin of the rejection, Fa Liuyuan felt a jolt in his heart. *"Zhang Yu?"*

At the same time, everyone could see the remark Zhang Yu had written: "Absurd. Reconsider."

Yi Yaosheng, hailing from the pharmaceutical faction, watched this unfold and said with deep concern, "It has been the rule for years that the Eight Divisions Great Gods must not descend in person to the Myriad Laws Sect unless absolutely necessary, to avoid excessive interference in sect affairs. Currently, this is neither a matter of establishing laws nor an Ascension Selection, so what need is there for the Eight Divisions Great Gods to come? I fear His Highness the Crown Prince might be unaware of this. We should join together to advise him."

After some thought, Su Xianchen stepped forward and said, "Since the Immortal Emperor entrusted the grave responsibility of supervising the sect to His Highness, the prince naturally has his own considerations."

The cultivators began arguing over this, unable to reach a consensus for the time being.

Bu Yuangui's eyes flickered, and he immediately used Bu Yingshu's account to send the ongoing debate to Zhang Yu, which Fu Ji received.

A moment later, as the applications for the Eight Righteous Gods' descent continued to stall without approval, a series of notifications traversed the Spirit Realm, crashing down in a thunderous descent.

"Relieved of all duties in the Immortal Ascension Hall, Yi Yaosheng shall no longer participate in the hall's deliberations..."

Looking at this dismissal notice, Yi Yaosheng froze for a moment. Before he could say anything more, his projection was expelled the very next instant.

As thirteen dismissal notices descended in succession, the application documents of the Eight Divisions Great Gods were also approved at lightning speed.

Before Zhang Yu, the divine power network seemed to boil over at this moment, the entire world illuminated by layers of golden light.

The Spirit Realm across a ten-thousand-mile radius seemed to grind to a halt. Every piece of information and every single character flowing through the Spirit Realm was subjected to the most rigorous surveillance. Even the Dharma Realm constructed atop the Spirit Realm was affected, undulating with ripples.

"From the Tax and Revenue Department, the Great God of Abundant Wealth greets Your Highness the Crown Prince."

"From the Ministry of All People, the Great God of Sentient Creation greets Your Highness the Crown Prince."

"From the Ministry of All People, the Great God of Heavenly Knowledge of All Questions..."

"From the Inspection Department, the Great God of the Eight Penal Circuits..."

Looking at the 1st rank Great Gods descending with resounding presence one after another, Zhang Yu spoke directly: "I need you to protect me twenty-four hours a day without interruption in the time to come. At the same time, take your posts within the Myriad Laws Sect and maintain order throughout the entire sect."

Hearing Zhang Yu's words, every Great God present felt a surge of excitement.

Extending their reach into the sects and interfering further with sect management had always been the wish of the Heavenly Court. Facing Zhang Yu's command at this moment, the Great Gods present naturally voiced their resounding agreement. 

The Great God of Abundant Wealth, who stood at the fore, personally delivered a Spirit Realm Book and said, "Through this Great God Seal Script Book, Your Highness may call upon our divine power at any time to cast talismans. This is your privilege."

Hearing this, Zhang Yu's eyes lit up. He took the Great God Seal Script Book and tapped his fingertips across it in rapid succession.

In an instant, lines of notifications surged forth from above Zhang Yu's head without end.

"Those who attack without authorization shall have all accounts frozen by the Tax and Revenue Department's Great God of Abundant Wealth and be subjected to the maximum fine..."

"Those who attack without authorization shall face a tax audit initiated by the Tax and Revenue Department's Great Gods of Tribute and Heavenly Supervision..."

"All injuries shall receive free medical treatment from the Ministry of Health's Great God of Healthy Spring Bloom..."

"Within the sensory range of the Spirit Realm, the Inspection Department's Great God of the Eight Penal Circuits may be summoned to detain any target..."

As one talisman after another was invoked, Zhang Yu felt layers upon layers of divine power traversing the Spirit Realm to surround him completely.

Anyone who launched an attack on him would face a frantic siege from the Eight Divisions Great Gods, a comprehensive strike spanning every dimension from the physical level to their accounts, credentials, and taxes.

Sensing all of this, Zhang Yu finally breathed a slight sigh of relief, a faint sense of security welling up in his heart.

Right at that moment, the divine power of the Inspection Department's Great God of the Eight Penal Circuits suddenly locked onto the shadow on Zhang Yu.

"Hmm? What is this..."

Amidst the surging divine power, Fu Ji was squeezed out with a squelch, immediately forced into her true form and turning back into her original tattered ragdoll.

In an instant, the Eight Divisions Great Gods present turned their gazes toward her in unison. Pair after pair of eyes and layer upon layer of divine power locked onto her firmly, poised to suppress her on the spot in the very next moment.

At this point, Fu Ji was so tense that the stitches on her body burst open, cotton fluff popping and tumbling out from the tears. In this split second, she felt as if she were suffocating, and the atmosphere in the room froze solid.

*"Aren't these Eight Divisions Great Gods too sharp? They can even detect this?"*

*"I'm doomed!"*

Just as Fu Ji thought she was doomed, she watched as the Great God of the Eight Penal Circuits stuffed the cotton back into her body and said, "Your Highness, this must be your magical artifact, correct? Please keep it safe."

As he spoke, he was already placing Fu Ji back.

Yet at the precise moment he reached out, divine power clashed, squeezing out the Auspicious God and the Herald God as well, who tumbled to the ground with a series of slaps.

The Auspicious God, having likewise reverted to his true form, was now a Seven-Colored Slime. Bouncing like jelly a few times, he trembled and wobbled until he rolled up beside the feet of the Great God of Abundant Wealth.

Amid the dead silence, the numerous Great Gods present silently turned their heads away.

The Great God of the Eight Penal Circuits spoke up, "Your Highness, these magical artifacts of yours easily trigger false alarms and errors from the Righteous Gods. Allow me to register them on a whitelist to exempt them from scans."

As he spoke, the Great God of the Eight Penal Circuits pointed a finger, lightly brushing over the bodies of Fu Ji, the Auspicious God, and the Herald God.

The next moment, an official certification from the Inspection Department appeared upon them. Any Righteous God scanning them would see the recorded data: "Imperial Magical Artifact belonging to the Crown Prince of the Myriad Laws Sect."

Watching the Great Gods' actions, Zhang Yu nodded slightly to himself. Browsing through his authority, he thought, *"Very good. It seems my authority is indeed high enough. As long as we are within the Myriad Laws Sect, it is sufficient for them to disregard the vast majority of Heavenly Court laws."*

Immediately afterward, Zhang Yu activated another Intelligence Talisman, ordering the Great God of Heavenly Knowledge of All Questions from the Ministry of All People to gather intelligence and check the current state of each of the Great Sects.

Looking over the information compiled by the other party, Zhang Yu mused, "Currently, the vast majority of cultivators across the Great Sects still have no idea what just happened, knowing very little about the battle that erupted among the Immortal Emperors."

"Apart from the 20th layer, which has vanished entirely, layers 11 through 19 have all suffered varying degrees of loss due to the fallout from the Immortal Emperors' duel, with numerous cultivator casualties, destroyed immortal abodes, and even massive amounts of supplies vaporized."

"Yet the ones most severely affected seem to be layers 21 through 29, where immense amounts of Immortal Dao resources have vanished into thin air, and there are even fallen Immortals..."

"As for layers 1 to 10, they were barely affected at all."

After swiftly confirming the situation across the layers, Zhang Yu continued to mobilize a vast number of Righteous Gods to garrison the sect, utilizing his own name to stabilize the state of affairs on each layer. 

Without interference from the Immortal Class, Zhang Yu's current title as the Crown Prince of Myriad Laws, combined with the might of the Eight Divisions Great Gods, granted him virtually unimpeded passage throughout the 11th to 19th layers. The situation within the sect was quickly brought under control. 

Seizing this opportunity, Zhang Yu began extensively promoting his subordinates, planting them in key positions one after another to replicate and expand his power, striving to gain control over more of the sect's resources and strength as fast as possible. 

With a light swipe of his fingertips across the Myriad Laws Sect management platform, cultivator after cultivator was reassigned to different posts. 

Zhang Yu glanced at the Great God of Abundant Wealth beside him and asked, "Merely giving them posts isn't enough. Can I directly grant rank and Realm Certificates?" 

The Great God of Abundant Wealth replied, "As for rank, an exceptional promotion is typically limited to at most one rank per year. As for Realm Certificates, you can indeed approve temporary Realm Certificates." 

After conducting a search, Zhang Yu indeed found the option for exceptional promotions on the management platform, but just as the Great God of Abundant Wealth had said, an exceptional promotion could only advance someone by one rank at most.

As for the temporary Realm Certificate, when Zhang Yu clicked on it, another light screen immediately appeared before his eyes. 

Writing the words "Ascension Certificate" across the screen, Zhang Yu issued himself a "Temporary Ascension Qualification Certificate valid for five years". 

Looking at this Ascension Certificate that he had approved for himself, a trace of emotion flashed through his heart. 

Back then, he had gone through immense hardship just to obtain a Foundation Establishment Qualification Certificate. 

Yet now, even a certificate as formidable as the Ascension Qualification Certificate could be casually written out by him at will. 

*"All the rules and regulations throughout Kunxu are nothing more than pliable sand before those truly at the top."*

However, with Zhang Yu's current authority, he could only issue one temporary Ascension Certificate, though he could issue two temporary Tribulation Certificates and four temporary Void Refinement Certificates. 

After a moment of wistfulness, Zhang Yu asked again, "What if I want an Immortal Certificate? It doesn't seem possible to grant one here." 

The Great God of Abundant Wealth replied, "Ever since the process of achieving Immortality came to be jointly managed by the Dao Masters of each sect's Ascension Dao Lineage, it has no longer been something the sect management platform can control."

"Ascension Dao Lineages?"

Zhang Yu nodded slightly and refrained from delving into it for the moment, continuing instead to promote his subordinate cultivators.

*"Yu Xinghan, Le Mulan, Teacher Gao, Teacher Magnetic Pole, Senior Sister Ye Xingli and the others..."*

*"Hmm... and the Spirit Transformation principals who came up with me from the lower realm. This is also the perfect chance to transition them from outsourced contractors to official members."*

At the same time, he sent a message to Bai Zhenzhen. After explaining the current situation, he left a note: "A-Zhen, come to the Myriad Laws Sect."

Zhang Yu hoped to transfer Bai Zhenzhen to the Myriad Laws Sect; after all, with him around, she would be able to grow much better here.

Just as Zhang Yu was busy stabilizing the situation in the Myriad Laws Sect, a projection of Immortal Venerable Profound Wisdom traversed the Spirit Realm and appeared before him.

Zhang Yu was not surprised in the slightest by this Immortal Venerable's appearance.

Ever since he learned that communications between the 19th and 21st layers had been restored, he had known that, sooner or later, she would come looking for him.

Immortal Venerable Profound Wisdom glanced at the Great God projections beside Zhang Yu and said, "Rest assured, Zhang Yu. I am not here to seize your power, and now is not the time for infighting."

The Sect Examination Clone snorted cold. *"If the Eight Divisions Great Gods weren't here presiding, I doubt you'd be talking like that."*

Immortal Venerable Profound Wisdom asked, "How much do you know about the situation with the ten Immortal Emperors?"

Zhang Yu replied, "The Immortal Emperors are fighting with all their might..."

Listening as Zhang Yu briefly explained the situation, Immortal Venerable Profound Wisdom nodded and asked, "Then how much do you understand about the concept of the Immortal Emperor of the End?"

Zhang Yu replied, "That is an existence who has broken through the Immortal Emperor Realm to stand above all living beings, truly standing at the apex of the Immortal Dao, intending to take control of the entire world's fate."

Immortal Venerable Profound Wisdom nodded eagerly. "Correct. Knowing that saves me the trouble of explaining. So you should also understand that the Immortal Emperors have gone all out in this war. Even if the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor falls, this battle will not cease."

Immortal Venerable Profound Wisdom sighed. "If the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor is besieged and falls, the remaining Immortal Emperors will divide and consume him."

"Once they finish devouring him, another Immortal Emperor will inevitably become too strong, drawing too close to the End, and become the next target of a siege. That second besieged Immortal Emperor might successfully break through and become the Immortal Emperor of the End, or he might likewise fall, be divided and consumed again, and trigger another round of fierce conflict."

"Thus, this battle among the Immortal Emperors has already become a death spiral where they will not rest until one side perishes; it will not stop until the very last moment."

"And the moment it stops will be the day the Immortal Emperor of the End is born."

"When that time comes, all the assets in this world will ultimately belong to the Immortal Emperor of the End, while all of us will become destitute paupers left with nothing."

Zhang Yu nodded deeply, thinking to himself, *"It seems that the Immortal Class within Kunxu has also realized by now... that the ten Immortal Emperors are currently the enemies of the highest priority."*

Immortal Venerable Profound Wisdom continued, "Ever since the Immortal Emperors departed, we have been keeping them under observation. Currently, the Dao Lineages held by the ten Immortal Emperors have entered active periods one after another. There is good reason to believe that as they go all out, they will continuously improve amid their repeated, fierce clashes. These intense battles are themselves a form of exchange that will gradually propel them into a state of technological explosion."

Zhang Yu was slightly startled. "A technological explosion?"

Immortal Venerable Profound Wisdom sighed. "War is the finest catalyst. An all-out war among the ten Immortal Emperors will cause their Immortal Dao Technology to advance by leaps and bounds, progressing by leaps with every passing moment."

"Devouring each other, coupled with a technological explosion... the birth of the Immortal Emperor of the End will likely come much sooner than imagined."

Hearing this explanation from Immortal Venerable Profound Wisdom, Zhang Yu's heart sank once more.

Immortal Venerable Profound Wisdom continued, "Just today, an Immortal Venerable attempted a breakthrough."

Zhang Yu's gaze sharpened, yet he was not surprised.

Some Immortal Venerables within the Ten Great Sects had constantly been suppressed by the Immortal Emperors, which was why they had long been unable and unwilling to attempt a breakthrough.

With the Immortal Emperors temporarily absent, it was only natural that some Immortal Venerables would try to break through.

However, Immortal Venerable Profound Wisdom delivered another piece of bad news: "Yet at the very final moment before breaking through, they failed."

Zhang Yu asked in surprise, "Failed? Why?"

Immortal Venerable Profound Wisdom sighed with emotion. "After all these years of baptism, whether it is the Immortals or the Immortal Venerables of the Great Sects today, they have all been suppressed by the Immortal Emperors for far too long."

"Our foundations and the Immortal Dao Technology we possess have already been thoroughly analyzed by the Immortal Emperors."

"As for the Immortal Class within the sects today, their Dao Lineages are either fractured, making breakthroughs difficult;"

"Either their own Dao Lineages exist to support an Immortal Emperor — such that breaking through would accelerate the birth of the Immortal Emperor of the End, leaving them not daring to break through — or... they have been secretly locked down by the ten Immortal Emperors, rendering them entirely unable to break through to the Immortal Emperor Realm."

Speaking up to this point, a glimmer of dejection flashed across Immortal Venerable Profound Wisdom's eyes. "From this perspective, the Immortal Emperors have long made thorough preparations for this final Dao Contest. Once the decisive battle begins, they will never allow anyone to reap the spoils from behind. One could say that within the Ten Great Sects today, every existing being who has achieved immortality is utterly incapable of breaking through to the Immortal Emperor Realm."

Immortal Venerable Profound Wisdom asked, "What are your thoughts on this situation?"

Zhang Yu's gaze darkened slightly. Even as he carried out various operations, he had kept part of his mind dedicated to analyzing the situation. The intelligence brought by Immortal Venerable Profound Wisdom deepened his contemplation even further, gradually bringing a conclusion to form in his mind.

Zhang Yu spoke slowly, "The growth rate of the ten Immortal Emperors' cultivation has already far surpassed ours. Throughout all of Kunxu, I fear no one can catch up to them, and the Immortal Emperor of the End will inevitably be born from among their ranks."

"Yet driven by their aspiration for the Immortal Dao, even when facing the Immortal Emperor of the End, the cultivators and the Immortal Class of Kunxu will never give up. They will surely persist in fighting against the Immortal Emperor of the End — against this ultimate monopolist — refusing to surrender their own chance to scale the peak of the Immortal Dao."

"Therefore, under the pressure of the Immortal Emperor of the End, the powerhouses of the Great Sects will have only one choice: to gradually join forces, with the ultimate goal of resisting the Immortal Emperor of the End, uniting all cultivators and the entire Immortal Class to forge an unprecedented coalition."

Zhang Yu thought to himself, *"This is the relationship of mutual generation and restraint between the Monopoly Faction and the Legacy Faction. When the Monopoly Faction pushes toward its extreme, it forces everyone else in the world to unite, turning them all into members of the Legacy Faction, the Open Faction."*

*"If the ten Immortal Emperors' side will give birth to the ultimate individual of the Monopoly Faction — the Immortal Emperor of the End — then the Ten Great Sects' side, in order to combat this emperor, will bring forth the very thing the Legacy Faction has always sought to forge... a united force spanning all of Kunxu."*

*"And should this force ultimately defeat the Immortal Emperor of the End, perhaps it could be called the Demise Civilization."*

A flash of insight crossed Zhang Yu's mind at this moment: *"This is not just the ultimate battle of the ten Immortal Emperors. Perhaps... it is also the final battle between the two paths of monopoly and inheritance. The winning side will prove its correctness."*

Listening to what Zhang Yu said, Immortal Venerable Profound Wisdom replied, "You think an unprecedented united force will be formed? Perhaps. But the Great Sects have not yet reached that point. While you were busy with sect affairs, the Immortal Class of each sect has already engaged in hundreds or thousands of rounds of discussions up there. Yet throughout these exchanges, no one has been able to produce a method convincing enough to persuade everyone on how to stop the birth of the Immortal Emperor of the End, or how to fight against him."

"Therefore, the Great Sects are currently making preparations according to their own respective plans."

Zhang Yu's interest was piqued, and he asked, "What kind of plans do they have?"

Immortal Venerable Profound Wisdom said, "No one knows. Right now within the Ten Great Sects, there are still minds, ears and eyes, and even incarnations left behind by the ten Immortal Emperors. Layers 30 to 36 in particular have now been completely sealed off, and we are still unable to enter them. No one knows what the ten Immortal Emperors have left behind there."

"That is why the high-ranking cultivators of each sect are unwilling to reveal their plans easily, especially to you..."

"Zhang Yu, you are the Crown Prince of Myriad Laws specially arranged by the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor. How can we be sure that you aren't a hidden card he left behind?"
