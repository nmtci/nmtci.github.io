# Chapter 1009: One Year Later, the Storm Is Coming

Inside the Immortal Grotto-Heaven.

Two figures suddenly collided, and surging power erupted with a deafening roar. It was as if even light and space were about to be torn apart, plunging the entire sealed space into darkness.

Even as the terrifying force crushed the space, leaving nowhere for sound to travel or light to pass through, the two continued to fight.

A string of fist techniques, sword techniques, palm techniques... all kinds of martial dao techniques burst forth one after another, as if intent on reducing heaven and earth to chaos.

If not for the Immortal Grotto-Heaven and the layers of spatial restrictions Zhang Yu had put in place, their clash alone would have been enough to destroy one Grotto-Heaven after another, causing hundreds of millions in economic losses.

The two clashing at this moment were none other than Bu Yingshu and Zhang Yu, both Tribulation Transcendence Cultivators.

Bu Yingshu had originally planned to study martial arts together with Zhang Yu, but the longer they fought, the more alarmed she became.

*“Could this guy Zhang Yu... really be improving so much faster than even an Immortal Clan prodigy like me?”*

In another world, perhaps Bu Yingshu might have marveled at Zhang Yu’s talent, hard work, and long-awaited breakthrough, and wondered how much hardship he had endured to reach such strength.

But in Kunxu, Bu Yingshu had only one thought: *“How much money must he have spent, and how many benefits must the Immortal Emperor have fed him, for him to have this kind of strength?!”*

*“Is this right? Which one of us is actually from the Immortal Clan — him or me?”*

*“His strength is improving so quickly. Isn’t he afraid of burning out? Isn’t he afraid his dao heart will become unstable?”*

As her power surged from the early stage of Tribulation Transcendence all the way to the peak, Bu Yingshu still felt that no matter how fiercely she unleashed her power or how she drove the energy within her, she couldn’t shake Zhang Yu in front of her in the slightest.

She even had the feeling that, even if she could unleash more of her reserves, Zhang Yu’s physical strength, magic power, and Dao Heart level would still each be one level above hers.

*“But my dao heart, physical body, and magic power — the three fundamental stats — are already at the Tribulation Transcendence peak. Could it be that Zhang Yu…”*

Bu Yingshu’s heart jolted. She immediately asked, “Little Yu, back when you were in the Void Refinement Realm, you could fight across realms against people undergoing Tribulation Transcendence. Can you even go beyond your realm again after reaching the Tribulation Transcendence Realm, and unleash power at the level of the Ascension Realm?”

Hearing this, Zhang Yu shook his head, indicating that the answer was no.

Bu Yingshu thought to herself, *“That makes sense. The higher the cultivation realm, the greater the gap between realms.”*

*“Zhang Yu could already fight across realms on the strength of his own abilities while at the Void Refinement stage. That alone is incredibly difficult. How could someone at the Tribulation Transcendence Realm possibly do the same?”*

*“He should simply be closer than I am to Ascension grade power.”*

At the same time, Zhang Yu thought to himself, *“A burst of power? No need. With Senior Supreme Truth’s legacy, even in my normal state... my ceiling at the Tribulation Transcendence Realm is higher than that of experts at the same level.”*

After a year of cultivation, he had built a solid foundation with the Chain Formation Diagram, Dao Seed, Dharma Realm, medicine, and immortal qi.

Even without using any burst of power, his Dao Heart level in his normal state had reached level 71 (100%), his physical strength was at 71.00 points, and his magic power limit had reached 200,000,000 units.

On top of that, all the cultivation arts he mainly used had been pushed to level 71, the standard of the Ascension Realm.

In other words, in his normal state, Zhang Yu already possessed the strength of the early Ascension Realm.

And by circulating the Kunpeng Swallowing Heaven Manual and the Blazing Sun Meridian in conjunction with Yun Taizhen’s [Emperor Creation Seed], he could forcibly raise his basic abilities to the mid-Ascension Realm level by burning his lifespan at ten thousand times the normal rate.

Zhang Yu said, “Little Bu, I can suppress you in a pure martial dao battle using only my normal strength. Why would I need to unleash a burst of power?”

As he spoke, Zhang Yu grabbed Bu Yingshu by the head and gently rubbed it. For a moment, Bu Yingshu was taken aback.

*“Zhang Yu? He’s holding my head?”*

In an instant, countless memories surfaced in Bu Yingshu’s mind: scenes of her stepping on Zhang Yu, kicking him flying, pinning him down, and grabbing his head again and again.

But now, the tables had turned. Zhang Yu’s palm rested lightly on Bu Yingshu’s head. Even as one fine notice after another popped up, he didn’t let go.

After a year of working in real estate, especially the revenue from selling land, Zhang Yu’s assets were now immense — more than enough to ignore the fines he was racking up by patting her head.

“How long are you going to keep doing that?”

Bu Yingshu glared at Zhang Yu, but a trace of apprehension she didn’t even notice flickered through her heart. *“What should I do?”*

*“Zhang Yu wouldn’t… wouldn’t…”*

Countless shameful images flashed through Bu Yingshu’s mind.

*“Could he be about to do what I used to do — stomp me into the ground, send me flying into a wall, or pin me face-down on the floor?”*

*“No! Wouldn’t that mean our positions would be reversed?!”*

*“Zhang Yu is my little pet! I don’t want to be his little pet!”*

At the thought, a rebellious spirit welled up in Bu Yingshu.

*“If I can’t beat him in a fight... then I’ll just have to rush over, cling to him, rub against him like crazy, and crush him with the harshest fines imaginable! I’ll scare him off with outrageously expensive tickets!”*

Just as Bu Yingshu steeled herself, determined that as a member of the Immortal Clan she would never endure such humiliation and would fight back to the bitter end, Zhang Yu had already withdrawn his hand.

Looking at the resolute expression on Bu Yingshu’s face, Zhang Yu thought, *“What a stingy girl. I’ve been stepped on so many times, but now I pat your head once and you start hissing at me?”*

At that moment, in Zhang Yu’s eyes, Bu Yingshu looked like a silly cat in Spinosaurus mode, hissing away. He had no idea how she planned to fight back, but he could predict it wouldn’t be anything good.

*“Little Bu has a mysterious background. I’d better not provoke her too much.”*

So Zhang Yu decided to stop provoking her for the time being. He immediately withdrew his hand and stepped back.

Watching Zhang Yu’s movements, Bu Yingshu gave a cold snort and said calmly, like a victorious warrior, *“Little Yu, good thing you stopped in time. If you’d been any later, once I used my secret move, you’d have been finished!”*

After issuing her warning, Bu Yingshu fled in haste, calling out as she ran, “My dad says I have to go back. I’ll come give you some pointers another time!”

The moment she left the Immortal Grotto-Heaven, Bu Yingshu immediately contacted Bu Yuangui, told him what had just happened, and asked, “Dad! What should I do? Is there any way to make Zhang Yu as obedient as before?”

Bu Yuangui said, “Look through the messages the cultivators below Tribulation Transcendence have sent you.”

Bu Yingshu paused, then casually skimmed through them. She saw a string of flattery, praise, and attempts to curry favor, and couldn’t help feeling a little better.

“Is Dad telling me to take it out on someone else first?” Bu Yingshu wondered.

Bu Yuangui continued, “Just copy the way they flatter you and use it to flatter Zhang Yu.”

“Having been on the receiving end of so much bootlicking over the years, you’ve actually built up a wealth of experience in Simpology. You just need to reverse your approach a little and bring it out, and winning Zhang Yu over should be a piece of cake.”

“After all, how long has Zhang Yu been around? He can’t possibly have as much experience in this area as you.”

Hearing this, Bu Yingshu felt a surge of anger.

But when she thought of how her chance to achieve immortality and her improvement in innate talent were both tied to Zhang Yu, and the image of the increasingly irresistible Zhang Yu lately flashed through her mind, she began to hesitate.

Just then, Bu Yuangui continued, “Yingshu, you have to understand that you’re no ordinary cultivator.”

Bu Yingshu nodded slightly. That was true, she thought.

Bu Yuangui said, “You get fined just for engaging in dual cultivation. On the path of the Immortal Dao, you’re basically disabled.”

“If you’re unwilling to do this and unwilling to do that, you’ll only end up farther and farther from Zhang Yu.”

“Right now, there are probably enough people eager to lick Zhang Yu’s boots to form a line from the 20th layer of Kunxu all the way down to the first layer. If you won’t do it, there are plenty of others who will.”

Only after a long while did a message from Bu Yingshu appear before Bu Yuangui.

Bu Yingshu: “Dad, how about you do the bootlicking for me? I’ll send you the chat history, and you can help me figure out what to say.”

Inside the Immortal Grotto-Heaven.

Fu Ji looked at the message Bu Yingshu had sent and said, “Hehe, Zhang Yu! After this exchange, I think Bu Yingshu has gradually realized how our strengths have shifted. Her messages are much more polite now.”

“That’s good.”

As Zhang Yu replied, a thought crossed his mind, and Liu Qianxian of the Heavenly Surveillance Hall appeared before him, transported there through space.

Liu Qianxian was already used to Zhang Yu’s methods.

She immediately cupped her hands and presented a low-grade book, saying respectfully, “Profound Monarch Zhang, this is the latest low-grade book we’ve received…”

As she handed it over, Liu Qianxian couldn’t help thinking, “Lately, more and more people who’ve been hoarding Books have started donating them of their own accord.”

She knew exactly why. Ever since Zhang Yu created the Chain Formation Diagram last year, low-grade books had gradually begun to lose their effectiveness.

At first, a few low-grade books related to the Chain Formation Diagram stopped working.

Later, low-grade books related to Dao Seeds began to fail as well.

And recently, even low-grade books specializing in various cultivation arts had started showing abnormalities.

Originally, once equipped, these low-grade books specializing in cultivation arts allowed their users to quickly learn and master a related cultivation art every day.

The prologue to Zhang Yu’s *Feather Book* was even more comprehensive: it could specialize in all kinds of cultivation arts. Once you selected a cultivation art to specialize in, you could cultivate it a set number of times to raise its level.

Liu Qianxian did not know what caused the books to stop working, much less that it was all part of the Immortal Emperor’s plan. She also did not understand the underlying principles, but she understood the consequences.

*“As more and more books stop working, their value has begun to plummet. Even books that still work are now regarded with suspicion — no one knows when they might stop being useful.”*

*“Not a single mid-grade book has stopped working so far, but many high-ranking cultivators believe it’s only a matter of time.”*

*“By contrast, many high-ranking believe the Four Books of Feathered Ascension will never stop working.”*

Liu Qianxian thought to herself, *“I wonder how many of the low-grade books in Profound Monarch Zhang’s hands have already stopped working?”*

Zhang Yu took the *Pill Refining Book* she handed him. Then, with a thought, he engulfed Liu Qianxian in a surging wave of Grotto-Heaven force and sent her back to the Heavenly Surveillance Hall.

With his spatial technology now at the pinnacle, Zhang Yu could teleport a mid-to-high-level Tribulation Transcendence cultivator like Liu Qianxian at will, without making a sound.

Next, Zhang Yu took the book to the depths of the Immortal Grotto-Heaven and inspected it. He thought to himself, *“As expected, this Pill Refining Book can no longer be used normally. It must have lost its effect too, right?”*

The next moment, Zhang Yu opened the Feather Book and swallowed the Pill Refining Book before him.

In the blink of an eye, a Pill Refining Cultivation Art specialization module had appeared in the Prologue.

In addition, the Prologue now contained more than twenty specialization modules derived from low-grade books.

Another dozen or so low-grade books from the Cultivation Notes series had given Zhang Yu a batch of Chain Formation Diagrams. Like [Peerless Daoist Presence], they all operated continuously, enhancing his comprehension, aptitude, constitution, physical body, magic power, and more.

Of all these many low-grade books, the one Zhang Yu found most useful was a copy of the Immeasurable Book.

After obtaining this copy of the Immeasurable Book, his magic power, like his physical strength, gained a “+” sign.

He only had to keep pouring magic power into the book to store it up, gradually accumulating magic power units. Then, whenever he needed them, he could spend them to directly increase his maximum magic power.

“Although this can’t break through the limits of my cultivation realm, I can use it right after breaking through to a major realm.”

Zhang Yu thought to himself, “That way, when I break through to the Ascension Realm in the future, or even attain the Immortal Realm, I’ll be able to release my reserves immediately, bringing both my physical strength and magic power to the limit of my current realm in an instant.”

At this moment, all the modules in the Feather Book were working normally, clearly unlike the Pill Refining Book, which had just been unusable.

Zhang Yu sighed inwardly. “Over the past year, the Immortal Emperors have been using some unknown method. Besides the Chain Formation Diagram, they’ve gradually begun severing the connections between Dao Seeds, Cultivation Art Specialization, and Books as well, causing all three functions — the Chain Formation Diagram, Dao Seeds, and Cultivation Art Specialization — to gradually stop working, starting with low-grade books.”

“However, mid-grade books — and my Feather Book, presumably because of its higher divine authority — have not stopped working yet. As for the abilities of the Four Books of Feathered Ascension, they have nothing to do with these three Dao Lineages, so they should be unaffected.”

Zhang Yu thought, “Still, although my Feather Book can be used for now, if I leave things as they are... As the connections between the three Dao Lineages and Books are severed bit by bit, once they are cut off completely, the Feather Book will surely lose the functions of the Chain Formation Diagrams, Dao Seeds, and Specializations too.”

“Fortunately, I was prepared.”

With a thought, Zhang Yu opened the Feather Book to the Immortal Foundation Chapter. In addition to the World-Domain Dao Lineage and the Technique-Chaining Dao Lineage, both of which had reached 100% Dao-Seeking Progress, he saw that the Dao Seed Dao Lineage had also reached 39%.

This was all thanks to the guidance left behind by the three Supreme Immortal Emperors of the lost history, which had enabled Zhang Yu to successfully parasitize the Technique-Chaining Dao Lineage and the Dao Seed Dao Lineage.

Zhang Yu thought, “Next, as long as I finish parasitizing the Dao Seed Dao Lineage and one other Dao Lineage, I won’t have to worry about the Book losing its Chain Formation Diagrams, Dao Seed, and Specialization abilities.”

“By then, I’ll be the intermediary the Feather Book uses to access these three Dao Lineages. As long as I’m here, the Feather Book in my hands will be able to keep those functions running.”

But Zhang Yu knew that he had already collected nearly all the books he could. To keep collecting books and advancing his Dao-Seeking Progress, he would have to emerge victorious at the Book-Wielder Assembly and obtain the books held by the sect’s book-wielders.

“I also have to get the remaining books: the Transformation Book, the Flight Book, and the Ascension Book.”

“The more books I obtain, the more Dao Lineages I’ll have a chance to master as soon as I achieve immortality again.”

Zhang Yu’s gaze swept over the information left behind by the three Immortal Emperor powerhouses in the lost history.

“Although time was limited, the three Seniors did their best to leave me information about their respective Dao Lineages in the lost history.”

“Senior Ying Xintian left me information concerning the World-Domain Dao Lineage and the Kunlun Dao Lineage.”

In the information Ying Xintian had left, he expressed relief that Zhang Yu had already become a host to the World-Domain Dao Lineage, and helpless regret over the fragmentation of the Kunlun Dao Lineage.

Ying Xintian said, “Take as much of the World-Domain Dao Lineage as you can. Although I’m not the Ying Xintian from your history, I believe that once I know everything, I’ll be willing to lend you a hand and let you use my Dao Lineage.”

“It’s just a pity that the Kunlun Dao Lineage has fallen into pieces. If you get the chance, suppress the various Dao Masters and, using the method I taught you, take control of this Dao Lineage again. You can then transform Kunxu back into Kunlun and turn the entire world into a great formation...”

After repeatedly reviewing the information Ying Xintian had left behind, Zhang Yu could tell that his understanding of the Kunlun Dao Lineage was probably beyond what even the current Great Dao Lords of the Kunlun Dao Lineage could match.

Immortal Emperor Heavenly Virtue had left Zhang Yu information about the Heavenly Tribulation Dao Lineage, the Karma Dao Lineage, and the God-Investiture Dao Lineage.

Immortal Emperor Heavenly Virtue said, “The Heavenly Sword Sect has taken control of the Heavenly Tribulation Dao Lineage? What a pity. With your Paragon of Virtue aptitude, this Dao Lineage should have been the most suitable for you.

“The Karma Dao Lineage is used to support the Heavenly Tribulation Dao Lineage. It can investigate the causes and effects of all things, letting you make predictions about your enemies or conceal your own thoughts and actions from their scrutiny. As long as you don’t trigger a rollback, it’s enough to ensure you remain invincible. If this Dao Lineage is still around and has no master, then you should seize the chance to master it as soon as possible...

“And then there’s the God-Investiture Dao Lineage. It can confer godhood upon living beings, allowing you to create and command pseudo-Dao Lineages. Though they aren’t true Dao Lineages, within a certain area and for a certain time, they can rival the real thing. The God-Investiture Dao Lineage may not seem impressive at first, but the longer you master it and the more gods you appoint, the more powerful it becomes...”

Yet the only Dao Lineage information the Monster Saint left Zhang Yu was the Supreme Nihility Dao Lineage.

Monster Saint: “You’re saying I’m the Fourth Monster Saint? And there are also First, Second, Third, Fifth, and even Sixth Monster Saints?”

“So this rollback has defeated me again and again — at least six times, and suppressed me each time?”

“Unfortunately, the only information I can provide now is about this Supreme Nihility Dao Lineage. If you get the chance to contact another version of me from a lost history, ask them about it. I believe that no matter which ‘me’ it is, they won’t refuse.”

In Zhang Yu’s understanding, the Fourth Monster Saint’s Supreme Nihility Dao Lineage could cause the consciousness and spirit of every cultivator in the world to give birth to a pure Mental World.

With the Monster Saint’s guidance, living beings would continue to multiply and embark upon the Immortal Dao, creating layer upon layer of Mental Worlds, ultimately without end.

Even if the cultivators themselves died, they could continue to exist within their own Mental Worlds.

And within the Mental World each person created, they were practically invincible.

The Fourth Monster Saint stood at the pinnacle of all Mental Worlds, governing the myriad heavens and worlds and becoming the ruler of them all.

Immortal Slayer looked at the messages on the Feather Book and said slowly, “The predecessors of history, you did not fail. Your experiences, your knowledge, and everything you accumulated have been passed down to us, becoming our hope for victory.”

Just then, a greeting message from Yun Zhiguang appeared before Zhang Yu’s eyes.

This year, the number of law establishment quotas had been reduced, and Zhang Yu had received only one third-generation law establishment quota, which he directly recommended to Ye Xingli.

As for Yun Zhiguang, Zhang Yu could only ask her to wait a little longer and tell her he would make arrangements for her when an opportunity arose.

From then on, Yun Zhiguang would send Zhang Yu greetings and messages from time to time, and give him gifts.

Zhang Yu couldn’t help replying, “You don’t need to message me about such trivial matters in the future.”

After a moment’s thought, he added, “You can also let Zhao Tianxing know.”

With the two Great Dao Seeds Zhang Yu had bestowed upon her, Ye Xingli also succeeded in law establishment training, establishing the Revision Extension Law Edict.

With Ye Xingli’s Law Edict as a supplement, Zhang Yu added a third item to Expiration Date Law Edict, alongside Extension Law Edict and Sealing Law Edict, the Revision Law Edict.

The so-called Revision Law Edict meant that during the review process, the provisions had to be amended according to the Review Department’s opinions. Only after the revisions were complete could the Law Edict Expiration Date be reset.

Zhang Yu thought to himself, “This way, we can gain control over the power to amend the law edicts, adding yet another layer of restraint on them.”

He understood that if they tried to push for this power right from the start, they would likely face even greater resistance than before. But by gradually raising the stakes and advancing things step by step, the cultivators’ opposition would be much less intense.

At the same time, Gao Chongguang sent Zhang Yu a message, asking, “What do you think of using this place as the new Artifact Refining Development Zone?”

They were in the outermost layer of the Immortal Grotto-Heaven.

A long string of people followed behind Gao Chongguang, surrounding him like stars clustering around the moon.

After seeing Zhang Yu’s reply, Gao Chongguang immediately pointed to a certain area and casually made a few strokes in the air. “This will be the Artifact Refining Development Zone from now on.”

With a sweep of Gao Chongguang’s finger, projections of the spirit realm appeared across the ground, forming a ring of light that demarcated the selected area.

Not long afterward, the major companies and high-ranking cultivators all began making their moves, pouring down vast sums of Immortal Coins. One building after another gradually rose from the ground.

Gao Chongguang sighed to himself. *“I’ve spent hundreds of years building houses, but I’ve never been as happy as I have been this past year selling land. The group must have brought in at least two billion Immortal Coins this year, right?”*

Gao Chongguang knew that although quite a lot of those two billion Immortal Coins had to go toward building the Immortal Grotto-Heaven, the rest would still amount to a handsome profit once divided among everyone.

Zhang Yu, for instance, had used at least one billion Immortal Coins of it.

As for Gao Chongguang, he was now one of the highly paid people whose salary desires had been satisfied. And because he was in charge of planning the Immortal Grotto-Heaven construction, countless companies treated him as an honored guest.

His subordinate, Tu Lishan, was responsible for the construction company under the group on the other side, and was likewise reaching a daily salary climax.

The moment Tu Lishan spotted the message from Gao Chongguang, he scrambled up off the Joyful Union cultivator and fired off a frantic reply. He quickly replied, “Teacher! I just finished an all-night meeting! Don’t worry, we’ll definitely finish the next phase of the project ahead of schedule!”

Tu Lishan immediately contacted Mo Shangjin again and asked, “Have you got everyone arranged?”

Mo Shangjin: “Don’t worry, Teacher. They’re all contracted recruits from the lower realm!”

Mo Shangjin glanced over the resumes, first filtering out those with Junior College to Bachelor’s Degree Program qualifications, then those from vocational colleges, lower-tier colleges, and upper-tier colleges, and then graduates from the Ten Greats. Yet he found there were still too many talented people.

In the end, he raised the education requirement to PhD level before he felt it would be more convenient to screen applicants.

Looking at the top cultivators from the Dao Techniques Department, Finance Department, and Artifact Refinement Department, Mo Shangjin couldn’t help but sigh to himself, *“There are simply far, far too many talented people in the lower realm graduating every year.”*

Just then, Mo Shangjin suddenly spotted a familiar resume: *“Feng Tingting?”*

He contacted her, only to hear her say, “Senior Brother Mo, I’m in Civil Engineering too. I also have a Civil Engineering degree.”

Meanwhile, atop a tower in the Immortal Grotto-Heaven, Bai Zhenzhen of the Heavenly Sword Sect had approved the funding and construction of a building.

At that moment, Bai Zhenzhen stood on empty air, looking down at the city taking shape below. She said, “Yu Zi, who could’ve imagined that we, who once lived under a bridge on the first layer, would achieve so much today?”

“A-Zhen.” Zhang Yu’s figure appeared behind her, sensing the strange fluctuations emanating from her.

By now, Bai Zhenzhen had also completed law establishment training and created her own Law Edict.

As far as Zhang Yu knew, her Law Edict was related to Sword Intent Conversion. It collected energy taxes and fees on all Sword Intent Conversion, making it one of the Heavenly Sword Sect’s tax laws.

Bai Zhenzhen said, “I made some inquiries for you. The information on the Flight Book is in the hands of the Heavenly Sword Sect’s Book-Wielder. Here’s what I found out about him.”

“He’s a disciple of the Heavenly Sword Immortal Emperor and one of the Ascension Seeds. Last year, he was behind at least 50000 trending topics. Setting the Immortals aside... within the Heavenly Sword Sect, he ranks in the top three.”

After receiving the file Bai Zhenzhen had sent him, Zhang Yu nodded slightly and said nothing more. He knew there was no need for the two of them to thank each other.

Bai Zhenzhen continued, “Yu Zi, according to what I’ve learned, the Book-Wielder Assembly is only an appetizer. Once it ends, the Ten Immortal Emperors will begin their so-called plan to revive the Immortal Ancestor.”

Bai Zhenzhen turned her head and said, “Something’s very wrong. I checked, and the Heavenly Sword Immortal Emperor hasn’t posted on social media for more than ten days.”

“Do you know what that means? It means that even he, an Immortal Emperor, has temporarily put aside his cultivation and is gathering all his strength, preparing to unleash a peerless sword strike.”

“And the Celestial Monster Sect’s breeding numbers have been falling over the past month.”

“The Joyful Union Sect’s dual cultivation transactions have also plummeted.”

“A large number of cultivators from the Immortal Armaments Bureau have put their physical bodies into hibernation...”

As Bai Zhenzhen revealed one piece of news after another, Zhang Yu felt as though a storm were brewing.

It seemed that every sect and every Immortal Emperor was silently accumulating strength, waiting for a world-shaking battle.

Bai Zhenzhen, who felt it all too, was overcome by an unprecedented sense of dread.

Her True Spirit Root seemed to sense something as well, occasionally giving off faint traces of fear.

At that moment, an impulse surged through Bai Zhenzhen: she wanted to stop Zhang Yu from attending the assembly and flee back to the first layer with him.

*“This fear is already affecting my ability to think.”*

Bai Zhenzhen thought to herself, *“This means that the Heavenly Sword Immortal Emperor’s sword intent has built up to an unprecedented level. Even after I came to Myriad Laws, even in this Immortal Grotto-Heaven, I can still faintly sense it.”*

She turned to Zhang Yu and said, “Yu Zi...”

Looking at Zhang Yu, who was about to participate in the assembly, Bai Zhenzhen felt as though she were watching an insect willingly leap into an Immortal Emperor’s storm.

Zhang Yu gave her a confident smile. “Don’t worry. I’ll definitely win.”

As Bai Zhenzhen looked at his confident smile, she was reminded of how he had looked before the Ten Greats League and before the final battle of the University War.

But at this moment, Bai Zhenzhen didn’t care whether Zhang Yu won or lost. She only hoped he would survive.

Myriad Laws Sect, 16th layer.

Tai Yuebai stared in astonishment at the figure before him and sprang to his feet. “You... came in person? What if this is a trap?”

The other person smiled faintly at him. “It’s such an elaborate trap that I had no choice but to come.”

“Yuebai, you and Zhang Yu have worked hard.”

“Leave the rest to me.”

“This responsibility should have been borne by the Immortal Class all along. It shouldn’t have been left on your shoulders.”
