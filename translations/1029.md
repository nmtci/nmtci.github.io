# Chapter 1029: Closed Loop Rollbacks?

Desolate Ox Great Sage said, "We placed our hopes on the future, but we never expected... it would be in such a distant future."

The next moment, the minds of Desolate Ox Great Sage and Zhang Yu gently collided at that instant.

In a flash, countless pieces of information flickered past Zhang Yu's eyes, gradually allowing him to understand what Desolate Ox's words truly meant.

Zhang Yu thought to himself, *"Back then, when the Immortal Ancestor revived and the ten Immortal Emperors initiated the rollback, I used the power unleashed by the rollback with the help of the Immortal Ancestor's will through the Book's Dao Lineage to communicate with the powerhouses in the lost history who were also facing the rollback."*

*"It was that very layout that led them to pass down vast amounts of information... through their bloodlines. Just like Immortal Emperor Supreme Truth back then, the information transmitted through bloodlines rode along the rollback, crossing lost history to arrive in official history."*

*"Then, within official history, passed down generation after generation through bloodlines, it provided support all the way to the future me."*

Continuing to sense the thoughts Desolate Ox Great Sage conveyed to him, a flash of realization crossed Zhang Yu's mind: *"And the Fourth Monster Saint, as a powerhouse who fought on despite repeated defeats and experienced multiple rollbacks, choosing different ways to resist, went a step further than the other powerhouses in the lost history, accomplishing an incredible feat..."*

Zhang Yu looked abruptly toward the Desolate Ox Great Sage, exclaiming in his mind in shock, "Starting from the Fourth Generation Monster Saint, every subsequent generation of Monster Saint passed down their memories when facing a rollback?"

"And until the moment you awakened, because the temporal node where I transmitted information about the future had already passed, the memories of the future regarding me truly surfaced deep within your sea of consciousness, no longer in danger of being forgotten or erased."

The Desolate Ox Great Sage nodded slightly and sighed with emotion. "Zhang Yu, do you know? Every single time, whenever we faced a rollback, the unwillingness in our hearts reached an absolute extreme. The power of the rollback... is far too immense. So immense that any cultivator facing it for the first time is utterly powerless to resist, and after being completely crushed, despair takes root in their hearts."

"When we discovered that sliver of possibility to fight against the rollback, can you imagine how joyful and full of anticipation we were in our hearts?"

Yet a trace of doubt surfaced in Zhang Yu's eyes. "Wait... Desolate Ox Great Sage, you only have a wisp of your consciousness remaining here and didn't preserve your own bloodline's physical body. How did you manage to recall the information transmitted through your bloodline?"

Desolate Ox Great Sage sighed and said through their telepathic connection, "The other mighty figures from the lost history could, at most, hide information within their bloodlines with the help of you and the Immortal Ancestor's Will. They waited for the future Immortal Ancestor's will to connect to their Bloodline Dao Lineage, deciphering these hidden messages one by one to provide support to the future."

"However, as monsterfolk, the generations of Monster Saints were far keener in their research and perception of bloodlines than human cultivators..."

A thought struck Zhang Yu, and he ventured a guess, "So... was it after the Fourth Monster Saint was rolled back that the Fifth Monster Saint, or perhaps the Sixth, detected the hidden information within the bloodline?"

"It was the Fifth Monster Saint," Desolate Ox said. "After undergoing the rollback, the Fourth Monster Saint lost his past memories, embarked on a different path, and became the Fifth Monster Saint."

"The Fifth Monster Saint sensed subtle changes in his own bloodline, so he began attempting to decipher those changes."

"He succeeded, yet he also failed. He succeeded because he managed to decipher a portion of the information. He failed because he remained unable to access the other part."

Zhang Yu instantly understood the thoughts conveyed by the Desolate Ox Great Sage and said, "When the Fifth Monster Saint performed the decryption, that exact point in time had already passed the point where the Fourth Monster Saint was subjected to the rollback. Thus, he retrieved the Fourth Monster Saint's memories and remembered everything about the Fourth Monster Saint."

Zhang Yu continued, "However, the future memories related to me were wiped out and forgotten on the spot. Ultimately, after losing a portion of them... he sealed and preserved them."

Thinking of this, Zhang Yu's heart gave a sudden jolt.

When the Fifth Monster Saint's acquired information contained only the Fourth Monster Saint's memories, but lacked the future memories related to Zhang Yu, what would he do?

Zhang Yu said, "Remembering his experience of being rolled back as the Fourth Monster Saint and perceiving the existence of the rollback, the Fifth Monster Saint would inevitably want to resist it."

The Desolate Ox nodded and said through its thoughts, "Correct. After decrypting the bloodline information, the Fifth Monster Saint learned of the rollback's existence. To fight against the Rollbacker, he pretended to know nothing and continued with his operations."

"As for the Fifth Monster Saint in the lost history, he forged a Pure Land to shelter a domain from the interference of subsequent Dao Lineages in order to study the underlying heavenly principles... all to find a way to resist the rollback and evade the influence of the Rollback Dao Lineage."

"Although the Fifth Monster Saint ultimately failed, he thoroughly mastered the Immortal Dao technology of transmitting and deciphering information through bloodlines, using it to pass messages to his post-rollback self."

"With this foundation, we were able to preserve our memories across rollback after rollback. And to counter the Rollbacker, we consistently pretended to know nothing about the rollbacks, for we knew that this was our only advantage..."

Listening to the Desolate Ox Great Sage's account, Zhang Yu thought to himself, *"The Monster Saints can preserve their rollback memories? Can the Desolate Ox Great Sage do so as well? Judging from the Desolate Ox's memories I previously viewed, there didn't seem to be any information regarding the rollbacks. Is it because the time node hasn't arrived yet? Or is there some other reason?"*

*"Furthermore, judging by the information the Celestial Monster Immortal Emperor just left for the Monster God, there seem to be contradictions here. Was the Celestial Monster Immortal Emperor mistaken? Or is what the Desolate Ox Great Sage is telling me right now incorrect?"*

Zhang Yu planned to ask the Desolate Ox Great Sage about this shortly. At the same time, the information the Desolate Ox Great Sage had provided caused a certain figure to suddenly emerge in his mind.

"Back then, after the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor grasped information about the rollbacks, he also lurked in secret, making preparations to confront the Rollbacker. It was just that the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor's advantage lay in the fact that he was not as conspicuous as the Monster Saints."

Then Zhang Yu thought of another question: "Desolate Ox Great Sage, if that's the case, doesn't that mean starting from the Fifth Monster Saint, all of you were influenced by the future me? Wouldn't that mean I have already changed history?"

The Desolate Ox Great Sage sighed. "It was only after I awakened and arrived in this era that I came to this conclusion. The history you find yourself in might already be one that has long been altered by you."

Zhang Yu was slightly taken aback. "What?"

The Desolate Ox Great Sage continued, "For example, in the process of fighting the Rollbacker, we might have saved someone... who should never have been saved."

Curious, Zhang Yu asked, "Who?"

The Desolate Ox Great Sage said, "After an unprecedented great rollback, a certain faction clashed fiercely with the Rollbacker. This faction was exceptionally powerful, and their familiarity with rollbacks far exceeded mine at the time. They were even able to retain their memories after a rollback without needing to wait for a temporal node."

"Yet this faction ultimately lost, completely defeated by the Rollbacker."

"Back then, in order to fight the Rollbacker, we decided to make contact with the leader of that faction... because the Immortal Dao Technology they possessed seemed, to us at the time, to be the only hope of countering the rollbacks."

"In the end, by utilizing bloodline technology, we passed down his memories through the bloodline of the human race, which ultimately awakened in a single individual. In a way, you could consider it a resurrection in disguised form."

Listening to Desolate Ox's account, Zhang Yu thought to himself, *"Memories awakening in future generations? Does that really count as a resurrection? Or is it transmigration? Or... is it merely a descendant inheriting the legacy and memories of a predecessor?"*

Zhang Yu felt that this sort of matter was impossible to clearly define in the moment.

Just as Zhang Yu was pondering, Desolate Ox Great Sage sighed emotionally and said, "Looking back now, that entity we resurrected in disguise was likely the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor."

At that moment, it was as if a bolt of lightning flashed through Zhang Yu's mind.

*"The Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor?"*

*"He was actually resurrected in disguised form by the Monster Saints?"*

*"And the Monster Saints only took those actions because they were influenced by the future me. Which means... in order to fight against the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor, I ended up creating the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor?!"*

In an instant, Zhang Yu's mind fell into utter chaos, the words Desolate Ox had just spoken echoing repeatedly in his thoughts.

"The history you reside in might have already been altered because of you long ago."

The Sect Examination Clone said in shock, "It was because of us? That the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor of today came to be?"

Immortal Slayer said incredulously, "Yet it was precisely because of the oppression from the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor and the others that we carried out that entire series of actions in the first place!"

The Desolate Ox Clone said, "History has already changed and formed a closed loop. As for whether the chicken or the egg came first... it is already impossible to tell."

At this moment, a tempest seemed to rage within Zhang Yu's heart. He was forced to suppress the myriad stray thoughts inside him, looking at the Desolate Ox Great Sage as he asked, "And then? Did you and the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor defeat the Rollbacker together?"

The Desolate Ox Great Sage shook his head. "No. Shortly after his memories returned, I tried to communicate with him in secret. But we had only exchanged a portion of Immortal Dao Technology when he vanished without a trace."

"Back then, we believed he must have felt that staying too close to us would make it too easy to draw the Rollbacker's gaze, so he hid himself in the shadows once more to build up his strength while he still wasn't drawing much attention."

"Still, just exchanging that portion of Immortal Dao Technology with him allowed us to make breakthroughs back then, enabling us to preserve rollback information within our divine souls, which was more covert and far safer than bloodline transmission."

"Unfortunately... it still failed to break through the limitation of the temporal nodes. All preserved rollback tnformation can only be accessed normally without being forgotten once the relevant rollback temporal node has been passed."

Zhang Yu recalled the records concerning the Sixth Monster Saint within the lost history: "Cast off flesh and blood, preserve only the spirit body, so that all living beings under heaven no longer possess distinctions of bloodline and are free from material needs..."

The Desolate Ox Great Sage said, "That was precisely what the Sixth Monster Saint did back in the lost history. On the surface, the goal was to eliminate bloodline distinctions, but in reality... we wanted to transform the divine souls of all living beings under heaven, enabling everyone to preserve rollback memories within their divine souls and become aware of the rollback's existence after passing the temporal nodes."

"Once everyone possesses rollback information, the advantages of a rollbacker will be weakened to the absolute limit."

Hearing this explanation from the Desolate Ox Great Sage, the Sect Examination Clone remarked, "That fellow Monster Saint always stirs up such massive commotions. Couldn't he just lie low, build up his strength, and then resist?"

Immortal Slayer replied, "The Monster Saint is different from the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor, after all. He was someone the Rollbacker already had their eyes on. Any slight move would draw their scrutiny, or even trigger a rollback. Therefore, he simply made a huge scene while using other plans as a cover."

Hearing Desolate Ox Great Sage's answer, a flash of realization crossed Zhang Yu's mind, and he said, "So it was the accumulations of the Sixth Monster Saint that allowed you to preserve the information within your own consciousness until fully awakening at this very moment?"

Then, Zhang Yu's brow furrowed slightly. "However, I also once browsed through some memories belonging to Desolate Ox Great Sage, and in those memories, Desolate Ox didn't seem to remember anything about the rollbacks."

After sensing the information conveyed by Zhang Yu, Desolate Ox Great Sage replied, "Because in the portion of memories you know, I had just experienced an exceptionally long rollback back then and hadn't yet passed the temporal nexus, so I hadn't awakened my rollback memories."

Desolate Ox Great Sage explained, "Furthermore, although the Fourth, Fifth, Sixth, and Seventh Monster Saints are all me, we are not entirely the same individual. Some are versions of me that walked different paths following a rollback, while others are versions of me that were completely suppressed and then revived in descendants through bloodline."

As Desolate Ox Great Sage's consciousness fluctuated, more information surged toward Zhang Yu, granting him a deeper understanding of Desolate Ox's experiences.

Zhang Yu summarized in his heart, *"So that's how it is..."*

*"Only after enduring failure after failure did the Desolate Ox Great Sage awaken his rollback memories."*

*"And it was also at that moment that he discovered..."*

The Desolate Ox Great Sage said, "Everything I encountered seemed like a setup. This game... was engineered by the Rollbacker constantly influencing the world, nudging me toward self-division. While dealing with him, I secretly pondered ways to break the deadlock."

"And during that period, I noticed the attention on me began to dwindle, and the various guidance and orchestrations directed at me started to decrease."

"Besides that, I also discovered a drastic change in the Rollbacker's style."

"The rollbacks grew increasingly frequent, and their spans became progressively shorter. I guessed back then that he might have run into something that required him to roll back repeatedly."

As Zhang Yu felt the Desolate Ox Great Sage's thoughts, he summarized in his heart, *"The Desolate Ox Great Sage sensed that the Rollbacker at the time might have been occupied with something else, unable to focus his attention here."*

*"Therefore, the Desolate Ox went along with the situation and split himself, producing the future Celestial Monster Immortal Emperor. In the eyes of the Desolate Ox Great Sage, the Celestial Monster Immortal Emperor — who had no rollback memories and grew his strength unscrupulously — was the perfect target."*

Seeing this, Zhang Yu sighed inwardly: *"Desolate Ox Great Sage and Celestial Monster Immortal Emperor both believed themselves to be the main body, viewing the other as merely a separated part of themselves? However, considering that Desolate Ox Great Sage possesses more memories — especially holding all kinds of information regarding the Monster Saint and the rollbacks — he ought to be the true main body."*

*"Meanwhile, not only is Celestial Monster Immortal Emperor missing a great deal of memories, but he also possesses an erroneous understanding of their split, perhaps having even been implanted with false memories woven by Desolate Ox Great Sage."*

Sect Examination Clone added, *"Yet in the end, Desolate Ox Great Sage was clearly the one who became the target, while Celestial Monster Immortal Emperor ascended to the supreme throne."*

Immortal Slayer could not help asking, "Desolate Ox Great Sage, what exactly happened?"

Desolate Ox Great Sage said, "Just when I thought I could use Celestial Monster Immortal Emperor as cover, for some reason... the Rollbacker sensed that I was aware of the rollback. At the time, I was never sure where I had slipped up, but looking back now, I fear I was betrayed by Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor."

"Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor hid in the shadows and used me as bait to draw the Rollbacker's attention, subjecting me to a relentless series of strikes."

"Celestial Monsters also took advantage of this opportunity to rapidly expand their strength..."

Zhang Yu summarized, "So Myriad Laws used you to defeat the rollback? Could it even be that he had already joined forces with Celestial Monsters back then, using you together as cover for them?"

Unfortunately, the Desolate Ox Great Sage had not observed the battle between the Rollbacker and the others with his own eyes. Still unaware of what had specifically transpired, he could only guess.

Before a victor was decided between the two sides, Desolate Ox had already been sealed away.

During that long seal, the Desolate Ox Great Sage had barely awakened once. At that time, he discovered that the entire Kunxu had already fallen under the rule of the Ten Great Sects. After that, he sank completely into a deep slumber until waking up today.

At this thought, Zhang Yu's mind stirred again. *"Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor should have known that the Desolate Ox Great Sage and the others were aware of the rollback. But did the other Immortal Emperors know?"*

Recalling the arrangements and actions of each Immortal Emperor, a suspicion arose in Zhang Yu's heart. *"Could it be that Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor concealed this from the other Immortal Emperors?"*

Meanwhile, the gaze the Desolate Ox Great Sage directed at Zhang Yu grew increasingly complicated.

After the Desolate Ox Great Sage awakened today, the future information related to Zhang Yu had thoroughly revived within his consciousness. Over this brief span of time, he had gradually digested it all, stitching it together with the memories of the Fourth Monster Saint, the Fifth Monster Saint, the Sixth Monster Saint, and Desolate Ox himself from the lost history.

Their failures, their regrets, their grievances, their hopes... all crossed time and space, surging into the heart of the newly awakened Desolate Ox.

And the person who had sparked all this hope and entanglement was none other than the Zhang Yu standing before him.

At this moment, having gained insight into the past and understood the present, the Desolate Ox Great Sage finally realized everything.

A flash of understanding crossed the Desolate Ox Great Sage's mind: *"A long time ago, we received a distress message from Zhang Yu."*

*"Over all these years, we have endured countless lives and deaths, traversed countless rolled-back lost histories, and finally stood before Zhang Yu today."*

*"I have also finally understood the meaning of it all. It was all so that we could join him in this future era to stop the Ultimate Doomsday."*

Just as the Desolate Ox Great Sage's thoughts flashed like lightning, Zhang Yu sensed the other party's consciousness encircling him, sending a request to link their minds together.

"The Fourth, Fifth, Sixth, and Seventh Monster Saints have arrived upon your invitation. Greetings, Daoist Friend Zhang."
