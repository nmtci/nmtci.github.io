# Chapter 1002 The Sky Darkens Over Kunxu

Hearing Zhang Yu mention the words "Immortal Emperor," Yu Xinghan was momentarily stunned.

Only after replaying the words repeatedly in his mind and confirming that he had not misheard did Yu Xinghan ask in a trembling voice, "Minister, are you... currently in contact with the Immortal Emperor?"

Zhang Yu replied, "Yes."

Upon seeing Zhang Yu's response, Yu Xinghan almost could not resist dropping to his knees, and he could not help but think, "Could the Immortal Emperor be observing Minister Zhang right this moment? Might His gaze have even swept over to me as well?"

Although Yu Xinghan was currently in the lower realm and merely speaking with Zhang Yu over a long distance, to Yu Xinghan's mind... it was entirely possible for the Immortal Emperor's gaze to sweep past along the Spirit Realm.

Meanwhile, on the other end, just after Zhang Yu sent his email, he received the Immortal Emperor's reply.

Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor: "Acknowledged. In the future, report minor matters of this sort to Jing Hui; there is no need to petition Us directly."

Seeing that the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor had sent Jing Hui's contact information, Zhang Yu added her as a contact.

Zhang Yu still remembered that she was the Immortal Emperor's secretary whom he had met on the 33rd layer. He understood well that many trivial matters were indeed inconvenient to bring directly before the Immortal Emperor.

*"This secretary to the Immortal Emperor should be more than enough to handle most matters."*

Just then, Jing Hui sent a message: "Minister Zhang, I am already aware of the situation."

"In principle... any sect cultivator wishing to head to the lower realm must obtain approval from the respective halls."

The next moment, Jing Hui shifted her tone: "However, Minister Zhang, you have the Immortal Emperor's special approval. So, I exercised a little bit of my authority and directly got you a pre-processed permit. You can just fill it out as you see fit."

Looking at the file Jing Hui sent over, Zhang Yu had just accepted it when he saw a Lower Realm Travel Permit materialize before him.

The permit was already stamped and approved by the Immortal Ascension Hall, but fields such as name, position, cultivation realm, and which layer of the lower realm he was traveling to were all left completely blank.

Even with all his experience, Zhang Yu was still slightly taken aback when he saw this blank Lower Realm Travel Permit.

Yet, remembering Jing Hui's identity, he quickly understood.

"The so-called ironclad laws of Kunxu, strictly controlling each layer, are nothing more than flimsy paper waiting to be punctured in the hands of authority at the Immortal Emperor's level."

Even as he lamented in his mind, Zhang Yu swiftly filled out the details on the Lower Realm Travel Permit.

Watching this, Fu Ji could not help but sigh with emotion. "Ah, having the Immortal Emperor's support truly puts one's heart at ease."

Zhang Yu had to admit that Fu Ji was right. Although he had climbed all the way up from the 1st layer, receiving support from high-ranking cultivators, guidance from seniors, and assistance from companions along the way, the one who had brought him the greatest sense of security over all these years... was still the Immortal Emperor.

"That's why I need to use the Immortal Emperor's momentum to grow."

Zhang Yu thought to himself, "The Immortal Emperor thinks of us as his Myriad Laws Divine Sword, but isn't the Immortal Emperor... also our Myriad Laws Divine Sword?"

The Sect Examination Clone praised, "Indeed! Even though the Immortal Emperor wants to consume us, he has to fatten us up before doing so. Meanwhile, we can already enjoy the benefits he brings, which means we hold the advantage right from the start! Using the Immortal Emperor, I shall suppress all enemies under heaven!"

---

Kunxu, Fifth Layer.

Just as Yu Xinghan ended his call with Zhang Yu, Divine Monarch Heavenly Slaughter beside him asked, "Dao Monarch Yu, what did Minister Zhang say?"

Divine Monarch Heavenly Slaughter was now the principal of Myriad Laws University, but facing Yu Xinghan, who was once a student of the university, he dared not put on any airs whatsoever.

After all, Yu Xinghan was now an upper realm Sect Staff member with a cultivation realm higher than his own, as well as one of Minister Zhang's trusted aides.

And Divine Monarch Heavenly Slaughter was all too eager to advance his career.

Yu Xinghan did not share the contents of the conversation with Divine Monarch Heavenly Slaughter, merely stating directly, "In a moment... if the Righteous Gods really make a move and both sides clash, I will step in to stop the fight."

Divine Monarch Heavenly Slaughter looked at Yu Xinghan in surprise.

When Yu Xinghan had descended to the lower realm earlier, the riot was already underway. Even though Yu Xinghan possessed Void Refinement strength, intercepting the Righteous Gods as they suppressed the riot... was a textbook violation of laws and regulations.

Yet, if he were to let things take their course and watch the intensity of the incident escalate further, that would not be a good thing either.

Yu Xinghan understood in his heart that a single campus in the Fifth Layer meant nothing to the sect. Even if everyone inside were to die right now, he believed Zhang Yu could suppress the fallout.

However, if someone made an issue out of this and used it as ammunition to attack Zhang Yu, the repercussions could be trivial or catastrophic.

"A campus in the Fifth Layer is nothing to the sect, but it holds immense influence over the lower realm."

"And the entire expanse from layers 1 to 10 exists solely to nourish the sect and supply it with cheap materials."

This was how Kunxu was constructed, layer upon layer, with each lower rank sustaining the one above it. Every layer supported the higher layers, funneling upward from Qi Refining all the way to Ascension... ultimately forming a productive force capable of empowering the Immortal Class.

Yu Xinghan thought to himself, *"From a price perspective, the lower layers might seem insignificant to the sect, but they are one of the foundations maintaining Kunxu's structure."*

*"As long as a high-ranking cultivator pushes for it, it wouldn't be impossible for them to use this riot to hold us accountable for dereliction of duty."*

Yu Xinghan knew that although Zhang Yu was formidable now, he also had no shortage of rivals and enemies within the sect.

That was why he had contacted Zhang Yu earlier, hoping he could leverage his influence to find a way to suppress the matter as quickly as possible.

However, after finishing his call with Zhang Yu, Yu Xinghan's mindset had completely shifted.

Straightening his back, he declared with righteous indignation, *"With duty upon my shoulders, how could I sit by and watch the incident escalate? How could I allow the losses to expand?"*

Yu Xinghan thought to himself, *"I wonder if Minister Zhang... or perhaps even the Immortal Emperor is observing this place right now? I need to put in extra effort in a moment."*

"Dao Monarch..." Divine Monarch Heavenly Slaughter glanced up at the radiance of the Righteous Gods gathering in the sky and said, "If the Righteous Gods refuse to let this go, do you plan to clash with them directly?"

Yu Xinghan said indifferently, "Me, avoid their edge? Heavenly Slaughter, remember this: if you're afraid of taking responsibility, don't become a Sect Staff member."

He thought to himself, *"In the sect's internal struggles, I still had to be apprehensive and control all kinds of costs. But now, with the Immortal Emperor at my back, marching down to wage war on the lower realm, who can possibly stop me?"*

At the same time, above the campus ahead, layers upon layers of divine light descended. Countless Righteous Gods clad in golden armor began to emerge like an army of thousands upon thousands of soldiers and horses, gradually encircling the rioting campus.

A cultivator at the Nascent Soul Realm stepped upon an auspicious cloud and arrived in the sky above the campus. As soon as he spoke, rolling thunder swept across the area: "I am Wu Zhongxiao. The inspection work for this campus... is overseen by me."

Divine Monarch Heavenly Slaughter said, "Dao Monarch Yu, should we stop them?"

Yu Xinghan replied indifferently, "No need. Let's see what he actually intends to do."

Amid the commotion in the campus, Wu Zhongxiao had already bellowed in anger, "Listen up, all of you! Collecting the books is sect policy — it benefits the sect above and benefits you below! I just don't understand, how could such a tremendously good thing fail to be implemented?"

"Now that the Righteous Gods are about to descend, how dare you still gather in a mob to resist?"

"Let me tell you, this is an affair personally ordered by Profound Monarch Zhang, Zhang Yu! If you keep resisting, you're costing the sect money, and you're slapping Profound Monarch Zhang in the face! Why haven't you surrendered without a fight already?"

At this moment, within the campus grounds below, there were Qi Refining junior college students, Foundation Establishment undergraduates, graduated Golden Core employees, and even Nascent Soul Realm leaders from companies or schools, as well as prisoners from the Overtime Institute.

Coming from all sorts of different places, they had all gathered here after receiving notices requiring them to undergo screening to determine whether they were holders of a Book.

Chang Buxiu stood among the crowd. He used to be a worker at a factory that manufactured spatial storage magic artifacts, and during the University War, he had even followed Zhang Yu... participating in the Ten Billion Heaven Mending project.

He had no idea what a Book was, but recalling everything that had happened over this recent period as he looked up at Wu Zhongxiao in the sky, his eyes were already burning with fury.

Initially, when everyone heard that this was a task arranged by Zhang Yu, they had all been extremely cooperative.

After all, the cultivators from the 2nd to 5th layers had lived through the University War. Many of them had received Zhang Yu's red packets and bought Yu Bonds, placing immense trust in him.

On top of that, subsequent news continuously arrived about Zhang Yu's meteoric rise within the sect, as well as his recruitment of lower realm cultivators into the sect...

It could be said that throughout the entire 2nd to 5th layers, Zhang Yu possessed immense prestige and commanded widespread support.

However, as the detention and interrogations dragged on, the cultivators were unable to work and earn money. Situations gradually arose involving overdue debts, insufficient cultivation resources, and even outright bankruptcy...

Yet no matter how they pleaded, the only response they received was: "No one may leave without authorization before passing the review."

Hearing Wu Zhongxiao's shout at this moment, Chang Buxiu thought furiously to himself, "If we continue to be detained like this, everyone will go bankrupt!"

Yet looking at the surging divine light in the sky, a flicker of despair rose in Chang Buxiu's heart once more. "So many Righteous Gods have come?"

"Surrendering means bankruptcy, and not surrendering means bankruptcy as well..."

Faced with Wu Zhongxiao's intimidating shout, indignation surged throughout the entire campus, and streaks of magic power, spiritual energy, and Gang Qi soared into the air.

Pushed to the brink by bankruptcy and death, even these Kunxu cultivators, who had always abided by the rules, seemed to erupt with the courage to fight to the death at this moment.

All at once, one shout after another echoed across the entire campus.

"If you keep detaining us, we'll all go bankrupt!"

"You forced our hand!"

"Where is Profound Monarch Zhuyi! We want to see Profound Monarch Zhuyi!"

Watching this scene, Yu Xinghan thought to himself, *"With their aspiration for the Immortal Dao driving everyone to pursue the apex of the Immortal Dao, they were willing to abide by Kunxu's rules for its sake. Yet, for that very same reason, they can erupt with desperate courage to fight to the death."*

At the same time, as layers upon layers of Righteous Gods descended from the sky, surging divine power began to converge. Inside the campus... warnings flashed before the eyes of countless cultivators all at once.

"Please shut down your physical bodies immediately and cooperate with law enforcement!"

Watching this scene, Yu Xinghan thought, *"It's my turn to make a move."*

He took a step forward, just about to make his entrance, when he saw the divine light surge even more violently as the number of descending Righteous Gods multiplied rapidly. Yu Xinghan was startled, "One thousand... five thousand... ten thousand... How could so many Righteous Gods have come?"

The sky looked as if a massive rift had been torn open, within which colossal divine silhouettes the size of mountains emerged.

Information flashed wildly across Yu Xinghan's Bionic Eye as he thought, *"Is this... the Righteous Gods reverently invoking the divine power of the Eight Divisions Great Gods? Are they about to cast a Great God Talisman?"*

He could not help but say, "Wait... is it really necessary to make such a huge fuss just to deal with some vocational college and undergraduate cultivators?"

At this moment, all the cultivators across the entire campus were instantly locked onto, every part of their bodies tagged with a price.

As the talisman activated, once the divine power descended, every inch of their flesh and blood, every strand of their magic power, every shred of their consciousness, every trace of their memory... would all be disassembled, transformed, reclaimed, and sold off, ultimately becoming currency drifting in the Spirit Realm.

And any force that attempted to resist would be directly suppressed by the ten thousand Righteous Gods in the sky and the Great God Divine Power they had humbly invoked.

Hearing Yu Xinghan's words, Divine Monarch Heavenly Slaughter explained, "Because of the lessons learned from the University War, the Heavenly Court established a dedicated Rapid Reaction Force in the lower realm. They can directly request the empowerment of the Heavenly Court Great Gods' divine power, maintain zero tolerance for all riots, and strike like thunder with absolute force."

"However, since the end of the University War, this is the very first riot to occur in the lower realm."

He reminded, "Dao Monarch Yu, if you try to stop them, I'm afraid you will also be treated as one of the rioters..."

Looking at the divine power of the great gods coiling in the sky, Yu Xinghan let out a bitter laugh and hurriedly sent the situation on-site to Zhang Yu. In the next moment, Yu Xinghan abruptly blocked the sky above the campus and said, "Righteous Gods, I am Yu Xinghan of the Myriad Laws Sect's Heavenly Surveillance Hall. I am in charge of the matter here..."

The leading Righteous God gave him no face whatsoever, saying coldly, "Dao Monarch Yu, they have occupied the campus, threatened production, and ignored the Righteous Gods' deterrence, severely disrupting the order of Kunxu! This is an outright act of terrorism! It must be suppressed immediately. Even your Heavenly Surveillance Hall has no right to interfere. Please withdraw at once."

Seeing that Yu Xinghan was still blocking the airspace above the campus, the Righteous God could not help but shout, "Yu Xinghan, are you going to participate in this riot as well?"

Looking over this Righteous God's data, Yu Xinghan said, "Inspector God Jia, Profound Monarch Zhang Yu will be here shortly. Please wait a moment..."

Inspector God Jia said coldly, "Suppressing all riots is an ironclad law of the Heavenly Court! Let alone Profound Monarch Zhang, even if he were Heavenly Monarch Zhang, it would be useless."

Right at that moment, light and shadow flashed in the atmosphere as invisible Earthly Fiend Gravity converged, and a figure stepped out from within.

This was a figure imbued with a peerless daoist presence. Every move and gesture of the figure, much like the Immortal Dao itself, exuded an allure to all cultivators.

Moreover, the face of this figure was exceedingly familiar to numerous cultivators. 

It was solely because, ever since the start of the University War, countless cultivators had seen his appearance time and time again in various videos across the Spirit Realm.

"Zhang... Profound Monarch Zhang!"

Chang Buxiu had just grown excited, but a wave of panic struck him the moment he recalled his current predicament.

*"We obstructed Profound Monarch Zhang's plan. Will he suppress us as well?"*

Meanwhile, gazing at Zhang Yu's figure, Yu Xinghan felt a slight wave of relief in his heart, along with an emerging sense of security.

Yet at the same time, he could not help but wonder: *"Why does the Minister... smell kind of sweet now?"*

At that moment, Zhang Yu glanced over the information of the Righteous God before him and said, "Inspector God Jia, I will be taking charge here next..."

Inspector God Jia thought to himself, *"Are you here to cover this up? Hmph, why should I help you conceal a mess caused by your Heavenly Surveillance Hall?"*

*"With so many Righteous Gods present now and the entire law enforcement process being recorded, do you expect me to disobey orders and stop suppressing the riot?"*

In the eyes of Inspector God Jia, Zhang Yu had rushed over solely to cover up this riot.

However, the riot had already been recorded in the Heavenly Court's archives, and he had been dispatched specifically to suppress it. How could he possibly turn back just because of a few words from the other man?

Seemingly noticing Inspector God Jia's resolve, Zhang Yu said, "Wait a moment. Let me contact someone."

Inspector God Jia said indifferently, "Profound Monarch Zhang, this venerable one is enforcing the law impartially. It will be useless no matter who you contact! Rioting is an absolute crime! Poverty is no excuse!"

He added, "As for your interception of our efforts to suppress the riot, I will record it in full and submit it to the Heavenly Court."

Yet in the very next moment, an avatar of a woman materialized before Inspector God Jia's eyes, and she spoke, "This is the Jade Pivot Directorate on the 33rd layer. I am Jing Hui, scribe of the Jade Pivot Directorate..."

Inspector God Jia froze slightly, thinking to himself, *"The 33rd layer? Why are they contacting me?"*

His first reaction... was that this was a fraudulent transmission.

However, after sensing the communication's Spirit Realm Certificate, he confirmed that this call indeed originated from the 33rd layer.

*"The Jade Pivot Directorate — isn't that the secretarial staff of the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor? A scribe of the Jade Pivot Directorate is a Rank 11 chief secretary, the grand steward of the Myriad Laws Sect... Jing Hui, the number one Ghost Immortal of Myriad Laws."*

Inspector God Jia was shocked in his heart, yet he also understood... Though the Heavenly Court and the sects had their own duties, they all ultimately served under the Immortal Emperors. And Jing Hui, as the scribe of the Jade Pivot Directorate on the 33rd Floor, represented an Immortal Emperor's will.

At this moment, Jing Hui's thoughts continued to transmit into Inspector God Jia's mind: *"This riot was merely a false alarm. Cooperate with Zhang Yu to clean up the aftermath properly, and defer to his opinions as much as possible."*

Inspector God Jia felt his thoughts stall for a moment, a wave of dizziness washing over his mind as four words surfaced: *Trespassing Upon Immortal Turf*.

Then, Inspector God Jia glanced at the occupied campus and thought to himself, *"This counts as a false alarm? Can even riots and rebellions... be swept under the rug like this nowadays?"*

Just as he was fretting over his orders from the Heavenly Court, he saw on his quest panel that the content of the mission he had accepted had completely changed. From suppressing a riot, it had turned into assisting Zhang Yu in patrolling the Fifth Layer.

Jia felt a tremor in his heart: *"The sky of Kunxu has turned dark..."*

As the communication ended, the gaze with which Inspector God Jia looked at Zhang Yu changed entirely.

Inspector God Jia said, "My apologies, Minister Zhang, I mistook the mission just now."

As he spoke, he had already dropped to his knees, thinking to himself, *"There's a crowd right now, so kneeling will look even more effective."*

Inspector God Jia continued, "We are here to fully cooperate with you. Just tell me whatever demands you have."

While speaking, Inspector God Jia sent a friend request to Zhang Yu, attaching 1,000,000 Immortal Coins along with it.

"Get up first." Zhang Yu accepted the friend request but returned the 1,000,000 Immortal Coins, saying, "And cut out this kind of nonsense. We have rules of discipline."

Filled with solemn respect, Inspector God Jia claimed he had only wanted to support sect construction and meant nothing else by it.

A moment later, Yu Xinghan sent a message to Inspector God Jia, informing him that if he wished to assist sect construction, he could purchase Immortal Crystals to donate to the Immortal Grotto-Heaven.

Yu Xinghan: "Extensive civil engineering works are currently underway in the Immortal Grotto-Heaven to construct the sect's newest transportation hub..."

Meanwhile, the Great God's divine power in the sky had dissipated without anyone noticing.

Zhang Yu swept his gaze across the area and looked over at Wu Zhongxiao not far away, frowning as he asked, "Are you the one in charge of overseeing the vetting process here?"

At first glance, Zhang Yu knew the man was merely a temporarily hired outsourced worker — an outsourced worker earning a daily wage of 1,000 Spirit Coins.

Wu Zhongxiao hurried forward to greet him, saying with a nervous expression, "Profound Monarch Zhang, these low-tier cultivators refuse to submit to discipline..."

Zhang Yu interrupted, "Who told you to round up so many people?"

Wu Zhongxiao replied, "Profound Monarch, ever since we learned that the Books were to be retrieved, we haven't dared to rest for even a moment. We only wished to ease your burdens, Profound Monarch, and locate the Books as quickly as possible. That is why we gathered them here..."

Zhang Yu said indifferently, "You drove so many cultivators to riot, and you call that easing my burdens?"

Wu Zhongxiao fell to his knees on the auspicious cloud. "Profound Monarch, the Books are rare treasure of heaven and earth! We were terrified of missing a single suspect, which would cause a loss of sect assets and result in our failure to complete the task you assigned to us, Profound Monarch. That is the only reason we detained them all here!"

*"The task I assigned?"*

Zhang Yu shook his head. He knew that the Heavenly Surveillance Hall's investigation into the Books involved very few suspects, and their targeted leads were almost all exceptionally precise.

*"For this to escalate step by step to such an extent, using my name as a pretext to launch such a wide-scale screening, there must be someone fanning the flames behind the scenes."*

Zhang Yu said, "Yu Xinghan, I leave him to you. Find out who is stirring up trouble."

Yu Xinghan nodded, reaching out and instantly suppressing Wu Zhongxiao on the spot.

Zhang Yu looked down at the campus below, and the numerous cultivators inside were also looking up at him at this moment.

Seeing how Zhang Yu had first blocked a Righteous God and then suppressed Wu Zhongxiao, a glimmer of hope gradually appeared in the eyes of the cultivators.

Zhang Yu simply said, "Everyone, I have returned."

Chang Buxiu knelt on the ground. "Profound Monarch Zhang! You have finally arrived."

Zhang Yu understood in his heart that although the vast majority of the cultivators below had been driven to riot, there were bound to be some who had acted excessively or even taken the opportunity to commit crimes during the commotion.

However, with Kunxu's powerful surveillance technology, they could all be investigated and identified one by one.

As for the majority of the cultivators who had merely joined in the chaos, Zhang Yu intended to let them leave.

Following Zhang Yu's arrangements, cheers erupted throughout the entire campus, and expressions of joy appeared on the faces of many cultivators. None of them had expected that after participating in a riot, they would be let off so easily.

Yet, a person's reputation precedes them. Across all of Kunxu's 1st to 10th layers... had anyone else said these words, the cultivators present would likely not have believed them so readily, and might have even suspected it to be a trap.

Only Zhang Yu was different. As an upper-tier cultivator who had once saved Kunxu's 2nd to 5th layers, accomplished the Ten Billion Heaven Mending Plan, established the Yu Bonds, and led countless people to fortune, Zhang Yu enjoyed a universal, stellar reputation in the 2nd to 5th layers.

Midair, Divine Monarch Heavenly Slaughter also flew up swiftly to greet him.

Zhang Yu said, "Heavenly Slaughter, you've arrived right on time. The losses of this campus will be covered by Myriad Laws University. As for the specific repair work... that can be handed over to the Civil Engineering Department."

"If additional workers need to be hired, you can hire these cultivators below."

Divine Monarch Heavenly Slaughter nodded. To him, this was a piece of cake.

Following this arrangement, the crowd of low-tier cultivators below — who had been out of work for a long time — erupted into another round of cheers.

"✊ Blazing Light ✊"

Some cultivators thought to themselves, "Profound Monarch Zhang... is truly so reliable. I really want to take the Sect Examination to enter his tutelage."

There were even cultivators who wanted to kill themselves on the spot to see if they could become Zhang Yu's Soul Cultivators, but they were immediately stopped.

Next, Zhang Yu looked toward Inspector God Jia and said, "As for the subsequent work, I will trouble you to cooperate with Yu Xinghan, Inspector God Jia..."

He looked at Yu Xinghan and said, "Xinghan, you stay behind to oversee the lower realm for now, and don't let the people below stir up any more trouble."

After handing down the follow-up tasks, Zhang Yu returned once more to the sect to continue his cultivation.

Not long after, Yu Xinghan sent a message to Zhang Yu: "Minister, I've looked into the matter. There are many contributing factors, but it's essentially just excessive escalation at every level, expanding the scope of suspects tier by tier. It's hard to say which specific person is leading it."

"If I had to say, I feel that those cultivators in the sect who haven't handed over their Books might have something to do with them."

A list materialized in Zhang Yu's mind, with names spanning numerous factions across the Myriad Laws Sect.

Looking at these names, Zhang Yu understood clearly that these cultivators were currently holding tightly to the Books in their possession. They were simply holding out for the best price, waiting for his side to make an offer to collect those Books, just like Liu Qianxian had done before.

"These guys are like holdouts facing demolition, all waiting for me to negotiate terms with them."

Having read Liu Qianxian's mission reports, Zhang Yu knew that more often than not, what these people wanted wasn't merely money. On the surface, they might only ask for a few tens or hundreds of thousands of Immortal Coins, but secretly, they would also demand other Immortal Dao resources and sect resources.

Just then, Zhang Yu's eyes flickered slightly as he noticed a change in the information on the Feather Book.

"Has Senior Sister Ye Xingli's second Dao Seed leveled up?"

Zhang Yu had ultimately bestowed the second Dao Seed nurtured by the Feather Book this year upon Ye Xingli.

After all, Zhang Yu intended to push Ye Xingli to break through to the Void Refinement Realm next year and participate in the Third-Generation Law Establishment.

Much like Magnetic Pole's situation, the preparation time given to Ye Xingli was simply too short. Therefore, Zhang Yu had directly helped her obtain a second Dao Seed, allowing her to equip two sets of Chain Formation Diagrams, vastly increasing her Immortal Dao Potential.

"Senior Sister Ye Xingli's first Dao Seed the Spirit Resonance Artifact Heart has reached level 5, allowing her to fuse five artifact refinement blueprints in one go."

"Now her second Dao Seed has reached level 5 as well..."

Ye Xingli's second Dao Seed was called Myriad Treasures Furnace, an ability capable of fusing physical magic artifacts.

"A level 5 Myriad Treasures Furnace can fuse Spirit Transformation-tier magic artifacts. If used in combat... it can degrade an opponent's magic artifact by fusing it with an inferior one, or enhance her own high-quality magic artifacts by fusing them together."

"However, its most important application is in research and development. Compared to the blueprint fusion of Spirit Resonance Artifact Heart, the physical artifact fusion of Myriad Treasures Furnace can drastically speed up experimentation and testing. Working together, the two can further enhance the efficiency of product iteration."

Thoughts flashed through Zhang Yu's mind, and he quickly analyzed the entire situation.

At the same time, a meeting notification from the Immortal Ascension Hall interrupted his train of thought.

"Is it about the Technique-Chaining Comprehensive Manual?"

Zhang Yu knew in his heart that the policy regarding the creation of Chain Formation Diagrams had finally been finalized.

He knew that the momentum he had been waiting for had finally arrived.
