# Chapter 1043 Offering Loyalty, Ancient God?

Everything happened just as the Great God of Sentient Creation had said. The Immortals' duel did not last long, quickly shifting to the negotiation stage as another round of maneuvering over their respective interests began.

Meanwhile, Zhang Yu smoothly returned to the 19th layer, where he was met with a grand welcoming ceremony.

When the Immortals' power had first projected into layers 1 through 19, most cultivators hadn't even realized what was going on. 

Only after that power receded, accompanied by various announcements, notices, and words of reassurance, did more and more cultivators realize what had happened, which in turn gave rise to a lingering dread.

Although there was the explanation that the "Crown Prince of Myriad Laws shouted back the multitude of Immortals," the prestige and authority the Immortals had accumulated over the ages still left many cultivators deeply unsettled.

What if the Immortals attacked again?

What if a prolonged war broke out between the Upper Realm and the Immortal Realm?

The Crown Prince had not yet achieved immortality — could he truly keep holding off the Immortals forever?

Would other sects take advantage of the chaos to swoop in and profit?

Zhang Yu knew in his heart that he could not allow this panic to spread.

Fu Ji said, "Indeed. Prolonged panic will affect economic growth, and poor economic growth will impact everyone's cultivation of the Immortal Dao, dragging down the growth rate of the sect's total cultivation. How then could they elevate us?"

Therefore, Zhang Yu quickly had the news released, announcing that he had reached a cooperation agreement and begun negotiations with the Immortals, all to soothe public sentiment.

Zhang Yu also specially ordered Chief Soul Cultivator Tianxing to arrange a grand welcome ceremony to further spread positive news and stabilize market confidence and hope within the sect.

Thus, when Zhang Yu returned to the 19th layer under the escort of the two Great Gods and two Reformist Faction Immortals, he was greeted by procession after procession of cultivators cheering for him.

Zhao Tianxing thought to himself, *"A total of 19 welcome formations, each manned by Myriad Laws cultivator representatives from layers 1 through 19, symbolizing that the cultivators of every layer are united firmly behind His Royal Highness the Crown Prince."*

Meanwhile, Zhao Tianxing's voice bridged the Spirit Realm, reaching the ears of the cultivators within each formation.

"Everyone, all the major platforms are livestreaming right now," Zhao Tianxing said. "Remember our rehearsal arrangements and do not make a single mistake."

"Think carefully about how, if not for His Royal Highness the Crown Prince's immense kindness and virtue, you wouldn't have this peaceful life today where you can earn money in peace and security every day."

In truth, according to the original Kunxu conventions, Zhao Tianxing had wanted to arrange for all layers of Kunxu to donate money and pay tribute together to His Royal Highness the Crown Prince.

"Only by welcoming the Crown Prince with Immortal Coins Filling the Sky can we match His Highness's monumental achievements."

By all accounts, this was standard practice in Kunxu, yet the moment Zhao Tianxing submitted the proposal, Zhang Yu rejected it.

Zhao Tianxing was startled. Looking back over the proposal, a trace of alarm rose in his heart. *"How have I become like this?"*

*"To fail to correctly fathom the Crown Prince's intentions? Am I growing out of sync with His Highness?"*

Zhao Tianxing immediately focused his mind, rapidly reading through Zhang Yu's life story ten times. Only then did he rearrange the welcoming ceremony, stripping away every bit of content regarding donations and tributes.

And at this very moment, as the delegations from the 1st to the 19th layers went into motion, strains of ceremonial music echoed across heaven and earth.

Meanwhile, the Righteous Gods responsible for law enforcement within the Myriad Laws Sect also manifested projections one after another, lining up to welcome Zhang Yu's return to the sect.

One of the Righteous Gods noticed the two Reformist Faction Immortals accompanying Zhang Yu and immediately moved to salute them, only to be stopped by another Righteous God beside him.

"What are you saluting for? Didn't you see the reason His Highness the God-Seizing King gave when he moved to dismiss the Great God of Heavenly Covenant and Equal Contract? Having no principles and engaging in blatant Immortal worship!"

Ever since Zhang Yu became the Heavenly Court God-Seizing King, holding the highest Heavenly Court authority among the Ten Great Sects, more and more Righteous Gods had begun to study his habits and figure out his preferences.

At the same time, the entire sky lit up as rows of Spirit Realm bullet chat were projected, twinkling between heaven and earth like a sea of stars.

Feng Tingting, who was currently livestreaming, said, "Approaching next is the Spirit Realm Bullet Chat Formation, spontaneously organized by the major sect enterprises and companies of the Myriad Laws Sect to welcome and thank the Crown Prince. It symbolizes all enterprises standing firmly united by His Highness the Crown Prince's side, basking in the profound grace of his boundless favor."

"First to perform a bullet chat variation are the representatives from the Former Days Graveyard, Soul Cultivator Company..."

"Following behind the corporate representatives are retired cultivators who have come to present flowers. Every single one of these retired Void Refinement Cultivators has already ascended to leisure, representing the past of the Myriad Laws Sect as well as the success of the Myriad Laws dream..."

"Next, His Highness the Crown Prince is about to deliver a speech. The sect's general trends and industrial landscape for at least the coming year will be closely tied to it, so listen closely, everyone. Whether you can make a fortune or not depends on how much you can understand..."

"Look, the two Immortals from the Reformist Faction are following right beside the Crown Prince! What's more, they haven't bestowed any immortal fortune, nor have they collected any Immortal Coins. This means even they have to abide by the new rules set by the Crown Prince, proving that the Crown Prince's negotiations were a resounding success!"

As Zhang Yu officially began his speech, the entire expanse from the 1st to the 19th layers tuned in to listen to his voice.

"Everyone, I have just concluded my negotiations with the Immortals."

"We have reached many crucial accords, finalized new trade agreements, and established a new relationship between mortals and Immortals. This will be a historic moment etched into the annals of history..."

Watching this scene unfold through the live broadcast, Dugu Ming mused inwardly, *"Attending the meeting alone to face the multitude of Immortals, consolidating his own authority..."*

At this moment, in Dugu Ming's eyes, the figures of the Crown Prince and the Immortal Emperor seemed to overlap more and more.

Dugu Ming thought to himself, *"From this day forth, across the entire 1st to 19th layers, I fear the cultivators of the Myriad Laws will only know the Crown Prince, and know no Immortal Emperor."*

Dugu Ming understood the nature of Kunxu cultivators all too well. The reason the Immortal Emperor could reign as Kunxu's sovereign and govern trillions of living souls was, on the one hand, undeniably due to his absolute power.

On the other hand, it was because the Immortal Emperor had brought about a relatively stable environment, allowing everyone to make a living within it and seek a glimmer of hope to ascend the Immortal Dao.

If the Immortal Emperor possessed merely sheer power, yet brought only chaos, slaughter, and violence, stripping people of the hope and environment to ascend the Immortal Dao, then... even if it cost them everything, the numerous cultivators of Kunxu would inevitably resist to the bitter end.

*"With their hearts set on the Immortal Dao, the Kunxu cultivators can be utterly obedient, yet they can also harbor an extraordinary spirit of rebellion."*

*"And now, His Highness the Crown Prince is orderly increasing the number of Realm Certificates, expanding the recruitment of Sect Staff, and adding Law Establishment Quotas — all of which have expanded the mobility between cultivation realms."*

*"On top of that, he turned the tide this time, shouting down the multitude of Immortals and successfully holding peace talks with them, proving that he can maintain the stability of the greater environment and allow cultivators to ascend the Immortal Dao more smoothly..."*

*"Having accomplished all this, in the eyes of the cultivators of the Myriad Laws Sect, he might as well be the Immortal Emperor of the Myriad Laws Sect."*

As these thoughts crossed his mind, the figure of the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor surfaced involuntarily in Dugu Ming's heart. To Dugu Ming, the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor was a being who was forever infallible in his calculations, forever seamless and flawless.

*"Grandpa Immortal Emperor, all of this must be part of your plan as well, right?"*

*"So my joining the Crown Prince faction now to assist him must also be advancing your plan, right? Therefore... being loyal to the Crown Prince is being loyal to the Immortal Emperor."*

On the other side, Fa Liuyuan was watching the livestream, looking at the projections of the two Reformist Faction Immortals standing beside Zhang Yu.

He thought to himself, *"Both Masters accompanied the Crown Prince down together? It seems the talks up there were indeed quite successful."*

In Fa Liuyuan's eyes, it was entirely normal for the Immortals to instantly change their attitude, doing a 180-degree turnaround from armed suppression to mutual cooperation, because the collective of Immortals were purely creatures of Immortal Dao interests.

The faster, more decisive, and more ruthless the turnaround, the more it proved that the Immortals were truly sincere.

On the contrary, if their shift in attitude was ambiguous, sluggish, or vacillating, it would only indicate that the benefits were not enough to make them change course.

Thus, at this very moment, Fa Liuyuan's previously taut nerves relaxed slightly.

*"Although the Ten Great Sects and the ten Immortal Emperors have always exploited the world and treated all living beings under heaven as resources for the Immortal Dao, it must be said... it is precisely because of their existence that countless cultivators were provided with a relatively stable environment for cultivation."*

*"Each sect can be said to be a stable cultivation environment with a different style. It is precisely because of this that the cultivators of Kunxu are willing to submit to the rule of the Immortal Emperors and climb the path of Immortal Dao within these environments."*

*"And the Crown Prince has now accomplished this as well..."*

As these thoughts raced through his mind, Fa Liuyuan had already begun considering how to congratulate His Highness the Crown Prince.

After all, at this very moment, Fa Liuyuan felt more acutely than ever that the entirety of layers 1 to 19 rested upon the Crown Prince's shoulders. If one wished to make progress on the path of Immortal Dao, one had to cling tightly to the Crown Prince's coattails.

He thought to himself, *"If it were like before, simply giving money would suffice."*

*"However, the Crown Prince has just abolished private accounts, and in the past, he has repeatedly expressed his distaste for monetary gifts..."*

Pondering Zhang Yu’s consistent attitude all along, Fa Liuyuan mused inwardly, *"Drafting a congratulatory message should be fine, but there will surely be no shortage of people writing to His Highness. I must study this thoroughly and figure out how to compose one that stands head and shoulders above the rest."*

While Fa Liuyuan was racking his brains over how to write his congratulations, Fu Ji, the Auspicious God, and the Herald God were roaming the Spirit Realm, observing the public sentiment within the Myriad Laws Sect.

Fu Ji said with satisfaction, "Not bad, not bad at all. All the layers are rejoicing to welcome the Crown Prince. This shows that the Crown Prince possesses great virtue, and the people's salaries can be put to good use."

The Herald God thought to himself, *"First allowing the Immortals to suppress them with armed force, and then turning the tide himself — with this series of maneuvers, the Crown Prince has thoroughly kneaded the entire 1st through 19th layers into a cohesive whole, keeping everything firmly within his grasp."*

*"Those subordinates who were originally loyal to him have gone through the process of being suppressed by the Immortals and then pulled back up by the Crown Prince. They have now profoundly realized that their interests and their immortal paths are entirely bound to the Crown Prince. In the future, they will naturally be even more devoted and loyal. It can be said that they have been completely purified..."*

At the same time, during his speech, Zhang Yu also revealed a new cross-realm trade agreement, stating that trade with the Immortal Realm would resume circulation.

And the first cross-realm transaction completed by the two sides was Zhang Yu's successful purchase of the Heaven-Opening cultivation art, Great Dao Comprehensive Manual: Revised Edition, from Immortal Venerable Profound Wisdom.

For a time, the contents of the speech and the success of the transaction led to a massive surge in market confidence. Countless stocks surged together, revitalizing numerous Myriad Laws cultivators.

---

Just as Zhang Yu was giving his speech.

Inside the Supreme Clarity Realm, Zhang Yu was also raising his doubts to the figure before him.

"Why am I so sure that the First Reincarnation Immortal Emperor originated from a Divine Child?" the figure said. "Because back then, I saw him with my own eyes."

Zhang Yu's gaze shifted. "Saw him with your own eyes?"

The figure said, "The Divine Emperor has already been obliterated; it is impossible for him to awaken, leaving behind nothing more than a remnant soul like me."

"Yet, it was this very remnant soul of mine that vaguely witnessed a scene back then — after the Divine Emperor was obliterated, the many Immortals led by the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor took control of the Heavenly Court and banished the Righteous Gods."

"It was precisely in that scene that I saw a Divine Child who had ascended and become an Immortal. It was none other than he who took the banished Righteous Gods into his fold, transforming those Righteous Gods into what are now known as Evil Gods."

"And this person was the first Reincarnation Immortal Emperor."

Hearing this, Zhang Yu was inwardly startled. "But someone told me that the Evil Gods are ancient gods, born of heaven and earth, divine from birth—"

The figure said, "Whoever told you that has a completely mistaken understanding of gods. It is a standard historical misconception, and whoever fabricated that misconception definitely harbored ulterior motives."

"Let me ask you another question: do you think a creature like a god seems like something nature would produce?"

"The so-called gods... were originally beings in the Divine Dynasty meant to serve the common people of the world. It was only later that they gradually became tools for the Immortals to govern the world, and the world's perception of gods was continuously altered as a result."

Seeing Zhang Yu nod, the figure continued, "You must be wary of the Reincarnation Faction's schemes. Their interference in the Heavenly Court's Divine Children Ritual this time undoubtedly carries unspeakable motives."

Images of the giant corpse and the Evil Gods of the underground world surfaced in Zhang Yu's mind.

"Although the giant corpse was the previous generation's Reincarnation Immortal Emperor, I'm afraid he no longer knows much useful intelligence."

"As for underground..."

Zhang Yu had not yet set foot in Kunxu's subterranean world, but he knew that the Netherworld controlled by the Reincarnation Immortal Emperor was deep underground, that the critical facilities of the Reincarnation Dao Lineage were underground, and that the Evil Gods and Evil God Kings were also underground. Furthermore, many ancient historical ruins were located down across the eighteen subterranean layers. 

It was fair to say that Kunxu's subterranean world hid far, far too many secrets. Zhang Yu had a feeling in his heart that sooner or later, he was bound to venture underground to uncover the secrets of the Reincarnation Immortal Emperor and the mysteries of ancient history.

Inside the Feathered Ascension Grotto-Heaven, a strand of Zhang Yu's consciousness was cultivating the Heaven-Opening cultivation art, the Great Dao Comprehensive Manual: Revised Edition. After cultivating it for just a little while, he already gained a profound understanding of this Heaven-Opening cultivation art.

"The essence of this cultivation art is to use Immortal Qi Technology to rapidly analyze and simulate various kinds of Immortal Dao Technology."

"Once this cultivation art is pushed to level 100, let alone ordinary True Inheritance, Strategic, or Sect-Guarding-Tier martial arts and daoist arts, even Heaven-Opening cultivation arts and the Law Edict technologies of various factions can be rapidly analyzed and replicated through Immortal Qi Technology."

"However, this kind of replication certainly won't be perfect..."

The Sect Examination Clone chuckled and said, "It seems our Immortal Venerable Profound Wisdom can truly be called a Little Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor."

Zhang Yu thought further to himself, *"And to achieve this, besides having sufficient Immortal Dao Technology, one must also obtain the blessing of the Immortal Qi Dao Lineage."*

*"A Dao Lineage..."*

Zhang Yu sighed inwardly. After browsing through the various Heaven-Opening cultivation arts that the Immortals were willing to trade, he had noticed a common pattern.

"Heaven-Opening cultivation arts were originally created by the Immortal Class. Among them, there are ancient and modern ones, strong and weak ones."

"Yet, in modern times, the Heaven-Opening cultivation arts that can still be continuously updated and maintained in the top tier were each created by the Immortals to break through limits and achieve the highest efficiency."

"To reach this unprecedented performance, and at the same time... to monopolize the technology, prevent theft, and stop the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor from prying, these latest top-tier Heaven-Opening cultivation arts are all deeply tied to a Dao Lineage."

Zhang Yu recalled the Innate Dao-Vindication Scripture he had cultivated before, which required relying on the Dao Lineage of Supreme Truth.

And the Saints' Spirit Source Wisdom Scripture he had cultivated not long ago, originating from the Desolate Ox Great Sage, required exclusive control over the Spirit Realm — meaning it needed the blessing of the Spirit Realm Dao Lineage.

Originally, Zhang Yu had thought this was unique to those two Heaven-Opening cultivation arts, but looking at it now...

"Truly superior Heaven-Opening cultivation arts probably cannot do without being bound to a Dao Lineage."

"So, only after becoming an Immortal, establishing a Dao Lineage, and binding it to a self-created Heaven-Opening cultivation art can it truly belong to oneself and unleash its maximum might."

Even with this realization, Zhang Yu did not abandon cultivating this Great Dao Comprehensive Manual: Revised Edition. After all, he and Immortal Venerable Profound Wisdom were still in a period of cooperation, and learning this cultivation art could vastly improve his cultivation efficiency.

*"Yet, in the end, I must still achieve immortality."*

*"The Ascension Selection should be starting soon, right?"*

Zhang Yu thought to himself, *"I'm afraid, though, that my path to achieving immortality will be beset by countless Immortals standing in my way."*

"Convene the Ascension Selection as soon as possible. Every sect needs new Immortals." Immortal Venerable Profound Wisdom's will rippled outward faintly.

Dharma Realm Immortal replied, "New Immortals, new Dao Lineages, new patron relationships... All of these will exert a tremendous impact on the current situation. We need to deliberate carefully on the specific candidates."

Immortal Roujia of the Reformist Faction asked, "What about Zhang Yu? He's been dropping hints to me again, asking about the Ascension Selection."

Innate Immortal said, "Stall him for now. This isn't the time to clash with him. Drag it out until the Ascension Selection begins, and then we'll see."

"When the time comes, he will understand on his own, and he won't go looking to humiliate himself any further."

"After all, 'Zhang Yu must not achieve immortality' is the consensus of the Ten Sects."
