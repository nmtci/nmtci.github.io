# Chapter 1049 Everyone Else's Assets Wiped Out, Only You Have a House and a Car

"Sis Zhenzhen's money-making ability is just too incredible. She's only been here for a few days, and she's already become a homeowner and car owner."

"Sis Zhen, won't you give everyone some pointers on how to get rich?"

"That's right, Sis Zhenzhen, we're all from the same sect after all..."

As Bai Zhenzhen posted pictures of the property deed and vehicle registration in the group chat, it immediately stunned the assembled heroes of the Heavenly Sword Sect.

Like a brick tossed into a cesspit, it provoked a flurry of responses, coaxing numerous Spirit Transformation whelps, Void Refinement juniors, and other cultivators out of the woodwork to lavish Bai Zhenzhen with endless flattery.

Watching the messages scroll by like a waterfall, Bai Zhenzhen laughed heartily and said, "Heh, how could I just easily reveal my secret to you guys? I need to maintain my wealthy and mysterious image in the group so I can command more authority."

The next moment, Bai Zhenzhen turned to Zhang Yu beside her and said, "Yu Zi, who knew your identity in this world would come with both a house and a car? This is a massive advantage."

"If the other cultivators from Kunxu come in completely penniless as well, and this New World becomes a sanctuary in the future, wouldn't that mean every single Kunxu cultivator's assets will be wiped out while you already have an apartment and a car?"

"You'd instantly become the richest person in Kunxu, standing at the very top of the cultivator food chain!"

Hearing that, Zhang Yu said, "Let's go, A-Zhen. Time to start our food delivery shift."

Bai Zhenzhen immediately sprang to her feet, brimming with fighting spirit. "Right! Even if we have a place and a car, we still need to maintain positive cash flow."

"Beyond covering our basic monthly living expenses, we should accumulate more capital to prepare for raising our wealth level and gaining more advantages in this New World in the future!"

Zhang Yu knew that even in this New World, Bai Zhenzhen's attitude toward working and earning money remained exceedingly proactive.

He thought to himself, *"To be more precise, once the cultivators from Kunxu arrive in this world, any of them wanting to boost their ability to mobilize resources here will be extremely keen on making money. It's just that some look down on working menial jobs, some choose to work to build capital, and others choose to gather intelligence first."*

Naturally, Zhang Yu wasn't counting on making a fortune delivering food; they were merely doing it for the time being to cover living expenses for himself and Bai Zhenzhen and sustain basic survival.

Zhang Yu thought to himself, *"After all, the real crux of the matter is this world itself."*

Watching the crowds of people coming and going on the street, he silently wondered, *"Is this... really the Earth where I once lived? Have I truly returned?"*

*"If so, then what on earth is the connection between Kunxu and Earth?"*

Zhang Yu recalled that the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor had once mentioned the word 'Earth' as well.

*"Back then, I assumed the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor had probably just read my memories."*

*"Looking at it now, though... could it be that he has been here too?"*

Once the work began, Bai Zhenzhen proved far more enthusiastic than Zhang Yu...

Standing outside a milk tea shop, Bai Zhenzhen looked at Zhang Yu in disbelief and said, "Yu Zi? You're clocking out already? But we've only been delivering for twelve hours! I haven't made enough yet!"

"Besides, I checked, and the delivery fees are even higher tonight! Are you really going to pass up such a golden opportunity to make money?"

Bai Zhenzhen showed him the weather forecast and said, "Look, strong winds, torrential rain, and a typhoon warning tonight! Delivery fees are surging. With such great weather, it would be a crying shame not to deliver takeout! How is this any different from watching money fall into the water and doing nothing?"

With that, Bai Zhenzhen looked at Zhang Yu with concern. "Yu Zi, are you sick?"

In Bai Zhenzhen's eyes, having climbed all the way up from the bottom of Kunxu together, Zhang Yu's moneymaking drive and work ethic... could never be this weak.

Therefore, there could only be one reason he wanted to clock out early—he had to be sick. Zhang Yu glanced around and noticed quite a few passersby drawn by Bai Zhenzhen's words.

Zhang Yu looked at Bai Zhenzhen and said in a low voice, "A-Zhen, we've already made quite a bit of money over the past few days, and I've prepared plenty of basic supplies you'll need to survive in the New World."

"From now on, we just need to cover our daily expenses. There's no need to spend so much time delivering takeout to make money for the time being."

"The capacity of our current physical bodies is limited. We need adequate rest, and we also need time to gather intelligence and figure out our next move."

Zhang Yu thought for a moment and added, "Even though we temporarily have some say in the Heavenly Sword Sect's group chat because we own an apartment and a car, relying solely on these won't be enough if we want to maintain our advantage in the long run."

In Zhang Yu's view, the New World had to be a vital target for conquest in the eyes of the Heavenly Sword Sect's many Immortals, and perhaps even to the Heavenly Tribulation Immortal Venerable himself.

If he could help Bai Zhenzhen gain an even greater voice during this period, or even have her recognized as a key figure in conquering the New World...

*"If that happens, not only will we be able to influence the Kunxu Cultivators' progress in conquering the New World, but we might also be able to use it as leverage, with A-Zhen serving as a bridge, to obtain intelligence on the Heavenly Tribulation Dao Lineage."*

Zhang Yu naturally hadn't forgotten that his original purpose in coming to the Heavenly Sword Sect was the Heavenly Tribulation Dao Lineage.

However, as he saw it, he had always lacked leverage—leverage substantial enough to interest the Heavenly Tribulation Immortal Venerable.

And this New World might just be that opportunity.

Of course, Zhang Yu was also very interested in this world himself.

"A-Zhen, as long as our jobs cover our daily expenses, that's enough. We should spend more time studying this world."

And so, following Zhang Yu's advice, Bai Zhenzhen clocked out and headed home with him.

After eating dinner on the way, Bai Zhenzhen sighed. "What a decadent world. Even the poor can afford such delicious pre-made meals. Both the businesses and the customers care far more about taste than nutrition and efficacy. Instead of adding medicine to the food, they pile on seasonings."

"Yu Zi, why did you buy milk tea again today? That's a non-essential food item. Not only does it increase expenses, but it also places a burden on the body, which might lower our efficiency at making money."

Bai Zhenzhen looked at Zhang Yu with concern and said, "Could it be that physical weakness has caused your willpower to slip, leaving you seduced by food?"

Zhang Yu smiled and said with a touch of nostalgia, "A-Zhen, do you still remember back in the First Layer, when I said I'd treat you to delicious food once I made money? Sadly, the higher one climbs in Kunxu, the less good food there is to eat."

"Yet here, I guess you could say I've fulfilled that promise along the way."

Bai Zhenzhen froze slightly upon hearing that. She took the milk tea Zhang Yu handed her, and the memories of the First Layer — which had grown somewhat hazy since arriving in the New World — seemed to surface once again in her heart.

After returning home, Bai Zhenzhen gradually began to feel an itch all over her body.

*"Sigh, how could we get off work so early?"*

Even though she knew what Zhang Yu said earlier made sense, the Kunxu Spirit etched deep within her heart still compelled Bai Zhenzhen to gaze out the window with reluctance. She asked, "Yu Zi, it's such a great rainstorm. Are we really not going to work some overtime?"

Zhang Yu shook his head and said, "A-Zhen, today's work is already done."

Bai Zhenzhen sighed. "We can't pursue Immortal Cultivation here, so what else is there to do if we don't work overtime?"

Skimming through the materials, Zhang Yu said, "Keep studying the knowledge of this world. It will help you understand it, or help you get things done here in the future."

Bai Zhenzhen knew that over the past few days, Zhang Yu had been constantly using phones, the internet, and books to learn about this new world. He seemed curious about every little thing here, studying and researching all kinds of materials whenever he had a spare moment.

Bai Zhenzhen said, "Sigh, I guess studying is the only way to numb myself and satisfy my urge to work overtime."

Before long, as the clock struck twelve, Zhang Yu said, "A-Zhen, time to sleep."

Zhang Yu understood that to the Kunxu Cultivators, sleeping was something they felt a deep disgust for from the bottom of their hearts.

*"If Earthlings went to Kunxu, they'd definitely be urged to work overtime. But if the Kunxu People come to Earth, they probably all have to be urged to sleep."*

*"To these Kunxu people, making them sleep is like reducing their daily disposable time — it feels like shortening their lifespan."*

Hearing this, Bai Zhenzhen said, "I got it, Yu Zi. I'll sleep after finishing this chapter."

Fifteen minutes later, Zhang Yu reminded her again, "A-Zhen, aren't you going to sleep yet?"

Bai Zhenzhen said helplessly, "Yu Zi, I can't sleep, I really can't sleep! Sleeping is just such a waste of my time. Every extra minute I sleep, I feel like I'm losing money. Just let me study a bit longer, let me read a bit more."

Zhang Yu forcefully dragged Bai Zhenzhen onto the bed and said, "A-Zhen, if you stay up late reading and studying again, I won't let you go to work tomorrow. I'll make you take the day off directly."

Zhang Yu knew that making Kunxu cultivators earn less money was the harshest punishment for them.

Then Zhang Yu reminded her again, "Different environments require different approaches. You must change the reading addiction and salary addiction you picked up in Kunxu now."

Bai Zhenzhen sighed helplessly, "People in this world waste a third of their time every day just by being alive. Isn't that too miserable? How on earth can they stand it? Yu Zi, why don't you inject me with some drugs? I looked into it, and this world has drugs too, right?"

In Bai Zhenzhen's eyes, getting drug injections was completely routine; as long as they weren't counterfeit, there was nothing wrong with it.

Zhang Yu waved his hand, turned to leave, and said, "Hang in there, A-Zhen. Getting used to sleeping again is a mandatory lesson in this world."

Over the next few days, Bai Zhenzhen constantly adapted to this world.

And as she continuously adjusted, she also felt her memories of Kunxu seeming to grow increasingly faint.

Yet, after Bai Zhenzhen brought this up to Zhang Yu time and again, she discovered that this change never seemed to happen to him.

"Your memories of Kunxu are still completely clear?"

Bai Zhenzhen asked, "Yu Zi, do you still remember all the details about Kunxu right now?"

Zhang Yu nodded and said thoughtfully, "It might have something to do with certain special experiences of mine."

Zhang Yu did not elaborate on what those special experiences were, so Bai Zhenzhen did not press further, always trusting what he said.

Especially when Zhang Yu spoke of the various details of Kunxu, an overwhelming sense of reality washed over her, letting Bai Zhenzhen feel that the place called Kunxu... was by no means an illusion.

And this realization brought Bai Zhenzhen a profound sense of relief.

"That's such a relief, Yu Zi... I was really terrified. Terrified that Kunxu was nothing more than a dream of mine."

"Just the thought of spending the rest of my life unable to pull all-nighters for overtime or studying terrifies me. How is that any different from dying young?"

In Bai Zhenzhen's eyes, schedules like 995, 996, and 997 were just excuses for the weak to slack off; a truly strong person should work 007.

*"What a pity that there is simply no room for the strong to survive in this world."*

*"It's not just this useless physical body holding me back; all sorts of absurd rules restrict working hours, too. It's like they're terrified of us living a good life."*

With lingering fear, Bai Zhenzhen said, "Compared to Kunxu, this new world without the Immortal Dao is just dreadful."

Yet, whether it was because Zhang Yu was right beside her or because he frequently recounted his memories of Kunxu, Bai Zhenzhen still held onto her belief in Kunxu, keeping that hope alive in her heart.

Meanwhile, looking at the messages popping up from time to time in the Heavenly Sword Cultivators group chat, Bai Zhenzhen could sense the changes in the other cultivators.

Hong Shanlang: "Does Kunxu really exist?"

Zhentian: "Is it possible that Kunxu was nothing more than a dream?"

Lu Jingsong: "How could so many of us have had the exact same dream?"

Hong Shanlang: "Maybe it's because we all read *Kunxu* — read the same novel — and then had similar dreams?"

Looking at the chat history in the group, Bai Zhenzhen said in surprise, "Yu Zi, the other cultivators seem to be undergoing the same change. Their memories of Kunxu are growing increasingly blurry and illusory, and they've even begun to question the reality of Kunxu, thinking that it doesn't exist at all. This isn't just an issue with their memories, is it? Even their mindsets and perceptions have begun to warp."

Zhang Yu nodded and guessed, "Perhaps it's some characteristic of this world that makes Kunxu cultivators gradually transform and adapt after entering, truly becoming a part of this world."

Recalling his previous encounter at the Heavenly Sword Sect, Zhang Yu said, "I've been pondering a question all this time: why were the cultivators we saw at the Heavenly Sword Sect so terrified in their sleepwalking state? Even if they arrived in a new world, couldn't cultivate the Immortal Dao, and became poor, as long as they could return to the sect, all of that wouldn't really matter."

Thoughtfully, Zhang Yu said, "Now I understand. It must be that after staying in this world for a long time, a feeling that Kunxu is fake gradually wells up inside them... making them believe they will live in this world forever. Consequently, they succumb to a sense of despair and panic over their poverty and their inability to cultivate immortality."

Over the days that followed, both Zhang Yu and Bai Zhenzhen increasingly sensed that the cultivators in the group chat were growing more and more accustomed to life on Earth.

For instance, fewer and fewer cultivators stayed up late. Hardly anyone posted after midnight, and most went to sleep early.

Moreover, people were beginning to lose enthusiasm for the previously planned meetups.

Hong Shanlang: "I won't be joining the next meetup; I have to work overtime."

Zhentian: "I don't have time either, got to entertain some clients."

Of course, Zhang Yu also noticed that a select few remained quite active in the chat.

Lu Jingsong: "Weekly investment commentary: over the past six days, the market first saw a catch-up drop before rebounding; stocks opened low and closed high..."

Lu Jingsong: "If anyone's interested in investing, we can chat face-to-face at the meetup."

Chu Jianshuang: "A reminder once again: paid dual cultivation is illegal here. Do not take chances and test the law."

Chu Jianshuang: "I have far more legitimate and efficient ways to make money, with plenty of high-quality resources on hand. Any sisters in the group who want to learn more, add me as a friend."

Looking at the chat history in the group, Zhang Yu thought to himself, *"Even if this bunch of cultivators from Kunxu are taking their memories of Kunxu less and less seriously, and even if they harbor feelings of despair and unwillingness toward this world, their nature and habits haven't changed."*

*"Someone took the shortcut to wealth so quickly, already leaning toward fraud, and their target might even be their fellow countrymen from Kunxu."*

Shaking his head, Zhang Yu continued researching intelligence about the new world.

*"From various historical records, based on my comparisons and research so far, this place is no different from the Earth I know. Or rather, at least I can't see any difference in history."*

*"Even my past, my education, my school, my past jobs... all match up perfectly."*

*"But is this really Earth? Then why... why is it connected to Kunxu? Why does it have all kinds of bizarre influences on the Kunxu cultivators? Why can Kunxu cultivators have corresponding identities here?"*

The more he studied, the more puzzled and astonished Zhang Yu felt.

*"Is there really something wrong with this world? Is this not the real Earth?"*

*"Or could Earth really be a special place to Kunxu as well? Then how did I travel from Earth to Kunxu in the first place?"*

Just then, a series of dull thuds echoed from the side.

Zhang Yu turned his head and saw Bai Zhenzhen holding an iron rod, relentlessly bashing at a newly purchased punching bag.

He understood that she was adapting to fighting with this frail, mortal body.

The reason she had begun training was tied directly to their recent observations and reflections.

"Right now, we basically understand the various changes that happen to Kunxu cultivators once they enter this new world."

Bai Zhenzhen spoke with a hint of anticipation, "And since the Heavenly Tribulation Immortal Venerable is studying this new world, he'll descend here sooner or later, won't he?

"If the Immortal Venerable behaves like the other immortals and cultivators, he won't be able to use Immortal Dao technology after entering this world, and once he leaves... he won't remember what happened here either.

"Doesn't that mean we could suppress him in this world and try to pry vital intel about the new world and the Heavenly Tribulation Dao Lineage out of his mouth before his memory blurs?"

Bai Zhenzhen, currently in the middle of training, panted as she looked at her aching hands and slowly said, "Without the empowerment of Immortal Dao Technology, fighting with such weak flesh leaves very little we can do."

"However, if it's like this for us, it's the same for the Immortal Class."

At this point, Bai Zhenzhen couldn't help but chuckle. "On the same starting line, if I train in advance, adapt in advance, and prepare weapons in advance, I'll have an absolute advantage."

"When the Immortal Venerable arrives, as long as he can't use Immortal Dao Technology, the two of us will beat him to the ground."

"Yu Zi, we really came to the right place in this New World."

Zhang Yu nodded slightly, thinking to himself, *"Suppressing an Immortal Venerable by force in the New World and extracting intelligence from him truly is a plan with a chance of success."*

Zhang Yu understood in his heart that if he wanted to achieve immortality, he had to overcome the Heavenly Tribulation of ascending to immortality. And to successfully overcome the Heavenly Tribulation, he had to obtain intelligence on the Heavenly Tribulation Dao Lineage's current state.

*"From the looks of it, making A-Zhen a key figure in the Heavenly Sword Sect's conquest of the New World, or ambushing the Heavenly Tribulation Immortal Venerable by force — both plans have a certain chance of success, and preparations for both can be carried out simultaneously."*

*"The only question is whether the Immortal Venerable will descend at all, what form he will take if he does, or even... whether he has already descended."*

*"However, we currently have no way of knowing or controlling the Immortal Venerable's actions."*

*"All A-Zhen and I can do right now is prepare as best we can."*

Lost in thought, Zhang Yu had already stood up and walked toward the punching bag, beginning his daily workout as well.

Meanwhile, within the Heavenly Sword Sect of Kunxu.

Countless pieces of information regarding the Dreamwalkers were circulating at high speed throughout the sect.

Bai Zhenzhen, Hong Shanlang, Lu Jingsong, Immortal Hengan... their current physical body conditions and behaviors within the Heavenly Sword Sect were all displayed across streams of light screens, observed by numerous consciousnesses.

"According to the observation data of vital energy and magic power, the panic levels of Hong Shanlang and Tianzhen are continually escalating, and they are exhibiting an increasing number of delirious symptoms."

"Lu Jingsong's condition is slightly better, but his vital energy circulation and the circulation speed of his magic power are also accelerating, indicating that his emotions are undergoing rapid shifts, and he has begun to mumble incoherently."

"Immortal Hengan's condition is somewhat better. For now, it seems that the higher the cultivation realm, the stronger the resistance indeed is."

Once the data of one Dreamwalker after another had been analyzed, the gazes of all consciousnesses converged onto Bai Zhenzhen.

"Bai Zhenzhen is the most exceptional case among the many current Dreamwalkers."

One consciousness remarked in astonishment, "At first, Bai Zhenzhen's physical body also showed conventional signs of panic, but those manifestations soon began to diminish gradually. Her vital signs grew increasingly normal and stable, indicating that her mental state was becoming ever calmer, as if she were gradually adapting to... the New World's negative influence on her."

Another consciousness noted enthusiastically, "Mm, according to Bai Zhenzhen's past data, she indeed possesses immense adaptability to various environments, cultivation, and even cultivation arts. It's likely related to her special Spirit Root."

The mysterious figure who had once instructed Bai Zhenzhen in sword techniques also spoke up through his consciousness at this moment, "Bai Zhenzhen may very well be a major key to conquering the New World."

"I suggest raising Bai Zhenzhen's clearance level and granting her access to more intelligence regarding the New World."
