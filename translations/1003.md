# Chapter 1003: Catching Them All in One Net

Outer layer of the Immortal Grotto-Heaven.

Sensing that the Grotto-Heaven had expanded a bit further, Gao Chongguang felt a flicker of satisfaction. He mentally calculated which Grotto-Heaven he should visit in a few days to haul back some unclaimed space.

At the same time, a notification flashed across his Bionic Eye, reminding him of a shortage of medicinal cultivation pills.

However, Gao Chongguang did not open a shopping platform. Instead, with a thought, he accessed a news platform via the Spirit Realm.

After all, the Spirit Realm's ad redirections were lightning fast, and its big data algorithms were exceptionally powerful. Every time Gao Chongguang wanted to buy something, he would just open the news platform, where giant ads accurately guessed what he wanted and swiftly redirected him to the shopping interface — a process far more efficient than opening the shopping platform directly.

Yet, as soon as he opened the news platform today, he found that the entire Spirit Realm seemed to be inundated with the same trending topic: "Chain Formation Diagram."

"Breaking! The latest immortal dao technology to transform Kunxu has arrived! What exactly is the Chain Formation Diagram unveiled by the Myriad Laws Sect?"

"Celestial Evolution Award winner Heavenly Monarch Overdrive's million-word analysis: How will the Chain Formation Diagram impact your life?"

"The Chain Formation Diagram: not merely a technical issue, but a class issue, with cultivation anxiety manufactured by consumerism lurking beneath the surface."

"Chain Formation Diagrams? You think you're taking a shortcut, but in reality, you're merely completing an obedience test set by the immortals, weakening the autonomy of your Dao Heart..."

"Cultivation secrets that Ascension Realm cultivators will never tell you: while you're still asking what a Chain Formation Diagram is, some people already have over a dozen sets prepared for them in their very first year of cultivation..."

"Still worried about not having a Chain Formation Diagram? I've already brought home an equivalent product..."

The messages flashing before Gao Chongguang's eyes refreshed constantly. Tracing the origin of the information all the way back, he discovered that the source of this storm in the Spirit Realm stemmed from an announcement released by the Myriad Laws Sect's Immortal Ascension Hall.

"Creating Chain Formation Diagrams? In charge: Zhang Yu?"

"Could this be the... policy Zhang Yu mentioned earlier?"

Gao Chongguang thought to himself, "Judging solely by this announcement, it's written rather vaguely, not even explaining what these so-called Chain Formation Diagrams are."

He scanned through the various analyses and discussions regarding Chain Formation Diagrams across the Spirit Realm, thinking, "Yet for it to spark such massive discussion, someone must be actively disseminating the news and driving the hype. This also proves the tremendous impact of these Chain Formation Diagrams."

With a thought, Gao Chongguang commanded, "Celestial Inquiry Star, summarize the current opinions in the Spirit Realm regarding Chain Formation Diagrams for me."

As more and more information poured in, a flash of realization dawned upon Gao Chongguang: "Creating a Chain Formation Diagram will be an immense authority capable of influencing the entire Kunxu. No wonder Zhang Yu said we won't need to worry about running out of money from now on."

Over these past days, Gao Chongguang had already researched the real estate industry in the sect layers.

"The sect's spatial technology is extremely advanced, with an application scope far beyond anything in the lower realm — teleportation is available everywhere. Therefore, the sect's real estate basically doesn't depend on location; what matters is background, cultivation, and technical strength."

"The deeper the background Zhang Yu displays, and the stronger his cultivation and technical strength, the more the real estate in the Immortal Grotto-Heaven will be perceived as having potential for appreciation."

"Creating a Chain Formation Diagram... this is a power capable of shaking a market of trillions."

---

Inside the Grotto-Heaven of the Myriad Laws Immortal Clan, messages from the Spirit Realm continuously flashed in Bu Yuangui's bionic eyes as he let out a sigh in his heart.

Right at that moment, Bu Yingshu's projection materialized before him, asking anxiously, "Dad! What is going on with this Chain Formation Diagram creation? Why didn't you tell me beforehand?"

Bu Yuangui replied indifferently, "I was only among the last to know about this as well."

Bu Yingshu looked at him with a face full of suspicion. "This is an announcement released by the Immortal Ascension Hall. You didn't know in advance?"

Bu Yuangui looked at Bu Yingshu helplessly and said, "This is a decision made by the Immortals. How could your dad possibly know in advance?"

Bu Yingshu asked in confusion, "The effects of a Chain Formation Diagram are just mediocre, and many Immortal Dao Technologies can replace them. Does it really require the Immortals to decide?"

Bu Yuangui shook his head and explained, "That was in the past. In the past, Chain Formation Diagrams were far too rare and had no versatility at all."

"This time, however, creating a Chain Formation Diagram means creating one for universal use. Anyone who cultivates the 10 cultivation arts within this Chain Formation Diagram will be able to obtain its effects."

"If one can create a Chain Formation Diagram for healing, self-recovery, or longevity extension, it would be enough to strike a blow against the entire pharmaceutical sector. If it can strengthen the divine soul, it will be enough to alter the competition between soul cultivators and artifact spirits, thereby influencing the Dao Lineage conflicts. If it can affect breathing techniques, spiritual energy, and magic power, it will trigger turmoil across the entire energy market..."

Listening to Bu Yuangui's explanation, Bu Yingshu gradually understood how wide-ranging the implications of "creating a Chain Formation Diagram" were. In theory, it could practically influence every single Dao Lineage conflict among the Ten Great Sects.

She sighed inwardly, *"Immortal Emperor, oh Immortal Emperor, the Myriad Laws Immortal Clan is your true bloodline, and I am your descendant! How could you hand over such crucial authority to an outsider like Zhang Yu? Just how much do you distrust your own descendants? And how wary of us are you?"*

Seeing the changes in Bu Yingshu's expression, Bu Yuangui seemed to have already guessed what she was thinking, and said indifferently, "Zhang Yu is nothing more than a tool in this matter."

"Look closely at the announcement. Although he is responsible for creating the Chain Formation Diagram, its design blueprints must still be reviewed and approved by the Immortal Emperor."

"At the end of the day, creating a Chain Formation Diagram... carries immense implications. Before this news ever reached us, the Immortal Emperor, Immortal Venerables, and Immortals had likely already gone through multiple rounds of discussions, and even coordinated and wrangled quite a bit with the Immortal Class of other sects before finally settling on this outcome."

"Zhang Yu is responsible for the execution, while the Immortal Emperor decides which Chain Formation Diagram to create — but even His Imperial Majesty has to balance the stakeholder interests and feedback of the broader Immortal Class."

Hearing this, Bu Yingshu nodded thoughtfully and asked, "Then if there were a way to get insider information in advance, how could one make money off this?"

Bu Yuangui glanced at her and said, "No matter what kind of Chain Formation Diagram it is, one ultimately needs to cultivate 10 cultivation arts. These 10 cultivation arts are like 10 stocks — once announced... the value of their copyrights will inevitably skyrocket."

"If one can hoard the copyrights in advance, they could make a profit of a hundredfold, a thousandfold, or even ten thousandfold."

Hearing this, Bu Yingshu's eyes immediately lit up. She thought to herself, *"With my relationship with Little Yu, wouldn't getting him to tell me the cultivation arts in advance before he creates the Chain Formation Diagram be as easy as turning over my hand? I'm rich now! My funds for achieving immortality will see massive growth again."*

Looking at the beaming Bu Yingshu, Bu Yuangui thought to himself, *"Silly girl, now that the news is out, you won't be the only one thinking of this."*

*"I'm afraid it won't be long before countless people inquire with Zhang Yu and use all kinds of methods to make deals with him."*

*"There will be even more companies, cultivators... recommending their own cultivation arts to Zhang Yu, hoping to be selected for the Chain Formation Diagram."*

*"When that time comes, whenever Zhang Yu opens his inbox every day, it'll probably be packed with cultivation art manuals. Who knows how many people will give him usage rights to their cultivation arts just to recommend them to him..."*

---

White Bone Cult.

Bai Gusheng looked at the messages in the Spirit Realm, his mind surging with shock: "Creating a Chain Formation Diagram that anyone can use? How could the Myriad Laws Sect suddenly possess such technical capability? It must be the Book — the one they acquired recently!"

"Zhang Yu..."

Looking at the name that appeared in the announcement, Bai Gusheng immediately linked it back to the previous incident of seizing the Book at the Immortal Armaments Bureau.

Reflecting on the entire incident now, along with the intelligence currently at his disposal, Bai Gusheng was eighty to ninety percent certain that the Book had granted Zhang Yu the ability to create the Chain Formation Diagram.

At this thought, Bai Gusheng felt a pang of heartache.

"That was my Book! How did that little thief Zhang Yu end up snatching it away?!"

"If I had known this would happen, I should have forcefully seized that Book back then, even if it meant triggering a conflict between our sects."

Though feelings of regret, jealousy, and resentment welled up inside him, Bai Gusheng calmed them in mere moments.

At the same time, his mind continuously gathered various pieces of information from the Spirit Realm, and he had already formulated a new round of analysis: "However... this might not necessarily be a bad thing."

"Creating a Chain Formation Diagram involves karmic ties and vested interests far too heavy for Zhang Yu — or even me — to bear."

"No matter what kind of Chain Formation Diagram he creates that favors a certain industry or a particular faction of Immortals, it will inevitably offend others, perhaps even causing conflicts of interest with Immortal Venerables or Immortal Emperors of other sects."

Bai Gusheng could almost foresee that if he were to use this book himself, countless high-ranking cultivators would interfere with and influence him, entangling him in the conflicts of many Immortals.

"Creating a set of Chain Formation Diagrams every ten years? This authority is a massive landmine, one that will detonate every ten years at that. The Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor letting Zhang Yu use it is likely just passing the buck to him."

---

The 16th layer of the Myriad Laws Sect.

Cang Juehuan was sensing the information within the Book in his hands.

As a heavenly chosen of the Artifact Spirit Faction, Cang Juehuan had once carried high hopes. He had participated in the first session of the third-generation Law Establishment, enacting the Artifact Spirit Human Rights Law Edict, and was now a cultivator of the Tribulation Transcendence Realm.

"If only I had obtained this book a few years earlier."

Looking at the Great Sage Book in his hands, Cang Juehuan sighed with emotion. "Had I possessed this book a few years ago, my research on high-intelligence artifact spirits would certainly have progressed much faster. In the third-generation Law Establishment... perhaps I could have even competed on equal terms with Zhang Yu."

However, Cang Juehuan knew that there were no 'what-ifs' in reality.

"If the sect hadn't started collecting the books, I probably wouldn't have gotten my hands on this one either."

The previous owner of this Great Sage Book believed they lacked the background to continuously resist the book collection campaign. Thus, upon noticing the movement to retrieve the books, they chose to sell it to Cang Juehuan.

"Others might fear Zhang Yu collecting their books, but I don't."

"The reforms of the third-generation Law Establishment will eventually be completed, and the next era will undoubtedly belong to high-intelligence artifact spirits. When that time comes, I will be the sect's new protagonist."

"If Zhang Yu wants to take this Great Sage Book from me, he would be delaying the sect's artifact spirit research and disrupting our strategic plan. He will have to pay a steep price!"

Cang Juehuan calculated inwardly, "At the very least... Zhang Yu must yield one Law Establishment Quota to me next year."

As a Heavenly Chosen of the Artifact Spirit Faction and the founder of the Artifact Spirit Human Rights Law Edict, Cang Juehuan had also been continuously trying to arrange subordinates to bolster him through Law Establishment, preparing for the future promotion of his Law Edict into a Law Decree.

Cang Juehuan glanced at the chat group in the Dharma Realm. He knew that within the sect, there were quite a few people like him who held Books, possessed powerful backgrounds, and planned to jack up their asking prices.

It was just like encountering a demolition project. Some cultivators had even used their means to seize several Books, becoming formidable holdouts solely to make a massive profit when selling them to Zhang Yu.

And just like the Immortal Clan member who had once sold a Book to Liu Qianxian for 100,000 Immortal Coins and subsequently received a Subordinate Merit, what most of these Book holders wanted to gain was not money, but Immortal Dao resources and power within the sect.

Just then, a massive influx of information surged out from the Spirit Realm and Dharma Realm, leaving Cang Juehuan slightly shocked. "Creating a Chain Formation Diagram? Zhang Yu?"

"The Immortal Emperor actually granted him such immense authority?"

Cang Juehuan frowned inwardly, sensing that something was amiss, and glanced at the messages in the group.

Lianzi Zi from the pharmaceutical company said, "So what if he has a powerful background? We're not Lower Realm Natives; we're Sect Staff. Everyone in this group is at Void Refinement, Tribulation Transcendence, or Ascension Realm. We are the ruling class of Kunxu. If he wants to buy our Books, he'll have to name a proper price."

Another cultivator from the Dharma Realm Dao Lineage faction said, "Everyone, stay calm. By all means, don't let him intimidate you! Zhang Yu is the weakest among the Ten Sects Book-Wielders. The longer we stall, the more pressure he'll face, and the higher his offer will be."

"As long as we stand united, he won't be able to do anything to us!"

Seeing the messages from the numerous cultivators in the group, Cang Juehuan nodded inwardly.

Right at that moment, a notification popped up.

"Inviting all Book-holders within the sect to a meeting to discuss matters regarding the creation of a Chain Formation Diagram?"

Upon seeing this notification, Cang Juehuan's eyes instantly lit up.

He understood all too well how immense the interests involved in creating a Chain Formation Diagram were.

Even just knowing in advance which cultivation arts would be included in the Chain Formation Diagram would be enough to make a fortune.

And if the created Chain Formation Diagram was related to Soul Cultivators and artifact spirits, it would have an even more profound impact on his High-Intelligence Artifact Spirit research.

At the same time, messages kept flooding the group chat.

Lianzi Zi: "Zhang Yu has invited us to discuss the creation of a Chain Formation Diagram. Heh heh... On the surface, he's asking for our experience with Books and Chain Formation Diagrams, but in reality, he wants to share some benefits with us in exchange for the Books in our hands."

Someone chimed in to remind the others: "During the meeting later, nobody agree to anything on the spot! Let's see what the highest price Zhang Yu can offer is!"

Cang Juehuan also felt slightly relieved. "Is Zhang Yu really going to compromise? Does he want to share benefits with us through creating the Chain Formation Diagram in exchange for the Books in our hands?"

Regardless of what Zhang Yu was thinking, creating a Chain Formation Diagram was simply too momentous, and the interests involved were far too immense — so immense that Cang Juehuan felt he had to go and see for himself.

---

Inside the Immortal Grotto-Heaven.

Cang Juehuan swept his gaze across the endless construction site and thought to himself, *"With Zhang Yu's background, this Grotto-Heaven should be developing quite well. It might be worth investing some money here."*

Next, guided by Zhao Tianxing, Cang Juehuan arrived at a building that had only recently been completed.

Upon entering the enormous conference room, Cang Juehuan noticed that many cultivators had already arrived early. Most eye-catching of all were the representatives of the Immortal Clan seated in the very front row.

*"Bu Yingshu came too? These Immortal Clan members... must have been brought here by Bu Yingshu as well, right?"*

Cang Juehuan found an arbitrary seat and sat down, watching as the rest of the attendees gradually filled the room. He thought to himself, *"After all, this involves the creation of a Chain Formation Diagram. Anyone with even a slight understanding of it would never miss this meeting."*

Meanwhile, watching the scene unfold from the other side of the room, Liu Qianxian couldn't help but sigh with emotion. *"To think he could summon all these holders of the Books from within the sect to a meeting in one breath..."*

Liu Qianxian knew full well that if she had been the one to issue the call, it would never have had such an effect.

She knew this wasn't because Zhang Yu had immense prestige, but rather the enticing prospect of creating a Chain Formation Diagram. The lucrative profits it promised were what had drawn all these cultivators here.

Just as Liu Qianxian was lost in thought, Zhang Yu appeared on the stage and began, "Everyone, the agenda of this meeting is related to the Chain Formation Diagram. It concerns the future direction of creating Chain Formation Diagrams and involves a trillion-level market..."

Following Zhang Yu's words, the cultivators, including Cang Juehuan, seemed to see the immense prospects ahead, their hearts stirring with excitement.

Zhang Yu continued, "And the Chain Formation Diagrams... originate from the Books. Therefore, in this conference room right now, there are holders of low-grade Books, holders of mid-grade Books, and myself, a holder of a high-grade Book. Anyone without a Book is not worthy of attending this meeting."

"So, before this meeting officially begins, I would like to inspect the Books in everyone's hands to ensure that every single person present is indeed a Book holder."

Before anyone could voice an objection, Bu Yingshu took the lead and stood up, saying, "No problem, I'm all for it! Those without a Book don't deserve to remain here."

As she spoke, Bu Yingshu handed a Book she had prepared long ago over to Zhang Yu, who reached out and accepted it.

This was the book that the Immortal Clan had promised Zhang Yu, and Bu Yingshu had only brought it out now to use as an admission ticket to the meeting.

At the same time, the other Immortal Clan representatives followed suit and presented their books before Zhang Yu for him to inspect.

Watching this scene, the other cultivators frowned slightly and began exchanging messages rapidly in the group chat.

Cang Juehuan: "Zhang Yu won't take this chance to just snatch the books away, will he?"

At this moment, Cang Juehuan was reminded of things that had happened in the past... like stealing Realm Certificates, Calculation Pools, company seals, and the like.

He knew that sometimes high-level struggles were just this simple and crude, just like how the Ten Immortal Emperors, facing Immortal Venerable Supreme Truth's breakthrough, had chosen to directly suppress him on the spot with force.

Right then, Zhang Yu's voice rang out. "Everyone, if you are unwilling to hand over your books for inspection, please leave the conference room. You will not be qualified to learn the upcoming information regarding the creation of a Chain Formation Diagram."

Enticed by immense profits, more people finally handed over their books.

Handing over his book, Lianzi Zi from the pharmaceutical company reminded him, "Minister Zhang, once the inspection is complete, don't forget to return it to us."

Zhang Yu still remembered this Lianzi Zi. The last time he had led people to storm the pharmaceutical company's Grotto-Heaven to take back Le Mulan, he had encountered this Tribulation Transcendence Realm cultivator.

As cultivator after cultivator handed their Books to Zhang Yu for temporary inspection, Cang Juehuan saw messages continuously refreshing in the group chat.

"If Zhang Yu dares to pull anything funny in a moment, everyone attack together and snatch the Books back first."

"We have so many Tribulation Transcendence and Ascension cultivators here. If he dares to make a move, we'll suppress him on the spot!"

After pondering for a moment, Cang Juehuan still handed over the Great Sage Book for the time being.

He thought to himself, *"With Ascension Realm cultivators present, it's unlikely Zhang Yu will try anything reckless."*

Meanwhile, looking at the Books being delivered before him one after another, Zhang Yu felt the Feather Book inside his body growing impatient, surging time and again to break free from his body and devour all these Books whole.

"5 Books... 10 Books... 21 Books..."

"A total of 18 low-grade Books and 3 mid-grade Books — enough for me to push the Dao-Seeking Progress of the World-Domain Dao Lineage to 100%... and leave plenty to spare for the next Dao Lineage."

Zhang Yu scooped up the 21 books before him, and the next moment, a massive gate materialized behind him.

It was Zhang Yu activating the Palace Gate Restriction, opening the gate to the 33rd layer.

Seeing the inscription indicating the 33rd layer on the gate, everyone felt a sudden jolt in their hearts.

Cang Juehuan said in alarm, "The 33rd layer? Is this... an Immortal Gate leading straight to the 33rd layer?!"

The next moment, they heard Zhang Yu say, "I'm taking the books to have them inspected, and I'll be back shortly. Please wait a moment, everyone."

The crowd watched helplessly as Zhang Yu clutched all the books and stepped into the 33rd layer, not a single person daring to stop him.

Lianzi Zi felt waves of sheer disbelief in his heart: *"The 33rd layer... Isn't that where the Immortal Emperor resides? Could it be that Zhang Yu can go straight to the 33rd layer and meet with the Immortal Emperor directly?"*

Just as everyone's minds were reeling with shock, astonishment, and sheer bewilderment, Zhang Yu had already stepped back out through the gate of the 33rd layer.

However, seeing that the 21 books were no longer on Zhang Yu, Cang Juehuan's heart sank.

Only to hear Zhang Yu say, "Everyone, the higher-ups need to borrow your books for a bit, so I'm afraid they'll be returned to you a little late."

Cang Juehuan's heart sank abruptly. *"Borrowed by the higher-ups? Could it be the Immortal Emperor who's borrowing them?"*

Lianzi Zi was filled with doubt and suspicion. *"Is that true or false? This fellow Zhang Yu couldn't have done it on purpose, right? Deliberately taking our books away like this?!"*

Someone asked, "Minister Zhang, when will those books be returned to us?"

Zhang Yu glanced at the new modules that had emerged after the Feather Book fused 21 books in one breath, and spoke up, "How long the higher-ups will study them, I don't know either. As soon as there's news, I'll notify everyone."

He said with utter righteousness, "Or, you can ask the people on the 33rd layer yourselves."

Hearing these words, Lianzi Zi felt immensely stifled and thought to himself, *"Damn it, how are we supposed to contact the 33rd layer? If they're really in the Immortal Emperor's hands, am I supposed to ask the Immortal Emperor when he'll return them to me?!"*

Someone else asked, "Minister Zhang, are those books currently with the Immortal Emperor..."

Zhang Yu shouted, "I knew that after all your dodging and deflecting, you'd end up pinning it on the Immortal Emperor! I really don't understand — this is all for the sake of collecting books, for the sect affairs of creating the Chain Formation Diagram. Why must you be so calculative? Why do you always have to scheme against the Immortal Emperor?"

Cang Juehuan cursed inwardly, *"Wasn't it you who sent it to the 33rd layer in the first place?"*

Yet, he couldn't help but wonder suspiciously, *"Then again... could it really have been taken by the Immortal Emperor?"*

Using the "creation of a Chain Formation Diagram" as bait, gathering everyone under the pretense of a meeting, tricking them into temporarily handing over their books, and then borrowing them for years without returning them...

The more Cang Juehuan thought about it, the more plausible it seemed. *"That Emperor Beast might actually do something like this!"*

On the other side, the Sect Examination Clone secretly thrilled with excitement, *"Since the Immortal Emperor wants to push us onto the dining table and has decided to indulge our growth unchecked, then we'll fully exploit every single bit of his influence."*

*"We'll use a hundred ways to utilize the Immortal Emperor to accelerate our growth!"*
