# Chapter 945: Introducing Special Talents from the Lower Realm

Ever since Zhang Yu moved to the Inner Enclosure Grotto-Heaven on the 16th layer, Zhao Tianxing, as his subordinate Chief Soul Cultivator, had been continuously traversing the Spirit Realm. 

His will traveled back and forth between the Former Days Graveyard and the Inner Enclosure Grotto-Heaven, conducting on-site monitoring of the various situations in the Netherworld Assets Department and the Soul Cultivator Company for Zhang Yu.

This ensured that even after Zhang Yu left the Former Days Graveyard, he still had a clear grasp of the actual situation on the ground.

And relying on the Void Refinement quotas given by Zhang Yu, people including Zhao Tianxing, Mo Tianyi, Che Yufei, Hu Yuntao, and the Ying Clan patriarchs had all obtained the qualification to freely break through their cultivation realms all the way to Void Refinement.

However, even for a Soul Cultivator, despite abandoning the cultivation of magic power and physical strength and saving countless hours of grinding on cultivation arts, breaking through to the Void Refinement Realm was by no means something that could be achieved in a short time.

Zhao Tianxing, who received the most support from Zhang Yu, had only recently broken through to the Spirit Transformation Realm, and the other Soul Cultivators under him were mostly at the Nascent Soul and Golden Core Realms.

Fearing that their progress was too slow and that Zhang Yu might replace their Void Refinement quotas, they all cultivated diligently every day in addition to their work. They hoped to break through to the Void Refinement Realm as soon as possible, holding tightly onto this once-in-a-lifetime opportunity.

But even Nascent Soul and Golden Core Soul Cultivators were already sky-high figures to Wang Hai.

To Wang Hai, the former gold-medal teacher of Songyang High School, whether he was a physical education teacher or the principal at Songyang High School on the First Layer, the highest cultivation realm of cultivators he could interact with in his daily work was only the Foundation Establishment stage.

Nascent Soul and Golden Core were already unattainable for him, and a Spirit Transformation cultivator was even more like a mythological existence.

Therefore, upon hearing that a Spirit Transformation soul cultivator had descended, the pixelated figure Wang Hai projected immediately knelt on the ground, kowtowing profusely.

Lacking the Spirit Realm voice transmission service, he could only quickly send lines of small text begging for mercy: "Spirit Transformation Lord, please spare my life! This lowly one hails from the First Layer and just arrived at the sect. I don't know the rules of the sect, nor do I know anything about a lower realm soul cultivator impersonating an upper realm soul cultivator. I definitely did not intentionally violate the sect's laws..."

At this moment, Wang Hai was shivering inwardly, terrified that the great figure before him would resurrect him with a single thought, causing him to fall into debt and be unable to find a job.

Although it hadn't been long since he arrived at the sect, Wang Hai had already heard many things. For example, for a Qi Refinement Soul Cultivator like him, the big shots in the sect could casually force him to take out a loan to resurrect.

And if he lost his status as a Soul Cultivator, with his identity as a living Qi Refinement person, he wouldn't be able to find a job in the sect at all. After all, the lowest-end jobs for the living had long been snatched up by artifact spirits and Soul Cultivators.

When that time came, not only would his resurrection have been in vain, but the interest on the loan would also compound, and he would ultimately have to become a Soul Cultivator all over again while burdened with debt.

Only in the state of a Soul Cultivator could he exist like a mere spare part, finding a bottom-tier job in the sect to barely manage to stay dead.

But the next moment, when Wang Hai heard the person opposite him call him Teacher Wang and ask how he had managed to die and end up in the sect, Wang Hai's heart trembled, and he cautiously observed the other party.

However, the lack of a high-definition visual service meant Wang Hai could only observe a blur of pixels.

Only the transmission of text information allowed him to clearly read the other party's name, cultivation realm, position, and other details...

"Zhao Tianxing?"

Earlier, when Wang Hai saw this name, he had assumed it was just someone with the same name and surname. But now, hearing the other party's voice transmission, a trace of disbelief couldn't help but surface in his heart.

Zhao Tianxing sensed the Soul Cultivator shivering incessantly in front of him, feeling a wave of emotion in his heart. 

Originally, when he received the news that a lower realm Soul Cultivator was impersonating an upper realm Soul Cultivator, he thought it was the doing of some junior college student from the lower realm. 

This wouldn't have been a surprise to him. After all, as far as he knew, today's junior college students in the lower realm... were becoming more and more different from the junior college students of their time. 

"Ever since the circulation of Soul Cultivators between the upper and lower realms was opened up, it's now full of ungrateful ghosts who desperately enter a sect for the sake of opportunism, going from undergraduates to taking the vocational college exams." 

"They're not like us junior college students from back in the day, who only took the vocational college exams because we had no other way out." 

But he never expected the situation to be much more serious and unimaginable than he had anticipated. 

"A First Layer Soul Cultivator impersonating an upper realm Soul Cultivator? Not even a junior college student would do something so despicable." 

Zhao Tianxing could already imagine that once this matter spread, it would inevitably cause a massive uproar. 

Thus, with a jolt of his will, the numerous Soul Cultivators under his command scattered in all directions, beginning to lock down the news. 

At the same time, Zhao Tianxing looked at Wang Hai in front of him and said, "Teacher Wang, do you still remember me? I'm Zhao Tianxing, who was in the same grade as Zhang Yu back then..."

Looking at the other party's pixelated figure, Zhao Tianxing's heart suddenly stirred, and he bought some basic Spirit Realm Services for him.

The next instant, Wang Hai said in shock, "Zhao... Divine Monarch Zhao, you!"

At this moment, endless regret surged in Wang Hai's heart. "Even Zhao Tianxing can reach Spirit Transformation? The sect really is full of opportunities everywhere... I died too late! I died too late!"

Zhao Tianxing asked, "Teacher Wang, how did you end up dying and coming to the sect?"

Wang Hai immediately explained, "The project for the circulation of Soul Cultivators between the two realms has now spread to the First Layer..."

Through Wang Hai's description, Zhao Tianxing finally realized that the current First Layer had undergone earth-shattering changes.

The news of the circulation of Soul Cultivators between the two realms, and the fact that one could die to enter the sect, had caused a massive sensation in the First Layer as soon as it spread.

There were sayings like, "Better to be a Sect Ghost than a First Layer Person."

Or, "Testing into the sect is worse than dying into the sect."

Or, "Dying early is the only way out for young people."

Wang Hai lamented, "If free suicides weren't banned, there probably wouldn't be many living people left in the entire First Layer right now."

In Wang Hai's description, many people no longer even referred to death as "death", but rather as ascension. Dying into the sect was ascending into the sect.

It was a step to heaven that surpassed the College Entrance Exam.

"Later, the leaders of the First Layer joined forces and held the first Death Tournament, selecting only the most outstanding Soul Cultivators to be able to die and enter the sect. This finally stopped a lot of trash from dying recklessly..."

Listening to Wang Hai's lament, Zhao Tianxing seemed to envision the countless rich people, geniuses, and academic overachievers in the First Layer... each one giving their all, competing madly, just to snatch an opportunity to die and enter the sect, becoming a mere cog in the machine and serving as a tablecloth for the sect's cultivators.

And all of this was solely for the chance to get better opportunities to cultivate in the legendary sects, the chance to break their own limits, and the chance to obtain higher-level Realm Certificates...

But looking at Wang Hai in front of him, Zhao Tianxing felt a wave of strangeness. "Teacher Wang, you... also competed to get here?"

In Zhao Tianxing's estimation, if the entire First Layer was competing to die, it shouldn't have been the turn of Wang Hai right in front of him... unless there was a massive quota.

Wang Hai said, "I originally couldn't grab a spot, but after they found out I was Zhang Yu's Teacher, they classified me as a special talent and recruited me into the sect."

Zhao Tianxing nodded slightly, flipping through Wang Hai's file. When he saw "Gold-Medal High School Teacher" filled in the special talent row, he felt it was even more bizarre.

"Does the sect need... a gold-medal high school teacher?"

Meanwhile, Wang Hai asked cautiously, "Lord... Lord Zhang is also in the sect, right? I wonder what rank and cultivation realm he is at now?"

Zhao Tianxing said, "Minister Zhang is already a Rank Six Void Refinement Cultivator, and the Myriad Laws Divine Sword of our Myriad Laws Sect."

There were also Zhou Chechen and Lan Ling, who were similarly from Songyang High School and later admitted into university...

Looking at these familiar names, avatars, and information one by one, a trace of cold light flashed in Zhang Yu's eyes.

Fu Ji said, "Having so many people connected to us die and come up here all at once, no matter how you look at it, it isn't a coincidence."

"Zhang Yu! We should take this opportunity to sever our connection with the lower realm and display our sect nature!"

Having Zhang Yu dispose of them like garbage was the best approach. It would also cut off some of the ties with the lower realm and win over the goodwill of many sect cultivators.

But unfortunately...

Fu Ji thought inwardly, *"Sigh, Zhang Yu just won't make this optimal choice."*

Zhang Yu thought for a moment and replied to Zhao Tianxing, "Gather the people temporarily, give them a little work, and monitor them 24 hours a day..."

After finishing the arrangements, Zhang Yu refocused his attention on the Law Edict Pilot in the 16th layer, since this was currently his most important task.

Although Zhang Yu's Law Edict Expiration Date received widespread support and the Thought Tide Amplification grew day by day, any reform or change could never win the support of absolutely everyone.

The same was true for the changes Zhang Yu brought.

It could be said that ever since Zhang Yu began expanding the scope of his Law Edict, causing more and more companies and employees to break away from the regulations of the "Four-Hour Workday" and "Paid Overtime" voices of both support and opposition had emerged.

First of all, for employees who originally had heavy workloads and were not highly capable at work, occasionally needing to pay overtime fees, they naturally fully supported no longer having to pay for overtime and being able to work for free every day until their tasks were completed.

However... for the cultivators who were originally highly capable at work and could finish their daily tasks within four hours, having to work four more hours every day compared to before meant a reduction in their time for part-time jobs and cultivation, which naturally made them feel dissatisfied.

It was just that all along, the opponents who were capable enough at work made up a smaller proportion and had not formed a wave of protest; instead, they were directly drowned out by the voices of the supporters.

But as Zhang Yu's Law Edict True Domain continued to expand, successively spreading across the minor realms of five people — Si Shian, Ban Jinchen, Lie Jinghong, Kuang Zhiqian, and Bu Yingshu — the proportion of opponents, although still not high, saw a sharp rise in absolute numbers.

Especially in the 16th layer, there were many cultivators with strong working capabilities who had been keeping a close eye on Zhang Yu's movements.

Upon realizing that the speed of Zhang Yu's expansion was constantly increasing and drawing ever closer to them, they successively expressed their concerns, lack of support, and even opposition within the Spirit Realm and the Dharma Realm.
