# Chapter 1011: If I Were an Immortal Emperor

Looking at the line of text that had surfaced, Zhang Yu was slightly startled in his heart. *"Could this also be a message left by the Immortal Ancestor?"*

Without the slightest hesitation, Zhang Yu silently chose "Willing."

The next moment, he felt his consciousness undergo a sublime elevation, as if he had crossed infinite time and space to arrive in another world.

At the same time, a booming interrogation exploded violently in Zhang Yu's mind: "The successor of my Dao must become an Immortal Emperor! If you ascend to the Supreme Seat, how shall you govern the world?"

The interrogating voice was like cascading thunder, detonating continuously within Zhang Yu's mind and overwhelming every trace of thought in his spirit.

Zhang Yu could not help but mutter in his heart, *"If I ascend to the Supreme Seat, how shall I govern the world?"*

As the echoes of self-questioning rang out, Zhang Yu came back to his senses to find himself perched high above the Nine Heavens. He felt the overwhelming might of an Immortal Emperor surging within his body — a single thought could sweep across the eight wildernesses and four seas, and a flip of his palm could force hundreds of millions of living beings to bow and submit.

The Sect Examination Clone laughed heartily, shouting in his heart, "After years of arduous cultivation, we have finally ascended to the Supreme Seat! Today, since we are the Immortal Emperor, we should naturally enslave the world and strike toward the Seat of the End!"

Immortal Slayer immediately cursed, "If we do that, how are we any different from that little beast Myriad Laws? Now that we have become Immortal Emperors, we must never run amok like Myriad Laws did."

The Sect Examination Clone retorted in annoyance, "Having achieved the emperor's throne, we are the sole sun of all living beings! If we still cannot act as we please, then what was the point of becoming an Immortal Emperor?"

Having stepped into the Immortal Emperor Realm, Zhang Yu felt not the slightest sense of dissonance at this moment.

He only felt that the world before his eyes and the power surging within his body were both utterly real and completely natural.

As for the fact that there were no other Immortal Emperors in this world, that too seemed entirely as it should be, beyond any shadow of a doubt.

Thus, at this moment, he only felt his thoughts expand with boundless ease, no longer feeling any worry, tension, or pressure.

In that fleeting instant, a single thought surged in his heart.

"I can act completely according to my own will. The only one who can stop me is myself!"

Zhang Yu's heart seemed free of any further scruples, feeling that from now on, all his actions and every move he made would stem purely from his true nature.

---

Meanwhile, clamorous discussions had already broken out inside and outside the Ascension Platform.

The book-wielders of the Ten Sects stood atop the stage, each entirely motionless, as though they had fallen into some deep dream.

Above the heads of these book-wielders, beams of light projected screens showing different scenes.

Bu Yingshu had bought front-row tickets long ago and projected herself here, prepared to observe this Book-Wielder Assembly up close.

Seeing the bizarre scene on stage now, she was filled with confusion as well. "Dad, what's going on? What are they doing?"

Bu Yuangui analyzed, "The books on them seem to have activated some kind of process just now."

At the same time, various pieces of Spirit Realm information surfaced before Bu Yuangui's eyes, compiling a large amount of intelligence.

"According to the news filtered down from above, this should be a process left behind by the creator of the books, that Immortal Ancestor, which can select a successor from among these ten book-wielders."

His gaze swept across the light screens above everyone's heads, and he guessed, "Right now, they seem to have fallen into an illusion, believing themselves to be Immortal Emperors."

Bu Yingshu said in shock, "So this is an Immortal Emperor Simulator? Does this Immortal Ancestor just want to see who plays the Immortal Emperor Simulator the best?"

---

On the other side of the Ascension Platform, Le Mulan looked at the scenes in the light screens and asked, "Hall Master, how is the situation now?"

Dugu Ming said indifferently, "This Illusionary Realm is fathomlessly deep, certainly not something anyone below the Immortal level can resist. Now that all the book-wielders of the Ten Sects have been drawn into it, I'm afraid none of them will notice anything amiss."

He observed all ten light screens simultaneously, recording each person's reaction and analyzing them at the same time.

"At this moment, every single one of them truly believes they have become the sole Immortal Emperor. They will make choices following their true hearts and use the Immortal Emperor's supreme might according to their deepest intentions."

"In my view, this Immortal Ancestor merely wishes to use this process to select the person with the best performance to become his successor."

Hearing this, Le Mulan asked curiously, "Then what counts as the best performance?"

In the Spirit Realm, Fa Liuyuan was watching the live broadcast of the assembly, with Immortal Yi Xuan's analysis echoing in his ears: "Although they are all simulated Immortal Emperors, it is deduced based on reality."

"It deduces their strength after becoming an Immortal Emperor based on their current cultivation."

"Therefore, the stronger their real-world strength, the stronger the Immortal Emperor's supreme might they display."

"Because of this, since Zhang Yu only possesses a Tribulation Transcendence cultivation base and lacks the accumulation of the Ascension Realm, the deduced Immortal Emperor's supreme might he possesses will also lag behind the others."

Fa Liuyuan understood what Immortal Yi Xuan meant. It indicated that the Immortal Emperor might displayed by the other book-wielders was deduced from their individual accumulations from Qi Refining all the way to Ascension Realm. Since Zhang Yu lacked the accumulation of the Ascension Realm, he had merely been given the baseline stats of a blank-slate Ascension Realm cultivator. Consequently, the Immortal Emperor might deduced for him was naturally a cut below the rest.

"So the gap in strength puts him a step behind right from the start?"

Meanwhile, the Ten Sects' book-wielders were already displaying their respective mystic powers.

"Hahahaha!"

Bai Gusheng burst into hearty laughter, and the world began to shake violently.

The very instant he became an Immortal Emperor, a thought surged through Bai Gusheng's mind: *"Cultivating honestly is too boring!"*

*"After all my hard work cultivating to the level of Immortal Emperor, wouldn't becoming one be completely pointless if I still had to keep cultivating honestly?"*

However, Bai Gusheng needed to gather more intelligence before deciding on his exact course of action. In an instant, his consciousness swept across the entire world, first surveying the current state of affairs.

"This is an ancient world? Innumerable sects dot the landscape, countless nations rule over the mortal realm, and there are even emperors?"

"Backward, backward — it's all far too backward!"

"How can there still be slaves? Can slaves work diligently? Can they be efficient?"

With that, Bai Gusheng's thoughts raced, and a world transformation plan was already complete.

"On the first day, let everyone under heaven have a job. On the second day, separate day and night shifts. On the third day, establish performance metrics for all living beings. On the fourth day, create currency. On the fifth day, set tax deadlines. On the sixth day, begin salary reductions. On the seventh day, make all living beings work overtime..."

However, right after Bai Gusheng finalized his plan and began taking action, an accident occurred.

"Huh? The living beings here..."

"Real-world strength correlates directly to how much Immortal Emperor might each book-wielder can unleash."

Bu Yingshu asked in confusion, "Are the people disappearing?"

Bu Yuangui replied, "They're not disappearing, but migrating. These ten Immortal Emperor Simulator worlds are interconnected, and it seems the people within them can migrate at any time."

Bu Yuangui sighed, "Their backward mindset cannot comprehend the advanced Immortal Dao Power brought by the book-wielders. Once they sense something is wrong, they flee to another book-wielder's world."

"Moreover, the book-wielders didn't notice this at first. Combined with the fact that the book-wielders themselves seemingly cannot travel to other worlds, it gave some people the opportunity to escape."

"Still, with all ten worlds are overseen by a book-wielder, even if they keep fleeing back and forth, they will only..."

The next moment, Bu Yuangui paused and looked toward Zhang Yu's light screen, noticing a trace of abnormality.

"What is Profound Monarch Zhang doing?" Bu Yuangui asked in surprise. "Building a school? Offering free education?"

At the same time, many in the Spirit Realm were also discussing Zhang Yu's situation.

Heavenly Monarch Overdrive quickly looked over, a trace of surprise showing on his face. "How foolish! Why hand over such fine Immortal Dao technology and advanced knowledge to these primitives for free?"

Beside him, Heavenly Monarch Redundancy said, "Overdrive, by saying that, you're underestimating Profound Monarch Zhang far too much."

Like Heavenly Monarch Overdrive, Heavenly Monarch Redundancy had suffered a catastrophic loss in the Thunder Tribulation Market back then and had been forced to turn to the zero-cost business of paid knowledge sharing.

Serving as a commentator alongside Heavenly Monarch Overdrive this time, he continued, "Profound Monarch Zhang is a cultivator who climbed all the way from the first layer to his current standing. Every single move he makes must have deep meaning."

"In my view, Profound Monarch Zhang's move is deliberately using technology to lure them in. Once they're fattened up, he'll catch them all in one fell swoop!"

Hearing this, Heavenly Monarch Overdrive dismissed the idea. "What's the point of luring in these primitives? Using the most advanced Immortal Dao methods to control them is the real blessing for them. Look, once the others realized that the common people could flee, they all immediately began asserting control."

"Heavenly Monarch Bai Gusheng has launched medical loans and longevity extension loans, supplemented by addictive medicinal pills to benefit all under heaven..."

"...to prevent all living beings from fleeing out of ignorance and losing the opportunity to enjoy advanced Immortal Dao Power."

Within Bai Gusheng's world, countless people gradually became addicted to the medicinal pills he provided, willingly letting themselves be infused with drugs and driven by debt.

"Heavenly Monarch Qian Airen has also stopped his previous actions and begun using the entire world as a formation diagram to set up a great formation. He intends to silently carry out Soul Extraction and Blood Refining on all living beings..."

"Tian Feibao is infusing all living beings with invisible Sword Intent, modifying their minds for free to grant them enlightenment!"

"Wan Yaosheng has established a Law Edict: until every soul is pregnant, he vows never to halt his work. He aims to achieve a great racial fusion, becoming the father of all living beings and truly making the whole world one family."

"Source Saint of the Immortal Armaments Bureau is open-sourcing himself, providing all living beings with his Sect Examination Memories and his work wisdom, allowing them to quickly become qualified modern people and race into modern society."

"And the book-wielder of the Joyful Union Sect is copulating with heaven and earth, pouring Essence Qi into the great rivers and streams. The book-wielder of the Vajra Sect is initiating the privatization of heaven and earth..."

Watching world after world fall under control, Heavenly Monarch Redundancy couldn't help but sigh in admiration, "The book-wielders truly live up to being the elites chosen by each sect. Every single one is brilliantly talented, every single one brings fortune to the world, and every single one possesses both virtue and ability."

In Heavenly Monarch Redundancy's view, since the book-wielders had gone to places untouched by Law Edicts, there was naturally no need to abide by Kunxu's laws. Instead, transforming the world in a more direct and effective manner without the constraints of red tape was what truly constituted a virtuous act.

Heavenly Monarch Overdrive nodded empathetically and said, "I understand. The purpose of this process initiated by the Immortal Ancestor is to see their ability to sweep across the cosmos and push the world into an era of civilization when placed in an ignorant, primitive world without the shackles of Kunxu's morals and laws."

Heavenly Monarch Redundancy also nodded slightly, thinking to himself, *"To become an Immortal Emperor, one naturally has to transform the world. As for why one must transform the world, it is naturally to make the world accommodate oneself and aid one's own cultivation. Otherwise, why else would one transform the world? Just for fun?"*

The world ought to accommodate the Immortal Emperors, the Immortal Emperors ought to transform the world, and benefiting the Immortal Emperors was benefiting the world... Heavenly Monarch Redundancy knew that all of this was Kunxu's immortal political correctness, because the Ten Immortal Emperors did things this way, and who would dare say the Immortal Emperors were wrong?

From a personal perspective, Heavenly Monarch Redundancy believed that after he became an Immortal Emperor, he would do the exact same thing.

At the same time, information was not only surging within the various livestream rooms, but news about the assembly had already begun spreading rapidly throughout the Spirit Realm.

In particular, the data concerning that Illusionary Realm and the performances of the various book-wielders were reported with sensationalized embellishments.

"With so many ancient cultivators, even if they rebelled, they could still be put to good use. Why kill them outright?"

Zhang Yu's gaze shifted slightly as his mind connected to the Spirit Realm. A massive influx of information refreshed in an instant, and he quickly understood what had transpired.
