# Chapter 1015: The Final Dao Battle?

"Back then, through observation after observation, the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor arrived at a conclusion: the Supreme Immortal Artifact capable of executing a World Rollback was also bound by various restrictions."

"If not for those various restrictions, the Rollbacker would have been able to carry out the World Rollback in an even more unscrupulous and arbitrary manner."

"Yet in the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor's observations, the Rollbacker was far from capable of reaching such a degree."

"Ultimately, the first restriction the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor deduced was this Supreme Immortal Artifact's cooldown time."

Looking at the words emerging upon the Feather Book, Zhang Yu continued summarizing in his heart: "A cooldown time? The specific rule is that rolling back time by one year requires waiting one year before it can be activated again. Rolling back two years requires waiting two years before it can be activated."

"For instance, rolling back from this October to last October is a rollback of one year."

"After rolling back that one year, the world returns to last October, and then until this October arrives, during this one-year cooldown period, another rollback cannot be performed."

"Then the further back the rollback goes, although it allows for greater changes to history, at the same time... it will plunge the Supreme Immortal Artifact into a longer cooldown period afterward. During this time, the Rollbacker will also remain in a state where they cannot roll back for an extended duration."

Zhang Yu thought to himself, "And during this window, it presents a prime opportunity for the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor to strike at the Rollbacker."

"Therefore, to seize the Supreme Immortal Artifact, the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor made two-pronged preparations."

"First, without alerting his opponent, he needed to steadily increase his strength, accumulating enough power to defeat his adversary."

"However, since he couldn't arouse suspicion, he couldn't make overly conspicuous breakthroughs, much less monopolize Immortal Dao resources that would drastically alter history. This constrained the growth of his personal strength."

"Thus, he began searching for partners. Through rollback after rollback, he nurtured and taught these companions, passing memories down to them. Gradually, he assembled a team lurking in the shadows — a team composed of extras whom no one cared about in history. The Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor trained and controlled them, intending to bridge the gap in power through sheer numbers."

"And his second step was to secretly influence history, misguiding the Rollbacker into the erroneous belief that the causes of certain events stemmed from a much more distant past. In this way... he inversely guided the Rollbacker, making them roll back the world as far into the past as possible, which in turn forced the Supreme Immortal Artifact into a prolonged cooldown period."

"He succeeded in this step as well. During that period of lost history, the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor and his team gained a considerable stretch of time to engage in an intense contest of arts against the Rollbacker, who was unable to initiate a rollback."

"Yet he ultimately failed as well. Thus, this stretch of history was completely rolled back in the end, and like countless once-dazzling figures of prominence, this experience of the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor was sealed away in lost history, unknown to outsiders."

Reading up to this point, Zhang Yu's heart sank. "He failed? Even the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor failed? Then the current Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor... could he have been molded by the Rollbacker after being defeated by them? Is he merely a puppet in the Rollbacker's hands as well?"

"No... that's not right. According to Senior Supreme Truth, the rollback was initiated by the ten Immortal Emperors, which indicates that the Supreme Immortal Artifact capable of rolling back the world is no longer in the Rollbacker's hands, but has fallen into the grasp of the ten Immortal Emperors."

Zhang Yu's thoughts flashed like lightning, cycling through several layers of reasoning in an instant. Meanwhile, his mind continued to read through the contents of the Feather Book, gradually resolving the lingering doubts in his heart.

"Although he suffered this defeat, the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor ultimately triumphed over the Rollbacker, and later seized the authority of World Rollback alongside the other Immortal Emperors."

"Yet precisely because of his victory, the entire process of how he won was never rolled back, becoming the official history of the world."

"All clues and intelligence regarding this official history have been completely sealed and erased, making it nearly impossible to ever know how the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor actually achieved victory."

"We, who can commune with lost history, are merely able to learn of his past failed attempts — nothing more..."

Reading the final lines of information surfacing on the Pseudo-Dao Chapter, Zhang Yu's thoughts seemed to fall completely silent in that moment.

Zhang Yu sighed inward, "The Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor... failed in his martial duel against the Rollbacker, yet under the watchful gaze of the Rollbacker, he somehow managed to turn defeat into victory?"

Immortal Slayer said, "The situation the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor faced back then might have been even worse than ours is now, yet he achieved final victory. That means he thoroughly understood the loopholes of the rollback, the weaknesses of the Rollbacker, and, even more so, how it might be possible for a lower-tier cultivator to defeat a Rollbacker."

The Sect Examination Clone said in terror, "If the being who once defeated a Rollbacker is the very same Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor and the others who now control the rollback... then how are we supposed to fight this?"

At that moment, a slight tremor seemed to ripple through Zhang Yu's conviction to resist the Ten Immortal Emperors.

Just as he gradually suppressed this distracting thought, a voice rang out in his mind once more.

"Keep reading. Completely digest the inheritance of the Four Books."

Zhang Yu's heart stirred. "It's you again. Who on earth are you? Are you the Immortal Ancestor?"

Zhang Yu still remembered that right after the Feather Book swallowed the other three books, this exact voice had sounded, reminding him to absorb the inheritance.

Yet this time, facing Zhang Yu's question, the other party gave no reply.

Zhang Yu had no choice but to continue comprehending, turning his gaze toward the final volume among the Four Books — the new chapter born from the Ascension Book.

At the same time.

Tai Yuebai's figure flickered rapidly as he darted like lightning toward a certain corner of the 11th layer.

Inside his Grotto-Heaven, Ye Xingli, Feng Tingting, Bei Wufeng, and the other companions left by Zhang Yu in the Former Days Graveyard were all suppressed by his Tribulation Transcendence Realm cultivation, having fallen unconscious one after another.

Apart from this, Tai Yuebai had also left behind several incarnations, using [Myriad Transformations] to mimic their appearances, temporarily pretending that they had never left.

Having accomplished all this smoothly, a flash of realization crossed Tai Yuebai's mind: "Under normal circumstances, if I did this, I could at most take away one or two people. Involving any more would definitely alert the Righteous Gods to something amiss."

Yet looking at the 11th layer, which remained calm and quiet at this very moment, Tai Yuebai could sense that a vast number of Righteous Gods had already been redeployed elsewhere.

"As expected, the ten Immortal Emperors have focused all their attention on the Immortal Ancestor's legacy, and even more than half of the Heavenly Court's forces have been requisitioned."

And the more this was the case, the more Tai Yuebai felt a suffocating sense of an impending storm, of an impending great war.

---

Inside the Reformist Faction's Grotto-Heaven.

Immortal Roujia's gaze swept across countless pieces of Spirit Realm information, and she said, "It has begun. It has truly begun."

Among the information she was browsing were notices of countless companies and cultivators either going on vacation, working overtime, temporarily closing down, or listing themselves for sale.

Beside her, Immortal Yi Xuan said with a grave expression, "The lower-tier cultivators remain utterly ignorant, still reveling in peace and prosperity. Meanwhile, the Immortal Class of the Great Sects has already begun shifting their asset structures, preparing for a wartime economy."

Immortal Yi Xuan understood that from this very moment, the costs of war were already quietly being transferred onto the Lower Cultivators, yet they were not even capable of noticing it.

Immortal Roujia's face grew solemn. "Once a great war becomes the consensus of the entire Immortal Class, then it must be fought whether they want to or not. Eldest Senior Brother has already succeeded."

Immortal Yi Xuan said, "As soon as war breaks out, we must voice our support at the earliest opportunity. Since Eldest Senior Brother and our Reformist Faction initiated this resistance, we should also have a sufficient say in the future coalition of Immortals."

---

"Profound Wisdom!"

Ying Xintian's intent crossed the 25th layer of Kunxu, violently clashing with the Immortal Venerable from the Myriad Laws Sect.

"The Immortal Qi Dao Lineage is one of the foundational lineages. Once the ten Immortal Emperors spark a civil war, you will surely be among the first to be devoured! If you do not join hands to resist now, must you wait until the blade is at your throat to finally learn fear?"

As Ying Xintian's intent saturated the space, an immense torrent of data erupted between the two in an instant, exchanging information across layers.

Although Immortal Venerable Profound Wisdom gave him no definitive answer, Ying Xintian knew... she had taken his words to heart.

The moment the situation shifted, she would inevitably respond without hesitation, turning from a loyal subject of the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor into one of those resisting the Ten Great Emperors.

Ying Xintian understood the Immortal nature of the Immortal Class better than anyone. Ordinarily, demanding that they strike upward against their superiors, fight the strong from a position of weakness, and risk total ruin in battle was completely out of the question; they would never agree to it.

It could be said that as long as they had a path of retreat, they would easily fall back again and again.

However, once the crisis of total ruin and Dao Dissipation was imminent, leaving them with no way out, then let alone the ten Immortal Emperors, they would fight to the death even against a hundred Great Immortal Emperors.

Sensing all of this, a thought could not help but rise in Ying Xintian's mind.

*"Perhaps this is not just a crisis, but an opportunity as well."*

*"An opportunity to change Kunxu, unite the multitude of Immortals, and fight together against the ten Immortal Emperors."*

Just as this thought crossed Ying Xintian's mind, a surging pressure swept over him.

Ying Xintian could feel that the ten Immortal Emperors pursuing him were drawing their power ever closer.

As the distance closed, both sides could even leave messages for each other, enabling a certain degree of dialogue.

*"I have to buy a little more time."*

With that thought flashing through his mind, Ying Xintian attempted to leave a message: "Myriad Laws, I've already helped Zhang Yu obtain the Immortal Legacy. Aren't you going to make your move yet?"

Ying Xintian knew full well that figures of the Immortal Emperor realm would never easily fall for such blatant discord-sowing.

Yet this was merely an excuse targeted at the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor. Ying Xintian believed that among the current ten Immortal Emperors, the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor held an advantageous position. Under such circumstances, there was a fair chance the other Immortal Emperors might seize on this excuse against Myriad Laws as a pretext and opportunity to join forces and suppress him together.

Yet, following this message left by Ying Xintian, the heavens and earth remained utterly calm. The wills of the ten Emperors tracking him were as still as an ancient well, without the slightest ripple of a response.

No matter how Ying Xintian transmitted messages or tried to converse with them after that, they gave no reaction whatsoever. Just like cold machines, they closed the distance to Ying Xintian step by step in a silent, repetitive manner.

Feeling all of this, a flicker of vigilance crossed Ying Xintian's mind.

*"Is there an unnatural tacit understanding among the ten Immortal Emperors?"*

*"Something is wrong. There must be some crucial piece of information I haven't grasped."*

Ying Xintian's intent swept across the Grotto-Heaven under his control, looking at the pieces of future information that were still counting down, and he sighed helplessly in his heart.

This future information had all been duplicated by Zhang Yu and given to Tai Yuebai, who then provided it to Ying Xintian.

*"Although Zhang Yu provided a great deal of future information, if the time hasn't arrived, even if I unseal it, I'll forget it anyway. Repeatedly viewing and sealing it... might also risk losing the information."*

And right now, only the final minute remained on the countdown of the future information.

---

Meanwhile, in the Sea of Myriad Laws on the 33rd layer of Kunxu.

The Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor swept his gaze across stream after stream of future information, musing inwardly, "What a pity, Ying Xintian. The truth lies right before you, yet you lack even the qualifications to glance at it."

"As the saying goes, one cannot speak of ice to a summer insect. A creature like you... is no longer worth conversing with."

These pieces of future information before him had been plundered time and again by the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor, just as he had during Zhang Yu's first imperial audience.

As his gaze swept over them, this future information was etched into his Sea of Consciousness without the slightest detail forgotten.

The contents read:

"They've already noticed! They know we hold information about the rollback!"

"Reincarnation let you in just to draw you into the game, intending to suppress you as well!"

"They've initiated a rollback once more. This is already the second rollback since the Immortal Ancestor's revival."

"And this time, they've decided to carve up and devour both you and the Immortal Ancestor completely."

"Either an Immortal Emperor will reach the End, or an Immortal Emperor will be slain. Therefore, after this incident concludes, the ten Immortal Emperors will no longer reach an agreement to trigger a rollback!"

"The final Dao Battle is coming!"

The Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor looked through the layers of Kunxu, seemingly observing his other nine rivals.

Indeed, only the other nine Immortal Emperors were opponents in his eyes.

Be it Zhang Yu, Ying Xintian, or even the Immortal Ancestor, they were merely Immortal Dao resources to be fought over — bargaining chips in their Dao Battle, and variables they used to decide whether or not to initiate a rollback.

---

With a soft thud, Immortal Yi Xuan fell to his knees.

Beside him, Immortal Roujia looked at the message sent by the Immortal Emperor, her heart reeling in shock.

Its contents were precisely the exchanges between Ying Xintian and the Reformist Faction, and it even contained messages the Reformist Faction had not yet sent out.

*"Has everything been under the Immortal Emperor's watchful eyes all along?"*

*"Could this be a trap set by the ten Immortal Emperors, sitting by and watching us collude just to find an opportunity to round us all up at once? To seize all our assets?"*

Feeling as though the Immortal Emperor's gaze had pierced through layers of space to fix upon them, Immortal Roujia also fell to her knees with a soft thud.

"Immortal Emperor..."

A faint aura of pressure suppressed the voices of the two Reformist Faction Immortals.

Only a single divine intent resonated by their ears:

"Join the Eight Righteous Gods and the Immortals of the various factions to completely seal off all of Kunxu."

Stepping out of the immortal abode, the two Immortals saw layers of heaven and earth pierced through, as boundless golden divine power formed overlapping chains that locked down all of spacetime across the skies.

---

Inside the Grotto-Heaven of the Myriad Laws Immortal Clan.

Bu Yingshu cursed, "Why did the livestream cut off? I was right in the middle of watching it!"

Soon, however, she discovered that it wasn't just the livestream that had disconnected — all of the various Spirit Realm Services and Dharma Realm Services she usually used had also lost connection. 

No matter how ultra-exclusive the VIP service was, it was completely disabled at this moment.

Bu Yingshu walked all the way outside the immortal abode, only to find that numerous members of the immortal clan had already gathered there.

As far as her eyes could see, observe, and perceive, the sky, buildings, and immortal abodes — which had always been surrounded by countless projections constantly displaying all sorts of Dharma Realm information — had now all reverted to bare surfaces.

It was as if all of Kunxu, which had just been so immensely prosperous and bustling, had plunged into dead silence at this very moment.

"Dad? What happened?"

Bu Yuangui did not speak, but abruptly looked toward the sky.

He saw that the sun, which illuminated all layers 11 to 20 and stood at the pinnacle of the sect layers, was also gradually vanishing at this moment, and the entire world was slowly being engulfed in darkness.

"The sun, immortal qi, spiritual energy... have all been diverted."

---

Tai Yuebai looked at the sealed heavens and earth, his heart jolting as he realized things had gone horribly wrong.

Yet in the very next instant, he sensed that the Spirit Realm and the Dharma Realm had also ceased functioning.

"No... that's not right. The Dharma Realm and the Spirit Realm haven't stopped running."

Sensing more carefully, Tai Yuebai could feel that both the Spirit Realm and the Dharma Realm were still operating — in fact, they were running at an unprecedented, massive capacity.

It was simply that he could no longer use any of their functions.

Regardless of VIP tier, regardless of how much money had been spent on services, regardless of how much vital energy, magic power, or immortal aura had been deposited... at this moment, it seemed that cultivators throughout all of Kunxu could not draw upon a single bit of it.

"Credit has been torn to shreds, and the financial order... has been completely shattered."

Tai Yuebai raised his head to gaze at the sky, thinking to himself, "Which means the war has begun."

At the site of the Book-Wielder Assembly.

Amidst the darkness, Bai Gusheng felt as though his perception of the outside world had vanished.

"No, it hasn't vanished."

A flash of realization struck his heart. "Rather, the world has been overwhelmed by something else entirely, leaving me unable to perceive its features."

"This feeling... it is as if an indescribable mist has shrouded all of creation, rendering me blind to everything."

Yet in the next moment, it seemed as though ten suns blazed into existence simultaneously across the sky.

Gradually, Bai Gusheng came to his senses. "Power so vast, so astonishing, so utterly irresistible... Is it an Immortal Emperor?"

"Have the ten Immortal Emperors descended together? Their power permeates every inch of space, so much so that my perception of the world has failed? No, that's not it — everything I could perceive has already fallen under the control of different Immortal Emperors, becoming their assets."

But why would such domineering, invincible Immortal Emperors descend? What on earth was their objective?

Bai Gusheng could not understand, nor could he fathom the answer.

Fortunately, in the next moment, the answer presented itself right before him.

For a sliver of perception had gradually returned to him — a sliver of perception focused entirely on Zhang Yu.

It was simply because everything he could perceive had already fallen under the control of the ten Immortal Emperors. Only Zhang Yu, right before his eyes, remained uncontrolled despite being surrounded by their might, becoming the sole entity he could clearly sense.

At this moment, Zhang Yu appeared uniquely and abruptly in the perception of numerous Book-Wielders.

"The Immortal Emperors' target is him... No, is it the Immortal Ancestor's legacy?"

The very next instant, this sole perceptible target completely vanished from Bai Gusheng's mind as well.

"Has Zhang Yu been submerged by the Immortal Emperors' power too?"

Unable to perceive anything anymore, Bai Gusheng could only ponder slowly in his heart, "Has everything in the world... been submerged by the power of the Immortal Emperors?"

And in the next moment, even his thoughts were completely stripped away.

His thoughts, his flesh and blood, his magic power... Everything he had was plundered and funneled toward an ultimate battle.

A climactic war waged by the ten Immortal Emperors.

A war where the victor would not agree to a rollback, and where the defeated could not trigger one either.
