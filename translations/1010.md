# Chapter 1010: Heir to the Immortal Ancestor

Inside the Heavenly Demon Sect.

Su Lingyou looked at the message appearing before her and felt a surge of joy.

Back then, she and Zhang Yu had clashed in the Former Days Graveyard, and because of her Supreme Truth Immortal Clan identity, she had been ruthlessly targeted.

Fortunately, she had seen the light and switched sides in time, successfully defecting from the Supreme Truth Immortal Clan to the Reincarnation Group’s camp.

From then on, she had worked hard to cling to the Reincarnation Immortal Emperor’s coattails.

For instance, she bought houses built by the Reincarnation Group, invested in its stocks, voluntarily began paying Reincarnation Gold in advance at the highest rate, paid out of her own pocket to send at least 100 people to be reborn every year, and donated money to the Reincarnation Immortal Emperor year after year.

She simply felt that there was always a gap between her and the Reincarnation Group, and that she had never fully fit in.

But Su Lingyou wasn’t in a hurry. She knew that since she could take part in missions involving the Reincarnation Group, the Giant Corpse, Zhang Yu, and Kuang Tianqing, that meant the Immortal Emperor still valued her.

“Hmph, who else knows that Zhang Yu, now a rising star in the Myriad Laws Sect and able to visit the 33rd layer to pay his respects to the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor at any time, has secretly been appointed the Heavenly Demon Prince of our Heavenly Demon Sect?”

“So what if he’s with the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor? He’s just a lowly beast whom our Reincarnation Immortal Emperor toyed with from the lower realm all the way to the upper realm, and the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor still holds him in his hand like a Divine Sword.”

And Su Lingyou, who had once been at the Tribulation Transcendence Realm, had already been promoted to the Ascension Realm this year.

“I knew the Immortal Emperor cared about me.”

“This just goes to show that choosing the right path matters more than working hard. If I were still under the Supreme Truth traitor faction, I might have had my home raided along with everyone else last time. How could I possibly turn around and raid someone else’s home, get promoted, and break through to a higher cultivation realm?”

At that moment, Feng Su Lingyou’s signature on the social platform read: “Born into the Primordial Immortal Clan, I was trapped and could not be myself. Only after escaping did I learn who I truly am.”

“So what if I was born into a traitor’s family? No matter how harsh the circumstances, I made it through.”

And now, as she looked at the information appearing before her, she knew she had once again been entrusted with an important responsibility.

“Welcome the new Book-Wielder?”

With the Book-Wielder Assembly about to begin, the Reincarnation Immortal Emperor had unexpectedly replaced the previous Book-Wielder, sparking plenty of discussion within the sect.

Everyone was doing their best to investigate the new Book-Wielder’s identity and gather information.

But...

Su Lingyou thought to herself, “A brand-new certificate, a brand-new file, a brand-new sect household registration...”

“Other than the name Wang Guiren and the fact that he’s at the Ascension Realm, almost everything else is a blank.”

“All we know is that he spent years cultivating on the 18th underground layer. Could he be...?”

At that moment, Su Lingyou couldn’t help thinking of a rumor that circulated within the Reincarnation Group.

“They say the Immortal Emperor detained a group of ancient powerhouses in the Netherworld, where they cultivated and learned the latest Immortal Dao Technology, eventually becoming a mysterious force that no other sect could detect.”

“Could this Wang Guiren be a powerhouse from the Netherworld?”

As Su Lingyou pondered this, she sent down an avatar, appearing on the Heavenly Demon Sect’s territory on the 11th layer.

Looking at the young man before her, who was called Wang Guiren, Su Lingyou introduced herself: “Heavenly Monarch Wang, the Immortal Emperor has sent me to assist you with the handover.”

The young cultivator before her gazed around at the sect’s surroundings with a wistful expression. “When I left last time, I thought it would be a long time before I could return. I never imagined how unpredictable the world could be…”

As Su Lingyou sensed his gaze sweeping over the area, she realized that he seemed to have just been observing several places where the spatial structure was abnormal. They were the ruins left behind by the multitude of Immortals during the Great War that Ying Xintian fought many years ago.

Just then, Wang Guiren turned around and looked at Su Lingyou. “Heavenly Monarch, please lead the way.”

A moment later, Su Lingyou had brought him to their destination, where they met the “former Book-Wielder.”

Su Lingyou knew a thing or two about this “former Book-Wielder.” He had been in charge of the Self-Slaughter Law Edicts, able both to prohibit all kinds of suicide and self-harm, greatly reducing the sect’s asset risks, and to impose self-slaughter punishments when necessary, speeding up the efficiency of reincarnation and dynamically adjusting the population structure at each layer.

He was also a staunch member of the Reincarnation Dao Lineage Faction, with seniority surpassing even hers.

Su Lingyou thought to herself, *“The Immortal Emperor sent me here to back this Wang Guiren and help him take over the Book-Wielder’s position, right?”*

Just as Su Lingyou was thinking this, she saw the former Book-Wielder summon a group of projections of lower-tier cultivators and say, “They have all the books. The relevant test records and experiment logs are all stored in their seas of consciousness…”

Seeing how readily the other party was handing everything over, Su Lingyou thought, *“It seems I was worrying over nothing. Who among the Immortal Emperor’s followers would go against the tide? Cooperating with the Immortal Emperor is only natural.”*

At the same time, though, Su Lingyou felt a little surprised. “You’re having these people hold the books for you?”

The former Book-Wielder chuckled. “When it comes to books, I only collect them. I don’t handle them.”

At this, Su Lingyou recalled the rumors about the Immortal Ancestor’s revival and thought to herself, *“It looks like attending this assembly may not be such a good idea after all.”*

Just then, Wang Guiren reached out and pulled something out. A stack of books appeared in his hand, and he said calmly, “They’re just a few books. I can carry them myself.”

Su Lingyou was startled. *“He saw the projection and used the Spirit Realm to transport things out of the other person’s body? Without triggering any restrictions? That would require him to identify the other person’s true identity and physical address in an instant, while avoiding layer upon layer of surveillance.”*

From that single action, Su Lingyou saw evidence of no less than three distinct statutory violations: doxxing, theft, and firewall circumvention.

But committing a crime was nothing. The key was being able to commit one without getting caught.

“Can he really do that? Then wouldn’t most places in the entire sect be a free shopping spree for him? He can just take whatever he wants?”

But Su Lingyou quickly realized that powerful people of the same level had stronger defenses, so it probably wouldn’t be that easy to take things from them.

Besides, many valuable items would surely have tracking measures. Even if no one noticed at the time, the theft would soon be traced.

And if there were large-scale irregularities in someone’s asset records, those would also be investigated before long...

Even so, even with all these limitations in mind, Wang Guiren now seemed unfathomable to Su Lingyou.

Meanwhile, information about Wang Guiren was also being scrutinized by all kinds of people through various channels.

---

Within the White Bone Cult.

Through the Soul Cultivator Dao Lineage’s channels, Bai Gusheng was looking over information about Wang Guiren.

“An unknown figure who appeared out of nowhere? But if the Reincarnation Immortal Emperor specifically sent him to take part in this assembly, his strength is probably nothing to scoff at.”

After some thought, Bai Gusheng ranked Wang Guiren fifth among the book-wielders of the various sects, placing him in the middle.

Next, Bai Gusheng looked at Zhang Yu, whom he had ranked tenth, and contacted him. “Profound Monarch Zhang, are you still unwilling to trade Books?”

Seeing that the other party still refused, Bai Gusheng thought to himself, “He’s clearly only a Tribulation Transcendence Cultivator, ranked last among the ten of us. What exactly is he relying on?”

Though he hadn’t managed to complete the trade, Bai Gusheng had at least sounded him out... Zhang Yu must have some kind of trump card.

Recalling the rumors that Zhang Yu often met with the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor and went in and out of the 33rd layer, Bai Gusheng still moved Zhang Yu up one place, tying him for ninth with another person.

Although Zhang Yu had many unusual qualities, there was still a gap of one cultivation realm between them, so Bai Gusheng could not think too highly of him.

As he pondered, his gaze turned to himself, ranked first. “At this assembly, the strongest competitors are ultimately the wielders of the Four Books.”

“Leaving Zhang Yu aside, that would be me, holder of the Transformation Book, the holder of the Ascension Book from the Netherworld Palace, and the holder of the Flight Book from the Heavenly Sword Sect.”

Just then, Bai Gusheng looked at the message appearing before him and took a deep breath. “Time to set out.”

---

Inside the Myriad Laws Sect, Zhang Yu was reading the intelligence Bai Zhenzhen had left him earlier.

“The holder of the Flight Book is Tian Feibao, an Ascension Realm cultivator from the Heavenly Sword Sect. He was surrounded by the Spirit Realm before he was even born. He started livestreaming from inside the womb and earned his first fortune that way, becoming the first person in Kunxu to livestream from the placenta. Back then, he was known as the Heavenly Sword’s Auspicious Omen.”

“After he entered the Immortal Dao and began cultivating the sword dao, he swore that the sword in his hand would challenge only those stronger than himself.”

“After that, in the Spirit Realm, he had Foundation Establishment cultivators curse Golden Core cultivators and Nascent Soul cultivators insult Void Refinement cultivators. Time and again, he used the weak to challenge the strong, successfully stirring up the emotions of myriad people. He was regarded as the Heavenly Sword Sect’s Innate Sword Dao Sacred Body...”

“According to A-Zhen, Tian Feibao believed that in the Spirit Realm, provoking anyone weaker than yourself would only let them ride your coattails and lower your own standing. Only by constantly provoking people stronger than yourself could you ride their coattails and raise your own standing. Later, this theory was also incorporated into many of the Heavenly Sword Sect’s sword dao cultivation arts.”

As Zhang Yu read through the intelligence, he also set aside a sliver of his consciousness to inspect the preparations he had made for this assembly.

Inside the Immortal Grotto-Heaven, a full 128 of Zhang Yu’s clones had gathered. Their bodies were a riot of color, armed to the teeth with all manner of magical artifacts and bionics.

This was one of the many results of Zhang Yu’s billion-unit investment.

In addition, Zhang Yu was continuously absorbing a vast amount of bloodline information.

After Immortal Emperor Supreme Truth had been dispossessed, her bloodline information had scattered across the various layers and sects. Zhang Yu had spent a great deal of money to buy it all back.

After devouring without pause day and night, Zhang Yu’s [Emperor Creation Seed] finally leveled up once more, reaching level 9. Upon reaching level 9, the [Emperor Creation Seed] helped Zhang Yu break through the limit again, raising the level cap of all cultivation arts by another level. “Now I can push several important cultivation arts from level 71 to level 72.”

Zhang Yu sighed inwardly, “Sure enough, having money lets you improve your strength faster. This is especially true if I want to collect more of Senior Supreme Truth’s bloodline information — I can’t do without Immortal Coins.”

Just then, sensing that the time had come, Zhang Yu’s gaze shifted. He thought, “It’s time to set out.”

But before leaving to attend the assembly, Zhang Yu still had to make his final report before departure, as the Immortal Emperor had requested.

As he had when he last paid his respects to the emperor, Zhang Yu made some preparations, then arrived outside the Grotto-Heaven on the 16th layer. He summoned the gate leading to the 33rd layer and, as the Immortal Emperor had requested, made his final report before departure.

Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor: “You’re more or less ready, aren’t you?”

Even though he couldn’t see the other party, Zhang Yu still felt an unfathomable pressure, along with a darkness that seemed capable of swallowing even his thoughts.

Zhang Yu did not dare raise his head, much less entertain any wild thoughts. He simply said respectfully, “I will not fail in my mission.”

The Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor said indifferently, “Do you know the significance of this assembly?”

Zhang Yu replied, “I do not.”

The Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor said, “You have probably heard the rumors of the Immortal Ancestor’s revival.”

“These are not false rumors, but they are not entirely accurate, either.”

“The union of the Four Books this time will indeed awaken the Immortal Ancestor, but not to bring him back to life. Rather, it is to find him a successor.”

Zhang Yu exclaimed in surprise, “A successor?”

The Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor said, “Once the seals on the Four Books are lifted and the books converge, they will trigger the opening of the Immortal Ancestor’s legacy and begin the selection process for his successor. The next generation of the Immortal Ancestor’s successor will be chosen to inherit the power of all the books.”

“The book-wielders of the Ten Sects attending this assembly are the ten candidates to become the Immortal Ancestor’s successor.”

“The last one among you to emerge victorious will obtain the Immortal Ancestor’s final legacy.”

“Zhang Yu, if you can become the Immortal Ancestor’s successor, I will grant you a Heaven-Opening cultivation art, promote you to Rank 9 Sect Staff, award you an Ascension Realm certificate, and recommend you for the next Ascension Selection, where you can compete for the chance to achieve Immortality.”

Hearing these words and this string of rewards, even Zhang Yu couldn’t help but feel shaken.

When Zhang Yu returned to the sect layers, still tense from head to toe, the Immortal Emperor’s words continued to echo in his mind.

“Become the Immortal Ancestor’s successor? Can something that good really be true?”

On his way to the assembly, Zhang Yu reviewed the memories within him once more and thought to himself, “That audience with the Immortal Emperor went just like last time, right? He didn’t notice anything, did he?”

But as he recalled what the Immortal Emperor had said, a chill crept over Zhang Yu. “If the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor was telling the truth, and this assembly really is meant to select a successor to the so-called Immortal Ancestor, then this is basically a proxy war, isn’t it?”

“The ten Immortal Emperors each send out a representative to compete for the Immortal Ancestor’s Legacy.”

“And the ultimate victor... will be eaten up, bones and all, by the Immortal Emperor?”

“Have me seize the legacy, then get promoted and break through, then take part in the Ascension Selection and attain an Immortal Position — is this their way of giving me extra growth feed?”

Immortal Slayer said, “If we seize the Immortal Ancestor’s legacy, they’ll recommend us for the Ascension Selection? Doesn’t that mean there are fewer than 19 years left?”

“Then does that mean that once we gather the Four Books, the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor will eagerly push us to achieve Immortality, and then eat us?”

Zhang Yu gave a slight nod inwardly. At this moment, he could almost see the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor slowly opening his enormous, blood-red maw toward him, while he had no choice but to charge toward it, step by step.

Fu Ji said anxiously, “This means that once we obtain the Immortal Ancestor’s legacy and achieve Immortality, eating us will give the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor a tremendous boost. That’s why he’s so impatient.”

“Zhang Yu, Zhang Yu, how about we find a chance to lose after all?”

Zhang Yu didn’t answer, but Fu Ji already understood his resolve.

Immortal Slayer said, “We absolutely can’t let this opportunity slip by.”

Zhang Yu knew he was right. Only by gathering the four Books would he have a chance to gain four additional Dao Lineages after achieving Immortality.

He thought to himself, “Once I get the four Books, I’ll be eager to achieve Immortality as soon as possible and master five Great Dao Lineages. Only then can I have the means to hold my own against the Ten Immortal Emperors, just as the three seniors Ying Xintian, Heavenly Virtue, and Monster Saint said.”

The next moment, Zhang Yu said calmly, “Fu Ji, the Immortal Emperor is ultimately moving faster than we are. The longer we drag this out, the more disadvantageous it will become for us.”

“Being able to take part in the Ascension Selection sooner and achieve Immortality through the proper channels is actually a great opportunity for me.” As he spoke, Zhang Yu relied on the special authorization document granted by the assembly to charge upward. He had already broken through layer upon layer of restrictions and reached the 20th layer of Kunxu.

High above the Central Apex Heaven on the 20th layer, a massive Immortal Dao construct called the Ascension Platform floated quietly at the very top of the sect layers.

This Ascension Platform was one of the venues where the Ascension Selection had been held throughout the ages.

The cultivators who had passed the Ascension Selection over the generations had ascended to Immortality here, rising to the worlds above the 20th layer.

Now that the Ascension Platform was being used to host the Book-Wielder Assembly, Zhang Yu could feel all the more how much the ten Immortal Emperors valued it.

Even from far away, he could already sense the Spirit Realm information inside and around the Ascension Platform, buzzing with activity.

Thanks to the special authorization for the event, countless cultivators who had bought tickets had already projected themselves there, surrounding the entire Ascension Platform as if seated in an audience.

Naturally, there were all kinds of advertisements and livestreams, too.

Zhang Yu had long since gotten used to this sort of thing. He wasn’t surprised in the slightest. He thought to himself, *“This is Kunxu, after all. How could they not sell tickets for something this important? Or sell ad space?”*

He even sensed announcements of what was to come amid the vast amount of information in the Spirit Realm.

*“Not only will the assembly itself be livestreamed, but the Immortal Ancestor’s revival afterward will be broadcast from start to finish? They’ve already sold all the ad slots?”*

With a casual glance, Zhang Yu saw that the livestream rights had already been sold to more than ten companies.

Those companies had also recruited many cultivators from the Heavenly Sword Sect, hoping to interview the Immortal Ancestor as soon as they emerged.

This was clearly a major event that would determine Kunxu’s future. Yet, as Zhang Yu sensed the changes at the scene, it felt more like he was watching a variety show or some entertainment event. Countless cultivators were thinking only of how to profit from it.

Zhang Yu couldn’t help thinking, *“If the day really comes when the Immortal Emperor of the End is born, perhaps that’ll be livestreamed too. Maybe some Kunxu cultivators will even risk their lives to interview the Immortal Emperor of the End and ask a question.”*

As the Spirit Realm information at the scene grew richer, and more and more cultivators descended via projections or watched the livestreams, Zhang Yu gradually sensed a series of subtle changes.

“The Dao Lineages of the Chain Formation Diagram and the others... seem to be accelerating their severance from the Books.”

Zhang Yu’s heart jolted. “Is it because so many people are paying attention? So many cultivators who carry the Chain Formation Diagram and have been influenced by Aspiration for the Immortal Dao are watching the Books converge, and that’s actually slightly speeding up the separation between the Dao Lineages and the Books? Is this also part of the Immortal Emperor’s calculations?”

With Zhang Yu’s arrival, countless cultivators’ projections immediately turned their attention to him, as did one livestream after another.

Some cultivators immediately wanted to greet Zhang Yu, some streamers wanted to ask him questions, and some companies wanted to buy advertising space on him.

But Zhang Yu rejected them all one by one. Advertisements for his own Grotto-Heaven real estate then began flashing across his body.

Just then, a streak of rainbow light pierced through heaven and earth, instantly drawing the attention of countless people.

Zhang Yu looked up and thought to himself, “An Ascension Realm cultivator from the Netherworld Palace — the holder of the Ascension Book, Qian Airen.”

Qian Airen spread his hand wide, and countless blood-red points of light fell across the entire venue.

The many cultivators exclaimed in surprise, “He’s throwing money?!”

“It’s Human Life Coins!”

“Quick, pick them up!”

In an instant, the whole venue erupted into commotion.

Fu Ji was also stunned by the sight. “Throwing money? There are actually upper-tier cultivators in Kunxu who throw money around like this?”

Zhang Yu explained, “Qian Airen is one of the people promoting Human Life Coins. These coins are a currency that circulates among the four Demonic Path sects: Netherworld, Heavenly Demon, White Bone, and Joyous Union. The currency is backed by Material Humans, and each Human Life Coin is equivalent to the life of one Material Human.”

“Qian Airen is using this opportunity to promote Human Life Coins…”

Just then, a surge of fiend energy shot into the sky, sending streaks of dark green light raining down across the venue. A voice rang out: “Material Humans are only widely used by your Netherworld, Heavenly Demon, White Bone, and Joyous Union sects. How can they compare to the far broader reach of our Celestial Monster Sect’s Monster Life Coins?”

“These days, all the great sects are recruiting monsterfolk talents. We monsterfolk are making our mark everywhere and have become indispensable to the great sects. Tell me, which of the ten great sects doesn’t need monsterfolk?”

Zhang Yu looked toward that towering surge of monsterfolk energy, and the relevant information had already flashed through his mind: *“Wan Yaosheng of the Celestial Monster Sect is a mixed-blood monster who carries the bloodlines of 99 kinds of monsterfolk. For the past few years, he’s been competing against Qian Airen and promoting the Celestial Monster Sect’s Monster Life Coin.”*

Transformed into a streak of light, Qian Airen let out a cold snort. “The Ascension Platform was perfectly fine, and you’ve turned the whole place foul with your mongrel stink.”

With one Book-Wielder after another taking the stage, the atmosphere seemed to grow ever more charged, and viewership in each livestream kept climbing.

The Books of Feathered Ascension, the Immortal Ancestor’s revival — after wave upon wave of rumors, gossip, and sensational news, all of these had drawn the attention of countless cultivators.

In particular, the previously established connection between the Chain Formation Diagram and the Book had made many cultivators realize that this event could be a major turning point for Kunxu.

At last, with the arrival of the final Book-Wielder, the Book-Wielder Assembly was about to begin.

One by one, titles appeared in the sky.

“Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor, Reincarnation Immortal Emperor, Heavenly Sword Immortal Emperor, Buddha Emperor...”

As the names of the ten Immortal Emperors appeared one by one, standing in the ten directions and encircling the entire venue, all the cultivators present understood that the will of the Ten Immortal Emperors had turned its attention here.

The fact that they showed only their names, without revealing any power or form, was an act of mercy toward the world on the part of the ten Immortal Emperors.

The Book-Wielder Assembly officially began!

An invisible ripple emanated from every Book-Wielder. At that moment... every Book-Wielder, including Zhang Yu, could feel the Books they held and the Books held by everyone else drawing intensely toward one another — a pull unlike anything they had ever felt before.

It was as if a long-suppressed power had burst forth from each Book, enveloping all the book-wielders.

The next moment, a line of text appeared in Zhang Yu’s mind.

*“Are you willing to become my successor?”*
