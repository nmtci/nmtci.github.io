# Chapter 1004: Making the Immortal Emperor Take the Blame

Watching Zhang Yu’s series of moves as he snatched the books right out of everyone’s hands, Fu Ji thought to herself, *“Zhang Yu must be feeling pretty pleased with himself right now, huh? When all is said and done, could the things the Sect Examination Clone says simply be Zhang Yu’s own hidden thoughts?”*

*“Should I seize the opportunity to… butter him up? Praise his tactics?”*

Just as Fu Ji was pondering this, Herald God said, “Profound Monarch, your plan today was not only meticulously arranged, but also executed with ruthless efficiency. Even the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor was caught up in your scheme, and you completely outmaneuvered these sect prodigies.”

*“You brat! How dare you steal my chance to flatter him?”* Fu Ji cursed under her breath. Alarm bells rang in her mind. *“Herald Guy… This guy’s been getting more and more ambitious lately. Is he trying to take my place?”*

At the same time, Auspicious Guy chimed in from the other side. “Exactly! Exactly! You pretended to enter the gate leading to the 33rd layer, but in reality, you teleported to the depths of the Immortal Grotto-Heaven, where you checked and merged all the books you’d collected. You invoked the Immortal Emperor’s authority to command all the cultivators! Truly brilliant!”

Immortal Slayer analyzed, “I wouldn’t call it brilliant. It’s more like a simple, heavy-handed approach. But sometimes, simple and heavy-handed methods can have remarkable effects.”

“Another key point is that our control over spatial technology is now powerful enough that no one here noticed we never entered the 33rd layer — we teleported straight into the depths of the Immortal Grotto-Heaven.”

Zhang Yu nodded slightly to himself, understanding that this was a bonus from his progress in seeking the Dao of the World-Domain Dao Lineage, which had surged repeatedly.

It meant that, in front of everyone, he could perform the function of the 33rd layer Great Gate and borrow the books from those present without actually entering the 33rd layer.

After all, entering the 33rd layer could mean facing the Immortal Emperor directly, which was always a bit of a hassle for Zhang Yu.

The Sect Examination Clone reminded him, “Those Sect Brutes have lost their books. They definitely won’t let this go. I know people like them too well — they must be figuring out what to do right now.”

Just as the Sect Examination Clone had warned, the many cultivators in the conference room were indeed considering how to respond.

And the first step they took was almost identical... gathering intelligence.

Cang Juehuan stared fixedly at the gate leading to the 33rd layer, and the Spirit Manipulation Technique within him abruptly sprang into action. “Brothers! Get to work!”

Tens of thousands of High-Intelligence Artifact Spirits abruptly awakened. Every last one of them was a good brother of Cang Juehuan’s.

Like thousands upon thousands of Celestial Inquiry Stars, they observed the spatial structure of the 33rd layer Great Gate.

Every day these High-Intelligence Artifact Spirits spent being tempered by Cang Juehuan, and with every large circulation of magic power... the dust in their hearts was swept away, unnecessary memories were cleared, and important work experience was retained. Day by day, they grew stronger.

In the perception of these High-Intelligence Artifact Spirits, each day they awoke was the first day they had gained Artifact Spirit Human Rights. They were full of drive and fighting spirit, combining the strengths of both humans and High-Intelligence Artifact Spirits.

In an instant, Cang Juehuan recorded an enormous amount of data. It then crossed the Dharma Realm and reached his private Calculation Pool, where it was analyzed and compared with the various datasets on spatial gates leading to higher levels that the Artifact Spirit Faction had collected.

*“The calculations are complete. This Spatial Gate does indeed point toward a place beyond the 30th layer of Kunxu.”*

*“So it really was that Emperor Beast! Only with his permission could they have opened a gate for Zhang Yu up there.”*

In Cang Juehuan’s eyes, this was clearly something the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor had arranged long ago. He had even specially approved a teleportation gate for Zhang Yu, specifically so he could openly snatch their books.

*“The Immortal Emperor himself has moved against us. We can hardly be blamed for losing this fight.”*

*“And there’s no point expecting that Emperor Beast to spit out anything he’s swallowed.”*

In an instant, numerous High-Intelligence Artifact Spirits sorted through and summarized one historical account after another about the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor. Cang Juehuan had already reached a decision.

*“Rather than obsess over sunk costs and keep getting entangled in this, I might as well seize this opportunity and try to gain something from the upcoming meeting.”*

With that thought, Cang Juehuan immediately changed his stance. With a thump, he was already kneeling in the direction of the 33rd layer Immortal Gate.

“Grandpa Immortal Emperor! Actually, as soon as I learned the sect needed books, I wanted to donate them of my own accord.”

“Minister Zhang, you’ve done a great job collecting these books! The sect can just keep them and put them to use. There’s no need to return them to me!”

Cang Juehuan’s actions immediately drew everyone’s attention.

A member of the book group sent him a message: “Are you scared?”

Cang Juehuan didn’t reply. Instead, he thought to himself, *“The Immortal Emperor is opening the gate... Who the hell wouldn’t be scared?”*

Meanwhile, elsewhere, Lianzi Zi, from the pharmaceutical company, circulated the magic power within his body and activated one of his forbidden techniques: the Myriad Manifestations Wisdom Eye.

This was a Dharma Realm Daoist Art maintained by the Immortal Class, capable of identifying all things.

Lianzi Zi was using it to identify Zhang Yu, hoping to get a clearer look at Zhang Yu’s origins and gather information about him.

*“Wisdom Eye! Open!”*

In an instant, it seemed as though a hazy layer of information had appeared over the entire world.

However, Lianzi Zi paid no attention to this indistinct information. He knew that, because of an account anomaly, his Myriad Manifestations Wisdom Eye had become a forbidden technique in his possession.

But as an upper-tier Tribulation Transcendence cultivator of the sect, he naturally had plenty of ways to use forbidden techniques.

He fixed his gaze on Zhang Yu before him.

*“Transfer the cost! Adopted sons, lend me your mystic powers!”*

With a mere thought, payment links were sent to his many adopted sons and subordinates.

Soon, his adopted sons began replying with one quote after another.

Lianzi Zi thought to himself, **“The analysis services for cultivators across Kunxu range from the absolute cheapest fee of 0.1 Immortal Coins, to several million for formidable Void Refinement and Tribulation Transcendence cultivators. Some cultivators in the Ascension Realm even charge tens of millions of Immortal Coins for an analysis…”*

Lianzi Zi knew he didn’t actually need to pay the analysis fee. He only needed a rough price to judge how deep the target cultivator’s roots went.

It was precisely because he had used the Myriad Manifestations Wisdom Eye this way for so long that his account had become anomalous, turning this Dharma Realm Daoist Art into a forbidden technique.

But in the next moment, as he looked at the quotes his adopted sons had sent him, he froze.

*“520,000,000? 500,000,000? 530,000,000?”*

Although the price varied slightly depending on how badly the user was gouged, the common denominator was obvious.

*“The analysis service for Profound Monarch Zhang actually costs over 500,000,000?!”*

At that moment, Lianzi Zi prominently marked Zhang Yu’s price as 500,000,000 Immortal Coins. In his eyes, Zhang Yu’s figure seemed impossibly imposing.

In all these years, Lianzi Zi had never investigated anyone whose analysis service cost as much as 500,000,000.

Perhaps an Immortal’s analysis would cost 500,000,000, but Lianzi Zi wouldn’t dare use this trick on one. The moment he activated it… with cause and effect set in motion, the other party would likely immediately receive information that they were being observed, then strike back.

Lianzi Zi was deeply shocked. *“Is Zhang Yu’s analysis level on par with an Immortal’s? Impossible… No Tribulation Transcendence cultivator could be like that.”*

*“That leaves only one possibility: something that could raise his analysis price a hundredfold…”*

He glanced once more at the entrance to the 33rd layer, and Lianzi Zi reached a conclusion. *“This person must have a deep connection with the Immortal Emperor, with a direct channel to the heavens. His social standing is as high as the 33rd layer, and he carries a faint connection to the Immortal Emperor. That’s why he can command this price.”*

He sighed inwardly. *“Our books really may have reached the 33rd layer. What Zhang Yu did today was the Immortal Emperor personally stepping in to back him up.”*

*“Even so... he only took our books and didn’t charge us a single cent extra. Profound Monarch Zhang is a man of virtue.”*

*“If we keep resisting now, it’ll be like a mantis trying to stop a chariot. Better to use this chance to get closer to him. Maybe we can even get a share of the pie when it comes to creating the Chain Formation Diagram.”*

In principle, Lianzi Zi didn’t want anyone taking his things, but now the principle himself was standing against him.

Thinking this, Lianzi Zi promptly stepped forward and said, “Minister Zhang, I’d like to donate my books to the sect too. You don’t need to return them.”

At the same time, Lianzi Zi glanced through the chat history of the group called “Myriad Laws Book Friends Group” and chose to send Zhang Yu... every single message from the others.

He sent Zhang Yu a private message: “Minister Zhang, I’m not like these anti-sect troublemakers. I’ve always supported you collecting books!

“From now on, I’ll keep an eye on them for you. If anyone still has books, I’ll report them to you immediately.”

He deliberately circled Cang Juehuan’s chat messages in the book friends’ group and said, “This Cang Juehuan loves stirring things up in the group.”

Zhang Yu glanced at them, then looked at the messages the others had just sent him.

Cang Juehuan: “It’s this Lianzi Zi who’s been trying to keep raising the price on you and delay the pace at which you collect books, so he can sell the books he has for a better price! Besides the book he brought today, he’s also hoarded two more in his Immortal Abode.”

Group owner: “Profound Monarch Zhang, why don’t you take over as group owner? Who could possibly be more suited to lead our Myriad Laws Book Friends Group than you?”

As the many cultivators present used their various methods one after another, some saw a few surface-level details, while others uncovered deeper information. But without exception... in the end, they all backed down.

Seeing so many cultivators cowed in an instant by Zhang Yu alone, Bu Yingshu’s gaze toward him gradually changed. “Meetings really are a great idea. I’ve heard the Immortal Emperor likes holding meetings too. From now on, I should hold more meetings with other members of the Immortal Clan.”

Zhang Yu looked at the cultivators, all wearing smiles, and sensed the hundred billion flying swords within him. A feeling suddenly welled up in his heart: though he had a sword in hand, he had no one to fight.

*“It’s a pity that although I have a divine sword in hand, I have no chance to put it to use.”*

The meeting continued. Zhang Yu said, “I called everyone here today mainly to discuss what kind of Chain Formation Diagram Kunxu needs. There’s no need to be shy — feel free to share any ideas you have. We can learn from one another and improve together.”

Lianzi Zi thought to himself, *“I hope the meeting really is mainly about creating a Chain Formation Diagram, and not about taking our books.”*

Zhang Yu spoke about all sorts of principles behind creating Chain Formation Diagrams — serving the sect, Kunxu, the Immortal Emperor... To the many cultivators listening, it all sounded like worn-out clichés.

But not a single cultivator present showed any impatience. Each one looked earnestly toward Zhang Yu, as if hanging on every word he said.

And they really were listening to Zhang Yu — they just weren’t listening to his speech in the meeting room, but to the private messages he was sending them.

Zhang Yu: “Cang Juehuan, Profound Monarch Cang, the people above are especially impressed by your research into High-Intelligence Artifact Spirits. What do you think about developing Chain Formation Diagrams in that direction? I know little about this field, and I’d like to ask you about some cultivation arts.”

Cang Juehuan’s heart jolted. *“So it’s true: minor matters get discussed in meetings, while major ones are handled in private chats. What Minister Zhang discussed with me privately today must be the key. Has my research... finally caught the attention of the higher-ups?!”*

*“Wait, a consultation about cultivation arts?!”*

*“Could it be... could one of these cultivation arts be a candidate for the Chain Formation Diagram Zhang Yu wants to create?!”*

Looking at the list Zhang Yu had sent him, Cang Juehuan’s heart skipped a beat. The next moment, a series of intense emotions surged through him. *“No, that’s not right. It can’t be that simple. Zhang Yu wouldn’t tell me this kind of information so easily.”*

Though he kept warning himself, lured by the enormous potential gains, Cang Juehuan was gradually swept along by the greed stirred up by his Immortal Dao Aspiration.

On the other side, Lianzi Zi was equally shaken by the private message Zhang Yu had sent him.

Zhang Yu: “The pharmaceutical industry has always been a major focus for the sect. However, I have limited experience in this area, and there are some questions I’d like to ask an expert like you about — for instance, a few cultivation arts I’ve been studying lately...”

Lianzi Zi thought to himself, *“Th-this... could these cultivation arts be the ones that are going to be added to the Chain Formation Diagram this time?”*

*“They’re all cheap cultivation arts, and they don’t cost much anyway. Should I buy out the copyrights first and stockpile them?”*

At the same time, as Zhang Yu chatted with everyone in the book club group, he looked at the Dharma Realm information appearing before him and thought, *“Oh? Someone’s already asking about the price for the full copyrights to these cultivation arts?”*

The reason Zhang Yu had received this information was that he had already arranged for someone to hold the copyrights on his behalf, stockpiling quite a few cultivation arts.

These cultivation arts were all obscure works that hardly anyone bought, so even their full copyrights were incredibly cheap.

But right now, quite a few cultivators from the book club group were sending people to ask about the full copyright prices for these cultivation arts, hoping to become their copyright holders.

Fu Ji burst out laughing. “These people are truly blinded by greed. They can’t wait to stockpile the copyrights, then make a killing once the Chain Formation Diagram comes out?”

Zhang Yu said, “They’re only asking about the prices. They haven’t taken the bait completely yet, but it’s only a matter of time.”

Watching the prices quoted by one prospective buyer after another gradually rise, Zhang Yu felt as though he were drawing lines by hand on the price charts for one cultivation art after another. The price trends of each cultivation art... would all change in accordance with the lines he drew.

Zhang Yu thought to himself, “So this is what it feels like to have the Immortal Emperor as your backing? A few casual remarks can bring in countless Immortal Coins.”

Immortal Slayer said, “That time, when the Immortal Emperor had us act as the Myriad Laws Divine Sword, we took the blame for him. But now the Immortal Emperor has become the Myriad Laws Divine Sword in our hands, so naturally he’ll take the blame for us.”

Fu Ji said delightedly, “They say it’s hard to swindle money out of rich people, but with the Immortal Emperor behind us, it really is convenient to fleece these wealthy sect guys.”

The Sect Examination Clone sighed, “We’ve made use of less than one percent of the Immortal Emperor’s potential so far.”

As the meeting came to an end, many cultivators recalled what Zhang Yu had privately told them on their way out. Their hearts were full of excitement. Each felt that they had established a close connection with Zhang Yu and gained important information, as if a chance at immense wealth and immortal fortune were about to fall right onto their heads.

But every cultivator remained calm. Neither their expressions nor their words betrayed the excitement in their hearts.

Cang Juehuan kept reminding himself: *“We absolutely must not let anything about today slip out.”*

Watching the cultivators depart, the Sect Examination Clone laughed. “For the cultivators of Kunxu, the more you tell them what they mustn’t say, the more likely they are to sell it as intelligence.”

“But as long as you make them think the news could make them rich, they’ll naturally keep their mouths shut, even if it kills them.”

Fu Ji glanced at the price trends for the cultivation arts. The cultivation-art copyrights Zhang Yu had spent roughly 5,000,000 stockpiling had already risen 20% in a short time.

Fu Ji sighed inwardly. *“Damn... setting policy to make money yourself feels great.”*

Fu Ji asked, “Zhang Yu, how much higher are you planning to let the prices go before you sell?”

Zhang Yu said, “I’ll let the copyright prices keep soaring for a while.”

Just then, Bu Yingshu came over, looking smug. “Well, Little Yu? I did a pretty good job cooperating today, didn’t I?”

Today, Bu Yingshu had brought the Immortal Clan representatives along to work with Zhang Yu, leading the way in the first wave by handing over the books they held.

Zhang Yu said, “Profound Monarch Bu, thanks to your support, everything went smoothly today.”

Bu Yingshu thought to herself, *“I should ask Zhang Yu about the information on the cultivation arts he considered for creating the Chain Formation Diagram. Ideally, I can ask about the specific candidates...”*

But thinking about how she had to ask this former underling and little pet of hers for information made Bu Yingshu feel uncomfortable all over.

*“Honestly, I came all this way to help him and show him my support. Couldn’t he be a little more proactive?”*

In Bu Yingshu’s imagination, Zhang Yu should have warmly and excitedly hosted her, bragging about how impressive she’d been when she used to look out for him.

Then, without Bu Yingshu having to say much, Zhang Yu would volunteer the cultivation arts he was considering for creating the Chain Formation Diagram.

*“...Then I’d stop him and tell him that this is a sect secret. How could he reveal it to just anyone? Even if I’m the most Immortal among the Immortal Clan, with hopes of ascending and becoming an Immortal, that still wouldn’t be acceptable.”*

*“Next, Zhang Yu would drop to his knees right there and say to me, ‘Profound Monarch Bu, if it weren’t for you back then, I’d still be on the front lines reviewing cultivation arts at the Edict Pavilion, busting my head every month over performance targets. No matter what, I have to repay you today.’”*

*“So I'd berate him for failing to distinguish public and private matters and lacking Sect Nature, then sweep my sleeves and leave, my expression calm, leaving him kneeling on the ground, pleading desperately for Profound Monarch Bu.”*

*“When I'd returned to my Immortal Abode, I'd find a letter from Zhang Yu lying quietly in my inbox, containing all the cultivation arts currently under consideration...”*

Watching Bu Yingshu’s smile grow ever brighter, Zhang Yu couldn’t help asking, “Profound Monarch Bu, is something the matter?”

After seeing Bu Yingshu hesitate for a long while, Zhang Yu said, “I understand.”

Bu Yingshu’s spirits lifted, only to hear Zhang Yu say, “The Immortal License Plate Shuttles you lent me before — I happen to be returning them to you now.”

As he spoke, Zhang Yu’s thoughts stirred, space shifted, and the two shuttles appeared before Bu Yingshu.

While Bu Yingshu stood stunned, Zhang Yu continued, “The Chain Formation Diagram is of great importance. Many people are watching my every move right now. If I keep these two shuttles, I’m afraid the Immortal Emperor might misunderstand.”

Watching Zhang Yu’s retreating figure, Bu Yingshu’s thoughts churned wildly.

The Sect Examination Clone said, “Look at the way she’s eyeing it, like a puppy begging for food. You can read her intentions right on her face. If we handed her a list of cultivation arts, I bet she’d put all her money on them and buy them even if it meant going bankrupt.”

Zhang Yu said, “There’s no need.”

With today’s meeting now over, Zhang Yu knew that there would inevitably be waves of repercussions to come.

For instance, the steady stream of friend requests, the growing flood of messages, invitations to all kinds of meetings and inspections, and recommendations for cultivation arts arriving in his inbox...

But for the moment, Zhang Yu simply sat back and let those ripples pass, returning instead to the deepest part of the Immortal Grotto-Heaven to sort through the books he had acquired this time.

“In total, I seized 21 books on the spot: 18 low-grade Books and 3 mid-grade Books...”

At this point, the Dao-Seeking Progress for the World-Domain Dao Lineage on Zhang Yu’s Immortal Foundation Chapter had already surged to 100%, with another 42% of Dao-Seeking Progress left over to use on another Dao Lineage.

Zhang Yu couldn’t help but marvel: “With the Feather Book in hand, and the title of Immortal Emperor, I can summon the sect’s resources to pour into me. No wonder cultivation is progressing so fast.”

As the Dao-Seeking Progress of the World-Domain Dao Lineage reached 100%, Zhang Yu could feel his spatial technology had received another epic-level boost.

“Now, perhaps I only need to build up power for a few decades to move an entire layer of Kunxu — for example, shifting the 3rd layer to the 5th, or moving the 5th layer to the 10th.”

“Most of the restrictions in Kunxu, and most of the seals on its grotto-heavens, are already ineffective against me.”

At this moment, Zhang Yu felt that with each breath, as magic power circulated through his body, the whole world was resonating with him. The spatial structure of all Kunxu seemed to be gradually merging with him, coming under his guidance.

“In this state, I can even interfere with my opponent’s spatial technology, causing their teleportation and transportation, as well as the expansion, contraction, and distortion of space, to go awry.”

“I can even temporarily seal away my opponent’s spatial technology. That is the boon granted by the World-Domain Dao Lineage.”

Of course, Zhang Yu could also sense that although his Dao-Seeking Progress had reached 100%, that merely meant he had met the requirements to parasitize this Dao Lineage. When he achieved immortality and established his Dao, he would be able to borrow the World-Domain Dao Lineage to establish his own.

“This is still a huge distance from being the true master of a Dao Lineage. After all, the World-Domain Dao Lineage stands at the pinnacle of all spatial technology in Kunxu. Its true power goes far beyond this.”

After sensing it for a while, Zhang Yu did not rush to test the new modules and functions brought by the 21 books. Instead, he looked at the writing that had appeared in the Feather Book.

“After the Feather Book merged with several of the books, quite a bit of writing appeared.”

Zhang Yu thought to himself, “Is this also a message from my future self, transmitted to the present through the Feather Book? But this time, the writing doesn’t seem to disappear automatically. It can remain in the Feather Book indefinitely. What could be the reason for that?”

As he pondered, Zhang Yu continued to carefully read the contents of the lines of text that had appeared in the Feather Book.

The first thing he read was the writing that had appeared in the Feather Book after it merged with the Great Sage Book.

“Does this record some historical events? Some secrets concerning the Monster Saints?”

Zhang Yu summarized, “The Second Monster Saint shattered the firmament with his strength. After establishing a Dao Lineage, he fought the assembled Immortals. Though he was suppressed, the battle also fused the various realms of Kunlun into one, turning the world into a boundless chaos in which the creatures of Heaven and Earth could scarcely survive.”

Immortal Slayer asked in confusion, “The realms joined into one? Heaven and Earth became chaos? There are no records of anything like this in Kunxu’s history. How ancient was this?”

Zhang Yu continued his summary. “The Third Monster Saint established a Dao Lineage and founded a Monster Kingdom, turning all the creatures of Heaven and Earth into monsters…”

Sect Examination Clone said, “Is this saying… that the Third Monster Saint infused the bloodline of the monsterfolk into all living beings, turning everyone into monsters? That doesn’t seem to match the current conclusions of the Immortal Research Realm, either. Humans and monsterfolk are clearly distinct in their bloodlines.”

Zhang Yu shook his head and continued reading and summarizing. “The Fourth Monster Saint established the Kunlun Ruins, with endlessly many levels above and below, making it impossible for the assembled Immortals to keep them in check…” [TL note: Kunlun turned into Kunxu, "xu" in Kunxu means ruins, so Kunxu is now confirmed to be short for Kunlun Ruins]

“The Fifth Monster Saint created a Pure Land, shielding one region from interference by later Dao Lineages in order to study the heavenly principles at the most fundamental level…”

“The Sixth Monster Saint removed flesh and blood, leaving only Spirit-Preserving Bodies, so that all the people of the world would no longer be divided by bloodline or have any material needs…”

Looking at the text, Zhang Yu only felt that the more he read, the more absurd it became. “It seems to be chronicling one Monster Saint after another, establishing astonishing Dao Lineages throughout Kunxu’s history and carrying out major deeds that affected the whole world. But did these things really happen?”

“If these Monster Saints truly accomplished such astonishing feats and had such a dramatic impact on the world, then Kunxu today shouldn’t look the way it does. Their Dao Lineages... seem to have left no trace at all.”

The Sect Examination Clone speculated, “Perhaps everything was correct, and then the Immortal Emperors erased all traces of it...”

“There’s another possibility,” said Immortal Slayer. “What these texts describe is lost history — the history before each World Rollback.”

The Sect Examination Clone said, “But if it’s lost history from before a rollback, why hasn’t this information disappeared?”

Zhang Yu’s heart stirred. “Maybe... because the time limit for its disappearance has already passed.”

“Just like the future information Senior Supreme Truth sent us: once the countdown has run out, the information won’t disappear when we look at it.”
