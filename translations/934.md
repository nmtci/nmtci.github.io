# Chapter 934: Those Who Block Me Shall Be Poor!

Hearing Zhang Yu open his mouth... and immediately slap them with the massive accusation of obstructing the sect's legal reform, Lianzi Zi and Yue Qinglan both felt their hearts tighten slightly.

Nowadays, Zhang Yu could be considered a red-hot figure within the Myriad Laws Sect. The storm of legal reform stirred up by the Law Edict Expiration Date... was sweeping across the entire sect with immense momentum, and under the will of the Immortal Emperor, it was about to become an unstoppable sect policy.

Lianzi Zi and Yue Qinglan hurriedly denied it: "Not at all, not at all. Dao Monarch Zhang, your words are too heavy. How could we obstruct the sect's legal reform?"

Zhang Yu said coldly, "Then I want to inspect the Balanced-Life Grotto-Heaven to see if I will carry out the law establishment here next January. Are you going to stop me?"

Ever since Zhang Yu chose the 16th layer as the pilot zone for his Law Edict, where exactly on the 16th layer he would choose to establish a Law Edict next January as a starting point had become a topic of speculation and discussion for countless people.

Hearing that Zhang Yu wanted to choose the Balanced-Life Grotto-Heaven right now, Lianzi Zi hurriedly said, "Dao Monarch Zhang, the Balanced-Life Grotto-Heaven is just a bunch of laboratories. It lacks commerce and doesn't have any factories. It is not suitable to be the starting point for the Law Edict Pilot."

Zhang Yu said indifferently, "Whether it is suitable or not will be decided by me after I take a look. It is not your place to say."

Lianzi Zi's mind raced: *"This guy... does he really want to choose this place? Or is it because of Le Mulan?"*

*"But how could it really be for Le Mulan, a mere lower beast?"*

*"Could it be that he knows the secret Le Mulan carries? But how would he know? Where did we leave a trace?"*

Countless pieces of information and thoughts flashed wildly in his mind, but Lianzi Zi just couldn't figure out Zhang Yu's true intentions. However, there was one thing he could be sure of: *"Le Mulan's secret is too important. I absolutely cannot let Zhang Yu see Le Mulan."*

But he also didn't want to bear the heavy accusation of obstructing the sect's legal reforms...

With a thought, he spoke up, "We certainly welcome Dao Monarch Zhang's inspection. However, there are many dangerous experiments currently taking place in the minor realm. For your safety, Dao Monarch, I will go in first to make arrangements and pause all the experiments before inviting you in..."

Yue Qinglan said, "Dao Monarch Zhang, please wait a moment, we will be right back."

With that said, Lianzi Zi and Yue Qinglan's figures shifted, flashing into the Balanced-Life Grotto-Heaven.

Zhang Yu stepped forward to chase after them, but he was ultimately a step too slow and could only watch helplessly as the gate of the Minor Realm closed.

Retreating inside, Lianzi Zi looked at the closed gate and immediately ordered, "Lock the gate tightly, hide Le Mulan first, and notify the higher-ups immediately..."

Before Lianzi Zi could finish speaking, a projection suddenly drilled through from the other side of the Minor Realm Gate.

"Warning! You may be standing in the path of the Sect Defense Shuttle XXXXXXXX. Please yield immediately and do not obstruct sect affairs."

Looking at the notification floating before him, Lianzi Zi's expression darkened: "It's the Immortal Shuttle bestowed upon him by the Immortal Emperor."

Yue Qinglan said, "The gate to the minor realm belongs to the sect; it cannot stop him."

As the Immortal Shuttle's notification broadcasted, the originally tightly shut Minor Realm Gate was forced open with a loud crash, and the tip of the Immortal Shuttle crashed inside.

The Tribulation Transcendence pressure around Lianzi Zi suddenly surged, and he clapped his hands together. 

Under Lianzi Zi's channeling of energy, the newly opened Minor Realm Gate was forcibly slammed shut again.

Zhang Yu suddenly sent a message over: "Lianzi Zi! Yue Qinglan! How bold of you! You dare to hinder my inspection of the Law Edict Pilot Zone? Are you trying to rebel against the Immortal Emperor and defy sect policy?"

Lianzi Zi replied: "Dao Monarch Zhang, the Minor Realm Gate has suddenly malfunctioned. I am currently trying to figure out a solution. Don't worry, we will open the gate as soon as possible."

At the same time, Lianzi Zi sent a voice transmission to Yue Qinglan: "What are you standing around for? Go and hide Le Mulan!" Yue Qinglan's figure flashed, and she vanished without a trace.

But before Yue Qinglan could get far, Lianzi Zi, who was bracing against the Minor Realm Gate, felt a surging power sweep over from the other side.

"An expert!"

Lianzi Zi's gaze narrowed. His Bionics operated at 120% power, and hundreds of cultivation arts ran simultaneously.

But just as he was about to resist with all his might, streams of chaos-colored currents emerged. With a single strike, they blasted the gate of the Minor Realm apart, instantly knocking Lianzi Zi away, and the surging currents also sent Yue Qinglan flying.

Shocked in his heart, Lianzi Zi thought, "Too... too high!"

Immediately after, the chaos-colored currents formed the figure of Bu Yingshu, her fierce eyes glaring at Lianzi Zi.

"How bold of you! Intercepting an Immortal Shuttle? Is this still the territory of Myriad Laws?! You are simply outrageously audacious!"

Hearing Zhang Yu say that someone dared to intercept the Immortal Shuttle, Bu Yingshu had been absolutely furious.

She hated it the most when people didn't follow the rules of the Myriad Laws Sect, especially the rules she needed to use.

Seeing Bu Yingshu's appearance and the streams of chaos-colored currents, Lianzi Zi's expression turned incredibly bitter: "A super high, high, high, high, high, high, high... high-grade Dharma Realm Incarnation?"

"Just how much money did this woman spend?"

The next moment, amidst a loud boom, the entire Balanced-Life Grotto-Heaven seemed to shake violently.

And Lianzi Zi had already been kicked flying by Bu Yingshu's Dharma Realm Incarnation, crashing from one end of the Grotto-Heaven to the other with a loud bang.

"So fierce!" 

Zhang Yu, who had just stepped into the minor realm, saw this scene and felt a shock in his heart.

Although he had long known that Bu Yingshu had profound capital and had somewhat anticipated her combat power within the same rank.

But seeing Bu Yingshu, who had just broken through to Tribulation Transcendence, kick another Tribulation Transcendence cultivator flying like this still made Zhang Yu's heart pound.

"Good thing I didn't offend her before."

But in the next moment, Lianzi Zi had already transformed into a thundercloud, shooting forward like lightning. The rumbling of thunder shook the heavens and the earth, turning into rolling thunderous resonance that spread in all directions.

Lianzi Zi said, "Profound Monarch Bu! This place is a private minor realm of the Life Origin Daoist Lineage. What are you trying to do by forcefully breaking in here?"

Bu Yingshu stood in front of Zhang Yu and sneered, "Private minor realm? No matter how private it is, isn't it still on the land of the Myriad Laws Sect?"

"Zhang Yu, under the orders of the Immortal Emperor, is using the 16th layer as a Law Edict pilot zone. Now he is here to inspect the starting location of the pilot and is piloting an Immortal Shuttle to execute sect affairs. How dare you obstruct him?"

At the same time, inside and outside the minor realm, Righteous Gods had already gathered. Countless streams of divine power swept in all directions, yet none took action to stop either side.

A Seventh-Rank Righteous God said, "Leader, are we not going to stop them?"

A Fourth-Tier Righteous God said, "Lianzi Zi is, after all, one of the people in charge here. His position is completely legitimate, so it wouldn't be appropriate for us to stop him."

That Seventh-Rank Righteous God said, "I meant stopping Bu Yingshu!"

Hearing this, the Fourth-Tier Righteous God directly brought up the chat logs Bu Yingshu had sent him and said sternly, "Profound Monarch Bu has an Innate Homicide Permit, an Innate Compulsory Dual Cultivation License, an Innate Assault Permit, an Innate Robbery Permit, an Innate God-Slaying Permit... Everything she does is reasonable and lawful. What exactly are you trying to stop?"

"Everyone, keep a close eye out. If Lianzi Zi makes a move and hurts Profound Monarch Bu, you must stop him from breaking the law."

The numerous Righteous Gods received their orders in unison, "Understood!"

That Seventh-Rank Righteous God exclaimed in shock, "But Lianzi Zi also has a Homicide Permit."

The Fourth-Tier Righteous God scoffed coldly and said, "Why are you so unfamiliar with the rules? A Homicide Permit can only be used to kill people. Profound Monarch Bu belongs to the Immortal Clan. If Lianzi Zi hurts her, that would be an illegal assault on a member of the Immortal Clan."

Bu Yingshu stood in front of Zhang Yu, her Dharma Realm Incarnation crossing its arms over its chest. She scoffed coldly and said, "Zhang Yu, I'll be watching right here today. You can inspect however you want. I'd like to see who dares to stop you."

Zhang Yu looked at Lianzi Zi's hesitant appearance and felt a burst of sighing in his heart. "Although I used to think that Bu Yingshu was too troublesome, I have to say... having her cover for me feels pretty good."

The next moment, Zhang Yu's figure flashed, shooting toward a corner of the minor realm like a bolt of lightning.

He thought to himself, *"According to the intelligence from the Reincarnation Group, Le Mulan should be right in..."*

Watching the direction Zhang Yu was moving, Lianzi Zi's expression changed. *"Why is he going in that direction?"*

Lianzi Zi hurriedly followed, but felt a pressure enveloping him. It was Bu Yingshu glaring at him fiercely.

*This Bu Yingshu...* Lianzi Zi gritted his teeth and thought, *"Earlier, Zhang Yu mentioned that Le Mulan's location was told to him by Bu Yingshu. Looking at it now, not only does Zhang Yu know that Le Mulan is in this Balanced-Life Grotto-Heaven, but he also seems to know the exact location."*

*"And this Bu Yingshu has even personally stepped onto the stage..."*

Thinking of this, Lianzi Zi couldn't help but speculate in his heart, *"Could it be that Bu Yingshu is the mastermind behind the scenes? Did she learn of my sect's secrets and encourage Zhang Yu to take action?"*

Just as Lianzi Zi was rapidly thinking things over, Zhang Yu was getting closer and closer to the target location.

Seeing that the other party was about to find Le Mulan, and as Lianzi Zi was racking his brains for a countermeasure, waves of pressure descended from the sky. An oppressive force, almost tangible, enveloped every inch of space within the Balanced-Life Grotto-Heaven.

Zhang Yu and Bu Yingshu's incarnations could not help but halt, staring fixedly at the figures descending from the sky ahead of them.

Surrounded by several Tribulation Transcendence Cultivators, the incarnation of the Ascension Heavenly Monarch Yi Yaosheng slowly walked over.

Looking at this Asencion Heavenly Monarch from the Pharmaceutical Faction before her, Bu Yingshu felt the surging pressure sweep over, tightly restricting every inch of space around her incarnation.

Bu Yingshu instantly realized that in front of this Heavenly Monarch, she could not put up even the slightest bit of resistance, even if the other party had only descended as a Dharma Realm incarnation.

In an instant, Bu Yingshu's fighting spirit dropped to rock bottom, and she constantly contacted her father: "Dad! Someone is hitting me!"

Meanwhile, Zhang Yu, being in the Void Refinement Realm, was even more alarmed. He only felt as if every shred of flesh and blood in his body had been frozen, and his entire body seemed to no longer belong to him.

Zhang Yu struggled to say, "What are you trying to do? Is this still the Myriad Laws Sect? Is it still under the rule of the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor? Are you going to defy the Immortal Ascension Hall and defy the Immortal Emperor's policies?"

Yi Yaosheng said indifferently, "We wouldn't dare. It's just that this minor realm holds many secrets of the sect's pharmaceutical company, and it even involves Immortal assets. The matter is of great importance, so we ask Dao Monarch Zhang to give us a little time to pack up."

"Once we are done packing, you can inspect it for as long as you want."

"Even if we surrender the entire minor realm to you, there will be no problem."

Just as Yi Yaosheng was speaking, the Tribulation Transcendence cultivators beside him, along with Lianzi Zi, had already begun executing their respective cultivation arts to pack up the entire minor realm.

Building after building suddenly shrank and was stored away into their robes, leaving behind massive expanses of empty land.

Zhang Yu thought to himself, *"I cannot let them take Le Mulan away just like this."*

Thus, his brows furrowed as he said, "What if I don't give you the time?"

Yi Yaosheng, Lianzi Zi, and the others didn't speak, acting as if Zhang Yu was just a fly and his words were nothing but an annoying buzz.

But in the very next moment, all the cultivators at the scene abruptly turned their heads and fixed their gazes upon Zhang Yu in unison.

A flying sword was seen emerging from Zhang Yu's body, and it was none other than that Ten-Billion Flying Sword.

The flying sword had not yet been unleashed, but its sword qi was already pouring out like a waterfall, sweeping towards the accounts of everyone present.

With just this sword in hand, the current Zhang Yu made all the cultivators present feel an intense sense of threat.

At this moment, it seemed that only this Ten-Billion Flying Sword remained in the entire world. All other magical artifacts, Bionics, and flying swords... seemed to be nothing more than second-hand junk about to be sold off, and the other cultivators were merely poor ghosts on the verge of bankruptcy.

Zhang Yu: "Those! Who! Block! Me! Shall! Be! Poor!"

Before you lies a path, infinitely deep downwards and invisible upwards, and the name of this path is... poverty. Poverty, a state more terrifying than death in this Kunxu, seemed to be manifested right here in Zhang Yu's hands.

As the Ten-Billion Flying Sword advanced by an inch, the cultivators blocking Zhang Yu's way, regardless of whether they were in the Tribulation Transcendence or Ascension stage, all took a step backward in unison.
