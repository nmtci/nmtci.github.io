# Chapter 950: Special Grade Law Edict, Truly Well-Deserved

Wan Wuyi stood not far behind Zhang Yu. Looking at Zhang Yu's back, he recalled the course of their exchange just now and couldn't help but sigh in his heart, *"This Zhang Yu... he is so strong that he doesn't seem like a kid in his thirties at all."*

"Information, assets, cultivation, Law Edicts..."

"Whether it is in the domain of frontal combat, his personal application of Law Edicts, the offensive and defensive battles of information and public opinion in the Dharma Realm and Spirit Realm, or the various games played with stock prices and capital... he is impeccable in all of them. He is no longer an opponent that can be easily suppressed."

As the person in charge of a gambling platform, Wan Wuyi had seen far too many cultivators throughout his Immortal Dao career who became rich overnight and experienced a skyrocketing increase in strength in a short period of time.

However, generally speaking, such cultivators who experienced a rapid surge in strength over a short period often had many shortcomings.

And it was precisely because of these shortcomings that they would be unable to truly grasp and protect their assets. As the old saying goes, their rise was sudden, and their fall was just as swift.

But the Zhang Yu before him shattered this layer of Wan Wuyi's experience.

"In just over 30 years, he climbed from the First Layer to his current position, with almost no shortcomings anywhere on him. It is hard to believe that it has only been a short half a year since he broke through to Void Refinement and established a Law Edict..."

Wan Wuyi sighed emotionally in his heart, "Relying on just the three of us, unless we risk everything we have, it's impossible to suppress him. Not to mention, he still has that Ten-Billion Flying Sword that hasn't been unsheathed yet."

At this moment, in Wan Wuyi's heart, this Ten-Billion Flying Sword that had remained unsheathed posed a far greater threat than if Zhang Yu had used it right from the start.

And besides all this, what made Wan Wuyi feel even more danger... was the style Zhang Yu had displayed in this battle.

"Especially the flexible application of the Law Edict, as well as the coordination with the Dual Rest Law Edict..."

Wan Wuyi looked at Zhang Yu's figure, looking at the tragic sight of companies being forced to halt operations, sensing the disaster of plummeting profits and stock prices...

At this moment, a tragic history and a terrifying figure suddenly flashed through Wan Wuyi's mind.

He remembered the Halting Myriad Industries Great Formation that Ying Xintian had once used to stir up Kunxu, and the super strike that had affected the ten sects.

It was exactly after that unprecedented disaster that even the word "strike" was strictly censored in the Spirit Realm and Dharma Realm.

"I originally thought this Zhang Yu was going to moderate Myriad Laws, act as the Dad of all Law Edicts himself, and practice the law of the Dad-way."

"But looking at it now, his method of using work stoppages and production halts to coerce and threaten is more like Traitor Ying's Will of Strike from back then..."

"Hmph, since the higher-ups don't seem to be in a rush right now anyway, why should I be anxious?"

"If this continues, I'll either burn more and more money, or the blame I'll have to shoulder will get bigger and bigger..."

Thinking of this, Wan Wuyi completely lost the will to keep fighting.

Meanwhile, Wang Guanyao on the other side was still thinking rapidly, "...Have we been abandoned?"

She had originally thought that the combined strength of the three of them would be enough to make Zhang Yu quickly bow his head and retain the profits that were already in their pockets.

But after a fierce battle, Wang Guanyao realized that the development of the situation was completely different from her expectations.

"Could this Zhang Yu be the reincarnation of some old monster? Having been at the Void Refinement stage for only half a year, not only can he withstand the attacks of a Tribulation Transcendence cultivator, but he actually doesn't show the slightest disadvantage in the application of Law Edicts, public opinion warfare, capital warfare, corporate warfare...?"

Wang Guanyao perceived the full picture of the battlefield, observing this fierce battle that had spread to different minor realms and different levels, echoing through the Spirit Realm, Dharma Realm, Finance Realm, and Material Realm, and affecting countless practitioners of lower cultivation.

Wang Guanyao did not feel surprised by the scope of this fierce battle or the people affected by it.

After all, in Kunxu, the higher a cultivator's rank and cultivation realm, the more assets they possessed, the more Immortal Dao Technology they mastered, and the more domains they were involved in. Once a fight broke out, it naturally stirred up storms, easily affecting the work, cultivation, and lives of countless lower-tier cultivators...

The amount of Immortal Coins involved in every battle was enough to drive the lower-tier cultivators mad.

If it reached the Immortal level, it was completely normal for the impact to span across several sects.

But what Wang Guanyao didn't expect was that Zhang Yu could actually engage in this all-out fierce battle with them so effortlessly.

And at the same time...

"The various shareholders, and the cultivators even higher up, haven't made a move even now."

Wang Guanyao thought to herself, "Is it because those guys at the top have already seen the situation clearly, so they aren't coming to stop it?"

"Then putting me in charge of this matter is just so I can take the blame? Make me bear the losses?"

The more Wang Guanyao thought about it, the more she felt this was the case, feeling as if the word "Danger" was growing larger and brighter above her head.

"Not good! I was almost blinded by greed and jumped into a fire pit."

Amidst a flurry of rapid thoughts, a trace of clarity suddenly flashed through Wang Guanyao's eyes.

All along, she knew that under the aspiration for the Immortal Dao, the countless cultivators throughout Kunxu were single-mindedly pursuing profit, just to go a step further on the Immortal Dao.

But precisely because of this, sometimes excessively pursuing profit, or under the temptation of profit, cultivators might make some wrong choices.

And at this very moment, Wang Guanyao felt that under her aspiration for the Immortal Dao... she was blinded by greed, dragging herself into a perilous situation.

"Retreat! Retreat! Retreat!"

Thinking of this, Wang Guanyao felt as if she had broken out in a cold sweat, and the thought of retreating immediately sprouted in her heart. "Substitute Magic! Activate!"

In the next moment, Wang Guanyao's figure flashed, and she disappeared.

Immediately after... a Spirit Transformation cultivator had been thrown in front of everyone by her.

"Everyone, I still have things to attend to. I will leave the subsequent negotiations entirely in his charge. He is the technical backbone of our data center, and we are all at ease with how he handles things."

Leaving behind this message, the leader Wang Guanyao had already withdrawn from the battlefield, leaving only that Spirit Transformation subordinate at a loss, trembling as he looked at the gazes directed at him by the several higher-tier cultivators in front of him.

"The achievements of the low-tier cultivators are the credit of the higher-tier cultivators, and the mistakes of the higher-tier cultivators are the responsibility of the low-tier cultivators."

Thinking of this, the Spirit Transformation subordinate cried out in bitterness inwardly.

Right at this moment, another figure suddenly descended: "Zhang Yu!"

Saying that, she waved her hand and said, "Forget it, Zhang Yu. Today, on account of Bu Yingshu, I can have a talk with you."

Hearing these words, Bu Yingshu's heart shook: *"Aunt, she... could she have also noticed the explosive growth of my innate talent over these past two years?"*

Bu Aixian said, "Zhang Yu, let's sign a contract, and then we'll back off at the same time so neither of us suffers a loss."

In fact, while both sides were thinking and conversing, the fierce battle between them had never stopped.

After all, the side that stopped first would naturally reveal a flaw, which was equivalent to handing their fate over to the opponent.

The contract Bu Aixian sent over at this moment formally stipulated that both parties would stop fighting at the exact same time to avoid losses.

Thus, a moment later, this fierce battle that had started from Zhang Yu's Inner Enclosure Minor Realm, eventually affecting multiple parties and spreading to various realms... temporarily came to an end.

Both sides returned to the Inner Enclosure Minor Realm and officially began negotiations regarding the upper limit of working hours.

And even now that they were sitting down to negotiate, Zhang Yu could still feel Bu Aixian and her group fighting fiercely for their interests, haggling over almost every minute and every second of the employees' working hours.

Fortunately, both sides possessed powerful cognitive and calculating abilities, and being located on the 16th layer, the progress of the negotiation was incredibly fast.
