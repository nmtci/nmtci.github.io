# Chapter 1042 Immortals: Welcome, Crown Prince 💐

Listening to the figure's account, Zhang Yu asked in surprise, "The Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor appeared during the era of the Divine Dynasty? And he was even a close friend of the Divine Emperor?"

"Indeed," the figure replied. "Connecting it with the intelligence data you gave me, I can confirm that that person was the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor."

"It was also this very Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor who originally proposed the experiment of reincarnation to the Divine Emperor."

"He claimed that he wanted to cast a person's consciousness and True Spirit into the future together through reincarnation, achieving an effect akin to traveling forward in time."

Zhang Yu felt a jolt in his heart upon hearing this. The Sect Examination Clone could not help but marvel, "The Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor's existence might be even older than we originally anticipated. This plan to reincarnate into the future might have been devised back then specifically to deal with the Rollbacker."

*"Did he want to skip past the history that the Rollbacker kept constantly rolling back by reincarnating into the future?"*

Even as thoughts surged wildly through Zhang Yu's mind, he followed up with another question, "What happened after that? Did he succeed?"

The figure sighed and said, "Later on, he activated the still-experimental reincarnation technique without authorization. No one knows why he did so. After all, the technology back then had not even completely overcome the mystery of the womb, and memory preservation posed a major problem as well."

"Now, I have gradually come to understand some of the things the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor said back then."

"Back then, the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor said there was no need to worry about memory loss, as someone in the future had already prepared his memories for him."

Hearing this, a thread of doubt surfaced in Zhang Yu's heart. "The future? Prepared? What does that mean?"

The figure said, "I only deduced this after combining the intelligence you gave me."

"Do you still remember the contents of your exchange with the Monster Saint? According to the Monster Saint, after observing that the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor was defeated by the Rollbacker, he had helped the emperor pass down his memories through the human race's bloodline transmission. Eventually, they were revived in a single individual, achieving a disguised resurrection."

"And that individual, according to my comparison of the data from both sides, is likely the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor's reincarnation coordinates."

Startled by this realization, Zhang Yu said, "The Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor reincarnated from the Divine Dynasty into the future, and then received the memories revived for him by the Monster Saint, achieving a true resurrection."

The Sect Examination Clone said in disbelief, "Myriad Laws prepared all of this so long ago? He planned out being defeated by the Rollbacker, resurrecting, and then counter-killing the Rollbacker?"

"Just how on earth did this guy pull that off?"

The figure sighed, "I don't know either, but I suspect that back then, after Heavenly Virtue Immortal Emperor passed the intelligence about the Rollbackers to the Divine Emperor, the Divine Emperor also shared this information with the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor of that era."

"That intelligence must have had a profound impact on the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor at that time."

Hearing this, Zhang Yu's heart sank once more. "In other words, does it tie back to my actions again? Did my interference in the past become one of the factors that shaped the present Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor?"

A thread of doubt also surfaced in the Sect Examination Clone's mind. "Something isn't right. This is far too suspicious. Once could be called a coincidence, but twice? The rumors we fed to Heavenly Virtue Immortal Emperor and the Monster Saint both rippled through to the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor of the past, which in turn influenced the present."

Immortal Slayer said, "There must be some connection here that we have yet to discover."

Even as thoughts raced through Zhang Yu's mind, the figure beside him continued, "Back then, the Divine Emperor and Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor had an agreement: they would join forces to eradicate the Rollbacker and bring an end to the era of rollbacks."

"And according to their plan, one would become the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor, presiding over all Immortal Dao Technology, while the other would become the Reincarnation Immortal Emperor, controlling the life, death, and reincarnation of all living beings, governing the entire world together."

Hearing this, the Sect Examination Clone was shaken once more. "There was actually a plan like that? But based on what we've experienced, the Rollbacker were indeed defeated in the end, yet both the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor and the Reincarnation Immortal Emperor also ended up becoming the Immortal Dao Monopoly Faction."

"In fact, the current Reincarnation Immortal Emperor might no longer be that original Divine Emperor."

Zhang Yu rapidly recalled his previous conversation with the giant corpse and thought to himself, *"The giant corpse was likely the previous Reincarnation Immortal Emperor."*

*"However, judging by his descriptions and understanding of the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor, he probably wasn't the Divine Emperor from back then, as he didn't know the true origins of the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor."*

Zhang Yu looked at the figure and asked, "Then how can you be sure, Senior, that the first generation Reincarnation Immortal Emperor was the Divine Child and had stolen the Divine Emperor's reincarnation technology?"

Just as Zhang Yu's consciousness was conversing with the figure, his other strand of will had already descended outside the Heavenly Court, traversing layers of Immortal power to arrive before a multitude of Immortal wills.

As the wills of the Immortals stirred, the space-time across heaven and earth appeared fragmented, simply because every inch of space had been utterly seized by the Immortals, turned into their private property.

Every inch of sunlight, every wisp of immortal aura, and every trace of spiritual energy was deeply imprinted with the marks of different Immortals. To Zhang Yu, it was like a phantom mirage, completely out of reach, let alone something he could control.

And when Zhang Yu made contact with the Immortals' wills, he felt countless Immortal Dao profundities surface, yet they took on a quality that was nearly impossible to observe or describe.

Zhang Yu understood in his heart that, theoretically speaking, any phenomenon of the Immortal Dao in this world could be contemplated and learned as long as it was observed. How much one could comprehend or learn merely depended on a cultivator's own comprehension and skill level.

It was just like a physicist observing physical phenomena, then studying, contemplating, and experimenting on them.

However, the Immortal Dao Technology utilized by the Immortals' wills had been subjected to layer upon layer of encryption to prevent it from being freely studied, spread, or cracked. Consequently, it manifested as an indescribable, incomprehensible, and unmemorable cognitive hazard.

Yet at the same time, it exerted a fatal allure, making cultivators unable to resist the urge to pursue its secrets, even if it meant offering up every asset they owned.

Beyond that, upon coming into contact with the Immortal Will, Zhang Yu also felt his inner magic power, vital energy, and every other force stir restlessly. Like moths to a flame, they yearned to throw themselves at the Immortals, longing to merge into them.

The next moment, the powers of the Great God of Sentient Creation and the Great God of Myriad Paths and Four Directions descended simultaneously, shielding Zhang Yu securely and temporarily cutting off the influence of the Immortals.

The Great God of Myriad Paths and Four Directions declared, "Your Highness the Crown Prince! We are here to protect you!"

Layers upon layers of divine power cascaded down, manifesting behind Zhang Yu like a divine temple and a throne, shielding him completely.

Zhang Yu cast a surprised glance at the Great God of Myriad Paths and Four Directions. Beside him, the Great God of Sentient Creation sent a telepathic message: "Your Highness, the Great God of Myriad Paths and Four Directions wishes to progress alongside you as well."

Then, the Great God of Sentient Creation began to explain the phenomenon before them.

He said, "The Immortal Realm is the apex of the Immortal Dao."

"As the Aspiration for the Immortal Dao continues to deepen its influence over the world, the wills of the Immortals find it increasingly easy to draw in cultivators."

"Under normal circumstances, when only a wisp of immortal will descends, it is still manageable. But facing so many immortal wills that are excessively intense spells real trouble."

"Especially in a situation like this, facing the wills of so many Immortals head-on all at once may entice cultivators into offering up their assets. Even the power within a cultivator's body can be drawn out, throwing itself willingly alongside those assets to pursue the apex of the Immortal Dao."

"This is the Immortal Calamity — a hazard radiated by the mere existence of Immortals themselves."

At this moment, Zhang Yu once again experienced the terrifying nature of the Immortals.

Although Zhang Yu had repelled the Immortals' interference time and again, and even now had the opportunity to negotiate with them, he had never underestimated these Immortals.

He was acutely aware that a vast gulf still lay between himself and the Immortals. Had it not been for his countless layers of assistance, his wielding of the authority of the Spirit Realm and the Heavenly Court, and the mutual checks and balances among the Immortals themselves, he likely would never have been able to converse with them as equals.

In the next instant, Zhang Yu felt the numerous immortal wills swiftly converge. What had just been an Immortal Calamity akin to a natural disaster transformed in a flash into benevolent, kindly elders. They presented appearances tailored perfectly to Zhang Yu's aesthetic preferences, bearing the friendliest countenances and what seemed to Zhang Yu to be the gentlest smiles.

Zhang Yu understood that this was the nature of the Kunxu Immortals. Even with a massive disparity in cultivation realms and cultivation bases, as long as Zhang Yu demonstrated sufficient value worth winning over, the Immortals could become exceptionally genial and approachable.

As for turning into kindly, benevolent elders... the minds of the Immortals were currently consulting their own Zhang Yu Simulators, only to hear the simulators say, "This image is the most suitable for negotiating with Zhang Yu."

The next moment, Immortal Roujia of the Reformist Faction stepped forward and smiled at Zhang Yu. "Your Highness Crown Prince, we all belong to the Myriad Laws Sect, bound by the same roots and sharing weal and woe. There was a slight misunderstanding earlier, but now that it has been cleared up, we are all fellow disciples and brethren."

On the other side, Immortal Yi Xuan agreed, "Indeed. We are all of the same sect. Now, facing the Ultimate Doomsday, it is precisely the time for us to watch over and help one another."

"Your Highness Crown Prince, we just discussed this among ourselves. Having you interface with so many immortal wills all at once would be far too exhausting. Therefore, it is best to conduct our negotiations in the manner you are most accustomed to."

As they spoke, a chat group invitation had already been sent out, appearing before Zhang Yu. "Myriad Laws Big Family?"

As Zhang Yu joined the group, the Immortals began posting messages one after another.

Immortal Roujia: "Welcome, Crown Prince 🎆🎇🎆"

Immortal Yi Xuan: "Welcome, Crown Prince, to our Myriad Laws Big Family 💐💐"

Watching the Immortals send their welcoming messages interspersed with various emojis of flowers, fireworks, and applause, Zhang Yu could not help but sigh inwardly, "This is honestly the first time I've ever seen so many gentle, amiable, and approachable Immortals."

The Immortal-Slaying Clone, however, only felt a chill run down his spine, as if watching some sort of monsters disguised as humans. "A bunch of pseudo-humans, truly."

The Sect Examination Clone nodded in agreement. "All they do is send emojis — not a single one has sent a red packet. A bunch of pseudo-humans indeed."

Zhang Yu kept his guard up at every single moment, for he knew that the Immortals before him were actually Immortal Dao Beasts who wished nothing more than to swallow him whole; it was only due to layers of mutual restraint that they were forced to cooperate with him.

The moment he exposed even the slightest flaw, these Immortal Dao Beasts circling around him would never pass up the chance to strike and devour him in one bite.

Immortal Venerable Profound Wisdom spoke up, "Crown Prince, we have already shown our sincerity. Now, we can have a proper talk about the specific terms of our cooperation, can we not?"

In an instant, an endless stream of messages cascaded through the chat group like a waterfall.

Zhang Yu also began detailed negotiations regarding cooperation with the Immortals.

Naturally, the first order of business was for Zhang Yu to revoke his actions against the Great God of Heavenly Covenant and Equal Contract, helping the Immortals of the Myriad Laws Sect restore the Great God's authority.

Next came discussions on various Heavenly Court proposals, primarily affecting layers 21 through 29. The Immortals wanted Zhang Yu to cooperate with them by passing certain proposals while obstructing others.

Looking over the contents of each proposal, Zhang Yu thought to himself, *"These Immortals of the Myriad Laws Sect aren't rushing for quick gains or desperately suppressing other sects right from the start. Instead, they're using salami-slicing tactics, accumulating advantages bit by bit to avoid pushing the other sects into a corner."*

Beyond the Heavenly Court proposals, Immortal Dao Technology exchanges were also within the scope of their cooperation.

The Immortals were willing to sell Zhang Yu a portion of their exclusive Heaven-Opening cultivation arts and other Immortal Dao Technologies, at a vastly discounted price, of course.

Just as the negotiations between both sides were progressing rapidly, a roar shook the heavens and earth. Streaks of bloody light, intermingled with terrifying immortal wills, had already closed in around them.

Shielding Zhang Yu at his side, the Great God of Sentient Creation said, "It is the Immortals from the Heavenly Demon Sect, the Netherworld Palace, the White Bone Cult, the Joyful Union Sect, and the Celestial Monster Sect."

The immortal wills swept in like a tsunami, only to be blocked by the joint efforts of the two Great Gods and the many Immortals of the Myriad Laws Sect.

Zhang Yu could only hear the shouts echoing within their collective consciousness: "Myriad Laws Sect, you cheated in secret and interfered with the Divine King selection by slipping in Zhang Yu and Zhang Pianpian! Our five sects will never recognize this result!"

"Hand over Zhang Yu immediately! Redo the Divine King selection, or we go to war directly!"

Confronted with the threats from the five major sects, Immortal Roujia yelled, "Heavenly Demon Sect, do you think everyone loves cheating and engaging in insider trading as much as you do? If you want to fight, then fight! Our Myriad Laws Sect has never feared war!"

As she spoke, four more majestic forces descended from the sky, belonging to the Vajra Sect, the Heavenly Sword Sect, the Immortal Armaments Bureau, and the Pill Cauldron Sect.

As allies, the Immortals of these four sects had already reached an agreement with the Immortals of the Myriad Laws Sect to jointly use Zhang Yu to interfere with the Heavenly Court; facing the Heavenly Demon Sect and its four allied sects, they now stood together in solidarity.

The next moment, heaven and earth plummeted into darkness. Zhang Yu could only sense countless indescribable, unobservable, and unnamable immortal forces colliding together. 

An invisible force even swept toward Zhang Yu, instantly locking down all the surrounding space and freezing him in place. 

The two Great Gods moved to shield Zhang Yu one after another. At the same time, an incomparably domineering force descended from the sky, deflecting all the attacks aimed at him. 

A figure appeared before Zhang Yu and declared coldly, "With me here, no one harms the Crown Prince of Myriad Laws."

The speaker was none other than Immortal Venerable Heavenly Fortune. With a flick of his Dao Lineage, he single-handedly rebounded the incoming forces from Heavenly Demon, Celestial Monsters, Netherworld, White Bone, and Joyous Union. 

Simultaneously sweeping his wide sleeves, he sent a telepathic message: "Roujia, Yi Xuan, quickly escort the Crown Prince back to the sect. I will hold them off."

Working alongside the two Great Gods, the two Reformist Faction Immortals swiftly guided Zhang Yu across Kunxu and returned to the sect layers. 

All the forces in pursuit were deflected by Immortal Venerable Heavenly Fortune with a wave of his hand. He stood like a towering divine mountain across heaven and earth, severing the connection between the Upper Realm and the Immortal Realm.

Watching this scene, Sect Examination Clone could not help but have a thought rise in his mind: *"When these Immortals aren't acting as our opponents, but are helping us for the sake of profit, they really are quite reliable."*

Zhang Yu, on the other hand, asked the Great God of Sentient Creation a question: "Will a large-scale civil war break out among the Ten Great Sects because of this?"

The Great God of Sentient Creation replied: "It is merely a conventional show of force and friction, utterly trivial compared to the interests they are currently fighting for... What is more critical right now is the negotiations taking place as their wills clash."

"The Immortals on the Myriad Laws Sect's side are very measured. Although they utilized your authority as Crown Prince, they will not push the Heavenly Demon Sect and the others too hard. They always leave some leeway, granting room for compromise."

"And Immortals are precisely the beings in Kunxu who are best at compromising for profit."

Sensing the thoughts of the two Reformist Faction Immortals beside them, the Great God of Sentient Creation added: "Of course, what I mean here applies to the Demonic Path factions like the Heavenly Demon Sect and Netherworld Palace."
