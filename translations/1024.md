# Chapter 1024: Great Sage's Heir (8/8)

A dense, sprawling array of black arts instantly manifested before Zhang Yu.

Naturally, the vast majority of them were merely shallow cultivation arts of the basic, advanced, and expert grades.

With a single thought, Zhang Yu immediately filtered out the Sect-Guarding and Heaven-Opening cultivation arts.

Information on countless cultivation arts flashed across his mind, rapidly skimmed, summarized, and selected.

"Among the black arts collected by the Edict Pavilion, there are twelve Heaven-Opening cultivation arts and thirty-four Sect-Guarding cultivation arts in total."

"The quantity is actually greater than I imagined, but each cultivation art has different requirements — especially certain daoist arts... which can't be cultivated so easily."

In the very next moment, Zhang Yu's gaze had already locked onto one Heaven-Opening cultivation art and two Sect-Guarding cultivation arts.

"Sure enough, just like the Blood Tide Essence Qi Sacred Contemplation I cultivated before, there really are martial arts of the Monster Saint Lineage among these black arts that can be used to improve the Great Sage's Heir Chain Formation Diagram."

Zhang Yu swiftly memorized these three cultivation arts and then began attempting to cultivate them.

The Heaven-Opening cultivation art was named the Saints' Spirit Source Wisdom Scripture, while the two Sect-Guarding cultivation arts were titled the Explanation of Indestructibility and the Explanation of Immortality.

"Both of these Sect-Guarding Techniques require numerous clones, multiple streams of thought, and base attributes that break through limits. They were clearly tailor-made for monsterfolk who have obtained the Monster Saint's legacy."

"On top of that, one must also be able to sense the Great Sage's Intent."

"As it happens, I meet every single one of these requirements."

Even though Zhang Yu was a member of the Human Race, he possessed the legacy of Immortal Emperor Supreme Truth, which allowed his attributes to exceed limits even more so than monsterfolk could.

Furthermore, cultivating the arts of the Monster Saint Lineage had allowed him to possess a full 256 layers of thought streams and 256 clones shortly after stepping into the Ascension Realm.

The Chain Formation Diagram's [Great Sage Researcher] enabled Zhang Yu to sense the Great Sage's Intent through the Spirit Realm.

In the past, Zhang Yu might have had to worry about Righteous Gods tracking down the Great Sage's Intent, but now that he wielded great authority and enjoyed the protection of the Eight Divisions Great Gods, he had no need for such concerns.

Thus, over the next few days, Zhang Yu began to cultivate these two Sect-Guarding Techniques continuously.

During his cultivation, he could frequently sense calls echoing from within the Spirit Realm.

Those calls came from the Great Sage's Intent deep within the Spirit Realm, forging a resonant connection with Zhang Yu as he cultivated the techniques.

Immediately afterward, guided by the Great Sage's Intent and aided by the Spirit Realm's empowerment, Zhang Yu felt his clones establish an even deeper connection and resonance with one another, making the cultivation of the techniques progress with consummate ease.

Zhang Yu thought to himself, "I see. Cultivating this Explanation of Indestructibility requires the recognition and guidance of the Great Sage's Intent. Then, by utilizing the environment of the Spirit Realm, a spiritual connection that didn't exist before is gradually formed between the clones and the main body."

"By continuing to cultivate next, I will be able to transfer all kinds of damage suffered by my main body entirely onto the clones."

"And right now, I have a full 256 clones to take damage for me."

"Moreover, these clones possess ultra-high resistance and rapid regeneration, enhanced by the Crippled Ox Body-Sacrifice Heart Art, Myriad Transformations Self-Sovereign Art, Blood Tide Essence Qi Sacred Contemplation, physical mystic powers, Azure Lotus Sword Embryo, Emperor Creation Seed, and so on."

"The damage is transferred to the clones. As they resist, they also adapt and regenerate, which in turn strengthens the main body. Combined with the clones, they can endure even more damage..."

---

Meanwhile, as the Great Sage's Intent continued to call out, numerous Righteous Gods were immediately alerted.

Bing Yisi, a Fourth-Tier Righteous God who had been tracking the Great Sage's Intent for years, was jolted to attention. "Here it comes again! I'll definitely catch you this time!"

With that, he tracked the Great Sage's Intent all the way to the Myriad Laws District on the 19th layer, thinking to himself, "How utterly brazen!"

"This Great Sage's Heir is actually so utterly reckless, responding to the Great Sage's Intent for such a prolonged period and with such high frequency. He simply doesn't put the Heavenly Court in his eyes at all!"

"Found it! Right up ahead!"

"Hm? This location is... the Crown Prince of Myriad Laws's office area?"

Leading a large host of Righteous Gods, Bing Yisi was charging heedlessly through the Myriad Laws Sect when an earth-shattering wave of divine power suddenly blocked his path.

Bing Yisi immediately bowed and said, "Greetings, Great God of Sentient Creation. We have been tracking the Great Sage's Intent all the way here..."

The Great God of Sentient Creation's voice spanned the Spirit Realm, manifesting directly within the thoughts of the Righteous Gods: "Where is this Great Sage's Intent?"

Bing Yisi paused slightly, immediately releasing various data as proof.

The Great God of Sentient Creation swept his divine power across it, remarking indifferently, "You are mistaken. This is merely an ordinary Spirit Realm signal."

Bing Yisi looked down and discovered that all relevant records had already been erased.

And though the Great Sage's Intent was clearly still transmitting, Bing Yisi could no longer receive any alerts — which meant that the other party had already been placed on the Whitelist.

Gazing at the space ahead, Bing Yisi sighed internally, "Even the Great Sage's Heir can now appear out in the open, and with the protection of a Great God-level figure to boot. The sky over Kunxu is truly growing darker and darker."

---

On the other side, as the level of Explanation of Indestructibility climbed steadily, Zhang Yu could clearly sense that once the cultivation art was in motion, the proportion of damage it could divert grew higher and higher.

When Zhang Yu pushed Explanation of Indestructibility to level 80, it could divert fully 80% of damage to his clones.

The next cultivation art Zhang Yu cultivated was Explanation of Immortality. This art could transfer virtually everything — his vital energy, magic power, memories, thoughts, soul, Primordial Spirit, and more — between his main body and his clones.

"Once I master Explanation of Immortality, it means I can transfer everything about myself into a clone at any time."

"Any of my clones can quickly turn into my main body, while my main body... can turn into a clone at a moment's notice."

"The only drawback is the transfer speed."

Zhang Yu knew that the higher one's cultivation and the greater one's power, the more time it took to transfer from the main body to a clone.

Pushing the cultivation art all the way to level 80, he felt that the transfer speed of Explanation of Immortality had improved tremendously, allowing him to complete the transfer from main body to clone in mere seconds.

"A few seconds sounds fast, but in a battle between Ascension Realm cultivators, a few seconds is enough time to do far too much."

"However, once Explanation of Immortality and Explanation of Indestructibility coordinate with each other, that's a whole different story."

"With my 256 clones sharing the damage for me, stalling for a few seconds to make the transfer won't be difficult at all."

In an instant, various simulated battle scenarios flashed through Zhang Yu's mind.

"To kill me, my opponent would have to deal enough damage within a few seconds to completely destroy my main body and all 256 clones in one breath."

"As long as even a single clone isn't completely wiped out, I can transfer over to it, regenerate swiftly, and escape certain death."

"What's more, they would still have to breach defensive measures like my Immortal Grotto-Heaven and Great God talismans."

Thinking about it for a moment, Zhang Yu felt that if he were to face such an opponent himself, their sheer tankiness would truly make him sick.

Sitting on Zhang Yu's shoulder, Fu Ji watched this scene and smiled. "Well done, Zhang Yu. You're the Crown Prince of Myriad Laws now. Your personal safety is of utmost importance, concerning the entire Myriad Laws State and the sect's greater situation. What you need is precisely this kind of Immortal Dao Technology that makes you hard to kill."

Hearing this, Zhang Yu asked, "How are things on Bu Yingshu's end?"

Fu Ji sighed, "Alas, it feels like the person chatting has switched back to Bu Yingshu lately. There's none of that past tenderness, wit, humor, or just the right amount of flattery anymore."

"Well, that's Bu Yingshu for you — what else did you expect? After being promoted to Rank 8 and transferred to the Edict Pavilion, she's been frantically scouring the internal black art cultivation art database, absorbing nourishment from it."

Zhang Yu nodded slightly. He had transferred Bu Yingshu to the Edict Pavilion to serve as the Pavilion Master of the Edict Pavilion and granted her exceptionally high authority precisely so she would cultivate more arts, preparing for the future elevation of the Myriad Laws Immortal Clan Dao Seed.

Next, Zhang Yu began cultivating that Heaven-Opening-Tier black art.

*"Besides sharing the same cultivation requirements as Explanation of Immortality, this Saints' Spirit Source Wisdom Scripture has another prerequisite."*

Zhang Yu thought to himself, *"And that is to possess a large Spirit Realm of one's own, along with the highest authority within it."*

In Zhang Yu's eyes, this single requirement alone was enough to bar the vast majority of cultivators.

After all, Kunxu only had that one Spirit Realm, governed by the Heavenly Court. How could it ever belong to an individual cultivator?

Many so-called local Spirit Realms merely added layers upon layers of restrictions, yet remained part and parcel of the external Spirit Realm.

Yet for Zhang Yu, this posed no real difficulty. The last time he had gathered books, he had acquired a volume of the Great Sage Book, which allowed him to use his own mind to forge an independent Spirit Realm. 

Currently, Zhang Yu had established this independent Spirit Realm within his own Feathered Ascension Grotto-Heaven. 

Thus, Zhang Yu smoothly initiated his cultivation of the Saints' Spirit Source Wisdom Scripture, feeling his consciousness and the Feathered Ascension Grotto-Heaven's Spirit Realm gradually merge into one. 

The independent Spirit Realm, which had originally served merely as an infrastructure for communication, seemed to slowly become a part of his mind — a tentacle through which his spirit extended into reality. 

*"This Saints' Spirit Source Wisdom Scripture refines the Spirit Realm into a part of oneself, and then, beneath the Spirit Realm's coverage, allows one to refine others into their own clones."*

*"In this process, one can even digest the other party's consciousness and memories, gradually establishing a collective consciousness network within the Spirit Realm."*

Just as Zhang Yu was analyzing this in his mind, the Desolate Ox, who had remained silent for a long time, suddenly spoke up, "This seems to be an Immortal Dao technology I developed in the past. To think it has become one of the Ten Great Sects' black arts."

Zhang Yu nodded slightly, recalling the memories and experiences regarding the Desolate Ox Great Sage that he had once obtained when sensing the Great Sage's Intent.

In those memories, the Desolate Ox Great Sage had devoured countless people, establishing a massive collective consciousness network.

However, Zhang Yu was unwilling to do that.

"Refining everyone into a collective consciousness is an approach that, even in Kunxu, would be difficult for cultivators to accept unless enforced through violence.

"Besides, digesting so many memories and minds means that, when the time comes, I won't be me anymore. To me, this is a form of contamination. It would assimilate more and more traits of Kunxu's people into my body, and in the end, wouldn't I also turn into an Immortal Dao Monopoly Faction member pursuing the Immortal Emperor of the End?"

Beyond these functions, the Saints' Spirit Source Wisdom Scripture could also strengthen all the cultivation arts currently within the [Great Sage's Heir] Chain Formation Diagram.

"With this Saints' Spirit Source Wisdom Scripture, my clones will become even more formidable. Whether it's the regeneration brought by the Crippled Ox Body-Sacrifice Heart Art, the processing speed of multiple parallel thoughts granted by the Immeasurable Heart Inner Dharma Body, or the transfer capabilities of Explanation of Immortality and Explanation of Indestructibility, all of them will become increasingly efficient.

"Particularly under the coverage of my Independent Spirit Realm, that enhancement effect is even more astonishing. For instance, the transfer speed of the Explanation of Immortality has increased tenfold, taking only a fraction of a second to swap my main body and clones."

Feeling the full set of enhancement effects brought by the Saints' Spirit Source Wisdom Scripture, Zhang Yu thought to himself, *"This entire series of cultivation arts forms an integrated whole. Its ultimate goal, I'm afraid, is to spread one's independent Spirit Realm across the world, and then refine every living creature covered by the Spirit Realm into one's clone, integrating them into a collective consciousness."*

*"However, even though I can't devour people, I can refine more animals as my clones. After all, animals lack subjective consciousness and won't affect my mind."*

*"When the time comes, every single animal will be able to bear damage for me and block an extra attack, which is practically an extra life."*

Meanwhile, as Zhang Yu cultivated the arts, his Chain Formation Diagram [Great Sage's Heir] also gathered a full nine sets of cultivation arts.

When the eighth set of cultivation arts was fulfilled, it had unlocked a new function for Great Sage's Heir.

Intermediate - Great Sage's Heir (8/8): Your intent is the Great Sage's Intent; within the Spirit Realm, it reaches everywhere.

As Zhang Yu carefully comprehended this ability, he discovered that his intent could penetrate any defense within the Spirit Realm and establish contact with anyone in it.

It was also during this process that Zhang Yu gradually sensed the source from which the Great Sage's Intent originated.

Beneath layers upon layers of the Righteous Gods' wards lay a consciousness that had been utterly suppressed.

Aided by the Great Sage's Heir, Zhang Yu's mind pierced through these barriers as he attempted to call out, "Can you... hear me?"

Seemingly sensing Zhang Yu's intent, the other party responded, "Come... find me..."

Yet Zhang Yu could tell that the entity's state was far from normal. It hovered somewhere between lucidity and delirium, speaking in disjointed, incoherent fragments, repeatedly doing nothing but telling Zhang Yu to come find it.

Immediately after, Zhang Yu sensed a surge of the Righteous Gods' divine power sweeping in. It did not attack him; it merely suppressed and displaced the Great Sage's Intent further, severing the connection between Zhang Yu and the Great Sage's Intent for the time being.

*"The Righteous Gods probably refrained from attacking me because of my identity and clearance level,"* Zhang Yu thought to himself. *"Yet clearly, they don't want me to have too much contact with the Great Sage's Intent either."*

Meanwhile, just as Zhang Yu was cultivating his cultivation art and studying the Great Sage's Intent, Tai Yuebai sent him another report.

Reviewing the contents of the report, Zhang Yu replied, "The cultivation progress of the Dao Seed bearers is looking good."

"Have you made contact with any other bearers of ultra-high-danger Dao Seeds?"

Tai Yuebai replied, "Still looking. People like that are held by the Immortal Class, so they aren't easy to get in touch with."

Zhang Yu nodded slightly, choosing to believe Tai Yuebai.

After all, having secretly acquired so many Dao Seeds, he should be professional in this regard.

"Ultra-high-danger Dao Seeds... If I can master a few more of them, it will also be a great help to improving my strength."

At the same time, Le Mulan sent Zhang Yu a message: "Did you submit an application to use an ultra-high-danger Dao Seed? Actually, there's no need to apply; I can just put in a word directly..."

Zhang Yu replied with righteous indignation, "The use of ultra-high-danger Dao Seeds must receive the approval of the Heavenly Surveillance Hall. This is a sect rule. Any holder of an ultra-high-danger Dao Seed must abide by it, and I am no exception."

As he spoke, Zhang Yu, in his capacity as the Crown Prince of Myriad Laws, received a document from the Heavenly Surveillance Hall — his own application to use an ultra-high-danger Dao Seed.

As the Crown Prince of Myriad Laws, whenever the Heavenly Surveillance Hall approved anyone to use an ultra-high-danger Dao Seed, it also had to be reviewed by him.

With a wave of his hand, Zhang Yu endorsed it: "Approved."

The next moment, Zhang Yu's application was instantly approved, granting him the right to use the ultra-high-danger Dao Seed.

Opening his mouth, he exhaled, and a vast surge of immortal aura gushed forth, manifesting within the Feathered Ascension Grotto-Heaven.

At this moment, he was recklessly operating the ultra-high-danger Dao Seed [One Qi Swallows the Universe], madly duplicating immortal aura to elevate the concentration of immortal aura throughout the entire Grotto-Heaven.

To Zhang Yu, this was not only improving the cultivation environment, but also creating wealth out of thin air.

As for whether this would lead to the inflation of immortal aura, Zhang Yu could no longer be bothered to care.

Right at this moment, Fa Liuyuan also sent a message to Zhang Yu.

Fa Liuyuan: "Your Highness the Crown Prince, I wonder if you have noticed that the crime rate within the sect on the 11th through 19th layers has risen recently."

Fa Liuyuan explained, "At first, there were just a lot more minor frictions, but later on, armed clashes began to break out. Over the past two days, people have even been killed without a permit, and the situation continues to worsen."

Fa Liuyuan then offered his own insight: "This situation is very similar to a Dao Lineage that the two Immortals once introduced to me."

Zhang Yu asked, "What Dao Lineage?"

Fa Liuyuan said, "The Destiny Dao Lineage."

The Destiny Dao Lineage, controlled by Heavenly Fortune Immortal Venerable, was commonly known by many as the Gambling Dao Lineage, but its scope of influence extended far beyond gambling.

According to Fa Liuyuan's explanation, this Dao Lineage could see through the past, divine the future, grasp the connections between things, and intervene in the changes between them.

According to Zhang Yu's understanding, Heavenly Fortune Immortal Venerable was able to perceive the underlying causes behind various events and deduce their trajectory. He could even use his Dao Lineage to remotely provoke events, meddle with how they unfolded, and drag unrelated individuals into the fray.

Zhang Yu analyzed, "In other words, as long as Heavenly Fortune Immortal Venerable circulates his Dao Lineage, he doesn't need to spend any money or lift a finger to alter the course of events and change people's destinies?"

Fa Liuyuan replied, "Indeed. The Dao Lineage of Heavenly Fortune Immortal Venerable is just that formidable. However, the stronger an individual is, the harder it is for this Dao Lineage to interfere with them. Generally speaking, those in the Immortal Class are extremely difficult to sway. As for the level of Immortal Emperors, not only are they hard to influence, but it is even difficult to deduce their past and future."

Fa Liuyuan lamented, "In the past, the ten Immortal Emperors held absolute sway over Kunxu and were tied to countless corporations, markets, and Dao Battles, making it difficult for even the Destiny Dao Lineage to make deductions and exert influence. It could be said that under the suppression of the ten Immortal Emperors back then, the influence of the Destiny Dao Lineage hit rock bottom, confined merely to the realms of gambling and fraud. But now that the ten Immortal Emperors have departed, Heavenly Fortune Immortal Venerable's power has been reinforced like never before. He may not be able to directly sway an Immortal, but he can influence cultivators to manipulate the situation, thereby indirectly affecting Immortals."

"For example, speeding up the development of their own factions, or weakening the development speed of those under other Immortals..."

Zhang Yu thought to himself, *"The ten Immortal Emperors originally kept Kunxu in check, keeping many of the Immortal Venerables suppressed. Now that the ten Immortal Emperors have departed, each and every one of these Immortal Venerables will inevitably reveal a different face than before, exerting a tremendous impact on Kunxu."*

Fa Liuyuan continued, "The current situation from the 11th to the 19th layer is like trouble arising out of thin air, with conflicts constantly escalating. If left unchecked, it will only become more and more severe, perhaps even erupting into a civil war."

Zhang Yu thought, *"Fortunately, Fa Liuyuan knew this and warned me. Otherwise, with such an insidious Dao Lineage, who knows when I would have realized it."*

Zhang Yu thought for a moment and asked, "Do you think it's Heavenly Fortune Immortal Venerable making a move against us?"

Fa Liuyuan nodded. "I had heard earlier that it was Heavenly Fortune Immortal Venerable who pushed for the sanctions. Now that he is mobilizing his Dao Lineage to suppress us, it comes as no surprise."

Zhang Yu asked, "Do you know why he's doing this?"

Fa Liuyuan shook his head. "All I know is that he wants you to hand over the Crown Prince Authority."

The two of them deliberated further, yet still failed to come up with any countermeasure.

Zhang Yu sighed inwardly, *"A Dao Lineage of the Immortal Venerable level is just too troublesome. They don't even need to lift a finger to exert a massive influence on us."*

While Zhang Yu was deep in thought, someone else contacted him.

Zhang Pianpian: "Little brother! Someone is trying to seize the highest control authority of the Heavenly Court."

Hearing this, Zhang Yu felt a shock in his heart.

Right now, he was able to maintain control over layers 11 through 19 primarily because of the armed protection provided by the Eight Divisions Great Gods.

If someone were to gain higher control authority over the Heavenly Court than him, depriving him of the protection of the Eight Divisions Great Gods, the Immortal Class of the Myriad Laws Sect would likely suppress him in an instant.

Zhang Yu immediately asked, "Who is it?"

Zhang Pianpian: "The monsterfolk of the Celestial Monster Sect. They are already attempting to release and control the Great Sage's Intent. This is very likely the Celestial Monster Immortal Emperor's contingency plan."

Zhang Yu made up his mind instantly: "I must stop them."
