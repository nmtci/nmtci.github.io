# Chapter 998: The 33rd Layer of Kunxu

"A new page... Is it still called the Technique-Chaining Comprehensive Manual?"

Zhang Yu gazed at the Feather Book before him, looking at the newly fused page of the Technique-Chaining Comprehensive Manual. In his mind, he gradually developed a clearer understanding of how the Books were categorized into low-grade, mid-grade, and high-grade.

"Low-grade Books often provide merely a modular function."

"Mid-grade Books, on the other hand, unlock an entirely new capability. Like Tai Yuebai's Book, some of the Books on the exemption list granted by the Immortal Emperor, and my Technique-Chaining Comprehensive Manual, they can all bring about a qualitative transformation for a cultivator. Yet at the mid-grade level, their capabilities vary, and the disparities between them are likely quite vast as well."

"As for high-grade Books, Feathered Ascension stands at the pinnacle of all Books. It is a true shortcut that allows one to achieve an Immortal position, establish a Dao Lineage, and subsequently reshape heaven and earth."

Zhang Yu understood in his heart... ultimately, achieving immortality and establishing a Dao was the single most important pursuit in the Kunxu Immortal Cultivation world.

At the same time, along with the integration of the *Technique-Chaining Comprehensive Manual*, Zhang Yu could feel that his Dao-Seeking Progress in the *Immortal Foundation Chapter* also surged, jumping all at once from 48% to 58%, making him feel that his spatial technology had made tremendous progress once again.

However, at this very moment, Zhang Yu's main attention was not on this aspect. He merely quietly comprehended the results after the *Technique-Chaining Comprehensive Manual* was integrated, thinking to himself, "There are no new functions. Sure enough, to improve upon the original book and add new functions like with the *Immortal Foundation Chapter*... it still relies on upgrading the Feather Book, doesn't it?"

Thinking back to the *Immortal Foundation Chapter* that had been integrated and upgraded previously, thoughts flashed through Zhang Yu's mind like lightning, "If the Feather Book upgrades next time, it might be able to enhance the capabilities of this *Technique-Chaining Comprehensive Manual*."

"Thinking about it this way, in the past, whenever my Feather Book upgraded, it felt like there wasn't much of a pattern."

"And now... by integrating different books and then upgrading, I might be able to slightly steer the upgrade direction of the Feather Book."

However, Zhang Yu understood in his heart that this was not something that could be confirmed immediately; it would still require more attempts from him in the future.

Then another thought crossed his mind: *"Just a single Technique-Chaining Comprehensive Manual has such a massive impact. If the Immortal Ancestor is truly revived over a year from now, what earth-shattering upheaval will that bring?"*

"What kind of power does the Immortal Ancestor possess? Is he an entity on the level of an Immortal Emperor?"

Zhang Yu posed this question to Bai Gusheng before him.

He thought to himself, *"Since Bai Gusheng wants to recruit me and make a deal with me, I might as well squeeze more intelligence out of him while I have the chance."*

At the same time, another thought surfaced in Zhang Yu's mind: *"Did the Immortal Emperors... encounter the revival of the Immortal Ancestor, find themselves powerless to deal with it, and thus perform a rollback? Or did they initiate the rollback due to some other event, and now plan to revive the Immortal Ancestor? This is closely tied to the Immortal Ancestor's own strength."*

Hearing the question, Bai Gusheng replied nonchalantly, "According to the intelligence I currently possess, the existence of the Books... allows the Immortal Ancestor to advance alongside the progress of Immortal Dao Technology."

"Theoretically speaking, he should be able to master the vast majority of Dao Seeds and Chain Formation Diagrams in this world, achieve proficiency in all kinds of cultivation arts, and connect to an unknown number of Dao Lineages..."

Bai Gusheng had no reservations about sharing vague intelligence regarding the Immortal Ancestor. After all, he believed that as long as one collected enough Books, such information could eventually be pieced together and deduced.

As for the value of this intel, Bai Gusheng saw it merely as conversation fodder.

*"What even is the Source Saint? Every time he opens his mouth, it's all open-source, piracy, plagiarism, iteration... It sounds so low-end the moment you hear it."*

*"Meanwhile, someone like me discusses things like the Four Books, the Immortal Ancestor, the plans of the Immortal Emperors... It gives off a truly high-end vibe."*

In Bai Gusheng's experience, whenever he spoke about these topics, it conveyed sincerity and made the other person view him as profound and unfathomable.

Bai Gusheng continued, "Looking at it from this perspective, he should possess power on the level of an Immortal Emperor. But in today's flourishing Kunxu, guarded by the Ten Immortal Emperors, there is naturally no need for us to worry. The Immortal Emperors would never allow someone like the Immortal Ancestor to ruin Kunxu's business environment and harm the profits of the great sects..."

Bai Gusheng assumed that Zhang Yu, having heard the news about the Immortal Ancestor, was worried that the Immortal Ancestor's revival plan in a year would cause a disaster and interfere with everyone making money.

Then, Zhang Yu naturally asked another question on his mind: "I saw that the Immortal Ancestor left messages on some of the Books. How was that accomplished? Can anyone still leave messages on the Books now?"

Bai Gusheng said, "As the creator of the Books, it's naturally not strange that the Immortal Ancestor could leave messages. As for the method of doing so... I don't know either."

At the same time, doubt arose in Bai Gusheng's heart: *"Why is Zhang Yu asking this? Does he want to leave messages on the Books? To advertise? Or to scam someone?"*

At this moment, Bai Gusheng thought of the Source Saint, who was communicating with Zhang Yu: *"Could these two fellows be thinking of collaborating on this?"*

The thought that the Books collected in the future might be plastered with dense advertisements and scam messages from the Myriad Laws and Immortal Soldiers factions made Bai Gusheng frown slightly. *"Even if someone is going to post them, it should be our White Bone Cult."*

*"Though it's useless to think about it. I tried long ago, and it's simply impossible."*

With his thoughts flashing in his mind, Bai Gusheng had already said indifferently, "The Books are heaven and earth rarities forged by the Immortal Ancestor. Leaving information on them is by no means an easy task. At least based on the intelligence I possess, no one should have developed such a technique."

Zhang Yu sighed inwardly, *"Back then, Senior Supreme Truth only managed to send back information from the future by relying on her Immortal Emperor-grade strength combined with her bloodline power."*

*"A year from now, it's unlikely I'll have Immortal Emperor-grade strength. The reason I could transmit a message must rely solely on the unique nature of the Books. So how exactly did I manage to... leave information in it?"*

The Sect Examination Clone said, "What a pity. If Mother Supreme Truth had learned about the rollback sooner and understood the mechanism behind the Books' ability to transmit messages through them, perhaps she could have researched a way to use a Book to send information in advance."

Zhang Yu sighed. Not only the Sect Examination Clone, but he himself was also missing Mother Supreme Truth's presence more and more.

Immortal Slayer said, "Although Senior Supreme Truth won't have the chance, if we're talking about who else in this world is trustworthy and might have the chance to figure it out..."

As Immortal Slayer spoke, Ying Xintian's figure had already surfaced in Zhang Yu's mind.

Zhang Yu thought to himself, *"Maybe... I should contact Tai Yuebai and share our current intelligence."*

Bai Gusheng looked at Zhang Yu, who fell silent from time to time before him, and knew the moment was ripe.

"I believe what I said today has already touched you deeply to your very soul."

"I won't say any more. After you return to the Myriad Laws Sect, you can mull over what we discussed today and think it through slowly. Once you've figured it out, come find me."

Bai Gusheng understood that he could not appear too eager about the new book Zhang Yu had obtained.

Once the other party saw his urgency, it might give rise to other ideas, such as demanding an exorbitant price... which would only add unnecessary complications when the time came.

Thus, Bai Gusheng felt that he had said enough for today, and that it was time to take his leave.

Just as Zhang Yu saw Bai Gusheng off, his other clone had also nearly concluded its exchange with the Source Saint.

Although the Source Saint did not possess as much intelligence as Bai Gusheng, communicating with him allowed Zhang Yu to verify... the intelligence he had obtained from Bai Gusheng's side.

"Regarding the intelligence on the Immortal Ancestor, Bai Gusheng probably didn't lie to me."

"A little over a year from now... 'When the Four Books are Complete, the Immortal Ancestor Appears?' What turbulent times."

Watching Bai Gusheng and the Source Saint depart, Zhang Yu thought to himself, "With my current strength, I'm afraid I can't compete against these veteran Ascension Realm cultivators. At the very least, I need to reach the Tribulation Transcendence Peak. Only then, relying on the foundation I've accumulated... will I have a chance of fighting above my realm."

Zhang Yu understood that whether it was to face potential competition from Ascension Realm cultivators like Bai Gusheng and the Source Saint, or to deal with the major event a year from now, increasing his strength was essential.

And to further enhance his strength, aside from his current step-by-step progress, the most important method was clearly collecting the Books.

"Especially in terms of advancing the parasitic Dao Lineage..."

He quietly sensed his own progress in spatial technology after his Dao-Seeking Progress reached 58%.

After breaking through 40% last time, he had faintly perceived the spatial structure of the lower realm, realizing that he could directly cross space and descend there.

Now, as Zhang Yu's understanding of Kunxu's spatial structure deepened even further, he felt confident that he could descend to the lower realm with far greater ease... and perhaps even without being detected.

Feeling all of this, Zhang Yu thought to himself, "More intelligence, greater strength, and faster parasitic Dao lineage assimilation... walking the shortcut to immortality... All of this requires me to collect more Books."

At this moment, Zhang Yu felt as though an invisible, colossal hand was behind his back, constantly pushing him forward to accomplish it all.

---

Soon after returning to the Myriad Laws Sect, Zhang Yu contacted Tai Yuebai.

The two then met deep within the Immortal Grotto-Heaven. Zhang Yu did not say much, merely displaying the countdown of the seal information.

Tai Yuebai, however, understood immediately. After all, countdowns, future information... these were key details regarding the World Rollback that only he and Zhang Yu knew, matters to which his mind was now on high alert and paying close attention.

*"Another World Rollback occurred? When? How does Zhang Yu know?"*

Zhang Yu continued, "Big Brother Tai, I wonder if you have heard the saying: 'When the Four Books are Complete, the Immortal Ancestor Appears'..."

Following that, Zhang Yu shared some intelligence regarding the Immortal Ancestor, placing special emphasis on... the point in time one year from now.

Understanding implicitly, Tai Yuebai thought to himself, *"The revival of the Immortal Ancestor one year from now is the precise moment the Ten Immortal Emperors rolled back the world time node?"*

*"But... how did Zhang Yu find out? Who passed it to him?"*

Looking at Zhang Yu before him, thoughts raced through Tai Yuebai's mind: *"Could it be that Immortal Ancestor?"*

At this moment, Tai Yuebai wondered if this so-called Immortal Ancestor had passed on a message before the rollback, just like Immortal Emperor Supreme Truth.

*"If that's truly the case, does it mean this so-called Immortal Ancestor will also be suppressed by the Ten Immortal Emperors and devoured?"*

*"If another Immortal Emperor-grade entity is devoured, it will probably trigger the Ultimate Doomsday once and for all. When that time comes, either a world-shattering battle among the Ten Immortal Emperors will erupt, or the Immortal Emperor of the End will be born..."*

At the mere thought of this, Tai Yuebai felt a dark cloud gather in his heart.

Meanwhile, Zhang Yu continued speaking with Tai Yuebai, "Big Brother Tai, this matter... is of grave importance. Can you contact Senior Ying?"

Tai Yuebai nodded. After his last contact with Ying Xintian, the other party had left him a new method of communication.

At that moment, Tai Yuebai replied, "This matter concerns the future of the trillions of living beings in Kunxu. I will notify him as soon as possible."

Tai Yuebai thought to himself, *"I'm afraid once Senior Ying learns of this news, he won't have any choice but to intervene in person... and rush to Kunxu to take part in this."*

Although he knew the risks of Ying Xintian returning in person were immense, Tai Yuebai also understood that at this juncture, they could no longer afford to worry about such things.

"*"The Ultimate Doomsday... is continuously accelerating. The situation is shifting worse than I originally anticipated, and it is already a dire emergency.""*

On the other side, Zhang Yu continued, "Furthermore, I obtained this information through a newly appeared Book."

Tai Yuebai was startled upon hearing this, then pressed on, "Are you saying... the Books can be used to transmit messages?"

Zhang Yu nodded and continued, "And the person who transmitted the message this time is very likely myself. I want you to ask Senior Ying if he has a way to leave a message in the Books."

"You?" Tai Yuebai looked at Zhang Yu in surprise, then nodded slightly. "I understand..."

However, in the next moment, he asked again, "But... can you be certain that the person who left the message is definitely you?"

"Is it possible that someone is impersonating you?"

Zhang Yu was taken aback for a moment, while Tai Yuebai continued, "If... I mean if the Ten Immortal Emperors in the future learn about the intelligence we possess, and then impersonate you to transmit messages in order to lure Senior Ying over..."

Zhang Yu frowned slightly, understanding what Tai Yuebai was talking about.

"The Ten Immortal Emperors... knew we had intelligence about the rollback, so they deliberately fed us false information to lure Senior Ying over... and devour him?"

"If that's truly the case, then we are the ones who accelerate Kunxu's doom."

Hearing this hypothesis, a sense of despair welled up in both the Sect Examination Clone and Immortal Slayer.

Immortal Slayer sighed. *"If that is truly so, I can no longer think of any way... to stop the apocalypse."*

After a moment of contemplation, Zhang Yu said solemnly to Tai Yuebai, "I cannot disprove your hypothesis. I can only say that such a possibility indeed exists."

Tai Yuebai sighed. "Yet even knowing this possibility exists, we still have to do what comes next."

He knew deep down that even if he knew this matter might be a trap, Ying Xintian would definitely return.

*"Knowing that there's a chance of failure, a chance of being devoured, a chance it's a trap... should we run away? Should we give up on stopping the apocalypse? No, Senior Ying would never do that."*

*"No matter how slim the chance of victory is, as long as it is to resist the apocalypse, he will definitely do everything he can to stop the birth of the Immortal Emperor of the End."*

With a heavy heart, Tai Yuebai left Zhang Yu's Immortal Grotto-Heaven.

Immortal Slayer asked, "What do we do next?"

Zhang Yu opened the Technique-Chaining Comprehensive Manual in the Feather Book and said, "Prepare to... reveal the Technique-Chaining Comprehensive Manual's ability to create Chain Formation Diagrams."

After much contemplation, Zhang Yu had reached a decision.

"Using the Technique-Chaining Comprehensive Manual to create a Chain Formation Diagram that only I can use is of little significance to me right now... It does nothing to change the current situation."

Having climbed all the way from Qi Refining to the Tribulation Transcendence Realm, Zhang Yu had acquired many Chain Formation Diagrams, and he knew that as long as he applied himself to learning, he could already amass many more Chain Formation Diagrams.

"However, the efficacy and strength of a Chain Formation Diagram are closely tied to the cultivation arts that comprise it. As I am now... unless I can learn several more Sect-Guarding or even Heaven-Opening cultivation arts, creating another set of Chain Formation Diagrams will offer only a very limited boost to my strength."

"On the contrary, once it's made public, as long as I hold the authority to create Chain Formation Diagrams, each opportunity to create one will be enough to shake an entire industry and stir up massive waves of Immortal Coins."

The Sect Examination Clone said, "Indeed! If we do that, the benefits offered to us by the major factions alone, when converted into Immortal Coins... would likely number in the hundreds of millions."

Immortal Slayer sighed, "Things have come to this, and the situation is already critical beyond measure. There really is no time to accumulate strength slowly anymore."

At the same time, Zhang Yu had also released the Evil Gods like Fu Ji, Auspicious God, and Herald God, and then explained the matter regarding the Technique-Chaining Comprehensive Manual.

Hearing Zhang Yu's plan, Fu Ji said in alarm, "If this is made public... who knows how many people will come to snatch it?"

Zhang Yu, however, said, "So what if they try to snatch it? Even if it really gets taken away, I will only lose out on a few Chain Formation Diagrams."

This was the conclusion of Zhang Yu's deliberations.

With the apocalypse looming once more, even if he kept a low profile and developed quietly with the Technique-Chaining Comprehensive Manual, how much time did he have left? With the Technique-Chaining Comprehensive Manual's ten-year cooldown period, how many Chain Formation Diagrams could he actually create? In the end, the enhancement to Zhang Yu himself would be limited.

The Sect Examination Clone said, "The greatest risk is merely missing out on a few Chain Formation Diagrams, while the highest return... could be in the hundreds of millions. It's well worth taking the gamble."

Fu Ji wanted to dissuade him, but she could sense in Zhang Yu's words... an urgency that had never been there before.

*"What on earth happened? It feels like Zhang Yu is in much more of a rush than in the past."*

Just as Fu Ji was lost in thought, Herald God spoke up, "If we are to make it public, we must secure the Immortal Emperor's support."

In the Herald God's view, regardless of why Zhang Yu was in a hurry, since the leader was anxious, he didn't need to ask why; he only needed to respond to the Leader's urgency.

"Correct," Zhang Yu said. "So I plan to report the intelligence regarding the Technique-Chaining Comprehensive Manual directly... to the Immortal Emperor."

Following Tai Yuebai's reminder just now, Zhang Yu had figured out yet another thing.

"The effect of the Technique-Chaining Comprehensive Manual is likely something the Immortal Emperors who initiated the rollback already knew long ago."

"If the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor wants this authority, there's no way I can stop him anyway, so I might as well report it directly right now."

"At the same time, this is also a test..."

Zhang Yu looked up at the sky, his gaze seeming to pierce through the layers of Kunxu to look toward that unreachable peak, toward where the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor resided.

Zhang Yu understood in his heart that the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor's cultivation of him had always been aimed at pushing him step by step onto the dining table, grooming him into a new edible immortal ingredient.

"Logically speaking, at least before I achieve immortality and establish my Dao, the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor should continue to nurture and invest in me as he did before. Just like choosing me to be the Book-Wielder this time, letting me continue to wield the Technique-Chaining Comprehensive Manual would also be a natural progression."

"Through the Immortal Emperor's response this time, I can get a glimpse of whether his attitude toward me has changed, and whether he will still allow me to grow unchecked."

As Zhang Yu pondered, a mail addressed to the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor was being quickly drafted, and then silently sent off to the Immortal Emperor's secret mailbox.

---

Meanwhile, on the other side, Bu Yingshu projected her avatar and arrived at his door with a hint of pride on her face.

*"Hmph, this time, out of respect for me, several great Immortal Clans are willing to send representatives to talk with Zhang Yu about collecting the Books."*

At the thought of this, Bu Yingshu seemed to already see the shock, admiration, and gratitude that would appear on Zhang Yu's face once he heard the news.

However, upon her arrival, Bu Yingshu found Zhang Yu standing right at the entrance of the Inner Enclosure Minor Realm.

*"Could it be that he has already received word and came specifically to wait for me? Or is he already preparing to head to my Immortal Abode?"*

Bu Yingshu smiled slightly and walked over. "Little Yu, it seems you already know what I've done?"

"That's right. The five great Immortal Clans of Myriad Laws have all sent representatives, and I have invited them to my Immortal Abode."

With that, Bu Yingshu grabbed Zhang Yu's hand and said, "Come, come back with me right now to discuss how to collect the Books held by the Immortal Clans."

However, when Bu Yingshu tugged at Zhang Yu, she found that he didn't budge at all.

Zhang Yu looked at her with an apologetic expression and said, "Profound Monarch Bu, something has come up. I'm afraid I can't go right now."

Bu Yingshu glared at him and said, "Set everything else aside for now. I'm the one hosting this gathering — do you want to make me lose face?"

Zhang Yu said, "I don't have a say in this matter either..."

Before his voice had even faded, a massive door abruptly manifested and swung wide open right in front of Bu Yingshu and Zhang Yu with a resounding boom.

Looking at this magnificent door and catching a glimpse of the inscriptions marking "Myriad Laws" and "33rd layer" upon it, Bu Yingshu's heart violently jolted.

Bu Yingshu asked, "Could... could this be leading to..."

Zhang Yu nodded toward Bu Yingshu and said, "The Immortal Emperor has some matters to ask me in person. I'll be going now."

Watching Zhang Yu's figure step into the grand door pace by pace, Bu Yingshu was astounded to the utmost limit: *"An audience with the Immortal Emperor? Zhang Yu... is actually going to the 33rd layer to meet the Immortal Emperor?!"*

Bu Yingshu knew that on the 33rd of Kunxu's 36 layers... resided the true body of the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor.

*"Even I've never seen the Immortal Emperor!!"*

At the thought of this, Bu Yingshu had already sent a message to Bu Yuangui: "Dad! Have you ever had an audience with the Immortal Emperor?"

Bu Yuangui replied with a hint of pride: "Over 100 years ago, before you were even born, I had the privilege of seeing a projection of the Immortal Emperor once."
