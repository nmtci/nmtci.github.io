# Chapter 1046: Test Your Way Up? Fight Your Way Up!

Looking at the messages sent by Bai Zhenzhen, Bu Yingshu, Tai Yuebai, and Desolate Ox Great Sage, Immortal Slayer sighed internally, "Do you see that? Sect Examination Clone, there are still people supporting us."

The Sect Examination Clone said disdainfully, "Bai Zhenzhen is merely acting on emotion."

"Bu Yingshu is blinded by greed and desire, hoping you'll bring her along to achieve immortality."

"Tai Yuebai, on the other hand, sees the situation clearly. He knows that only we can confront the doomsday, and that the current Immortal Class is simply unreliable."

"As for Desolate Ox Great Sage, that old rebel has probably been thinking about rebelling against the Immortals for ages. If you want to achieve immortality... that suits him just fine."

The Sect Examination Clone analyzed indifferently, "Yet when all is said and done, their support for us is ultimately temporary. As long as we keep advancing and keep charging toward the apex of the Immortal Dao, the day will inevitably come when our paths diverge."

"Zhang Yu, you are destined to be entirely alone. The only ones who can stay by your side are clones like us..."

Zhang Yu suppressed the Sect Examination Clone's thoughts once more, saying in his heart, "Cut the nonsense. The urgent priority right now... is to achieve immortality."

"I must achieve immortality," Zhang Yu thought to himself. "Only by achieving immortality is it possible to stand against the ultimate doomsday."

Immortal Slayer analyzed, "If you want to achieve immortality right now, you need to satisfy two requirements."

"First, without being able to participate in the Ascension Selection, you must bypass the Ascension Dao Lineage of the Ten Great Sects and achieve immortality illegally."

"Second, after illegally achieving immortality, you must withstand the combined suppression of the many Immortals from the Ten Great Sects, forcing them to acknowledge our Immortal position."

After finishing, Immortal Slayer sighed, "It's difficult. The first point is already exceedingly difficult, and the second is even harder."

The Sect Examination Clone laughed heartily and said, "That's how it should be! From the 1st to the 19th layer, we advanced layer by layer through examinations. If we want to climb the remaining 21st to 36th layers quickly, we ought to fight our way up layer by layer!"

While these thoughts were being exchanged in Zhang Yu's mind, a vast amount of information regarding achieving immortality and establishing the Dao also surfaced before him.

As the Crown Prince of Myriad Laws, he had naturally mastered this information long ago. Pondering over achieving immortality and establishing the Dao at this moment naturally brought this data to mind once again.

Sect Examination Clone: "Bypassing the Ascension Selection simply means establishing a Dao Lineage directly and forcing your way to achieve immortality."

Immortal Slayer said, "According to existing records, the process of establishing a Dao Lineage involves gradually altering objective rules, which inevitably triggers all manner of anomalies. The Ten Great Sects possess such records, and the Immortals understand this intimately. If you directly establish a Dao Lineage, it would be far too easy to detect."

Hearing this, Zhang Yu's heart stirred. He recalled that during the process of Supreme Truth forging the Supreme Dao Lineage, numerous anomalies had indeed been triggered.

How did Supreme Truth cover it up then? By using another Dao Lineage to mask her own, leading others to believe that a different Dao Lineage was being established and breaking through.

Zhang Yu: "Whose Dao Lineage could I use as cover?"

Immortal Slayer: "Who would be willing to cover for us?"

Sect Examination Clone: "A Dao Lineage is just like a Yang Artifact. Everyone wishes their Yang Artifact could be used more often, yet no one is willing to lend it out for others to use."

Even as Zhang Yu pondered, the other thoughts within his mind were engaged in similar reflections.

And in the very next moment, the exact same inspiration arose across his manifold thoughts.

"The Ascension Selection!"

"When the winners of the Ascension Selection establish their Dao Lineages, that will be my best opportunity. I can use the numerous phenomena triggered by the establishment of their Dao Lineages as cover for establishing my own."

Lines of information immediately surfaced before Zhang Yu's eyes, all of them detailing the current Ascension Selection.

"Past Ascension Selections generally lasted around a year. However, this session's process has been greatly streamlined and sped up, and it will be settled in about three months."

"So three months from now, when the winners of the Ascension Selection establish their Dao Lineages, will be my optimal window."

"In other words, I only have three months to prepare."

At the same time, Immortal Slayer raised another issue: "There is another problem. The process of achieving immortality will trigger a Heavenly Tribulation. The winners of the Ascension Selection have the protection of an Ascension Dao Lineage and do not fear the Heavenly Tribulation."

"However, when the time comes, I'm afraid we will have to face the Heavenly Tribulation head-on. It's just unknown how much our Paragon of Virtue status will help us then."

The Sect Examination Clone said, "So what if we're Paragons of Virtue? The Heavenly Tribulation Dao Lineage is currently in the hands of the Heavenly Sword Sect. Did you forget the seventh Thunder Tribulation? We could only undergo it — and obtain the Paragon of Virtue evaluation — because the Heavenly Sword Sect permitted it."

"The Immortal Ascension Heavenly Tribulation is very likely a similar situation, having already been stripped of the influence of moral character by the Heavenly Sword Sect. When the time comes for us to achieve immortality, we'll have no choice but to forcefully cross the tribulation."

"Setting aside how powerful the tribulation wielded by the Heavenly Sword Sect is, the moment we undergo this Immortal Ascension Heavenly Tribulation, I'm afraid we'll be discovered as well."

Zhang Yu thought to himself, "If I want to learn the specific details of the Heavenly Tribulation, or if I want to avoid it, I'm afraid my only option is to make a trip to the Heavenly Sword Sect."

Immortal Slayer raised another issue: "You can hide for a while, but you can't hide forever. Based on the information we currently have, even if we can secretly establish a Dao Lineage, the moment we achieve immortality, our Divine Soul and physical body will both completely transform into an Immortal Body."

"Having an additional Immortal Body appear in the world would be like detonating a nuclear bomb on Earth; with the shifts in all kinds of environmental data, it still won't stay hidden."

"So even if we successfully establish a Dao Lineage, evade the Heavenly Tribulation, and achieve immortality in three months, we will still have to face a siege by the Ten Sects Immortals."

The Sect Examination Clone said, "Which is why we must possess absolute power once we achieve immortality!"

Zhang Yu thought to himself, "In terms of power, I can improve across four directions."

"The first direction is a foundation of multiple Dao Lineages based on the Four Books, allowing me to master several Dao Lineages after achieving immortality."

"The second direction is a Heaven-Opening cultivation art. I need one that is better suited to me and my Dao Lineage after becoming an Immortal, which will enhance the combat power converted from my Dao Lineage."

"The third direction is an Immortal Artifact... If I can obtain an Immortal Artifact suited to me, it will also greatly enhance my combat power."

"The fourth direction is the Books, Dao Seeds, and Chain Formation Diagrams I am currently accumulating; they still require continuous improvement, as all of this constitutes my foundational reserves."

As Zhang Yu's thoughts raced, he pondered internally, "I have been advancing across these four directions all along, but now I must accelerate the pace."

"After all, I only have three months left. In three months... I must achieve immortality."

Immortal Slayer said, "Enhancing our strength has been progressing steadily, but as for the Heavenly Tribulation Dao Lineage, we still have no clue up to now. It seems a trip to the Heavenly Sword Sect is unavoidable."

Zhang Yu nodded slightly and began conversing with Bai Zhenzhen, asking about the Heavenly Sword Sect's current situation.

The moment Bai Zhenzhen heard him making inquiries, she said straightforwardly, "Yu Zi, you want to learn about the Heavenly Tribulation Dao Lineage to prepare to achieve immortality, don't you?"

Hearing this, Zhang Yu smiled helplessly to himself. "Has A-Zhen figured it out? It makes sense. Given my current status, I'm afraid any move to look into the Heavenly Tribulation might be seen as... being unwilling to accept my fate, still harboring thoughts of achieving immortality."

Immortal Slayer reminded him, "Zhang Yu, if you want to use the Ascension Selection as a cover to ultimately achieve immortality illegally, it can't just be a concealment in terms of Immortal Dao Technology; it has to be a comprehensive cover-up."

"For example, we should send signals to the outside world expressing our intention not to achieve immortality for the time being, lowering the Immortals' vigilance as much as possible to keep them steady."

---

When Su Xianchen looked at Zhang Yu before him, a hint of surprise still lingered in his heart. "What does His Royal Highness the Crown Prince mean by suddenly granting me an audience?"

Zhang Yu spoke words of encouragement, "Xianchen, since you are representing the Myriad Laws Sect this time, you must perform well in the Ascension Selection. The celebratory wine has already been opened for you, so be sure not to get ahead of yourself too soon."

Hearing this, Su Xianchen's heart stirred. "Is the Crown Prince extending an olive branch? He... supports me in achieving immortality?"

Moments later, fueled by wave upon wave of speculation, the news spread throughout the Spirit Realm.

"His Highness the Crown Prince granted an audience to Su Xianchen! He directly said the celebratory wine has already been opened for him, which is an unmistakable blessing for Su Xianchen to achieve immortality and establish the Dao."

"Drinking wine is an ancient rite. His Highness is promoting ancient customs once again."

"I told you so! If even you all know to back down before the Ten Sects Immortals, how could the Crown Prince not know? Didn't he bend the knee far more gracefully than any of you?"

"As long as there's no war, everything's fine! As long as there's no war, everyone can make money!"

For a time, the Myriad Laws Sect's Spirit Realm was filled with jubilation.

Yet watching this unfold, Zhang Yu found it utterly comical.

With the apex of the Immortal Dao as its goal, Kunxu had charged relentlessly forward for countless years under the banner of aspiration for the Immortal Dao. Yet at this very moment, its countless cultivators were actually rejoicing over him, the Crown Prince, 'failing to achieve immortality.'

Right then, Immortal Roujia of the Reformist Faction also contacted Zhang Yu.

Immortal Roujia projected her avatar downward and said with a benevolent look, "Crown Prince, I heard you granted Su Xianchen an audience?"

"Indeed." Zhang Yu nodded and said, "After careful consideration, given the current situation, Su Xianchen is indeed more suited to achieve immortality than I am."

Immortal Roujia's eyes lit up, and she said, "That Your Highness the Crown Prince possesses such insight is truly a blessing for the Myriad Laws Sect, a blessing for Kunxu. Once the time is ripe in the future, we will certainly give our full support for you to achieve immortality and establish the Dao."

Zhang Yu, however, shook his head and said with righteous conviction, "If my Law Edict Expiration Date were truly elevated into a Dao Lineage, where would that leave the other senior Immortals?"

"The Immortals have leaped beyond the Three Debts, exist outside the Five Insurances, are free from karmic labor and spiritual leaks, enjoying both financial freedom and freedom of cultivation. How could they allow themselves to be bound by a new Dao Lineage?"

"If I were an Immortal, I wouldn't allow anyone to establish such a Dao Lineage either."

"And if I were to pass the Law Edict Expiration Date to someone else, I wouldn't allow him to achieve immortality either."

"Therefore, I have made up my mind: I will change my Law Edict and establish a different Dao Lineage in the future."

Upon hearing this, Immortal Roujia looked at him with an approving gaze, nodding slightly. "The Crown Prince speaks with great wisdom. It is for the best that you can understand this truth."

To Immortal Roujia, Zhang Yu's reaction could not have been more natural.

*"After all, even with Zhang Yu's current power and influence, as long as he is in his right mind, he couldn't possibly hope to single-handedly oppose the entire Immortal Class of the Ten Great Sects."*

Yet she also understood that Zhang Yu naturally would not be willing to give up on achieving immortality.

*"Zhang Yu climbed all the way to the top and holds immense power, but isn't that all just to achieve immortality? How could he give up so easily?"*

Taking all this into consideration, changing his Dao Lineage to achieve immortality was the optimal and most reasonable choice. *"If I were Zhang Yu, I would make the same choice."*

*"On the path of the Immortal Dao, one must learn to compromise. Without compromise, how could one climb higher? How could one achieve immortality?"*

*"Even the ten Immortal Emperors must compromise with one another, so how could it be any different for the rest of us?"*

Thoughts raced through Immortal Roujia's mind. After conversing with Zhang Yu for a while longer, she withdrew her projection and consciousness.

Immediately afterward, the telepathic thoughts of Immortal Venerable Profound Wisdom and Immortal Venerable Heavenly Fortune arrived one after another, inquiring about Zhang Yu's situation.

Immortal Roujia replied, "Rest assured, Immortal Venerables. Zhang Yu is no fool. Under the pressure of the Ten Sects Immortals, he never had any choice other than to kneel and admit defeat. He has already decided to change his Dao Lineage. All we need to do now is watch his performance."

On the other side, Zhang Yu summoned Yu Xinghan directly and said to him, "Xinghan, I intend to change my Law Edict. In the future, when establishing my Dao... I will also choose a different target."

Zhang Yu did not choose to tell Yu Xinghan the truth. After all, he knew that a performance had to be fully committed to reduce the chances of being discovered by the Immortals.

Upon hearing this, Yu Xinghan was slightly startled, but the next moment, he let out a profound sigh of relief.

*"It's good that he's changing it. It's really for the best..."* Yu Xinghan sighed inwardly. *"The Crown Prince truly lives up to his title. He did not, under the distortion of his Immortal Dao aspiration, proactively make enemies of the Ten Sects Immortals for the sake of short-term gains."*

In Yu Xinghan's eyes, if the current Zhang Yu were to rashly wage war against the Ten Sects Immortals, defeat would be an absolute certainty. When that time came, the fruits of their years of hard work would vanish in an instant.

Zhang Yu continued, "Xinghan, organize the Law Edict Management Committee to see who throughout the sect is most suitable to take over the Law Edict Expiration Date, and help me design something to see what kind of Law Edict would be suitable for my use."

"I'll give you... three months to finish it."

"In three months, I will formally change my Law Edict."

As word of Zhang Yu's moves spread, it was as if a dark cloud had dissipated over the Myriad Laws Sect. The atmosphere across the entire sect grew increasingly stable, and even the stock prices rallied through several surges.

Furthermore, several Immortals — including Immortals of the Innate Faction — took turns recommending their Dao Lineages to Zhang Yu, each hoping he would support and elevate them, and they offered quite generous terms.

Sensing the shift in the sect's atmosphere, Zhang Yu sighed. "Just because of my superficial compromise, the Immortals have grown friendly, public sentiment has settled, and all at once, there seems to be peace across myriad markets."

Immortal Slayer said, "That is the allure of compromise. Because compromise actually works, because it truly buys temporary stability and immense short-term gains, it has become the choice of countless people."

Over the next few days, Zhang Yu divided his focus into numerous strands of consciousness to communicate with the Innate Immortals regarding the elevation of their Dao Lineages, seemingly having genuinely given up on achieving immortality for the time being.

Meanwhile, on the other side, the Ascension Selection had already kicked off in full swing. 

The candidates from the Ten Great Sects burned through countless Immortal Coins, promoting their philosophies, their Law Edicts, and their Dao Lineages. 

In the end, the three with the highest approval ratings would earn the qualification to achieve immortality, becoming legal Immortals of Kunxu and establishing their own Dao Lineages. 

Everyone knew that this new cohort of Immortals would sway the balance between sects and Dao Lineages, determining the distribution of countless interests. 

Countless cultivators and Immortals would inevitably make their moves surrounding this Ascension Selection. 

For a time, more and more eyes throughout Kunxu were drawn to it. 

---

Meanwhile, within the Heavenly Sword Sect. 

Bai Zhenzhen opened a package, and a flying sword crafted by the Myriad Laws Sect shot out like lightning, circling around her. 

As the flying sword vibrated, gentle breezes rippled forth, gathering at Bai Zhenzhen's side.

"A-Zhen, I'm here."

It turned out that Zhang Yu had separated a strand of his will and a clone, using the Blood Tide Essence Qi Sacred Contemplation to transform into a breeze. Concealing his traces with his Dao Seed and cloaking his information with the Spirit Realm Dao Lineage, he had disguised himself as the air inside the package all the way here, finally arriving before Bai Zhenzhen.

Bai Zhenzhen nodded, using her Evil God Power to transmit her voice directly into Zhang Yu's mind: "Yu Zi, you came at just the right time. As I've expanded my Law Edict, I have already brought Heavenly Tribulation Corp within its scope. If you want to investigate, you can come with me."

Heavenly Tribulation Corp was a massive corporate conglomerate controlled by Heavenly Tribulation Immortal Venerable. The operations of the thunder tribulation Zhang Yu had experienced in the past were part of Heavenly Tribulation Corp's business.

Zhang Yu's goal in coming to Heavenly Sword Sect this time was precisely to investigate the Heavenly Tribulation Dao Lineage through Heavenly Tribulation Corp — particularly the situation regarding the Immortal Ascension Heavenly Tribulation.
