# Chapter 1028: Resurrect, My Great Sage

Although wave after wave of human-monster conflicts had already erupted across the entire sea of conciousness world because of the Monster God, followed by a succession of mutual attacks that continuously dealt conceptual shocks, the impact brought by this group of cultivators from the Myriad Laws Sect was still far too immense for the sea of conciousness world's numerous wills at this moment.

Race, region, rank, income, cultivation realm, death... their extreme pursuit of virtually every interest in the world, their twisted classification of all hierarchies, and their bone-deep, morbid competitiveness and greed all profoundly shocked the Desolate Ox Great Sage's collective will.

From where had such demonic heretics come running?

At that moment, the entire sea of conciousness world erupted into an incomparably violent tremor.

Countless strands of the Desolate Ox Great Sage's collective will burst forth with an instinctive anger and hostility toward the cultivators from the Myriad Laws Sect.

Under this stimulation, a long-dormant will gradually began to awaken from the deepest reaches of the sea of conciousness world.

Zhang Yu knew very well in his heart that this was likely the primary will belonging to the Desolate Ox Great Sage — the supreme controller of the myriad collective wills in this world today.

On the other side, the Herald God swiftly analyzed the situation before him in his mind: *"Although doing this can rouse the Desolate Ox Great Sage, what happens after he wakes up? What do we do then?"*

Having reviewed the information on the Desolate Ox Great Sage, just thinking about it was enough for the Herald God to guess his reaction upon awakening.

*"The gap between ancient and modern perspectives is far too vast. When the Desolate Ox wakes up and sees Kunxu as it is today, he'll only see a chaotic revelry of demons, devils ruling the highest heavens, and in the end, he'll wish for nothing more than to destroy everything and start anew, won't he?"*

*"If that's the case, then Zhang Yu and the others might also... become targets of the Desolate Ox Great Sage's attacks? He might even unleash instinctual attacks during the very process of awakening."*

Meanwhile, on the other side, Fu Ji said in surprise, "Your Highness, did you anticipate summoning the cultivators of the Myriad Laws Sect from the very beginning?"

The Sect Examination Clone laughed heartily and said, "Indeed, Little Fu. How could I possibly place my hopes and chips on the uncivilized rabble of this sea of conciousness world?

"Monster Gods? Collective will lifeforms? What a joke. I, the Crown Prince, command the Myriad Laws Sect. How could those collective wills ever be as useful as my Sect Staff and outsourced cultivators?

"What era do they think it is, still playing these games? Today, I'll let them witness the superiority of the new-era Kunxu mindset."

Fu Ji agreed, "Well said! The Crown Prince truly lives up to his title. How could the mindset of these monster livestock ever compare to yours?"

Right then, the tremors across the entire world grew increasingly violent. The Sect Examination Clone narrowed his eyes and said, "This Desolate Ox Great Sage is about to throw a morning tantrum. Fu Ji... hold onto me tightly."

The Sect Examination Clone gripped Fu Ji's small hand tightly. In the next moment, not only did the entire sea of conciousness world tremble, but tremors also rippled through the layers of the Spirit Realm.

At the very same time, countless cultivators across Kunxu experienced lag, latency, and even outright service outages in the Spirit Realm.

Zhang Yu could sense that the glitch caused by the Desolate Ox Great Sage in just that single instant was enough to incur massive losses in Immortal Coins.

Fortunately, it lasted only a split second before the Spirit Realm returned to normal.

Zhang Yu thought to himself, *"The current situation is at most like the Desolate Ox Great Sage rolling over in bed, briefly affecting the Spirit Realm. If he were to fully awaken, the impact on the Spirit Realm would be immeasurable. I must secure this power."*

The Monster God felt all this as well, his desire for the Desolate Ox Great Sage's power burning even stronger: "With the Immortal Emperors temporarily away, the Desolate Ox serves as the foundation of the Spirit Realm, holding the highest authority... in the operation of the Spirit Realm Dao Lineage."

"If I can grasp this authority, it will be enough to rein in the Heavenly Court, and even erect a high wall within the Spirit Realm between the 19th and 21st layers, preventing the Immortal Class from easily interfering with the sects through the Spirit Realm..."

At that moment, a powerful surge of will rose up, sweeping across the entire sea of conciousness world.

Boom!

In that instant, Zhang Yu felt as if a colossal tsunami, tens of thousands of meters high, had slammed head-on into him. His thoughts violently convulsed, and his mind came to a momentary halt.

At this instant, Zhang Yu felt as if he had been struck head-on by a tsunami tens of thousands of meters high. His thoughts violently shook, and his mind came to a momentary halt.

"Is this... the mental impact of the Desolate Ox Great Sage?"

In just a fraction of a second, Zhang Yu felt as though he was on the verge of being blasted out of the sea of consciousness world.

He could feel even more clearly that the Desolate Ox Great Sage's mental impact was like an earth-shattering storm sweeping across the world, currently sweeping toward all the interlopers within the entire sea of consciousness world, aiming to expel them all.

At the same time, the Monster God on the other side suffered the mental impact of the Desolate Ox Great Sage as well.

He felt as though his mind was being bombarded by a force like an earth-shattering mountain-and-sea deluge, relentlessly squeezed out of the Desolate Ox Great Sage's sea of consciousness world with an irresistible momentum.

*"Is the Desolate Ox Great Sage instinctively expelling the invaders of the sea of consciousness world?"*

The Monster God inwardly marveled, *"As expected of the one who was once the other half of the Celestial Monster Immortal Emperor. Even with only this remnant wisp of will left behind, the shockwave it creates can still easily expel an Ascension Realm cultivator from the sea of consciousness."*

*"However, I am no ordinary Ascension Realm cultivator."*

"Monster Gods, heed my command!"

With a thunderous roar from the Monster God, his countless collective wills scattered across the sea of consciousness world now linked together. They seemed to transform into a century-old tree, sinking tentacles deep into every corner of the sea of consciousness world to staunchly resist the Great Sage's mental onslaught.

"Desolate Ox Great Sage! Look closely! I am your true successor!"

The Monster God thought to himself, *"I have inherited the will of the Desolate Ox Great Sage, and as a collective lifeform, our minds are united as one. With unified thought, we are far better equipped to withstand the Great Sage's mental impact."*

"In contrast, Zhang Yu's side is full of conflicting agendas and mutual prejudice right now. Even if they manage to string their wills together, they remain nothing more than a loose sheet of sand. How could they possibly resist the impact like I can? They are bound to be driven out of the sea of consciousness world by the Great Sage."

The Monster God knew the nature of Kunxu cultivators all too well. Every single one of them had their own interests to pursue, every single one had their own petty schemes, and every single one was trying their hardest to fish in troubled waters, deceive their superiors and oppress subordinates, and take whatever kickbacks they could... Faced with a crisis, how could they ever unify their thoughts to resist together?

Thinking of this, the Monster God could not help but sigh inwardly, *"In the end, there is only one way to resist the apocalypse... and that is for an absolute powerhouse to step forth and suppress everything. Looking across all of Kunxu today, only the Celestial Monster Immortal Emperor can achieve this."*

Just as the Monster Saint was pondering this, he suddenly felt an iron wall rise within his sea of consciousness world, stubbornly withstanding the Desolate Ox Great Sage's apocalyptic, tsunami-like onslaught.

"That is..."

In the Monster God's perception, he saw Zhang Yu's will converge with that of countless Myriad Laws Sect cultivators. Their thoughts linked together, remarkably forming a great formation to directly confront the mental assault of the Desolate Ox Great Sage.

Especially those Sect Staff members of the Myriad Laws Sect — at this very moment, their wills whole-heartedly supported Zhang Yu, acting like countless hands braced firmly against his back to help him withstand the Desolate Ox Great Sage's violent onslaught.

To the Monster God's astonishment, because their gathered will was so vast and potent, these cultivators were actually more stable than he was while resisting the Great Sage's mental onslaught.

*"How is this possible? Millions of minds united as one? A fortress of solidarity? Are these even Kunxu cultivators?"*

At the same time, Zhang Yu's intent echoed in the minds of the many Myriad Laws Cultivators: "Cast aside all distracting thoughts and obey my every command! Once the mission is accomplished, everyone gets a one-grade salary raise!"

Bu Yuangui: "Everything as His Highness the Crown Prince commands!"

Fa Liuyuan: "His Highness's thoughts are my thoughts!"

Lu Hengzhang: "I have no thoughts of my own! Crown Prince, please treat me as a tool and use me as you wish!"

At this moment, only a single thought remained in the minds of the numerous cultivators: "Just like that, we get a one-grade salary raise? The Crown Prince is truly virtuous!"

In this instant, the many Myriad Laws Cultivators were bound together by their shared salary, and all ideological clashes, conceptual conflicts, and opposing wills... were temporarily cast aside as if they never existed in the face of a pay raise.

At this very second, while connected to Zhang Yu's intent, they obeyed him wholeheartedly, as if becoming an extension of his own body, moving effortlessly at his command.

Zhang Yu felt that as the Great Sage's Intent he had manifested circulated, there was not the slightest obstruction. Every thought and impulse of his met neither dissent nor resistance, but was instead propelled forward by countless individuals.

Sensing all of this, Fu Ji burst out laughing. "Your Highness! You are so right. Money and power... are far more effective than any collective will! What kind of collective lifeform could ever be as obedient as a Kunxu Cultivator who's had money thrown at them?"

The Sect Examination Clone said smugly, "That bunch of monster livestock from the Celestial Monster Sect has always lacked discipline, and their Sect Nature ranks near the bottom among the Ten Great Sects. How could they possibly compare to our Myriad Laws Sect?

"Besides, even if they knew, would a mere beast have deep pockets like this Crown Prince? How many people's salaries could he possibly raise? If he can barely give anyone a raise, how could he ever connect heart-to-heart with the people? Today, this Crown Prince will show these fellows what true 'all hearts bound by salary' really means!"

Under the Monster God's shocked gaze, Zhang Yu withstood the impact of the Great Sage's Intent in a manner far more stable than the Monster God could manage. When the impact ceased, Zhang Yu was also closer to the center of the sea of consciousness world than the Monster God was.

And at this moment, in the center of the sea of consciousness world, a tall, powerfully built man with two long ox horns on his head was slowly emerging.

The true form of the Desolate Ox in the sea of consciousness world possessed both the physiological traits of the monsterfolk and the appearance of a human, looking like a symbol of the fusion between the human and monster races.

*"It's the Desolate Ox Great Sage. He is completely awake."*

The Monster God's original plan had been to influence the Desolate Ox Great Sage's collective will step by step, altering his thoughts gradually before finally waking him up.

However, at this moment, the Desolate Ox had been rashly startled awake by Zhang Yu, so that plan was clearly no longer viable.

*"I can only resort to the secondary plan. The very first step now is to establish communication with the Desolate Ox Great Sage."*

The Monster God understood that having slumbered for so long, the Desolate Ox Great Sage lacked a full grasp of Kunxu's current state.

Therefore, during this brief window when the Great Sage had just awakened and had not yet pieced everything together, whoever communicated with him first and won his trust would gain the upper hand.

Just as the Monster God prepared to seize the initiative and connect minds with him, he saw Zhang Yu, who was positioned closer, already making his move — his intent crashing toward the Desolate Ox Great Sage a step ahead.

*"Damn it, that bastard beat me to it."*

Meanwhile, Zhang Yu's intent, bearing a massive volume of information, was already pouring into the Desolate Ox Great Sage before him. "Senior Desolate Ox, the situation in Kunxu right now is in dire peril. Allow me to explain our current circumstances."

In the next moment, Zhang Yu's intent transmitted his various experiences, the conditions and changes in Kunxu over the years, the impending crisis of the End, and much more, streaming it all directly into the Desolate Ox Great Sage.

As their consciousnesses clashed, the Desolate Ox Great Sage also felt Zhang Yu's struggles, his perseverance, and even more so the pressure of modern Kunxu, the aberrations of the era, the threat of doomsday...

There were the bizarre and grotesque high schools, universities, and sects; the Ten Great Sects and ten Immortal Emperors who lashed the realm and exploited all living beings; and the common people of the world who, driven by the Aspiration for the Immortal Dao, had in the Desolate Ox Great Sage's eyes gone completely mad.

"Celibate cultivation, original gender, First Death Experience..."

"Material Humans, working after death, paid reincarnation..."

"Downward compatibility, upward socializing, an era of wage oppression..."

"Illegal treatment, legal homicide, licensed hit-and-runs..."

Everything in modern Kunxu was enough to bring wave after wave of shock to the Desolate Ox Great Sage at this moment. "Has the world... become like this?"

No matter how far the Desolate Ox Great Sage had stretched his imagination in the past, he could never have conceived that the future Kunxu would come to this.

At the same time, the Desolate Ox Great Sage's view of Zhang Yu before him, as well as the many cultivators of the Myriad Laws Sect, underwent a transformation.

If, in the immediate moments after awakening, he had looked back at the performance of Zhang Yu and the numerous cultivators of the Myriad Laws Sect in the sea of consciousness world, he would have wondered whether they were a bunch of heretical demons. 

However, after absorbing the vast amount of information Zhang Yu had sent, and contrasting it against the Ten Great Sects, the various high-ranking cultivators, and the Immortal Emperors, the Zhang Yu before him seemed enveloped in layers of radiant halos in the eyes of the Desolate Ox Great Sage. He was already the most suitable candidate in this era to become the Great Sage's Heir.

Yet right at that moment, the Monster God's thoughts swept forward as well.

Zhang Yu warned, "Senior Desolate Ox, this Monster God is a contingency left behind by the Celestial Monster Immortal Emperor to control you. Do not let him influence you."

The Monster God, meanwhile, continued transmitting his thoughts toward Desolate Ox: *"Great Sage! Perhaps you no longer remember, but you and the Celestial Monster Immortal Emperor were originally one and the same! The Celestial Monster Immortal Emperor is currently the only being who can save Kunxu and halt the apocalypse!"*

Hearing the title of the Celestial Monster Immortal Emperor, the Desolate Ox Great Sage's thoughts stirred, but in the end, he did not stop the Monster God. Instead, he accepted all the information transmitted by the other party.

A flicker of delight surfaced on the Monster God's face, and he continued transmitting his thoughts: *"Great Sage! To ascend to the Supreme Seat, to rule Kunxu in the future, and to create the End's golden age, the Immortal Emperor endured immense humiliation and split into two with you..."*

Right at that moment, Zhang Yu felt the Desolate Ox Great Sage's consciousness sweep toward him, pouring in a vast influx of information along with it. 

Zhang Yu was slightly taken aback, but after processing it, he realized, *"The Desolate Ox Great Sage... gave me a copy of the information the Monster God transmitted to him as well? No, it's not just the Monster God's information. It seems there are also the Desolate Ox Great Sage's own memories?"*

Meanwhile, the Celestial Monster Immortal Emperor seized that opportunity to become a new Immortal Emperor..."*
