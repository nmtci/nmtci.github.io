Meanwhile, just as the Lower Realm Mutual Aid Group was bustling with lively activity, Zhang Yu was sensing the Blazing Sun Meridian on the Dao Seed Register, carefully comprehending the new abilities within the Dao Seed.

Accompanied by a surge of magic power within his body, Zhang Yu's Profound Cold Magic Power suddenly transformed into Blazing Sun Magic Power.

A surging current of heat gushed out from his Dantian. In the blink of an eye, it penetrated all the meridians in Zhang Yu's body, flowing into every fiber of his flesh and blood.

Wisps of hot currents, like tiny flames, shot out from Zhang Yu's eyes, ears, mouth, and nose, and even from every inch of his skin.

At this moment, Zhang Yu felt his vital energy and physical power suddenly surge, even giving rise to a feeling of losing control.

"If I move recklessly now, I'm afraid I might accidentally destroy the surrounding environment, right?"

"The power of this physical body has already far surpassed the level I can perfectly control."

Zhang Yu exhaled a breath, and billowing hot currents surged out. Carrying an astonishing heat that slightly distorted the atmosphere, it rushed madly toward the surroundings, instantly turning into a fierce gale.

At the same time, the data in the Feather Book shifted, and Zhang Yu's current physical strength appeared before his eyes.

"61.5 points? It directly broke through the Void Refinement upper limit."

"But every shred of flesh and blood, as well as my vital energy, is operating beyond its limits. My lifespan is burning at almost 10,000 times the normal rate."

The next moment, Zhang Yu abruptly circulated the Immortal Sect's Strategic-Tier Technique, the *Kunpeng Heaven-Devouring Art*, and his entire being entered the Overlimit Development stage.

His physical body, which was already like an erupting volcano, now felt as though it had been injected with tens of thousands of tons of oil and natural gas, ushering in an explosive enhancement to Zhang Yu's physical body.

61.7 points!

61.9 points!

62.5 points!

The data for his physical strength changed drastically in front of Zhang Yu, finally reaching 63.1 points in a single breath. Only then did the explosive growth stop, beginning to fluctuate back and forth between 63.1 points and 62.9 points.

"Is this the physical strength that can only be tempered in the Tribulation Transcendence Realm?"

At this moment, Zhang Yu felt that with just a single punch, the explosion caused by his fist shattering the air could whip up a storm that would blow across the entire Immortal Grotto-Heaven.

Zhang Yu silently sensed the condition of his body and estimated in his heart, "Under the continuous operation of the level 4 Blazing Sun Meridian, this beyond-limits state... I can maintain it right up until the moment my lifespan is exhausted."

"With my current lifespan... maintaining this state for a few months wouldn't be a big problem."

The next moment, Zhang Yu abruptly retracted the Blazing Sun Meridian within his body, returning his physical body back to its normal state.

"Unless it's extremely lucrative work where the hourly profit can at least cover the price of the lifespan I consume, otherwise it's too much of a loss."

Zhang Yu immediately realized that the beyond-limits state brought by this Blazing Sun Meridian was only suitable for use at critical moments.

Then, he tried other uses for the Blazing Sun Meridian.

"Just like before, injecting it into a magical artifact can improve its efficiency, but it will drastically reduce its lifespan, leaving it scrapped after just a few hours of use."

"However, if paired with [Empress Wa Creations], it can allow temporary magical artifacts... to exceed their 50% performance limit within an hour, reaching 85% of their standard performance."

Zhang Yu thought to himself, "This actually adds another rental item. Maybe not many people will rent it, but it should still increase my monthly income by over a hundred thousand, right?"

Finally, Zhang Yu sensed the new function of the level 4 Blazing Sun Meridian.

"It can inject magic power into someone else's body, increasing their efficiency while... increasing their lifespan consumption."

"Of course, it's also possible to... only increase their lifespan consumption without improving their efficiency."

Zhang Yu thought, "This has directly become an attack method. Instead of injuring people, it deducts their lifespan."

Naturally, after a brief moment of comprehension, Zhang Yu understood that this method of deducting lifespan required injecting more magic power to drain it faster.

To burn lifespan at ten thousand times the efficiency, just like the Overlimit State, one needed to pierce through the opponent's entire body with Blazing Sun Magic Power.

The Sect Examination Clone also noticed this new function of the Blazing Sun Meridian, and upon hearing this, he cheered loudly, "Excellent! Right when the freezing of Law Edicts happens next year, we might just have to fight someone."

"With the enhancement of this Blazing Sun Meridian, if our punch could originally cause 10,000 Immortal Coins of damage to a person's wallet, adding this layer of lifespan deduction will cause at least 15,000 Immortal Coins, or even 20,000 Immortal Coins of damage."

Zhang Yu nodded slightly, thinking to himself, "Sigh, the higher the cultivation realm and rank, the more one can... ignore the rules when necessary."

He recalled the process of himself and Bu Yingshu causing a ruckus in the pharmaceutical company's Balanced-Life Grotto-Heaven, thinking to himself, "If it were in the past, if I dared to cause a scene in this kind of private minor realm, I would probably have been taken down on the spot. But now, it has become a means of struggle, simply because I have also become a controller of a part of the rules."

Immortal Slayer said, "In martial duels of upper-tier cultivators, martial prowess is naturally indispensable. Only with sufficient martial force to hold the field and a sufficient combat threat can you avoid losses and allow many matters to be resolved peacefully. As this Blazing Sun Meridian continues to improve in the future, it might become an increasingly greater help to you..."

"Also, you must remind Xuan Ge to try his best not to expose the upgraded Blazing Sun Meridian too early. Keep it hidden for as long as possible."

Zhang Yu nodded slightly. Then, he switched his Dao Seed, replacing the Blazing Sun Meridian with the Profound Cold Meridian.

"The biggest flaw of the Blazing Sun Meridian is the sharp increase in lifespan consumption; the cost is too high."

"Then, can I use the Profound Cold Meridian to reduce the cost?"

As the Profound Cold flowed through his entire body, Zhang Yu felt a wave of icy coolness wash over him. Every trace of his vital energy seemed to be tightly locked in place, and the depletion of his lifespan instantly slowed down by a hundredfold.

Zhang Yu secretly calculated in his mind, "I just tested the Blazing Sun Meridian for about 4 to 5 minutes, and it directly consumed nearly a month of my lifespan."

"But if I can continuously circulate the Profound Cold Meridian, it will take about seven hours to achieve a longevity extension of one month, which can make up for the depletion of the Blazing Sun Meridian."

"In other words, for every second I use the Blazing Sun Meridian, I need to maintain the Profound Cold Meridian for about 100 seconds just to balance out the Lifespan consumption."

Thinking of this, Zhang Yu nodded slightly. The existence of the Profound Cold Meridian significantly reduced the price of using the Blazing Sun Meridian for him.

*"It's right here, right in the very center of this Myriad Gold Mystic Realm."*

*"Besides, why would this ancestor's corpse be in this location? Whose corpse did Ying Xintian leave here?"*
