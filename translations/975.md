# Chapter 975: Reverse Bloodline Inheritance, Tribulation Transcendence Bet

Accompanied by the nourishment of various Void Refinement cultivation arts, the Azure Lotus Sword Embryo continuously absorbed Immortal Dao nutrients from them, and ultimately, driven by Zhang Yu, it finally stepped into level 6.

In an instant, Zhang Yu felt the Feather Spirit Root within his body erupt with a brand-new change.

Ever since successfully replicating Bai Zhenzhen's Azure Lotus Sword Embryo, Zhang Yu could feel the uniqueness of this Dao Seed, which was a terrifying adaptability.

So much so that the exact same Dao Seed could actually have different evolutionary directions in him and Bai Zhenzhen. The effects of each upgrade were also completely different, becoming the Feather Spirit Root and True Spirit Root respectively.

For example, at this very moment, Zhang Yu felt the power of the Feather Spirit Root inside his body gradually expanding deep into his flesh and bloodline, even gradually forming a connection with the Emperor Creation Seed concealed within his bloodline, as well as the Heaven-Opening cultivation art called Innate Dao-Vindication Scripture.

Zhang Yu thought to himself, "The Azure Lotus Sword Embryo will always undergo adaptive evolution based on my changes."

"During university, I mastered more and more Chain Formation Diagrams, and after the Azure Lotus Sword Embryo upgraded... it increased my adaptability to the Chain Formation Diagrams."

"Later, as I became increasingly tanky and more resistant to beatings, upgrading... further enhanced my strike resistance."

"When I developed the Immortal Grotto-Heaven, it allowed me to anchor to the Immortal Grotto-Heaven and obtain the Tyrant Armor ability."

"And over this recent period, the things that have influenced and changed me the most... are undoubtedly the Emperor Creation Seed passed down by Senior Supreme Truth and the Heaven-Opening cultivation art, the Innate Dao-Vindication Scripture."

Feeling the drastic changes within his body, Zhang Yu sighed inwardly, "As expected, after the Azure Lotus Sword Embryo upgraded this time, it also underwent a targeted evolution..."

Along with the evolution of the Feather Spirit Root in his body, Zhang Yu felt a wondrous reaction occur within his bloodline power and physical mystic power. 

Within his bloodline senses, his clones, as well as Bai Zhenzhen, Yu Xinghan, Le Mulan, Magnetic Pole, Old Gao... all seemed to be transmitting some sort of information to him.

Zhang Yu was surprised inwardly, "The extensions of my bloodline are feeding information back to me?"

Zhang Yu could sense that for his clones currently participating in the testing work at the factory, every time the Supreme Yang Radiance God erupted with scorching heat, every instance of their resistance, endurance, and suffering... all of it transformed into bloodline information that was fed back to the Feather Spirit Root, which then acted upon Zhang Yu's main body.

In addition to this, there was also continuous information coming from Bai Zhenzhen regarding sharp sword qi.

From Yu Xinghan's side, he gradually felt information regarding impact damage.

And from Le Mulan, what Zhang Yu felt was information about medical poisons damaging the body...

"Except for Brother Dao and Senior Supreme Truth — perhaps because the distance is too far? Or their cultivation realm is too high? Or perhaps due to some blockade, I can't sense the information they feed back, but for everyone else... I can sense them all."

"Incredible, my Feather Spirit Root is actually allowing me to absorb the bloodline information they send back, and subsequently produce a corresponding evolution?"

Next... following a series of tests and sensations, Zhang Yu finally understood exactly what effects the Level 6 Feather Spirit Root had brought him.

Zhang Yu named this newly evolved ability of the Feather Spirit Root "Reverse Inheritance".

The so-called inheritance is the phenomenon where biological traits are passed down from parents to offspring through genes.

Meanwhile, at this moment, through the feedback of information from the extension of the bloodline, Zhang Yu's body automatically produced an adaptive transformation.

"The clone endured a high-temperature attack, and my main body's resistance to high temperatures improved a little."

"The bodies of A-Zhen, Yu Xinghan, and the others suffered some kind of damage, and my main body will also improve a slight resistance."

"Truly... unbelievable."

Zhang Yu hadn't expected that the combination of the Azure Lotus Sword Embryo, Emperor Creation Seed, and Innate Dao-Vindication Scripture could produce such a heaven-defying effect.

The Sect Examination Clone rejoiced, "My great mother, worthy of being an Immortal Emperor, the Immortal Emperor Legacy left to me is simply a miracle everywhere!"

Zhang Yu couldn't help but nod slightly. It was exactly because of the power of this Immortal Emperor Legacy that, with a little combination of other Immortal Dao Technology, it could burst out different sparks.

During the testing, Zhang Yu discovered that the efficiency of this reverse inheritance varied depending on the person transmitting it.

"The clone's efficiency are the highest, and the closer the distance, the better the effect."

"As for others... the longer they have been given a Dao Seed me, or I replicated theirs, and the closer the distance, the better the effect."

Feeling the subtle changes in his body, Zhang Yu thought to himself, "However, even for a clone, this process is extremely subtle."

Immortal Slayer commented, "Just like bloodline descendants passed down from generation to generation, this reverse inheritance... also requires gradual improvement over years and months. It is an accumulation of every little bit... over countless years."

"Its advantage lies in being passive, growing and becoming stronger naturally."

Zhang Yu said, "I can't just let this growth happen naturally... Some adaptations, like a drug resistance that reduces the efficacy of medicine, need to be actively eliminated by me."

Sensing the constant feedback within the bloodline information, Zhang Yu immediately understood that he could not accept it in its entirety.

"My body, my rules. Every little change must be approved by me personally. I can't just leave it all to the Azure Lotus Sword Embryo to blindly agree to everything."

At this moment, Zhang Yu felt that after his physical body received the bloodline information, it was as if pop-up windows were constantly appearing.

Based on the high-temperature damage information, make the following adjustments to the body... [Agree/Deny].

Based on the impact damage information, make the following adjustments to the body... [Agree/Deny].

As streams of information continuously came in one after another, Zhang Yu decided that every single one must be approved by him, rather than agreed to by default.

"The real benefit is probably its breadth, isn't it? As my bloodline spreads out... getting wider and wider, with more and more people, and encountering more and more different environments, I will receive more bloodline feedback. Under this accumulation over time, my main body will be able to receive all-around enhancements," Zhang Yu thought to himself.

Thinking of this, Zhang Yu looked at Bai Zhenzhen with a face full of emotion and thought to himself, *"A-Zhen's Spirit Root is truly an amazing thing. It has always given me so much assistance."*

*"Especially this kind of adaptability, it always manages to adapt to my growth, making my cultivation yield twice the result with half the effort."*

The originally surging emotions and sword intents all calmed down in an instant. Her entire person seemed to transform from a passionate and surging active volcano into a frozen, snowy mountain. 

"The Sentient Heaven and Earth of the Heavenly Sword Sect is about using spirit to control matter."

"Right now, I can treat emotions as a pure form of power to drive my sword techniques."

"This means my utilization of emotions has reached an unprecedented level."

"Even..."

"It seems that as I've cultivated multiple cultivation arts and repeatedly transformed and enhanced emotions like Supreme Bliss, Shock, Sorrow, and Extreme Emotion, the True Spirit Root has undergone a new evolution upon its upgrade."

Bai Zhenzhen tried it out a little and directly converted all the emotions generated in her body into the emotion of supreme bliss, infusing it into the Bliss Sword Dao.

In an instant, she not only felt the power of her Bliss Sword Dao surge but also felt her efficiency in upgrading the grade of her sword techniques drastically increase alongside it.

"If I transform and infuse other feelings and emotions as well, then... the efficiency of my execution for any Emotion Sword Technique could increase fivefold, or even tenfold!"

Thinking of this, Bai Zhenzhen felt a surge of great joy in her heart, sensing that her next Strategic-Tier Sword Technique was already close at hand.

"I'm fine, I'm great, I'm absolutely fantastic!"

Those privileges of Bu Yingshu are useless on me, and her backers would even turn around and stop her."

"If the scale is huge, so big that it exceeds what the cultivator needs for themselves and can be sold in large quantities... then it might even be completely free of charge, with high rewards."

"In general, the larger the scale of the Thunder Tribulation triggered and the shorter the time needed to transcend the tribulation, the higher the yield."
