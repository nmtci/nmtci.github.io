# Chapter 1037: Honestly Grinding Merit Must Be Boring

Within the Supreme Clarity Realm, Zhang Pianpian had been keeping a close eye on Zhang Yu's situation.

When she saw that Zhang Yu was actually planning to waive the Spirit Realm data fees for all cultivators across the world, an expression of shock involuntarily appeared on her face.

Through repeated construction by the Ten Great Sects, the Spirit Realm had long since become an indispensable infrastructure for cultivators.

In Zhang Pianpian's understanding, a cultivator who could not connect to the Spirit Realm was practically unable to live, work, and cultivate in Kunxu. They would become the living dead, even worse off than Soul Cultivators.

"Only in places like the First Layer, where a vast amount of ancient facilities still exist, can cultivators live detached from the Spirit Realm. But how many cultivators are actually willing to live a life in the First Layer?"

Thus, in Zhang Pianpian's eyes, the Spirit Realm's data fees had gradually become a tax levied upon all cultivators — a necessary cost that cultivators had to pay if they wanted to continue surviving in Kunxu.

"What a massive undertaking..." Zhang Pianpian marveled. "An earth-shattering move indeed. With just this single stroke, he extends benevolence to the countless living beings in all of Kunxu, turning every single one of them into targets for his moral exploitation. This will surely generate a colossal amount of Morality Points."

"Yet the price is steep as well. How many people in this world could abandon such an immense fortune? Such an earth-shattering revenue?"

"Not to mention that this is mortgaging countless years of future earnings."

At the same time, the gaze of the figure beside Zhang Pianpian trembled slightly as well.

Through continuous conversations with Zhang Pianpian, the figure had developed a deeper understanding of the overall situation in Kunxu, particularly the human nature of the current era.

By now, the figure deeply understood the importance of money in this era, as well as how much the people of this age valued money and cultivation, and how madly they pursued them.

*"In the eyes of people today, by giving up the Spirit Realm's steady income, Zhang Yu is essentially severing his own arm and deliberately lowering the growth rate of his cultivation — a feat that defies common sense."*

*"Yet this move truly brings blessings to all under heaven."*

In the figure's view, this so-called Spirit Realm... ought to be free for the entire realm to use; even if covering the costs proved difficult, the fees should have been drastically reduced.

It could be said that Zhang Yu's current actions aligned perfectly with his thoughts, evoking a heartfelt sigh from within: *"To think that in this new era, such an astonishing act of benevolence could still exist."*

In particular, the figure considered that Zhang Yu was born in this era, raised in this era, influenced by the ideology of this era, and could be considered a pure modern Kunxu person.

After climbing to the position of Crown Prince of Myriad Laws, he could still perform such a good deed that benefited the whole world...

The figure sighed internally, *"It is truly exceedingly rare."*

Zhang Pianpian, standing nearby, said, "Senior, Zhang Yu hasn't disappointed you, has he?"

Sensing that the figure had no intention of rebutting, Zhang Pianpian continued in a swift exchange, "Even though Zhang Yu's Morality Points are about to surge, he might not necessarily surpass the Ten Greats Good People in the short term."

"When the Ten Greats Good People besiege Zhang Yu next, I'm afraid he won't be able to withstand it."

The figure seemed to smile slightly and said, "No matter. Let us watch a little longer."

"I want to see what else Zhang Yu is capable of doing next."

---

Meanwhile, inside the Morality Field.

Urged on by the Immortal Class, the mega-fund of the Ten Great Good People was unleashed with ferocious power. 

"Hahaha, farming Merit honestly is far too boring. Let us give you a hand!"

Amid the shouts of the Ten Great Good People, the surging mega-fund had already been injected into the account. The successful injection of such a massive sum immediately caused all Ten Great Good People to breathe a collective sigh of relief.

"It's in! It's in! It's all in! Your Highness the Crown Prince, you won't be able to escape this time."

"Hahahaha, Zhang Yu, do you feel our boundless capital now?"

"Now that you've been thoroughly infused with our funds, let the whole world witness the pathetic spectacle of you, the dignified Crown Prince of Myriad Laws, being morally exploited by us!"

"You want free traffic to morally exploit the people of the world? Then we shall exploit you in turn!"

Inside the Morality Field, the Ten Great Good People let out wild roars. A violent Virtue Pressure swept in all directions, illuminating everything like a blazing holy light.

In a corner, Divine Virtue Scum Children trembled as they watched the scene unfold.

"Too kind! Their morality has reached such heights that they are no longer on the same dimension as us — truly, these are the Ten Great Good People capable of moving all of Kunxu!"

"Fall back, quickly! If their goodwill even glances at us, our Morality Limit will be completely locked down, and we'll never be able to improve again, never raise our moral standard, and never become better people!"

At this moment, inside the Morality Field, all the Divine Children beneath the Ten Great Good People desperately concealed their presence, terrified of being targeted by them, inundated with vast capital, and turned into their moral puppets.

Yet in the very next instant, suspicion and unease stirred within the hearts of the Ten Great Good People.

This was simply because, even after they had taken turns pouring immense amounts of capital into him, the Zhang Yu before them showed not the slightest change.

"What is going on? Why? Why haven't we bestowed our grace upon him after pouring in so many Immortal Coins? Why haven't we been able to morally exploit him?"

"Could it be that because Zhang Yu is the Crown Prince of Myriad Laws, his net worth is so staggering that the money we gave isn't enough?"

"Impossible! Even a drop of grace should be repaid with a gushing spring, let alone with so much money transferred to him! Under the judgment of the Morality Field, every good deed he does ought to be influenced by us, and he should be offering up Morality Points to us!"

The Ten Great Good People conducted a rapid search and pinpointed the reason something felt off almost in the blink of an eye.

"Damn it! This isn't his account! This is a corporate account of the Myriad Laws Sect."

"Where is his account? A corporate account... another corporate account... still a corporate account... Why are all the accounts associated with him corporate accounts? Where is Zhang Yu's own account? Where is his own money kept?"

Backed by their immense backgrounds, the divine consciousness of the Ten Great Good People swept through the mega-corporations and platforms of various sects, combing heaven and earth in search of Zhang Yu's personal accounts.

"I don't believe it! He can't possibly hide his own accounts completely, or how else would he receive money? How would he line his own pockets? How would he conduct capital operations? It must be hidden somewhere..."

Yet, even though their consciousness delved down to the nine nethers and soared high into the heavens, and even though they rallied countless cultivators in an unprecedented massive search, they still could not find a single private account belonging to Zhang Yu.

"This one has been cancelled."

"That one was cancelled too. Why have all of them been cancelled?"

"Impossible! How could a person not have their own personal bank account? Are they even human at that point?"

Then, an inconceivable conjecture surfaced in their minds.

At the same time, Zhang Yu was seen entirely enveloped in a blazing radiance of morality, and the Morality Points above his head were skyrocketing with every passing second.

"I hold no private accounts."

Zhang Yu's voice resonated throughout the Heavenly Court and beyond at this moment. "My food, clothing, daily expenses, and Immortal Dao resources — everything comes from the countless billions of living beings throughout Kunxu."

"As the Crown Prince of Myriad Laws, I possess no personal property whatsoever; I merely retain the right to use them twenty-four hours a day."

Hearing Zhang Yu's words, the many Divine Children within the Heavenly Court wore looks of utter shock, and the numerous Immortals outside the Heavenly Court were equally taken aback.

A Divine Child cried out in disbelief, "What? No personal property? No private accounts either? Is this still Immortal Cultivation? Isn't he afraid that the Immortal Dao resources formerly under his name will become sect-owned assets and get swallowed up by others?"

Another Divine Child remarked grimly, "Of course he isn't afraid. He is now the Crown Prince of Myriad Laws and holds supreme authority on his own turf. If we tried doing that, our assets would probably be devoured clean within a few days."

A Divine Child said in shock, "Without a personal account, he virtually has no moral weakness! Zhang Yu used supreme power to forge an indestructible golden body for himself!"

"Dammit, he transformed the wealth competition in this Morality Field into a competition of power against us, hoping to surpass us!"

"Withdraw our money first."

"It's already gone! Those bastards from the Myriad Laws Sect carved up and cleaned out the money the moment it entered, not leaving a single copper behind."

At the same time, under the watchful eyes of the Ten Greats Good People, Zhang Yu's Morality Points were not only skyrocketing, but the rate of their surge was constantly increasing.

Even the Moral High Ground beneath Zhang Yu's feet was rapidly ascending at this moment, drawing closer and closer to them.

A Divine Child analyzed, "Dammit, waiving the Spirit Realm data fees is an act of universal benevolence. Zhang Yu has effectively hijacked the trillions of sentient beings across Kunxu into his moral assets."

"From now on, as long as a single person uses his free data, his Morality Points will continuously grow without stopping for a moment! This is truly... moral exploitation of the entire world."

Their exchange concluded in an instant, and the Ten Great Good People, holding hands, immediately decided to alter their strategy.

"You don't want to compete in wealth, but in power?"

"Then we won't compete in power — we'll compete in martial might!"

"Before his Morality Points surpass ours, attack together!"

**Boom!**

A surging Virtue Pressure rose into the air, carrying the earth-shattering might of a collapsing sky as it swept over Zhang Yu layer upon layer.

Under the shroud of the Morality Field, the higher a being's Morality Points were, the more of the Divine Emperor's power they could receive — a terrifying strength that vastly exceeded that of ordinary cultivators.

And at this moment, the Ten Great Good People took advantage of the fact that Zhang Yu's Morality Points had not yet surpassed theirs in such a short window, attempting to forcibly suppress their opponent with brute force.

"Zhang Yu, we originally intended to cultivate morality with you, perform good deeds, and bring blessings to the world together. But since you refuse to follow us, you might as well leave the stage now."

With a resounding crack, Zhang Yu — who had just been soaring upward at breakneck speed — was brutally pinned down the very next moment beneath the enveloping Virtue Pressure of the Ten Great Good People.

"Zhang Yu, savor our first-mover moral advantage."

"This is prior virtue suppressing later virtue. This realm of supreme virtue has already been sealed by us. Without our permission, there can only ever be, at most, the Ten Greats Good People in this world."

---

Outside the Heavenly Court, Immortal Roujia and Immortal Yi Xuan of the Reformist Faction had been closely observing the situation within.

Seeing that Zhang Yu had actually canceled all his personal accounts, Immortal Yi Xuan marveled, "This man's ambition and desire are truly immense. No private property? This is clearly treating the entire sect's assets as his own."

Immortal Roujia analyzed, "It is obvious that he wants to permanently maintain supreme power over the Myriad Laws Sect, for only then can he retain the ultimate authority to dispose of all its wealth."

"Do you think Zhang Yu had planned to reform the sect like this all along, or did he resort to this move just to win in the Morality Field?"

Immortal Yi Xuan shook his head and said, "Whichever it is, he has underestimated what it takes to decide this contest."

"Bestowing grace upon the world? Reforming the sect? So what?"

"If he believes this can sway the Immortals' judgment and determine who claims the Heavenly Court, then one can only say... he has been blinded by greed and lost all reason to ambition."

"Alas..."

With a soft sigh, Immortal Venerable Heavenly Fortune said impassively, "The people of this world stop at nothing, sinking to any depth simply for personal gain, power, and cultivation... all to thwart me from saving Kunxu. Is this the Immortal Dao's curse upon the entire world?"

Beside him, the Great God of Heavenly Covenant and Equal Contract bowed and said, "Immortal Venerable, the common people are ignorant. How could they ever understand your painstaking intentions?"

As he spoke, the Great God of Heavenly Covenant and Equal Contract turned his gaze toward the Heavenly Court, continuing to monitor the battle.

Yet no matter how the tides of battle shifted, both Immortal Venerable Heavenly Fortune and the Great God of Heavenly Covenant and Equal Contract remained utterly serene.

To the Great God of Heavenly Covenant and Equal Contract, this was only natural.

He thought to himself, *"The Immortals will never allow the majestic Heavenly Court to fall into the hands of a mere mortal like Zhang Yu, someone who hasn't even achieved immortality."*

*"Zhang Yu, have you truly failed to realize this? No, surely you are merely fighting for self-preservation, desperately gambling everything in a final struggle, aren't you?"*

The Great God of Heavenly Covenant and Equal Contract analyzed in his heart: *"Yet how lamentable. That the Morality Field can continue to exist, that the Divine Children can keep playing this Morality Game in the Heavenly Court — all of this is only because... the Immortals allow it."*

*"Because the Immortals allow all of this to happen, it can proceed, and the Divine Children can act as representatives of various factions to vie for Heavenly Court's authority."*

*"And the reason the Immortals allow it is that this proxy competition reduces conflicts among them, minimizes internal friction, cuts costs, and enables them to seize control of the Heavenly Court more smoothly."*

In the eyes of the Great God of Heavenly Covenant and Equal Contract, with the Immortal Class of the Ten Great Sects joining hands to divide the Heavenly Court, there was simply no one in Kunxu who could stop them at this moment.

The moment the outcome within the Morality Field failed to align with the Immortals' interests, they could immediately intervene by force, overriding everything inside the Morality Field through raw might.

When that time came, even with the blessing of the so-called Divine Emperor's power, not a single person inside the Morality Field could possibly withstand the combined violence of the Immortals from the Ten Great Sects.

Observing Zhang Yu being utterly suppressed by the Virtue Pressure of the Ten Greats Good People, the Great God of Heavenly Covenant and Equal Contract thought to himself, *"Zhang Yu, this is a game that must be played until the Immortals win."*

*"Even if you aren't suppressed by the Ten Greats Good People, trying to claim a share of Heavenly Court's authority... you will ultimately only be suppressed by the Immortals."*

---

Meanwhile, inside the Heavenly Court.

**Boom!**

Blanketed by the ceaseless Virtue Pressure, Zhang Yu's physical body was ruthlessly suppressed, plummeting toward the ground of the Heavenly Court like a meteor.

*Crash!*

Yet even after he slammed into the ground, the Ten Greats Good People still did not withdraw their Virtue Pressure. It continued to crush Zhang Yu's form like a colossal hand... forcefully sweeping him away.

And so, under the watchful eyes of the Ten Greats Good People, Zhang Yu's physical body was instantly swept out of the Heavenly Court, vanishing beyond the Morality Field.

Above Zhang Yu's head, no Morality Points appeared anymore.

Watching this scene, the Ten Greats Good People each wore a faint, satisfied smile.

"Get lost. It's not your turn to do good deeds here."

Although expelling Great Philanthropist Zhang Yu caused a sharp drop in their Morality Points, their scores quickly skyrocketed once again as they continuously traded deeds among themselves.

Inside the Heavenly Court, as if nothing had ever happened, the Morality Game continued.

Meanwhile, Zhang Yu paid no attention to any of this, for just a moment ago... his mind had sensed Zhang Pianpian within the Supreme Clarity Realm.

Inside the Supreme Clarity Realm at this moment, a sliver of Zhang Yu's consciousness descended. Looking at Zhang Pianpian before him, he said in surprise, "Sis, you're back in the Supreme Clarity Realm?"

Saying that, Zhang Yu turned his gaze toward the figure beside Zhang Pianpian in bewilderment. This was the first time he had ever seen anyone other than himself and Zhang Pianpian within the Supreme Clarity Realm.

Looking at the figure, Zhang Yu said, "Sis, just now I sensed your thoughts in the Supreme Clarity Realm, telling me not to resist the Ten Greats Good People for now. Does that have something to do with him?"

It was precisely because of Zhang Pianpian's message that Zhang Yu chose to trust his elder sister and took the opportunity to withdraw from the Morality Field.

The figure smiled faintly. "Zhang Yu, if you had continued to resist, the Immortals of the ten sects outside the field would have intervened. I don't know what other countermeasures you had prepared, but rather than competing head-on with these Immortal Livestock of the new era, it is better to take another approach to vie for Heavenly Court's authority."

Zhang Pianpian sent a private message to Zhang Yu: "Little brother, this person might be an ancient deity, or even a Divine Emperor. Having awakened, he doesn't know much about present-day Kunxu. After I ran into him..."

Zhang Pianpian quickly informed Zhang Yu of her experiences and everything she knew.

After reading it in an instant, Zhang Yu looked at the figure with a somewhat more polite expression: "Senior, thank you for saving my elder sister."

The figure said indifferently, "A trivial matter, not worth mentioning. Time is pressing now, so drop the pleasantries. Let's discuss the situation directly."

Zhang Yu nodded and said, "Senior, avoiding a direct confrontation with the Immortals would naturally be the ideal scenario."

"However, I have already been swept out of the Heavenly Court and lost the empowerment of the Morality Field. How can I still seize the Heavenly Court's authority?"

"If I barge back in now, wouldn't it just be the same situation as before?"

The figure chuckled and said, "Who said you aren't in the Heavenly Court?"

"The area you were in just now was merely the Outer Court of the Heavenly Court."

"And this place is the true Inner Court of the Heavenly Court."

"Not to mention, you are still a Paragon of Virtue."
