# Chapter 1021: Please Address Me as Crown Prince, Teacher

After stepping into the Ascension Realm, Zhang Yu sensed subtle, marvelous changes occurring within his physical body.

"After Tribulation Transcendence, my Primordial Spirit and physical body have already merged into one. My entire being now possesses both the macroscopic traits of a physical body and the microscopic traits of a Primordial Spirit."

"And upon stepping into the Ascension Realm, my physical body has entered what is known as the Immortal Embryo state."

Having entered the Immortal Embryo state, Zhang Yu felt his dao heart, physical body, and magic power break through their limits once again.

"In the Ascension Realm, the upper limit for the dao heart is level 80, the magic power cap is one billion, and the physical strength limit is 80.00 points."

"Of course, with the Emperor Creation Seed within me, my limits will be even higher — enough to reach the stats of an Immortal."

Furthermore, Zhang Yu felt the bottlenecks of all his cultivation arts loosening. He could raise their levels once more, comprehending and executing even higher tiers of Immortal Dao Technology.

Naturally, before his breakthrough, Zhang Yu had already studied the relevant data on the Ascension Realm.

He knew that this so-called Immortal Embryo state was an intermediate state — or a transitional phase — between human and Immortal.

"The so-called Ascension Realm is simply the realm where one makes their final preparations to ascend and become an Immortal."

"In the state of the Immortal Embryo, one gradually completes the transformation from mortal to Immortal, accumulating everything required, and finally takes that decisive step... to achieve immortality and establish the Dao."

Yet Zhang Yu also understood that being able to break through at will and achieve immortality freely belonged to an age long forgotten.

"As far back as the era of Immortal Emperor Heavenly Virtue, the Heavenly Tribulation Dao Lineage was already established. It was no longer just the Tribulation Transcendence Realm that required surviving lightning tribulations; one had to overcome the Heavenly Tribulation at the peak of the Ascension Realm to achieve immortality."

"And nowadays, there are even the shackles of the Ascension Dao Lineage."

As a cultivator who had climbed up step by step from the very bottom of Kunxu, Zhang Yu possessed rich grassroots experience and had personally experienced the strict control exerted by the Ten Great Sects over cultivation arts, realms, and law establishment.

When it came to something as momentous as achieving immortality and establishing the Dao, he didn't even need to think to know that the Ten Great Sects would only manage it with far greater severity, leaving no blind spots whatsoever.

"Based on the information I've gathered so far, the Ascension Dao Lineage is a lineage jointly built by the Ten Great Sects to co-manage the matter of achieving immortality. It is responsible for governing the ascension process among the Ten Great Sects, becoming the objective law governing ascension within Kunxu."

"And this entire process can be called the Ascension Selection."

Zhang Yu thought to himself, "Simply put, the first step is internal competition within each of the Great Sects, selecting a single ascension representative from among the sect's favored ascension candidates."

"The second step is the competition among the ten ascension representatives from the Ten Great Sects. They showcase the new Dao Lineage they intend to establish, utilizing publicity, splashing money, smearing each other, and all manner of Kunxu-style sparring to win over support ratings across different regions within the Ten Great Sects."

"The reason for doing this is reportedly because the Ascension Dao Lineage can directly utilize the cultivators' support ratings to empower Law Decrees, satisfying the requirements to elevate a Law Decree into a Dao Lineage."

"Ultimately, the top three with the highest support ratings can receive the blessing of the Ascension Dao Lineage, taking that final step to achieve immortality and establish their Dao, thereby becoming Immortals."

Just as these records flashed through Zhang Yu's mind, Immortal Slayer couldn't help but sigh, "Back when the Ascension Dao Lineage was established and this entire Ascension Selection process was laid down, the hope was that every newly ascended Immortal would establish a Dao Lineage beneficial to the whole of Kunxu."

Fu Ji chuckled and said, "The original intention was good, but the execution went horribly wrong, didn't it?"

Flipping through the materials on the Spirit Realm, Fu Ji continued, "Today's Ascension Selection has devolved into a game where whoever has the most money mounts the fiercest publicity campaign, deploys the most formidable Spirit Realm troll army, and commands the largest lobbying groups, promoting themselves across the Great Sects and frantically harvesting approval ratings."

Fu Ji sighed, "This Ascension Selection is practically a money-burning contest. It's a clash of wealth, connections, and resources, all about painting grandiose illusions for the whole of Kunxu."

"After going through such an ordeal, even upon achieving immortality, one's vitality is severely depleted, leaving them even more desperate to frantically rake in money and resources."

Immortal Slayer let out a sigh and said, "If it were only that, it might be bearable. The crucial issue is that under Kunxu's current ecosystem, the cultivators of the Great Sects are forced to be shortsighted, eager only to back whichever Dao Lineage offers them the greatest immediate benefits."

"Yet the moment an ascension candidate achieves immortality and their Dao Lineage is established, they have no further need for the approval of the lower-tier cultivators. Before long, their Dao Lineage operates purely for their own self-interest and the interests of the Immortal Class."

While Fu Ji and Immortal Slayer were conversing, Zhang Yu was pondering the current situation.

"Now that the ten Immortal Emperors have departed, how will the Ascension Selection proceed?"

"Will it still take place eighteen years from now? That would be far too late — the ten Immortal Emperors won't obediently wait around for us."

Thinking this, Zhang Yu could not help but feel a sudden void in his heart.

He had originally thought he would have at least over eighteen years to prepare and make a bid for the next Ascension Selection.

Yet the sudden uprising of the ten Immortal Emperors had torn all his plans completely to shreds. Zhang Yu was helpless against this; after all, the Immortal Emperors could not possibly act according to his expectations.

Still, he had long since grown accustomed to such situations. Ever since he had stepped onto the Immortal Path, this was hardly the first time something like this had happened.

Whether it was the upheaval Wang Yin had brought to the 1st layer, or the financial crisis during his university years, the Evil Cults sieging the university city, the University War, the emergence of the Great Sage's Intent within the sect, the Heaven Collapse at the Former Days Graveyard, the rollback triggered by Immortal Emperor Supreme Truth... Changes in the world were always so abrupt and caught people off guard, never leaving anyone time to prepare beforehand.

Zhang Yu thought to himself, "This is fucking Kunxu for you. The upper-tier cultivators are always altering the environment, rendering every preparation made by lower-tier cultivators completely useless."

Faced with such an abrupt shift in the situation, Zhang Yu had no choice but to go all out to adapt to the changes.

Gathering intelligence on Ascension Dao Lineages and the Ascension Selection at this moment gave him an intuition.

"The Ascension Selection is very likely to be held ahead of schedule. I must advance as fast as possible and seize this opportunity to achieve immortality and establish my Dao."

Even as Zhang Yu pondered this, streaks of his clones had already begun cultivating various cultivation arts, tempering his dao heart, magic power, and physical strength.

Meanwhile, even more of his clone consciousnesses began reaching out to person after person in his contacts, making preparations for his various plans.

At the same time, on the 17th layer of the Myriad Laws Sect.

Tai Yuebai’s consciousness was linked to both the Spirit Realm and the Dharma Realm. Watching the flood of ever-changing news, he was struck utterly dumbfounded.

The vanishing without a trace of the ten Immortal Emperors alongside Ying Xintian was something he could somewhat understand; to him, it simply seemed like the Immortal Emperors had changed the venue of their battle.

Yet Zhang Yu...

"Crown Prince of Myriad Laws? How could Zhang Yu become the Crown Prince of Myriad Laws? And he's even acting on behalf of the sect? Granted full discretionary authority?"

"How could the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor grant Zhang Yu such immense power?"

"Could it be that they want to split Kunxu? Divide one sect into two? Rule by dividing the layers?"

Tai Yuebai's mind raced. "Is this a good thing or a bad thing? Still, compared to the Immortal Class up there, Zhang Yu is indeed somewhat more trustworthy."

"But what if Zhang Yu has been brought under control by the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor? Is it possible that the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor has already subdued Zhang Yu and is using him to remotely control the Myriad Laws Sect?"

Just then, a flurry of notifications appeared before Tai Yuebai's eyes.

"Huh? My post has been dismissed?"

Just as Tai Yuebai felt a surge of alarm, a new order arrived: "I have been appointed as the Crown Prince's scribe? Is this Zhang Yu's appointment? Does he want me to be his secretary?"

With that in mind, Tai Yuebai immediately contacted Zhang Yu to inquire about the specific situation.

Zhang Yu thought to himself, *"Big Brother Tai, with so many Dao Seeds on you, I can't just pretend I don't see them."*

The reason he arranged for Tai Yuebai to be his secretary was so that he could observe him up close and continuously duplicate Dao Seeds from him.

However, with the Ten Great Sects currently beset by crises from both within and without, the Spirit Realm drifting in turbulence, and countless Righteous Gods patrolling the Myriad Laws Sect, Zhang Yu had no intention of revealing his abilities. He simply said, "Big Brother Tai, Senior Ying Xintian left alongside the ten Immortal Emperors to wage the final battle.

"Now that the End is approaching, the Immortal Class of the Great Sects are still squabbling over power and profit. How can we possibly save Kunxu alongside vermin like that?"

"After thinking it over, the person I can trust most in the Myriad Laws Sect, the one who can best help me save Kunxu, is only you, Big Brother Tai."

Hearing Zhang Yu's words, Tai Yuebai felt a tremor in his heart, a deep sense of being trusted welling up within him.

*"Indeed. In the current Myriad Laws Sect, perhaps only the two of us truly trust one another, truly inheriting the will of Senior Ying Xintian."*

At the same time, another thought surfaced in Tai Yuebai's mind: *"Then I shall be his secretary and help him save Kunxu. Meanwhile, I can keep an eye on him. If he truly falls under the control of the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor, or if his resolve wavers, I will take Senior Ying Xintian's place to stop him."*

Upon seeing the news that Zhang Yu had become the Crown Prince of Myriad Laws, Yu Xinghan felt a sudden surge of magic power coursing through his entire body.

"Your Highness the Crown Prince, it is I, your most loyal old servant, Yu Xinghan."

The news regarding the final battle of the ten Immortal Emperors and the impending Ultimate Doomsday was currently circulating only among the upper echelons of the Great Sects, and had not yet been announced to all cultivators.

Yu Xinghan was naturally entirely oblivious to this. In his view, the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor had simply decreed that Zhang Yu be directly appointed the Crown Prince of Myriad Laws to oversee the sect on his behalf.

"Calling this reaching the heavens in a single bound doesn't even begin to describe it."

"Even a cultivator who climbed up from the 1st layer can become the Crown Prince of Myriad Laws. Our Myriad Laws Sect's selection system and cultivation realm mobility are truly the best among the Ten Sects!"

"And Grandpa Immortal Emperor truly has an eye for talent!"

Yu Xinghan was extraordinarily thrilled at this moment. He considered himself a veteran loyalist of the Crown Prince faction, and now that Zhang Yu had attained the position of Crown Prince of Myriad Laws, their faction members were bound to rise alongside him like chickens and dogs ascending to heaven.

He was eagerly anticipating news of promotions and appointments, yet what reached him first were messages from various quarters.

Divine Monarch Starfire: "Xinghan, what on earth happened with Zhang Yu?"

Yu Xinghan: "Teacher, please address him as Crown Prince!"

Right then, several notifications flashed across Yu Xinghan's vision in rapid succession, sending one tremor of shock after another through his mind.

"Specially promoted to Rank 6."

"Granted a Temporary Tribulation Certificate."

"Appointed as Minister of the Law Edict Review Department."

Yu Xinghan's heart leaped. *"Isn't this His Highness the Crown Prince's former position? Does he want me to review law edicts on his behalf?"*

Even as he pondered this, Yu Xinghan hastily sent Zhang Yu a message of gratitude.

Zhang Yu replied, "Xinghan, I envision you replacing Su Xianchen in the future and serving as the President of the Law Edict Management Committee."

"When the time comes, you shall be the Myriad Laws Divine Sword in my grasp, purging law edicts for me and reigning over all laws."

As the creator of the Law Edict Expiration Date, now that Zhang Yu had become the Crown Prince of Myriad Laws, he naturally had to seize total control of the Law Edict Management Committee.

Zhang Yu mused silently, "The access I once left open for the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor is now mine to use.

"Whenever there's a Law Edict I want sealed, I'll just have Yu Xinghan review it and submit the application, and then I'll approve and execute it myself."

Hearing this, Yu Xinghan felt a surge of invigorated energy throughout his body, a feeling even more exhilarating than when he had broken through to the Void Refinement Realm.

Yu Xinghan replied, "Rest assured, Your Highness. Even if it costs me everything I have, I will surely carry out the mission you have entrusted to me."

In her view, the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor lived an eternal life — why would he ever need a crown prince to exist?

Beside her, Old Gao chuckled and said, "Maybe during the Book-Wielder Assembly, the Immortal Emperor took a liking to Zhang Yu's talent and confidence and wanted to groom him properly."

Magnetic Pole dismissed the idea, but she understood that since it was the Immortal Emperor's arrangement, everyone had no choice but to go with the flow.

"Regardless of the reason, let's reap the benefits first."

Thinking this, Magnetic Pole nudged Old Gao beside her and said, "Hurry up and send a congratulatory message to Zhang Yu. Then find an opportunity to ask him for a good position."

Old Gao said, "I'm doing just fine with the construction work here in the Grotto-Heaven. What other position would I need?"

Magnetic Pole said, "No matter how large an Immortal Grotto-Heaven is, it's still just one Grotto-Heaven. Now that Zhang Yu has become the Crown Prince of Myriad Laws, it's the perfect time for him to spread his wings. Don't you want to help him out more?"

"Never mind, if you won't say it, I'll talk to him. At a time like this, he must be thinking about how to promote us as well."

Right at that moment, a series of notifications materialized before Old Gao.

"I'm to serve as the Vice Pavilion Master of the Grotto-Heaven Pavilion, overseeing the spatial structure issues of each Grotto-Heaven?"

Reading this far, Old Gao's spirits lifted, and he thought to himself, *"Is Zhang Yu planning to have me handle the public graft areas across the various Grotto-Heavens?"*

Right at that moment, Zhang Yu sent over a message: "Teacher, when it comes to conducting the Grotto-Heaven Land Survey, re-registering the Grotto-Heaven Tax, and reclaiming the public graft Grotto-Heavens in the Myriad Laws Sect, I'll have to trouble you from now on."

On the other side, Magnetic Pole received a notification as well.

Magnetic Pole thought, *"Pavilion Master of the Treasure Archive Pavilion, responsible for managing the sect's magic artifacts?"*

The Myriad Laws Sect possessed a massive number of sect magic artifacts, which formed a vital component of the sect's assets.

Zhang Yu knew that he would inevitably mobilize, purchase, and even sell these sect magic artifacts in large quantities moving forward. Naturally, for a position with so much room for lucrative perks, he needed to place someone he trusted.

Zhang Yu said, "Teacher, I wouldn't feel at ease with anyone else in this position, so I'll have to trouble you to take it on."

Magnetic Pole replied, "Don't worry. I'll make sure every single magic artifact in the entire sect is managed with complete clarity."

Zhang Yu thought to himself, *"Once Teacher Magnetic Pole studies the data on all kinds of sect magic artifacts, it will also properly enrich the database of the Encyclopedia of Celestial Technology and enhance the artifact refinement effects of this Dao Seed."*

Zhang Yu planned to wait until Magnetic Pole had made further progress here, absorbed the essence of the Myriad Laws Sect's various magic artifacts, and improved her cultivation before placing her in a more important position to oversee the forging of all kinds of magic artifacts for him.

Meanwhile, inside the Immortal Grotto-Heaven, Tu Lishan looked at the appointment notice before him and thought to himself, *"President of the Feathered Ascension Grotto-Heaven Management Committee? The entire committee reports directly to the Crown Prince."*

The so-called Feathered Ascension Grotto-Heaven was the new name for Zhang Yu's Immortal Grotto-Heaven.

Reading through the responsibilities of this Feathered Ascension Grotto-Heaven Management Committee, Tu Lishan could not help but feel a surge of excitement. *"This is intended to forge the Feathered Ascension Grotto-Heaven into the center of the Myriad Laws Sect, build it into the foremost city of Kunxu, and launch a Ten Thousand Year Construction plan."*

To Zhang Yu, Dugu Ming's conduct had always shown too much loyalty to the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor.

Heaven only knew whether the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor had placed any restrictions or methods upon him.

One could say that the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor's ongoing schemes and his display of profound foresight and cunning calculation filled Zhang Yu with extreme wariness.

Particularly after ascending to the position of Crown Prince of Myriad Laws and facing the pressure of the ten Immortal Emperors and the Immortal Class, Zhang Yu felt that the Myriad Laws Sect was full of loopholes and problems everywhere.

Zhang Yu thought to himself, "If only I could monitor every single space and person throughout the sect."

Then I would immediately know who possesses which Dao Seeds, who still holds a Book, who can be trusted, who is loyal to the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor, and who is loyal to me, the crown prince..."
