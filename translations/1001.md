# Chapter 1001 The Deceiver

Looking at the message that surfaced on the Feather Book, Zhang Yu was shaken, his thoughts flashing like lightning: "Is this another message from myself?"

"I am the one who controls the Feather Book. The ability to leave behind this kind of future message — one that only surfaces after being merged into the Feather Book — means there is an extremely high probability that it was left by me in the future."

"Do not believe? Is it telling me not to believe... future messages like the one on the Technique-Chaining Comprehensive Manual? Not to believe future messages that surface on other books?"

"And instead, I should believe... the future messages that only manifest on the Feather Book after being merged into it?"

Relying on his extraordinary mental capabilities at the Tribulation Transcendence Realm, Zhang Yu had already finished thinking it through almost the instant he scanned the sentence.

"There are two possibilities as to why I would leave this kind of message."

"The first possibility: the future me... believes that the future messages appearing on the Feather Book can only be seen by me and are the safest method to transmit information from the future. Meanwhile, future messages transmitted on other books might be discovered by others... or even the Immortal Emperor."

"Thus, using the Feather Book to transmit true intelligence and using other books to transmit false intelligence, thereby achieving the effect of deceiving others, or even deceiving the Immortal Emperor."

"The second possibility is that the future me believed that information from the future, whether from the Feather Book or other channels, might be spied upon, and thus used this message as a smokescreen..."

Even as these thoughts flashed through his mind in an instant, Zhang Yu had already continued reading, swiftly scanning the subsequent information that continuously emerged on the Feather Book.

"Originally, it was nearly impossible for us to deceive the Immortal Emperors."

"Because they can investigate anything they wish to know at any time and verify any truth they wish to uncover at any moment. Within the sect... they are virtually omniscient."

"Coupled with the ability of World Rollback, even if they suffer a temporary failure, they can start over anew, seeing through all lies and schemes."

"This allows the Immortal Emperors to always maintain the highest vantage point, holding an information advantage that surpasses the entirety of Kunxu."

"Yet what makes them can also break them. Precisely because of the rollback, an information blind spot is created — one they temporarily cannot investigate or verify."

A flash of insight crossed Zhang Yu's mind. "Indeed... this blind spot is the future."

"Once the Immortal Emperors roll back the world to this year, they can no longer investigate or verify the circumstances of next year."

"So, the information from next year that they didn't know before the rollback... is also impossible for them to investigate this year, after the rollback."

"And my future self, who left this message, exists within this blind spot of information. The Immortal Emperors of the present... cannot trace the future me, nor can they easily verify the information about the future that the future me provides."

Next, further messages from the future continued to emerge on the Feather Book.

"If the Immortal Emperors intercept only a limited amount of the future information I transmit, we can exploit their arrogance."

"We will let them believe they stand at the absolute peak, discerning everything, and let them treat the messages we transmit as the truest intelligence."

"If the opposite happens, and the Immortal Emperors intercept a vast amount of the future information I transmit, then we exploit their greed."

"For every additional piece of unverifiable future information they acquire, the likelihood of them being deceived and making a wrong judgment will increase."

"As the Immortal Emperors intercept more and more... unverifiable future information, they will be forced to make judgments time and time again."

"The more judgments they make, the higher the chance of error. In the end, the likelihood of the Immortal Emperors being deceived and making the wrong choice... will approach inevitability."

"As such, their rollback loses a considerable part of its purpose. The future, to them... will be shrouded in a fog cast by me."

"The more they wish to control everything and know everything, the more they will be deceived, and the more mistakes they will make."

Reading up to this point, Zhang Yu was already shaken to the core. "So, my future self passed on a vast amount of future intelligence through the Book?"

"Furthermore, this future intelligence is likely a mix of truth and falsehoods, intricately tied to various interests, and situated in blind spots outside the view of the different Immortal Emperors — unverifiable to them at this very moment."

"Will the Ten Immortal Emperors' judgments on the veracity of this intelligence be unanimous? Impossible. Unless all ten of them share every piece of information, their differing perspectives and conflicting interests will inevitably lead to discrepancies in how they assess this intelligence."

Thinking up to this point, Zhang Yu's spirits lifted. "Future intelligence of unknown veracity, varying judgments... and the Ten Immortal Emperors who distrust one another... The existence of these variables makes the future increasingly uncertain."

"This is dragging down the perspective of the Ten Immortal Emperors, putting them on the same level as us... where no one can make a one hundred percent accurate judgment about the future. It drags everyone into chaos — an intentional creation of variables, striving for victory amidst turmoil, seeking life from the jaws of death."

While pondering, Zhang Yu continued reading the rest of the information from the future.

"At the same time, while the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor wants us to believe we aren't completely under surveillance, we must make him believe he is completely monitoring us."

"Only by doing so can we leverage his maximum indulgence during this rare window of opportunity to wildly seize every advantage, achieve the highest growth, and scale the peak of the Immortal Dao at the fastest speed."

"The arrogance brought about by the World Rollback and the greed born from their Immortal Dao Aspiration — only by exploiting these two things can we strike back against the Immortal Emperor."

"Lastly, if you can preserve this information, pass the message to Ying Xintian. He is a trustworthy and dependable companion."

Instantly finishing the intelligence left behind by his future self and watching the disappearing message begin to fade, Zhang Yu swiftly came to a conclusion in his mind.

"Indeed... Ying Xintian is trustworthy."

"However, according to what this message said at the very beginning, since I have already met the Immortal Emperor, I shouldn't preserve the information."

"Is it because trying to preserve it before it completely vanishes and reminding myself to pass it to Ying Xintian would alert the Immortal Emperor? Or is there some other reason?"

One idea after another flashed through Zhang Yu's mind in an instant, but in the end, he chose to trust his future self and decided not to preserve the information before him after having "already met the Immortal Emperor."

"The information I currently possess is limited, and time is even more limited. There are still many parts of the entire plan... that I haven't figured out."

"After all, the present me still doesn't know what other arrangements my future self has left behind besides this letter... Since the present me cannot make an effective judgment, then I'll just believe and trust in my future self."

With that, Zhang Yu made no further moves, merely watching the future information appearing on the Feather Book as it was about to vanish completely in an instant...

At the same time, a realization emerged in Zhang Yu's heart... just before this memory faded away entirely.

"Not saving this information means that, including Ying Xintian and myself, probably... no one in this world will know it."

At this moment, the fighting spirit within Zhang Yu's heart blazed continuously: "In other words, I must deceive everyone, including myself. Is this the strategy my future self devised?"

The next moment, as the future information and memories completely dissipated, Zhang Yu forgot everything he had just pondered and remembered.

Only sensing the slight acceleration of the magic power within his body, he realized that he seemed somewhat excited.

Combined with the knowledge in his mind, this trace of excitement made Zhang Yu think to himself, "With the successive help of Senior Supreme Truth and Senior Ying Xintian, even the Immortal Emperor failed to notice my trump card. He doesn't know that I... that we have already noticed the rollback and can even transmit information from the future."

"Right now, the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor thinks he is in full control of everything, allowing me to grow unchecked."

"I must seize this historic opportunity and continuously leverage the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor's momentum to develop madly, accumulating enough capital to resist the Immortal Emperor and prevent the apocalypse in the future..."

Right at that moment, Zhang Yu suddenly froze slightly, sensing something amiss: "Wait, the time is off. Just now... I lost a brief moment of time."

"Could it be like last time? Did I see future information again?"

A moment later, in the deepest depths of the Immortal Grotto-Heaven, Zhang Yu found that piece of future information with its countdown ticking away.

He immediately made a judgment in his heart: "This didn't happen after I read the Saber Technique Book. Is this information sealed after the Feather Book fused with the Saber Technique Book?"

"Could it be that after this book is fused, future memories emerged from the Feather Book?"

"That's it. This is a method that suits me very well... a way to transmit future messages through books given to me by others."

The cluster of future information before his eyes was the result of Zhang Yu intercepting and sealing the most insignificant, smallest fraction of the future information before he completely lost the relevant memories.

As for the complete future information that had emerged on the Feather Book this time — whether it was about the layout of the future or the plans to rebel against the Immortal Emperors — it had all vanished at this moment along with the dissipation of Zhang Yu's memories.

At this moment, Zhang Yu had completed the deception of himself.

Right now, carrying that surging fighting spirit in his heart, he began yet another ascent on the path of the Immortal Dao under the Immortal Emperor's indulgence.

---

In the outermost layer of the Immortal Grotto-Heaven, construction sites everywhere were in full swing.

Tu Lishan struck out with a palm, and surging Earthly Fiend Gravity gathered, causing countless pre-fabricated components to interlock like building blocks, completing the infrastructure across vast stretches of land.

On the other side, Mo Shangjin pressed his palms together, and building materials swept in, blotting out the sky. In the blink of an eye, layer after layer of ground was completely paved.

Watching the Civil Engineering Faction cultivators working with all their might, a flicker of worry gradually surfaced in Gao Chongguang's eyes.

*"The funds... are almost gone."*

Although Zhang Yu was quite wealthy now, he had just recently taken out a loan of one billion to purchase a sect-guarding technique, which meant the capital he could continuously invest in the real estate development of the Immortal Grotto-Heaven remained limited.

Gao Chongguang thought to himself, *"To build a new transportation hub, substantial capital is indispensable. If Zhang Yu's funds remain tight... the entire project's progress will likely have to slow down."*

Even though Gao Chongguang had tried everything to cut costs, having various suppliers and cultivators front money whenever possible, he knew this was not a sustainable solution.

*"If someone figures out... that our cash flow is failing, leading to a stampede of panic and turning this into an abandoned, unfinished project... then we'll be in serious trouble."*

So, Gao Chongguang contacted Zhang Yu and explained the current problem.

However, Zhang Yu replied, "Teacher Gao, don't worry. The policies from above are about to change, and a massive influx of capital will be arriving soon."

Hearing this, Gao Chongguang wanted to test the waters, so he followed up, "What kind of policy is it roughly?"

Zhang Yu replied, "As for the specific details... I don't know either. The Immortal Ascension Hall probably hasn't even reached a conclusion yet."

"Even the Immortal Ascension Hall hasn't reached a conclusion?" Gao Chongguang asked in confusion. "Then how do you know?"

Zhang Yu answered, "Because the Immortal Emperor told me himself."

The policy Zhang Yu was referring to at this moment was the one concerning the Technique-Chaining Comprehensive Manual and the creation of a true Chain Formation Diagram.

During his audience with the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor, the other party had confirmed... that this responsibility would be entrusted to him.

Besides that, news of his audience with the Immortal Emperor would likely spread before long anyway.

Therefore, Zhang Yu had no intention of deliberately keeping it a secret.

Moreover, he was well aware in his heart that once this news began to spread one after another, this transportation hub he was currently developing would inevitably attract the attention of countless people.

When that time came, whether by accepting investments or selling the usage rights to several plots of outer land in the Immortal Grotto-Heaven, he would be able to secure a massive influx of funds... to continue pushing forward with construction.

On the other side, upon hearing Zhang Yu's reply, Gao Chongguang fell completely silent in an instant, his eyes filled with shock and doubt.

*"The Immortal Emperor? Told Zhang Yu?"*

*"Zhang Yu can already speak directly with the Immortal Emperor? And he can even learn about the sect's next policies straight from the Immortal Emperor?"*

If anyone else had said such a thing, Gao Chongguang would have definitely suspected they were trying to scam him, or that they had been scammed themselves.

However, he knew Zhang Yu would never lie to him about something like this, nor was he likely to be deceived by such matters.

At this moment, Gao Chongguang's perception of Zhang Yu was thoroughly reshaped once again.

*"Could Zhang Yu be the Immortal Emperor's son?"*

*"No, no, that's not right. Why would a descendant of the Immortal Emperor end up stranded in the First Layer? And why would he even need a university degree?"*

*"So... is it really just that the Immortal Emperor appreciates Zhang Yu? That he's investing in him?"*

Recalling Zhang Yu's journey — climbing all the way from university to the sect, and then ascending rank by rank within it — Gao Chongguang's mind flashed back to how he himself had promoted Zhang Yu back then, followed by Magnetic Pole, Bu Yingshu, Fa Liuyuan, the Reformist Faction...

And then there were the Expiration Date Law Edict, the Myriad Laws Divine Sword...

As Gao Chongguang reminisced, he felt more and more that his guess was close to the truth.

Old Gao thought with deep emotion, *"I always knew this kid was great at networking upward, but I never imagined he was this capable... Not only did he make a connection all the way up to the Immortal Emperor, but he can even talk to him directly?*

*"Then does that mean our Civil Engineering Gang... is technically part of the Immortal Emperor Faction now?"*

Doing real estate business with the Immortal Emperor as backing and support?

Thinking of this, Gao Chongguang felt a surge of excitement in his chest.

***Boom!***

A tremendous roar echoed!

Hearing the noise, Tu Lishan looked toward the direction of the blast and thought in amazement, "Master truly is an old-school cultivator. His bond with Minister Zhang is so deep that he's working this hard without even taking pay."

After ending his call with Gao Chongguang, Zhang Yu was cultivating while simultaneously sensing the messages arriving from all sides.

He thought to himself, "As for what Chain Formation Diagram the *Technique-Chaining Comprehensive Manual* will ultimately create, that still requires careful consideration. I should wait for the Immortal Emperor's specific appointments and policies to come down."

"As for collecting books, Liu Qianxian, Tai Yuebai, Yu Xinghan, and Teacher Magnetic Pole are busy handling it for now, and Bu Yingshu can leverage the Immortal Clan's connections. Unless there are matters requiring my personal presence, I should seize the time to elevate my cultivation in the Tribulation Transcendence Realm."

Zhang Yu's primary goal in cultivation at present was to max out all of his foundational stats in the Tribulation Transcendence Realm.

"As for the books in the sect that are harder to collect, they can wait a bit until my momentum builds..."

Zhang Yu understood in his heart that as long as he waited a bit longer — as the news of his audience with the Immortal Emperor gradually spread and the policies regarding the *Technique-Chaining Comprehensive Manual* were implemented — he would steadily build immense momentum. Combined with his improving cultivation, collecting the books when the time came would achieve twice the result with half the effort, making the task far easier.

---

A few days later, just as Zhang Yu was devoting himself wholeheartedly to his cultivation, a message sent by Yu Xinghan shattered his peace.

Yu Xinghan: "Minister Zhang! A riot has broken out on the Fifth Layer! Students are storming universities and corporations, launching an outright rebellion."

Yu Xinghan: "It's said to be over the collection of the books, which has led to the arrest of a massive number of cultivators."

Puzzled, Zhang Yu asked, "Collecting the books... Why would that lead to the arrest of so many cultivators? How could it even drive them to rebel?"

In Zhang Yu's view, people who possessed the books were exceedingly rare; it should have had nothing to do with the broader mass of cultivators. 

As for driving the lower realm cultivators to revolt, that seemed even more unbelievable to him.

Zhang Yu thought to himself, *"Under the strict control of the Righteous Gods, how could those junior college and university students in the lower realm ever dare to rebel?"*

Yu Xinghan said, "From what I currently understand, it seems the lower layers kept escalating the measures at every step, arresting more and more suspects. In the end, they suppressed the cultivators of an entire school, detained them past the legal limits, and drove many of them into bankruptcy, which ultimately triggered the riot."

Zhang Yu furrowed his brows upon hearing this. Fu Ji gave a cold snort and said, "They're ramping up enforcement at every layer, aren't they? Is someone trying to cause trouble for us? Using our name to sabotage the business environment?"

Yu Xinghan said, "Minister, the Righteous Gods already plan to wipe out the entire school on the spot. I'm afraid that if they go through with it, people will attack you for it, and it will create even more resistance to the book collection campaign below..."

Zhang Yu replied, "I understand. Hold them off; I'm coming over right now."

Yu Xinghan was slightly startled. He had originally wanted Zhang Yu to find a way from above and use his connections to stop the operation, never expecting that Zhang Yu would make the trip in person.

Yu Xinghan hurriedly said, "Minister, you haven't applied for a Lower Realm Transit Pass yet, right? I'm afraid there won't be enough time."

Yu Xinghan knew that any sect cultivator who wanted to head down to the lower realm had to apply in advance and go through standard procedures, requiring approval before proceeding—it was not something that could be done immediately.

Zhang Yu replied, "Don't worry, I asked an acquaintance. It should be processed right away."

Bu Yingshu's figure flashed in Yu Xinghan's mind, and he thought to himself, *"Even the Immortal Clan can't just casually let a sect cultivator head down to the lower realm, can they?"*

Yu Xinghan could not help asking, "Minister, who did you ask?"

Zhang Yu: "The Immortal Emperor."
