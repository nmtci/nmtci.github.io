# Chapter 1023: Immortals, Please Address Me as Crown Prince

Facing the suggestions brought by the two Immortals, Zhang Yu shook his head and said bluntly, "To cope with the End, the sect must undergo drastic reforms, and the current Myriad Laws System is no longer capable of meeting this need."

"Therefore, I have already planned adjustments to the personnel of the Immortal Ascension Hall, which will be the first step in transforming the Myriad Laws Sect moving forward."

"If you two Immortals still remember the purpose of the Reformist Faction, and still remember Senior Ying Xintian, then please lend me a helping hand."

Neither Immortal Yi Xuan nor Immortal Roujia had expected that Zhang Yu would turn around and use the tenets of the Reformist Faction to checkmate them.

Especially since Zhang Yu had even mentioned Ying Xintian.

Zhang Yu was hoping to leverage Ying Xintian's Immortal Network and personal ties to persuade the two Immortals.

What Zhang Yu did not know, however, was that Ying Xintian had stripped Immortal Yi Xuan and Immortal Roujia of their authority over the Kunlun Dao Lineage. Hearing Zhang Yu mention Ying Xintian at this moment provoked anger rather than goodwill in both of them.

Immortal Roujia frowned and said, "Zhang Yu! Back when the Ten Sects went on strike and we stood face-to-face against the Immortal Emperor, you weren't even born yet!"

"Later, when the Ten Sects bloodily purged the Reformist Faction, it was also us who shouldered the burden."

"When it comes to advancing sect reforms, it is not your place to lecture us."

Immortal Yi Xuan nodded and said, "Ying Xintian has already failed. His unauthorized actions this time have further exacerbated the conflict with the Immortal Emperors, bringing about the Battle of the End ahead of schedule."

He glared at Zhang Yu and demanded harshly, "Right now, all of Kunxu is teetering on the brink of collapse. Must you stir up internal strife within the sect and play right into the hands of the ten Immortal Emperors?"

Zhang Yu refused to yield an inch. "I do not trust the Immortals above. I cannot leave the trillions of living beings from the 1st to the 19th layers of the Myriad Laws Sect in their hands to govern, nor can I leave the precious resources for fighting the End for them to squander."

Hearing Zhang Yu's outright attack on the Immortal Class, Immortal Roujia and Immortal Yi Xuan felt that the young man before them had become thoroughly arrogant, domineering, and treasonous.

In an instant, surging power descended from the Dharma Realm Projections of the two Immortals.

A continuous series of deafening blasts erupted, squeezing and popping the spatial structure around the Feathered Ascension Grotto-Heaven.

Immortal Yi Xuan took a step forward and bellowed, "Zhang Yu!"

Yet, facing the two towering Immortals, the power of the Eight Divisions Great Gods suddenly surged forth, shielding Zhang Yu as tens of thousands of talismans blanketed the world.

Surrounded by a sea of divine light, Zhang Yu looked directly at the two Immortals and enunciated every single word: "Immortals, please address me as Crown Prince!"

Immortal Yi Xuan and Immortal Roujia stared at Zhang Yu, but in the end, they did not strike in the presence of the Eight Divisions Great Gods.

The surging power receded like a tide, and the figures of the two Immortals slowly dissipated, leaving behind only a single sentence echoing in Zhang Yu's ears.

"Consider yourself warned."

Beside him, Fa Liuyuan had watched the entire scene unfold, his mind swimming with incredibly complicated thoughts.

Just moments ago, when the two Immortal Projections unleashed their oppressive might, Fa Liuyuan had felt almost completely paralyzed, unable to move an inch, his magic power barely even able to circulate.

Such was the absolute gulf between a cultivator and an Immortal. He could sense that with just a single breath, the projections of those two Immortals could have snuffed out his life.

Fa Liuyuan thought to himself, *"Even with the protection of the Eight Divisions Great Gods, those two Immortals represent the will of the majority of the Immortal Class within the Myriad Laws Sect."*

*"If I were the one facing such coercion, would I be able to stand firm like Zhang Yu and shoulder it all just to persist with the reforms?"*

Fa Liuyuan placed a massive question mark on that thought, and a hint of admiration colored his gaze as he looked at Zhang Yu.

*"To push through with the reforms, Zhang Yu chose to head-on withstand the pressure from the Immortal Class... I am far inferior to him."*

At this moment, Fa Liuyuan felt that Zhang Yu seemed far more like a true spokesman for the Reformist Faction.

Meanwhile, having just ended their exchange with Zhang Yu, the two Immortals suddenly saw an unimaginably colossal figure materialize before their eyes — one that seemed to fill the entire heaven and earth, bearing the firmament upon its spine.

Immortal Yi Xuan was startled, realizing that the newcomer was none other than Heavenly Fortune Immortal Venerable of the Myriad Laws Sect.

The reason this Immortal Venerable manifested the appearance of bearing the firmament upon his back was that he, together with the Immortal Venerables of the ten sects, was currently working to breach the Gate of the Heavens' Summit on the 29th layer, seeking to open the path to the 30th layer and step into the restricted zone set by the ten Immortal Emperors.

Heavenly Fortune Immortal Venerable's projection parted its lips slightly, and an intangible ripple resonated directly within the minds of the two Immortals.

"How did Zhang Yu respond?"

The two Immortals sighed inwardly. They had originally intended to use the Reformist Faction's ties with Zhang Yu to serve as a bridge of communication between this Crown Prince of Myriad Laws and the Immortal Class... acting as middleware between the 19th layer and the 21st layer, functioning as middlemen to profit off the difference.

Yet Immortal Yi Xuan and Immortal Roujia had never imagined that Zhang Yu would remain so unyielding even when facing the Immortal Class.

Faced with Heavenly Fortune Immortal Venerable's inquiry, Immortal Roujia explained, "After becoming the Crown Prince of Myriad Laws, that boy genuinely believes he calls the shots in the sect. Refusing to relinquish the authority in his hands now, he has likely been blinded by greed beneath his aspiration for the Immortal Dao, thoroughly corrupted by the Crown Prince Authority. He is willing to disregard the bigger picture just to use that authority for his own personal gain."

Immortal Yi Xuan said, "Once we employ a few slight measures, he will surely see the bigger picture clearly..."

Heavenly Fortune Immortal Venerable's sigh seemed to drift down from beyond the Nine Heavens: "There is no longer time to waste humoring his antics."

Heavenly Fortune Immortal Venerable's goal was to turn the Myriad Laws Districts from the 1st to the 19th layers entirely into his dojos to elevate his Dao Lineage, attempting to break through the blockade imposed on him by the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor.

This had nothing to do with Law Edicts or Thought Tides; it was his own uniquely developed method for upgrading a Dao Lineage.

In his view, within the Ten Sects today, only he... had any chance of breaking the Immortal Emperor's blockade and saving the building from imminent collapse.

He thought to himself, *"Profound Wisdom, you may have given up on your own breakthrough, but I have not."*

He had no intention of explaining or disclosing this plan, knowing full well that the moment he revealed it, whether it was the other Immortal Venerables who distrusted him or the lingering powers left behind by the Immortal Emperors, they would all move to stop him.

Heavenly Fortune Immortal Venerable said, "Desperate times call for thunderous measures."

"I have already given Profound Wisdom fair warning. Initiate the sanctions."

"Make Zhang Yu yield of his own accord and surrender the Crown Prince Authority."

Not long after the two Immortals departed, all manner of sanction measures originating from the Immortal Class began to be publicized through the Dharma Realm and the Spirit Realm.

First to grind to a halt were the enterprises, corporations, factories, and platforms backed by the Immortals.

Immediately following, the orders and demands issued by the Immortals to the sects were retracted one after another.

Even many cultivators of the Void Refinement Realm, Tribulation Transcendence Realm, and Ascension Realm were summoned to the upper realm by the Immortals, taking with them numerous production lines, laboratories, research institutes, and related assets, as they sought to transfer all manner of Immortal Dao productive forces upward.

Watching these high-tier cultivators depart one by one, the hearts of many Sect Staff and outsourced personnel churned with waves of envy, jealousy, panic, and confusion.

"Heavenly Monarch, are you... being taken up by the Immortals to work just like that?"

"Above is where the Immortals live. I hear the environment is countless times better than down here."

"Heavenly Monarch, I beg of you, please take me up with you!"

Some lower-tier cultivators began clinging to the legs of the high-tier cultivators, desperate to be brought along.

Every high-tier cultivator who bade farewell to the sect layers and headed for the 21st layer stirred immense longing throughout the Spirit Realm and the Dharma Realm.

Ling Chenji sent a message to Zhang Yu: "My apologies, Crown Prince. Though the Immortal Ascension Hall is wonderful, the 21st layer offers far broader horizons after all."

Faced with the Immortals' summons, Ling Chenji of the Innate Dao Lineage Faction ultimately decided to head to the 21st layer to take part in the work.

On the other hand, Yi Yaosheng of the pharmaceutical company set off for the 21st layer without even saying goodbye.

Though he had previously pleaded with Zhang Yu, wishing to return to the Immortal Ascension Hall, for his pharmaceutical company, the high-end medicines demanded by the Immortals were, after all, where their greatest interests lay.

Looking down at the swathes of shuttered factories and office districts below, Yi Yaosheng felt a deep sense of lament: "When has the sect ever been this desolate? Zhang Yu is in deep trouble now; one wrong move and the economy will collapse."

"When the time comes, and all the sect's essence has been sucked upward by the Immortals, all that will be left below is an empty shell."

The departure of each high-tier cultivator also took away vast amounts of assets, further stoking panic and triggering widespread drops in stock prices.

Watching all this unfold, Fu Ji lamented, "So many resources, just sucked away by the Immortals like that."

Zhang Yu said calmly, "They were the Immortals' people and assets to begin with. It's just as well that they left — it means fewer hindrances, which makes it all the easier for me to push forward with my reforms."

Immortal Slayer said, "Indeed. The Immortals assume we covet things that do not belong to us. In reality, entirely obedient and controllable layers 1 to 19 hold far greater value for us."

Zhang Yu replied, "Still, we cannot allow the turmoil to persist."

Before long, Zhang Yu stepped forward to deliver an address.

As the Crown Prince of Myriad Laws, the very first matter he addressed was a promise to arrange salary increases for the sect's cultivators in layers 11 to 19.

Additionally, the annual quota of Realm Certificates issued across layers 1 to 19 would be gradually and systematically expanded.

"In the future, rank will no longer be tied to Realm Certificates. Without disrupting the economy, I will steadily raise the number of Realm Certificates issued."

Everyone understood that while Realm Certificates could be granted more freely, rank dictated all sorts of benefits and privileges and could not be handed out without restraint. Therefore, increasing the certificates inevitably required decoupling them from rank.

Furthermore, following the surging price inflation in the Immortal Armaments Bureau and the Netherworld Palace caused by their full market opening, Zhang Yu's promise to steadily and progressively expand certificate issuance won far greater approval from the cultivators.

"In addition, the Dual Rest Law Edict will also be extended for all layers 1 to 19..."

"Regarding new trade orders, I have already begun negotiations with the Netherworld Palace and the Immortal Armaments Bureau. Their demand has exploded, prices are skyrocketing, and they are very interested in our Myriad Laws Sect's goods."

Many cultivators did not believe Zhang Yu's series of promises, but because the potential profits were lucrative enough, greed and wild hopes inevitably flared in their hearts.

Yet many other cultivators harbored greater hope precisely because of Zhang Yu's past track record of raising salaries at the Former Days Graveyard, as well as his various performances in the Immortal Emperor Simulator.

In any case, beneath Zhang Yu's series of appeasements, hearts within the sect finally stabilized a little.

The Sect Examination Clone sighed in admiration. "That's the great thing about Kunxu's cultivators. As long as the promised benefits are rich enough, under their aspiration for the Immortal Dao, their greed will be stoked enough to make them fight for it and take the gamble."

"By the way, Zhang Yu, you're just tricking them, right?"

"You didn't really decide to give them so many benefits, did you?! The sect is meant to sustain us, not them."

---

Inside the Spirit Realm.

Su Xianchen looked at Zhang Yu with a face full of apology. "Crown Prince, I am sorry."

Although Zhang Yu had urged him to stay multiple times, Su Xianchen had still decided to take his assets and numerous subordinates directly to the 21st layer.

Because in the end, he was a cultivator belonging to the Immortal Qi Dao Lineage, and his future prospects rested entirely in the hands of Immortal Venerable Profound Wisdom.

Now that the Immortal Class was imposing sanctions, he had to leave.

At the same time, Su Xianchen thought to himself, *"Zhang Yu wields the Law Edict Expiration Date, so his path to achieve immortality in the future will likely be fraught with difficulties as well. The Immortal Class... certainly won't want him sitting on top of their heads."*

---

In another corner of the Spirit Realm.

Bu Yuangui looked at Zhang Yu before him, his mind firmly made up.

"I am already an Ascension Realm cultivator. Without the chance to achieve immortality, how much higher can I really climb?"

"Besides, as a member of the Myriad Laws Immortal Clan, the Immortals above will never trust us, much less allow me to achieve immortality."

"Only by following His Highness the Crown Prince will I have the sole chance in my lifetime to achieve immortality."

Just then, a message from Bu Yingshu appeared before his eyes.

Bu Yingshu: "Dad! What are your plans?"

Bu Yingshu thought to herself, *"Although Zhang Yu might suppress me, the Immortals won't give me even a sliver of a chance. Only by staying down here might Zhang Yu help me break the shackles of the Immortal Clan and allow me to achieve immortality..."*

*"Dad, if you want to leave, then I'll just have to abandon you."*

Looking at the message sent by his daughter, Bu Yuangui thought to himself, *"Silly girl, just watch closely as your father becomes the very first Immortal of our clan to break the rules."*

With thoughts flashing through his mind in an instant, Bu Yuangui knelt down once more, declaring his determination.

"Greetings, Crown Prince. This subject is willing to assist Your Highness, to restore order to Kunxu, never resting through nine deaths, and striving until utter ruin!"

Within the Feathered Ascension Grotto-Heaven, Zhang Yu looked at Fa Liuyuan before him and said in surprise, "Heavenly Monarch Fa! You..."

Fa Liuyuan smiled, cupped his hands, and said, "Crown Prince, this subject is willing to serve as Your Highness's wings, assisting you in launching reforms and revitalizing the Myriad Laws Sect."

He thought to himself, *"The Reformist Faction has long existed in name only. How is the Immortal Class up there any different from the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor today?"*

Fa Liuyuan went over every Immortal and Immortal Venerable he knew, yet he could not see who among them was fundamentally different from the ten Immortal Emperors.

After pondering repeatedly, he reached a conclusion: *"These Immortals will only accelerate the End; there is absolutely no possibility of them stopping it."*

*"This Crown Prince, Zhang Yu... at least at this very moment, he still holds the desire to change things. Perhaps his strength is insufficient, and the possibility is infinitesimally small, but at the very least, there is still that sliver of hope to prevent the End."*

"Good, good, good!" Zhang Yu immediately stepped forward and firmly took Fa Liuyuan's hands. He truly had not expected that Fa Liuyuan would be willing to stay behind.

Zhang Yu asked again, "Heavenly Monarch Fa, what about Immortal Yi Xuan and Immortal Roujia..."

Fa Liuyuan forced a wry smile and said, "I will slowly repay the debts I owe them. You need not worry yourself over it, Crown Prince."

Zhang Yu sighed. "Senior Fa, it's been hard on you."

Fa Liuyuan replied, "Your Highness, if you ask me... right now, you bear the weight of the entire Myriad Laws District from the 1st to the 19th layer on your shoulders, and you also have to face the Immortals' sanctions directly. You are the one in the Myriad Laws Sect who bears the greatest pressure and suffers the most."

Zhang Yu gave Fa Liuyuan an odd look, thinking to himself, *"I never expected Heavenly Monarch Fa to be so good at flattering, either."*

The Sect Examination Clone sneered, "Heh, among the cultivators in this Kunxu who managed to climb all the way to the Ascension Realm, who among them doesn't know how to flatter their superiors?"

---

Inside the Heavenly Surveillance Hall, Dugu Ming had likewise chosen not to head to the 21st layer.

He knew that as a lone minister originally loyal to the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor, going up there now with the End approaching and falling into the Immortals' hands would almost certainly spell his doom.

Looking through the sect, from Dugu Ming's perspective, Zhang Yu was the only person left he could pledge his loyalty to.

*"Only His Highness the Crown Prince can shelter me now."*

It was the fifth day since the Immortals' sanctions on the Myriad Laws Sect had begun.

After Zhang Yu's thorough reshuffle, only three Ascension Realm cultivators remained in the Immortal Ascension Hall: Bu Yuangui, Fa Liuyuan, and Dugu Ming.

Although the sharp drop in numbers greatly diminished the Immortal Ascension Hall's underlying influence, this series of maneuvers allowed Zhang Yu to gain complete control over it, turning this central hub of the Myriad Laws Sect into his personal mouthpiece.

*"Dugu Ming is a smart man. He knows that only I can protect him right now. Transferring him from the Heavenly Surveillance Hall to the Immortal Ascension Hall allows me to continue leveraging his influence in the intelligence network while gradually severing his ties with the Heavenly Surveillance Hall."*

*"Once the other cultivators grow capable enough, I will replace him."*

Zhang Yu thought to himself, *"Next, I must seize the opportunity presented by the Immortals' sanctions to break down the old and build the new... Step by step, I will reform the Myriad Laws Sect and firmly grasp layers 1 through 19 entirely in my hands."*

After taking control of the Myriad Laws Sect, the very first policy Zhang Yu issued today was an all-sect salary increase.

Every sect cultivator remaining on layers 11 through 19 was eligible for this raise.

Conversely, any cultivator heading to layer 21 would have their salary suspended immediately.

Bu Yuangui, Dugu Ming, and Fa Liuyuan were all startled upon seeing this order, yet after a brief hesitation, they chose to pass it.

In an instant, a salary increase notification floated before the eyes of countless Myriad Laws Sect cultivators across the sect layers, their faces quickly breaking into joy.

"It went up! It really went up!"

"His Highness the Crown Prince didn't lie to us — everyone gets a raise!"

Watching this unfold, Fu Ji and the Sect Examination Clone both wailed in agony.

"How could he just scatter so many Immortal Coins like that? The sect's money is our money now!"

Zhang Yu then proceeded to push forward other policies.

A portion of outstanding outsourced personnel were converted to permanent staff, and a policy was established ensuring that high-performing outsourced personnel each year would be granted quotas for permanent status.

---

In the Former Days Graveyard, Lu Hengzhang stared in excitement at the notice of permanent appointment before him, thinking to himself, *"Even Soul Cultivators can get permanent status?! To think that one day, I could actually return to the Sect Staff system!"*

Inside the Feathered Ascension Grotto-Heaven, Nether Spring and the other Spirit Transformation Cultivators — the former Spirit Transformation Principals — were overjoyed to the utmost. "We're made permanent! We're truly permanent now!"

At the same time, Nether Spring discovered that her son, Wang Yin, whom she had not heard from in a long time, had actually sent a message to congratulate her.

Wang Yin thought to himself, *"This biological mom of mine has hopped onto Zhang Yu's Promotion Accelerator. She might end up rising through the ranks even faster than me in the future. It's well worth calling her 'Mom' once again."*

Not long after the outsourced cultivators were made permanent, a massive number of Realm Certificates, ranging from Foundation Establishment all the way to Ascension Realm, were issued.

Furthermore, a policy to increase the annual quota of issued certificates was also established.

Of course, unlike the Immortal Armaments Bureau and the Netherworld Palace, which had opened up completely in a single day, Zhang Yu had considered the impact on the market and resources while formulating the policy, expanding the issuance of Realm Certificates step by step in an orderly manner.

Consequently, the sect cultivators who obtained Realm Certificates were plunged into sheer ecstasy.

In addition, the scale of this term's Sect Examination had been expanded several-fold, with future annual examinations set to expand even further on this basis, and their frequency increased from once a year to twice a year.

Looking over these policies one by one, the Herald God analyzed, "I get it now. First, raise their salaries to stabilize morale."

"Then arrange for the sect's cultivators to break through in batches. This creates demand and gets them to spend that money back out. And unlike the reckless approach of the Immortal Armaments Bureau, Myriad Laws' breakthroughs proceed in planned batches, avoiding sudden market overheating and skyrocketing prices for cultivation resources."

"Once these cultivators break through, market demand in the lower-tier cultivation realms will start to decline, so by transitioning outsourced workers to regular status, combined with expanding the Sect Examination to recruit more low-tier cultivation practitioners, those new recruits will fill the market demand."

"By releasing and tightening control like this, we not only establish an economic cycle and cultivate talent, but also gradually fill the void left by the Immortals' sanctions and boost tax revenue. The situation has stabilized considerably."

At the same time, Zhang Yu was looking at a list that Tai Yuebai had sent him.

Every single cultivator on the list was someone Tai Yuebai had searched for and confirmed to possess a Dao Seed.

Right now, these people had all been granted Realm Certificates and were on the verge of making their breakthroughs.

Zhang Yu thought to himself, *"Big Brother Tai really does know many cultivators with Dao Seeds."*

*"Once they finish their breakthroughs, I can arrange for them to come see me."*

Zhang Yu sighed internally, *"Now that I wield real power, collecting and cultivating Dao Seeds has truly become so much easier."*

Over the following days, Zhang Yu continued to transform the Myriad Laws Sect step by step through the Immortal Ascension Hall.

A large number of Dharma Realm channels were shut down, and various transactions within the sect began shifting over to the Spirit Realm.

"After all, the Dharma Realm is controlled by the Immortals above; we still need to gradually decouple from it."

"In contrast, with the Spirit Realm under the Great Gods' control, its information is completely open to me. I can see the records of almost every single transaction, tracking where every bit of money flows."

---

Meanwhile, backed by a series of policies, various loans, lands, and construction projects were swiftly approved. Feathered Ascension Grotto-Heaven bustled with fervor, and a colossal urban agglomeration had already sprung up from the ground.

Watching the space expand relentlessly day and night, Old Gao silently marveled to himself, "Now that Zhang Yu holds all the power, the development efficiency of Feathered Ascension Grotto-Heaven is vastly superior."

"With public graft territories continuously being transferred over from all sides, the entire Grotto-Heaven is growing at an unfathomable speed."

Zhang Yu hadn't halted his oversight of Law Edicts.

Following Su Xianchen's departure, the entire Law Edict Management Committee had fallen under his control.

Furthermore, his Expiration Date Law Edict, combined with the Law Edicts that Yu Xinghan, Magnetic Pole, and Ye Xingli held aloft to sustain them, allowed Zhang Yu to initiate procedures for review, sealing, amendment, and more on various Law Edicts at any time.

Zhang Yu thought to himself, *"Fortunately, I had Yu Xinghan and the others complete the Law Edicts they held aloft beforehand; this is the perfect time to use them."*

Thus, the Four-Hour Workday Law Edict was officially revised to an eight-hour workday.

The Dual Rest Law Edict was also rolled out across the entire 11th to 19th layers.

Even Bu Yingshu's "Once Certified, Temporarily Certified" entered the amendment process.

After submitting her revised version once more, Bu Yingshu soon received a reply from Zhang Yu: "Erroneous. Redraft."

"Ahhh!!!"

Bu Yingshu fumed in frustration, "I've already lowered the recertification requirements again and again! Just how far does Zhang Yu want to suppress my Law Edict?!"

---

Inside the Feathered Ascension Grotto-Heaven.

Behind Zhang Yu, sealed Law Edicts emerged one after another, condensing into a fractured crown.

As the Law Edicts entered the procedures of review, sealing, and revision, whether they could be enacted or not gradually fell under Zhang Yu's control.

The Sect Examination Clone laughed, "Hahahaha, this way, every single one of these Law Edicts is in our hands! We can activate them whenever we want, and shut them down whenever we want!"

Right at that moment, several messages flashed before Zhang Yu's eyes.

"The Immortal Armaments Bureau and the Netherworld Palace... have they finally agreed?"

Due to the full deregulation of the Realm Certificates, prices and demand had skyrocketed in both the Immortal Armaments Bureau and the Netherworld Palace. Following repeated negotiations, Zhang Yu had finally secured a portion of their trade orders.

Thus, between his series of maneuvers and this trade order from the Immortal Armaments Bureau and the Netherworld Palace, the situation across the 11th to 19th layers of the Myriad Laws Sect had gradually stabilized, even though many upper cultivators and industries had been relocated elsewhere.

Through the procedures of the Immortal Ascension Hall, Zhang Yu also finalized the reforms to the Edict Pavilion's relevant protocols.

Now, he opened the Edict Pavilion's database and began searching for black arts within it.
