# Chapter 1044: The Expiration Date Matrix Takes Shape, Law Decree

Time flew by, and mid-February soon arrived.

More than two months had passed since the Divine Child incident in the Heavenly Court.

Ever since then, Zhang Pianpian had been stationed in the Heavenly Court, handling numerous court affairs on Zhang Yu's behalf with the assistance of the Divine Emperor's remnant soul.

Naturally, the two of them maintained constant communication, allowing Zhang Yu to stay updated on the Heavenly Court's latest developments in real time.

Meanwhile, inside the Feathered Ascension Grotto-Heaven...

With Zhang Yu's ever-growing cultivation, his steadily advancing spatial technology, and Old Gao's diligent efforts after becoming Vice Pavilion Master of the Grotto-Heaven Pavilion — which had funneled in more and more embezzled public space — the Feathered Ascension Grotto-Heaven was now divided into a total of twenty-one layers.

The outermost first layer, which Zhang Yu had developed with great focus as an economic hub, had already turned into a gargantuan city.

At every single moment, tens of thousands of spatial fluctuations stirred with a mere thought from Zhang Yu, driving an immense volume of transportation and logistics operations.

Meanwhile, within the Spirit Realm that blanketed the area, vast torrents of information surged relentlessly.

On the other hand, across the various inner layers of the grotto-heaven, Zhang Yu's different clones were busy carrying out all manner of tasks.

Under Zhang Yu's control, one of the clones clenched its fist slightly. A staggering physical strength of 82.12 points erupted with a roar. In the palm of its hand, it was as if a world had undergone creation and destruction — first, countless particles of matter were annihilated, immediately followed by the shattering of the spatial structure, collapsing into pure chaos.

As Zhang Yu slightly reined in his power, that patch of chaos swiftly recovered, returning to normal spatial structure once more.

Zhang Yu thought to himself, *"Is this the physical strength of an Immortal? Just this physical might alone boasts horrifying destructive capability, enough to crush any grotto-heaven with bare hands."*

After more than two months of continuous cultivation, backed by a frenzied infusion of sect resources, Zhang Yu's basic stats — including his dao heart, magic power, and physical strength — had once again achieved a tremendous leap.

At the same time, thanks to the Supreme Truth Legacy's Emperor Creation Seed, his dao heart had reached level 82, his physical strength 82.12, and his magic power exceeded two billion units. All of them had broken the conventional limits of an Ascension Realm cultivator, reaching the level of an Immortal.

Yet precisely because of this, Zhang Yu felt all the more acutely how unfathomable the Immortals were in their power.

Having stepped into this realm, Zhang Yu could sense that with every tiny bit he improved, he pulled vastly further ahead of his past self.

"Even though my base stats have reached the preliminary level of achieving immortality, trying to contend head-on with Immortals relying solely on this strength is undoubtedly a fool's dream. After all, Immortals rely on far more than just some basic numbers."

The closer he got to immortal strength, the more Zhang Yu felt the sheer power of the Immortal Class, and the more he yearned to ascend and become an Immortal.

Just then, Tianxing sent a message: "Your Highness Crown Prince, Vice Pavilion Master Gao, President Shi, and the others have all arrived."

With a shift in Zhang Yu's thoughts, he cast down a projection, appearing in the Spirit Realm Conference Room.

Inside the conference room, Old Gao, Tu Lishan, Yun Zhiguang, and Teacher Starfire had all arrived early and were waiting.

Now that Law Establishment had been changed to twice a year, the four of them, under Zhang Yu's full support, had just concluded the early-year Law Establishment Conference. Each had finished their own Law Edict and been assigned different trial regions for them. At this moment, their faces were glowing with vitality and brimming with drive.

Seeing Zhang Yu appear, Yun Zhiguang wore a look of utter respect, sighing inwardly, *"Back when I took up the post of heading to the Lower Realm to manage the university on an interim basis, I thought it was just an ordinary errand. Who would have thought it would turn out to be the greatest opportunity of my life?"*

Zhang Yu looked at Yun Zhiguang and said, "Little Zhi, your newly drafted Temporary Extension Law Edict is quite well done. It plugs the loophole of the Expiration Date Law Edict and shows a deep understanding of my concerns regarding the management of Law Edicts."

The so-called temporary extension meant that when there was no time to renew a Law Edict before its expiration date, certain Law Edicts would be granted a temporary extension of three to six months.

Zhang Yu continued, "As you go on to pilot this Law Edict, I have a phrase for you: 'Tyrannical governance is fiercer than Immortals.'"

Yun Zhiguang hurriedly replied, "Your Highness's words are profound indeed. I will certainly bear them in mind and reflect on them constantly."

At the same time, she thought to herself, *"Does the Crown Prince mean we should exploit the realm even more fiercely than the Immortals?"*

Zhang Yu looked at Old Gao, Starfire, and Tu Lishan, sighing with emotion, "I give this phrase to the three of you as well. Now that the sect has just regained stability, the countless cultivators spanning the 1st to the 19th layers need to live, work, earn money, and cultivate. All these responsibilities rest upon our shoulders."

"Any minor change in a Law Edict, rippling through all layers, could affect the fates of countless people. If mishandled, it could prove far more destructive than the deeds of some Immortals, and it would ultimately rebound upon us. We must proceed with utmost caution."

Next, Zhang Yu turned his gaze toward Tu Lishan and said, "President Tu, your 'Temporary Unban' is also very necessary. Once an original Law Edict is banned, the formal process to unban it is far too slow, so your Law Edict plays an important role in that regard."

A flicker of delight crossed Tu Lishan's face, and he thought to himself, *"Now that I have successfully supported the Crown Prince's Law Edict and aided His Highness in establishing his Law Decree, once the Crown Prince ascends and becomes an Immortal in the future — or even ascends to the throne of Immortal Emperor — my contribution will be that of following an Immortal and an Emperor!"*

Yet before Tu Lishan's joy could fully register, Zhang Yu continued, "Tu Lishan, I heard that the civil engineering university students recruited into the sect this year are all calling you 'Ancestor'?"

Tu Lishan's expression tightened. Unsure of what Zhang Yu meant for a moment, he could only nod and say, "Some people do like to call me that."

Zhang Yu snorted coldly and said, "What ancestor? Whose ancestor? In Ying Xin's family of six generations of civil engineers, only the dead are called ancestors. Do you want to be a dead man too?"

Terrified, Tu Lishan dropped to his knees and began admitting his fault, his mind racing to figure out which of his actions had displeased the Crown Prince.

"Did I take too many red packets? Or is it a scheduling issue? Or a problem with salaries?"

Zhang Yu turned his gaze to Old Gao and Starfire, his tone softening slightly. "Teachers, thank you for your hard work this time. The two Law Edicts, 'Permanent Sealing' and 'Exemption', are of grave importance. I can only rest easy by entrusting them to you."

Seeing that Zhang Yu, who now held supreme power in his hands, was still so polite and trusting toward them, Starfire, who had already become a Void Refinement Dao Monarch, stood up and solemnly said, "This old minister will never fail the Crown Prince's trust."

Watching this scene unfold from the side, Yun Zhiguang thought to herself, *"It truly is as the rumors say. His Highness the Crown Prince admires the ancient cultivators and respects teachers and the Dao above all else. Even the teachers who taught him in high school are still treated with such respect by him today."*

*"Heh, if ordinary people saw this, they would surely think of currying favor with Starfire, Gao Chongguang, and Magnetic Pole. Those slightly smarter might think of donating money to the Crown Prince's high school or even paying visits to those high school teachers."*

*"However, those are all inferior approaches that completely fail to align with the spirit His Highness the Crown Prince has always conveyed — capturing merely the form without grasping its essence. A truly intelligent person who witnesses this scene... should understand that every single move the Crown Prince makes is signaling the direction the wind is blowing!"*

Yun Zhiguang's mind continued to race: *"Then why does His Royal Highness the Crown Prince revere the ancient? All sectarian trends are inextricably linked to economic foundations. The essence of the Crown Prince revering the ancient... is that with the departure of the ten Immortal Emperors and the End drawing near, Kunxu's governing power is insufficient, order is unstable, and massive resources must be poured into confronting the apocalypse. At such a time, it is no longer suitable to consume vast resources on surveillance, stability maintenance, and control like in the past."*

*"Therefore, the Crown Prince promotes the ancient ways because he wants to reduce friction and use human relationships to replace part of the governance costs, freeing up more Immortal Dao productivity to face the apocalypse."*

*"At this juncture, I should learn from the Crown Prince, study the things he has done and the words he has spoken, and revere the ancient and respect teachers just as he does, helping to advance the Crown Prince's sect policy."*

At this thought, figures immediately flashed through Yun Zhiguang's mind: *"However, my teachers are either already dead or have been outcompeted by me and ended up becoming my disciples. What do I do now?"*

*"I have no choice but to recognize them back as my teachers."*

While Yun Zhiguang was deep in contemplation, Zhang Yu quickly discussed the specific plan for the trial implementation of the Law Edicts with the four of them.

A moment later, as their conversation drew to a close, Zhang Yu quickly dispatched the four to their respective pilot zones for these Law Edicts.

When the four Law Edicts rose with a resounding boom, Zhang Yu felt that, amidst the unseen depths of fate, his Expiration Date Law Edict, which had already expanded across layers 11 through 19, was once again buoyed upward by four distinct forces.

Adding the previous support from Magnetic Pole, Ye Xingli, and Yu Xinghan, there were now seven Law Edicts bolstering his Expiration Date Law Edict all at once, gradually elevating it into a Law Decree.

From this moment on, with the Expiration Date Law Edict at the core, Zhang Yu could freely mobilize these seven supporting Law Edicts at will, enabling him to review, freeze, seal, exempt, renew, impose permanent sealing on, and perform various other operations upon Law Edicts.

Beyond relying on the standard 50-year expiration date, he could now make ad hoc adjustments at any crucial moment.

Just as Zhang Yu was savoring this sublimation of the Law Edict, good news also arrived from his Sect Examination Clone and the Evil Gods on the other side.

Surrounding the Sect Examination Clone were Fu Ji, Auspicious God, Herald God, and a host of other Evil Gods, all gathered around him offering their congratulations.

Fu Ji: "Congratulations, Your Highness! Felicitations, Your Highness! Another Dao Seed has ascended!"

Auspicious God: "It is all thanks to Your Highness's wise and martial brilliance, and your skillful teaching. Only by properly training these piglets could they advance so rapidly."

Herald God said nothing, merely thinking to himself, *"You two fools, flattering the Sect Examination Clone is completely useless. This fellow only looks at performance, profits, and losses — purely the temperament of old Kunxu."*

And speaking of performance, Herald God could not help puffing out his chest.

Over the past two months, whether in the number of Evil God worshippers developed or the effectiveness in unleashing the potential of the Heavenly Chosen, Herald God had performed quite well.

With the assistance of the evil gods, the Sect Examination Clone had been continuously guiding numerous Heavenly Chosen of the Myriad Laws Sect during this period, raising their cultivation while simultaneously elevating the rank of their Dao Seeds.

During this process, Zhang Yu had also successfully replicated several Dao Seeds.

For instance, at this very moment, along with the upgrade of the Dao Seeds, Zhang Yu saw the information shift within the Feather Book.

The Sect Examination Clone also shared this information in real time, thinking to himself, *"Qi Sea Return to Source, which rapidly recovers magic power; Formless Vajra, which drastically increases physical resistance; and Primordial Spirit Certification Grade, which can replenish the dao heart — have they all increased by one level?"*

*"As expected, only by holding supreme power in Kunxu can one's strength soar at such an astonishing pace."*

*"Ever since I became the Crown Prince of Myriad Laws, from basic stats to Heaven-Opening cultivation arts, and then to Law Edicts and Dao Seeds, my strength has been advancing faster than ever before in my life."*

The Sect Examination Clone sighed inwardly, *"Just as the saying goes, earning the first hundred million takes the longest. But going from the first hundred million to the second, and then to the third, it snowballs, growing faster and faster."*

*"And our current increase in strength has reached that accelerating phase."*

*"Yet in comparison, what about the rate of improvement for the ten Immortal Emperors right now?"*

The Sect Examination Clone looked toward the realm beyond the heavens, thinking to himself, *"With the changes in the flow of time, are they still surpassing all cultivators and Immortals within Kunxu?"*

*"We... still need to achieve immortality as soon as possible."*

Right at this moment, Zhang Yu received a message from Tai Yuebai.

Tai Yuebai: "I still need a bit more time."

Zhang Yu thought, *"Has he not stolen an ultra-high-danger Dao Seed yet? Still, these ultra-high-danger Dao Seeds are connected to Immortals after all and are heavily guarded. It makes sense that they aren't so easy to steal."*

---

Meanwhile, within the sixth layer of the Feathered Ascension Grotto-Heaven.

An endless series of rumbling roars sounded, like hundreds of millions of thunderclaps exploding simultaneously.

Two figures were like streaks of flowing light, hurtling through this space one after another at the speed of light. With every collision, an earth-shattering might erupted.

Dugu Ming, Fa Liuyuan, and Bu Yuangui all stood watching this scene together.

Dugu Ming remarked, "His Highness the Crown Prince's cultivation has grown substantially once more. With supreme martial dao techniques at his fingertips, he has already broken through the limits of the Ascension Realm and attained the level of an Immortal."

Fa Liuyuan nodded repeatedly, adding, "His Highness's knowledge bridges heaven and mankind, his accomplishments marveling creation. Just a few months after breaking through to the Ascension Realm, whether in base stats or the tier of his cultivation arts, he is already comparable to an Immortal. Truly unprecedented and never to be matched!"

"Even in today's intense battle against the Great God of Myriad Paths and Four Directions, he hasn't fallen behind in the slightest."

Listening to their praise from the side, Bu Yuangui thought to himself, *"Two flatterers."*

In Bu Yuangui's view, though Zhang Yu's strength was earth-shattering, it was ultimately impossible for him to directly contend against Great Gods and Immortals.

After all, Immortals commanded Dao Lineages, while Great Gods possessed Pseudo-Dao Lineages — a form of formidable power reliant on the Heavenly Court.

*"Immortals use Heaven-Opening cultivation arts combined with their own Immortal Artifacts to maximally leverage the power of the Dao Lineages, projecting various forms of interference from the Dao Lineages onto the physical world."*

*"The Pseudo-Dao Lineages of the Great Gods, coupled with talismans, achieve a similar effect."*

*"This isn't something any cultivator who has yet to achieve immortality can compare to."*

In Bu Yuangui's eyes, the Great God of Myriad Paths and Four Directions was currently deliberately suppressing his combat power to cooperate with Zhang Yu's test; otherwise, the victor would have been decided in no time.

Even though he thought this in his heart, Bu Yuangui still voiced his praise, "Your Highness's strength grows ever more unfathomable. Among the Ten Great Sects today, I believe no Ascension Realm cultivator is a match for Your Highness. In the next Ascension Selection, Your Highness will undoubtedly ascend and become an Immortal."

After all, as far as Bu Yuangui was concerned, he himself, as a leader, preferred subordinates who were obedient, eager to please, and proactive. Otherwise, why ignore those subordinates just to favor people who were passive, unmotivated, unfriendly, and kept a cold face all day? What if such a person was placed in an important position only to disobey orders or even turn on him?

*"Still, subordinates like that have probably long been eliminated from the sect. Perhaps they can still be found in the Lower Realm?"*

Right then, Zhang Yu and the Great God of Myriad Paths and Four Directions suddenly came to a halt simultaneously.

Bu Yuangui was slightly startled. Just as he was wondering whether he had said something wrong, a message through the Spirit Realm shook all quarters, making him understand why Zhang Yu had stopped.

*"The Ascension Selection has begun? So quickly? There wasn't the slightest rumor beforehand."*

Bu Yuangui then looked at the representatives of the Great Sects, and his heart immediately sank.

*"The representative for the Myriad Laws Sect isn't the Crown Prince? His Highness... doesn't take part in this Ascension Selection?"*
