# Chapter 1025: Joining the Great Sage

*"We can never let the cultivators of the Celestial Monster Sect seize supreme control over the Heavenly Court."*

This thought flashed through Zhang Yu's mind.

The Sect Examination Clone agreed as well, "If monsterfolk seize the authority of the Heavenly Court, wouldn't Righteous Gods become monster gods and beast gods? Wouldn't that leave the heavens above and the earth below reeking of foul stenches everywhere? How could that possibly be allowed?"

The Sect Examination Clone declared with righteous indignation, "Furthermore, saving Kunxu is our inescapable duty. From Kunxu's eighteen layers below to the thirty-six layers above, who else besides us could shoulder such a momentous task? We are this world's sole hope!"

"If this bunch of monsterfolk is allowed to wrest control over the Heavenly Court, preventing the Eight Divisions Great Gods from aiding us when the time comes, that would be hindering us from defending Kunxu! That would mean destroying the only hope, devastating Kunxu, and annihilating the world!"

The more the Sect Examination Clone thought about it, the more furious he grew, shouting, "These monsterfolk from the Celestial Monster Sect are sheer scumbags who are anti-Kunxu, anti-Immortal Dao, anti-human, and anti-monsterfolk!"

Meanwhile, Zhang Yu's consciousness quickly descended into the Supreme Clarity Realm, intending to speak face-to-face with Zhang Pianpian to learn more about the situation.

Inside the Supreme Clarity Realm, clouds and mist still swirled as mountains encircled the area.

The layout of the Daoist Temple originally situated at the center had not changed much, but its scale had expanded several times over. 

Zhang Yu knew this was because Zhang Pianpian had been continually acquiring equipment support from the outside, enhancing the basic computing power of the Supreme Clarity Realm all along and developing more application directions. 

At the same time, Zhang Pianpian stood before a Great Void Illusionary Realm in the Side Hall. 

As Zhang Yu's mind swept over it, he discovered it was currently deducing the entire future trajectory of Kunxu. 

The surging, colossal stream of data caused even Zhang Yu's thoughts to slow for a brief moment. 

He was slightly astonished inwardly: *"Elder Sister's control over the Supreme Clarity Realm and the algorithms she developed have grown even more formidable. Her deductive capacity is astonishing — she's actually factoring all the various factions and powerhouses into the equation?"*

And in just that brief instant, he had already witnessed hundreds and thousands of instances of Kunxu's destruction, yet not a single outcome showed them successfully surviving the Ultimate Doomsday. 

Beside him, Zhang Pianpian said, "Little brother, I ran deductions using my newly refined Chaos Heavenly Fate Algorithm combined with the underlying intelligence gathered by the Righteous Gods, but up until now, I haven't deduced a single possibility of surviving the doomsday."

"Perhaps in the end, all of Kunxu will be forced to unite and face the doomsday together, but that would be too late... far too late."

Zhang Pianpian sighed. "This might mean that all of Kunxu is doomed to fail, or it might be that I have yet to grasp the data necessary to survive the doomsday."

Watching this scene, Zhang Yu sighed with emotion. "Do all humanly possible, and leave the rest to fate."

In fact, Zhang Yu had no certainty of victory either, nor did he possess absolute confidence that he could lead all of Kunxu through the Ultimate Doomsday.

Just as he lacked full assurance that the black arts he practiced were truly beyond the calculations of the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor.

All he could do was give his all within the scope of his capabilities.

Zhang Pianpian nodded and said, "You are right. Sparing no effort is our only choice."

With that, at a thought from Zhang Pianpian, the Illusionary Realm before their eyes transformed once more, turning into the scenery of the Myriad Laws Sect.

Zhang Pianpian's voice rang out, "Across the entire area from layers 1 to 19 of the Myriad Laws Sect, it has likely fallen under the influence of Heavenly Fortune Immortal Venerable's Dao Lineage. You should have noticed it as well, right?"

Seeing Zhang Yu nod slightly, Zhang Pianpian continued, "Deducing from this current state, the conflict within the entirety of layers 1 through 19 belonging to the Myriad Laws Sect will grow increasingly fierce, eventually even triggering a civil war."

Within the illusionary realm, the Myriad Laws Sect from top to bottom had already descended into strife, ultimately escalating into an all-out war that even invited an invasion from foreign sects.

Zhang Pianpian said, "Heavenly Fortune Immortal Venerable likely wants to use this pressure to force your submission. Judging by his current actions, he will probably offer you some benefits in the end, binding you to a community of shared interests and having you surrender control over layers 1 to 19."

Zhang Yu said, "I won't yield layers 1 to 19 to him."

Immortal Slayer said, "Well said! Right now, the entire Immortal Class in Kunxu belongs to the Immortal Dao Monopoly Faction! Not a single one of them can be trusted. If you want to save the world, you must abandon all illusions about them."

Zhang Pianpian seemed to have anticipated Zhang Yu's answer. Her face showed no surprise as she simply continued, "Then you need to figure out a way to counter the Destiny Dao Lineage as soon as possible."

"And before that, you still have the matters regarding the Heavenly Court and the Spirit Realm to resolve."

With a single thought from Zhang Pianpian, the Illusionary Realm before their eyes shifted once again, transforming into countless starlights drifting through the darkness.

Zhang Yu asked in confusion, "This is...?"

Zhang Pianpian explained, "This is the depths of the Spirit Realm, the Desolate Ox Great Sage's Will Cluster, and also the underlying architecture of the Spirit Realm. The entire Spirit Realm is constructed atop the Desolate Ox Great Sage's will. Even the Righteous God's divine power network was built upon this foundation."

"You know about the Desolate Ox Great Sage, right?"

Seeing Zhang Yu nod again, Zhang Pianpian was not surprised. After all, in her eyes, it was only natural for her younger brother to know about the Desolate Ox Great Sage after reaching his current heights.

Meanwhile, Zhang Yu looked at Zhang Pianpian, a hint of surprise in his eyes.

To Zhang Yu, this Elder Sister of his always seemed to know countless secrets, exuding an aura of mystery that clearly hinted at an extraordinary background.

Zhang Pianpian continued, "In theory, as long as one controls the Desolate Ox Great Sage, one can control the Spirit Realm."

"And as long as one controls the Spirit Realm, one can gradually seize the authority of the Righteous Gods. If the Great Gods cooperate, the entire process will be even faster."

As she spoke, with a single thought from Zhang Pianpian, countless intents from the Celestial Monster Sect could be seen inside the Illusionary Realm, chasing after the Desolate Ox Great Sage's collective will like a cluster of satellites.

"Recently, a large number of cultivators from the Celestial Monster Sect have used the permissions granted by the Celestial Monster Immortal Emperor to infiltrate the Desolate Ox Great Sage's sea of consciousness."

"Constrained by their own permissions, the Eight Divisions Great Gods cannot disclose this situation."

"After all, under the rules established by the ten Immortal Emperors, the Desolate Ox Great Sage's sea of consciousness has always been a Righteous God Forbidden Zone. Not only are the Eight Divisions Great Gods forbidden from entering, but they are also barred from revealing the slightest bit of intelligence regarding it."

"When the ten Immortal Emperors were presiding over Kunxu, naturally no one could enter the Desolate Ox Great Sage's sea of consciousness, let alone awaken or control it."

Zhang Pianpian then further displayed the state of the Desolate Ox Great Sage's collective will; one could seemingly see that streams of starlight within it had already grown dim.

Zhang Pianpian continued, "However, now that the ten Immortal Emperors have departed, the Celestial Monster Immortal Emperor seized this opportunity to lay down countermeasures upon leaving. Numerous cultivators from the Celestial Monster Sect are currently contaminating the Desolate Ox Intent, seeking to bring it entirely under their control..."

"Fortunately, no Monster Immortals from the Celestial Monster Sect have made a move yet."

Zhang Yu guessed in his heart: *"Is it because the Celestial Monster Immortal Emperor can't even trust his own Monster Immortals? Afraid that handing over this kind of opportunity to a Monster Immortal would only make them do everything they could to hoard the benefits for themselves? Or perhaps... are there actually Monster Immortals already hiding in the shadows?"*

Along with Zhang Pianpian's detailed explanation, Zhang Yu felt the gravity of the situation grow ever more severe.

The Sect Examination Clone said, "No! Rather than letting the Desolate Ox be controlled by the Celestial Monster Sect, it would be better for us to take control of it."

Immortal Slayer said, "Indeed. The Spirit Realm is of paramount importance. To survive the doomsday, such power must be held in our hands."

The Desolate Ox said, "I am the Desolate Ox, and I think so too."

Zhang Yu thought to himself, *"The question is how to control it."*

The Sect Examination Clone said, *"Communicate directly with the Collective Will in the Desolate Ox's sea of consciousness. We are the Great Sage's Heirs. Would it refuse to help us and help the Celestial Monster Immortal Emperor instead?"*

Immortal Slayer said, *"We can first find out how the Celestial Monster Sect is doing it and what technology they intend to use to control the Desolate Ox."*

After pondering for a moment, Zhang Yu asked, "Sis, I tried to establish contact with the Desolate Ox Great Sage's will earlier, but it seemed to have been intercepted by a Righteous God?"

Zhang Pianpian said, "Remote communication is forbidden. If it weren't for the high authority you possess, the Righteous Gods would have come straight for you."

Zhang Yu said, "So you're saying that if I want to communicate with the Desolate Ox Great Sage, I need to personally enter his sea of consciousness, just like these cultivators of the Celestial Monster Sect?"

Hearing this, Zhang Pianpian nodded and said, "Theoretically speaking, yes. However, you are currently being watched by Heavenly Fortune Immortal Venerable. If your consciousness descends into the Desolate Ox Great Sage's sea of consciousness for an extended period, it is very likely he will notice. If you aren't within the protection range of the Eight Divisions Great Gods at that time, he might very well make a move against you to try and control you."

Zhang Yu said, "So I need to evade the Heavenly Fortune Immortal Venerable and sneak into the Desolate Ox Great Sage's sea of consciousness..."

Even as Zhang Yu and Zhang Pianpian were discussing a plan, Zhang Yu had already split off a wisp of his consciousness to attend to matters regarding the Great Sage Cultivation Art.

At this very moment, Zhang Yu had already gathered nearly a complete set of arts under the Great Sage's Heir Chain Formation Diagram, lacking only the final art to fully activate all the functions of the Chain Formation Diagram.

"If I can fully activate [Great Sage's Heir], it might work wonders when communicating with the Desolate Ox."

Yet, Zhang Yu had still not managed to acquire this very last cultivation art.

His gaze swept over 'My Favorites' on the cultivation art sales platform, thinking to himself, *"The Immortal Sect Heaven-Opening-Tier cultivation art, Celestial Monster Dharma Body."*

This Celestial Monster Dharma Body was a Heaven-Opening cultivation art Zhang Yu had spotted on the platform long ago. It had been sitting quietly in his favorites all along, but in the past, he had never possessed the funds to buy it.

By now, as the Crown Prince of Myriad Laws, Zhang Yu could be said to hold both wealth and influence, yet he had still encountered difficulties in the process of purchasing this cultivation art.

This was solely because Celestial Monster Dharma Body was a cultivation art sold to outsiders by the Celestial Monster Sect.

Furthermore, because the purchase of such Heaven-Opening-Tier cultivation arts involved Immortal Dao Technology that was overwhelmingly powerful, as well as an astronomical amount of circulating capital, the complexity of the entire transaction process was no less intricate than a corporate merger or corporate reorganization.

If it involved a foreign sect, approvals from both the buyer's and seller's sects were often required, making the process all the more convoluted.

From initial communications to negotiations and approvals, and then to delivery, inspection, and payment transfer... It was entirely normal for the process to take weeks, or even months.

There were even historical instances where unforeseen complications caused transactions to drag on for several years.

Yet this time, when Zhang Yu expressed his desire to purchase, the Celestial Monster Sect did not agree right away; instead, they chose to criticize.

"Your Highness Zhang Yu, our sect has heard that widespread monsterfolk discrimination has emerged in the Myriad Laws Sect ever since you came to power."

"We suspect that large-scale persecution of monsterfolk is occurring within your sect's territory."

"We cannot sell the Celestial Monster Sect's technology to a sect that persecutes monsterfolk, a place lacking Monster Rights."

At first, Zhang Yu sent his trusted chief attendant, Zhao Tianxing, to negotiate and present their defense to the Celestial Monster Sect. But every single time, according to Zhao Tianxing's reports, the monsterfolk on the Celestial Monster Sect's side would accuse such-and-such company under the Myriad Laws Sect of fostering human-monster inequality, demanding that said company, enterprise, or even region make rectifications.

They even demanded sanctions on the relevant Myriad Laws Sect enterprises, claiming that in order to prevent forced labor among monsterfolk, all Myriad Laws Sect projects involving monsters had to accept the guidance of the Monsterfolk Ethics Committee.

"The Ethics Committee will dispatch experts to conduct audits and score the enterprises. All expenses incurred by the experts during this period will be borne by the audited enterprise, and only after receiving a passing score may the enterprise proceed with compliant operations."

At that moment, Zhao Tianxing reported once more, "Crown Prince, those negotiators from the Celestial Monster Sect are showing no sincerity whatsoever. They're making exorbitant demands, and those monsterfolk even want to meddle in our Myriad Laws Sect's internal affairs."

Zhang Yu sighed inwardly. *"Those bastards from the Celestial Monster Sect... seeing my status as the Crown Prince of Myriad Laws and knowing I'm eager to buy this cultivation art, they take me for a fat sheep ripe for the slaughter."*

Zhang Yu knew that the more critical the moment, the calmer he had to be. If he showed any impatience, they would only jack up the price further.

Meanwhile, Zhang Pianpian and Zhang Yu had nearly completed their preparations.

Suddenly, an earth-shaking roar erupted across the entire 11th to 19th layers of the Spirit Realm.

It was Zhang Yu and his multitude of clones, directly activating the power of the Great Sage's Heir. Clone after clone unleashed a resounding call throughout the 11th to 19th layers of the Spirit Realm.

In an instant, a terrifying tempest seemed to sweep through the Spirit Realm. Countless cultivators sensed with shock the successive roars rising from every corner of the Spirit Realm, their hearts filled with doubt and bewilderment.

It was at this precise moment that a strand of Zhang Yu's intent similarly transformed into Great Sage's Intent. Under Zhang Pianpian's disguise, it slipped into the divine power network of the Righteous God and plunged all the way down toward the 11th Layer.

Zhang Pianpian's voice rang out, "Desolate Ox Great Sage's collective will has constantly been drifting at random throughout the entire Spirit Realm. This was a protective measure established by the ten Immortal Emperors back then."

"According to my observations and calculations, the next direction it will wander toward is the 11th layer."

As she spoke, Zhang Yu felt his intent surging through the ocean of the Spirit Realm, gradually picking up a familiar intent amidst the vast sea of chaotic information.

*"Is that... where the Desolate Ox Great Sage's sea of consciousness is located, the position of Desolate Ox's collective will?"*

In Zhang Yu's perception, countless strands of Great Sage's Intent converged before him, resembling an incomparably colossal planet formed within the Spirit Realm.

Yet just as Zhang Yu was about to make contact with this planet, he sensed two phantom silhouettes — one of a chicken and one of a duck — emerging around its exterior.

"Audacious! Who permitted you to trespass into this restricted zone?!"

Zhang Yu swept his gaze across these two projections, relevant information instantly surfacing in his mind. *"Ascension Realm cultivators of the Celestial Monster Sect?"*

Zhang Yu knew they were guarding this place to prevent anyone from breaking into the Desolate Ox's sea of consciousness, so he ignored them completely and flew right past them.

The two monsterfolk immediately launched an assault in the Spirit Realm to stop him, only to discover that Zhang Yu's mind seemed like an inherent part of the Spirit Realm itself, impossible to block.

Zhang Yu understood that this was the second ability of the Great Sage's Heir, which granted his mind all-knowing perception and rendered all manner of Spirit Realm restrictions completely useless.

Seeing Zhang Yu plunge into the planet formed by the Desolate Ox's sea of consciousness, the two Celestial Monster Cultivators immediately used advanced Fu Zhu to summon a god to stop him.

"Great God of Myriad Paths and Four Directions! Stop this person at once!"

The Great God of Myriad Paths and Four Directions replied, "He has the authority to enter the Restricted Zone; I have no power to stop him."

The chicken monster said angrily, "A mere member of the Human Race — what right does he have to enter our monster race's sacred ground?"

The duck monster grumbled in annoyance, "Hmph, you Human Race Righteous Gods are utterly unreliable! Kunxu is at a critical juncture of life and death, yet you still refuse to surrender your authority and let superior monsterfolk shoulder the great responsibility of leading the plan against the End."

"Once His Highness the Monster God takes control of the Spirit Realm and unifies the Eight Divisions, we'll replace all of you! We shall govern the Heavenly Court and usher in a golden age for the Monster Dao once more."

On the other side, Zhang Yu's mind suddenly surged into the planet before him.

When he came to his senses again, he found himself standing on a wide street. Bustling crowds moved along both sides, while rows of soaring skyscrapers stretched into the distance.

*"Where is this..."*

*"Did I transmigrate again?"*

As Zhang Yu stood slightly bewildered by the bustling modern metropolis before him, a hand clapped down on his shoulder.

Another young man — looking almost identical to him, yet carrying a faint air of wicked malice — was watching him. He spoke up, "This should be the Desolate Ox Great Sage's subconscious world. All of his collective wills have manifested into individual lives here, living in this realm."

At the same time, another voice chimed in, "Could this be considered a virtual world? Or perhaps a dream? The Desolate Ox Great Sage has already fallen into a slumber, and his collective will mistakes this dream for reality."

Zhang Yu turned to look and found that the speaker was none other than Immortal Slayer.

And standing beside Immortal Slayer was a monsterfolk who bore a slight resemblance to him, yet manifested as an ox monster: "I'm afraid it was the doing of the ten Immortal Emperors. They put the Desolate Ox Great Sage into a deep sleep, shattering his collective will within this dream so he could never awaken, remaining forever as the foundation of the Spirit Realm."

"And multiple consciousnesses like ours have likewise been split apart here, becoming individual entities."

Just as the four Zhang Yus were discussing their current situation, another voice rang out: "Zhang... Yu? Why are there four Zhang Yus?"

Zhang Yu whipped his head around and found a tattered ragdoll holding a carrot, a seven-colored slime, and a doll wrapped in layers of newspaper standing not far from them.

They were none other than the three Evil Gods: Fu Ji, Auspicious God, and Herald God.

Zhang Yu asked in surprise, "How did you three get here?"

Fu Ji asked in confusion, "I don't know. By the time we realized it, we were already here."

Looking at the world before him, Herald God thought to himself, *"A virtual world constructed purely from a dream? Then here... how is strength determined?"*

After a round of discussion, the crowd reached a conclusion.

Herald God analyzed, "I suspect it's because we were occupying Your Highness the Crown Prince's Spirit Realm Communication Channel to deliver messages and handle official business for you. That Desolate Ox must have mistaken us for your multiple consciousnesses and dragged us all along."

Zhang Yu nodded slightly. "It's good that you're here, too. You can help me deal with our current situation."

"The priority right now is to figure out the specifics of this world, and what that group of monster cultivators sent by the Celestial Monster Immortal Emperor has been doing..."

Just as Zhang Yu was speaking, the Sect Examination Clone chuckled and said, "What else could they be doing? In this Great Harmony World within the Desolate Ox's dream, they're exporting extremist ideology, using identity politics to win the approval of the majority's collective will, and brainwashing the Desolate Ox's collective will into the shape they need to achieve total control."

Zhang Yu looked at him in surprise. "How do you know that?"

Pointing toward a large screen not far away, the Sect Examination Clone said with utter disdain, "The stench is so overpowering... Anyone who's taken the Sect Examination Written Test could smell it a mile away."

Zhang Yu turned his head and saw a dragon monster shouting on the big screen: "Mankind's persecution of monsterfolk has lasted for countless years! Monsterfolk have always suffered under human oppression, treated like livestock and used as mere tools!"

"Now, although our society has progressed, we still need systematic compensation to comprehensively elevate the status of monsterfolk! To right a wrong, one must bend in the opposite direction; without overcompensating, the injustice cannot be redressed!"

"The original sins of the human race must be thoroughly settled! Monsterfolk must take priority in everything, and the proportion of monsterfolk in departments at all levels must reach over ninety percent..."

Along with the speech on the big screen, countless monsterfolk on the street erupted into cheers.

Only then did Zhang Yu realize that nearly all the living beings he could see in this world seemed to be monsterfolk.

Meanwhile, the few humans he could spot were walking cautiously along the curbs amidst the shouts of the monsterfolk, looking as if they were terrified of offending the monsterfolk around them.

At the same time, the voice of the Sect Examination Clone sounded once more: "Also, Zhang Yu, now that we're in a world where collective will and multiple consciousnesses can be separated, I think you should show a little more respect for our thoughts as well."
