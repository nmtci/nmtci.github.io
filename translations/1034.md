# Chapter 1034 Divine Children Compete for the Throne

Zhang Yu, who was in the middle of contacting Zhang Pianpian, immediately focused his gaze upon hearing the words of the Great God of Sentient Creation: "What happened?"

The next moment, Zhang Yu and the Great God of Sentient Creation had already begun rapidly exchanging information through the Spirit Realm.

The Great God of Sentient Creation said, "Zhang Pianpian — she has now been suppressed by the Great God of Heavenly Covenant and Equal Contract."

Zhang Yu's heart sank. He knew that the so-called Great God of Heavenly Covenant and Equal Contract was a Great God of the law department in the Heavenly Court.

The countless contracts throughout Kunxu supervised and enforced by the Righteous Gods were, in theory, under the jurisdiction of this Great God of Heavenly Covenant and Equal Contract.

Thinking that his elder sister had actually been suppressed by the other party, a trace of worry could not help but well up in Zhang Yu's heart.

He recalled the plan he and Zhang Pianpian had made previously: *"Before this, I was discussing with Elder Sister about using the Spirit Realm Dao Lineage to restrict the Great Gods and gradually seize control over the authority of the Heavenly Court's Eight Divisions."*

*"Now that the Great God of Heavenly Covenant and Equal Contract has suddenly struck, and the Great God of Sentient Creation says that the Divine Emperor is about to revive, something must have gone wrong in the Heavenly Court."*

At the same time, Zhang Yu realized another point: the fact that the Great God of Sentient Creation before him had come to tip him off already demonstrated where the deity's inclinations lay.

Indeed, the Great God of Sentient Creation did not leave Zhang Yu wondering for long, speaking directly, "The root cause of the matter should be Immortal Venerable Heavenly Fortune. He secretly utilized the Destiny Dao Lineage to interfere with the fates of numerous Divine Children, binding and gathering them together, which accelerated the Divine Emperor's revival."

A thought stirred in Zhang Yu's heart, recalling what Zhang Pianpian had discussed with him during this period.

Zhang Yu said, "By devouring one another, the Divine Children can gradually become the Divine Emperor Vessel."

The Great God of Sentient Creation nodded and said, "Correct. Although the so-called Divine Emperor Vessel must bear immense side effects, they can become the agent of the Divine Emperor, wielding the Divine Emperor's power and authority."

"However, the Divine Emperor himself was turned into an Immortal Artifact by the ten Immortal Emperors long ago, and he lost his own consciousness just as long ago. The so-called revival is merely the revival of the Divine Emperor's power and authority."

"Therefore, this process of screening the Divine Children merely follows the Divine Emperor's instincts, passively operating over the years. In the past, when the ten Immortal Emperors were still around, a true Divine Emperor Vessel was never born, nor did any Divine Child ever grasp the power and authority of the Divine Emperor."

"And now, Immortal Venerable Heavenly Fortune is doing this precisely to create a Divine Emperor Vessel through the Divine Child ritual, seizing the Divine Emperor's power and authority to ultimately command the Heavenly Court."

"However, an accident occurred during the process, causing the entire revival ritual to undergo a mutation."

The Great God of Sentient Creation sighed. "Judging from the clues I have investigated so far, I'm afraid this was a contingency plan secretly set up by the Reincarnation Immortal Emperor before he departed."

Hearing this, Zhang Yu began recalling vast amounts of information regarding the Reincarnation Immortal Emperor.

Immortal Slayer thought inwardly, *"The numerous Evil Gods under the Reincarnation Immortal Emperor's command belong to the previous generation of old gods. Moreover, his own Reincarnation Dao Lineage directly conflicts with the process by which the Heavenly Court Righteous Gods guide Soul Cultivators."*

The Sect Examination Clone said, "If you say the Reincarnation Immortal Emperor has designs on the Heavenly Court, that is indeed very likely. That fellow might want the Evil Gods to take charge of the Heavenly Court, or even sever the Heavenly Court Righteous Gods' interference in the cycle of life and death. That way, when people die in the future, they would simply stay dead rather than entering the Spirit Realm, which would further accelerate reincarnation."

Having said that, the Sect Examination Clone stated resolutely, "No, whether it's Heavenly Fortune or Reincarnation, they are all devoid of virtue. How could the Heavenly Court ever be left in the hands of such parasites? We must be the ones in control for anyone to rest easy."

Fu Ji thought to herself, *"Us taking charge of the Heavenly Court? That's not out of the question either. If Zhang Yu takes over the Heavenly Court, I won't ask for much — just let me run the Tax and Revenue Department."*

While Zhang Yu pondered this in his heart, the Great God of Sentient Creation continued, "After the revival ritual underwent an anomaly, the original process of the Divine Children devouring each other was paused, and another kind of... screening mode has commenced instead."

"At the same time, the Heavenly Court has become a sealed space; currently, only the numerous Divine Children are inside."

"And now, all the Divine Children who have stepped inside the Heavenly Court have been enveloped within a special field."

"We have named this field the Morality Field. All Divine Children within the Morality Field will receive the Divine Emperor's empowerment according to their Morality Points, obtaining immense divine power. Ultimately, they will share in the Divine Emperor's authority and seize power over the Heavenly Court based on their Morality Points."

*"Morality Field?"* Zhang Yu thought to himself. *"After the ritual mutated, instead of devouring each other, the Morality Points determine how much authority over the Heavenly Court one possesses? Why does this sound... a bit like something Heavenly Virtue Immortal Emperor would cook up?"*

The Great God of Sentient Creation nodded and said, "After the mutation of the ritual and the termination of the devouring process, judging by the current new rules, it seems likely that once the Divine Emperor revives, the ten Divine Children with the highest Morality Points will share the Divine Emperor's authority and power. They will be able to vote together to decide the direction of the Heavenly Court and how the Divine Emperor's power is used."

Hearing this, Zhang Yu secretly frowned. *"Ten Divine Children sharing the Divine Emperor's authority? Doesn't this completely fracture the power that originally allowed the Divine Emperor to rule the Heavenly Court alone?"*

The Sect Examination Clone thought irritably, *"Ruling the Heavenly Court should be our right! Now it has to be split among ten people?"*

Zhang Yu asked again, "What is the specific situation inside this Morality Field right now?"

The Great God of Sentient Creation continued, "According to the intelligence I currently possess, within this Morality Field, the higher one's Morality Points, the stronger their Virtue Pressure. Those with high Morality Points can easily release Virtue Pressure to suppress those with low Morality Points."

"And when those with low Virtue Points face those with high Virtue Points, it is like looking up at a moral high ground; no matter what methods they exhaust, they can hardly harm the other party in the slightest."

Listening to all this, Zhang Yu asked in surprise, "In today's Kunxu, are there still people with high Morality Points among these Divine Children?"

The Great God of Sentient Creation said, "At the beginning, their Morality Scores were all 0, but after figuring out some of the patterns, they gradually mastered how to increase their Morality Points."

"Some activated the Ancient Person Simulator to farm Morality Points. Some farmed Morality Points by making donations. Others farmed points by delivering Soul Cultivators into the afterlife, or by releasing monsterfolk..."

As a witness to history, the Great God of Sentient Creation was not at all surprised by this, saying, "Crown Prince, you should understand that for Kunxu Cultivators of the new era, figuring out performance rules, finding loopholes in those rules, improving performance, and pursuing the limits of efficiency... this is their everyday routine, as well as a survival instinct honed over generations under the influence of their Immortal Dao Aspiration."

"Judging by their Morality Points, the Divine Children in the Morality Field right now seem more morally upright than the last, each outdoing the other in claiming the moral high ground. Every single one of them appears to be a paragon of virtue, a great philanthropist."

"Yet this is merely the surface shown by their Morality Points. In truth... after adapting, the Divine Children have gradually developed a code of conduct based solely on Morality Points, ushering in a survival model where the early moral accumulators exploit those who lag behind."

"Those with low virtue points have steadily become tools for high scorers to farm and siphon points from. If this continues, those who fell behind at the start might remain trapped at the bottom with low Morality Points for the rest of their lives."

Listening to the description from the Great God of Sentient Creation, Zhang Yu could already picture the situation inside the Morality Field. Having spent so many years in Kunxu, he understood all too well what cultivators of this era were really like.

There was nothing in this world they couldn't turn into a hyper-competitive rat race. Whether it was morality, work hours, grades, or cultivation, to them it was simply switching from one metric to another.

Zhang Yu thought to himself, *"When morality is tied to the power of the Divine Emperor and the Heavenly Court Authority, they'll inevitably take the rat race to absolute extremes."*

The Sect Examination Clone chuckled and said, "Goes without saying, doesn't it? Right now, every Divine Child in the Morality Field has likely adapted at lightning speed, taking point-farming as their solemn duty and treating anyone with a low score like garbage. It's all about how high-virtue people are supreme, low-virtue people are dregs, and how the low ones deserve to be fiercely condemned under the banner of punishing evil and promoting good...

"Moreover, good deeds that award points can only be performed by those with high virtue scores. The gap between rich and poor — no, the moral gap will only grow wider and wider. The virtuous will rise higher and higher, while the lowly will sink further and further. In the end... I'm afraid every single good deed that could raise one's score will be steadily monopolized."

At this moment, Zhang Yu could almost see it: the low-scoring Divine Children in the Morality Field had already plummeted into a Morality Lowland, becoming Virtue Scum at the bottom of the Morality Chain, berated by the high-scoring Divine Children: "You Virtue Scum! You don't deserve to do good deeds alongside us at all! Good deeds are only meant for good people!"

While Zhang Yu was lost in thought, the Great God of Sentient Creation continued, "And this situation quickly alarmed the Immortals of the Great Sects as well.

"Whether it was the Divine Emperor's revival ritual, the emergence of the Morality Field, or the Divine Children vying for Heavenly Court authority using Morality Points, it was impossible for the Immortals to simply sit by and watch."

"Right now, the Immortals of the Ten Great Sects have descended with their power, completely surrounding the entire Heavenly Court."

"At the same time, they have begun seizing control of the Divine Children who have not yet entered the Heavenly Court, manipulating them and sending them into the Morality Field to join the competition."

"With the support of the Immortals, once these Divine Children are deployed inside, their Morality Points increase rapidly. I fear they will ultimately become the top ten in Morality Points, becoming the ten individuals who wield the supreme authority of the Heavenly Court."

Zhang Yu nodded slightly, already able to imagine the scene both inside and outside the Heavenly Court at this moment.

*"I suspect that right now, the Divine Children are competing and racking up points inside the Morality Field to vie for the Divine Emperor's power and authority."*

*"Meanwhile, the Immortals outside are negotiating the ranks of the Divine Children and interfering with the competition within, carving up the power of the Heavenly Court in advance."*

Just as thoughts flashed through Zhang Yu's mind, the Great God of Sentient Creation said, "As for Zhang Pianpian, the reason she was suppressed by the Great God of Heavenly Covenant and Equal Contract is that Immortal Venerable Heavenly Fortune and his faction do not want any of your people to participate in the competition."

Hearing this, Zhang Yu understood clearly and said, "It seems the Great God of Heavenly Covenant and Equal Contract has chosen Immortal Venerable Heavenly Fortune and the others."

Zhang Yu understood in his heart that after this round of competition among the Divine Children, they would jointly hold the supreme authority of the Heavenly Court in the future, while the Immortals would control the Divine Children from behind the scenes. In this way, the Immortal Class of the Ten Great Sects could replace the past ten Immortal Emperors and become the supreme rulers of the Heavenly Court.

Meanwhile, the Eight Divisions Great Gods, bound by the Heavenly Court, were likely also seeking out their own future backers at this moment.

Thinking of this, Zhang Yu could not help but look at the Great God of Sentient Creation before him.

"Great God of Sentient Creation," Zhang Yu asked, "Right now, the Immortals of the Ten Great Sects have surrounded the Heavenly Court, and Zhang Pianpian has already been suppressed by the Great God of Heavenly Covenant and Equal Contract. Yet I did not even receive any news of this. It shows that the Immortals have no intention of letting me participate, nor are they the least bit willing to share the Heavenly Court's power with me. Under such circumstances, why... did you still come to find me?"

The Great God of Sentient Creation said, "The Immortal Class moves exceptionally fast, especially when it comes to major matters of self-interest. In truth... the Immortals of the Ten Great Sects have only just begun sealing off the perimeter of the Heavenly Court. Even if I hadn't told you, given your control over the Spirit Realm, it wouldn't have taken long for you to find out."

"As for Kunxu's current Immortal Class, in my view, if they were capable of rebelling against the ten Immortal Emperors, they would have done so long ago."

"In fact, before the Ultimate Doomsday, I don't believe they can succeed."

At this moment, the Great God of Sentient Creation looked at Zhang Yu, his gaze seemingly piercing through the layers of the divine power network to see the classified information regarding Zhang Yu: Paragon of Virtue.

And the Great God of Sentient Creation understood even better that this piece of classified information was likely known only to him — the Chief Great God of the Ministry of All People — along with the long-departed Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor and Heavenly Sword Immortal Emperor.

The Great God of Sentient Creation said calmly, "Compared to the Immortal Class, I actually see a greater glimmer of hope in you, Your Highness the Crown Prince."

Zhang Yu said, "Then I wonder if the Great God of Sentient Creation would be willing to accompany me on a tour of the Heavenly Court?"

The Great God of Sentient Creation smiled faintly and said, "It would be an honor."

Meanwhile, on the 29th layer of Kunxu.

Layers 30 through 36 of Kunxu were the Immortal Emperor Domain. And atop the 29th layer of Kunxu lay the pinnacle of Kunxu outside the realm of the Immortal Emperors, as well as the location of the Heavenly Court.

At this moment, deep within the Heavenly Court, Zhang Pianpian felt her thoughts drifting, as if on the verge of scattering with the wind and sinking into an abyss.

Right at that moment, a voice sounded deep within her mind.

"Zhang Pianpian, wake up."

Zhang Pianpian snapped her eyes open and discovered she was actually sitting cross-legged in the Supreme Clarity Realm.

"I remember I was..."

Just as shock gripped Zhang Pianpian's heart, a voice came from the side: "Your physical body was suppressed by a Great God, but I preserved your divine soul."

Zhang Pianpian whipped her head around and saw a figure standing with hands clasped behind his back, facing away from her as he surveyed the surroundings of the Supreme Clarity Realm.

On high alert, Zhang Pianpian asked in her heart, "Who are you?"

The person said faintly, "You need not concern yourself with who I am. I merely overheard your conversation with the Great God of Heavenly Covenant and Equal Contract — you mentioned Zhang Yu, did you not? How well do you know this Zhang Yu?"

Seeing that Zhang Pianpian remained silent, the figure scoffed dismissively, "Who do you take this seat to be? Do you think that asking you about a mere junior means I intend to harm him? Someone has simply been hyping this Zhang Yu up to me, praising him as if he were unique under the heavens and unmatched across the earth. I merely wish to see what makes him so extraordinary."

Hearing this, Zhang Pianpian felt a wave of oddity.

After all, Zhang Yu was renowned throughout the world these days. Anyone could just log into the Spirit Realm and gather mountains of information on him, so why would anyone need to ask around?

Just as Zhang Pianpian was lost in thought, the figure gave a low shout and said, "Junior, I am questioning you in person today. Why haven't you spoken the truth at once?"

Zhang Pianpian felt as though her consciousness were slipping out of her control, and everything she knew and thought in her heart poured out at that moment: "Zhang Yu... climbed his way up from the first layer of Kunxu..."

Listening to Zhang Yu's experiences, the figure said nonchalantly, "What sacrifices has he made, then? Does he have any moving deeds?"

Hearing this, Zhang Pianpian paused slightly in her mind. This was truly the first time anyone had ever asked her such a question.

Her thoughts racing like lightning, Zhang Pianpian said, "He raised everyone's salaries..."

Hearing this, the figure sneered disdainfully, "Raised salaries? Does that even count as something noteworthy? What else?"

Zhang Pianpian said, "He kept the high-ranking cultivators in check and dared to confront Immortals..."

The figure shook his head again. "Dared to be outspoken? That's decent enough. From what you just said, he made a lot of money. Has he ever contributed any of it? Has he ever donated to charity?"

Zhang Pianpian paused again upon hearing this, then continued, "How could that be possible? Knowing him as I do, he wouldn't donate a single cent in his entire life."

The figure's tone darkened as he asked, "Then does he indulge in women?"

A flicker of gratification and pride in her brother flashed through Zhang Pianpian's eyes as she said, "He's a renowned dual cultivation genius in the Myriad Laws Sect. As for whether his partners are mostly men or women, I'm not really sure."

Hearing this, the figure's tone grew even heavier. "Listening to what you just said about his past, he clung to his teachers' coattails in university, clung to an Immortal Clan noblewoman's coattails in the sect, and later clung to the Immortal Emperor's coattails. Has he spent his entire journey looking for benefactors?"

Zhang Pianpian spoke her mind directly, saying with a hint of admiration, "Zhang Yu admires strength. He's also the kind of person who is exceptionally skilled at upward networking, mingling with the powerful, and finding people to lift him up."

The figure snorted coldly upon hearing this. "To think he's merely a bootlicker. And here I thought this Zhang Yu would be a man of noble character and high prestige, but he turns out to be such a disappointment."

Zhang Pianpian said, "Senior, you might not fully understand the present era. In this day and age, Zhang Yu is already a rare good leader, a good boss, and a good partner."

The figure spat with disdain, "Even if human morals have decayed and world ethics have fallen to rock bottom, could they really drop this low? To allow a sycophantic, dual-cultivation-addicted, completely penny-pinching miser to ascend the supreme throne?? I will never permit a creature like this to defile the Heavenly Court. I'm going to choose someone actually worthy."
