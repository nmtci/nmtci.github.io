# Chapter 1035: Zhang Yu, the Conscience of Kunxu?

Listening to what the figure said, Zhang Pianpian shook her head, a deep look of disbelief showing in her eyes.

Zhang Pianpian said, "Senior, if possible, you could try browsing the Spirit Realm to observe the current state of the entire Kunxu. Then you would understand."

Curious, the figure asked, "What is the Spirit Realm? Has someone opened up a new realm in this world?"

A flicker of surprise flashed in Zhang Pianpian's eyes. Although she had already guessed that he might not belong to this era — or might even be an unregistered living dead whose account had been deleted, suffering informational and social death and unable to access the Spirit Realm for information — she was still taken aback that he had no concept of the Spirit Realm at all.

"The so-called Spirit Realm is..."

Zhang Pianpian explained, trying to use the simplest, most comprehensible, and most accessible terms to help him grasp what the Spirit Realm truly was.

After listening to Zhang Pianpian's explanation, the figure said thoughtfully, "Oh? To be able to gather all things in the heavens, the earth, and the world on an informational level... this is a powerful instrument that can vastly improve the efficiency of governing the realm and allow an Immortal Emperor to instill his will into every corner."

"Yet this Spirit Realm Dao Lineage is actually being used to restrict the Righteous Gods? No, it should be said that they restrict and bind each other."

"Hmph, the powerhouse who originally designed this architecture probably wanted these two Great Dao Lineages to check and balance each other, making them easy to manipulate without expending much effort. Truly unwilling to invest even a single bit of extra cost — it is exquisite to the extreme, yet also petty to the extreme."

Listening to the figure's analysis and assessment of the Spirit Realm, Zhang Pianpian suddenly asked, "Senior, since you aren't even familiar with the Spirit Realm, how did you bring my Divine Soul into this Supreme Clarity Realm?"

In Zhang Pianpian's view, the Supreme Clarity Realm lay in the depths of the Spirit Realm, reachable only by passing through the Spirit Realm. If the other party didn't even know of the Spirit Realm, how had he crossed it to descend upon the Supreme Clarity Realm?

The figure replied indifferently, unconcerned, "I did not connect you to the Spirit Realm. I merely brought you directly here.

"It is just that the Supreme Clarity Realm is one of the Divine Court's hubs. Now that the Righteous God System has merged with the Spirit Realm, the Supreme Clarity Realm has naturally been moved into the Spirit Realm as well."

Hearing this, Zhang Pianpian was astonished in her heart. *"Arriving directly at the Supreme Clarity Realm without going through the Spirit Realm?"*

To Zhang Pianpian, this was like accessing a secret website deep within the network without ever connecting to the internet.

Even as the figure spoke, Zhang Pianpian felt a surge of information from the surrounding Spirit Realm.

The figure continued, "Though I have no such thing as your Spirit Realm accounts, since the Supreme Clarity Realm is linked to the Spirit Realm, I shall borrow this Supreme Clarity Realm of yours to take a stroll with me."

In an instant, Zhang Pianpian felt the entire world within the Supreme Clarity Realm undergo a drastic shift, everything beginning to turn transparent and intangible.

A moment later, scenes from the material world gradually emerged.

*"This is... the Heavenly Court?"* Zhang Pianpian thought in shock. *"Is he directly extracting information from the Heavenly Court? And projecting it here?"*

Zhang Pianpian could feel that their consciousnesses still remained within the Supreme Clarity Realm, yet the projection before their eyes presented scenes of the Heavenly Court, making it feel as though they were suddenly wandering through its grounds.

Zhang Pianpian cautioned, "Senior, the Immortals of the Ten Great Sects have currently blockaded the Heavenly Court. Every movement within and without is screened by countless individuals. Won't extracting information like this get us discovered?"

The figure replied faintly, "No matter. Anywhere else might be an issue, but here... if I do not wish for them to see us, they cannot perceive us."

With a slight wave of the figure's right hand, the light and shadows before them shifted rapidly, giving Zhang Pianpian the sensation that they were moving at high speed through the Heavenly Court.

The next moment, the figure chuckled. "And you said times have changed? That actions like Zhang Yu's are as rare as phoenix feathers and unicorn horns in this world?"

Pointing in a certain direction, he said, "Look, this person already has a hundred thousand Merit to his name. In my view, his virtue surpasses Zhang Yu's; he is a first-rate philanthropist of this world, far more fit to bear great responsibilities than Zhang Yu."

Zhang Pianpian looked over at his words, immediately learning the other party's identity through the Spirit Realm information that surfaced before her eyes.

"A Divine Child from the Netherworld Palace?"

At the same time, Zhang Pianpian noticed she could see a number hovering above his head that was slowly climbing, gradually rising from 100,000 to 110,000.

"Is this the so-called Merit? Or rather, Morality Points?"

Beside her, the figure remarked with feeling, "Is he performing Salvation on wandering souls? Not bad, not bad at all. This Spirit Realm of yours is quite marvelous — able to be used to manage wandering souls, it provides tremendous aid in maintaining the order of the mortal world."

Yet in Zhang Pianpian's observation, a vast multitude of Soul Cultivators were continuously converging into the Divine Child's Bionic, only to melt away like ice and snow within a burst of white light.

Zhang Pianpian said in shock, "He's killing all those Soul Cultivators? How can that count as an act of virtue?"

The figure beside her waved a hand and said, "How can you call this killing? Becoming ghosts after death was originally a Dao Lineage meddlesomely added by some old fogeys, which led to ghostly spirits running rampant between heaven and earth, sparking countless catastrophes."

"Now, someone is sending them into Samsara to be swiftly reincarnated and live another life, maintaining the balance and stability between heaven and earth. Is that not an act of merit?"

Hearing the figure's words, Zhang Pianpian explained, "But I took a look. These soul cultivators were lured here through fake job recruitment interviews. They were killed the moment they arrived, and they haven't even finished spending the money in their accounts..."

Listening to the thoughts conveyed by Zhang Pianpian's mind, the figure wondered with curiosity, "Recruitment?"

And so, Zhang Pianpian began to describe the current ecosystem of Soul Cultivators in this day and age. After hearing it, the figure's thoughts seemed to stall for a moment.

What did she mean, people of Kunxu nowadays still had to pay off debts even after dying? They still had to work? They could still earn money? They could even extend their lifespans? They could still cultivate? They could still make breakthroughs?

Then what was the difference between that and being alive?

At this moment, having gradually adapted to the Spirit Realm, the figure also began to perceive more details clearly.

Countless Soul Cultivators had crossed into the Spirit Realm and arrived at the Heavenly Court, their faces brimming with joy and excitement.

"Awesome! A job with a 3% pay raise actually exists!"

"The workplace is actually on the 29th layer of the Heavenly Court? This is the first time in my life I've ever been to such a high layer."

"As expected of the Heavenly Court, the immortal aura is surging sky-high! Is this really our work environment?"

Yet in the next moment, as the white light surged upward, countless Soul Cultivators rapidly dissolved, reaching the final moments of their lives.

"Ah! Weren't we here to work? Why can't I see anything anymore?"

"Why did my Spirit Realm signal drop? No, don't do this! I can't reach the interviewer, I'm going to miss my interview!"

"Where is this place? I can't even open my overdue debt notifications!"

Sensing the flood of information bursting forth from those countless Soul Cultivators right before their demise, the figure beside Zhang Pianpian noticed that he didn't feel the serene aura that should accompany salvation from these ghosts and demons. Instead, they were filled with resentment, panic, and utter bewilderment...

Zhang Pianpian said, "In our era, he's simply tricking people into coming here just to slaughter them. Can this even be considered Merit?"

The figure frowned and said, "This... is likely a gap between ancient and modern moral concepts. Ancient ghosts and demons needed salvation, but your new era... is a bit different."

The figure continued, "The real issue is, once this metric for Morality Points was implemented, did no one adjust it to keep up with the times? What are the Righteous Gods doing these days?"

"Still, generally speaking, reincarnation isn't necessarily a bad thing for them. Lingering in the mortal realm as a yin spirit is hardly a long-term solution anyway."

Zhang Pianpian remarked, "They don't have enough savings, so they won't get a good reincarnation. They'll probably never see an income as high as what they had as ghosts again."

In Zhang Pianpian's view, a Soul Cultivator who could be lured to the 29th layer by job scams must have originally worked on at least the 10th layer, earning Immortal Coins. But after reincarnating like this, they would likely have to live in the lower realm in their next life, earning Spirit Coins. The disparity in income between this life and the next was a gap they might never close after reincarnating.

The figure beside her asked in bewilderment, "What do you mean?"

So Zhang Pianpian immediately explained the latest Reincarnation System, the segregation between the sect layers and the lower realm, as well as the details regarding Spirit Coins and Immortal Coins.

Sensing the vast amount of information conveyed through Zhang Pianpian's thoughts, the figure murmured, "Reincarnation depends on money now?"

Zhang Pianpian replied, "If not money, then what else? The amount of money one has is the gold standard that best reflects a cultivator's value, achievements, and contributions to Kunxu."

The figure said indignantly, "Shouldn't it be based on Merit?"

Zhang Pianpian said, "Well... wouldn't that still come down to money in the end? People with money can definitely grind out more Merit."

The figure paused slightly. If anyone had described the new era like this to him in the past, he would have definitely said, "There is no such future at all." But looking at the Heavenly Court at this moment and Zhang Pianpian before him, the figure suddenly felt a trace of fear.

Right then, his gaze sharpened as he looked ahead.

He saw a bear monster striding forward with his head held high. A radiant aura of morality surged around him, with tens of thousands of Morality Points pouring in every moment, casting upon him the compassionate aura of an eminent monk who pitied all living beings.

Seeing this bear monster, the figure perked up, a glimmer of hope rising in his heart: "Tens of thousands of merit points increasing every second? Maybe there really are good people in this era after all."

However, this time he did not rush to make a judgment out loud, instead observing the other party carefully first.

The bear monster was contacting the Celestial Monster Sect: "Send another batch of monsterfolk! Send them all to the First Layer for release."

Opening his bloody maw, the bear monster spoke to the other end of the Spirit Realm, "You're asking me why the First Layer? You think I haven't researched the other places? From Kunxu's 1st to 29th layers, where can you even find a wild environment anymore? But then it hit me — a zoo is actually what resembles a wild environment the most."

The bear monster grinned. "And the First Layer is the most like a zoo, the farthest from civilization, and the best fit for the criteria of releasing captive animals."

"You're afraid the Righteous Gods won't permit illegal passage? Humph, the Immortals of the Ten Great Sects have already surrounded the Heavenly Court. In the future, the Heavenly Court won't be run by those Righteous Gods. Rest assured, they have no time to bother with such things right now."

"Those monsterfolk aren't willing to go down? Tell them there's a living allowance for business trips to the First Layer."

"You're worried about costs? Hahahaha! Once they reach the First Layer, just fire every single one of them. Won't that eliminate the cost? Besides, if we don't fire them, how could it count as releasing them into the wild?"

"Right, right, right. Just pick the employees who were going to be laid off anyway and prioritize them for release into the wild..."

Listening to this conversation from the bear monster, Zhang Pianpian wore an unfazed expression, having grown accustomed to such things, while the figure beside her furrowed his brows tightly.

With a wave of the figure's hand, the projection of the entire Heavenly Court shrank away.

At the same time, the avatars, locations, and Morality Points of various Divine Children surfaced.

Zhang Pianpian thought to herself, "Is he looking for other Divine Children with high Morality Points?"

The next moment, light and shadow flashed before Zhang Pianpian's eyes, revealing a group of Divine Children fleeing in panic.

"Oh no! It's Strange Daoist Merit Accumulator!"

Behind them, a Daoist was hot on their heels, the moral brilliance all over his body surging violently as an intense Virtue Pressure swept across them.

"Ah! Such intense Virtue Pressure! Just how much money did he spend? How many good deeds did he do?"

"I can't hold on anymore! In the presence of such a good person, I can't help but fall to my knees!"

"Oh no! Merit Accumulator wants to transfer money into my account! He wants to bestow favor upon me and unleash the Moral Binding of 'taking someone's hand makes one beholden'!"

"Damn it! If it weren't for this Morality Field making accepting money a moral burden, would I be afraid of him?"

Under the surging Virtue Pressure, the numerous Divine Children felt ashamed of their own unworthiness, one after another. Their cultivation bases were completely suppressed as they fell to the ground on their knees in succession.

Meanwhile, Strange Daoist Merit Accumulator chuckled and said, "Hehe, let me see what your Spirit Realm wallet account numbers are."

A Divine Child struggled, shouting, "No! Don't shower me with money!"

Yet Merit Accumulator was rapidly investigating the other party's Spirit Realm information. "Found your account! My massive funds are coming right in!"

"You Virtue Scum! The money has already been transferred to you! Now go and use my money to do good! From now on, all of your charitable and meritorious acts will be under my guidance, and a portion of the Morality Points from everything you do will be credited to me!"

Zhang Pianpian watched this scene, then glanced at the figure beside her, finding that the person's entire body seemed to grow increasingly pitch-black, making them impossible to see clearly.

Right at that moment, the soles of a pair of shoes appeared with a crash, directly transcending above all the Divine Children present, as well as Strange Daoist Merit Accumulator.

"This is..." Strange Daoist Merit Accumulator's expression changed abruptly. "It's the moral high ground! Someone's Virtue Pressure completely suppresses mine, forcing me to only ever see the soles of their feet when facing them, making it impossible to even look directly at their true form."

Hearing this, Zhang Pianpian looked curiously at the figure beside her. "There's such a thing as this Immortal Dao Technology?"

The figure replied awkwardly, "The moral high ground is meant to protect good people. In the past, we never unleashed it indiscriminately like they do."

At the same time, Merit Accumulator suddenly fell to his knees and threatened, "If you dare send me money, bestow grace upon me, and then morally exploit me, I'll blast all those with lower virtue than mine to death! I'll lose Merit right along with you — at worst, we go down together!"

Yet the very next moment, pair after pair of feet soles materialized before Merit Accumulator's eyes.

Simultaneously, ethereal voices rang out from atop the moral high ground, drifting into Merit Accumulator's ears as if from beyond the heavens.

"Exploiting the low-virtue demographic? We abandoned such low-level tactics long ago."

"Heh, the truly high-virtue luminaries proudly stand atop the pinnacle of the moral high ground, monopolizing the operations of moral capital. How could a Virtue Scum like you ever understand?"

Accompanied by the voices echoing from the moral high ground, pairs of soles materialized before his eyes, one after another.

Four, five, six...

Merit Accumulator counted silently in his heart, "Ten people? There are ten of them?"

At the same time, from the shadows cast upon the ground, Merit Accumulator could see that these ten individuals were holding hands, forming a circle.

Within the Spirit Realm, a massive torrent of Spirit Realm information could be felt circulating among the ten of them.

Pushing with all his might, Merit Accumulator exerted his Spirit Realm Techniques to their absolute peak, barely managing to catch a glimpse of countless funds flowing between these ten people.

It was an intensely intricate, mutually nested, and self-reinforcing network of interests.

Various corporations, industries, and even employees were interconnected within this web of interests.

*"They are... investing in one another, donating to one another? Party A donates to Party B's gambling foundation, Party B donates to Party C's medicinal pill addiction foundation, and Party C donates back to Party A's life-release foundation..."*

At this moment, though he had merely glimpsed the tip of the iceberg, Merit Accumulator felt that a colossal Merit Interest Network had already surfaced.

Most notably, behind every single one of these ten individuals lay the capital of the Ten Great Sects, cast under the shadows of the Immortals themselves.

*"Did the Immortals of the Ten Great Sects choose the final representatives?"*

*"Interconnected like capillaries, they formed a single entity, funneling money and farming merit among themselves, maxing out their Merit Points with unimaginable efficiency."*

*"Too virtuous! They are simply too virtuous! They don't even need to exploit Virtue Scum like us. As long as they keep us excluded, as long as the top ten moral capital giants remain united and maintain their monopoly, they can stand forever at the pinnacle of the moral high ground, forever looking down upon us."

Thinking of this, a bitter smile appeared on Merit Accumulator's face. *"It's over. It's all over. They have achieved the ultimate goal of this competition. The Moral Class has completely solidified, and they have become the ultimate arbitrators of virtue in this realm."*

*"All this talk of Morality Field judgments and doing good deeds — it was all just to toy with us!"* Merit Accumulator said indignantly. *"In the end, doesn't it just come down to whoever has the most money is the most virtuous? Whoever has the backing of the Immortal Class is the most moral?!"*

*"Why the hell didn't the Immortals choose me? In what way am I inferior to any of you?!"*

Meanwhile, Zhang Pianpian, who had been watching this unfold, continued to explain the underlying logic and context of how Kunxu operated beneath these surface appearances to the figure beside her.

And now, having truly gained some understanding of this new era, the figure murmured, "This is clearly a flourishing Demonic Path that has already come to rule all of Kunxu."

Zhang Pianpian denied it, saying, "The Righteous Path won the Great Righteous-Demonic War back then. What we have now is the Righteous Use of Demonic Arts, not a flourishing Demonic Path."

The figure beside her did not answer, but Zhang Pianpian felt its form grow increasingly profound and dark.

After a long silence, the figure said, "There is nothing wrong with moral assessments in themselves. It is the current Immortal Emperors who have corrupted the moral climate. What this world lacks is a leading figure to rectify the standards and oversee all under heaven."

In the next moment, the figure said faintly, "Tell me more about this Zhang Yu in detail."

And so, more information was exchanged between their minds.

The figure said in surprise, "When he was on the First Layer, just entering Foundation Establishment, he gave up a high salary, defied tyrannical power, and fought a bloody battle against the representative leader sent down from the Second Layer? Why didn't you mention this in detail before?"

Zhang Pianpian replied as if it were only natural, "The First Layer is of limited value. Compared to what Zhang Yu did later, it's not worth mentioning."

A moment later, the figure exclaimed in surprise again, "During the University War, he set off a Ten Billion Heaven Mending operation? Carried everyone's debts on his own shoulders? And even intervened in a Spirit Transformation Deathmatch while only at the Nascent Soul realm? Why didn't you explain these things in detail to me just now either?"

Zhang Pianpian said, "Compared to affairs within the sect, various events in the lower realm... have a relatively low impact on the Immortal Dao Production Value, so I naturally prioritized recounting matters inside the sect."

After reviewing more details about Zhang Yu, the figure sighed. "Looking at it this way, in this era, Zhang Yu truly is a rare conscience of Kunxu."

"Zhang Pianpian, given the current situation, do you think he will come to the Heavenly Court?"

Zhang Pianpian nodded and said resolutely, "Once he finds out something has happened to me, he will definitely come. He will find a way to save me."

The figure asked, "Why?"

Zhang Pianpian replied, "I believe in him. That's just the kind of person he is."

Although the figure did not speak, Zhang Pianpian sensed a shift in their mood.

So Zhang Pianpian said, "Senior, did you smile? Because of Zhang Yu?"

The figure said, "No, because of you. Because of you, who has been slightly changed by Zhang Yu. Perhaps this shows that this era... is not entirely beyond saving."

"Then let us wait for Zhang Yu to arrive. I want to see whether he has changed now that he occupies a high position and has become the Crown Prince of Myriad Laws."

Zhang Pianpian said, "Senior, you want to see how Zhang Yu performs in the Morality Field? But he isn't a Divine Child..."

The figure said indifferently, "No matter. Securing a spot for him in the Morality Field is still well within my power. I simply wish to see what his choice will be within the Morality Field."

Looking at the figure before her, Zhang Pianpian kept guessing at the other party's identity.

"Is it an ancient god from the Heavenly Court's past? Or a revived Divine Emperor? But hasn't the Divine Emperor already lost consciousness and become a pure Immortal Artifact?"

---

Meanwhile, outside the Heavenly Court.

At this moment, the Heavenly Court was wrapped in layers upon layers of formations, restrictions, and immortal powers; coming and going required the permission of Immortals.

And the will of numerous Immortals now hovered above the Heavenly Court, as if looking down at a sand table to discuss battle strategies.

Delving deep inside, one could sense the roaring resonance of countless streams of will and information, with Immortals engaging in ultra-high-frequency exchanges at every passing moment.

"No, the Ministry of All People should belong to our Netherworld Palace. Talent management is our domain."

"The Heavenly Court Law Department should naturally be controlled jointly by all sects. How can it be the sole say of your Myriad Laws Sect?"

"Among the Heavenly Court's Eight Divisions, all authority related to monsterfolk must take into account the opinions of the Celestial Monster Sect and hire monsterfolk consultants!"

"The Eight Divisions are far too few; break them up. For instance, the taxation of each sect cannot be managed by a single division."

"All of the current Righteous Gods have human appearances; we should cultivate some Monster Gods!"

Meanwhile, Immortal Roujia and Immortal Yi Xuan of the Reformist Faction were also taking part at this moment. Feeling the surges of divine Immortal wills, it felt as though they were watching the entire Heavenly Court being swallowed whole, flesh and bone, by the Immortals of the Ten Great Sects, ultimately to be dismantled into different organizations.

Immortal Roujia thought to herself, *"Since the Immortal Emperors are absent, it is only right that the Immortals manage this Heavenly Court."*

Right at that moment, a message surfaced before her.

The Crown Prince of Myriad Laws requested to join this meeting.

*"Hmm?"* Immortal Roujia sneered inwardly. *"Zhang Yu wants to attend this meeting too?"*

*"Does he even qualify?"*

---

Outside the venue.

Far away from where the myriad Immortal's wills gathered.

As soon as Zhang Yu's projection arrived, it was blocked by layers of divine power.

The projection of the Great God of Myriad Paths and Four Directions descended, looking at Zhang Yu's projection and saying, "Your Highness, only Immortals are qualified to attend this meeting. You cannot participate yet."

Zhang Yu understood clearly in his heart: the Immortals of the Ten Great Sects were dividing up the Heavenly Court and scrambling for power and profit, so how could they possibly allow him to join and take a slice of the pie?

In particular, none of the Great Sects had cultivators below the Immortal realm participating, making it even more impossible for him to break this tacit understanding. Looking at Zhang Yu's projection, the Great God of Myriad Paths and Four Directions thought to himself, *"He shouldn't be foolish enough to... use the Spirit Realm as a threat."*

The Great God of Myriad Paths and Four Directions knew well in his heart that when Zhang Yu used the Spirit Realm Dao Lineage to threaten the Immortals of the Myriad Laws Sect alone, the Immortals of the other nine Great Sects were merely happy to watch the show from the shadows, and would even secretly give it a few pushes.

It was precisely because of the presence of the other nine Great Sects that the Immortals of the Myriad Laws Sect were hesitant to act ruthlessly, ultimately conceding to Zhang Yu's threats.

However, if Zhang Yu dared to use the Spirit Realm Dao Lineage now to threaten all the Immortals in an attempt to partake in the division of the Heavenly Court, then perhaps... he would instantly be suppressed by the joint efforts of the Immortals from all Ten Great Sects.

"Understood." Zhang Yu nodded and said, "It is fine if I do not attend this meeting, but I would like to ask where Zhang Pianpian is."

With the division of the Heavenly Court imminent, Great Gods from all factions were seeking new backers.

Toward Zhang Yu, this Crown Prince of Myriad Laws, the Great God of Myriad Paths and Four Directions originally had no desire to get involved in his affairs, much less disclose any information to him.

Right at that moment, however, a message from the Great God of Sentient Creation appeared before his eyes: "Help him out. You won't regret it."

The Great God of Myriad Paths and Four Directions had always held considerable respect for this chief deity of the Ministry of All People, for presiding over that ministry made them one of the figures among the Eight Divisions Great Gods with access to the highest tier of privileged information.

Thus, the Great God of Myriad Paths and Four Directions replied: "You chose him? Why? Zhang Yu isn't even an Immortal, is he?"

The Great God of Sentient Creation replied: "Because he is kind."

Their exchange began and ended in an instant. The Great God of Myriad Paths and Four Directions said to Zhang Yu, "Zhang Pianpian... has been suppressed within the Heavenly Court by the Great God of Heavenly Covenant and Equal Contract."

As he spoke, the Great God of Myriad Paths and Four Directions projected a map of the Heavenly Court directly, marking the location where Zhang Pianpian was suppressed — right in the very center of the Heavenly Court.

The Great God of Myriad Paths and Four Directions cautioned, "However, the Heavenly Court is currently under total lockdown. Inside, the Divine Children are waging a war of morality, a conflict fraught with extreme peril. With Your Highness's current cultivation, recklessly intruding right now makes it exceedingly easy to be suppressed by the high-virtue partisans within."

"Your Highness, Crown Prince, it is best not to take such risks. Once matters settle down, there will be plenty of ways to find her."
