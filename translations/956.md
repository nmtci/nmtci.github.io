# Chapter 956: Slashing Down, Don't Turn Around, I'm Bu Yingshu

Although Zhu Yan was already mentally prepared to face this Ten-Billion Flying Sword, and although he was ready to face poverty head-on and had even transferred part of his assets in advance.

But when Zhang Yu's sword actually slashed toward him, Zhu Yan still couldn't help but feel a burst of heart-pounding terror.

What if the transferred assets were seized?

What if the Celestial Monster Immortal Emperor failed to deliver on his promises?

Wallets emptied, accounts frozen, companies bankrupted, bionics sold off, physical body dismantled... terrifying scenes of descending into poverty continuously surged into his mind at this moment.

But Zhu Yan knew he could not retreat!

It was a rare opportunity to latch onto the Celestial Monster Immortal Emperor, to participate in the Immortal Emperor Dao Struggle, to establish great merit, to join the establishment of the new Dao Lineage, and to become a Monster above Monsters. He was absolutely unwilling to miss it.

He did not want to hole up in the Myriad Laws Sect for his entire life, secretly being called a sect-born beast by certain human race supremacists. He did not want to be trampled underfoot by the human race forever, and even less did he want to never stand out or climb to the pinnacle of the Immortal Dao.

"Why won't you ever let me become an Ascension Realm cultivator?"

"Are the Sect Staff who got promoted all these years, the cultivators who stepped into the Ascension Realm, really all more outstanding than me?"

"Or is it because of my monsterfolk identity that I've always been ostracized by your Myriad Laws Sect? You're unwilling to give me a spot for Ascension?"

The longer he cultivated in the Myriad Laws Sect, the more Zhu Yan could feel that there was always an invisible ceiling above the heads of the monsterfolk within the sect.

Especially at the stage from Tribulation Transcendence to Ascension, almost no monsterfolk could break through.

The more this was the case, the more it made Zhu Yan, who was single-mindedly striving for the Apex of the Immortal Dao... every day he stayed in the Myriad Laws Sect was a torment.

An intense fighting spirit surged madly from the bottom of Zhu Yan's heart, making him resolve to take down the battle before him with all his strength!

"As long as I'm given a chance, I will prove that I, Zhu Yan, am not weaker than anyone in my entire life!"

***Boom!***

Zhu Yan's figure sank abruptly, and he had already knelt heavily on the ground.

"Zhang Yu! I surrender!"

Kneeling!

Begging for mercy!!

Zhu Yan's full-strength response at this moment was precisely to fight for a split-second opportunity.

As long as Zhang Yu hesitated even slightly, as long as the slash of the Ten-Billion Flying Sword was delayed by a mere fraction of a second, then...

Zhu Yan would be able to quickly pass the purchase application for the Permanent Tribulation Certificate within the "Once Certified, Always Certified" domain... then complete the payment and continue to legally possess his Tribulation Transcendence power.

"The Ten-Billion Flying Sword! It is a method that is most deterrent and capable of yielding endless profits only when kept unplayed in one's hand."

"Are you really willing to just throw away such a precious sword strike?"

"Zhang Yu! You should know best in your own heart that your status in the eyes of others will be vastly different depending on whether or not you hold this Ten-Billion Flying Sword."

All sorts of thoughts flashed through Zhu Yan's mind like lightning. At this moment, he was taking a gamble!

He was betting that Zhang Yu would have a trace of reluctance, a trace of hesitation toward the Ten-Billion Flying Sword in his hand.

"Zhang Yu, as long as you think about... saving this sword strike during our martial duel, you will undoubtedly lose!"

In Zhu Yan's view, if it were him, he would definitely hesitate.

Especially after seeing that the opponent had already surrendered, a trace of greed to save this strike and continue holding onto it would inevitably well up from the bottom of his heart.

After all, holding this sword to deter opponents, keeping it hidden without releasing it, should have been the truly most commonly used method after possessing this Ten-Billion Flying Sword.

But in the next moment, the surging sword light did not pause or hesitate for even a split second before thoroughly slashing down.

At this moment, Zhu Yan seemed to hear countless explosions coming from the void; it was the wailing of his countless accounts, funds, and companies being frozen.

"No!"

Along with the Bionic services, Spirit Realm services, Dharma Realm services, and Soul Cultivator services... all suspending operations due to halted payments.

Accompanied by the Monsterfolk Factory within his body gradually stopping production...

Under this sword, he was stripped of his three accounts for stocks, currency, and bonds were completely wiped out the five factories for breeding, cultivation, R&D, Calculation Pools, and Cold Storage within his chest were closed.

In an instant, Zhu Yan only felt the power within his body rapidly draining away.

The Sect-Guarding Technique, the Ten Thousand Children and Thousand Grandchildren Power, which was frantically surging within his body just a moment ago, also crashed to a halt as a result.

The assets he had accumulated over many years would all be completely wiped out under this sword.

This made Zhu Yan's heart ache so much he wanted to vomit blood.

"Beast!"

He looked at Zhang Yu with shock and anger, but a trace of admiration couldn't help but flash in his heart.

"Not even the slightest hesitation?"

"Perhaps... it is exactly this resolve — the courage to draw this sword and firmly swing it — that makes Zhang Yu the best wielder of this Ten-Billion Flying Sword, and elevates its deterrent power to the absolute limit."

"Firm and unyielding, his will cannot be taken..."

At this moment, Zhu Yan couldn't help but recall an evaluation someone had given Zhang Yu, and in his heart, he grew increasingly agreeable with it.

At the same time, looking at his own vision, seeing his status stuck on the payment page for the Permanent Tribulation Certificate, Zhu Yan thought to himself: "I was just a tiny bit short. If his Ten-Billion Flying Sword had been delayed even a little bit longer, I could have bought the permanent certificate."

"The Ten-Billion Flying Sword, combined with 'Once Certified, Always Certified' — he purposely wanted to make me run out of money to buy the certificate, rendering me unable to maintain my Tribulation Transcendence cultivation base."

"Truly worthy of the Myriad Laws Divine Sword. Even fighting a Tribulation Transcendence expert with a Void Refinement cultivation base, he was able to battle me to such an extent."

However, this inward acknowledgment didn't stop Zhu Yan from continuing to kneel on the ground, looking up at Zhang Yu with a pained expression.

Disguising himself as a loser, he roared angrily: "The Ten-Billion Flying Sword hasn't slain a single human, yet it slays me, a monsterfolk? And you still say you aren't discriminating against monsterfolk?!"

Meanwhile, Zhu Yan thought to himself, *"There is still a chance... Zhang Yu doesn't know that someone from above will cover this ten billion debt for me."*

*"So be complacent, Zhang Yu. Think that you've won, Zhang Yu."*

*"As long as you are immersed in this victory and don't make any further moves, just give me a little time..."*

***Boom!***

At this moment, it was as if a surging force in the Spirit Realm was poured into Zhu Yan's body, causing his frozen accounts, sealed companies, and factories to start operating again.

"It's here!"

"The Celestial Monster Immortal Emperor's promised backing is here!"

Zhu Yan immediately realized that someone had taken on this ten billion Debt Poison for him.

However, the other party didn't clear this ten billion Debt Poison all at once... Instead, they bought the debt as a package, making him repay it by the minute.

At the same time, waves of Immortal Coins were poured under Zhu Yan's name, constantly offsetting the debt.

"I see, it's about becoming my creditor. As long as I continue to be obedient, they will continue to suppress the Debt Poison for me, but if I'm disobedient..."

At this moment, Zhu Yan felt as if a large hand was firmly pressed against his back, constantly pouring Immortal Coins into him to suppress the Debt Poison.

And as long as this large hand was withdrawn, the Debt Poison in his body would need to be suppressed by himself, and the slightest carelessness would cause it to erupt explosively.

Regarding this, Zhu Yan did not feel surprised at all.

With the dignified status of an Immortal Emperor, how could the method of shouldering the Debt Poison for him be a one-time settlement?

*"This is fine too. Only by controlling me can the Immortal Emperor trust me more and be more willing to let me work for him! So many people want to be controlled by an Immortal Emperor but don't even have the chance."*

Like Zhang Yu in front of him, in Zhu Yan's eyes, for the other party to be able to become the Myriad Laws Divine Sword, he must have been firmly controlled by the Myriad Laws Immortal Emperor.

If not so, would the Immortal Emperor actually use someone he couldn't control to promote legal reforms within the sect? At this moment, Zhu Yan's mood was not only completely devoid of frustration, but instead became even more exhilarated.

And although Zhu Yan had thought a lot in his heart, and his account and funds had undergone massive changes, all of this only happened in the blink of an eye.

Almost at the very first moment when his ten billion debt was temporarily shouldered and his funds started flowing again, Zhu Yan had already tried to pay for the Permanent Tribulation Certificate in front of him with the fastest speed.

But at the same time, Zhang Yu had already made his move.

Without caring about Zhu Yan's reaction, and without any hesitation in his heart, Zhang Yu had already made his decision when he was heavily injured by the other party's two consecutive moves earlier.

*"Right now, I'm afraid this already involves the Immortal Emperor Dao Struggle, involving the birth of a new Dao Lineage."*

*"Since the opponent dared to attack directly, there is probably no room for mediation left."*

*"At this time, any hesitation could lead to defeat."*

Therefore, when Zhu Yan begged for mercy and surrendered, Zhang Yu did not stop.

Even after Zhu Yan was struck down by the Ten-Billion Flying Sword, Zhang Yu still did not stop.

Since he had to make a move, and since he had already used the Ten-Billion Flying Sword, Zhang Yu wanted to ensure success in one battle and ensure the opponent was completely suppressed.

So after the Ten-Billion Flying Sword struck down, Zhang Yu had already launched his second move.

Surging vital energy gushed out. At this moment, Zhang Yu impressively activated the [Blazing Sun Meridian], operated the "Kunpeng Swallowing Heaven Manual", pushed his physical body to a transcendent state at the Tribulation Transcendence level, and ruthlessly blasted toward Zhu Yan in front of him.

At the same time, the power of the Dao Seed [Sovereign of the Nine Heavens] also suddenly activated, vibrating toward Zhu Yan in front of him. 

Sovereign of the Nine Heavens was originally Di Xuan's Dao Seed Ability, which was copied by Zhang Yu after being stolen by Tai Yuebai.

The level 2 Sovereign of the Nine Heavens could temporarily dissipate an opponent's power and stall the circulation of spiritual energy and magic power. The level 3 Sovereign of the Nine Heavens could even shake an opponent's will, causing their thinking to briefly pause.

However, under normal circumstances, even if Zhang Yu used the power of Sovereign of the Nine Heavens, it would probably be difficult for him to shake Zhu Yan, who was at the Tribulation Transcendence Realm.

But at this moment, Zhu Yan had not yet renewed his certificate, making him unable to exert his full cultivation, which provided Zhang Yu with an exploitable opportunity.

Thus, just as Zhu Yan suppressed the Debt Poison and was about to complete the payment for his permanent certificate, Zhang Yu ruthlessly blasted a strike onto his body.

The dual shock to his physical body and mind caused Zhu Yan's thinking to directly stagnate.

Looking at Zhu Yan, whose body had grown stiff, Zhang Yu glanced at the fine for injuring others sent by the Righteous Gods, directly paid the fee, and then blasted another ruthless strike at Zhu Yan.

He did not focus on destroying Zhu Yan; instead, he continuously shocked the opponent's physical body and mind, strike after strike, to prolong the duration of his cognitive stagnation.

At the same time, a Spirit Realm Projection suddenly descended upon the minor realm; it was none other than Venerable Serpent Lust.

It turned out that the Venerable Serpent Lust who had struck out alongside Zhu Yan was merely an incarnation. His true body had been observing the battle remotely, ready to support Zhu Yan at any time.

However, he never imagined that Zhang Yu, despite being ambushed by Zhu Yan who was a cultivation realm higher than him, would actually manage to block two consecutive moves and then continuously shift the battlefield.

"Troublesome... the situation is out of control."

Originally, according to the plan of Venerable Serpent Lust and Zhu Yan, Zhang Yu should have been subdued by Zhu Yan at the very first moment, allowing them to control the scene. There was supposed to be no resistance at all, let alone transferring the battlefield to another minor realm.

"With such a commotion now, I'm afraid that with every passing second, the possibility of someone coming to Zhang Yu's aid grows."

"Fortunately, the vast majority of the sect's manpower and resources have been constantly drawn to the Immortal Emperor's side these past few days..."

And when Venerable Serpent Lust finally managed to find this minor realm and descend his projection, what he saw was the scene of Zhu Yan collapsed in front of Zhang Yu, having completely lost the power to resist.

"How is this possible?!"

Looking at this scene, Venerable Serpent Lust was shocked: "Zhang Yu used his Void Refinement cultivation to strike above his realm against Tribulation Transcendence, and actually suppressed Zhu Yan?"

But soon, Venerable Serpent Lust saw through the secret behind it: "No, Zhang Yu didn't suppress him with sheer strength. He relied on the methods of the Ten-Billion Flying Sword and Once Certified, Always Certified to temporarily prevent Zhu Yan from exerting his cultivation, thus controlling him."

"Right now, Zhang Yu seems to have the absolute upper hand, but in reality, with his strength, it is simply impossible for him to truly harm Zhu Yan in a short period of time."

Venerable Serpent Lust understood in his heart that although Zhu Yan was temporarily unable to exert his Tribulation Transcendence cultivation base, his terrifying physical strength was still there; it was simply not something Zhang Yu could easily destroy.

*"As long as Zhu Yan is given a sliver of a chance to break free from control, he can immediately turn defeat into victory..."*

Venerable Serpent Lust glanced at the time and thought to himself, *"I must use the Coitus Conscription."*

Originally, Venerable Serpent Lust had planned for Zhu Yan to launch a sneak attack and suppress Zhang Yu before initiating the Coitus Conscription himself.

But the situation had suddenly changed. To lend Zhu Yan a helping hand, he could only choose to directly initiate the Coitus Conscription now. Once Zhang Yu began fulfilling the Coitus Conscription, Zhu Yan would be able to break free.

The next moment, accompanied by a surge of hidden Immortal Qi, Zhang Yu felt a certain Law Edict power being activated against him.

His gaze sank as a Coitus Conscription notice stemming from the Dual Cultivation Obligation emerged before his eyes. *"Coitus Conscription?"*

*"My matched partner is..."*

Seeing the other party's information, Zhang Yu furrowed his brows. *"Hmph, trying to use the Coitus Conscription to pin me down and allow Zhu Yan to break free?"*

Just as this thought flashed through Zhang Yu's mind, the space in front of him rippled, and a graceful figure gradually materialized before him.

Before that figure had even truly descended, bursts of a terrifying aura rushed at him, stimulating Zhang Yu so much that his vital energy surged in an instant, and his cheeks turned flushed.

"If I get entangled with this person, Zhu Yan will probably slip out of my control immediately, and then I'll be the one being controlled by them instead."

Thus, watching this scene, Zhang Yu grabbed Zhu Yan, using Sovereign of the Nine Heavens to knock him unconscious again while simultaneously flashing forward at lightning speed.

"I can't pull the current Zhu Yan into the Immortal Grotto-Heaven. Once we leave the range of Once Certified, Always Certified, he will immediately recover his cultivation, and I won't be able to control him..."

A series of explosive booms rang out in the air as Zhang Yu dashed over ten kilometers away in a flash.

But in the next moment, another ripple in space appeared, and that graceful figure followed closely behind.

Immortal Slayer read through the details of the Coitus Conscription and said, "You can't run away. The Coitus Conscription is part of the sect's laws. It can directly borrow the tracking, locking, and teleportation services within the sect."

"Unless you run outside the territory of the Myriad Laws Sect, you might be able to temporarily shake them off."

Zhang Yu glanced at Zhu Yan in his hand and thought, "But as long as we leave the minor realm of Once Certified, Always Certified, I'm afraid this Zhu Yan will immediately recover."

"Then could I... right here in this minor realm, fulfill the Coitus Conscription while keeping Zhu Xing under control?"

Immortal Slayer said, "Are you sure you can handle your opponent in this Coitus Conscription?"

Zhang Yu thought to himself, "At worst, we'll just have a fight! My sword is not dull either!"

Fu Ji advised, "Zhang Yu! Others call you a social butterfly and a dual cultivation prodigy, do you really believe it yourself? Have you looked at the other party's information? What do you have to fight with? I'm afraid you'll be sucked dry in a single encounter!"

The Herald God thought to himself, *"Who could have imagined that Zhang Yu, famous for his dual cultivation innate talent, actually has nothing but shortcomings in this aspect. Hehe, just like how valuing old ties is also a layer of his disguise, this guy Zhang Yu... is simply a person who excels at disguising himself with a fake persona to seek benefits for himself."*

On the other side, Venerable Serpent Lust was observing the scene of Zhang Yu constantly dodging the Coitus Conscription.

"What will you choose, Zhang Yu?"

The graceful figure being repeatedly teleported by the Coitus Conscription was getting closer and closer to Zhang Yu, looking as if she would entangle him at any moment to begin fulfilling the Coitus Conscription.

"If you keep running in circles in this minor realm, you are going to be caught very soon, Zhang Yu."

Venerable Serpent Lust speculated inwardly, "As soon as Zhu Yan leaves the area of Once Certified, Always Certified, he will recover immediately. So if you want to continue avoiding the Coitus Conscription and hide somewhere else, you must leave Zhu Yan behind."

"If you want to continue using Once Certified, Always Certified to suppress Zhu Yan, you have to fulfill the Coitus Conscription here. And then... Zhu Yan will still be able to break free."

"You have no choice left."

But in the very next moment, Venerable Serpent Lust heard Zhang Yu's explosive shouts echoing through the sky, spreading in all directions like rolling thunder.

"Here is a Tribulation Transcendence Realm monsterfolk who has temporarily lost consciousness, doesn't have a Tribulation Certificate, and is lying on the ground completely defenseless! Is there anyone who can come and save him?"

Hearing Zhang Yu's explosive shouts, Venerable Serpent Lust's expression changed.

In fact, the moment Zhang Yu and Zhu Yan descended into the minor realm and engaged in their fierce battle, many surrounding cultivators had already turned their attention to them.

And when Zhang Yu broadcasted this message, it drew the focus of even more cultivators.

"Is it really a Tribulation Transcendence Profound Monarch?"

"He seems to have lost consciousness?"

"Within the domain of Once Certified, Always Certified, he actually didn't pay the fee? He didn't buy a new certificate?"

Under the observation of countless cultivators, all sorts of information regarding Zhu Yan surfaced.

If a conscious Tribulation Transcendence Cultivator inspired awe and reverence, then Zhu Yan's current state was akin to a child holding an Immortal Crystal in a busy market, with all the surrounding cultivators acting like demons.

To the Kunxu Cultivators, who harbored an aspiration for the Immortal Dao and pursued profit their entire lives, Zhu Yan's defenseless state at this moment was... simply too tempting.

And Zhang Yu understood that although Kunxu's various laws and rules were strict, they were never meant to protect the weak.

To survive in the sect, one needed to stay clear-headed at all times and always possess the ability and strength to protect oneself.

When faced with a wealthy individual who had temporarily lost the ability to think, the Kunxu Cultivators had plenty of ways to legally and compliantly bite off chunks of meat from them.

Meanwhile, as the manager of 'Once Certified, Always Certified', Di Xuan currently had most of his energy and strength tied up by sudden emergencies within the sect. Because he was busy dealing with numerous official duties, he did not immediately notice the descent of Zhang Yu and Zhu Yan, nor their instantaneous clash.

But now, following a series of abnormal commotions, Di Xuan had also noticed the presence of Zhang Yu and Zhu Yan.

Quickly reviewing the combat recording, when Di Xuan saw Zhang Yu slash the Ten-Billion Flying Sword down at the monsterfolk Zhu Yan, as a supporter of the Innate Dao Lineage... he couldn't help but cheer in his heart.

"A fine slash! We should ruthlessly deal with these monsterfolk."

To him, the monsterfolk Zhu Yan taking this sword strike, and Zhang Yu losing the threat of the Ten-Billion Flying Sword, were both absolutely good things.

And at this very moment, when he saw Zhu Yan fall to the ground without any power to resist, a sharp light flashed in Di Xuan's eyes.

After confirming that more and more intents were gathering over, Zhang Yu's figure moved, already crossing the Grotto-Heaven Bridge and leaving this minor realm.

As for Zhu Yan on the ground, a large circle of cultivators had unknowingly surrounded him, looking at him covetously.

The next moment, a Spirit Realm Projection transformed into a large hand and grabbed toward Zhu Yan.

"Everyone get out of the way! I am a good friend of Profound Monarch Zhu, I will take him home."

But soon, another Dharma Realm Projection descended, shouting, "Profound Monarch Zhu has clearly suffered severe injuries. I am from a pharmaceutical company, I will give him first aid."

With a loud boom, a Tribulation Transcendence Realm power suddenly descended — it was Di Xuan making a grab for Zhu Yan.

"Profound Monarch Zhu is now an unlicensed cultivator, so naturally, he falls under my jurisdiction."

Another burst of power descended: "Given that Profound Monarch Zhu has temporarily lost consciousness, according to the contract, we need to temporarily reclaim the leased magic on him..."

Bursts of power descended one after another to surround Zhu Yan, and even many Soul Cultivators gathered around. Like a dense swarm of flies swarming rotten meat, they gradually and completely enveloped Zhu Yan's figure.

"Damn it! As fellow Sect Staff of the Myriad Laws Sect, do these guys only know how to kick someone when they're down?"

Watching this scene, Venerable Serpent Lust felt furious in his heart: "In the end, it's still because of our identity as monsterfolk. The cultivators within this Myriad Laws Sect have never treated us as their own."

While selling off the shares of Zhu Yan's company, Venerable Serpent Lust observed the latest location where the Coitus Conscription had descended: *"I only hope that that person can ruthlessly subject Zhang Yu to suppression during the Coitus Conscription, delaying his continued Law Edict freezing..."*

When Zhang Yu's figure reappeared, he had already arrived at Tai Yuebai's minor realm.

At the same time, the graceful figure teleported by the Coitus Conscription also appeared, getting closer and closer to Zhang Yu.

Wafts of a sweet and cloying scent blew against his face from the other party, causing a sudden impulse to arise in Zhang Yu's heart... an impulse to turn around and embrace her.

The Profound Cold Magic Power suddenly circulated, freezing Zhang Yu's figure and allowing him to temporarily suppress these distracting thoughts.

"This guy... is his scent alone radiating attraction?"

Venerable Serpent Lust had tracked the Coitus Conscription information all the way here. Watching this scene, he thought to himself, "Has he come to Tai Yuebai's place? But today is not a Dual Rest day."

And as soon as he arrived at Tai Yuebai's minor realm, Zhang Yu shouted loudly, "Comp time!"

His figure flickered repeatedly, constantly dodging the pursuit of the Coitus Conscription, while he shouted, "Big Brother Tai! I'm taking comp time!"

Right at this moment, the graceful figure paired for the Coitus Conscription finally emerged, smiling faintly at Zhang Yu as they said, "Dao Monarch Zhang, I have been looking forward to this Coitus Conscription for a long time."

Zhang Yu looked at the familiar face of the other party; it was surprisingly the exact likeness of Bu Yingshu.

But Zhang Yu knew that the other party was definitely not Bu Yingshu.

"The Joyful Union Sect's Myriad Laws Envoy... the Yin-Yang Immortal Clan's Venerable Myriad Affinity." Zhang Yu stared at the other party and said, "Are you cooperating with Venerable Serpent Lust to obstruct the Myriad Laws Sect's legal reform and interfere in another sect's internal affairs?"

Venerable Myriad Affinity, wearing the same face as Bu Yingshu, smiled faintly and spoke with a gruff male voice, "Isn't Coitus Conscription a legal and compliant matter in the Myriad Laws Sect? I am abiding by your esteemed sect's laws, so what is the problem?"

"Speaking of which, I once offered terms to Bu Yingshu, willing to bear ninety percent of the fine to invite her to cultivate together, but she refused."

The next moment, Venerable Myriad Affinity spoke again in a crisp female voice, "To my surprise, she has interacted with you quite a few times. I heard she even paid a hundred percent of the fine?"

As Venerable Myriad Affinity spoke, he adjusted his voice, his tone sounding more and more like Bu Yingshu: "This makes me very curious about you."

"Dao Monarch Zhang, just treat me as Bu Yingshu today, and use all the moves you used on her on me as well."

"Consider it a technical exchange between our two sects."

Watching the other party draw closer and closer, Zhang Yu's eyes filled with wariness. Having read the other party's file, he knew their fierce reputation was simply too infamous.

"The Yin-Yang Immortal Clan, with one generation as Yin and the next as Yang, is an Immortal Clan capable of self-reproduction and self-inheritance."

"What they excel at most is twisting the desires of others, turning them into lunatics obsessed with them and addicted to the Dao of Yin-Yang harmony."

"They can even transform others into their cauldrons, using the Heaven-Man Incarnation Method to squeeze dry all of the cauldron's potential in order to breed the next generation of themselves."

"The innate talent of the Yin-Yang Immortal Clan grew stronger with each generation because of this. Every time they reproduced themselves, their innate talent and aptitude would advance a step further."

"And the cultivation art this Venerable Myriad Affinity is most skilled in is a degraded version of the Heaven-Opening cultivation art *Ten Suns' Demise*, the Sect-Guarding-Tier *Eighteen Days' Demise*..."

"Just listen to this name! Demise! And Eighteen Days!"

Zhang Yu thought to himself, "This kind of opponent is still too early for me to face."

Venerable Myriad Affinity looked at Zhang Yu's reaction and thought inwardly, *"Just from my observations so far, this guy's lust quotient doesn't seem high. Is he deliberately hiding his strength?"*

The words Venerable Myriad Affinity had just spoken to Zhang Yu were naturally all lies.

*"Inside and outside the Myriad Laws Sect, it is currently a troubled time, with open strife and veiled struggles everywhere."*

As a Tribulation Transcendence cultivator of the Joyful Union Sect, he had received instructions from his superiors this time to take the opportunity to obstruct the Myriad Laws Sect's institutional reforms, and ideally, completely destroy Zhang Yu, this rising star of dual cultivation who had suddenly emerged within the Myriad Laws Sect.

"If this Zhang Yu's resume is real, then he might... be the heavenly chosen who will lead the rise of the Myriad Laws dual cultivation world."

"This boy absolutely cannot be allowed to stay hard."

"In passing..." Venerable Myriad Affinity licked the corners of his mouth, "I'll first taste a bit of Bu Yingshu's flavor on him."

The next moment, Venerable Myriad Affinity's figure had already appeared behind Zhang Yu, reaching out to pull him into an embrace.

"Don't turn around, Dao Monarch Zhang, I am Bu Yingshu."
